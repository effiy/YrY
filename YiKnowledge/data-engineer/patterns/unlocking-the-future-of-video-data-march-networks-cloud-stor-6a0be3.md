<h2 id="the-challenge-managing-enterprise-video-at-scale">The challenge: Managing enterprise video at scale</h2> 
<p>Historically, enterprise video has been stored on local network video recorders (NVRs) and on-premise servers deployed at each site. Although this model provides localized control, it creates fragmented storage environments that require frequent hardware expansion, ongoing maintenance, and inconsistent retention policies across locations. This also limits organizations’ ability to centrally access, analyze, and govern video data across their enterprise.</p> 
<p>As organizations increase video retention periods for compliance, liability protection, and operational intelligence, infrastructure requirements grow rapidly. Adding local storage hardware across hundreds or thousands of sites increases operational complexity and introduces lifecycle management challenges.</p> 
<h2 id="the-economic-impact-of-cloud-video-storage">The economic impact of cloud video storage</h2> 
<p>Cloud storage introduces a more flexible model by consolidating distributed video data into centralized, elastic storage infrastructure. Even partial migration (such as moving long-term retention or compliance archives to the cloud), can significantly reduce infrastructure overhead while enabling centralized data management and analytics.</p> 
<p>The financial impact of this shift can be substantial. For example, one retail organization evaluated the benefit of moving to a hybrid cloud storage model to extend video retention for a period of up to 5 years — a common retention window driven by compliance standards and laws — without adding new on-premise hardware. This customer operated more than 580 cameras, generating approximately 5,600 TB of archived video. The total storage required depends on factors such as video bitrate and quality, camera count, and backup duration. Their estimated cloud storage cost using a third-party cloud provider was approximately $347,000 per year, compared to roughly $1.7 million annually to store the same volume of video on-premise. For long-term cloud storage, data is not expired or deleted; customers are notified as their storage quota approaches capacity and can purchase additional storage as needed. By retaining recent footage locally while archiving older video to a third-party cloud provider, the organization significantly reduced storage costs while maintaining access to archived footage when needed.</p>

<h3 id="video-ingestion-and-storage">Video ingestion and storage</h3> 
<p>March Networks securely uploads video recorded on local NVRs to Amazon S3 buckets using encrypted transmission protocols. <a href="https://aws.amazon.com/pm/serv-s3/?trk=7f874f1e-8322-484a-89b5-535f065224e5&amp;sc_channel=ps&amp;ef_id=CjwKCAjwpcTNBhA5EiwAdO1S9qfXU2UL6x8--JqsDMUk-QtluP9Oz6xYC6n8scnr6YKPAr6jHoiDYhoCNv0QAvD_BwE:G:s&amp;s_kwcid=AL!4422!3!798517304589!e!!g!!amazon%20s3!23606217092!193598648053&amp;gad_campaignid=23606217092&amp;gbraid=0AAAAADjHtp8_4H7KGURsZ00zv9ckIWWGs&amp;gclid=CjwKCAjwpcTNBhA5EiwAdO1S9qfXU2UL6x8--JqsDMUk-QtluP9Oz6xYC6n8scnr6YKPAr6jHoiDYhoCNv0QAvD_BwE" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Amazon S3</a> provides highly durable object storage designed to store large volumes of data while enabling efficient retrieval and lifecycle management.</p> 
<p>Once stored, organizations can retain video data for active investigations or operational review. Organizations configure lifecycle management policies that automatically move older footage to lower-cost storage tiers based on their access patterns.</p> 
<h3 id="tiered-storage-with-amazon-s3-and-amazon-s3-glacier">Tiered storage with Amazon S3 and Amazon S3 Glacier</h3> 
<p>Video storage requirements vary depending on how frequently footage must be accessed. The platform uses multiple <a href="https://aws.amazon.com/pm/serv-s3/?trk=7f874f1e-8322-484a-89b5-535f065224e5&amp;sc_channel=ps&amp;ef_id=CjwKCAjwpcTNBhA5EiwAdO1S9qfXU2UL6x8--JqsDMUk-QtluP9Oz6xYC6n8scnr6YKPAr6jHoiDYhoCNv0QAvD_BwE:G:s&amp;s_kwcid=AL!4422!3!798517304589!e!!g!!amazon%20s3!23606217092!193598648053&amp;gad_campaignid=23606217092&amp;gbraid=0AAAAADjHtp8_4H7KGURsZ00zv9ckIWWGs&amp;gclid=CjwKCAjwpcTNBhA5EiwAdO1S9qfXU2UL6x8--JqsDMUk-QtluP9Oz6xYC6n8scnr6YKPAr6jHoiDYhoCNv0QAvD_BwE" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Amazon S3</a> tiers to align performance and cost with real-world video access patterns.</p> 
<p><a href="https://aws.amazon.com/s3/storage-classes/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Amazon S3 Standard</a> and <a href="https://aws.amazon.com/s3/storage-classes/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Amazon S3 Standard-Infrequent Access</a> (S3 Standard-IA) support video that must remain readily accessible for investigations, operational review, or analytics. For long-term retention, the platform uses <a href="https://aws.amazon.com/s3/storage-classes/glacier/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Amazon S3 Glacier</a> storage tiers to provide ultra-low-cost archival storage for footage that must be preserved but is rarely accessed.</p> 
<p>Lifecycle policies automatically transition videos between tiers according to customer-defined retention policies. This allows organizations to store high-value recent video on high-performance storage while archiving older footage economically.</p> 
<h3 id="supporting-aws-services">Supporting AWS services</h3> 
<p>Several <a href="https://aws.amazon.com/?nc2=h_home&amp;refid=7f874f1e-8322-484a-89b5-535f065224e5" rel="noopener" target="_blank">AWS</a> services support the reliability, scalability, and operational visibility of the platform:</p> 
<ul> 
 <li><a href="https://aws.amazon.com/sqs/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Amazon Simple Queue Service</a> (Amazon SQS) manages asynchronous messaging between system components, enabling reliable communication between ingestion, processing, and storage services.</li> 
 <li><a href="https://aws.amazon.com/ses/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Amazon Simple Email Service</a> (Amazon SES) provides notification capabilities for operational alerts and system events.</li> 
 <li><a href="https://aws.amazon.com/cloudwatch/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Amazon CloudWatch</a> monitors system performance, logs activity, and provides operational visibility into cloud infrastructure.</li> 
 <li><a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/STS/latest/APIReference/API_Types.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank">AWS Security Token Service</a> (AWS STS) enables secure authentication and temporary credentials for system components accessing cloud resources.</li> 
</ul> 
<p>For metadata management and caching, the platform uses <a href="https://aws.amazon.com/rds/postgresql/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">PostgreSQL</a> and <a href="https://www.amazonaws.cn/en/elasticache/redis/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Amazon ElastiCache for Redis</a> to maintain high-performance access to video metadata and system state.</p> 
<p>Together, these services enable <a href="https://www.marchnetworks.com" rel="noopener" target="_blank">March Networks</a> to deliver a secure, scalable cloud architecture capable of supporting petabyte-scale video workloads across distributed environments.</p>
