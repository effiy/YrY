<div class="upx-component upx-embed-url"><div class="embed-holder"></div></div><p>Does the <a href="https://uproxx.com/dimemag/" target="_blank">NBA Finals</a> between the <a href="https://uproxx.com/music/mf-doom-new-york-knicks-merch-collection-info/" target="_blank">New York Knicks</a> vs. <a href="https://uproxx.com/dimemag/victor-wembanyama-other-players-messages-join-spurs/" target="_blank">San Antonio Spurs</a> have you in the mood for all the basketball you can get? You&#8217;re in luck: <em>NBA The Run</em>, a 3v3 arcade-style video game inspired by early 2000s streetball, is out today, June 9.</p>
<p><em>NBA The Run</em> brings the game to the streets with current NBA stars and fictional streetball legends competing in fast-paced online multiplayer Knockout Tournaments on 11 courts spanning the globe, including Venice Beach and Harlem&#8217;s Rucker Park. You can play solo or with a squad. The game&#8217;s announcer is legendary DJ, radio host, and the voice of <em>NBA Street Vol. 2</em> and <em>NBA Street V3</em>, Bobbito Garcia.</p>
<p>“As fans of pretty much every basketball video game ever made, we have very big shoes to fill &#8211; and we don&#8217;t take that lightly. Our partnership with the [National Basketball Association ] &#038; [National Basketball Players Association] takes us to a whole new level and we&#8217;re beyond excited about the collaboration and the potential of <em>NBA The Run</em>,&#8221; said Play by Play Studios founder and CEO Scott Probst. “We’re a small team, but we’re scrappy, focused, and relentless about creating an all new smash hit.&#8221;</p>
<p>He added, &#8220;This game is for every hoops fan across the globe.”</p>

There&#8217;s also a deluxe edition — which includes rookie variants of Kevin Durant, Stephen Curry and Luka Dončić, among other features — for $39.99. </p>
