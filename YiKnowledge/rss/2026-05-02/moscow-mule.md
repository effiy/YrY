<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-reasons-to-make-a-moscow-mule">reasons to make a moscow mule</h2>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Easiest cocktail ever.</strong> A Moscow Mule is exceptionally easy to make, perfect for beginners. Specialty equipment like a shaker or blender is not required. And with these easy to follow tips (more on that below), this recipe is fool-proof!</li>



<li><strong>Short ingredient list.</strong> All you need is four ingredients here &#8211; vodka, lime, ginger beer, fizzy water (if using), and mint to garnish.</li>



<li><strong>So refreshing.</strong> We&#8217;re talking crisp, refreshing, tangy and slightly fizzy with just a hint of spicy goodness without it being overly sweet, perfect for warm, summer nights or a fancy dinner party.</li>
</ul>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="Moscow Mule cocktail ingredients" class="wp-image-45644" height="1140" src="https://damndelicious.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Moscow-Mule-1-760x1140.jpg" width="760" /></figure>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-what-is-a-moscow-mule">What is a moscow Mule?</h2>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-a-moscow-mule-is-a-classic-cocktail-made-with-vodka-ginger-beer-and-lime-juice-typically-served-over-ice-in-a-copper-mug">A Moscow Mule is a classic cocktail made with vodka, ginger beer and lime juice, typically served over ice in a copper mug.</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The Moscow mule and copper mug actually all came together in 1941 in the United States in order to popularize Smirnoff vodka as a marketing campaign. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A Russian immigrant was trying to sell her inventory of copper mugs that were made in her father&#8217;s copper factory back in Russia. She essentially found buyers to promote their cocktail (vodka + ginger beer, two undesirable ingredients at the time), essentially giving the Moscow Mule their signature look to stand out while also keeping the drink exceptionally cold. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Shortly after, Smirnoff vodka became the vodka of choice for Moscow Mules.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-why-are-moscow-mules-served-in-copper-mugs">Why are moscow mules served in copper mugs?</h2>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-copper-is-an-excellent-conductor">Copper is an excellent conductor.</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">While Moscow Mules are certainly not required to be served in copper mugs, the copper metal is highly conductive (think superior insulation), keeping the drink refreshing and frosty cold for longer periods of time.</p>
</div></div>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-key-ingredients-in-a-moscow-mule">Key Ingredients in a moscow Mule</h2>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-vodka">Vodka</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Choose a premium vodka you will drink – it does not have to be anything fancy or overpriced.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-lime-juice">Lime juice</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Freshly squeezed lime juice is always best.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-ginger-beer">Ginger beer</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Opt for ginger beer with strong, bold ginger flavors. Fever-Tree Premium Ginger Beer is a favorited choice for its strong ginger flavors without it being overly sweet.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-fizzy-water">Fizzy water</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Use sparkling water or club soda to balance out some of the strong flavors (such as the ginger beer) while also making the cocktail lighter and more refreshing.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-optional-sweetener">Optional sweetener</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If you prefer a sweeter Moscow Mule, add additional ginger beer (4 oz total) in place of the fizzy water and/or add a splash of <a href="https://damndelicious.net/2015/07/08/perfect-iced-coffee/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">simple syrup</a>.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-garnish">Garnish</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Finish off your cocktail with a lime wedge and mint sprig.</p>
</div></div>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-tips-and-tricks-for-perfect-moscow-mules-every-single-time">Tips and tricks for perfect Moscow Mules every single time</h2>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Chill beforehand.</strong> Chill the vodka and copper mugs beforehand, ensuring that the cocktail is immediately cold upon serving and preventing the ice from melting too quickly particularly in hot weather.</li>



<li><strong>Use ginger beer, not ginger ale.</strong> Be sure to use premium, high quality ginger beer such as Fever Tree or Q.</li>



<li><strong>Use freshly squeezed lime juice.</strong> While bottled lime juice can certainly work in a pinch, freshly squeezed lime juice will provide the best, refreshing, zesty flavor in a Moscow Mule.</li>



<li><strong>Stir gently.</strong> Avoid shaking the cocktail vigorously &#8211; a gentle stir is ideal to mix the ingredients together without losing any of the fizziness (carbonation).</li>



<li><strong>Use copper mugs with food-safe lining.</strong> Pure copper mugs can leach copper into drinks. Opt for copper mugs with a safe interior coating such as stainless steel or nickel.</li>
</ul>



<div class="wp-block-group is-style-qna block-ingredients"><div class="wp-block-group__inner-container is-layout-constrained wp-block-group-is-layout-constrained">
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-pro-tip">Pro tip</h2>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-make-it-virgin">Make it virgin.</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Skip the vodka, add 1-2 oz additional fizzy water, and use non-alcoholic ginger beer.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-variations-of-moscow-mule">Variations of moscow Mule</h2>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-mexican-mule">Mexican Mule</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Substitute tequila for vodka</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-kentucky-mule">Kentucky Mule</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Substitute bourbon for vodka</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-gin-gin-mule">Gin Gin Mule</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Substitute gin for vodka</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-rum-mule-caribbean-jamaican-mule">Rum Mule (Caribbean/Jamaican Mule)</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Substitute dark or spiced rum for vodka</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-irish-mule">Irish Mule</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Substitute Irish whiskey (ex. Jameson) for vodka</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-strawberry-mule">Strawberry Mule</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Add muddled fresh strawberries</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-spicy-jalapeno-mule">Spicy Jalapeño Mule</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Add muddled jalapeño slices</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-pineapple-mule">Pineapple Mule</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Add pineapple juice</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-watermelon-mule">Watermelon Mule</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Add fresh watermelon juice</p>
