<p>This tangy relish from Nova Scotia native Florence Jackson makes the most of green tomatoes, the ultimate shoulder-season produce. Serve it alongside meat and fish dishes to add a bright note of sweetness. While this chow chow can be used immediately, consider making it ahead or canning a few jars, as its flavor improves with time.</p>



<p><strong>Featured in “</strong><a href="https://www.saveur.com/roots-of-african-nova-scotian-cuisine/" target="_self"><strong>The Underexplored Roots of Black Cooking in Nova Scotia</strong></a><strong>” by Chantal Martineau in the <a href="https://www.zinio.com/publications/saveur/3287/issues/398254" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">2018 Volume 3 issue</a>.</strong> <strong>See more recipes and stories from <a href="https://www.saveur.com/tags/issue-195/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_self">Issue 195</a>.</strong></p>


<div class="orgnc-Recipe" id="recipe">
        
                        <div class="yield">
                <b>Makes:</b> 2 cups            </div>
                            <div class="yield">
                <b>Time</b>: 1 hour 10 minutes             </div>
                
            <h3 class="ingredients">Ingredients</h3>
        <div class="ingredients_section">
                            <div class="section">
                                        <ul class="ingredients" id="recipe-ingredients">
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                  1¼ lb. green (unripened) tomatoes, cored and finely chopped (3 cups)                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                  5 oz. onion, finely chopped (1 cup)                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                  ¼ cup finely chopped green or red bell pepper                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                  1 Tbsp. kosher salt                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                  ¼ tsp. coriander seeds                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                  ¼ tsp. brown mustard seeds                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                  3 allspice berries                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                  3 black peppercorns                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                  1 bay leaf                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                  1 clove                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                  ½ small cinnamon stick                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                  Ground cardamom                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                  Ground ginger                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                  ⅔ cup distilled white vinegar                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                  3 Tbsp. sugar                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                  2 Tbsp. light brown sugar                            </li>
                                            </ul>
                    <div class="chicory-order-ingredients"></div>                </div>
                    </div>
                <h3 class="instructions">Instructions</h3>
        <ol class="instructions multiple">
                            <li class="instruction">
                    In a large bowl, combine the tomatoes, onion, bell pepper, and salt. Cover the bowl with a plate and set aside at room temperature for at least 6 hours, or up to 12 hours.                </li>
                            <li class="instruction">
                    Place a fine-mesh strainer over a medium bowl and drain the tomato mixture, gently pressing to extract as much liquid as possible. Discard the liquid. Transfer the tomato mixture to a medium pot.                </li>
                            <li class="instruction">
                    To a double-layer square of cheesecloth, add the coriander, mustard, allspice, peppercorns, bay leaf, clove, cinnamon, and a pinch each of cardamom and ginger, then use kitchen twine to tie into a tight bundle.                </li>
                            <li class="instruction">
                    Add the vinegar, both sugars, and the spice sachet to the pot and bring to a boil over medium heat. Turn the heat to medium-low and simmer, stirring occasionally, until the chow chow has reduced to a fragrant, dry relish, 40–50 minutes. Refrigerate until chilled. The chow chow will keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 1 month.                </li>
                    </ol>
    </div>
