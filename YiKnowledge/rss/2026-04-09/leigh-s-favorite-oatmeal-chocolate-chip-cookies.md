<p><strong>Ingredients</strong></p>
<p><br />1 1/2 cup (200 grams) all-purpose flour</p>
<p><br />1 teaspoon baking soda</p>
<p><br />1 teaspoon fine sea salt</p>
<p><br />1 teaspoon cinnamon</p>
<p><br />16 tablespoons (228 grams) unsalted butter, softened</p>
<p><br />1 cup (188 grams) packed brown sugar</p>
<p><br />1/2 cup (118 grams) granulated sugar</p>
<p><br />2 large eggs, at room temperature</p>
<p><br />4 tablespoons heavy cream</p>
<p><br />4 teaspoons pure vanilla extract</p>
<p><br />3 cups (216 grams) old-fashioned rolled oats</p>
<p><br />2 cups (278 grams) semi-sweet chocolate chips</p>
<p><br />1 cup (126 grams) cayenne-pepper-toasted, chopped pecans**</p>
<p>**toast pecans:<br /> 1 cup chopped pecans,<br /> 1 teaspoon ground cayenne, 1 tablespoon melted butter</p>
<p> </p>
<p><strong>Directions</strong></p>
<p>Step 1. Preheat oven to 350 F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. Toss the<br />pecans, cayenne, and butter together. Spread out on the baking sheet and bake for 10<br />minutes. Let the nuts cool before using.</p>
<p><br />Step 2. Keep oven at 350 and line cookie sheets with parchment paper.</p>
<p><br />Step 3. Whisk the flour, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon in a small bowl. Whisk it good.<br />Mix the butter and both sugars in a large bowl with a wooden spoon until it’s creamy<br />and screaming for mercy. Beat in the egg until incorporated, then stir in the cream and<br />vanilla.</p>
<p><br />Step 4. Add the flour mixture and gently stir until no traces of flour remain. Add the<br />oats, chocolate chips and nuts, folding them in until evenly distributed. Loosely scoop a<br />rounded ball of dough - I use a 2 tbsp ice cream scoop - and drop onto prepared sheet,<br />spacing your balls 2 inches apart.</p>
<p><br />Step 5. Bake, 1 sheet at a time, until golden brown, 12 to 14 minutes. Cool on the sheet<br />for 1 minute, then transfer cookies to wire rack to cool completely.</p>
