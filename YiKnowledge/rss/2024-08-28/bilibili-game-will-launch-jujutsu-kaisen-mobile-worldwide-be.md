<p>Fans of Jujutsu Kaisen and beautiful, anime-inspired JRPGs received a special reason to rejoice this week following the announcement that <em>Jujutsu Kaisen Phantom Parade</em>, the mobile game adaption of the acclaimed manga series, will finally receive its global release before the end of 2024.</p>
<p>This announcement was part of Juju Fest 2024, which featured several notable reveals for fans of Jujutsu Kaisen, including a Hidden Inventory movie slated for 2025 and a Season 2 Guide Book set to be released for Japanese audiences in October. However, the most significant revelation was that publisher BILIBILI GAMES would be bringing <em>Jujutsu Kaisen Phantom Parade</em> to players across all regions this year, with pre-registration already underway.</p>

<p><em>Jujutsu Kaisen Phantom Parade</em> is free-to-play and you can pre-register right now via <a href="https://jujutsuphanpara.biligames.com/">the game’s official website</a> as well as stay up to date with the game’s latest developments via <a href="https://discord.gg/jujutsuphanpara">Discord</a>, <a href="https://x.com/JJKPP_GL">Twitter/X</a>, and <a href="https://www.facebook.com/jujutsuphanparaEN">Facebook</a>. But, if you’re only just now hearing of <em>Jujutsu Kaisen Phantom Parade</em> and would like to know what it’s all about then here’s a quick breakdown of what lies in store.</p>
<p><b><font size="+1">Gameplay Overview</font></b></p>
<p>Developed by Sumzap, Inc. and initially launched in Japan by TOHO Games in 2023, <em>Jujutsu Kaisen Phantom Parade</em> gives players the chance to immerse themselves in a dark and mystical world where gifted sorcerer&#8217;s battle cursed spirits to keep humanity from being destroyed.</p>
<p><img alt="" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-328329" height="900" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Jujutsu-kaisen-mobile-character-dialogue.jpg" width="1600" /></p>
<p>The core gameplay involves players forming teams of four sorcerers from various classes, such as tank, support, and damage dealers, and engaging in turn-based battles against cursed spirits. Players will have the opportunity to utilize the abilities of beloved characters like Yuji Itadori, Megumi Fushiguro, Nobara Kugisaki, and Satoru Gojo, all of which faithfully capture the character traits that fans have grown to love through the manga and anime series.</p>
<p><em>Jujutsu Kaisen Phantom Parade</em> allows players to revisit several pivotal moments from the first season of the tv anime, while also introducing an entirely new storyline set in the Fukuoka Branch Campus, offering a fresh and unique narrative experience.</p>
