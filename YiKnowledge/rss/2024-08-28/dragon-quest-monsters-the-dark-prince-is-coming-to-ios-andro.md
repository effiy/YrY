<p><a class="app-icon" href="/games/dqm-the-dark-prince" style="float: left; margin-top: 4px; margin-right: 10px;"><img alt="" src="https://toucharcade.com/wp-content/images/app/6449183990/100x100bb.jpg" style="border-radius: 20px;" /></a> When Square Enix released the monster collecting RPG <em>Dragon Quest Monsters: The Dark Prince</em> on Switch last year, <a href="https://toucharcade.com/2023/12/11/dragon-quest-monsters-the-dark-prince-switch-review-performance-tevi-gothic-classic-2-port/">I loved my time with it</a>. It had technical issues, but the charm and gameplay loop elevated it well beyond other <em>Dragon Quest</em> spinoffs on the platform bringing up to the same level as the superb <em>Dragon Quest Builders 2</em>. I always expected <em>Dragon Quest Monsters: The Dark Prince</em> to be ported to PC soon after just like <em>Dragon Quest Treasures</em>, but I didn&#8217;t even consider it hitting mobile anytime soon. Today, Square Enix announced that the Switch-exclusive <em>Dragon Quest Monsters: The Dark Prince</em> <a href="http://toucharcade.com/link/http://itunes.apple.com/us/app/id6449183990?mt=8">(&#36;29.99)</a> is coming to iOS, Android, and Steam on September 11th with all prior DLC included. This means the <em>Dragon Quest Monsters: The Dark Prince</em> Digital Deluxe Edition content will also be included. Watch the trailer for <em>Dragon Quest Monsters: The Dark Prince</em> below:</p>

<p>The <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/2175540/DRAGON_QUEST_MONSTERS_The_Dark_Prince/">store pages</a> also confirm that the network mode for Online Battles from the Switch version where players battle one another in real time will not be included in the Steam and mobile versions.</p>
<p><em>Dragon Quest Monsters: The Dark Prince</em> is currently available on Nintendo Switch priced at $59.99 for the standard edition and $84.99 for the Digital Deluxe Edition. Having loved this on Switch, I&#8217;m looking forward to replaying it for review on iPhone, iPad, and Steam Deck for the new platforms launch on September 11th. It is great to see Square Enix bring more <em>Dragon Quest</em> to mobile soon after the original platform launch. I expected we might see this one by 2027 on mobile given delays between console and mobile these days for the series like we saw with <em><a href="https://toucharcade.com/2022/05/31/dragon-quest-builders-mobile-review-controller-support-icloud-save-optimization-heat-performance-frame-rate-vs-ps4-switch-vita/">Dragon Quest Builders</a></em>. It is priced at $29.99 on mobile and $39.99 on Steam. Check it out on the App Store for iOS <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dqm-the-dark-prince/id6449183990">here</a> and on Google Play for Android <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.square_enix.android_googleplay.DragonQuestMonsters3ww&#038;hl=en_IN">here</a> where you can pre-register for it. Have you played <em>Dragon Quest Monsters: The Dark Prince</em> before on Switch or will you try it out on mobile and Steam in two weeks when it releases?</p>
