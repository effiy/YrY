We&#8217;ve got a bit of news, a look at all the new games on the eShop today, plus the usual lists of new and expiring sales for the day. Lots to see, so let&#8217;s get going!</p>

<p>Sandwiching together two slightly smaller versions of the usual types of Directs was an interesting choice, and it means we got a bunch of announcements. I can&#8217;t go through all of them here, but highlights included some shadow drops we&#8217;ll talk about in the New Releases section, <em>Capcom Fighting Collection 2</em>, the<em> Suikoden I &amp; II</em> remakes, <em>Yakuza Kiwami</em>, <em>Tetris Forever, MySims, Worms Armageddon: Anniversary Edition</em>, new <em>Atelier</em> and <em>Rune Factory</em> games, and many, many more. I&#8217;d advise just setting aside some time and watching the video. Skim if you must. Plenty of great games there for a variety of tastes.</p>
<h3><strong>Select New Releases</strong></h3>
<h3><strong>Castlevania Dominus Collection ($24.99)</strong></h3>
<p><img alt="" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-328306" height="562" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/castlevaniadominuscollection.jpg" width="1000" /></p>
<p>One of the many cool shadow drops from the Direct was a third <em>Castlevania</em> collection. This one includes the three Nintendo DS games: <em>Dawn of Sorrow, Portrait of Ruin,</em> and<em> Order of Ecclesia</em>. It also includes the absolutely rancid arcade game, <em>Haunted Castle</em>, and as an extra, a remake of <em>Haunted Castle</em> by the folks at M2. It&#8217;s a lot better. As you would expect from this developer, this is a top-notch treatment that features great emulation and pretty much all of the features you&#8217;d want. An absurd value for the price.</p>
<h3><strong>Pizza Tower ($19.99)</strong></h3>
<p><img alt="" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-328307" height="562" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/pizzatower.jpg" width="1000" /></p>
<p>The <em>Wario Land</em>-inspired frenetic platformer dashes on to the Switch with this Direct shadow drop. Make your way through the five massive floors of the Pizza Tower in order to destroy it and save your restaurant. If you miss Wario&#8217;s handheld adventures, you&#8217;ll really want to grab this. If you have no strong Wario feelings but like platforming, you should also give this a look. We&#8217;re hoping to review this one, but we&#8217;ll have to see how things go.</p>
<h3><strong>Goat Simulator 3 ($29.99)</strong></h3>
<p><img alt="" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-328308" height="562" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/goatsimulator3.jpg" width="1000" /></p>
<p>The shadow drops continue. Look, it&#8217;s a Wednesday. If it weren&#8217;t for these, we wouldn&#8217;t have much at all. Anyway, <em>Goat Simulator 3</em>. You know how <em>Goat Simulator</em> works by now. I can&#8217;t verify how this game runs on Switch, but I know more powerful consoles struggle with it in places. So, you know, proceed with caution. Though I suppose even if it does run badly, it might just play into the whole sh**post nature of the game. Well, I leave it to you to decide. Silly goats doing silly things in an open world, may or may not make your Switch vomit blood.</p>
<h3><strong>Peglin ($19.99)</strong></h3>
<p><img alt="" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-328309" height="562" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/peglin.jpg" width="1000" /></p>
<p>I know it&#8217;s cliche to armchair quarterback and talk about companies leaving money on the proverbial table, but I genuinely think Electronic Arts made a giant mistake by not putting Popcap&#8217;s games on the Switch in some form or another. Maybe even a collection? Well, they didn&#8217;t. They aren&#8217;t going to. And so we must scratch that itch in other ways, and for my fellow <em>Peggle</em> fans I can&#8217;t recommend <em>Peglin</em> enough. This one has been available on mobile for a while, and it&#8217;s just as good here on Switch. It&#8217;s basically <em>Peggle</em> blended into a turn-based RPG roguelite. We&#8217;ll have a review of this soon in case you need more details.</p>
<h3><strong>Doraemon Dorayaki Shop Story ($20.00)</strong></h3>
<p><img alt="" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-328310" height="562" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/doraemondorayakishopstory.jpg" width="1000" /></p>
<p>How can Kairosoft spice up its now incredibly well-worn formula for simulation games? Well, I suppose one way is to add a license to the mix. That&#8217;s what we&#8217;ve got here with <em>Doraemon Dorayaki Shop Story,</em> a standard Kairosoft shop sim featuring the characters from the popular <em>Doraemon</em> manga and anime series. A decent effort has been made to do the license justice, and you might even spot some characters from the manga artist&#8217;s other works popping in as customers. Cute.</p>
<h3><strong>Pico Park 2 ($8.99)</strong></h3>
<p><img alt="" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-328311" height="562" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/picopark2.jpg" width="1000" /></p>
<p>More<em> Pico Park</em> for the <em>Pico Park</em> fans out there. Up to eight players can join in via local or online multiplayer, and this is one game where the rule of the more, the merrier is in full effect. Play through a bunch of puzzle stages that require some sharp thinking and cooperation to solve. One for those who didn&#8217;t get their fill with the first game, but not really different enough to be likely to pull in anyone new. And that&#8217;s fine.</p>
<h3><strong>Kamitsubaki City Ensemble ($3.99)</strong></h3>
<p><img alt="" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-328312" height="562" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/kamitsubakicityensemble.jpg" width="1000" /></p>
<p>An affordable little rhythm game featuring music from Kamitsubaki Studio. Hit the notes, follow the story, enjoy the tunes. Not the fanciest dining, but for that price it doesn&#8217;t have to be.</p>
<h3><strong>SokoPenguin ($4.99)</strong></h3>
<p><img alt="" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-328313" height="562" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/sokopenguin.jpg" width="1000" /></p>
<p>Yes, that&#8217;s a <em>Sokoban</em>-style crate-pushing puzzler starring a penguin, alright. One hundred levels. You know if you want it or not.</p>
<h3><strong>Q2 Humanity ($6.80)</strong></h3>
<p><img alt="" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-328314" height="562" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/q2humanity.jpg" width="1000" /></p>
<p>More quirky physics-based puzzles, over three hundred this time around. You&#8217;ll have to make use of your character&#8217;s abilities in addition to drawing to solve the problems now. Up to four players can get in on the fun, either via local or online multiplayer.</p>
