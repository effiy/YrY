<p>This simple cake recipe came to us from a <em>Cuisinart</em> magazine published in 1979, and it originally called for red raspberries. The crumb is moist and fluffy thanks to the buttermilk in the batter, and the topping pairs sweet, intensely flavored black raspberries with dark chocolate to delicious effect. If you’d like to use red raspberries, consider upping the amount to 1¼ cups (since red raspberries are larger than their black counterparts, there would be fewer berries in a cup measure).</p>



<p><strong>Featured in the July/August 1997 issue.</strong></p>


<div class="orgnc-Recipe" id="recipe">
        
                        <div class="yield">
                <b>Makes:</b> One 8-inch cake            </div>
                            <div class="yield">
                <b>Time</b>: 1 hour 10 minutes             </div>
                
            <h3 class="ingredients">Ingredients</h3>
        <div class="ingredients_section">
                            <div class="section">
                                            <h4 class="part-title">
                            For the topping:                        </h4>
                                        <ul class="ingredients" id="recipe-ingredients">
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                ½ cup packed light brown sugar                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                2 Tbsp. all-purpose flour                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                1 Tbsp. unsalted butter                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                ½ oz. semisweet chocolate, finely chopped                            </li>
                                            </ul>
                    <div class="chicory-order-ingredients"></div>                </div>
                            <div class="section">
                                            <h4 class="part-title">
                            For the cake:                        </h4>
                                        <ul class="ingredients">
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                5 Tbsp. unsalted butter, melted and cooled, plus more for the pan                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                1 cup all-purpose flour, plus more for the pan                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                ¾ cup sugar                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                ½ tsp. baking powder                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                ¼ tsp. baking soda                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                1 tsp. kosher salt                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                ⅓ cup buttermilk                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                ½ tsp. vanilla extract                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                1  large egg, lightly beaten                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                ¾ cup fresh or thawed frozen black raspberries                            </li>
                                            </ul>
                    <div class="chicory-order-ingredients"></div>                </div>
                    </div>
                <h3 class="instructions">Instructions</h3>
        <ol class="instructions multiple">
                            <li class="instruction">
                    Make the topping: In a small bowl, using your hands, mix together the brown sugar, flour, and butter. Stir in the chocolate. Set aside.                </li>
                            <li class="instruction">
                    Make the cake: Position a rack in the center of the oven and preheat to 375°F. Butter and flour an 8-inch springform pan. Set aside.                </li>
                            <li class="instruction">
                    Into a medium bowl, sift the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. In a second medium bowl, whisk together the buttermilk, melted butter, vanilla, and egg. Add the buttermilk mixture to the flour mixture and stir to combine.                </li>
                            <li class="instruction">
                    Spread the batter into the prepared pan. Sprinkle the raspberries over the batter, then the topping. Bake until the cake is well browned, 50–55 minutes. Transfer the pan to a wire rack and set aside to cool for 10 minutes, then run a knife around the edges of the pan and remove the springform ring. Serve warm.                </li>
                    </ol>
    </div>
