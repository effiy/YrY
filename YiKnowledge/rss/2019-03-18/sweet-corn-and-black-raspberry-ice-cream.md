<p><a href="https://jenis.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener nofollow" target="_blank">Jeni’s Splendid Ice Creams</a>, which originated in Columbus, Ohio, has some of the most amazing flavors around, but they all start with one <a href="https://www.saveur.com/article/Recipes/Jenis-Ice-Cream-Base/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_self">simple base</a>. This recipe combines the silky flavor of <a href="https://www.saveur.com/summer-corn-recipes/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_self">corn</a> with the tartness of black raspberries. You can use fresh or thawed frozen black raspberries for the sauce, or substitute blackberries. If you’d like a thicker sauce that’s easier to swirl into the ice cream, you can add ⅛ teaspoon of xanthan gum when cooking the berries and sugar.</p>

<div class="orgnc-Recipe" id="recipe">
        
                        <div class="yield">
                <b>Makes:</b> About 1 quart            </div>
                            <div class="yield">
                <b>Time</b>: 25 minutes             </div>
                
            <h3 class="ingredients">Ingredients</h3>
        <div class="ingredients_section">
                            <div class="section">
                                            <h4 class="part-title">
                            For the ice cream:                        </h4>
                                        <ul class="ingredients" id="recipe-ingredients">
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                1 Tbsp. plus 1 tsp. cornstarch                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                2  cups whole milk, divided                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                1¼  cups heavy cream                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                ⅔ cup sugar                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                2 Tbsp. light corn syrup                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                ¼ tsp. kosher salt                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                1  ear of corn, kernels removed, cob cut into large chunks                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                3 Tbsp. cream cheese, softened                            </li>
                                            </ul>
                    <div class="chicory-order-ingredients"></div>                </div>
                            <div class="section">
                                            <h4 class="part-title">
                            For the sauce:                        </h4>
                                        <ul class="ingredients">
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                1 cup fresh or thawed frozen black raspberries                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                ½ cup sugar                            </li>
                                            </ul>
                    <div class="chicory-order-ingredients"></div>                </div>
                    </div>
                <h3 class="instructions">Instructions</h3>
        <ol class="instructions multiple">
                            <li class="instruction">
                    Make the ice cream: In a small bowl, stir together the cornstarch and ¼ cup of the milk to make a slurry. Set aside.                 </li>
                            <li class="instruction">
                    In a medium pot, whisk together the cream, sugar, corn syrup, salt, and remaining milk. Add the corn kernels and cob and bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Cook for 4 minutes, then stir in the slurry. Return to a boil and cook, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened, about 2 minutes. Place a fine-mesh strainer over a medium bowl, then strain the mixture into the bowl, discarding the solids.                </li>
                            <li class="instruction">
                    To a second medium bowl, add the cream cheese. Whisk in ¼ cup of the hot milk mixture until smooth, then whisk in the remaining milk mixture. Allow to cool slightly, then transfer to a large resealable plastic bag, seal, and submerge in a bowl of ice water until chilled.                 </li>
                            <li class="instruction">
                    Pour the mixture into an ice cream maker and process according to the manufacturer&#8217;s instructions.                </li>
                            <li class="instruction">
                    Make the sauce: To a small pot over medium-high heat, add the berries and sugar and cook,  stirring frequently, until thick, about 8 minutes. Place a fine-mesh strainer over a small bowl, then strain the mixture into the bowl, pressing on the solids to extract as much liquid as possible then discarding the solids. Refrigerate until chilled.                </li>
                            <li class="instruction">
                    Alternate layers of the ice cream and berry sauce in a storage container, then freeze.                </li>
                    </ol>
    </div>
