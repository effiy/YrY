<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I thought it would be fun to do a series of posts on some of the world’s small luxury hotel groups that often don’t get much attention. I’ll kick this off with <a href="https://airelles.com/en" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Airelles Collection</a>, which is probably <a href="https://onemileatatime.com/guides/best-luxury-hotel-brands/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">my favorite boutique luxury hotel group</a> in the world, with just over half a dozen properties.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I don’t want to bury the lede — Airelles properties are priced at the very top end of the market, but unlike so many other hotel brands, they actually deliver an amazing experience. You spend a lot, but you also get a lot. I stayed at my first Airelles property in 2022, and since then, I’ve visited all of the brand’s property (for the record, while paying the standard going rate).</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Admittedly there’s no great points angle here, but for those who love luxury hospitality and value an amazing experience, I think the group deserves a shoutout. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">As you can see, in the title I mention how the hotel group flies under the radar. Some people might quibble with me on that, as there’s no denying that some of the individual properties are quite well known. However, I think the group overall doesn’t get enough international attention.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-background-on-airelles-collection-hotels">Background on Airelles Collection hotels</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Airelles Collection is a boutique French hotel group. Technically, Airelles started all the way back in 1988 in the French Alps, with the opening of Les Airelles Courchevel. However, this was really elevated in 2007, when French entrepreneur Stéphane Courbit acquired the property, and took it to the next level. </p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img alt="Airelles Courchevel's ski chalet exterior in the French Alps, where the Airelles brand got its start" class="wp-image-266668" height="900" src="https://cdn.onemileatatime.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Les-Airelles-Courchevel-5.jpeg" width="1200" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Airelles Courchevel is where the brand started</figcaption></figure>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This was one of the first hotels to get <a href="https://onemileatatime.com/guides/france-palace-hotels/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">France’s “Palace” distinction</a>, reflecting the high standards. That was the start of the brand, and it grew from there. Obviously Courbit has an eye for properties with great potential, and turning them into something special.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The reason I mention this background is because from the perspective of a guest, the whole brand basically feels like a billionaire’s passion project, where despite the high rates, you can’t help but wonder if they’re actually making money, given the level of service and inclusions provided.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Fun fact — Courbit also owns Ladurée, and that’s something you’ll definitely notice at Airelles properties, as macarons are basically force fed to guests, and you’ll see them at every turn. Yum.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img alt="A display of colorful Ladurée macarons at Airelles Château de Versailles" class="wp-image-338737" height="855" src="https://cdn.onemileatatime.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Airelles-Versailles-Le-Grand-Controle-129.jpeg" width="1200" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Airelles properties have more macarons than you can count</figcaption></figure>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-what-makes-airelles-properties-special">What makes Airelles properties special</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">When I think of what I enjoy in a luxury hotel stay — sense of place, anticipatory service, amazing dining, and a real effort into surprising guests and exceeding expectations — Airelles checks all the boxes. I’d say that in many ways, Airelles sums up what so many people love about traveling to France, on the high-end.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Obviously there’s some variance in terms of the experience across properties, but just to give some examples of the things that stand out to me:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Airelles properties are incredibly family friendly; it’s not just that they tolerate kids, but they embrace them, and have amazing kids clubs, game rooms, etc.</li>



<li>Airelles properties have phenomenal dining, and best of all, most properties have either a half board or full board rate, with virtually no restrictions in terms of what you can order (sure, you can add truffle your dish!)</li>



<li>Airelles properties offer highly personalized service, and it really seems like they go out of the way to surprise guests at every turn, even with the littlest of details</li>



<li>Airelles properties just feel generous, despite their high prices; minibars are complimentary and ridiculously well stocked, there’s a gift on your bed every night, at some properties the house car is a Rolls-Royce, there’s just a dessert spread randomly sitting in the lobby, etc.</li>
</ul>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img alt="A family-friendly space at Airelles Courchevel reflecting the hotel's kid-friendly amenities" class="wp-image-266830" height="900" src="https://cdn.onemileatatime.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Les-Airelles-Courchevel-166.jpeg" width="1200" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Airelles properties are very family friendly</figcaption></figure>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">As I said earlier, we’ve paid out of pocket for all of our Airelles stays, and while they’re very expensive, they’ve left me wanting to come back for more each time, because the properties are that great. To me, there’s not a higher quality hotel group in the world.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img alt="A Rolls-Royce house car parked at Airelles Saint-Tropez, Château de la Messardière" class="wp-image-272285" height="811" src="https://cdn.onemileatatime.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Airelles-Saint-Tropez-Chateau-de-la-Messardiere-161.jpeg" width="1200" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Airelles has some pretty swanky house cars!</figcaption></figure>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-airelles-hotel-locations-amp-basics">Airelles hotel locations &amp; basics</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Airelles currently has seven properties, with six being in France, and one being in Italy. If you’re a Francophile, you can’t help but appreciate just how varied these properties are — among the six locations in France, you have two ski properties, two properties in Saint-Tropez, one on the grounds of the Palace of Versailles, and one in a charming village in the countryside.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Let me briefly go over some details of each property, and then I’ll link to a review of each. While Airelles’ individual properties are increasingly getting onto the radars of international travelers, most people still aren’t aware of the overall hotel collection. There aren’t many hotel brands where I think this is the case, but Airelles properties are worth blindly staying at, and worth checking out, as you’re assured quality.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-airelles-courchevel">Airelles Courchevel</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://airelles.com/en/destination/courchevel-hotel" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Airelles Courchevel</a> is where the brand began. The 44-room property is located in Courchevel 1850, which is probably France’s swankiest ski town. This is my favorite Airelles property, and my favorite ski resort in the world. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The level of service, luxury, amenities, and coziness here, are simply unrivaled. The property has ski-in, ski-out access, perfect service, the most over-the-top selection of restaurants, an incredible bar, and amenities ranging from a stunning indoor pool, to a cool arcade room that even adults will want to hang out in, to a theater.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-airelles-val-d-isere">Airelles Val d’Isère</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://airelles.com/en/destination/val-d-isere-hotel" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Airelles Val d’Isère</a> is the brand’s other ski property, also located in the French Alps. The 41-room property is stunning, also with ski-in, ski-out access. Honestly, purely in terms of the physical building, I think the Val d’Isère property might even be a little more impressive than the Courchevel property.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This is just an incredibly well rounded luxury ski resort. I’d say the main difference is the Courchevel property is a little more over-the-top, which just reflects Courchevel at large. The guest profile at Val d’Isère is more European, while Courchevel tends to attract more guests from Russia, the Middle East, etc.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-airelles-chateau-de-versailles-le-grand-controle">Airelles Château de Versailles Le Grand Contrôle</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://airelles.com/en/destination/chateau-de-versailles-hotel" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Airelles Château de Versailles</a> is a breathtakingly unique 13-room property, located on the grounds of the Palace of Versailles.  Airelles Château de Versailles is unlike any hotel I’ve stayed at before. The biggest selling point of this hotel is that it gives you special access to the Palace of Versailles, even at times when it’s closed to the public (you get a morning and evening visit). The level of access is truly incredible.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Even beyond that, this hotel offers an incredible experience, with so much effort put into transporting guests back in time. This isn’t your typical luxury hotel experience — I’d say it’s a hybrid between a luxury hotel, a history safari, and an immersive, performative experience.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-airelles-gordes-la-bastide">Airelles Gordes, La Bastide</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://airelles.com/en/destination/gordes-hotel" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Airelles Gordes</a> is located in the medieval hilltop village of Gordes, in Provence, and this place feels like it’s out of a fairytale. It’s not often you get to stay in a 16th-century palace. This is such a lovely place for a spring or fall visit, with a beautifully restored property, a village you can just roam around, and phenomenal dining. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I’d highly recommend this property if you’re looking to relax, and enjoy the beautiful scenery in this part of France.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-airelles-saint-tropez-chateau-de-la-messardiere">Airelles Saint-Tropez, Château de la Messardière</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://airelles.com/en/destination/chateau-de-la-messardiere-hotel" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Airelles Saint-Tropez, Château de la Messardière</a>, is probably the most well known Airelles property. I’d argue that it might just now be taking on Hotel du Cap-Eden-Roc as France’s hotel with the most “scene.” That trend will only continue, as <a href="https://onemileatatime.com/news/the-white-lotus-season-4-hotel/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Season 4 of HBO’s “The White Lotus” was filmed here</a>.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This 86-room property is definitely becoming Airelles’ most popular property. The piece of land that this resort is on is just unbelievable, as it’s on a hill overlooking the sea in the distance, and then the hotel also has its own beach club and restaurant (which you can be driven to in a Rolls-Royce, natch).</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The grounds of this property are beautiful, though as someone who appreciates Airelles for its amazing service and experience, this is probably the property I’m least excited about, as this has basically turned into one big place to see and be seen.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-airelles-saint-tropez-pan-dei-palais">Airelles Saint-Tropez, Pan Dei Palais</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://airelles.com/en/destination/pan-dei-palais-hotel" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Airelles Saint-Tropez, Pan Dei Palais</a>, is the one Airelles property that I’ve visited, but haven’t actually stayed at. It’s the sister property of Château de la Messardière, and it’s ideal if you want to be in the town of Saint-Tropez, rather than above it.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Think of the 12-room property as being more like staying in someone’s huge villa, rather than a resort. If you’re looking for a resort vibe, lots of dining, and a scene, Château de la Messardière is definitely for you. Meanwhile if you want to be closer to town and want something more intimate, this is a great option. There’s also the opportunity to travel between the two properties, when staying at either.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-airelles-palladio-venice">Airelles Palladio Venice</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://airelles.com/en/destination/venezia" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Airelles Palladio Venice</a> is the brand’s newest property, having only opened in the spring of 2026, and it’s also the company’s first location outside of France. The 45-room hotel is located on Venice’s Giudecca Island, just a five-minute boat ride from Piazza San Marco.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The property is housed within the former Bauer Palladio complex, consisting of three restored buildings, a 16th century heritage church, and expansive gardens, with views of St. Mark’s Basilica and the Doge’s Palace. This really feels more like an urban resort than what you’d typically expect from Venice, given the sheer amount of space that guests get to enjoy, and it’s easily one of the city’s best properties.</p>
