<p>Jane Schoenbrun’s latest movie, <em>Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma, </em>has horror fans and critics buzzing. I love the film’s tongue-in-cheek critique of the horror genre, slasher films, and the recent obsession with “elevated horror.” I love the sapphic romance, and I can’t get enough of the creepy, abandoned summer camp setting. </p>



<p>It’s no surprise that with all of the buzz, we’re all looking for some way to take a trip back to Camp Miasma after we leave the cinema. While these three horror books might not star Gillian Anderson and Hannah Einbinder, they are deliciously queer and set in creepy summer camps that will haunt your dreams.</p>
