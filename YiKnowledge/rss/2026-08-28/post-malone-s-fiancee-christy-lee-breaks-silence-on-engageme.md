<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/post-malone/"><strong>Post Malone</strong></a>’s fiancée, <strong>Christy Lee</strong>, is pulling back the veil on the singer’s <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/pictures/celebrity-engagements-of-2026-rhocs-gina-kirschenheiter-more-stars/">romantic proposal</a> more than one week after their engagement made headlines.</p>
<p>Lee told <a href="https://www.vogue.com/article/post-malone-christy-lee-engagement-exclusive" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><em>Vogue</em></a> on Friday, August 28, that Malone (real name Austin Richard Post) popped the question on August 17 at his new ranch in Utah after dropping hints that he was ring shopping earlier in the year. (News broke on August 19 that <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/post-malone-engaged-to-christy-lee-after-rekindled-romance/">the pair were engaged</a> after Lee was spotted with a diamond sparkler on her left hand in several photos posted by her BFFs.)</p><div class="mb1-wrapper" id="starter">

<p>“A couple months before the proposal, Austin started casually slipping these very ‘hypothetical’ questions about rings into conversation, trying to figure out what I liked without giving himself away,” she recalled. “One day, completely out of the blue, he asked me, ‘Pear, heart, or cushion?’ I told him I’d always thought there was something so chic about an oval.”</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/post-malone-engaged-to-christy-lee-after-rekindled-romance/" target="_blank">Post Malone Is Engaged to Christy Lee After Quietly Rekindling Their Romance</a></p>
<p>Lee remembered telling <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/stylish/news/post-malone-strips-down-to-briefs-for-skims-flaunts-tattooed-skin/">the “Sunflower” singer</a>, 31, that instead of a ring with diamonds around the band, she “preferred the opposite — something very simple, with a delicate, thin gold band.”</p>
<p>When Malone asked if there was such a thing as a ring being too big, she “jokingly told him I didn’t think so.”</p>
<p>Little did Lee know, the rapper was keeping track of all her answers and proposed with <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/stylish/news/post-malones-jeweler-shows-off-his-20-carat-engagement-ring/">a custom 20-plus carat natural oval diamond ring</a> with a gold band — just like she imagined.</p>

<p>“When I saw it, I was honestly speechless,” she said. “He had somehow taken all of those little conversations and created exactly what I would have chosen for myself.”</p>
<p>The fashion stylist, who was first linked to Malone in January 2025, noted that it was “incredibly sweet knowing how much thought he put into creating something that felt entirely my own.”</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/stylish/news/post-malones-jeweler-shows-off-his-20-carat-engagement-ring/" target="_blank">Post Malone's Jeweler Shows Off His 20-Carat Engagement Ring for Christy Lee</a></p>
<p>Malone’s sweet gestures didn’t end with the ring design. Lee revealed that the <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/news/post-malone-teams-up-with-dolly-parton-on-new-country-album/">“Pour Me a Drink” singer</a> planned the perfect week for her Utah full of surprises.</p>
<p>“Austin first surprised me with a baby Highland cow, bunnies, and chickens,” Lee told the outlet.</p>
<p>Malone later hosted a “beautiful intimate dinner at the ranch” with Lee’s friends and parents waiting to surprise her.</p>
<figure class="wp-caption alignnone" id="attachment_4421420" style="width: 576px;"><img alt="Post Malone Fiancee Christy Lee Breaks Silence Over Engagement Honestly Speechless" class="size-large wp-image-4421420" height="1024" src="https://www.usmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Post-Malone-Fiancee-Christy-Lee-Breaks-Silence-Over-Engagement-Honestly-Speechless.jpg?w=576" width="576" /><figcaption class="wp-caption-text"><span class="caption">Post Malone and Christy Lee after getting engaged.</span> <span class="credit">Courtesy of Brooke Donovan/Instagram</span></figcaption></figure>
<p>As the group dined under twinkle lights at a long table covered in white florals, a light rain began to fall.</p>
<p>“That’s when he proposed,” Lee said of the magical moment, adding that Malone asked for her hand in marriage while standing under a greenery arc with white flowers.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/post-malone-says-his-daughter-saved-his-life/" target="_blank">Post Malone Says His Daughter Saved His Life</a></p>
<p>The evening quickly turned into an engagement party with one of Lee’s friends pulling out a “bride to be” sash for her to wear as she and Malone posed for photos.</p>
<p>“There was something so special about being at our ranch, surrounded by the people we love most and all of our new little animals,” Lee concluded. “The whole evening felt incredibly intimate and personal. It was the most special moment of my life.”</p>
<p>Prior to his relationship with Lee, Malone was engaged to <strong>Hee Sung “Jamie” Park</strong>, with whom he <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/post-malone-and-ex-jamie-settle-court-battle-over-3-year-old/">shares a daughter</a> born in May 2022. Malone and Park split in November 2024.</p>
