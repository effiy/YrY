<p>An on-duty Baltimore police officer allegedly raped a woman at her apartment after giving her and her son a ride home, authorities said.</p>
<p>Officer <strong>Daniel Oxendine</strong> was arrested on Thursday, August 27, following the results of a DNA analysis, which was said to have linked him to the alleged assault, according to the <a href="https://www.facebook.com/HarfordCountySheriffsOffice/posts/pfbid02zXeaE1tpSRkmFXt1GsSf38wj16K6Ku5HUCrtuc9LGmoK49h7VWBdeZZwpnt5Gn4ul?rdid=8l2LBuNsm0sH7szq#">Harford County Sheriff’s Office</a>.</p><div class="mb1-wrapper" id="starter">

<p>He’s charged with two counts of first-degree rape, two counts of second-degree rape, and two counts of a law enforcement officer engaging in a sexual act with a victim or witness of an investigation, the sheriff’s office said in a news release shared the day of his arrest.</p>
<p>The rape investigation began on June 3 as detectives met with an Edgewood woman in the emergency department of a local hospital, where she reported she had been sexually assaulted, according to authorities.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/crime-news/news/california-man-arrested-for-raping-woman-he-kidnapped-car-crash-scene/" target="_blank">California Man Arrested for Raping Woman He Kidnapped From Car Crash Scene</a></p>
<p>Investigators learned that before the assault, the woman had encountered Oxendine while he was on-duty in Baltimore, the sheriff’s office said.</p>
<p>On June 2, officers had been responding to a “domestic altercation” involving the woman, who was described as the victim of the incident, according to charging documents obtained by the <a href="https://www.baltimoresun.com/2026/08/28/baltimore-officer-on-duty-rape-charging-documents/"><em>Baltimore Sun</em></a>.</p>
<p>After the woman told police that she and her son were in need of a ride to their home in Edgewood, Oxendine’s supervisor allowed him to transport them, the charging documents viewed by the newspaper said.</p>
<p>“After arriving at the victim’s residence, Oxendine entered her apartment and sexually assaulted her,” the sheriff’s office said.</p>
<p>Oxendine allegedly appeared outside her door soon after she and her son had already gone inside their apartment, according to charging documents, the <em>Baltimore Sun</em> reported.</p>
<p>He told the woman he needed to “make sure it was safe,” then went into her residence and instructed her to tell her son to step outside, the charging documents said.</p>
<p>Oxendine is then accused of raping her, a detective wrote in the charging documents, according to the newspaper.</p>

<p>The woman was taken to the hospital after the Harford County Sheriff’s Office was alerted to the assault, the sheriff’s office said.</p>
<p>There, a sexual assault kit exam was performed and DNA was collected for an analysis, according to the agency.</p>
<p>“When DNA test results returned conclusively identifying Daniel Oxendine as the offender, detectives obtained an arrest warrant,” the sheriff’s office said.</p>
<p>Investigators believe it’s possible Oxendine was involved in “similar offenses” and are “working diligently to identify any additional incidents,” according to the agency.</p>
<p>Baltimore Police spokesperson <strong>Lindsey Eldridge</strong> said Oxendine is currently suspended without pay, per the<em> Baltimore Sun</em>.</p>
<p>On Friday, August 28, Oxendine had his initial court appearance and was denied bond by a judge who considered him a danger to the community, <a href="https://foxbaltimore.com/news/local/bond-denied-for-suspended-bpd-baltimore-officer-charged-with-on-duty-rape-daniel-oxendine-harford-county-maryland">WBFF</a> reported.</p>
<p>An attorney representing him at the hearing chose not to provide a comment, according to the TV station.</p>
