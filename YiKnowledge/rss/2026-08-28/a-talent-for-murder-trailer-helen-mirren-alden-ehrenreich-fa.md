<p>After yesterday&#8217;s exciting announcement for a series adaptation of Benjamin Stevenson&#8217;s <em>Everyone in My Family Has Killed Someone</em>, we&#8217;ve got news about another murder mystery with a trailer for <em>A Talent for Murder</em>, starring <a href="https://www.joblo.com/tag/helen-mirren/">Helen Mirren</a>, <a href="https://www.joblo.com/tag/alden-ehrenreich/">Alden Ehrenreich</a>, and Olivia Cooke. Described as a psychological chamber thriller, this delightfully deranged film drips with atmosphere, intrigue, and sinister intent. Color me intrigued!</p>



<p>Anton Corbijn (<em>The American</em>, <em>A Most Wanted Man</em>, <em>Control</em>) directs <em>A Talent for Murder</em> from a script written by Joanna Murray-Smith. The film is adapted from Murray-Smith&#8217;s 2014 stage play, <em>Switzerland</em>.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-a-high-stakes-battle-of-wits">A High-Stakes Battle of Wits</h2>



<p>Set during the later years of famed crime novelist Patricia Highsmith (Mirren), the story centers on Edward Ridgeway (Ehrenreich), a mysterious young man from a New York publisher who is sent to Switzerland to persuade the renowned writer Patricia Highsmith to write one final installment of the Ripley anthology.</p>



<p>However, rather than a garden-variety brainstorming session, the duo&#8217;s encounter quickly sparks an intense battle of wits and talents as both writers attempt to outfox each other throughout the creative process. Will their rivalry lead to murder, or will they write the greatest finale a beloved character could ask for?</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-channeling-classic-misery-vibes">Channeling Classic <em>Misery</em> Vibes</h2>



<p>The premise and trailer are giving me <em>Misery</em> vibes. Does anyone else feel that? I&#8217;m loving the on-screen chemistry between Mirren and Ehrenreich, and I&#8217;m interested to see how Olivia Cooke&#8217;s character navigates their twisted imaginations. With movies like Rian Johnson&#8217;s <em>Knives Out Mysteries</em> helping to restore Hollywood&#8217;s interest in the murder mystery genre, I would love to see more projects like this get a green light.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-from-the-swiss-alps-to-the-silver-screen">From the Swiss Alps to the Silver Screen</h2>



<p><em>A Talent for Murder</em> is getting a theatrical release from <a href="https://bleeckerstreetmedia.com/">Bleecker Street</a> and <a href="https://www.ldentertainment.com/">LD Entertainment</a> <strong>on October 23, 2026</strong>, following its premiere at the Toronto International Film Festival. Production began in January 2025. Although set in the Swiss Alps, filming took place across Rome, Italy, London, and on location in Switzerland.</p>



<p>I&#8217;ve become something of a murder mystery enthusiast over the past year as I&#8217;ve gotten back into reading. I like the idea of Murray-Smith adapting her own stage play, as it lends authenticity to the project and makes it feel genuine and on-model.</p>
