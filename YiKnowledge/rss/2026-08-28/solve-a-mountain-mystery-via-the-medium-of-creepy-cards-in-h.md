<img src="https://assetsio.gnwcdn.com/hillthorn.jpg?width=690&amp;quality=85&amp;format=jpg&amp;auto=webp" /> <p>Attention, dusty creeps who want to play poker with the Wickerman! I found one, a videogame you might enjoy. That game is Hillthorn, a spooky investigative cardgame from Fool Moon and Finnegan Motors - the latter also known for beaming corpo thriller <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/3379360/Inhuman_Resources_A_Literary_Machination/">Inhuman Resources: A Literary Machination</a>.</p>
<p>Hillthorn is the story of a detective hired to investigate a disappearance at a secluded mountain community. One of the first things you do, after hassling a worryingly stained forest ranger for directions, is cross a line of salt on the trail. Seems like something a person familiar with the occult would avoid doing, but you are a Private Eye, armed with logic and cynicism. Besides, all of this is presented via the medium of cards. Who's afraid of cards? What are they going to do, give you a paper cut? Ouch! Oh, maybe don't bleed on the salt.</p> <p><a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/solve-a-mountain-mystery-via-the-medium-of-creepy-cards-in-hillthorn-which-has-an-excellent-demo">Read more</a></p>
