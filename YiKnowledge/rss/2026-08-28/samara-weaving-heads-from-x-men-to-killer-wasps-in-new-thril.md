<p>Samara Weaving may be joining the Marvel Cinematic Universe, but she isn&#8217;t leaving her horror roots behind. <a href="https://deadline.com/2026/08/samara-weaving-nicholas-braun-to-star-hivemind-thriller-1237051333/">Deadline</a> reports that the <em>Ready or Not</em> star has been cast alongside <em>Succession</em>&#8216;s Nicholas Braun in <em><strong>Hivemind</strong></em>, a new environmental thriller about a terrifying new breed of killer wasps. Irish filmmaker Ciarán Foy, whose credits include <em>Sinister 2</em>, <em>Eli</em>, and <em>The Haunting of Bly Manor</em>, is directing the movie.</p>



<p><em>Hivemind</em> is currently filming in Dublin. The story follows a small-town group of authorities who find themselves dealing with the evolution of a new breed of killer wasps and must work together to prevent the threat from developing into a deadly global outbreak.</p>



<p>The movie comes from Thunder Road and Badlands, with Peter Gaffney (<em>Yeti</em>, <em>Don&#8217;t Go in the Water</em>) writing the screenplay. Amazon MGM Studios plans to release <em>Hivemind</em> internationally on Prime Video.</p>



<p>For Weaving, <em>Hivemind</em> represents another opportunity to dive into horror and genre filmmaking at a particularly busy point in her career. The actress was recently cast as Emma Frost in Marvel&#8217;s upcoming <em><a href="https://www.joblo.com/x-men-samara-weaving-cast-as-emma-frost/">X-Men</a></em> movie, adding a major comic book movie role to a filmography that already includes plenty of memorable genre performances.</p>



<p>Weaving became a horror favorite with her starring roles in <em>The Babysitter</em> and <em>Ready or Not</em>. She recently returned for <em>Ready or Not 2: Here I Come</em>, and has also appeared in projects like <em>Over Your Dead Body</em> and <em>Carolina Caroline</em>. Her next movie may put her in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, but <em>Hivemind</em> proves that Weaving isn&#8217;t abandoning the horror and thriller territory that helped make her a genre fan favorite.</p>



<p>Braun is best known for playing Cousin Greg on HBO&#8217;s <em>Succession</em>, earning three Emmy nominations for the role. He recently appeared in Amazon MGM&#8217;s <em>The Sheep Detectives</em> and will next be seen in Ruben Östlund&#8217;s <em>The Entertainment System Is Down</em>. I first saw him in Kevin Smith&#8217;s <em><a href="https://www.joblo.com/red-state-best-horror/">Red State</a></em>.</p>



<p>Behind the camera, Foy brings a good amount of horror experience to <em>Hivemind</em>. His feature debut, <em>Citadel</em>, premiered at SXSW and won the festival&#8217;s Midnighter Audience Award. He subsequently directed <em>Sinister 2</em> and the Netflix horror movie <em>Eli</em>, as well as episodes of <em>The Haunting of Bly Manor</em> and <em>Sweet Tooth</em>.</p>



<p><em>Hivemind</em> is produced by Basil Iwanyk, Erica Lee, and Diarmaid McGrath for Thunder Road; Scott Strauss for Badlands; Macdara Kelleher and John Keville for Wild Atlantic Pictures; Adam Rodin; and Gaffney. The project is backed by Renegade Capital, with Jon Shiffman and Andrew Schwartzberg serving as executive producers.</p>
