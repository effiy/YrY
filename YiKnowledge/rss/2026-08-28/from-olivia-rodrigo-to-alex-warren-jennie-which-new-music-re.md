<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	With her inaugural Daisy Chain Fields just around the corner, Rodrigo unleashed &#8220;Serena Joy&#8221; &#8212; poignantly named after the <em>Handmaid&#8217;s Tale </em>character &#8212; earlier in the week. The fiery standalone track calls out those who turn a blind eye to injustice and fittingly arrives in time for the pop star&#8217;s all-women festival in Irvine, Calif., which will raise money for organizations supporting women and girls with a lineup that includes Chappell Roan, Doechii, Mitski, The Breeders, Stevie Nicks, Karen O, Sarah McLachlan and more. </p>

<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	Building on the momentum of his 2025 album <em>You&#8217;ll Be Aright, Kid</em>, Warren returns with a new full-length titled <em>WILDCHILD</em>, while JENNIE enters her next solo era with EP <em>Fallen Angel</em>. The BLACKPINK star&#8217;s latest set comes as two of her bandmates &#8212; JISOO and LISA &#8212; are also gearing up for new solo endeavors, as <a href="https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/blackpink-lisa-jisoo-solo-projects-release-dates-1236322863/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">recently announced</a>.</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	Other new projects include Rod Wave&#8217;s <em>Don&#8217;t Look Down</em>, Billy Strings&#8217; <em>So Much for Goodbyes</em>, Alabama Shakes&#8217; <em>I Must Be Dreaming</em>, Natanael Cano&#8217;s self-titled album, Ladrones&#8217; <em>Arriba La L</em>, Erykah Badu and The Alchemist&#8217;s <em>Before The World Blows</em>, Ky-Mani Marley&#8217;s <em>Love and Energy</em> and Carly Pearce&#8217;s <em>Honest Woman</em>. Plus, new singles from flowerovlove and Willow, Angèle and Chase &amp; Status featuring Debbie &#8212; &#8220;girls will be girls,&#8221; “Une seule vie” and “Gone Too Far,&#8221; respectively &#8212; are out now for fans to dive into.</p>
