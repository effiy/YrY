Millions of people worldwide are affected by diseases that cause scarring of the lungs, often leading to breathing difficulties and reduced quality of life. Progressive scarring of the lungs can severely impair breathing and is a hallmark of many chronic lung diseases, most notably pulmonary fibrosis, for which treatment options remain very limited and ineffective.
