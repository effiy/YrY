President Trump will not be traveling to New York City for the 25th anniversary of the Sept. 11 terror attacks, according to The Hill’s sister network NewsNation.  A White House official told the network that Vice President Vance is “still traveling to NYC and all three sites will be represented with administration officials in attendance.”&#8230;
