<p>Shields went makeup-free in the pic, closing her eyes while tilting her head towards the sun. She gave the camera a soft smile and wore her wet brunette hair down and pushed back.</p>
<p>In another clip, she shared a video of herself jumping off a boat in the same swimsuit and into the rapids. When out of the rushing water, she lounged on a dock with friends.</p>
