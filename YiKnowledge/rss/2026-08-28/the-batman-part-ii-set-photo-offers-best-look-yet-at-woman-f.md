<p>A new photo taken from the set of <strong><a href="https://www.comingsoon.net/tag/the-batman-part-ii">The Batman Part II</a></strong> offers a better look at the mysterious blonde woman who some fans think is <a href="https://www.comingsoon.net/tag/scarlett-Johansson">Scarlett Johansson</a>. With much of The Batman Part II’s plot and characters still being shrouded in mystery, there’s no guarantee this is indeed Johansson, but fans do certainly have some theories.</p>



<p>A The Batman Part II set video emerged online last week that sees a blonde woman in the passenger seat of <a href="https://www.comingsoon.net/tag/dc">DC</a>&#8216;s Batmobile. Given the video was taken from a pretty good distance away, it wasn’t immediately clear if this woman actually was Johansson or if fans were just jumping to conclusions.</p>



<p>Now, a <a href="https://x.com/TheBatmanSaga/status/2093129997548458335" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">better-quality image</a> has found its way onto the internet. Similar to the video, it shows a blonde woman sitting in the passenger seat of the Batmobile; however, this image does provide a clearer glimpse of her face. Many don’t think that’s Johansson seen in the picture, but rather they believe this could potentially be her stunt double, as it appears they’re filming a big action or chase scene.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-what-else-do-we-know-about-the-batman-part-ii">What else do we know about The Batman Part II?</h2>



<p>The Batman Part II follows the events of 2022’s <a href="https://www.comingsoon.net/tag/the-batman">The Batman</a> and 2024’s The Penguin, all of which are set within <a href="https://www.comingsoon.net/tag/the-batman-epic-crime-saga">The Batman Epic Crime Saga</a>. <a href="https://www.comingsoon.net/tag/matt-reeves">Matt Reeves</a> is returning to direct The Batman Part II, while <a href="https://www.comingsoon.net/tag/robert-pattinson">Robert Pattinson</a> is reprising his role as the titular Dark Knight.</p>



<p>The cast of the movie also includes Jeffrey Wright as Jim Gordon, Andy Serkis as Alfred Pennyworth, Colin Farrell as Oz Cobb/The Penguin, Sebastian Stan, and Charles Dance. Production on the project officially kicked off this summer.</p>



<p>The Batman Part II is currently dated for February 18, 2028, by Warner Bros. Pictures and <a href="https://www.comingsoon.net/tag/dc-studios">DC Studios</a>. Both The Batman and The Penguin can be streamed on HBO Max.</p>
