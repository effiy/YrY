<p><strong><a href="https://www.superherohype.com/tag/spider-man-beyond-the-spider-verse">Spider-Man: Brand New Day</a></strong> appears to be on track to beat one of <a href="https://www.superherohype.com/tag/james-cameron">James Cameron&#8217;s</a> most beloved films at the box office. After breaking several box office records, the new Spider-Man film has grossed $2.32 billion globally. That puts it just under <strong><a href="https://www.superherohype.com/tag/titanic">Titanic</a></strong>, which it is expected to pass this weekend.</p>



<p>According to <a href="https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/spider-man-brand-new-day-theaters-box-office-1236684381/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">The Hollywood Reporter</a>, Spider-Man 4 is expected to gross between $22-27 million at the domestic box office this weekend. Following that, the film will have beaten Titanic to become the fifth highest-grossing film of all time. It has already collected more at the domestic box office than Cameron&#8217;s 1997 romantic tragedy.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-what-movies-have-still-made-more-money-than-spider-man-4-at-the-worldwide-box-office">What movies have still made more money than Spider-Man 4 at the worldwide box office?</h2>



<p>At the time of writing, there are five films that have made more money than Spider-Man: Brand New Day. The list includes three films from Cameron, another Marvel film, and Ne Zha 2. The current order of highest-grossing films at the worldwide box office is Avatar, Avengers: Endgame, Avatar: The Way of Water, Ne Zha 2, and Titanic.</p>



<p>The previous Spider-Man film, Spider-Man: No Way Home, currently sits at the ninth position on the list, having grossed over $1.9 billion. Brand New Day continues to bring in money, collecting an average of almost $4 million daily at the domestic box office this week.</p>



<p>Besides continuing the wall-crawler&#8217;s story, the film also sets up the future of the MCU. The film introduced audiences to Sadie Sink&#8217;s Jean Grey, who will also appear in Marvel&#8217;s X-Men film in 2028. At the time of writing, Marvel Studios has not confirmed when and where Spider-Man will return in the MCU. Fans expect the hero to make an appearance in either Avengers: Doomsday this year or in Avengers: Secret Wars in 2027.</p>
