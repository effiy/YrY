<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/jax-taylor/"><strong>Jax Taylor</strong></a> has broken his silence after estranged wife <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/brittany-cartwright/"><strong>Brittany Cartwright</strong></a> was granted a <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/brittany-cartwright-gets-restraining-order-against-jax-taylor/">temporary restraining order</a> amid allegations of domestic violence.</p>
<p>“I made a lot of mistakes and behaved in ways during that period of my life that I’m truly ashamed of and regret and take full responsibility for,” Taylor, 47, told <em>Us</em> in a statement on Friday, August 28. “The person I was in 2024, during the darkest time of my life and at the height of my addiction, is not the person I am today.”</p><div class="mb1-wrapper" id="starter">

<p>The <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/shows/vanderpump-rules/"><em>Vanderpump Rules</em></a> alum noted, “I got help, went to treatment, and today I’m 1 year and 9 months sober. I’ve remained committed to treatment and therapy, working on myself and changing my life.” (Taylor checked himself into a 90-day treatment program in 2024 after being vocal about his mental health issues.)</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/jax-taylor-was-served-divorce-papers-while-filming-the-valley/" target="_blank">Jax Taylor Served Divorce Papers From Brittany While Filming 'The Valley'</a></p>
<p>Taylor, who shares son Cruz, 5, with Cartwright, 37, claimed that “there are allegations being made that I strongly dispute.”</p>
<p>He added, “The full story, <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/jax-taylor-accuses-brittany-cartwright-of-blatant-lies/">context and evidence</a> will be addressed where it belongs — in court. I will respect the court’s orders and the legal process.”</p>

<p>Taylor — who was married to Cartwright from 2019 to 2024 before <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/jax-taylor-denies-holding-up-brittany-cartwright-divorce/">she filed for divorce</a> — insisted, “My focus continues to be my sobriety, working on my mental health, being the best father I can be to Cruz and moving forward in a healthy and responsible way.”</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/brittany-cartwright-once-said-what-would-push-her-to-divorce-jax-taylor/" target="_blank">Brittany Cartwright Once Said What Would Push Her to Divorce Jax Taylor</a></p>
<p>Taylor’s statement comes shortly after <em>Us</em> confirmed on Friday that a judge granted Cartwright’s temporary restraining order against him, which she filed one day prior.</p>
<p>According to court viewed by <em>Us</em>, Cartwright claimed that Taylor exhibited “coercive control, financial abuse, emotional and psychological abuse, gaslighting and even physical violence perpetrated upon” her for nearly a decade.</p>
<figure class="wp-caption alignnone" id="attachment_4421171" style="width: 682px;"><img alt="Jax Taylor Breaks His Silence on Ex Brittany Cartwright Restraining Order Abuse Allegations Throwback" class="size-large wp-image-4421171" height="1024" src="https://www.usmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Jax-Taylor-Breaks-His-Silence-on-Ex-Brittany-Cartwright-Restraining-Order-Abuse-Allegations-GettyImages-1186834523.jpg?w=682" width="682" /><figcaption class="wp-caption-text"><span class="caption">Jax Taylor and Brittany Cartwright.</span> <span class="credit">Frazer Harrison/Getty Images</span></figcaption></figure>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/shows/the-valley/"><em>The Valley</em></a> star alleged in the Thursday, August 27, filing that Taylor once threatened to “gut [her] like a fish” and physically attacked her during their marriage.</p>
<p>Cartwright further alleged that while <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/brittany-cartwright-says-jax-taylor-was-hostile-at-sons-school/">attending their son’s school orientation</a> earlier this month together, “Jax became visibly aggressive and hostile towards me.”</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/brittany-cartwright-breaks-silence-on-jax-taylor-divorce-filing/" target="_blank">Brittany Cartwright Cries as She Breaks Silence on Divorce Filing</a></p>
<p>She claimed that Taylor, who is now dating Cartwright’s <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/brittany-cartwright-addresses-jax-taylors-publicist-romance/">former publicist <strong>Lori Krebs</strong></a>, called her “gross” and that others who saw the interaction were “rather uncomfortable.”</p>
<p>Cartwright alleged that Taylor “has a tendency of not being able to control his anger and emotions.”</p>
<p><em>Us</em> confirmed on Friday that a judge ordered Taylor to stay 100 yards away from Cartwright. Her request for sole custody of Cruz was denied pending a court hearing.</p>
