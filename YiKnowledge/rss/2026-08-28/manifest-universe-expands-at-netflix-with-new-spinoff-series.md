<p>Netflix&#8217;s <em><a href="https://www.joblo.com/tag/manifest/">Manifest</a></em> is preparing for takeoff again as the streamer maps a flight plan for <em>Arrivals</em>, a spinoff of the original series. Today&#8217;s announcement comes exactly five years after <a href="https://www.netflix.com/browse">Netflix</a> announced it would revive <em><a href="https://www.joblo.com/manifest-season-4-part-2-trailer-previews-the-final-episodes-of-the-netflix-series/">Manifest</a></em> for a fourth and final season following its cancellation on <a href="https://www.nbc.com/">NBC</a>. The new series, titled <em>Arrivals: A New Manifest Mystery</em>, introduces new characters and storylines to the sci-fi universe.</p>



<p><em>Arrivals</em> hails from <em>Manifest</em> creator, executive producer, and showrunner Jeff Rake and co-executive producers Margaret Easley and Laura Putney; the trio serves as co-creators, executive producers, and showrunners of the spinoff series.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-what-to-expect-from-arrivals-a-new-manifest-mystery">What to Expect From <em>Arrivals: A New Manifest Mystery</em></h2>



<p>According to Netflix, <em>Arrivals</em> tells the twisty story of what happens &#8220;when a mysterious plane crash-lands in Denver, the passengers emerge with no memory of who they are, where they came from, or why they&#8217;re here. A young social worker and a novice Homeland Security agent team up with the new arrivals in an attempt to stop a humanity-ending catastrophe, discovering along the way that they are all part of the very puzzle they&#8217;re trying to solve.&#8221;</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-charting-a-new-flight-path">Charting a New Flight Path</h2>



<p>While there is no indication of whether or not <em>Arrivals </em>will connect with <em>Manifest</em>, Rake is excited to expand the story in new ways: &#8220;The world of <em>Manifest</em> has more stories to tell. To now bring fans a brand-new mystery from that world is quite simply beyond my wildest dreams,&#8221; Rake said. &#8220;<em>Arrivals</em> co-creators and co-showrunners Laura Putney and Margaret Easley have been my trusted colleagues for over a decade. Their contribution to the <em>Manifest </em>mothership cannot be overstated. It was a no-brainer to make them my partners on this spinoff. Together we&#8217;ve set out to create something bold, surprising, deeply human, and full of the kind of big ideas that spark conversations long after the credits roll. We can&#8217;t wait for a whole new audience – and longtime Manifest fans – to discover <em>Arrivals</em>.&#8221;</p>



<p>If you&#8217;re concerned about not being able to board <em>Arrivals</em>, don&#8217;t be. Rake and his team are determined to make the spinoff accessible enough for newcomers to book a flight on the new series, while still giving fans of the original more of what they love.</p>
