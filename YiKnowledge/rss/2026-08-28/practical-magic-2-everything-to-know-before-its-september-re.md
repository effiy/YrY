<p><em>Practical Magic 2</em> will hit theaters nearly 30 years after <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/pictures/practical-magic-cast-where-are-they-now/">the original movie premiered</a> — with <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/nicole-kidman/"><strong>Nicole Kidman</strong></a> and <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/sandra-bullock/"><strong>Sandra Bullock</strong></a> reprising their starring roles.</p>
<p>Warner Bros. announced in June 2024 via TikTok that <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/news/nicole-kidman-practical-magic-2-has-been-in-the-works-for-a-while/">a follow-up</a> to the original film, based on the 1995 novel of the same name by <strong>Alice Hoffman</strong>, was officially in “development.”</p><div class="mb1-wrapper" id="starter">
						<div class="htlad-mb1" id="mb1-static"></div>
						<div class="htlad-leader2" id="leader2-inline-static"></div>
					</div>
<p>In addition to Kidman and Bullock appearing in the film as sisters Gillian and Sally Owens, respectively, the costars are also set to produce alongside <strong>Denise Di Novi</strong>. Filmmaker <strong>Akiva Goldsman</strong> (<em>The Client</em>, <em>Batman Forever</em>, <em>I, Robot</em>, <em>Cinderella Man</em>) was tapped to write the screenplay.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/news/sandra-bullock-is-excited-about-practical-magic-2-with-nicole-kidman/" target="_blank">Sandra Bullock Is ‘Excited’ About ‘Practical Magic 2’ With Nicole Kidman</a></p>
<p>The highly-anticipated sequel will transport viewers from the idyllic New England setting from the 1998 movie to England when it hits theaters in September 2026.</p>
<p>Scroll down for everything we know about <em>Practical Magic 2</em>:</p>
<h2>Who Is in the ‘Practical Magic 2’ Cast?</h2>
<figure class="wp-caption alignnone" id="attachment_4360673" style="width: 900px;"><img alt="Everything to Know About Practical Magic 2 Owens Family" class="size-large wp-image-4360673" height="393" src="https://www.usmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Everything-to-Know-About-Practical-Magic-2-SANDRA-BULLOCK-MAISIE-WILLIAMS-JOEY-KING-NICOLE-KIDMAN-PRACTICAL-MAGIC-2.jpeg?w=900" width="900" /><figcaption class="wp-caption-text"><span class="caption">Sandra Bullock, Maisie Williams, Joey King, Nicole Kidman as Sally, Antonia, Kylie and Gillian.</span> <span class="credit">Courtesy of Warner Bros. Pictures</span></figcaption></figure>
<p>Kidman confirmed in June 2024 that she and Bullock will return to star in the sequel as the Owens sisters.</p>
<p>“Yes, I will be in it,” Kidman told <a href="https://people.com/nicole-kidman-confirms-reuniting-sandra-bullock-practical-magic-sequel-exclusive-8663236" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><em>People</em></a>. “And Sandy will be in it. And that’s that. There’s a lot more to tell which is why we go, ‘OK, this is kind of interesting now to be able to do this.”</p>
<p>Seven more stars were added to the cast in July 2025, including some returning faces. <strong>Dianne Wiest</strong> and <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/stockard-channing/"><strong>Stockard Channing</strong></a> will be reprising their roles of Aunt Jet and Aunt Franny, respectively. <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/joey-king/"><strong>Joey King</strong></a> will portray Sally’s daughter, portraying the grown up version of Kylie. <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/maisie-williams/"><strong>Maisie Williams</strong></a> will play Sally’s youngest daughter, Antonia. The rest of the cast includes, <strong>Lee Pace</strong>, <strong>Xolo Maridueña</strong> and <strong>Solly McLeod</strong>.</p>
<p>Unfortunately, <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/evan-rachel-wood/"><strong>Evan Rachel Wood</strong></a> revealed she would <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/practical-magic-2-cast-why-evan-rachel-wood-is-not-returning/">not be returning</a> to the sequel to reprise her role of Kylie Owens.</p>
<p>“I am getting asked about this a lot, so I’ll just clear it up now,” she wrote via her Instagram Stories in July 2025. “I was not asked to come back and be in the #PracticalMagic sequel.”</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/pictures/famous-tv-and-movie-witches-201363/" target="_blank">Celebrities Who've Played Witches in Movies and TV</a></p>
<h2>When Does ‘Practical Magic 2’ Come Out?</h2>
<p>Warner Bros. confirmed September 11, 2026 is the release date for <em>Practical Magic 2</em>. The film will roll out early in some markets on September 9 or September 10, including select theaters in the U.S.</p>
<h2>What Is the Plot of ‘Practical Magic 2’?</h2>
<figure class="wp-caption alignnone" id="attachment_4360674" style="width: 900px;"><img alt="Everything to Know About Practical Magic 2 Joey King" class="size-large wp-image-4360674" height="395" src="https://www.usmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Everything-to-Know-About-Practical-Magic-2-JOEY-KING-PRACTICAL-MAGIC-2.jpeg?w=900" width="900" /><figcaption class="wp-caption-text"><span class="caption">Joey King as Kylie.</span> <span class="credit">Courtesy Warner Bros. Pictures</span></figcaption></figure>
<p>Per IMDb, the synopsis is, “A multi generational family of witches, cursed to be loveless for centuries, attempts to break the spell by confronting dark secrets and sacrificing for each other.”</p>
<p>Most details for the movie are under wraps and when asked about the plot, Kidman teased to <em>People</em>, “You have to ask Sandy.”</p>
<p>“We’ve been circling it for a while, but you know, it’s still in [the early stages],” she added at the time.</p>
<p>The 1998 original film, directed by <strong>Griffin Dunne</strong>, followed sisters Gillian and Sally, descendants of a long line of witches raised by their aunts after their parent’s death from a family curse. After Sally accidentally kills Gillian’s abusive boyfriend, the pair must use their practical magic to destroy his evil spirit before it kills them.</p>
<p>Although the film received mixed reviews after its release and lost money during its box office run, it has since gained a cult following.</p>
<h2>What Have Nicole Kidman and Sandra Bullock Said About a ‘Practical Magic’ Sequel?</h2>
<figure class="wp-caption alignnone" id="attachment_4360670" style="width: 900px;"><img alt="Everything to Know About Practical Magic 2 Nicole Kidman" class="size-large wp-image-4360670" height="600" src="https://www.usmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Everything-to-Know-About-Practical-Magic-2-NICOLE-KIDMAN-PRACTICAL-MAGIC-2.jpeg?w=900" width="900" /><figcaption class="wp-caption-text"><span class="caption">Nicole Kidman as Gillian Owens.</span> <span class="credit">Jaap Buitendijk/Warner Bros. Entertainment</span></figcaption></figure>
<p>The sequel may have taken over two decades to get made, but Kidman and Bullock have said nothing but positive things about filming the original.</p>
<p>“I love that movie,” Kidman said of <em>Practical Magic</em> when she reunited with Bullock at the 2018 Academy Awards red carpet. “I showed that movie to my kids. It’s a little above their [level of understanding] … But we’re really good sisters.”</p>
<p>Bullock, for her part, said she had a “great time” filming with Kidman before reminiscing about the iconic “midnight margaritas” scene.</p>
<p>“We weren’t drunk when we shot these parts or when we did our close-ups,” Bullock recalled in a clip from <em>Today</em> that resurfaced in 2019. “But we had to reshoot a scene where it was far away and we were on our backs and Nicole just put the bottle on the table and said, ‘You know what? There’s no reason we shouldn’t be drunk here.’ And I said, ‘You know what? That’s true.’ ”</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/sandra-bullock-and-joey-king-enjoy-rare-night-out-at-coldplay-concert/" target="_blank">Sandra Bullock Enjoys Rare Night With ‘Practical Magic’ Costar Joey King </a></p>
<h2>Will Griffin Dunne Direct the ‘Practical Magic’ Sequel?</h2>
<p>Dunne will not be directing the project, but Bullock praised his storytelling abilities while attending a book party celebrating his memoir, <em>The Friday Afternoon Club: A Family Memoir</em>.</p>
<p>“You feel like you were always in the back seat experiencing it all with him. He has lived more lives than a cat,” Bullock <a href="https://people.com/sandra-bullock-playfully-warns-practical-magic-reunion-will-be-good-trouble-at-director-griffin-dunne-s-book-launch-8666112" rel="noopener" target="_blank">told</a> reporters. “I don’t know anyone who has experienced what he has and can still manage to find the love and humor in even the darkest of places.”</p>
<p><strong>Susanne Bier</strong> serves as director of the sequel.</p>
<h2>Is There a 1st Look of ‘Practical Magic 2’?</h2>
<p>After kicking off filming in July 2025, Kidman took to <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/DMQswYkSd2e/">Instagram</a> to share a clip of her and Bullock hugging in a scene.</p>
<p>“The witches are back ✨ Owens sisters’ first day on set!” Kidman <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/news/fans-react-to-first-look-of-practical-magic-2-with-sandra-bullock-nicole-kidman/">captioned the reel</a>.</p>

<p>The first <em>Practical Magic</em> movie took place in a fictional New England town but was filmed in Coupeville, Washington, on Whidbey Island.</p>
<p>The sequel will <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/news/where-is-practical-magic-2-being-filmed-the-sequels-location-revealed/">take fans across the pond</a>, as it was filmed at Warner Bros. Studios Leavesden in England. Additional scenes were shot at various locations around London.</p>
<h2>Nicole Kidman Announces ‘Practical Magic 2’ Has Wrapped</h2>
<p>Kidman confirmed in September 2025 that the <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/news/nicole-kidman-and-sandra-bullock-wrap-practical-magic-2-filming/">magical sequel finished filming</a>, sharing a video of her and Bullock walking together off into the sunset.</p>
<p>“That’s a wrap on #PracticalMagic2! Thank you to the cast & crew for all your magic ✨,” she captioned the <a href="https://www.instagram.com/reels/DOjHQihjnOw/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Instagram post</a>.</p>

<h2>Is There a ‘Practical Magic 2’ Official Trailer?</h2>
<figure class="wp-caption alignnone" id="attachment_4360676" style="width: 900px;"><img alt="Everything to Know About Practical Magic 2 Sandra Bullock" class="size-large wp-image-4360676" height="395" src="https://www.usmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Everything-to-Know-About-Practical-Magic-2-SANDRA-BULLOCK-PRACTICAL-MAGIC-2.jpeg?w=900" width="900" /><figcaption class="wp-caption-text"><span class="caption">Sandra Bullock as Sally Owens.</span> <span class="credit">Courtesy Warner Bros. <p>Warner Bros. released <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/news/practical-magic-2-trailer-shows-owens-sisters-fighting-curse/">the official trailer in June 2026</a>, giving fans a taste at Sally and Gillian back in action — this time to stop Sally’s daughter Kylie from getting hurt while trying to break their family’s love curse.</p>
<p>“No magic is stronger than sisterhood. Cast by fate, tested by time and never broken,” Aunt Franny says <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?app=desktop&amp;v=Ho10_4IX1jE&amp;ra=m" rel="noopener" target="_blank">in the trailer</a> as clips of Sally and Gillian and Franny and her sister, Aunt Jet, sit around a table with midnight margaritas.</p>
<p>Their happiness is short-lived after Kylie learns firsthand that their family’s curse — any man an Owens woman loves is doomed to die — leads to her boyfriend getting hit by a car.</p>
<p>Amid her heartbreak, Kylie vows to “fix the curse” and runs off to the U.K. where Sally and Gillian follow in hopes of saving her and lifting the curse once and for all.</p>
<p>“When you have someone who knows who you were and who you will always be, there is no deeper tie, no stronger bond,” Sally says as glimpses of her and Gillian’s rocky quest unfolds. “I want you to know if anything happens to me, Gilly it’s you. You are my person.”</p>
