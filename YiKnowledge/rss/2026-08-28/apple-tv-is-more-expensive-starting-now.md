<img src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2025/11/26103440/MixCollage-26-Nov-2025-10-34-AM-5124.jpg" /><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Remember, less than a decade ago, when streaming services were presented as a cheaper alternative to cable? We recognize this isn&#8217;t a novel observation anymore, but, well, they just keep getting more expensive. The latest example of this is Apple TV, which announced another $2 price hike this morning, effective immediately. </span><a href="https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/apple-tv-price-increase-streaming-service-monthly-plan-1236845906/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Per </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Variety</span></i></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, the new price for the monthly plan is $14.99, up from $12.99. (The annual plan also jumped $20, from $99 to $119.) For reference, this is three times as costly as the plan was when it launched for $4.99 in 2019.&nbsp;</span></p><!-- RevContent --> <div id="revcontent-hidden"> <!-- revisit --><!-- admarker --> 
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">We&#8217;ll admit that Apple TV does have a significantly more robust slate of original programming than it did when it launched (and that programming doesn&#8217;t tend to tell you what&#8217;s happening on screen </span><a href="https://www.avclub.com/matt-damon-netflix-the-rip-reiterate-dialogue-phones"><span style="font-weight: 400;">three or four times per episode in case you&#8217;re looking at your phone</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">). The streamer&#8217;s already had its best-ever year at the Emmys, at least nomination-wise; both </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Widow&#8217;s Bay</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Pluribus</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> scored nearly 20, behind only </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">The Pitt</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Hacks</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">, both on HBO Max. Still, it&#8217;s certainly a pretty steep climb in seven years; as </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Variety</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> notes, the price of the service has gone up each of the past four years.&nbsp;</span></p><!-- inlinecontent_1 --> </p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">It&#8217;s not just Apple TV; prices of streaming services keep going up. </span><a href="https://www.avclub.com/netflix-raising-prices-again-20-bucks-a-month-standard"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Earlier this year</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, for example, Netflix raised the price of its ad-free plan to $19.99 per month. Its cheapest plan, which has ads in it, went up to $8.99 at the same time.&nbsp;</span></p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p>
