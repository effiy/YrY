<p><strong><a href="https://www.comingsoon.net/tag/fall">Fall</a></strong>, the 2022 survival horror movie starring <a href="https://www.comingsoon.net/tag/virginia-gardner">Virginia Gardner</a> and Grace Caroline Currey, is now available to stream for free ahead of the upcoming sequel. The new movie, titled <a href="https://www.comingsoon.net/tag/fall-2-deadpoint">Fall 2: Deadpoint</a>, is scheduled to arrive in theaters next week.</p>



<p>Directed by Scott Mann, Fall is about two best friends who decide it’d be a fun idea to climb a 2,000-foot-tall television broadcasting tower. Upon arriving at the top of the structure, however, the two of them become stranded and have no way to get down. The rest of the movie is then about them trying to safely find a way back to the ground before dehydration, vultures, or other threats kill them.</p>



<p>While not a record-breaking hit at the box office, Fall brought in around $21 million at the box office off a modest $3 million budget. It received positive reviews from critics and movie-goers alike, as it has a 79 percent score on both the Tomatometer and Popcornmeter on <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/fall" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Rotten Tomatoes</a>.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-what-is-the-new-fall-sequel-movie-about">What is the new Fall sequel movie about?</h2>



<p>Directed by Peter and Michael Spierig, Fall 2: Deadpoint stars Harriet Slater, Arsema Thomas, Tom Brittney, and Grace Caroline Currey. Rather than a broadcasting tower, the sequel sees the main characters become stuck on a dangerous mountain plank walk.</p>



<p>The official synopsis reads, “On a perilous climb across Thailand&#8217;s Mount Kwan, two climbers become trapped thousands of feet above the ground, where vertigo-inducing heights, sheer exposure, and impossible odds turn every moment into a fight for survival. <a href="https://www.comingsoon.net/tag/lionsgate">Lionsgate</a> and Capstone Studios present, Grindstone Entertainment Group presents, a Tea Shop and Capstone Studios production, a Flawless production, in association with Cousin Jones.”</p>



<p>Fall 2: Deadpoint will be released in the United States on September 2, 2026, by Lionsgate.</p>
