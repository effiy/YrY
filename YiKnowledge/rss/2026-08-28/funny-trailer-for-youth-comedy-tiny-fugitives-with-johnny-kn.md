<em>"There is nothing waiting for us back home..." "What if we never went home?"</em> Inaugural Entertainment has unveiled an official trailer for a movie called <strong><em>Tiny Fugitives</em></strong>, an extra fun coming-of-age <a href="https://www.firstshowing.net/category/humor/">comedy</a> about kids going wild at Jewish summer camp. Produced by Seth Rogen & Evan Goldberg, this adventure is hitting theaters in September. A reckless group of 12 year olds set out on a mission to spend the rest of their lives at sleepaway camp – with a scheme that will allow them to avoid reality. They’ll just have to get past Bob – the camp's deranged, relentless, 50-year-old lifer counselor. Played by <strong>Johnny Knoxville</strong> himself, Bob is dead set on shutting down their plans. Knoxville co-stars with <strong>Benjamin Pajak</strong>, <strong>Aaron Harris</strong>, <strong>Isa Steiner</strong>, <strong>Jai Soundar</strong>, <strong>Alyssa Gervasi</strong>, <strong>Blake Anderson</strong>, and <strong>Jay Baruchel</strong>. Reminds me of growing up and all my summer camp memories! This actually looks surprisingly hilarious and not as cliche or cringe as most camp comedies nowadays. There is even a riff to the <em>Jurassic Park</em> fence scene (seen in this trailer).
