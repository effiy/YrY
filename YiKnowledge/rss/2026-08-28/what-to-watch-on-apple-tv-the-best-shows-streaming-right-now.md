<img src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2025/12/28155014/MixCollage-28-Aug-2026-01-17-PM-1848.jpg" /><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Once upon a time, Apple TV was one of the most underrated and underappreciated streamers out there. These days, not so much. Thanks to the critical and cultural momentum of shows like </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Ted Lasso, Severance, Slow Horses, </span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">and </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Widow’s Bay</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">, it’s made a name for itself as a destination for quality, high-end comedies and dramas. (It also racked up </span><a href="https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/07/apple-scores-record-89-emmy-award-nominations/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">89 Emmy nominations </span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">this year.) But although a handful of its programs have become bona fide hits, there are many underrated gems in Apple’s library of originals, which are all worth your time. Here are our picks for the shows you need to stream on Apple TV right now.</span></p>
<div class="wp-caption aligncenter" id="attachment_1859219418" style="width: 754px;"><img alt="" class="size-full wp-image-1859219418" height="400" src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2025/12/28151716/Apple-The-Buccaneers.jpg" width="744" /><p class="wp-caption-text" id="caption-attachment-1859219418">(Photo: Apple TV)</p><!-- inlinecontent_1 --> </p></div>
<h2><b><i>The Buccaneers&nbsp;</i></b></h2>
<p><b>Stars</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Kristine Froseth, Alisha Boe, Josie Totah, Christina Hendricks, Josh Dylan, Imogen Waterhouse, Simone Kirby, Matthew Broome, Leighton Meester</span></p>
<p><b>Number of seasons:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Two&nbsp;</span></p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">A colorful, aggressively youthful adaptation of Edith Wharton’s classic novel, </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">The Buccaneers’ </span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">whole vibe is basically &#8220;what if The CW made a period drama?” The show centers on Annabel “Nan” St. George (Froseth), who moves to London after one of her best friends lands a titled husband. Love triangles, high society hijinks, and a plot that moves at a breakneck pace ensue. </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">The Buccaneers</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> features some pretty great performances, even greater gowns, and fun music choices. The series has been renewed for a third season.</span></p><!-- admarker --> 
<div class="wp-caption aligncenter" id="attachment_1859219419" style="width: 754px;"><img alt="" class="size-full wp-image-1859219419" height="400" src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2025/12/28151758/Apple-Criminal-Record.jpg" width="744" /><p class="wp-caption-text" id="caption-attachment-1859219419">(Photo: Apple TV)</p></div>
<h2><b><i>Criminal Record</i></b></h2>
<p><b>Stars</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Peter Capaldi, Cush Jumbo, Cathy Tyson, Shaun Dooley, Charlie Creed-Miles</span></p>
<p><b>Number of seasons</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Two</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">This British thriller features an enticing cat-and-mouse between two detectives: Daniel Hegarty (Capaldi), a veteran, and June Lenker (Jumbo), a rookie. An anonymous phone call puts them on the same investigation when an old case is reopened, which leads to June discovering the details of Hegarty’s sordid past. The second season sees them legitimately join forces to thwart a terrorist plot, which muddies their relationship even further.&nbsp;</span></p><!-- RevContent --> <div id="revcontent-hidden"> <!-- revisit -->
<div class="wp-caption aligncenter" id="attachment_1859219420" style="width: 754px;"><img alt="" class="size-full wp-image-1859219420" height="400" src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2025/12/28151828/Apple-Dickinson.jpg" width="744" /><p class="wp-caption-text" id="caption-attachment-1859219420">(Photo: Apple TV)</p></div>
<h2><b><i>Dickinson</i></b></h2>
<p><b>Stars:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Hailee Steinfeld, Ella Hunt, Anya Baryshnikov, Jane Krakowski, Toby Huss, Adrian Blake Enscoe, Amanda Warren</span></p>
<p><b>Number of seasons</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Three</span></p><!-- admarker --> 
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Alena Smith’s</span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Dickinson</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is the bold, surreal coming-of-age story of famed poet Emily Dickinson (Steinfeld). An audacious aspiring writer who doesn’t fit into the era she was born in, Emily conspicuously fights to break barriers to be a published author, even if it means rebelling against her strict parents or frequently talking to an imaginary figure known as Death. </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Dickinson </span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">also blooms as a love story for the ages between Emily and her best friend, Sue (Hunt), one that allegedly inspired Taylor Swift’s best song yet, “ivy,” from the album</span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> evermore</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">. Now that’s how you do romance.</span></p>
<div class="wp-caption aligncenter" id="attachment_1859219421" style="width: 754px;"><img alt="" class="size-full wp-image-1859219421" height="400" src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2025/12/28151851/Apple-For-All-Mankind.jpg" width="744" /><p class="wp-caption-text" id="caption-attachment-1859219421">(Photo: Apple TV(</p></div>
<h2><b><i>For All Mankind</i></b></h2>
<p><b>Stars:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Joel Kinnaman, Krys Marshall, Jodi Balfour, Wrenn Schmidt, Sonya Walger, Michael Dorman, Sarah Jones, Cynthy Wu, Coral Peña, Casey W. Johnson, Nate Corddry</span></p>
<p><b>Number of seasons</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Five</span></p>
<p><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">For All Mankind</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is one of the best dramas on Apple TV, period. The alt-history sci-fi series follows NASA astronauts on a mission to reach the moon (and later Mars!) in the ’70s after Russia won the initial Space Race. It’s a gripping, emotional workforce tale of the American dream that’s perfectly tied to the characters’ complex personal relationships.</span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> FAM</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> also boasts some of the most appealing visual effects out there, whether it’s showing us a stunning lunar base, space flights, or the surface of the Red Planet. The show has been renewed for a sixth and final season.</span></p>
<div class="wp-caption aligncenter" id="attachment_1859219425" style="width: 754px;"><img alt="" class="wp-image-1859219425 size-full" height="400" src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2025/12/28153744/Apple-Foundation.jpg" width="744" /><p class="wp-caption-text" id="caption-attachment-1859219425">(Photo: Apple TV)</p></div>
<h2><em><strong>Foundation</strong></em></h2>
<p><strong>Stars:</strong> Lee Pace, Jared Harris, Alexander Siddig, Cherry Jones, Troy Katsur, Lou Llobell, Terrence Mann, Pilou Asbæk</p>
<p><strong>Number of seasons</strong>: Three</p><!-- admarker --> 
<p>Based on a series of stories by Isaac Asimov, this ambitious, sprawling sci-fi epic is a millennia-spanning story of empire, prophecy, faith, and survival. (With a dash of space wizardry on top.) Featuring dozens of characters, exceptional CGI, and a willingness to wrestle with complex themes and weighty subject matter, it’s a series that’s unafraid to be both bold and difficult. A fourth season is in the works</p>
<div class="wp-caption aligncenter" id="attachment_1859219426" style="width: 754px;"><img alt="" class="wp-image-1859219426 size-full" height="400" src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2025/12/28153812/Apple-Manhunt.jpg" width="744" /><p class="wp-caption-text" id="caption-attachment-1859219426">(Photo: Apple TV)</p></div>
<h2><b><i>Manhunt</i></b></h2>
<p><b>Stars</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Tobias Menzies, Anthony Boyle, Hamish Linklater, Lili Taylor, Patton Oswalt, Betty Gabriel, Matt Walsh</span></p>
<p><b>Number of seasons: </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">One</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Based on the book </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Manhunt: The 12-Day Chase For Lincoln’s Killer</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">, this limited series turns the search for John Wilkes Booth following his escape from Ford’s Theatre into a gripping historical cat-and-mouse chase. Boyle is predictably excellent, but it’s Menzies who shines as Secretary Of War Edward Stanton, whose grief over Lincoln’s death is the emotional engine that powers the show.&nbsp;</span></p>
<div class="wp-caption aligncenter" id="attachment_1859219427" style="width: 754px;"><img alt="" class="size-full wp-image-1859219427" height="400" src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2025/12/28153814/Apple-Margots-Got-Money-Troubles.jpg" width="744" /><p class="wp-caption-text" id="caption-attachment-1859219427">(Photo: Apple TV)</p></div>
<h2><b><i>Margot’s Got Money Troubles</i></b></h2>
<p><b>Stars:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Elle Fanning, Michelle Pfeiffer, Nick Offerman, Greg Kinnear, Thaddea Graham, Michael Angarano, Nicole Kidman</span></p>
<p><b>Number of seasons:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> One</span></p><!-- admarker --> 
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Created by David E. Kelley and based on Rufi Thorpe&#8217;s 2024 novel, the series follows a young woman who starts an OnlyFans to make ends meet after an affair with a professor leaves her pregnant. Hilarious and heartwarming by turns, Offerman and Pfeiffer are particularly fantastic together. The series has been renewed for a second season.&nbsp;</span></p>
<div class="wp-caption aligncenter" id="attachment_1859219439" style="width: 754px;"><img alt="" class="size-full wp-image-1859219439" height="400" src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2025/12/28154047/Apple-Maximum-Pleaseure-Guaranteed.jpg" width="744" /><p class="wp-caption-text" id="caption-attachment-1859219439">(Photo; Apple TV)</p></div>
<h2><b><i>Maximum Pleasure Guaranteed</i></b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Stars:</strong> Tatiana Maslany, Kiarra Hamagami Goldberg, Charlie Hall, Jake Johnson, Jessy Hodges, Nola Wallace, Jon Michael Hill, Dolly de Leon</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Number of seasons</strong>: One</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">An addictive thriller whose release got a bit overshadowed by the simultaneous arrival of </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Widow’s Bay, </span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">the story follows Paula (Maslany), a divorced fact-checker who gets scammed by a camboy online and finds herself pulled into a high-stress world of crime. Surprisingly funny and with a banging soundtrack, it makes for an easy, satisfying binge.</span></p>
<div class="wp-caption aligncenter" id="attachment_1859219429" style="width: 754px;"><img alt="" class="size-full wp-image-1859219429" height="400" src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2025/12/28153817/Apple-Murderbot.jpg" width="744" /><p class="wp-caption-text" id="caption-attachment-1859219429">(Photo: Apple TV)</p></div>
<h2><b><i>Murderbot&nbsp;</i></b></h2>
<p><b>Stars:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Alexander Skarsgård, David Dastmalchian, Noma Dumezweni, Sabrina Wu, Clark Gregg, John Cho, Akshay Khanna</span></p>
<p><b>Number of seasons:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> One</span></p><!-- admarker --> 
<p><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Murderbot</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">, based on Martha Wells&#8217; darkly comedic sci-fi novels, centers on a private security cyborg (Skarsgård) who gains free will and emotions. But this bot must hide this truth and keep completing dangerous missions, even if all it wants to do is watch soap operas. A second season is on its way.</span></p>
<div class="wp-caption aligncenter" id="attachment_1859219440" style="width: 754px;"><img alt="" class="size-full wp-image-1859219440" height="400" src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2025/12/28154223/Apple-Mythic-Quest.jpg" width="744" /><p class="wp-caption-text" id="caption-attachment-1859219440">(Photo: Apple TV)</p></div>
<h2><b><i>Mythic Quest</i></b></h2>
<p><b>Stars</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Rob Mac, Charlotte Nicdao, Danny Pudi, Ashly Burch, David Hornsby, Imani Hakim, F. Murray Abraham, Jessie Ennis, Caitlin McGee, Naomi Ekperigin</span></p>
<p><b>Number of seasons</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Four</span></p>
<p><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Mythic Quest</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> hails from the team behind </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">It’s Always Sunny In Philadelphia</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">, so you already know this workplace comedy is full of hijinks, wacky comedy, and an even wackier set of characters. The show chronicles a video game company run by the egotistical Ian Grimm (Mac), who often clashes with fellow lead engineer and egoist Poppy Li (Nicdao) and the rest of his team. But even with all the wackiness, </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Mythic Quest</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> somehow finds inventive ways to be relevant, sharp, and surprisingly emotional. The fourth and final season of the show wrapped up in March 2025.</span></p>
<div class="wp-caption aligncenter" id="attachment_1859219430" style="width: 754px;"><img alt="" class="wp-image-1859219430 size-full" height="400" src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2025/12/28153819/Apple-Pachinko.jpg" width="744" /><p class="wp-caption-text" id="caption-attachment-1859219430">(Photo: Apple TV)</p></div>
<h2><b><i>Pachinko</i></b></h2>
<p><b><i>Stars</i></b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Minha Kim, Youn Yuh-jung, Lee Min-ho, Jin Ha, Soji Arai, Jeong In-ji, Han Jun-woo, Kaho Minami, Anna Sawai, Jimmi Simpson, Jung Eun-chae</span></p><!-- admarker --> 
<p><b><i>Number of seasons</i></b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Two</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Based on Min Jin Lee’s sprawling novel of the same name, </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Pachinko</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is an endearing, intricately woven tale of multiple generations of a single Korean immigrant family. The focal point is Sunja, whose journey is covered from her simple, traumatic childhood to her twilight years in Japan. Despite tackling some heavy topics, </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Pachinko </span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">consistently remains heartwarming and visually stunning (hat-tip to Justin Chon and Kogonada’s alluring direction).</span></p>
<div class="wp-caption aligncenter" id="attachment_1859219431" style="width: 754px;"><img alt="" class="wp-image-1859219431 size-full" height="400" src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2025/12/28153821/Apple-Platonic.jpg" width="744" /><p class="wp-caption-text" id="caption-attachment-1859219431">(Photo: Apple TV)</p></div>
<h2><b><i>Platonic</i></b></h2>
<p><b>Stars</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Seth Rogen, Rose Byrne, Luke Macfarlane, Carla Gallo, Rachel Rosenbloom, Guy Branum, Janet Varney</span></p>
<p><b>Number of seasons:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Two&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">In this endearing comedy, former college besties Will (Rogen&#8217;s hippie brewmaster) and Sylvia (Byrne&#8217;s event planner) end their five-year estrangement after the former&#8217;s divorce. Both of them quickly resume their co-dependent, slightly toxic, and yet sweet friendship that tends to get in the way of their respective careers and relationships. The series has been renewed for a third season.</span></p>
<div class="wp-caption aligncenter" id="attachment_1859219432" style="width: 754px;"><img alt="" class="size-full wp-image-1859219432" height="400" src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2025/12/28153823/Apple-Pluribus.jpg" width="744" /><p class="wp-caption-text" id="caption-attachment-1859219432">(Photo: Apple TV)</p><!-- admarker --> </div>
<h2><b><i>Pluribus</i></b></h2>
<p><b>Stars</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Rhea Seehorn, Karolina Wydra, Carlos Manuel Vesga, Samba Schutte, Miriam Shor, Davis Taffer</span></p>
<p><b>Number of seasons: </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">One</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Vince Gilligan returns to his</span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> X-Files</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> roots (and Albuquerque) in this sci-fi thriller about romance author Carol Sturka (Seehorn), who&#8217;s one of a dozen people unaffected by an extraterrestrial virus that has turned the rest of the population happy. The series has been renewed for a second season.&nbsp;</span></p>
<div class="wp-caption aligncenter" id="attachment_1859219441" style="width: 754px;"><img alt="" class="size-full wp-image-1859219441" height="400" src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2025/12/28154520/Apple-Schmigadoon.jpg" width="744" /><p class="wp-caption-text" id="caption-attachment-1859219441">(Photo: Apple TV)</p></div>
<h2><b><i>Schmigadoon!</i></b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Stars:</strong> Keegan Michael-Key, Cecily Strong, Ariana DeBose, Jane Krakowski, Jaime Camil, Alan Cumming, Kristin Chenoweth, Dove Cameron, Ann Harada</span></p>
<p><b>Number of seasons</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Two</span></p>
<p><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Schmigadoon!</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is a colorful love letter to the art of the movie musical. In the first season, the show follows a couple stranded in the whimsical titular town whose residents perpetually live in Golden Age-style musicals. In the second, the pair arrives in “Schmicago,” which is brimming with the sexy musicals of the ’60s and ’70s. The original tracks are quirky earworms, and Jane Krakowski gives an awards-worthy performance. What’s not to love?</span></p>
<div class="wp-caption aligncenter" id="attachment_1859219433" style="width: 754px;"><img alt="" class="size-full wp-image-1859219433" height="400" src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2025/12/28153825/Apple-Severance.jpg" width="744" /><p class="wp-caption-text" id="caption-attachment-1859219433">(Photo: Apple TV)</p><!-- admarker --> </div>
<h2><b><i>Severance&nbsp;</i></b></h2>
<p><b>Stars: </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">Adam Scott, Patricia Arquette, Britt Lower, John Turturro, Zach Cherry, Christopher Walken, Tramell Tillman, Dichen Lachman</span></p>
<p><b>Number of seasons: </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">Two</span></p>
<p><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Severance</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is pretty damn stunning. Created by Dan Erickson (with Ben Stiller as a producer and director), the thriller builds tension and anxiety with each passing episode. In the show, employees of a conglomerate named Lumon have severed memories (that is, they forget their private lives once they reach work). But they soon realize the company is up to nefarious things. </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Severance</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> also boasts career-best turns by Adam Scott and Zach Cherry, plus commendable breakout performances by Britt Lower and Tramell Tillman.</span></p>
<div class="wp-caption aligncenter" id="attachment_1859219434" style="width: 754px;"><img alt="" class="size-full wp-image-1859219434" height="400" src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2025/12/28153828/Apple-Shrinking.jpg" width="744" /><p class="wp-caption-text" id="caption-attachment-1859219434">(Photo: Apple TV)</p></div>
<h2><b><i>Shrinking</i></b></h2>
<p><b>Stars:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Jason Segel, Jessica Williams, Harrison Ford, Christa Miller, Ted McGinley, Michael Urie, Brett Goldstein, Lukita Maxwell</span></p>
<p><b>Seasons:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Three</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">A grieving therapist (Segel) adopts a radical new treatment method in which he tells his patients exactly what he thinks about them and their problems with varying results. Poignant, profound, hilarious, and heartwarming by turns, it’s a drama about family both biological and found. (And Ford, in particular, is fantastic.) A fourth season is in production.&nbsp;</span></p>
<div class="wp-caption aligncenter" id="attachment_1859219435" style="width: 754px;"><img alt="" class="size-full wp-image-1859219435" height="400" src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2025/12/28153830/Apple-Slow-Horses.jpg" width="744" /><p class="wp-caption-text" id="caption-attachment-1859219435">(Photo: Apple TV)</p><!-- admarker --> </div>
<h2><b><i>Slow Horses</i></b></h2>
<p><b>Stars: </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">Gary Oldman, Jack Lowden, Kristin Scott Thomas, Saskia Reeves, Rosalind Eleazar, Christopher Chung</span></p>
<p><b>Number of seasons: </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">Five</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Based on Mick Herron’s novels,</span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Slow Horses</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is a fantastic (and quite funny) British spy drama. The show centers on a team of MI5 screw-ups in Slough House, a sort of career purgatory led by Jackson Lamb (Oldman), who find themselves racing against the clock to save London from various disasters. Season six premieres in September.</span></p>
<div class="wp-caption aligncenter" id="attachment_1859219437" style="width: 754px;"><img alt="" class="size-full wp-image-1859219437" height="400" src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2025/12/28153834/Apple-The-Studio.jpg" width="744" /><p class="wp-caption-text" id="caption-attachment-1859219437">(Photo: Apple TV)</p></div>
<h2><b><i>The Studio&nbsp;</i></b></h2>
<p><b>Stars:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Seth Rogen, Ike Barinholtz, Chase Sui Wonders, Kathryn Hahn, Catherine O&#8217;Hara, Dewayne Perkins, Bryan Cranston</span></p>
<p><b>Number of seasons:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> One</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">In this highly entertaining satire, co-creator Rogen stars as Hollywood exec Matt Remick, who takes over as the new head of Continental Studios and hopes to make the movies of his dreams alongside his co-workers. If only it were that easy. Expect a ton of cameos from the likes of Martin Scorsese, Sarah Polley, Olivia Wilde, Zoë Kravitz, Dave Franco, and Adam Scott. The series has been renewed for a second season.</span></p>
<div class="wp-caption aligncenter" id="attachment_1859219436" style="width: 754px;"><img alt="" class="size-full wp-image-1859219436" height="400" src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2025/12/28153832/Apple-Ted-Lasso.jpg" width="744" /><p class="wp-caption-text" id="caption-attachment-1859219436">(Photo: Apple TV)</p><!-- admarker --> </div>
<h2><b><i>Ted Lasso</i></b></h2>
<p><b>Stars</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Jason Sudeikis, Hannah Waddingham, Brett Goldstein, Juno Temple, Nick Mohammed, Brendan Hunt, Toheeb Jimoh, Sarah Niles, Phil Dunster</span></p>
<p><b>Number of seasons:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Four</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">This award-winning comedy’s debut not only blew up Apple TV subscriptions, but it became a surprisingly hot topic for internet discourse. Jason Sudeikis plays the relentlessly charming Ted Lasso, who moves from Kansas to the U.K. to coach a struggling English football team and impart some folksy life lessons along the way. Following a third season finale that saw Ted return home to his family, season 4 sees him back in Richmond to help Rebecca (Waddingham) and Keeley (Temple) get a women’s team off the ground.&nbsp;</span></p>
<div class="wp-caption aligncenter" id="attachment_1859219438" style="width: 754px;"><img alt="" class="size-full wp-image-1859219438" height="400" src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2025/12/28153836/Apple-Widows-Bay.jpg" width="744" /><p class="wp-caption-text" id="caption-attachment-1859219438">(Photo: Apple TV)</p></div>
<h2><b><i>Widow’s Bay</i></b></h2>
