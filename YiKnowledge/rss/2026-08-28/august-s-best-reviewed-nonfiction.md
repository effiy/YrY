<p style="text-align: center;"><strong>1. <a href="https://bookshop.org/a/40/9780063465145" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><em>Don’t Be Evil: Bad Bosses, Fake Promises, and My Escape from Big Tech</em></a> by Claire Stapleton</strong><br />
<strong>(William Morrow and Company)</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><strong>4 Rave • 1 Positive • 1 Mixed</strong></p>
<p>“A blunt account of one woman’s transformation from eager-to-please corporate striver to clear-eyed activist. It well earns its place alongside other recent accounts of the misdeeds of Big Tech like Sarah Wynn-Williams’ <em>Careless People</em> and Anna Wiener’s <em>Uncanny Valley</em> … Books like Stapleton’s will at least arm people determined to reform them with information they’ll need to make that effort.”</p>
<p style="text-align: right;">–Harvey Freedenberg (<a href="https://www.bookpage.com/reviews/dont-be-evil-claire-stapleton-book-review/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><em>BookPage</em></a>)</p>
<p><a href="https://bookshop.org/a/40/9780385550246" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><img alt="" class="aligncenter wp-image-136716 size-medium" height="300" src="https://s26162.pcdn.co/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/08/66ddbd98cdecf722aa836070a0835414-197x300.gif" width="197" /></a></p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><strong>2. <a href="https://bookshop.org/a/40/9780385550246" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><em>The Ego Trip: Psychedelic Toads, a Trail of Deaths, and the Guru Who Peddled Transcendence</em></a> by Kimon de Greef<br />
(Doubleday)</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><strong>4 Rave • 1 Positive</strong></p>
<p>“A marvelous trip, exploring centuries of the Seris’ tribulations, the growth of commercial toad milking in a region dominated by narco gangsters, quarrels among rival gurus and competition between the sons of an English countess and a German baroness to develop 5-MeO-DMT into a pharmaceutical.”</p>
<p style="text-align: right;">–Annie Bartlett (<a href="https://www.bookpage.com/reviews/ego-trip-kimon-de-greef-book-review/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><em>BookPage</em>)</a></p>
<p><a href="https://bookshop.org/a/40/9781324098423" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><img alt="The Rise and Fall of the Artificial State" class="wp-image-136833 size-medium aligncenter" height="300" src="https://s26162.pcdn.co/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/08/f726495dd54ab0c442e192bdfef1c67d-198x300.gif" width="198" /></a></p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><strong>3. <a href="https://bookshop.org/a/40/9781324098423" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><em>The Rise and Fall of the Artificial State</em></a> by Jill Lepore</strong><br />
<strong>(Liveright)</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><strong>3 Rave • 2 Positive • 1 Mixed</strong></p>
<p>“Staggering, sobering, yet essential reading for all who want to understand the arc of this increasingly digital, online, corporate, and out-of-control world.”</p>
<p style="text-align: right;">–Carol Haggas (<a href="https://www.booklistonline.com/products/9825772" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><em>Booklist</em></a>)</p>
<p><a href="https://bookshop.org/a/40/9781668206751" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><img alt="Days of Miracle and Wonder" class="wp-image-136770 size-medium aligncenter" height="300" src="https://s26162.pcdn.co/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/08/88e4f390241f74537037ae7087e653fc-198x300.gif" width="198" /></a></p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><strong>4. <a href="https://bookshop.org/a/40/9781668206751" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><em>Days of Miracle and Wonder: Paul Simon and the Trials and Triumphs of Graceland</em></a> by Ahley Kahn</strong><br />
<strong>(Atria)</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><strong>4 Rave • 1 Mixed</strong></p>
<p>“What a welcome surprise … Has only made me appreciate the album’s intricacies and paradoxes all the more … Kahn lets his historical actors speak for themselves as often as possible, collecting a remarkably wide array of voices for so short a book.”</p>
<p style="text-align: right;">–Mark Braude (<a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/15/books/review/days-of-miracle-and-wonder-ashley-kahn-graceland.html?eafs_enabled=false" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><em>The New York Times Book Review</em></a>)</p>

<p style="text-align: center;"><strong>5. <a href="https://bookshop.org/a/40/9780593473658" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><em>The People’s Historian: The Outsized Life of Howard Zinn</em></a> by Dave Zirin<br />
