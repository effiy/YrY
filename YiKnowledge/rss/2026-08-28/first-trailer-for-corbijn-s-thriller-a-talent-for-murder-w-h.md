<em>"I'll give you one night... to show me if you can inhabit the mind of a killer."</em> Bleecker Street has revealed the first official trailer for the film titled <strong><em>A Talent for Murder</em></strong>, the latest feature directed by photographer / filmmaker <strong>Anton Corbijn</strong>. It's his first brand enw narrative feature since making <a href="https://www.firstshowing.net/2015/watch-new-us-trailer-anton-corbijns-life-featuring-dane-dehaan/"><em>Life</em></a> way back in 2015, and his latest since the doc <a href="https://www.firstshowing.net/2023/official-trailer-for-squaring-the-circle-the-story-of-hipgnosis-doc/"><em>Squaring the Circle: The Story of Hipgnosis</em></a> back in 2023. Though he has been making more music videos recently as well. This smoldering chamber thriller from director Anton Corbijn stars Oscar winner <strong>Helen Mirren</strong> + <strong>Alden Ehrenreich</strong> as opponents in a strange confrontation between a youthful literary agent and <em>The Talented Mr. Ripley</em> author Patricia Highsmith. A young man travels from New York to Switzerland to try to persuade acclaimed thriller writer Patricia Highsmith to write one final installment of <a href="https://amzn.to/45YeS9A" rel="noopener" target="_blank">her Tom Ripley series</a>, with his efforts gradually turning more sinister. Along with Mirren & Ehrenreich, this also stars <strong>Olivia Cooke</strong> & <strong>Juliet Stevenson</strong>. Premiering at TIFF this fall then in theaters starting <a href="https://www.firstshowing.net/schedule2026/#oct">in October</a>. This looks quite sneaky & tricky!
