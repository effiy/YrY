<div class="upx-component upx-embed-cnx"><div class="cnx-embed-holder">&amp;&amp;&amp;&amp;</div></div><p>Since releasing her debut album in 2019, <a href="https://uproxx.com/tag/ari-lennox/" target="_blank">Ari Lennox</a> has become one of the biggest names in R&#038;B. She has millions of monthly listeners on streaming, released Billboard-charting hits, and been nominated for a Grammy. </p>
<p>Lennox released her latest album, <em>Vacancy</em>, to standout reviews earlier this year, and as soon as we heard it, we knew we needed to invite her to perform on <em>Visionaries</em>, Uproxx&#8217;s first-of-its-kind performance show on a state-of-the-art XR stage. Lennox has many special songs to choose from, but for this event, she picked &#8220;Horoscope.&#8221; Surrounded by a dynamic visual world built in real-time to her music, voice, and movements, Lennox delivers a stunning performance of the <em>Vacancy</em> standout track. </p>
<p>Lennox&#8217;s performance captures what makes her such a compelling artist. “Horoscope” gives her plenty of room to showcase her powerhouse vocals that&#8217;s both classic yet modern, while the immersive XR environment adds a dynamic new dimension to the song. The result is a performance that feels both cinematic and deeply personal; it’s a proper showcase for a visionary artist who continues to push R&#038;B forward.</p>
<p>Watch Ari Lennox perform &#8220;Horoscope&#8221; for <em>Uproxx</em>&#8216;s Visionaries above (and check out previous performances from <a href="https://uproxx.com/music/coco-thang-4-u-performance-visionaries/" target="_blank">Coco Jones</a>, <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hrTDpDWnSY4" target="_blank">BigXthaPlug</a>, and <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-iuK60CNLpQ" target="_blank">Gunna</a>).</p>
