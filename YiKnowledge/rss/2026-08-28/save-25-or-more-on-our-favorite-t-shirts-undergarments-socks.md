<p class="article-paragraph skip">Buying new underwear isn&#8217;t fun. But you know what&#8217;s even less fun? Suffering through a long day of work with an uncomfortable bra or boxer briefs bothering your bits. Duluth Trading has taken 25 percent off its entire basics category in what it&#8217;s calling the <a href="https://www.duluthtrading.com/huge-savings/" rel="noreferrer noopener sponsored nofollow" target="_blank">Basics Sale</a>, and the 229 items involved cover boxer briefs, undershirts, T-shirts, tanks, bras, and socks. The color you pick matters as much as the item you pick, and the deals worth chasing are buried inside the color pickers rather than on the sale grid.</p>
