<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>AstraZeneca detailed the surprise failure of a closely watched heart disease clinical trial, laying out how its experimental drug offered no additional benefits for patients who were largely already on another type of medicine, the latest twist in a multibillion dollar market</strong>, <a href="https://www.statnews.com/2026/08/28/astrazeneca-ionis-wainua-eplontersen-attr-cm-study-failure-bridgebio-alnylam/" id="https://www.statnews.com/2026/08/28/astrazeneca-ionis-wainua-eplontersen-attr-cm-study-failure-bridgebio-alnylam/">STAT</a> explains.&#xa0;The study indicated that patients with a progressive condition known as ATTR-CM who received AstraZeneca&#x2019;s drug, what&#x2019;s called a silencer, saw no improvements if they were already taking another type of drug called a stabilizer, an effect so powerful that it tanked the whole Phase 3 study.</p>
