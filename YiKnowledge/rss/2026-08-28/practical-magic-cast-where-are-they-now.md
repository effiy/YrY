<p><em><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/news/nicole-kidman-celebrates-practical-magic-movie-anniversary/">Practical Magic</a></em>’s spellbinding tale of the Owens sisters’ quest to break their family curse isn’t just a film for the Halloween season — although <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/pictures/tv-shows-and-movies-thatll-give-you-the-fall-feels/">it’s a must-watch during the fall</a>.</p>
<p>The 1998 film starred <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/sandra-bullock/"><strong>Sandra Bullock</strong></a> as Sally Owens and <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/nicole-kidman/"><strong>Nicole Kidman</strong></a> as Gillian Owens, both of whom are unlucky in love thanks to a curse placed on the women of their family because of their witch roots.</p><div class="mb1-wrapper" id="starter">

<p>While Sally mourns the death of her husband, Michael (<strong>Mark Feuerstein</strong>) — and moves her daughters in with her aunts, Frances (<a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/stockard-channing/"><strong>Stockard Channing</strong></a>) and Jet (<a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/dianne-wiest-oscar-winner-says-shes-having-trouble-paying-rent-2015261/"><strong>Dianne Wiest</strong></a>) — Gillian gets into an abusive relationship. The siblings must come together to rid themselves of Gillian’s boyfriend, Jimmy (<a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/pictures/er-cast-where-are-they-now/"><strong>Goran Višnjić</strong></a>), and his ghost while embracing their witch side.</p>
<p>The cast’s bond — and <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1Ihb6FLSh64">famous midnight margaritas scene</a> — has continued to resonate with moviegoers for decades. The beloved film <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/news/practical-magic-2-cast-plot-everything-to-know-about-the-sequel/">sparked a sequel</a>, which Bullock and Kidman returned for, premiering in September 2026.</p>
