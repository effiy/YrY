<p>All thrillers have a few things in common: tension, suspense, high stakes, and danger—physical, psychological, or both. Often, there’s a ticking clock or a mystery to solve. Sometimes, a big twist. Narrators may be unreliable, and alibis may be fabricated. </p>



<p>There’s a formula to thrillers, and that formula is a feature, not a bug. Thriller writers at the top of their game know this: the constraints provide a framework that forces them to be creative if they want to stand out in one of the most popular genres of the day. </p>



<p>Whether you’re looking for the grittiest crime fiction, sci-fi psychological suspense, or a locked-room story that will have your blood pressure all the way up, there’s something for you in this collection of pulse-pounding reads from some of the best authors in the business.</p>

<p>The terror is coming from inside the house in this chilling work of psychological suspense. </p>



<p>A young woman at home with her kids during a blizzard hears footsteps coming up the stairs in the middle of the night and sees a man hiding in the shadows. (Is your heart racing yet?) After (quietly) waking her children, she bundles them into a secret room hidden behind a wall, where they hide together as the man tries to lure the children out. As she tries to decide whether to fight or flee, it hits her: she knows who he is and what he’s after. <em>Nighwatching</em> will have you sleeping with the lights on.</p>
</div></div></div></td></tr></table>

<p>If you like your thrillers with a side of sci-fi, you’ll love this brain-bending novel that blends quantum mechanics with string theory and existential angst. </p>



<p>Jason Dessen is a college professor living a pretty unremarkable life when he is abducted and knocked unconscious. When he comes to, the world around Jason looks the same, but his life is…not his life. He’s married to the wrong woman. His son doesn’t exist. And he isn’t a professor but a rockstar scientist being celebrated for a history-making discovery. But is he happy? </p>



<p>Also named as one of the best thrillers of the century, <em>Dark Matter</em> was adapted into a series starring Joel Edgerton and Jennifer Connelly, available on Apple TV. Season 2 drops August 28. </p>
</div></div></div></td></tr></table>
