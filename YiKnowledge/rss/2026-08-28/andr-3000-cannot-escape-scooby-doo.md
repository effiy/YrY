<img src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2025/05/05221018/andre-3000-feature.jpeg" /><p>There are some things we cannot escape in this life, any more than André &#8220;3000&#8221; Benjamin can get away from <a href="https://www.avclub.com/andre-3000-announces-album-new-blue-sun-1851020809">his love for restful, ambient flute music</a>. Or, say, giant Great Danes who sort of, but not completely, command a grasp of the English language, as <a href="https://deadline.com/2026/08/andre-3000-cathy-moriarty-scooby-doo-origins-series-cast-1237061273/"><em>Deadline&nbsp;</em>reports today</a> that Benjamin has returned to the&nbsp;<em>Scooby-Doo&nbsp;</em>fold more than 20 years after his last brush with Mystery Machine greatness.</p><!-- RevContent --> <div id="revcontent-hidden"> <!-- revisit -->
<p>And, sure, you might be asking, &#8220;What the hell does André 3000 have to do with <em>Scooby-Doo</em>?&#8221; But that&#8217;s only because a) you&#8217;ve yet to learn in fuller detail about his involvement in <a href="https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESfAHrOzAVcSreDDfDKjTSVXJLRkwzWYA6MrblMtINy7IiD0jHN6OxwmPw9s4czqPsrFfKpmBnYU-MW4c7n6WnRxc_D1YcDrPMAGdtEMg1A2pGdlpUUTJz0SX7zVBGE7Lts7zfGEMZmUzOI1ciQlEY1CWaHJnIo_Cr2xy3oIY">Netflix&#8217;s Greg Berlanti-helmed live-action revival of the franchise</a>, and b), you&#8217;ve totally forgotten about &#8220;Land Of A Million Drums,&#8221; the song he and Big Boi did, as OutKast, for the soundtrack of <a href="https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESZAHrOzAVqst3nMMT__WrV6X014VB4gKw-cU2xiTL3wuwfvUtp0G-Rk58iO7rrdZ3yHszFjhfKafJ2SXr10QuVm9y7QQ-YRv0u5Cxm5mEB3sEtmOXD4qzPJvmsRNF6OcN1xoGDVg">the 2002 <em>Scooby-Doo&nbsp;</em>movie</a>. (A song whose major cultural effect in 2026 is to make the listener go &#8220;Wait, is that Killer Mike rapping about Scooby-Doo back in 2002?&#8221;) (Yes.)&nbsp;</p><!-- inlinecontent_1 --> </p><!-- admarker --> 
<p><span class="mce_SELRES_start" style="display: inline-block; width: 0px; overflow: hidden; line-height: 0;">﻿</span></p>
<p>All of which is an admittedly long-winded way to note that Benjamin has just been formally cast in the new series, adding to a fairly eclectic acting resumé that&#8217;s seen him pop up in shows like&nbsp;<em>American Crime</em>, in Noah Baumbach&#8217;s&nbsp;<em><a href="https://www.avclub.com/film-review-white-noise-baumbach-driver-gerwig-1849816523">White Noise</a>&nbsp;</em>adaptation, and in his own Cartoon Network series from the 2000s,&nbsp;<em>Class Of 3000</em>. He&#8217;ll be joining the&nbsp;<em>Scooby&nbsp;</em>show alongside veteran character actress Cathy Moriarty, who doesn&#8217;t have any credits on the soundtracks of any&nbsp;<em>Scooby&nbsp;</em>projects that we know about, but who does have a long track record spanning dozens of TV shows and films (including Martin Scorsese&#8217;s <em>Raging Bull</em>, and a memorable turn as the baddie of the &#8217;90s <em>Casper </em>movie). The duo joins a previously announced (and stacked) cast for the live-action project, which includes Paul Walter Hauser, Rusty Schwimmer, Peter Macon, Jona Xiao, Dani Deetté, Elysée Sanvillé, Alex Isles, Avery Kristen Pohl, Pamela Mitchell, Ross Kimball, Sara Gilbert, Wynn Everett, Sauriyan Sapkota, and Bruce McGill, plus Mckenna Grace, Tanner Hagen, Abby Ryder Fortson, and Maxwell Jenkins as new versions of Daphne, Shaggy, Velma, and Freddy.</p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p>
<p><em>Scooby-Doo: Origins&nbsp;</em>is currently aimed at a 2027 release.</p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p>
