<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-new-casting"><em>New Casting</em></h3>



<p>The <em>Miami Vice</em> film reboot is picking up steam as more casting for the project gets announced. <a href="https://deadline.com/2026/08/danny-ramirez-michael-b-jordan-austin-butler-miami-vice-85-1237061066/">Deadline</a> is now reporting that Danny Ramirez, who can previously be seen in <em>Top Gun: Maverick</em>, <em>The Last of Us</em> and <em>Captain America: Brave New World</em>, has now been added to the growing cast of <em>Miami Vice &#8217;85</em>. The role Ramirez is set to play has yet to be announced, along with the rest of the details on the film. </p>



<p>This year, Ramirez is set to reprise his role as the new Falcon in <em>Avengers: Doomsday</em>.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-miami-vice-85"><em>Miami Vice ’85</em></h3>



<p>The big-screen adaptation of the&nbsp;<a href="https://www.joblo.com/miami-vice-changed-tv/">classic 1980s TV series</a>&nbsp;is set to star Austin Butler (<em>Caught Stealing</em>) and Michael B. Jordan (<em>The Thomas Crown Affair</em>) as James “Sonny” Crockett and Ricardo “Rico” Tubbs. Other recent additions to the cast include&nbsp;<a href="https://www.joblo.com/alden-ehrenreich-miami-vice-85/">Alden Ehrenreich</a>&nbsp;(<em>Weapons</em>) and&nbsp;<a href="https://www.joblo.com/miami-vice-85-whitney-peak/">Whitney Peak</a>&nbsp;(<em>Gossip Girl</em>), with Peak said to be playing Tubbs’ love interest. <a href="https://www.joblo.com/miami-vice-85-camila-morrone/">Camila Morrone</a> was recently announced and could be playing Crockett’s love interest, as she had chemistry tests with Butler before she was offered the role.</p>



<p>As you might tell from the&nbsp;<a href="https://www.joblo.com/miami-vice-movie-title-austin-butler-michael-b-jordan/">title</a>, the new&nbsp;<em>Miami Vice</em>&nbsp;will be a period piece, unlike Michael Mann’s 2006 movie. Production will kick off later this year, and Kosinski plans to shoot the film with IMAX cameras. The project has been scripted by Dan Gilroy (<em>Nightcrawler</em>).</p>



<p>Plot details are being kept under wraps, but it’s said that the movie “will explore the glamour and corruption of mid-80’s Miami, inspired by the pilot episode and first season of the NBC television series that influenced culture and set the style of everything from fashion to filmmaking.“</p>



<p>The film is set to be&nbsp;<a href="https://www.joblo.com/the-mummy-4-release-miami-vice-85/">released</a>&nbsp;on&nbsp;<strong>May 19, 2028</strong>.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-the-original-tv-series"><em>The Original TV Series</em></h3>



<p><em>Miami Vice</em>&nbsp;aired for five seasons on NBC from 1984 to 1989, totalling 111 episodes. It starred Don Johnson as Sonny Crockett and Philip Michael Thomas as Ricardo Tubbs, alongside Edward James Olmos as Lieutenant Castillo. The show followed undercover vice detectives in Miami as they investigated drug trafficking, organized crime, and other criminal activity during the height of the 1980s drug trade.</p>



<p>It was a major hit, with the show having a more cinematic feel than audiences were used to on television. Toss in the hottest pop music and fantastic ’80s fashion, and it’s easy to see why&nbsp;<em>Miami Vice</em>&nbsp;has become such an iconic series.</p>
