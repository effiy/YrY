<p><img alt="Jamie T live at Reading 2026. Credit: Andy Ford for NME" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1270" src="https://www.nme.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/NMEAR_NME-READING-2026_AndyFord_JamieT-627125.jpg" width="2000" /></p><p><a href="/ARTIST/JAMIE-T">Jamie T</a> kicked off <a href="https://www.nme.com/reading-leeds-festival-2025">Reading 2026</a> with a last minute indie celebration of a set on the new main stage, The Grid. Check out photos, footage and the setlist below.</p>
<p>After <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/reading-leeds-2026-jamie-t-asks-to-join-line-up-at-last-minute-3965063">teasingly begging the festival to let him play</a> before <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/reading-2026-jamie-t-confirmed-for-surprise-secret-33-minute-10-second-set-3965114">confirming his appearance last night</a>, the London indie icon delivered his opening &#8220;33 minute and 10 second&#8221; performance (in honour of <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/jamie-t-announces-new-album-ghosts-100-days-of-morning-with-anthemic-single-3310-3963626">his new single &#8216;3310</a>&#8216;) to get things going on the Friday (August 28).</p>
<p>&#8220;We got on the bill then,&#8221; he said with a smile, arriving on stage to introduce &#8216;Hippodrome&#8217; before rolling into &#8216;The Old Style Raiders&#8217; for the early arrivals. &#8220;Thank you very much,&#8221; he said after to the applause of the field. &#8220;How are you Reading Festival? We&#8217;re here to open the celebrations.&#8221;</p>

<p>&#8216;Salvador&#8217; and &#8216;3310&#8217; landed well, but the crowd didn&#8217;t appear to warm up until the indie classic &#8216;If You Got The Money&#8217; dropped. &#8220;This one goes out to all of you,&#8221; said Jamie in tribute. &#8220;Ready for a little dance?</p>
<p>Admitting that he &#8220;felt like Bono out here&#8221; on the gangway laid out for tonight&#8217;s headliners <a href="/artists/charli-xcx">Charli XCX</a> and <a href="/artists/fontaines-d-c">Fontaines D.C., </a>before he offered: &#8220;Have you had a good time ladies and gentlemen? You&#8217;re gonna have to shout louder than that&#8230;&#8221;</p>
<p>A boisterous outing &#8216;Sticks N Stones&#8217; got the best response of the set before a sing-along of &#8216;Zombie&#8217; closed things out.</p>
<p>The next &#8216;secret&#8217; surprise set will come from <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/royal-blood-reading-festival-2026-secret-set-interview-3964930">Royal Blood performing on The Gallery stage at 2pm</a>. Neither Jamie T or the Brighton rock duo will be going on to perform a secret set at the sister site in Leeds, where <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/watch-kasabian-play-first-ever-leeds-festival-thursday-headline-slot-setlist-3964993">Kasabian opened proceedings with the first ever Thursday night headline set</a>.</p>

<p>Jamie T&#8217;s Reading appearance comes on the heels of a <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/jamie-t-london-scala-gig-photos-footage-setlist-2026-uk-tour-tickets-3963883">secret sold-out performance at London’s Scala </a>last week – where he played at the venue for the first time in 20 years and previewed some currently unreleased songs from his new album.</p>
<p>‘Ghosts (100 Days of Morning)’ will arrive on October 9 and marks the highly anticipated follow-up to his fifth record ‘<a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/album/jamie-t-the-theory-of-whatever-review-3274200">The Theory of Whatever</a>’ which dropped in 2022, and the slot at Reading tomorrow comes ahead of a seven-date UK headline tour at the end of the year.</p>
