<div class="rssBody"><div><p>I provided the exclusive launch coverage for the premium <a href="https://thepointsguy.com/oc/221211836?name=Chase+Sapphire+Reserve%C2%AE&amp;issuer=Chase&amp;utm_campaign=the-points-guy&amp;utm_content=393559&amp;utm_medium=syndication&amp;utm_source=The+Points+Guy&amp;utm_term=221211836" target="_blank">Chase Sapphire Reserve®</a> (see <a href="/tc/221211836?pid=221211836&amp;name=Chase%20Sapphire%20Reserve%C2%AE&amp;data-testid=ratesFees" target="_blank">rates and fees</a>) in 2016, and now the card is celebrating a milestone anniversary — 10 years!</p><p>As I look back, it&#x27;s been an amazing journey over the last decade for me both personally and professionally as I&#x27;ve watched both my company and my family grow, and there&#x27;s been some pretty awesome memories made (usually with the help of my Sapphire Reserve and Chase Ultimate Rewards points).</p><h2 class="rssH2">Points Boost-maxxing</h2><p>It&#x27;s no secret that I&#x27;m a Points Boost maniac to the core — I think this is one of the best perks Chase has ever rolled out for cardmembers — and I&#x27;ve been able to take some pretty amazing trips because of it. Not only do I earn more points when booking through Chase Travel℠, but Points Boost gives me access to luxury hotels that are not traditional points hotels and usually at a 1.5 to 2 cent per point value.</p><figure class="rssFigure"><img alt="" src="https://runway-media-production.global.ssl.fastly.net/us/originals/2026/08/20260806_Chase-Sapphire-Always-On-Q3-26-3.jpg" /><figcaption>BRIAN KELLY/THE POINTS GUY</figcaption></figure><p>My Chase Sapphire Reserve also includes up to $500 in statement credits annually for The Edit hotels, making it a no-brainer to book through the Chase Travel portal. Plus, I can use the flexible $300 annual travel credit for any of my travel purchases, including purchases through the portal, so I can use it to help cover some amazing hotel stays.</p><h2 class="rssH2">Valuable transfer partners</h2><p>Chase Ultimate Rewards points have become a huge part of my lifestyle. Beyond just Points Boost, I love the option to transfer my points to <a href="https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/chase-ultimate-rewards-transfer-partners/?utm_campaign=the-points-guy&amp;utm_content=393559&amp;utm_medium=syndication&amp;utm_source=The+Points+Guy" target="_blank">over a dozen airline and hotel transfer partners</a> like Air France-KLM Flying Blue (especially when there&#x27;s a transfer bonus of 20% or more) so I can book lie-flat business-class seats to Europe.</p><figure class="rssFigure"><img alt="" src="https://runway-media-production.global.ssl.fastly.net/us/originals/2026/08/20260806_Chase-Sapphire-Always-On-Q3-26-1.jpg" /><figcaption>BRIAN KELLY/THE POINTS GUY</figcaption></figure><p>Hyatt is another transfer partner that I love, and with the Sapphire Reserve, cardmembers can transfer Chase points 1:1 to World of Hyatt. The <a href="https://thepointsguy.com/hotel/reviews/park-hyatt-sydney-hotel-points-redemptions/?utm_campaign=the-points-guy&amp;utm_content=393559&amp;utm_medium=syndication&amp;utm_source=The+Points+Guy" target="_blank">Park Hyatt Sydney</a> is one of my absolute favorite hotels in the world — you can&#x27;t beat the iconic Sydney Harbour views, and the service is phenomenal. Redemptions <a href="https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/hyatt-award-chart/?utm_campaign=the-points-guy&amp;utm_content=393559&amp;utm_medium=syndication&amp;utm_source=The+Points+Guy" target="_blank">start at 35,000 Hyatt points a night</a> for a hotel that costs $1,200 per night. This is the value that points can provide.</p><h2 class="rssH2">A total lifestyle card</h2><p>The Sapphire Reserve received a <a href="https://thepointsguy.com/news/chase-sapphire-reserve-refresh-2025/?utm_campaign=the-points-guy&amp;utm_content=393559&amp;utm_medium=syndication&amp;utm_source=The+Points+Guy" target="_blank">refresh before its tenth anniversary</a>. Now cardmembers can get over $2,700 in total annual value, which includes $1,500 in annual lifestyle value with statement credits for StubHub, Apple Music, Apple TV and DoorDash. To me, this is a no-brainer, as the benefits clearly offset the card&#x27;s annual fee.</p><p>My Sapphire Reserve has always been a card that just fits my lifestyle, and the <a href="https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase-sapphire-reserve-credits/?utm_campaign=the-points-guy&amp;utm_content=393559&amp;utm_medium=syndication&amp;utm_source=The+Points+Guy#overview-of-sapphire-reserve-credits" target="_blank">benefits make it easier for me to travel</a> for a living ... from the travel credits to the lounge access. I&#x27;m a hotel snob and love trying out new luxury properties to share with TPG readers.</p><p>Airport experiences are seamless now, thanks to the TSA PreCheck/Global Entry statement credit (up to $120 every four years) and Sapphire Lounge access.</p><figure class="rssFigure"><img alt="" src="https://runway-media-production.global.ssl.fastly.net/us/originals/2026/08/20260806_Chase-Sapphire-Always-On-Q3-26-4.jpg" /><figcaption>BRIAN KELLY/THE POINTS GUY</figcaption></figure><p>The Sapphire Lounges are fabulous when traveling — I almost exclusively fly out of Philadelphia International Airport (PHL) and John F. One of my favorite perks of being a Sapphire Reserve card member is being able to relax in a Sapphire Lounge whenever I&#x27;m flying that day, whether I&#x27;m traveling on United or another airline.</p><p>Eating out is also a given during my travels, and receiving up to $300 in dining credits each year for Sapphire Reserve Exclusive Tables provides opportunities to try new and exciting restaurants.</p><p>I&#x27;m not kidding when I say this is a card that pays for itself many times over.</p><h2 class="rssH2">Bottom line</h2><p>The <a href="https://thepointsguy.com/oc/221211836?name=Chase+Sapphire+Reserve%C2%AE&amp;issuer=Chase&amp;utm_campaign=the-points-guy&amp;utm_content=393559&amp;utm_medium=syndication&amp;utm_source=The+Points+Guy&amp;utm_term=221211836" target="_blank">Chase Sapphire Reserve®</a> has been my premium go-to card for a decade now, and recent additions to the Sapphire family have only enhanced my ability to earn points.
