<p class="p1">Hollywood is in mourning<a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/dolly-parton-dead-iconic-country-music-singer-was-80/"><span class="s1"> following the death</span></a> of country music icon and actress <strong><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/dolly-parton/"><span class="s1">Dolly Parton</span></a></strong>.</p>
<p class="p1">“A rhinestone life that shone bright enough for the world to see, Dolly will forever stand as an inspiration not only through her timeless music and prolific songwriting, but also her wit, warmth and kindness that made us all feel like family,” Parton’s rep said in a statement. “Dolly Parton’s legacy is one of love, compassion and resilience. … Her songs will continue to resonate with people of all ages, and her philanthropic work will have a lasting impact.”</p><div class="mb1-wrapper" id="starter">
						<div class="htlad-mb1" id="mb1-static"></div>
						<div class="htlad-leader2" id="leader2-inline-static"></div>
					</div>
<p class="p1">Although a cause of death was not revealed, Parton’s team said the <em>9 to 5</em> actress died peacefully in Nashville on Tuesday, August 25, at the age of 80.</p>
<p class="p1">A small, private service will be held for immediate family at a later date.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/pictures/dolly-parton-husband-carl-deans-relationship-timeline/" target="_blank">A Look Back at Dolly Parton and Husband Carl Dean’s Nearly 60-Year Romance</a></p>
<p class="p1">In lieu of flowers, the family is asking for donations to be made to <a href="http://www.imaginationlibrary.com/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><span class="s1">Dolly Parton’s Imagination Library</span></a>, a program that mails free, high-quality books to children from birth to age 5, no matter their family’s income.</p>
<p class="p1">Without hesitation, several actors and performers quickly paid tribute to Parton on social media. Keep reading to see heartfelt messages from <strong>Kacey Musgraves</strong>, <strong>Reba McEntire</strong> and more stars.</p>

<p>She continued, “She more than deserves her rest, and now it’s up to us to do our best follow in her brilliant, adorable, one-of-a-kind, bedazzled footsteps so that we still feel that magic in our world. Dolly, you have always been one of my angels. I will always love you xx.”</p>
