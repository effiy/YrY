<section class="article-page"><img src="https://assets-prd.ignimgs.com/2026/03/11/xbox-project-helix-gdc-blogroll-1-1773251677515.jpg" /><section></section><p>Xbox CEO Asha Sharma has suggested <a href="https://www.ign.com/tech/project-helix-next-generation-xbox"><u>Project Helix</u></a> will encompass more than one device, saying that Microsoft has been working on &quot;a great family of devices.&quot;</p><p>As Microsoft attempts to <a href="https://www.ign.com/articles/xbox-boss-asha-sharma-announces-3200-layoffs-including-1600-today-with-4-studios-leaving-for-new-management-read-the-email-to-staff-in-full">navigate mass layoffs across its gaming branch</a>, the company has mostly played its cards close to the chest when it comes to its mysterious next-gen Xbox console. Project Helix was formally <a href="https://www.ign.com/articles/xbox-confirms-project-helix-its-next-gen-console-that-will-also-play-pc-games"><u>announced</u></a> with the promise of PC game-playing capabilities back in March of this year, and now Sharma may be teasing something more.</p><p>The Xbox CEO teased some of what's to come for Project Helix during an interview with <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=txuYKwrbq1M">the <u>BBC</u></a> at gamescom 2026. While Microsoft wants to stay tight-lipped, some of what she had to say suggested the gaming hardware might be more than a simple box release.</p><section></section><p>&quot;We're not sharing more on Helix, I know you had to ask it,&quot; Sharma said when asked if Project Helix will be a &quot;discless&quot; console. &quot;But look, we've been hard at work on a great next generation and a great family of devices for Helix, and we'll share more soon.&quot;</p><p>With so little to go on, Sharma's comment could mean a lot of things. This past generation, for example, saw the release of not one but two Xbox consoles with its Series X/S releases. The X, considered a more powerful device, features a disc drive, while the S is an all-digital device.</p><p>The mention of a &quot;family of devices&quot; for Project Helix doesn't exactly mean that there will be something like a Project Helix Series X/S, but it does suggest Microsoft may be preparing for a next-gen console that could be just as untraditional as the one it follows.</p><section></section><p>Despite Sharma suggesting in the past that Project Helix may be <a href="https://www.ign.com/articles/new-xbox-memo-holiday-2027-project-helix-rumors"><u>targeting a holiday 2027 release</u></a>, we don't know exactly when the new gaming hardware will launch. In June, she <a href="https://www.ign.com/articles/xbox-boss-says-a-mass-audience-cant-afford-thousands-of-dollars-to-spend-on-a-next-gen-console-so-microsoft-must-look-at-new-business-models"><u>acknowledged</u></a> that a mass audience won't be willing to spend &quot;thousands of dollars&quot; on a &quot;console generation,&quot; teasing that Microsoft will explore &quot;new business models&quot; as it looks to the future.</p><p>While we wait to see what exactly Project Helix and its family of devices are, you can see <a href="https://www.ign.com/articles/xboxs-project-helix-must-clearly-communicate-its-strengths-in-order-to-succeed"><u>why we think the next-gen Xbox console needs to clearly communicate its strengths</u></a> and be <a href="https://www.ign.com/articles/i-hope-xbox-project-helix-is-still-a-gaming-pc-when-it-launches"><u>more than a simple Series sequel</u></a>. While it's <a href="https://www.ign.com/articles/xbox-ceo-asha-sharma-opens-up-on-future-of-physical-games-for-next-generation-console-project-helix"><u>unclear if it will allow fans to use physical game discs in a traditional sense</u></a>, what is clear is that <a href="https://www.ign.com/articles/xbox-is-letting-players-digitize-their-physical-games-starting-next-week-for-select-users"><u>Microsoft will soon allow Xbox fans to digitize their video games</u></a>.</p><section></section><p><em>Michael Cripe is a freelance writer with IGN.
