<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Summer will be over before you know it, and now is as good a time as any to prepare for new television. Whether school will consume your weekdays or you’re working daily anyway, broadcast and cable networks and streaming services have plenty of new and returning shows to offer a break from hard work and study.</p>

The broadcast networks have already announced their full schedules from September and on.
