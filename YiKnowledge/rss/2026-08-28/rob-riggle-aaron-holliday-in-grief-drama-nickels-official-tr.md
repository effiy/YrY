<em>"Things are happening that I don't understand..."</em> Briarcliff Entertainment has debuted the official trailer for a film titled <strong><em>Nickels</em></strong>, yet another new grief drama arriving to watch this fall. <em>Nickels</em> is directed by indie filmmaker Spencer King and is set for a US release in theaters <a href="https://www.firstshowing.net/schedule2026/#oct">this October</a>. This is the first we've heard or seen anything about this – no festival premieres either. It's a heavy emotional film about a man named Tom who lost his brother while he was riding a motorcycle 35 years ago. Tom Monroe, a successful entrepreneur, is haunted by his brother's death in a motorcycle accident. Years later, a neighbor's son ends up in a bike crash, leading Tom to suspect his brother's spirit has returned. This trailer hints that it's about grief but also about the possibility of reincarnation and coincidences that might be something <em>more</em>. Starring <strong>Rob Riggle</strong> giving an unusually dramatic, emotional performance as Tom, with <strong>Aaron Holliday</strong> as his neighbor's son, <strong>Bitsie Tulloch</strong>, <strong>June Schreiner</strong>, <strong>Tom Arnold</strong>, & <strong>Odessa A'zion</strong> as his daughter. It's a tad saccharine, but also looks endearing with them trying to make sense of all the feelings they are all feeling.
