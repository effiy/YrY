<div class="rssBody"><div><p>Southwest Airlines&#x27; Rapid Rewards points have gone through some devaluations in the last several years, but the <a href="https://thepointsguy.com/news/southwest-airlines-rapid-rewards-variable-redemptions/?utm_campaign=the-points-guy&amp;utm_content=397865&amp;utm_medium=syndication&amp;utm_source=The+Points+Guy" target="_blank">introduction of variability in the airline&#x27;s award pricing</a> has continued to offer some sweet spots for bargain hunters.</p><p>Today, Aug. 28, bargain hunters will undoubtedly find outsize value in <a href="https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/southwest-rapid-rewards/?utm_campaign=the-points-guy&amp;utm_content=397865&amp;utm_medium=syndication&amp;utm_source=The+Points+Guy" target="_blank">Southwest Rapid Rewards</a> points. That&#x27;s because the carrier just unveiled a limited-time sale exclusively on Rapid Rewards points bookings. Flights on a number of routes for more than six months of Southwest&#x27;s 2026 and 2027 calendar are available with this sale at some truly jaw-dropping discounts.</p><figure class="rssFigure"><img alt="Southwest Rapid Rewards flight sale August 2026" src="https://runway-media-production.global.ssl.fastly.net/us/originals/2026/08/Southwest-Rapid-Rewards-Points-Sale-Aug-2026.jpg" /><figcaption>SOUTHWEST AIRLINES</figcaption></figure><h2 class="rssH2">Rapid Rewards points worth 2, 3 or even 4 cents each</h2><p>In some initial searches with this sale, we found award flights available at prices that deliver double and triple the usual value.</p><p>Based on TPG&#x27;s August 2026 <a href="https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/monthly-valuations/?utm_campaign=the-points-guy&amp;utm_content=397865&amp;utm_medium=syndication&amp;utm_source=The+Points+Guy" target="_blank">valuations</a>, Southwest Rapid Rewards points are worth 1.25 cents per point. In my personal Southwest flight bookings for 2026, my redemptions fall near that mark. The best value I&#x27;ve been able to get so far this year was 1.63 cents per point, but I&#x27;m sometimes stuck booking at just 1.1 cents per point. Occasionally, I have spotted deals at 1.8 or 1.9 cents per point, but those are usually at highly undesirable flight times.</p><p>So, beating 2 cents per point in value is a great deal. Beating 3 cents per point is even better. How does 4 cents per point sound? We found it.</p><p>A Nov. 4 flight from Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport (PHX) to Las Vegas&#x27; Harry Reid International Airport (LAS) is currently priced at $136 for a Basic fare but can be booked with Rapid Rewards points for just 3,000 points. That&#x27;s a value of 4.3 cents per point.</p><figure class="rssGallery"><img alt="Southwest Screenshot PHX LAS Points Price" src="https://runway-media-production.global.ssl.fastly.net/us/originals/2026/08/Southwest-Screenshot-PHX-LAS-Points-Price.jpg" /><figcaption>SOUTHWEST AIRLINES</figcaption></figure><p>Even highly sought-after routes, like flights from the mainland to Hawaii, are on sale at rates that offer 2 to 3 cents per point in value. For example, we found a Nov. 8 flight from Sacramento International Airport (SMF) to Honolulu&#x27;s Daniel K. Inouye International Airport (HNL) priced at $279 available for just 10,000 points.</p><figure class="rssGallery"><img alt="Southwest Screenshot SMF HNL Cash Price" src="https://runway-media-production.global.ssl.fastly.net/us/originals/2026/08/Southwest-Screenshot-SMF-HNL-Points-Price.jpg" /><figcaption>SOUTHWEST AIRLINES</figcaption></figure><p>Holders of the <a href="https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/how-to-use-southwest-companion-pass/?utm_campaign=the-points-guy&amp;utm_content=397865&amp;utm_medium=syndication&amp;utm_source=The+Points+Guy" target="_blank">Southwest Companion Pass</a> can extract even more value from the sale, as booking two flights for the price of one could yield redemptions worth over 8 cents per point. At that price, it&#x27;s definitely worth <a href="https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/transfer-chase-ultimate-rewards-southwest/?utm_campaign=the-points-guy&amp;utm_content=397865&amp;utm_medium=syndication&amp;utm_source=The+Points+Guy" target="_blank">transferring Chase Ultimate Rewards points to Southwest</a> to book a couple of award flights.</p><h2 class="rssH2">Southwest deal details</h2><p>This points-only sale runs for a short four-day window and only applies to flights booked at least 21 days in advance. Eligible flights include those in fall, winter and early spring. (Southwest&#x27;s current calendar is open through June 2, 2027.) Note, though, that there are expected blackout dates around peak travel periods like Thanksgiving and Christmas.</p><p>The full deal restrictions and details are as follows:</p><div><ul><li><span>You must book by Aug. 31 at 11:59 p.m. PDT.</span></li><li><span>You must travel between Sept. 18, 2026, and March 3, 2027.</span></li><li><span>Blackout dates apply.</span><ul><li><span>For continental U.S. and interisland Hawaii flights, blackout dates are: Nov. 28 and 30, 2026; Dec. 23, 26, 28 and 30, 2026; Jan. 2, 3, 15 and 18, 2027; and Feb. 12 and 15, 2027.</span></li><li><span>For travel from the continental U.S. to Hawaii; St. Thomas; San Juan, Puerto Rico; and international destinations, blackout dates are: Nov. 19 to 25, 2026, and Dec. 17, 2026, to Jan. 5, 2027.</span></li><li><span>For travel from Hawaii, St. Thomas, San Juan and international destinations to the continental U.S., blackout dates are: Nov. 27 to Dec. 1, 2026, and Dec. 17, 2026, to Jan. 5, 2027.</span></li></ul></li></ul></div><p>The deal technically only applies to nonstop flights, but we&#x27;ve found some comparable deals on connecting itineraries. As a result, it&#x27;s worth pricing out any and all routes you&#x27;re considering.</p><p>For example, many October and November flights from Boise Airport (BOI) to Long Beach Airport (LGB) in California — with connections in PHX or LAS — that currently cost $76 are available for just 3,000 Rapid Rewards points, offering a value of over 2 cents per point.</p><p>Similarly, on Nov. 1, nonstop flights from LAS to HNL are available for $333. Adding one connection at Hilo International Airport (ITO) on Hawaii Island doesn&#x27;t save any money if you book with cash (that trip is priced at $338). However, if you book the connecting itinerary with Rapid Rewards points, you&#x27;ll redeem just 10,000 points, giving you a value of over 3 cents per point.</p><p><strong>Related: <a href="https://thepointsguy.com/airline/new-playbook-booking-flying-southwest-airlines/?utm_campaign=the-points-guy&amp;utm_content=397865&amp;utm_medium=syndication&amp;utm_source=The+Points+Guy" target="_blank">The new playbook for booking and flying with Southwest Airlines</a></strong></p><figure class="rssFigure"><img alt="Southwest plane wingtip over Hawaii" src="https://runway-media-production.global.ssl.fastly.net/us/originals/2026/08/Southwest-Plane-Over-Hawaii.jpg" /><figcaption>LESLIE HARVEY FOR THE POINTS GUY</figcaption></figure><h2 class="rssH2">How to search for deal availability</h2><p>Finding the sweet spots with this deal definitely takes a bit of effort, but the potential payoff makes it worth it.</p><p>If you are in the habit of searching for cash prices first, it&#x27;s very easy to miss the deals. During this sale, cash prices appear entirely usual and normal. In some cases, cash prices are even a bit higher than they were a few days ago during a different Southwest sale.</p><p>To ensure you see the discounts, switch over to the points search results. I found searching with the Low Fare Calendar particularly helpful, as using it lets you view a wide range of dates and easily toggle between the cash and points views.</p><figure class="rssFigure"><img alt="Southwest Screenshot PHX to LAS Low Fare Calendar" src="https://runway-media-production.global.ssl.fastly.net/us/originals/2026/08/Southwest-Screenshot-PHX-LAS-Low-Fare-Calendar-Points.jpg" /><figcaption>SOUTHWEST AIRLINES</figcaption></figure><p>The clock is ticking, so if this deal is of interest, act fast.
