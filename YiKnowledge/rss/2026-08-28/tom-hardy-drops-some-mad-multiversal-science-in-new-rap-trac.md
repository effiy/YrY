<img src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2026/08/28144412/hardy-header.jpg" /><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Rap supergroup Czarface (7L &amp; Esoteric and Wu-Tang Clan&#8217;s Inspectah Deck) has released a new album, </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Czarface</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Meets</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Frankie</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Pulitzer</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">, and they&#8217;ve brought along a few friends. A new track from this album, &#8220;Raw Forever,&#8221; is making the rounds on the internet today, and rightly so—it&#8217;s not bad, a thumping four-part flow with each guest star, Busta Rhymes, Method Man, and EL-O, setting the stage for their extra-special guest, Frankie Pulitzer, also known to some as the <a href="https://www.avclub.com/tom-hardy-tom-hardys-latest-weird-voice-co-star-in-cap-1842894162">vocally eclectic </a></span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Venom</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">The</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Dark</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Knight</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Rises</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> actor Tom Hardy. You can watch the new video below.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Certainly, there&#8217;s something to admire about how especially nerdy this collaboration is, featuring Messrs. Rhymes, Man, and Pulitzer charting a pop culture road map with scads of references both popular and, uh, not so much (it&#8217;s not every day you find your head nodding to a reference to Scott Cooper&#8217;s 2015 crime movie </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Black</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Mass</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">). With lines like &#8220;With the raps we go to bat like Jim Gordon,&#8221; we can almost hear the former Bane actor getting amped up before he even takes the mic.&nbsp;</span></p><!-- inlinecontent_1 --> </p>
<p><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">Among the many references in this righteous dork love-in: a passing nod to Martin Scorsese before Hardy evokes the name of Frank Pierce, Nic Cage&#8217;s character in the 1999 film&nbsp;<em>Bringing Out The Dead</em>; noting that he&#8217;s &#8220;something of a scientist&#8221; like Willem Dafoe (followed by the declaration that he is, in fact, &#8220;Goblin up crumbs&#8221;); a fleeting nod to&nbsp;<em>Dog Day Afternoon</em>&nbsp;is followed by a very-awesome nod to the DC Comics war hero Sgt. Rock; the bar &#8220;rip you out your costume&#8221; is accompanied by a Batman Halloween mask in the video; a very timely line that should only make you wince a little goes, &#8220;Doctor Doom, how I&#8217;m the &#8216;victor&#8217; of the scripture.&#8221; If that&#8217;s not enough, you can listen to another track,&nbsp;<a href="https://open.spotify.com/track/75LP4DCV9N0qBJsmrlucph" rel="noopener" target="_blank">&#8220;Knull &amp; Void,&#8221;</a> a reference to the villain behind&nbsp;<em>Venom: The Last Dance</em>&nbsp;and Marvel Comics&#8217;&nbsp;<em>King In Black</em> saga.</span></p><!-- admarker --> <!-- RevContent --> <div id="revcontent-hidden"> <!-- revisit -->
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Long-time Hardy heads will know the actor is serious about the rap game. Before he broke out in 2001 with roles in Ridley Scott&#8217;s </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Black Hawk Down</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and the HBO series </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Band Of Brothers</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">, Hardy recorded with childhood friend Edward Tracy as Tommy No 1 + Eddie Too Tall, cutting a mixtape in 1999 called </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Falling On Your Arse</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> that </span><a href="https://www.vice.com/en/article/tom-hardys-rap-mixtape-from-1999-is-actually-kind-of-fire/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">hit unsuspecting eardrums in 2018.</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Hardy&#8217;s not new to the Czarface ensemble, either; </span><a href="https://www.billboard.com/music/rb-hip-hop/tom-hardy-czarface-album-frankie-pulitzer-brothers-grimm-1236281485/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">he&#8217;s been recording with the group since 2021</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, and </span><a href="https://www.discogs.com/release/21073843-Czarface-Music-From-Venom-Let-There-Be-Carnage?srsltid=AfmBOooYSzTeeThVFLKU5rlzZnEVYeUum3qdv-qJdR16DbEjCPd-mqw5"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Czarface tracks featured on the soundtrack </span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">to Hardy&#8217;s 2021 </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Venom </span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">sequel, </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Let There Be Carnage</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">.&nbsp;</span></p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p>
