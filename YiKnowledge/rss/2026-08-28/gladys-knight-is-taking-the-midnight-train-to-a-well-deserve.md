<img src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2026/08/28125534/gladys-knight-feature.jpg" /><p>Though the American work ethic demands people work well into their 90s and die on the job, legendary soul singer Gladys Knight is taking a more European approach to her golden years. At 82 years young and after three-quarters of a century in the music business, the seven-time Grammy winner <a href="https://www.instagram.com/reels/DckLXpGRhnx/">announced on Instagram</a> that &#8220;after much soul searching&#8221; she&#8217;s going to be &#8220;spending more time on projects with [her] husband and visiting with [her] grandbabies.&#8221; That means she&#8217;ll be &#8220;easing into fewer performances&#8221; as she transitions &#8220;into the next stage of [her] career and life moving forward.&#8221;</p><!-- RevContent --> <div id="revcontent-hidden"> <!-- revisit -->
<p>&#8220;I&#8217;d like to take a moment, in advance, to thank all my fans over this last three quarters of a century for making a little girl&#8217;s dream come true and supporting her every step of the way,&#8221; she writes. &#8220;I am immensely grateful to all of you and send my eternal love back to you.&#8221;&nbsp;</p><!-- inlinecontent_1 --> </p><!-- admarker --> 
<p>Like many within the music industry, this wouldn&#8217;t be Knight&#8217;s first time announcing her retirement. She staged a farewell tour of the UK in 2009, only to return six times in the last decade. However, Knight&#8217;s concertgoers have noticed some concerning behavior on stage, including appearing dazed and confused. During a July performance at the Hollywood Bowl, Knight reportedly turned her back to the crowd and began walking off stage in the middle of &#8220;Midnight Train To Georgia,&#8221; leading to concerns about her mental acuity. <a href="https://www.tmz.com/2026/08/27/gladys-knight-touring-concerns/">Husband and manager William McDowell tells <i>TMZ</i></a> she has not been diagnosed with dementia. &#8220;No one is ever gonna make me say, &#8216;My wife is well,'&#8221; he says, &#8220;but all she wants to do is sing.&#8221;</p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p>
