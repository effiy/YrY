<p class="wp-block-paragraph">It took Cytokinetics <a href="https://www.statnews.com/2025/12/19/cytokinetics-heart-drug-myqorzo-fda-approval/">27 years</a> to win its first regulatory approval. The second one could happen much faster than that.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The biotech on Friday unveiled the full pivotal study data of its drug aficamten in an inherited heart condition called non-obstructive hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, showing that the medicine improved how patients felt and their exercise capacity. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">While some experts have described the benefits as limited, Cytokinetics plans to submit the drug to the Food and Drug Administration by the end of the year.&#xa0;If approved, it would be the first treatment specifically cleared for what&#x2019;s known as nHCM.&#xa0;</p><p><a href="https://www.statnews.com/2026/08/28/cytokinetics-myqorzo-aficamten-nhcm-acacia-trial-success/?utm_campaign=rss">Continue to STAT+ to read the full story&hellip;</a></p>
