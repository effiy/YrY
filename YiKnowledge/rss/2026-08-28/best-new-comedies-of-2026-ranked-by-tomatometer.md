<p class="p1 wp-block-paragraph">Yo momma is so funny… she could be on our list of the best and newest comedies of 2026! </p>



<p class="p1 wp-block-paragraph">This year we’re in for a riot, with new movies from Boots Riley (<em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/i_love_boosters">I Love Boosters</a></em>), David Wain (<a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/gail_daughtry_and_the_celebrity_sex_pass"><em>Gail Daughtry and the Celebrity Sex Pass</em></a>), and Olivia Wilde (<a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/the_invite"><em>The Invite</em></a>). We&#8217;re also getting the return of Ben Stiller&#8217;s awkward family of Fockers in <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/focker_in_law">Focker In-Law</a></em>, the Minions wrecking havoc Cthulhu-style with <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/minions_and_monsters"><em>Minions &amp; Monsters</em></a>, and Cindy running away from a very stoned Ghostface in <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/scary_movie_2026">Scary Movie</a></em>.</p>



<p class="p1 wp-block-paragraph">We rank them by Tomatometer, but all of them are sure to make you piss your pants (from laughter, not because of that other thing). — <a href="https://editorial.rottentomatoes.com/author/bryce-marrero/"><strong>Bryce Marrero</strong></a></p>
