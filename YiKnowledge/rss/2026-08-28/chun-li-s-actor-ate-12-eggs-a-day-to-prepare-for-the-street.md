<p><strong>"By the end of the shoot my thighs had grown".</strong></p><p>Chun-Li is one of the most iconic video game characters of all time, and this October, she'll return as a live-action character in the new <a href="https://www.nintendolife.com/games/nintendo-switch-2/street-fighter-6-years-1-2-fighters-edition">Street Fighter</a> movie. Canadian-born actor Callina Liang has been cast in this huge role, and in an interview with <a class="external" href="https://go.redirectingat.com/?id=92X1161492&amp;xcust=grd_au_7879537751524770367&amp;xs=1&amp;url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.greatmagazines.co.uk%2Fempire-magazine&amp;sref=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.gamesradar.com%2Fentertainment%2Flive-action-movies%2Fstreet-fighters-chun-li-actor-ate-12-eggs-a-day-grew-her-thighs-6-5cm-each-and-studied-every-game-in-slow-mo-to-play-the-iconic-high-kicking-fighter%2F">Empire magazine</a> recently, she's now revealed how she prepared.</p><p>Firstly, she "downloaded every single version of Street Fighter" (on PS5) and recorded Chun-Li's moves in slow-mo, so she could then play them back and study them. As "Chun-Li is all kicks", Liang trained in a bunch of martial arts, did some leg strengthening and made some drastic changes to her diet, eating 12 eggs a day.</p><p>Read the <a href="https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/08/chun-lis-actor-ate-12-eggs-a-day-to-prepare-for-the-street-fighter-movie">full article on nintendolife.com</a></p>
