<h3>&#8220;Please try to go to hell frequently because you will find the light there.&#8221;</h3>
<hr />
<p><a href="https://www.amazon.com/Kissing-Multiverse-Hannah-Emerson/dp/1571315497/?tag=braipick-20" target="_blank"><img alt="How to Be Reborn Each Day" class="cover with-border alignright size-medium" height="494" src="https://i0.wp.com/www.themarginalian.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/TheKissingOfKissing_HannahEmerson.jpg?fit=320%2C494&amp;ssl=1" width="320" /></a></p><p>In their strange cosmogony predating Copernicus by two millennia, the ancient Greek scientific sect of the Pythagoreans placed at the center of the universe a ball of fire. It was not hell but the heart of creation. Hell, Milton told us centuries and civilizations later, is something else, somewhere else: &#8220;The mind is its own place,&#8221; he <a href="https://www.themarginalian.org/2014/02/13/william-blake-paradise-lost/">wrote in <em>Paradise Lost</em></a>, &#8220;and in it self can make a Heav’n of Hell, a Hell of Heav’n.&#8221; </p>
<p>Grief and despair, heartache and humiliation, rage and regret &#8212; this is the hellfire of the mind, hot as a nova, all-consuming as a black hole. And yet, if are courageous enough and awake enough to walk through it, in it we are annealed, forged stronger, reborn. </p>
<p>That is what the non-speaking autistic poet <strong>Hannah Emerson</strong> celebrates in her shamanic poem &#8220;Center of the Universe,&#8221; found in her extraordinary collection <a href="https://www.amazon.com/Kissing-Multiverse-Hannah-Emerson/dp/1571315497/?tag=braipick-20" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong><em>The Kissing of Kissing</em></strong></a> (<a href="https://www.worldcat.org/title/1262119570" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><em>public library</em></a>), song of the mind electric, great bellowing yes to life.</p>
<p></p>
<blockquote><p><strong>CENTER OF THE UNIVERSE</strong><br />
<em>by Hannah Emerson</em></p>
<p>Please try to go<br />
to hell frequently<br />
because you will<br />
find the light there</p>
<p>yes yes &#8212; please<br />
try to kiss the ideas<br />
that you find there<br />
yes yes &#8212; please</p>
<p>try to get that<br />
it is the center<br />
of the universe<br />
yes yes &#8212; please</p>
<p>try to help yourself<br />
by kissing the hot hot<br />
hot life that is born<br />
there yes yes &#8212; please</p>
<p>try to yell in hell<br />
yes yes &#8212; please<br />
try to free yourself<br />
by pouring yourself</p>
<p>into the gutter all<br />
guttural guttural yell<br />
yes yes yes &#8212; please<br />
try to get that you</p>
<p>become the being<br />
that you came there<br />
to be yes yes &#8212; please<br />
try to go to the great</p>
<p>great great fire that you<br />
created because you<br />
become the light<br />
that the fire makes</p>
<p>inside of you<br />
yes yes &#8212; please<br />
try to kiss yourself<br />
for going there</p>
<p>yes yes &#8212; please<br />
get that you are<br />
reborn there<br />
yes yes &#8212; please</p>
<p>begin your day</p></blockquote>
<p>Drink in more soul-slaking poetry <a href="https://www.themarginalian.org/tag/poetry/">here</a>, then revisit the story of how Dostoyevsky, just after his death sentence was repealed, found himself <a href="https://www.themarginalian.org/2019/12/05/dostoyevsky-execution-life/">&#8220;regenerated into a new form&#8230; reborn for the better.&#8221;</a></p>
