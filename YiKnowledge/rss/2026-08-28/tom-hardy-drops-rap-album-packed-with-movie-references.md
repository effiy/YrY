<p>Tom Hardy records music under the alias Frankie Pulitzer, and the actor has just dropped a collaborative rap album that is absolutely packed with movie references. It includes lyrics about Martin Scorsese movies, Spider-Man, Doctor Doom, Stephen King, and even a Shakespearean monologue spoken in his Bane voice. It&#8217;s&#8230;something.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-czarface-meets-frankie-pulitzer"><em>Czarface Meets Frankie Pulitzer</em></h2>



<p>The album, titled <em>Czarface Meets Frankie Pulitzer</em>, finds Hardy joining forces with underground rap group <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCzIkDtJbnq8DM71uQZ0FN2A">Czarface</a>, which consists of duo 7L &amp; Esoteric and Wu-Tang Clan’s Inspectah Deck. Busta Rhymes, Method Man, and more also guest on the album.</p>



<p>In <em>Raw Forever</em>, Hardy packs in the pop-culture references</p>

<p>In <em><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AeH_TkPCOYg">Grim-Visaged War</a></em>, Hardy recites the famous monologue from Richard III, using a voice that sounds quite like the one he used for Bane in <em>The Dark Knight Rises</em>.</p>



<p>And in <em><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ex6qyu8e-o0">Mad Technology</a></em>, he references two Stephen King stories while also dropping a rather rude comment about Kathy Bates.</p>

<p>Tom! My goodness.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-mobland"><em>MoBLand</em></h2>



<p>Hardy will be returning as fixer Harry Da Souza in the <a href="https://www.joblo.com/mobland-season-2-new-trailer/">second season of <em>MoBLand</em></a>, which will debut on Paramount+ on <strong>September 18</strong>. A third season is in development, but for a moment, it looked as though Hardy wouldn&#8217;t return. It was initially reported that the actor left the series after butting heads creatively with showrunner Jez Butterworth, but thankfully, <a href="https://www.joblo.com/tom-hardy-returning-to-mobland-season-3/">matters were resolved</a>, and Hardy has returned.</p>
