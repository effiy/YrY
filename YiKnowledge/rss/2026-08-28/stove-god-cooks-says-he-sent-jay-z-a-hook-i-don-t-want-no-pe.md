<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	<a href="https://www.billboard.com/artist/stove-god-cooks">Stove God Cooks</a> insinuated that he may or may not be on this mythical album <a href="https://www.billboard.com/artist/jay-z/">JAY-Z</a> is supposedly making.</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	In a clip that&#8217;s been floating around rap social media circles, the Syracuse MC stopped by <em><a href="https://www.patreon.com/joebudden/posts/your-reign-is-167873720?utm_campaign=postshare_fan" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">The Joe Budden Podcast</a></em> recently and revealed that he sent Jigga a hook to use however and whenever he wants to.</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	&#8220;I sent Hov a hook. Don&#8217;t let me get on this album. I&#8217;m really gonna dance on you n&#8212;as. Now we gonna talk,&#8221; he proclaimed, before making sure he mentioned that he has no clue if a new album is coming or not. &#8220;I&#8217;m not confirming that big homie got an album coming out, but big homie, if you got an album coming out, use that hook I sent you. You know I was talking on that verse. Let&#8217;s do that.&#8221;</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	He added that he doesn&#8217;t want any money or percentage points for his contribution to whatever Jay has cooking; all he wants is a Roc-A-Fella chain. &#8220;I don&#8217;t want nothing. Keep everything,&#8221; he said. &#8220;Just send a Roc chain. I don&#8217;t want no percentage. Just send a Roc chain.&#8221;</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	Stove has been dealing with his own issues when it comes to albums. Fans have been clamoring for his sophomore project since he dropped the <a href="https://www.billboard.com/lists/roc-marciano-errol-holden-656-track-by-track-breakdown">Roc Marciano</a>-produced classing <em>Reasonable Drought</em> back in 2020 and had a show-stealing hook on <a href="https://www.billboard.com/music/rb-hip-hop/clipse-fico-stove-god-cooks-video-1236111303/">&#8220;F.I.C.O.&#8221;</a> off the Grammy-winning <a href="https://www.google.com/search?client=safari&#038;rls=en&#038;q=tracks+ranked+clipse&#038;ie=UTF-8&#038;oe=UTF-8" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">Clipse album</a>.</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	However, after many pump fakes and back-and-forths with his current label Babygrande, however, it looks like they&#8217;ve finally hashed things out and found common ground because he posted that they renegotiated a new deal on his Instagram Stories earlier this week. &#8220;I salute real sh&#8211;, always,&#8221; he wrote. &#8220;Respect to Chuck [Wilson] at Babygrande. He came and sat down face-to-face and we got the renegotiation done! Album on the way.&#8221;</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	As far as a new JAY-Z album is concerned, though? <a href="https://www.billboard.com/music/rb-hip-hop/jay-z-new-album-rumors-roc-nation-statement-1236312893/">Roc Nation deaded those rumors</a> earlier this month, calling the unsubstantiated claims &#8220;fake news&#8221; on the company&#8217;s IG Stories.</p>
