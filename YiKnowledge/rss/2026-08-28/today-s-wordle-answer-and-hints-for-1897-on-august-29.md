<p>It’s for good reason that the game’s popularity hasn’t dwindled: it&#8217;s free to play and boasts a simple concept for a fun and engaging puzzle game that doesn’t take too much time. To play, you&#8217;re given six tries to work out each day’s answer, with each try providing another hint until you solve (or guess) your way into getting the right answer.</p>
<p>Even if you&#8217;ve been playing daily since launch, <em>Wordle</em> stumps the best of us every so often. Some answers are easier than others, and some might even be words you might not have ever heard of (<i>cough, cough…</i> WOOER).</p>
<p>But we&#8217;re here to assure you that there&#8217;s no shame in needing a little hint every now and then. Don’t worry, we’ll try to give you clues without giving away too much of the answer.</p>

<p>We update this page each day to ensure it&#8217;s got the latest <em>Wordle</em> answer, but if you don&#8217;t want to make checking a habit, there are some handy tips to work out each day&#8217;s puzzle yourself. Try to open your guesses with a word that has plenty of vowels &#8211; <a href="https://jonathanolson.net/experiments/optimal-wordle-solutions">as mathematician Jonathan Olson identifies</a>, some great words to use first include &#8216;Salet&#8217;, &#8216;Rance&#8217;, &#8216;Alter&#8217;, and &#8216;Crate&#8217;.</p>

<h2>Similar games to Wordle</h2>
<p>If you find yourself struggling to land each answer every day, good news: there&#8217;s plenty of other similar games that can test your knowledge in other areas. The success of <em>Wordle</em> has meant that everyone has tried their hand at making similar games &#8211; even <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/gaming-news/weezers-rivers-cuomo-launches-wordle-spin-off-weezle-3175637">Weezer frontman Rivers Cuomo chipped in to launch <em>Weezle</em></a>, which predictably tasks players with working out a lyric from the band&#8217;s songs each day.</p>
<p>If <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/weezer">Weezer</a>&#8216;s not the band for you, there&#8217;s always <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/gaming-news/taylordle-puzzles-out-taylor-swift-lyrics-in-the-style-of-wordle-3149347">Taylordle &#8211; which is the same game, except with Taylor Swift lyrics</a>.</p>
<p>Beyond that, there&#8217;s also a <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/gaming-news/theres-now-a-battle-royale-version-of-wordle-3161986">battle royale</a>, a <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/gaming-news/dungleon-is-a-wordle-like-that-uses-fantasy-characters-instead-of-words-3159796">dungeon crawler</a>, and <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/gaming-news/worldle-is-a-wordle-spin-off-with-countries-instead-of-words-3154713">one that tests your knowledge of geography</a> &#8211; so there&#8217;s plenty to choose from at the moment. There&#8217;s even a <a href="https://www.nme.com/guides/gaming-guides/lewdle-answer-today-3190741">NSFW version for adults-only</a>!</p>
