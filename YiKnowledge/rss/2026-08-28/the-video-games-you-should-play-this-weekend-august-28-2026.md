Yesterday, Rockstar revealed the first official look at Grand Theft Auto VI, and <a href="https://gameinformer.com/2026/08/27/grand-theft-auto-vi-trailer-provides-lengthy-first-look-at-gameplay-including-story">it's shaping up to be as huge as we expected</a>. But most of all, it's the week of Gamescom: most of our editorial team is overseas in Germany running the official <em>Game Informer</em> booths, mingling with games industry folks from around the world, and taking lots of appointments to see games. <a href="https://gameinformer.com/preview/2026/08/27/coming-out-of-retirement">We saw The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt – Songs of the Past</a>, <a href="https://gameinformer.com/gamescom-2026/2026/08/26/control-resonant-continues-to-look-weird-and-strange">Control Resonant</a>, and more: you can <a href="https://gameinformer.com/gamescom-2026/2026/08/26/the-most-dope-games-weve-played-at-gamescom-2026">see the whole rundown of our most notable demos here</a>.</p><p>It's a lot, but luckily for us, there's an easy solution for the fatigue of a busy work week: video games. Here's what you should be playing this weekend.</p>

												<h2>
            <div class="field field--name-field-list-item-title field--type-string field--label-hidden gi5-field-list-item-title gi5-string field__item">Blood Dungeon</div>
      </h2>
																	
            <div class="clearfix text-formatted field field--name-field-copy field--type-text-long field--label-hidden gi5-field-copy gi5-text-long field__item"><p>The simplest way to explain Blood Dungeon is "Vampire Survivors with jumping," though that's a bit reductive. This roguelike bullet heaven adds platforming into the genre's signature power crawl, and has you hop your way through the chaotic titular dungeon. That said, it's definitely a bit of an acquired taste – the art style is intentionally crude, and our reviewer Kyle Hilliard didn't love that, for example, he couldn't get a fart cloud upgrade without hearing a barrage of flatulent sounds during his run. Still, for some of you, details like that are all you need to pull the trigger and pick the game up, so to each their own.</p><p>Here's a quote from our review:</p><p class="bq-long">Blood Dungeon is clearly born from Vampire Survivors’ undeniable loop and sense of progression, but developer Messhof addresses arguably the primary shortcoming of the 2022 Bullet Heaven game (I think that’s what we’ve agreed to call these, right?): movement. Moving in Blood Dungeon is fun and it sets the game apart within the genre and demands enjoyable skill from the player, but some balance annoyances keep the game from soaring.</p><p><a href="https://gameinformer.com/review/blood-dungeon/skilled-ugly-chaos">You can read reviewer Kyle Hilliard's full thoughts here.</a></p></div>

												<h2>
            <div class="field field--name-field-list-item-title field--type-string field--label-hidden gi5-field-list-item-title gi5-string field__item">Star Wars Zero Company</div>
      </h2>
																	
            <div class="clearfix text-formatted field field--name-field-copy field--type-text-long field--label-hidden gi5-field-copy gi5-text-long field__item"><p>"XCOM meets Star Wars" is such a solid premise that I knew I'd be sold on Zero Company from the moment it was announced, and now that I've started playing it, it's everything I hoped it would be. It's set during the Clone Wars (between <em>Attack of the Clones </em>and <em>Revenge of the Sith</em>) and follows a ragtag group of fighters as they track down a dangerous cult called The Coil. Our reviewer, guest contributor James Galizio, didn't love the story, but said the excellent tactics gameplay mostly makes up for the narrative faults. </p><p>Here's a quote from our review:</p><p class="bq-long">Developer Bit Reactor largely succeeds at what it sets out to do, and if you’re a fan of either Star Wars or XCOM it’s an easy recommendation, even if I wish the story had just a bit more juice. If you’re looking for a sci-fi tactics fix, Zero Company delivers the goods.</p><p><a href="https://gameinformer.com/review/star-wars-zero-company/more-than-a-clone">You can read reviewer James Galizio's full thoughts right here.</a></p></div>

												<h2>
            <div class="field field--name-field-list-item-title field--type-string field--label-hidden gi5-field-list-item-title gi5-string field__item">Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy</div>
      </h2>
																	
            <div class="clearfix text-formatted field field--name-field-copy field--type-text-long field--label-hidden gi5-field-copy gi5-text-long field__item"><p>This game is technically a spin-off of the other two Plague Tale games, and while fans of the series will find plenty of this setting familiar, Resonance is easily enjoyed as a standalone game. It's a prequel about a supporting character, so you don't need to go in with prior knowledge. Ultimately, we think it works very well as an action-adventure title in the vein of an Uncharted or Tomb Raider, and the protagonist Sophia is fun to fight as and makes for a great protagonist.</p><p>Here's a quote from our review:</p><p class="bq-long">Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy borrows from many games. There are puzzles with Tomb Raider flavors; like Nathan Drake, Sophia has never known the comfort of a stable platform; and the combat and ties to Greek mythology recall games like God of War. What’s impressive here is how well Asobo Studio executes those familiar genre staples and delivers a moving story starring a charismatic pirate on what may be the most impactful and tragic adventure of her life.</p><p><a href="https://gameinformer.com/review/resonance-a-plague-tale-legacy/shes-going-to-be-king-of-the-pirates">You can read reviewer Kyle Hilliard's full thoughts here</a>.</p></div>

												<h2>
            <div class="field field--name-field-list-item-title field--type-string field--label-hidden gi5-field-list-item-title gi5-string field__item">Elden Ring Tarnished Edition</div>
      </h2>
																	
            <div class="clearfix text-formatted field field--name-field-copy field--type-text-long field--label-hidden gi5-field-copy gi5-text-long field__item"><p>This week, Elden Ring comes to the Nintendo Switch 2, meaning you can take one of the greatest games of all time on the go. Dubbed the Tarnished Edition, this version includes the base game, some new cosmetics and armor, and the Shadow of the Erdtree DLC. That said, <a href="https://gameinformer.com/impressions/2026/08/26/elden-ring-on-switch-2-the-worst-way-to-play-the-best-game">this is probably the worst way to play Elden Ring</a>. The game is still amazing, but if you're used to the performance of a PS5, Xbox Series X/S, or PC, the performance on Switch 2 is a noticeable downgrade. It's far from unplayable, though, and if this is the first time you've owned a console that can play it, we can't recommend it highly enough. Any other platform would likely give you a better experience, but at the end of the day, it's still Elden Ring, <a href="https://gameinformer.com/review/elden-ring/absolutely-astonishing-adventure">a game we scored a 10/10</a>.</p></div>
