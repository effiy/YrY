<p><strong>The Lands Between has more to find.</strong></p><p><strong>Elden Ring's Tarnished Edition</strong> adds more content to an already-packed world - from new Armour Sets to Weapons, NPC Invasions, and Spectral Steed Attire options (new looks for Torrent, in other words).</p><p>It's all available either as part of the <a href="https://www.nintendolife.com/games/nintendo-switch-2/elden_ring_tarnished_edition">Elden Ring: Tarnished Edition</a> - which sees FromSoftware's modern masterpiece make its way to a Nintendo platform for the first time - or as a separate Tarnished Pack DLC for the PC, PS5 and Xbox versions. (If you're playing on one of these platforms with an existing save file, then as well as buying the DLC pack, you also need to ensure the game has been updated to version 1.17 or higher.)</p><p>Read the <a href="https://www.nintendolife.com/guides/elden-ring-tarnished-edition-weapons-armor-npc-invasions-steed-attire-content">full article on nintendolife.com</a></p>
