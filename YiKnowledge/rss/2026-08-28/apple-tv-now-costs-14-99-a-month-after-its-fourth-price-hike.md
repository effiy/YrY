<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Apple raised the price of its streaming service for new and current subscribers on Friday, bumping it up from $12.99 per month to $14.99, <a href="https://deadline.com/2026/08/apple-tv-raises-rates-monthly-annual-plans-1237061804/"><em>Deadline</em></a> and <a href="https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/apple-tv-price-increase-streaming-service-monthly-plan-1236845906/"><em>Variety</em></a> are reporting. An annual subscription now costs $119, up from $99. Apple also increased the cost of its individual Apple One subscription, which includes Apple TV and Apple's other subscription services, from $19.95 per month to $21.95. </p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">According to <em>Deadline</em> and <em>Variety</em>, the price change is effective immediately, and current subscribers will be notified of it before their next bill.  </p>
