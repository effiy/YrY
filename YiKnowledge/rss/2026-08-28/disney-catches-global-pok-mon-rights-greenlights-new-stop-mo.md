<img src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2026/08/28191251/pokemon-tales-feature.jpg" /><p>Never let it be said that Disney doesn&#8217;t know where the money is, at least when it comes to characters as financially exploitable as they are adorable: <a href="https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/new-pokemon-stop-motion-series-disney-plus-deal-1236846186/"><em>Variety&nbsp;</em>reports</a> today that the Mouse House has just signed a big distribution deal with Japanese brand The Pokémon Company, one that&#8217;ll grant the company global distribution rights for Pokémon shows—including that stop-motion Aardman Animation series we <a href="https://www.avclub.com/pokemon-aardman-series-teaser-pokemon-tales-the-misadventures-of-sirfetchd-pichu">basically haven&#8217;t stopped thinking about since it was announced back in July of 2025</a>.</p>
<p>News of the partnership rolled out at Pokémon XP, the new &#8220;Comic-Con but it&#8217;s only for lightning rats and <a href="https://kotaku.com/drifloon-steals-children-and-was-created-by-ghosts-1833158849">balloons that want to drag children off to hell</a>&#8221; event running in San Francisco this weekend. It also comes just a few weeks after the original&nbsp;<em>Pokémon&nbsp;</em>TV series (now billed as&nbsp;<em>Pokémon The Series: The Beginning</em>) began running on DisneyXD, and means that there&#8217;s now quite a bit of&nbsp;<em>Pokémon&nbsp;</em>content floating around over on, for instance, Disney+.</p><!-- inlinecontent_1 --> </p><!-- RevContent --> <div id="revcontent-hidden"> <!-- revisit --><!-- admarker --> 
<p>The big news, though, is that part of the deal means Disney has issued its own greenlight (and a fresh new teaser trailer) to <em>Pokémon Tales: The Misadventures Of Sirfetch’d &amp; Pichu</em>, the stop-motion animation show from the creators of&nbsp;<em>Wallace &amp; Gromit</em>. Announced last year, the series has a pretty silly plotline—one that kind of sounds like <em>Don Quixote&nbsp;</em>for third-graders—as the titular pocket monsters roam the Galar Region, trying to help their fellow &#8216;mons. But we can&#8217;t imagine we&#8217;ll care all that much about the narratives, when the chance to see The Pokémon Company&#8217;s legendary stable of creatures moving around in stop-motion like this is on the table. (Looking through the trailer, we&#8217;re especially charmed by all the effects work going on around various Pokémon&#8217;s moves; there&#8217;s something especially pleasantly silly about seeing Aardman animate Appletun&#8217;s signature and goopy blast of Apple Acid.)</p>
<p><em>The Misadventures Of Sirfetch&#8217;d &amp; Pichu&nbsp;</em>is currently aiming at a 2027 release.</p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p>
