Chronic kidney disease (CKD) is a major health burden affecting approximately one in 10 adults globally. Even with improved treatments, many patients continue to experience worsening kidney function, resulting in the need for dialysis or transplantation. Oxidative stress and mitochondrial dysfunction have long been suspected to contribute to CKD, but direct evidence linking these processes to disease development has been limited.
