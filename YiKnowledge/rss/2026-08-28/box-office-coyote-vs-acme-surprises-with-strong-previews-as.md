<p>One of the biggest question marks hovering over the box office this summer has been how Ketchup Entertainment’s <em>Coyote vs. Acme</em> would fare at the box office. The film was infamously dumped by Warner Bros. after they doubted its commercial viability, only to be picked up following fan outrage. Judging by <a href="https://deadline.com/2026/08/box-office-coyote-vs-acme-dog-stars-spider-man-1237061934/">Deadline’s early numbers</a>, WB might have made a big mistake, with the film having a stronger-than-expected $1.7 million preview night. Numbers like that suggest a high-teens opening, which is more <a href="https://www.joblo.com/the-dog-stars-wont-outswing-spider-man/">than we predicted</a>, with it possible the film could even hit second place at the box office this weekend, next to <em>Spider-Man: Brand New Day</em>.</p>



<p>If this happens, it will be quite the David vs. Goliath story, as WB dumped a movie that might actually outperform some of the movies they’ve put out this summer. If <em><a href="https://www.joblo.com/coyote-vs-acme-interviews-john-cena/">Coyote vs. Acme</a></em> had the kind of marketing muscle WB gave <em>Supergirl</em>, it may well have turned out to be a hit. That Ketchup has been able to get such a strong showing with likely a fraction of WB’s marketing spend is impressive.</p>



<p>Sadly, Ridley Scott’s latest, <em>The Dog Stars</em>, is looking like it will be D.O.A. at the box office, only earning a poor $850K. Disney is hoping for an opening in the mid-teens, but that seems impossible despite the combined star power of Scott and stars Jacob Elordi, Josh Brolin, and Margaret Qualley. With a budget north of $100 million, it looks like it will be one of the summer’s last big flops—which is too bad, <a href="https://www.joblo.com/the-dog-stars-review/">as it ain’t half bad</a>. It actually didn’t earn all that much more than Roadside Attractions’ <em>Buddy</em>, which made over $500K, with it looking at a weekend potentially in the $5 million range, which isn’t bad at all for such <a href="https://www.joblo.com/buddy-sundance-review/">a deeply strange (but interesting) film</a>.</p>
