<img src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2026/08/28193239/tale-of-two-cities.jpg" /><p>The winter of our despair continues <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pf4sOGLOV9Y">in the trailer</a> for the new version of Charles Dickens’ <i>A Tale Of Two Cities</i>. Typically the stuff of <i>Masterpiece Theater</i>, <a href="http://www.avclub.com/a-tale-of-two-cities-kit-harington-first-look-photos">this co-production with the BBC</a> will be slumming it on the newish streaming service MGM+, which is owned by Amazon but isn’t Prime Video. <i>Game Of Thrones</i>’ Kit Harrington stars as Sydney Carton, the unpredictable young barrister charged with defending Charles Darnay (François Civil), a messenger arrested for treason after bringing word to Lucie Manette (Mirren Mack) that her long-thought-dead father was still alive. Lucie hires Sydney in hopes that Darnay can lead them to dear dad but starts a love triangle that results in all that best of times, worst of times stuff. Frankly, this should come as no surprise to the esteemed readers of <i>The A.V. Club</i>, who no doubt stayed awake in A.P. English long enough to get the gist of Dickens’ revolutionary novel.&nbsp;</p><!-- RevContent --> <div id="revcontent-hidden"> <!-- revisit -->
