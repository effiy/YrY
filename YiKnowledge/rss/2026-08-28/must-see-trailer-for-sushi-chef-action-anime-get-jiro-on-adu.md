<em>"In the end, judgement comes for all of us."</em> Adult Swim has unveiled their first official trailer for a new late-night adult <a href="https://www.firstshowing.net/category/animation/">animated</a> series called <strong><em>Get Jiro</em></strong>, based on the graphic novels <a href="https://amzn.to/4qHwKiA" rel="noopener" target="_blank">by</a> Anthony Bourdain & Joel Rose. It's a full-on, hyper-violent action anime series in English featuring a stoic sushi chef master working in a brutal near-future Los Angeles, who is caught in the middle of some crazy restaurant war. His sushi joint is even located right in a classic LA strip mall ha. Of course this seems inspired by the <em>real</em> sushi maestro Jiro (seen in the doc <a href="https://www.justwatch.com/us/movie/jiro-dreams-of-sushi" rel="noopener nofollow " target="_blank"><em>Jiro Dreams of Sushi</em></a> also a genuine legend). In a future LA controlled by elite chefs, a sushi master named Jiro gets embroiled in a vicious culinary conflict, seeking vengeance through his cooking skills amidst the cutthroat restaurant scene. Starring the voice of <strong>Brian Tee</strong> as Jiro, with <strong>Titus Welliver</strong>, <strong>Alison Pill</strong>, and <strong>Justin Kirk</strong>. Maybe it's a bit cheesy but this footage is legit cool and looks like the perfect stoner late night anime to watch right when the fresh order of sushi arrives.
