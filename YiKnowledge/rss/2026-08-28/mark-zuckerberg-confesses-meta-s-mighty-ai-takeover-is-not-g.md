<img src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2026/08/28153454/feature-1.jpg" /><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">After a number of courts ruled against Facebook&#8217;s parent company Meta in recent weeks to the tune of </span><a href="https://www.avclub.com/meta-facebook-settlement"><span style="font-weight: 400;">some $18 billion</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> in cases related to addicting minors to its products and unfair business practices, the company sheepishly admitted to another major setback in its plans for world domination.&nbsp;</span></p><!-- RevContent --> <div id="revcontent-hidden"> <!-- revisit -->
<p><a href="https://www.reuters.com/investigations/mark-zuckerberg-had-bold-plan-replace-meta-staff-with-ai-heres-how-it-imploded-2026-08-26/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">According to a recent Reuters report</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg has admitted that his plan to unplug 60 percent of the company’s pesky human workforce in favor of AI has crashed and burned. After laying of approximately 10 percent of Meta’s workforce in May, the company was flooded with angry online rants from former employees. In addition, employees filed lawsuits alleging AI-aided discrimination in firing workers with disabilities, there were complaints about plans to install AI-training spyware on remaining employees’ computers, and other collateral damage that comes from forgetting you employ actual people with needs and feelings and such.&nbsp;</span></p><!-- inlinecontent_1 --> </p><!-- admarker --> 
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">In addition, Zuckerberg’s planned AI revolution has allowed malicious, AI-generated code to proliferate across Meta’s platforms, necessitating teams of those darned humans to try to fix things. In short, Zuckerberg’s grand “Organizational Transformation” plan (because all tech stuff has to have super-cool names) has fizzled, with no resulting increase in revenue, a PR nightmare and plenty of headaches. Not even Zuck-mandated team building and promises of </span><a href="https://futurism.com/artificial-intelligence/mark-zuckerberg-employees-fun-hackathon-layoffs"><span style="font-weight: 400;">better human snacks</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> could quell what the article terms Meta’s “employee rebellion.”&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Even as Zuckerberg admitted that his “AI-native playbook” was stalled in a morass of unmet expectations and understandable ire, Meta maintains that scrapping humanity from the workforce is still very much on the table. Even if, as a spokesperson confusingly explains of the aborted 60 percent roster cuts once so loftily extolled by Meta’s founder, “Ultimately, we didn’t move forward with every scenario from the exercise — and it was never assumed we would.”</span></p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p>
