<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Bilt is the platform known for providing rewards for housing payments. Earlier this year we <a href="https://onemileatatime.com/news/new-bilt-credit-card-rent-rewards-details/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">saw massive changes at Bilt</a>, including <a href="https://onemileatatime.com/guides/bilt-credit-cards/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">an overhaul of the credit card portfolio</a>, plus <a href="https://onemileatatime.com/guides/rewards-housing-payments-bilt-credit-card/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">new ways that housing rewards are offered</a>.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">There are many innovative things about Bilt, including that there’s a Bilt Rent Day promotion on the first day of each month. With a new month being right around the corner, we’ve just learned the details of the September 2026 Bilt Rent Day offer, and it includes a Virgin Atlantic Flying Club transfer bonus.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-details-of-the-september-2026-bilt-rent-day-promotion">Details of the September 2026 Bilt Rent Day promotion</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">There are a couple of aspects to the Bilt Rent Day promotion, including the ability to earn bonus points (which is the same every month), plus at least one additional promotion.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Note that Bilt Rent Day promotions are generally valid from 12:00AM ET until 11:59PM PT, so technically you have around 27 hours to take advantage of them.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-earn-double-bilt-points-on-rent-day-purchases">Earn double Bilt points on Rent Day purchases</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://onemileatatime.com/guides/bilt-credit-cards/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Bilt has a portfolio of credit cards</a>, including the no annual fee <a href="https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/bilt/bilt-blue-card/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Bilt Blue Card</a> (<a href="https://onemileatatime.com/go/cc-cardless-biltblue/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">learn more</a>), $95 annual fee <a href="https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/bilt/bilt-obsidian-card/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Bilt Obsidian Card</a> (<a href="https://onemileatatime.com/go/cc-cardless-biltobsidian/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">learn more</a>), and $495 annual fee <a href="https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/bilt-palladium-card/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Bilt Palladium Card</a> (<a href="https://onemileatatime.com/go/cc-cardless-biltpalladium/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">learn more</a>) (<a href="https://legal.cardless.com/rates_and_fees/bilt/rates_and_fees.pdf">Rates & Fees</a>). Personally, I think <a href="https://onemileatatime.com/insights/bilt-palladium-card-worth-it/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">the Bilt Palladium Card is by far the most lucrative</a>.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">On the first day of each month, you earn double those rewards on these cards, and earn up to 1,000 bonus points each month. It could be worth strategically making some purchases on the first day of each month to maximize this as much as possible.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">For example, with double points, the <a href="https://onemileatatime.com/go/cc-cardless-biltpalladium/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Bilt Palladium Card</a> offers 4x points on everyday spending on the first day of the month, so that’s pretty awesome (up to a cap of $500 worth of spending, since that would earn you 1,000 bonus points).</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-get-an-up-to-100-virgin-atlantic-flying-club-transfer-bonus">Get an up to 100% Virgin Atlantic Flying Club transfer bonus</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">On September 1, 2026 only, Bilt is offering a transfer bonus when you move points to Virgin Atlantic Flying Club (or Virgin Red, which allows 1:1 transfers to Flying Club). The transfer bonus you’re eligible for depends on your <a href="https://onemileatatime.com/guides/bilt-elite-status/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Bilt elite status</a>, as follows:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Bilt Blue members can receive a 25% transfer bonus</li>



<li>Bilt Silver members can receive a 35% transfer bonus</li>



<li>Bilt Gold members can receive a 45% transfer bonus</li>



<li>Bilt Platinum members can receive a 75% transfer bonus</li>



<li>Bilt Platinum members can redeem $200 in Bilt Cash to buy up to a 100% transfer bonus</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The maximum number of Bilt points that can be transferred with this offer while receiving the bonus is 150,000.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Virgin Atlantic belongs to SkyTeam, and Flying Club can be a useful program for redemptions on Virgin Atlantic, on SkyTeam partners, and even on some non-SkyTeam airlines. I do consider it to be a niche program, but with a transfer bonus, I think the value is quite good. For more details, see <a href="https://onemileatatime.com/guides/virgin-atlantic-flying-club-points/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">my post about the best uses of Flying Club points</a>.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I’m usually <a href="https://onemileatatime.com/insights/speculative-points-transfers/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">against speculative transfers</a>, but with a 100% bonus (thanks to <a href="https://onemileatatime.com/guides/bilt-cash/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">more Bilt Cash than I can practically redeem</a>), I am sort of tempted…</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-bottom-line">Bottom line</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">On the first day of each month, Bilt offers a Rent Day promotion. In addition to earning up to double points for purchases, for September 2026, there’s the opportunity to take advantage of an up to 100% transfer bonus to Virgin Atlantic Flying Club.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">While this offer won’t be for everyone, this is potentially a huge transfer bonus, so I think there could be value here, especially for premium cabin redemptions.</p>
