<p>In Episode 606 of The TouchArcade Show we kick things off talking about my plans for this weekend, which has resulted in this week&#8217;s show being a bit shorter than normal. We also go over some more updates on our Patreon situation, which has been trending upwards lately. Thank you for the support! We briefly touch on the latest version of iOS 18 before hopping into listener emails which leads into some interesting discussion, as always. Like, is the internet more dangerous now for kids than it was when I was growing up in the &#8217;90s? We weigh in! Finally Eli gives us a rundown of his Dungeons &#038; Dragons situation and the hardships between physical and digital ownership. </p>
