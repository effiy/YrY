<h1 class="wp-block-heading">All <em>Insidious</em> Movies Ranked by Tomatometer</h1>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Between&nbsp;<a href="https://editorial.rottentomatoes.com/guide/saw-movies-ranked/"><em>Saw</em></a>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<a href="https://editorial.rottentomatoes.com/guide/all-conjuring-universe-movies-ranked/"><em>The Conjuring</em></a>, James Wan directed&nbsp;<a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/insidious"><em>Insidious</em></a>, creating a new modern horror franchise with frequent collaborator Leigh Whannell. The first two Wan-directed&nbsp;<em>Insidious</em>&nbsp;films focus on the Lambert family (with Patrick Wilson and Rose Byrne as the parents) and their connection to demonic realm, the Further.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">As psychic Elise Rainier, Lin Shaye has been the lynchpin of the franchise, moving up the cast billing when Whannell made his directorial debut in prequel&nbsp;<em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/insidious_chapter_3_2015">Insidious: Chapter 3</a></em>, and then becoming the lead in&nbsp;<a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/insidious_the_last_key"><em>The Last Key</em></a>.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">And as the Lamberts thought they were done with the spooky spirit shenanigans after a decade of relative peace, Wilson made his debut as director with&nbsp;<em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/insidious_the_red_door">Insidious: The Red Door</a></em>. Now, with the help of Wan, Whannell, and Jason Blum as producers, Director Jacob Chase is presenting the next chapter: <em>Insidious: Out of the Further</em>. See where it ranks on the Tomatometer out of all six <em>Insidious</em>&nbsp;movies. </p>
