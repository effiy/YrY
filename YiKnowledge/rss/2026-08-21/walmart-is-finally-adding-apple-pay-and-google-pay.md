<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Walmart will soon allow you to pay for your items <a href="https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-pay/tap-to-pay-google-pay-walmart/">with Google Pay</a> or Apple Pay. In <a href="https://corporate.walmart.com/news/2026/08/21/more-ways-to-pay-tap-to-pay-is-coming-to-walmart-and-sams-club">an announcement on Friday</a>, Walmart says it's going to bring tap-to-pay capabilities to "select" Walmart and Sam's Club locations starting August 24th, before rolling out support to all US stores by the end of 2026 and gas stations by mid-2027.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This launch has been a long time coming, as Walmart was one of the last major retailers not to support Apple Pay or Google Pay. The retail giant even backed the launch of the <a href="https://www.theverge.com/2016/5/16/11686922/mcx-delays-currentc-lays-off-30-employees">short-lived CurrentC</a> mobile payment service before rolling out <a href="https://www.theverge.com/2016/7/3/12090048/walmart-pay-expansion">Walmart Pay in 2016</a>, which lets you quickly check out with a QR code at its stores. …</p>
