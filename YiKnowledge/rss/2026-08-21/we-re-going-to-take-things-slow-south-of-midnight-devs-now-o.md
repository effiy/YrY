We Happy Few and <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/south-of-midnight">South of Midnight</a> developers Compulsion Games have announced that they are fully broken up with Microsoft and Xbox, after completing a management buyout. They now once again own the rights to all their own original intellectual properties. Amongst other things, this means that you can buy South of Midnight &ndash; <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/south-of-midnight-review">summarised by Brendy</a> (RPS in peace) as "a beautiful folkloric tale" and a "plain and unambitious" action-adventure &ndash; while still taking part in <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/microsoft-confirm-theyve-supplied-ai-tech-to-the-israeli-military-for-use-in-gaza-following-bds-xbox-boycott">the BDS boycott</a> against Microsoft's Israeli military connections. When you buy the Steam version of South of Midnight, all your money (after Valve's revenue cut) will go to Compulsion.
