Among Shadows is the kind of videogame genre arithmetic that often proves less than the sum of its parts, but I'm cautiously enthused by its talk of disease, betrayal and <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/elden-ring-nightreign-tips-tricks">Elden Ring: Nightreign</a>-flavoured swordwizarding. The gist: this is a fantasy action-RPG with an <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/among-us">Among Us</a>-style social deduction element. You are a member of the Order of the Secret Fire, sent into a supernaturally blighted region to track down sources of corruption, while looting gear and levelling yourself up.
