<h1 class="wp-block-heading">50 Best New Animated Movies</h1>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If you&#8217;re someone who&#8217;s drawn to the possibilities of movies, where sitting down means tripping to worlds and galaxies beyond our own, or through memories of the perilous, personal, and beautiful journeys taken right here on our big blue marble, then check out our guide to the best new animated movies, recently released in theaters and at home!</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The list starts with Certified Fresh movies, including <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/coyote_vs_acme">Coyote vs. Acme</a></em>, a nostalgic blast from the past that critics are calling &#8220;funny and fiercely faithful to the spirit of these classic Looney Tunes characters who so many of us spent our childhood with.&#8221; <a href="https://editorial.rottentomatoes.com/guide/all-pixar-movies-ranked/">Pixar</a> came back in a big way this year with <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/toy_story_5">Toy Story 5</a></em> and <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/hoppers">Hoppers</a></em>, and Netflix has shown they&#8217;re taking their animation game to the next level with Academy Award-winning <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/kpop_demon_hunters">Kpop Demon Hunters</a></em>. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">You can also find Fresh movies, like Sony Pictures&#8217; and Crunchyroll&#8217;s <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/chainsaw_man_the_movie_reze_arc">Chainsaw Man &#8211; The Movie: Reze Arc</a></em>, and Rotten movies we love, from <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/sneaks">Sneaks</a></em> to <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/spellbound_2024">Spellbound</a></em>.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Enjoy our guide to the best of new animated movies, added as they come out! &#8220;<em>beep, beep</em>.&#8221;</p>
