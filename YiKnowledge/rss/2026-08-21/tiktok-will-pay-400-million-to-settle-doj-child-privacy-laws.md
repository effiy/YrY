<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The US Department of Justice announced on Friday that TikTok will pay $400 million to settle a lawsuit filed <a href="https://www.theverge.com/2024/8/2/24212095/us-vs-tiktok-sue-children-privacy-law">in 2024</a> over allegedly violating the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). In the lawsuit, the DOJ alleged that TikTok collected data from children without notifying parents or obtaining consent and did not delete the accounts when parents requested.</p>
