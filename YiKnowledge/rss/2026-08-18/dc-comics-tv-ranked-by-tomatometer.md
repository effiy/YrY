<h1 class="wp-block-heading">DC Comics TV Ranked by Tomatometer</h1>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>The Latest</strong>: In brightest day, in blackest night, the first season of <strong><em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/lanterns">Lanterns</a></em></strong> has captured our sights! Check out our list below to see where the new DC show ranks!</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Though DC Comics has enjoyed a television presence since 1953 with the <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/george_reeves">George Reeves</a>–starring <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/2155"><em>Adventures of Superman</em></a> — and made genuine cultural touchstones out of the 1966 <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/batman"><em>Batman</em></a> and the late 1970s <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/wonder_woman_1976_"><em>Wonder Woman</em></a> — it has never had so many of its titles translated on television at the same time before.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The HBO Max series <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/peacemaker_2022"><em>Peacemaker</em></a>, starring <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/john_cena">John Cena</a> as the titular anti-hero, was spun-off of <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/james_gunn">James Gunn</a>’s feature film <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/the_suicide_squad"><em>The Suicide Squad</em></a>, and its second season the latest to join the list. It joins fellow HBO Max titles <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/titans"><em>Titans</em></a>, the live-action adaptation of the comic of the same name starring <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/brenton_thwaites">Brenton Thwaites</a> as Dick Grayson (a.k.a. Robin); <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/doom_patrol"><em>Doom Patrol</em></a>, about a misfit group of superheroes under the tutelage of an eccentric old rich guy; and animated series <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/young_justice">Young Justice</a></em>, which debuted its first season in 2010 and launched its fourth in 2021. But not all DC Universe titles are exclusive to the HBO streaming service; the CW premiered live-action series <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/stargirl/s01"><em>Stargirl</em></a>, which shot straight to the top of DC&#8217;s TV offerings on its first reviews before settling in the top 15.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">And the company&#8217;s been extra busy in recent years, bolstering the collection with new DCU kick-off <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/creature_commandos"><em>Creature Commandos</em></a>, <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/the_penguin">The Penguin</a></em>, <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/my_adventures_with_superman">My Adventures with Superman</a></em>, <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/batman_caped_crusader"><em>Batman: Caped Crusader</em></a>, <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/dead_boy_detectives">Dead Boy Detectives</a></em> (RIP), <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/kite_man_hell_yeah">Kite Man: Hell Yeah</a></em>, <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/naomi">Naomi</a></em>, <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/dmz">DMZ</a></em>, <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/the_sandman">The Sandman</a></em>, and <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/gotham_knights">Gotham Knights</a></em>.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Want to know where each show stands in the DC-on-TV rankings? Check out our scorecard of DC Comics–based television by Tomatometer. Some titles — like <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/batman_the_animated_series"><em>Batman: The Animated Series</em></a>, 1990&#8217;s <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/swamp_thing_1990"><em>Swamp Thing</em></a>, <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/super_friends"><em>Super Friends</em></a>, and <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/teen_titans_go"><em>Teen Titans Go!</em></a> — don&#8217;t have a series-level Tomatometer score and, so, are not represented below.</p>
