<p>Welcome back to the Big Guessing Game! This year, Lifehacker, along with the other CNET Group sites (CNET, ZDNet, Mashable, and PCMag), has been giving readers a chance to win the latest Apple Watch by making predictions about Apple's big 2026 announcements. We are officially in the third and final round, so make sure to get your guesses in ahead of Apple's fall event! </p><div class="raw-embed">

<p>If you've played Lifehacker's or CNET Group's Big Guessing Game in the previous two rounds, you'll know the gist. But for any new guessers, here's a summary. The Big Guessing Game is made up of three rounds:</p><ul><li><p><a href="https://lifehacker.com/tech/big-guessing-game-apple-edition-round-1" target="_blank"><strong>Round 1</strong></a>, which ended on June 2. This round was focused on Apple's software announcements (iOS, iPadOS, macOS, watchOS, visionOS, etc.). <a href="https://lifehacker.com/tech/lifehackers-big-guessing-game-results-round-1" target="_blank">You can see the results of this round here</a>. </p></li><li><p><a href="https://lifehacker.com/tech/big-guessing-game-apple-edition-round-2" target="_blank"><strong>Round 2</strong></a>, which ended July 21. This round had questions about Apple hardware (iPhones, iPads, Macs, Apple Watches, Vision Pro, etc.). <a href="https://lifehacker.com/tech/heres-what-lifehacker-readers-think-apple-will-announce-in-the-fall" target="_blank">You can see the results of this round here</a>. </p></li><li><p><strong>Round 3</strong>, which started Aug. 18, runs through Sept. 1. This round focuses on Apple's fall event. (You're here now.)</p></li></ul><p>Each round features five questions about what Apple might announce during various keynotes and presentations. As such, we won't know the answers for sure until Apple confirms them&mdash;but once we do know, each correct prediction will earn you one entry into CNET Group's grand-prize drawing. This drawing will commence at the end of the third round, and, if you win, you'll walk away with the latest version of Apple's smartwatch. </p><p>To be eligible, you must be at least 18 years old and a resident of the 50 U.S. states or Washington, D.C. As alluded to above, you aren't limited to Lifehacker's guessing game, either: All CNET Group sites are hosting their own games, each with their own questions, which means there are 75 possible guesses across all three rounds. You can answer any or all of the questions each round, but note that you can only possibly win once, even if you play across multiple sites.
