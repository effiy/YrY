<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This year <strong><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/jessica_williams">Jessica Willams</a></strong> earned her third Emmy Nomination for <em><strong><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/shrinking">Shrinking</a></strong></em>, marking a momentous achievement for the actress in a category littered with talent and primetime prestige. It&#8217;s a moment that Williams does not take for granted, confessing she is giddy every time it happens. For this week&#8217;s episode of The Awards Tour Podcast, the comedian-turned-actress discusses her season 3 role as Gaby and how the character has changed over years.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img alt="" class="wp-image-295514" height="380" src="https://editorial.rottentomatoes.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/JJ.jpg" width="700" /></figure>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Williams also recounts Gaby’s evolution throughout the series, balancing laugh-out-loud comedy with emotionally charged moments, and the creative freedom she receives from the show’s writers and producers. She also explains how co-creator Bill Lawrence’s “best idea wins” philosophy fosters a supportive, collaborative set and helps bring out the strongest version of the show. She also reflected on her time as a correspondent on <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/the_daily_show">The Daily Show</a></em>, the support <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/jon_stewart">Jon Stewart</a> gave her as she developed her comedic voice, and the importance of having a safe space to experiment and grow.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Read on for a preview of the chat with Jessica Williams for <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/shrinking/s03"><em>Shrinking</em>: Season 3</a></strong>.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Jacqueline Coley for Rotten Tomatoes: You are Emmy nominated again, just continuing this incredible run with <em>Shrinking</em>. Does it feel like old hat at this point to keep getting recognized for this work, or do you still get a little giddy on Emmy morning?</strong></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Jessica Williams:</strong> I still get giddy. It does not feel like old hat. I&#8217;m so thankful every time it happens. Definitely not old hat. I&#8217;m stressed every time it happens. You know who&#8217;s <em>not</em> stressed   I think he&#8217;s really thrilled it happened — is Michael [Urie]. Because I always end up being the one who calls him and wakes him up to tell him he was nominated for an Emmy, and I go, &#8220;Why is it me? Calling to congratulate you?&#8221; So this year I called him just to say, &#8220;Congrats, I love you.&#8221; This was right after the nominations were announced, and he had a sleeping mask on. He was like, &#8220;Hello?&#8221; And I was like, &#8220;Hi, congratulations, you got nominated for an Emmy again.&#8221; And he goes, &#8220;Oh my god, I did?&#8221; I&#8217;m like, &#8220;<em>What&#8217;s happening</em>?&#8221; [<em>laughs</em>] But me? I&#8217;m stressed. He&#8217;s not. Different people struggle with it differently.</p>
