<img src="https://assetsio.gnwcdn.com/malborn-offspring-mortal-shell-2.jpg?width=690&amp;quality=85&amp;format=jpg&amp;auto=webp" /> <p>Formidable fantasy soulslike <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/mortal-shell-2">Mortal Shell 2</a> has been getting it in the shins from certain players over the decision to 'lock them out' of Steam achievements, when they equip the notorious Slayer Seal.</p>
<p>Trailers have <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/mortal-shell-2-will-let-you-lower-its-difficulty-by-making-executions-refill-your-health-or-up-the-challenge-by-turning-off-the-sun">characterised</a> the Slayer Seal as an in-game 'easy mode'. As Jeremy summarises in our own <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/mortal-shell-2-review-a-monumental-heavy-metal-sequel-with-the-spirit-of-a-90s-pc-game">Mortal Shell 2 review</a>, it gives "you a pulverising punch and your ranged weapons the ability to deal Break damage", which goes a fair way in an action-RPG this vicious. Not being able to earn achievements with the Seal equipped (or even after you unequip it) has sparked a few <a href="https://www.reddit.com/r/MortalShell/comments/1vuwshz/yes_we_know_slayer_seal_is_op_but/">Reddit threads</a> protesting that people are being punished for declining to punish themselves&hellip; while other people (hello!) wonder why anybody cares this much about Steam achievements at all.</p>
