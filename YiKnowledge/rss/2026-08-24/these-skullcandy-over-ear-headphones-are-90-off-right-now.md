<p>The ear cushions boast a breathable design that is also made to withstand sweat. (They're actually water resistant, too, so you can give them a good wipe down after an intense workout.) You might want to note that these headphones are noise-<em>isolating</em> rather than noise-canceling. <a href="https://www.cnet.com/tech/mobile/noise-canceling-vs-noise-isolating-headphones-whats-the-difference/" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="open in a new window">As CNET explains</a>, "The fundamental difference is that noise canceling is an active, electronic process. Noise isolating is, basically, passive&mdash;something to wedge in your ears to block out sound. They&rsquo;re basically earplugs, except they can pipe in your Spotify."</p><p>When fully charged, these headphones will give you up to 40 hours of battery life. If you need a quick boost of power, the 10-minute "rapid charge" feature will give you up to four hours of listening time. The Skullcandy Crusher headphones are compatible with lots of devices, including laptops, Smartphones, tablets, gaming consoles, smart watches, and more.
