<p>For gaming, both versions keep the same core appeal, so the decision comes down to how much speed you need. The F5&rsquo;s 500Hz refresh rate gives competitive shooters and racing games more headroom, while the W1&rsquo;s 240Hz panel is already fast enough to feel noticeably smoother than a standard 144Hz LCD. Both pair that speed with a 0.03ms response time and FreeSync Premium support, which helps keep motion clear and reduces tearing when frame rates fluctuate. Their 1440p resolution also strikes a practical balance, offering a sharper image than 1080p without demanding the same graphics power as 4K.</p><p>The adjustable stand is another useful touch, especially if the monitor will double as an everyday desktop display, since you can fine-tune the height, tilt, swivel, and pivot instead of working around a fixed position. Beyond ergonomics, the OLED panel is what gives both monitors an advantage besides refresh rate, especially in games where contrast does more for the image than raw speed&mdash;dark scenes stay properly black, while highlights stand out more clearly and colors have more depth, so horror games, space titles, and anything with a lot of nighttime environments tend to look noticeably better than they do on a typical IPS panel. Burn-in is still the main concern with OLED, but Acer covers that under a three-year warranty, which makes the risk easier to live with.</p><hr /><div class=" relative flex justify-center py-16 md:left-1/2 md:w-[780px] md:max-w-max md:-translate-x-1/2">
