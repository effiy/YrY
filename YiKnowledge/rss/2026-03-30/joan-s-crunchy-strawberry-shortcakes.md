While the original recipe was for a traditional layer cake, Weed decided to take a more biscuit-based approach (with delicious results).</p>
<p><strong>Ingredients:</strong></p>
<p>3 cups sifted flour</p>
<p>4-1/2 tsp baking powder </p>
<p>1tsp salt </p>
<p>1/3 cup sugar</p>
<p>3/4 cup shortening (butter)</p>
<p>2 eggs</p>
<p>2/3 cup milk (about)</p>
<p>2 tbsp melted butter</p>
<p>2 qts strawberries </p>
<p>1 cup heavy cream</p>
<p> </p>
<p><strong>Directions: </strong></p>
<p>Sift dry ingredients, then cut in shortening.</p>
<p>Add eggs and enough milk to moisten dry ingredients.</p>
<p>Drop 1/4 cups of dough on greased baking sheet 2” apart till dough is used up. </p>
<p>Bake at 425 for 20 mins or until nicely browned on top.</p>
<p>Clean, slice and sweeten berries while biscuits bake.</p>
<p>After cooling some, split or crumble biscuits.</p>
<p>Spoon berries over top. Top with whipped cream.</p>
<p> </p>
<p><strong>The biscuits freeze well and also make for a great treat on their own!</strong></p>
