<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Joe Liemandt is the principal of Alpha School and the founder of Trilogy Software and ESW Capital.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Liemandt dropped out of Stanford to build Trilogy,&nbsp;became the youngest member of the Forbes 400, then vanished from public life for 25&nbsp;years. But he didn’t stop building. Through ESW Capital, he quietly became one of the most prolific acquirers of software businesses in the world.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Now he’s back with a $1 billion bet that AI can help kids learn ten times faster, and that school as we know it is broken.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">At Alpha School, students spend two hours a day on AI-driven instruction and score in the top 1% on standardized tests. The rest of the day is devoted to what Liemandt calls life skills: leadership, entrepreneurship, teamwork, and real projects that kids actually care about. There are no lectures, and kids don’t move forward until they master the material.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">He argues the traditional classroom was designed for a narrow slice of students and wastes everyone else’s time. The fix isn’t more money or better teachers; it’s rebuilding from scratch around mastery, motivation, and AI. The role of a &#8216;teacher&#8217; dramatically changes.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This conversation covers everything from sleeping on the floor at Trilogy to being mentored by Jack Welch to the thinking behind Alpha School.</p>
