<p>Okay, now over to the ever-popular fighting game, <em>Marvel Contest of Champions</em>. New events for this game typically bring some new playable fighters with them, and at this point in the game&#8217;s life some of these cuts are getting very deep indeed. I don&#8217;t think we&#8217;ll ever see a Marvel fighting game again with a roster this varied. Like, Count Nefaria? Seriously? As an old-butt Marvel fan from a very long time ago, I like seeing these less common characters show up at all, let alone as playable characters. Over to the update notes to give you the full skinny on all of this:</p>
<p><img alt="" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-328755" height="900" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/marvelcocevent.jpg" width="506" /></p>
<blockquote>
<p dir="ltr">&#8220;NEW CHAMPIONS</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p dir="ltr">Count Nefaria</p>
<p dir="ltr">Count Luchino Nefaria was the descendant of a long line of Italian noblemen, and used his wealth and connections to become a powerful leader within the Maggia crime syndicate. He furthered his rise to power by subjecting himself to scientific experiments that granted him superhuman abilities, but cost him his life. He was later resurrected as a being made entirely of Ionic energy, rendering him effectively immortal as long as he drains other Ionic beings to maintain his power.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p dir="ltr">Shathra</p>
<p dir="ltr">Daughter of the Elder Goddesses Oshtur and Gaea, Shathra comes from the world that would come to be known as Loomworld. Shathra was tasked with creating the Celestial Map of Humanity, however, after being outdone by her younger sister Neith, she became enraged with jealousy and spite towards her sister and the Great Web she had designed. Consumed by her vengeance and envy, Shathra gave into her feral nature to tear down everything her sister created, one Spider at a time.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p dir="ltr">NEW QUESTS AND EVENTS</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p dir="ltr">Event Quest &#8211; Lupus In Fabula</p>
<p dir="ltr">There has been an undertaking to overthrow The Collector’s ship! The Summoner is being called in to evict these evil-doers! But as they make it deeper into the ship they also find themselves deeper in trouble as each villain seems to be making their own plans for how best to make the most of The Collector’s treasures. Will The Summoner be able to manage these miscreants? Or will they go down with the ship? Find out in LUPUS IN FABULA!</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p dir="ltr">Side Quest &#8211; Ludum Maximus</p>
<p dir="ltr">The Maestro has declared four months of celebratory games to honor his return. The festivities begin with the Circus Maximus, an onslaught of games and challenges hosted by Count Nefaria. The Count will not accept anything less than the best, the mightiest, the greatest of games. So dare to enter LUDUM MAXIMUS!</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p dir="ltr">Nefaria knows that a true battle is a mix of skill and luck, so 5x weekly Maps will open which present randomized Paths filled with fearsome foes!</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p dir="ltr">Act 9; Chapter 1</p>
<p dir="ltr">Glykhan has self-destructed but the sinister plotting of Ouroboros is far from over. However clues for where to head next seem few and far between. Luckily (depending on your definition of luck) Superior Kang has some secrets to share in the form of holo-tapes scattered around Battleworld. Mister Fantastic and Doctor Doom send The Summoner on an intel retrieval mission, but they aren’t the only ones looking for answers. Will the past come back to haunt The Battlerealm? Find out in Act 9 &#8211; Chapter 1: THE RECKONING</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p dir="ltr">Glorious Games</p>
<p dir="ltr">Introducing our third Saga: Glorious Games! To celebrate the history of the Contest and his triumphant return, The Maestro has declared four months of celebratory games. Each month of the Saga will revolve around a different element of games, starting with September&#8217;s Circus Maximus and culminating in the celebration of December&#8217;s Grand Banquet! Featuring a classical antiquity aesthetic, an exciting Champion chase, surprise Champion reworks and brand new kinds of Events and Quests, Glorious Games is sure to usher in our 10-year anniversary celebration in style!</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p dir="ltr">Realm Events</p>
<p dir="ltr">Get ready to work alongside every Summoner in the Battlerealm! Realm Events are a brand new type of Event in which points will be contributed on a global level. Milestone rewards can be claimed once global and individual point contribution thresholds have been reached. For those more competitively-inclined Summoners, ranked rewards will also be up for grabs, including an exclusive and unique player title."</p>
</blockquote>
