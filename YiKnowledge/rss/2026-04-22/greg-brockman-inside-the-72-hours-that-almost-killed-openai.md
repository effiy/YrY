<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The AI race, the future of AGI, and the inside story of OpenAI. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Greg Brockman is the co-founder and President of OpenAI, the company behind ChatGPT and GPT-5. He was the first engineer at Stripe before leaving in 2015 to help start OpenAI.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In this rare&nbsp;conversation, Greg goes inside the moments that built, and nearly broke, the most important AI company in the world.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Greg explains how the original Napa offsite produced the three-step technical plan OpenAI has followed for a decade and the real reason OpenAI had to abandon its pure nonprofit structure. He then walks through the 72 hours after Sam Altman was fired: where he was when he got the board call, why he quit the same day, how the &#8220;Phoenix&#8221; backup company was designed at Sam&#8217;s house the next morning, and the moment Ilya Sutskever&#8217;s tweet changed everything.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">From there, the conversation turns forward: whether we&#8217;re in a global AI race, how much of OpenAI&#8217;s own code is now written by AI (&#8220;it&#8217;s hard to know what percent is&nbsp;<em>not</em>&#8220;), why OpenAI stopped showing reasoning traces, what a compute-constrained world means for who gets access to AGI, and Greg&#8217;s answer to the question everyone is really asking: <em>What happens to your job?</em></p>
