<p>Nature, Published online: 26 August 2026; <a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10949-y">doi:10.1038/s41586-026-10949-y</a></p>A robotic, cell–free platform rapidly prototypes redesigned genetic codes, enabling the translation of proteins with reassigned codons and non-standard amino acids without altering living genomes.
