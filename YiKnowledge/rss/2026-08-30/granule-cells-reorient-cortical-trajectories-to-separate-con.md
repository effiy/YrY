<p>Nature, Published online: 26 August 2026; <a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10946-1">doi:10.1038/s41586-026-10946-1</a></p>Simultaneous imaging of premotor cortex and cerebellar granule cells in mice learning two skills in parallel shows that trajectories generalize in cortex but coherently reorient apart in granule cells, supporting generalization and separation between contexts.
