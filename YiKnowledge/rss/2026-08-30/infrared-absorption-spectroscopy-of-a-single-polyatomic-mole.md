<p>Nature, Published online: 26 August 2026; <a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10915-8">doi:10.1038/s41586-026-10915-8</a></p>Non-destructive single-molecule absorption spectroscopy is achieved by detecting photon recoil from a trapped molecular ion, enabling single-photon-sensitive measurements of molecular vibrational transitions.
