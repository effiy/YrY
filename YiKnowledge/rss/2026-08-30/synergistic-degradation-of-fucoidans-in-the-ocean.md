<p>Nature, Published online: 26 August 2026; <a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10980-z">doi:10.1038/s41586-026-10980-z</a></p>Efficient degradation of fucoidans depends on complementary bacterial guilds that cooperatively break backbone and side-chain sugars, revealing a conserved, globally relevant mechanism that shapes marine carbon cycling.
