When Mangenda Kamara became pregnant as a teenager in Sierra Leone, her community and secondary school teachers made her feel “very discouraged”, she recalls. But her father, a teacher himself, always told her “not to focus on them, but to focus on what I’m going to become in the future. He supported me to go to the hospital and encouraged me to go back to school after I gave birth”. With his support, Kamara completed secondary education after the birth of her daughter Philecia. Many personal and professional milestones followed.
