Anthony Fauci's US senate committee hearings were a chilling political circus. Fauci, the 85-year-old retired Director of the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases (NIAID; part of the US National Institutes of Health), is a renowned scientist and public servant with an unwavering commitment to saving lives. He has helped lead the USA through major health crises including the emergence of HIV/AIDS and, most notably, the COVID-19 pandemic. Republican politicians are pursuing a politicised obsession with COVID-19's origins, alleging that the NIAID, led by Fauci, funded what they characterise as dangerous gain-of-function research (in which the lethality and transmissibility of pathogens is enhanced) in Wuhan, China.
