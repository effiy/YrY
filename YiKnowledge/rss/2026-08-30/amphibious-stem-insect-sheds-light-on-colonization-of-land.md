<p>Nature, Published online: 26 August 2026; <a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10961-2">doi:10.1038/s41586-026-10961-2</a></p>Restudy of a challenging fossil using cross-polarized light imaging and phylogenetic reconstruction identifies Chosha praecursor, a probable early-diverging insect, helping to bridge the longstanding ‘hexapod gap’ in the fossil record.
