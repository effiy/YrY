<p>Nature, Published online: 26 August 2026; <a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10967-w">doi:10.1038/s41586-026-10967-w</a></p>A key role for retrograde neuromodulatory signalling in motivated behaviour is shown through dynamic endocannabinoid release within a thalamostriatal circuit that regulates behavioural engagement during reward seeking.
