<p class="wp-block-paragraph">You can read all the reviews you want about new hardware, but if it&#8217;s hardware you touch, there is one test that can only be performed in person, and it&#8217;s important: you hold it in your hand and you <em>feel</em> the answer to the question, <a href="https://daringfireball.net/linked/2026/03/06/jobs-500-computer">&#8220;Is this a piece of junk?&#8221;</a></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I was skeptical about the MacBook Neo until I walked to my local Apple Store and held the machine in my hand. <strong>This is not a piece of junk.</strong></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Similar to <a href="https://randsinrepose.com/guides/apple-charging/apple-charging-guide.html">The Apple Charging Guide</a>, I researched and built a <a href="https://randsinrepose.com/guides/macbook-neo/macbook-neo-guide.html">MacBook Neo Guide</a> to what&#8217;s inside, but, more importantly, what&#8217;s changed in Apple&#8217;s DNA to allow them to build the Neo. The punchline: Apple&#8217;s DNA didn&#8217;t change. Economics did &#8212; Apple Silicon made it possible to hit $599 without compromising the experience. The most expensive component was already paid for.</p>
