<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-why-you-ll-love-this-easy-simple-side-salad">why you&#8217;ll love this easy, simple side salad</h2>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Goes with everything.</strong> This is just one of those side salads that is so easy to whip up, packed with maximum flavor, and will go well with anything and everything (from chicken to pizzas to sandwiches to your favorite soups), balancing out protein-heavy mains with refreshing, crisp, veggie-loaded goodness.</li>



<li><strong>Recipe includes an all-purpose dressing.</strong> With just 5-ingredients, this simple all-purpose vinaigrette will be your go-to dressing for all your salads!</li>



<li><strong>Also works as an entree.</strong> Dress it up, dress it down! Add in your desired protein (ex. <a href="https://damndelicious.net/2015/06/22/easy-grilled-chicken/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">grilled chicken</a> or <a href="https://damndelicious.net/2024/04/19/baked-healthy-salmon/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">baked salmon</a>) to transform this into an easy main dish.</li>



<li><strong>Clean-out-the-fridge salad.</strong> This is a great time to add in any leftover vegetables and pantry staples such as avocado, olives, celery, bell peppers, banana peppers, fruits or anything else left in the fridge.</li>



<li><strong>The croutons are the best part. </strong>The homemade croutons are so crunchy, so garlicky and just so lovely. It&#8217;s truly the best part about this side salad &#8211; so good, it&#8217;ll outshine the main entree at dinnertime.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-key-ingredients-in-a-simple-side-salad">Key ingredients in a simple side salad</h2>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-croutons">Croutons</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Homemade garlic croutons can be made with ciabatta, sourdough, or baguette, baked until crisp and golden brown.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-all-purpose-vinaigrette">All-purpose vinaigrette</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This 5-minute dressing requires just a handful of pantry staples &#8211; extra virgin olive oil (EVOO), red wine vinegar, Dijon mustard, dried oregano, garlic powder, salt and pepper.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-greens">Greens</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Use leafy, hearty greens such as romaine or pre-packaged spring mix.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-tomatoes">Tomatoes</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Cherry and grape tomatoes are best for salads as they are smaller and bite-sized. Always use tomatoes that are firm to the touch to avoid a mushy, soggy salad.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-cucumbers">Cucumbers</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Persian cucumbers have an extra crunch, and are small, sweet and firm with minimal seeds (and don&#8217;t require peeling). English cucumbers can also be used.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-carrot">Carrot</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Use a box grater or food processor to shred or grate carrots.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-onion">Onion</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Raw red onions are ideal in salads for their crisp, mild flavors.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-nuts">Nuts</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Add a bit of crunch to your salad with almonds, pecans or walnuts. Toasted nuts will add even more robust flavors.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-parmesan-cheese">Parmesan cheese</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This recipe utilizes freshly grated Parmesan cheese, but feta, gorgonzola, pecorino, cheddar, mozzarella or goat cheese are all great alternatives.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-how-to-make-a-simple-side-salad">How to make a simple side salad</h2>



<ol class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Make the croutons.</strong> Using stale, sturdy, day-old leftover bread (such as ciabatta, baguette or sourdough), bake the bread cubes in olive oil-parsely-garlicky goodness until golden. Avoid overcrowding the <a href="https://amzn.to/3G4qThB" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">sheet pan</a> for a crispy finish.</li>



<li><strong>Make the dressing.</strong> Toss all the dressing ingredients together, storing in the fridge up to 1 week in advance. Always shake well before adding to the salad.</li>



<li><strong>Assemble the salad.</strong> Combine everything together, adding only half the dressing to start, and adding more, to taste, if needed.</li>



<li><strong>Serve.</strong> Serve immediately (preferably within 2 hours) with any main to elevate your dinner &#8211; <a href="https://damndelicious.net/2019/01/15/instant-pot-rotisserie-chicken/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">chicken</a>, <a href="https://damndelicious.net/2014/04/11/garlic-butter-shrimp/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">shrimp</a>, <a href="https://damndelicious.net/2016/06/23/perfect-steak-wtih-garlic-butter/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">steak</a>, <a href="https://damndelicious.net/2015/01/31/easy-pork-chops-with-sweet-and-sour-glaze/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">pork</a>, <a href="https://damndelicious.net/2014/03/29/spaghetti-carbonara/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">pasta</a> &#8211; all of the things.</li>
</ol>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-variations">Variations</h2>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-protein">Protein</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Turn a salad into a meal by adding protein such as <a href="https://damndelicious.net/2019/05/24/lemon-garlic-chicken-thighs/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">chicken</a>, <a href="https://damndelicious.net/2014/04/11/garlic-butter-shrimp/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">shrimp</a>, <a href="https://damndelicious.net/2016/06/23/perfect-steak-wtih-garlic-butter/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">steak</a>, <a href="https://damndelicious.net/2018/06/22/instant-pot-perfect-hard-boiled-eggs/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">eggs</a>, or tofu.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-grains">Grains</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Add grains (such as quinoa, farro, couscous, wheat berries or brown rice) for a heartier, more filling salad.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-fruit">Fruit</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Berries, citrus, stone fruits and apples can be added for sweetness and texture.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-candied-pecans">Candied pecans</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Swap out the almonds for <a href="https://damndelicious.net/2023/11/17/apple-cranberry-pecan-salad/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">candied pecans</a>.</p>

<div class="wprm-recipe-notes-container wprm-block-text-normal" id="recipe-46390-notes"><h3 class="wprm-recipe-header wprm-recipe-notes-header wprm-block-text-bold wprm-align-left wprm-header-decoration-none">Notes</h3><div class="wprm-recipe-notes"><ul>
<li><strong>Romaine lettuce is best.</strong> Romaine is a hearty, crisp, durable green, holding up very well to dressings. If romaine lettuce is not available, use iceberg lettuce or spring mix.</li>
<li><strong>Homemade croutons go a long way</strong>. Homemade croutons are so much easier to make than you think, allowing you to repurpose stale, leftover bread, and tasting so much better than store-bought croutons. They can also keep for at least 1 week when stored properly!</li>
<li><strong>Use a good quality sheet pan.</strong> A <a href="https://amzn.to/3G4qThB" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">durable sheet pan</a> is ideal when making the homemade croutons – it will last for years with minimal wear and tear, and will help achieve even cooking and avoid warping at high temperatures.</li>
<li><strong>Prep ahead of time.</strong> Save time by making the dressing up to 1 week in advance, and prepping and dicing the vegetables 1-2 days prior and storing them in airtight containers in the fridge.</li>
<li><strong>A bag mix is a speedy shortcut.</strong> Bagged salad mix is pre-washed, pre-chopped greens, conveniently cutting down prep time even further.</li>
<li><strong>Cut in uniform size.</strong> Cut all of the salad ingredients in similar size pieces for a consistent bite.</li>
<li><strong>Use extra virgin olive oil.</strong> <a href="https://amzn.to/3PGHRKw" rel="noopener" target="_blank">High quality extra virgin olive oil</a> is ideal for salad dressings for maximum flavor.</li>
<li><strong>Toss everything just before serving.</strong> Prevent soggy greens! Toss in the dressing just before serving, within 5-10 minutes, to maintain a crisp texture, seasoning lightly with salt and pepper, to taste.</li>
