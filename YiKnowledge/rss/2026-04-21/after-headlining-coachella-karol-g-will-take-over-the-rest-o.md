	<p><a href="https://uproxx.com/pop/karol-g-latina-foreva-video/" target="_blank">Karol G</a> has had a fantastic April so far. Most notably, she headlined both weekends of <a href="https://uproxx.com/music/coachella-2026-weekend-one-justin-bieber-wet-leg/" target="_blank">Coachella</a> (<a href="https://uproxx.com/music/coachella-2026-lineup-sabrina-carpenter-bieber/" target="_blank">alongside Sabrina Carpenter and Justin Bieber</a>). Now, she&#8217;s keeping the fun going on <em>Viajando Por El Mundo Tropitour</em>, a tour she just announced today (April 21).</p>
<p>The run will keep Karol busy for a whole year, from July 2026 to July 2027. The North American shows span from this July to November, while December on will see her visiting South America and Europe.</p>
<p>There&#8217;s a pre-sale starting on April 27, registration for which is open now until April 24 at 10 a.m. ET. The timing of the general on-sale varies by market and more information can be found on <a href="https://shopkarolg.com/pages/tour" target="_blank">Karol&#8217;s website</a>.</p>
<p>Find the full list of dates below.</p>
