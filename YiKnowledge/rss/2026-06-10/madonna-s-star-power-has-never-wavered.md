	<p>The list of artists who can maintain elite relevance for more than 40 years is extremely small. So when we talk about <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC81VD6eeuLLSfyY_D-N8sVw">Madonna</a>, there isn&#8217;t really anyone to compare her to. Her first hit was &#8220;Holiday&#8221; in 1983, and now in 2026, she&#8217;s still such an icon that she can balance <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/DXR91rODdfv/">a Coachella special guest slot with Sabrina Carpenter</a> on one hand and <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/DZVmmtYDxWX/?img_index=1">a Tribeca Film Festival short film premiere</a> on the other for <em>Confessions II – The Film</em>, <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yJtckcMHM2g">which is now available on YouTube</a> and has garnered more than a million views in its first day.</p>
<p>All this revolves around her first album in seven years, also titled <em>Confessions II</em>, due out on July 3rd and featuring guest spots from Latin superstar Feid and the aforementioned Carpenter. After that, she&#8217;ll headline the <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/DYNsUtojVLp/?img_index=1">World Cup</a> halftime show alongside global icons BTS and Shakira. There isn&#8217;t a spotlight too bright or a stage too big for Madonna, and her reach touches the realms of music, film, and sports.</p>
<p>Four decades in, there&#8217;s still no one like her.</p>
