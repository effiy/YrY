<div class="upx-component upx-embed-cnx"><div class="cnx-embed-holder">&amp;&amp;&amp;&amp;</div></div><p><a href="https://uproxx.com/pop/super-bowl-commercial-mountain-dew-seal-becky-g/" target="_blank">Mountain Dew</a> Baja Blast and UPROXX kicked off summer in Chicago this past weekend by bringing Latin trap superstar Dei V to Moonlight Studios and transforming the space into the ultimate beach getaway. Sure, when you hear the words &#8220;beach getaway,&#8221; you don&#8217;t exactly picture Chicago — but if anyone can turn the city into a coastal escape, it&#8217;s, well, us.</p>
<p>On June 26th, Mountain Dew Baja Blast and UPROXX officially rolled out the first stop of the Baja Blast Summer Series, a two-city free concert event celebrating the culture and music of two of the <a href="https://uproxx.com/music/latin-music-culture-drivers-you-need-to-know-more-about/" target="_blank">fastest-rising Latin artists</a> in the game right now. </p>
<figure class="wp-caption alignnone" id="attachment_404362169"><img alt="" class="size-medium wp-image-404362169" height="1056" src="https://uproxx.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/DSC05053.jpg?w=704" width="704" /><figcaption class="wp-caption-text"><span class="wp-media-credit">UPROXX</span></figcaption></figure>
<p>And Chicago showed up. Lines wrapped around the block before doors opened, where guests were treated to an exclusive shirt drop from cult-favorite streetwear label Half Evil. </p>
<p>Inside, a cocktail menu of tropical tequila, vodka, and rum-based drinks — each one infused with real Baja Blast (yes, really, and yes, it&#8217;s delicious) — kept the party going, while a tamale vendor made sure nobody was dancing on an empty stomach.</p>
<p>DJs Mamicana, M-Dok, and Mo Mami kept the energy locked in before Dei V hit the stage and delivered an electrifying set that had the crowd fully gone from song one.</p>
<p>The Summer Series is just getting started. Next up: Latin trap princess De La Rose takes over Los Angeles in late August. See you there! Until then, check out the recap from the weekend&#8217;s event above. </p>
