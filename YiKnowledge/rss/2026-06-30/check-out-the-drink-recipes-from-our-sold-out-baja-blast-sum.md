	<p>If you were in Chicago this weekend and didn&#8217;t make it to Moonlight Studios for the kickoff of the <a href="https://uproxx.com/?p=404362165&amp;preview=1&amp;_ppp=5fbd90507b" rel="noopener" target="_blank">UPROXX Baja Blast Summer Series</a>, we&#8217;re genuinely sorry — because you missed a great performance by Latin trap star Dei V, and some seriously great Baja Blast-infused cocktails.</p>
<p>The menu &#8212; made in partnership with <a href="https://uproxx.com/life/guachimonton-1800-tequila-release-travel-guide/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">1800 Tequila</a> and centered around Baja Blast (aka Mountain Dew&#8217;s gift to humankind) &#8212; featured four drinks built to complement the tropical beach-getaway theme of the night: the Mountain Dew Baja-Rita, the Baja Wave, and the Mango Baja Splash. If we had to pick a favorite, it&#8217;s a toss-up between the two tequila-based drinks — the bright, grassy notes of 1800 pair with <a href="https://uproxx.com/pop/super-bowl-commercial-mountain-dew-seal-becky-g/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Baja Blast</a> like a match made in cocktail heaven. They&#8217;re that delicious.</p>
<p>Today, we&#8217;re giving you the full recipes to recreate the Summer Series vibes from the comfort of your own home. Sure, you won&#8217;t have Dei V performing live, but throw on &#8220;VeLDÁ&#8221; or &#8220;Contigo Na Más&#8221; and you can pretend you were there. It won&#8217;t be quite the same, but it&#8217;ll still taste delicious.</p>
<p>Check out the recipes below, and be ready to come to the Baja Blast Summer Series in Los Angeles in mid-August with De La Rose headlining. If the cocktail menu is anything like Chicago&#8217;s, you&#8217;ll want to arrive thirsty.</p>
<h2>The Mountain Dew Baja-Rita</h2>
<figure class="wp-caption alignnone" id="attachment_404362196"><img alt="" class="size-medium wp-image-404362196" height="469" src="https://uproxx.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/DSC04584.jpg?w=704" width="704" /><figcaption class="wp-caption-text"><span class="wp-media-credit">UPROXX</span></figcaption></figure>
<p><strong>Ingredients:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Salt for the rim</li>
<li>Ice</li>
<li>1 oz 1800 Tequila</li>
<li>½ oz orange liqueur</li>
<li>1 oz fresh-squeezed lime juice</li>
<li>1 oz simple syrup</li>
<li>4 oz Mountain Dew Baja Blast</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Method:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Salt the rim of your glass, fill it with ice, and set it aside.</li>
<li>Combine the tequila, orange liqueur, lime juice, and simple syrup in a shaker with ice.</li>
<li>Give it a good shake then strain into your prepared glass.</li>
<li>Top with Baja Blast.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Drink Notes:</strong></p>
<p>The Baja-Rita is exactly what it sounds like: a classic margarita infused with Baja Blast for a fizzy twist. 1800 Tequila is the move here, it&#8217;s clean, agave-forward, and provides a nice kick to the otherwise sweet drink.</p>
<h2>Mango Baja Splash</h2>
<figure class="wp-caption alignnone" id="attachment_404362195"><img alt="" class="size-medium wp-image-404362195" height="469" src="https://uproxx.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/DSC04054.jpg?w=704" width="704" /><figcaption class="wp-caption-text"><span class="wp-media-credit">UPROXX</span></figcaption></figure>
<p><strong>Ingredients:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Chamoy and Tajín for the rim</li>
<li>Ice</li>
<li>1.5 oz 1800 Blanco Tequila</li>
<li>1 oz mango purée</li>
<li>¾ oz fresh lime juice</li>
<li>¼ oz agave</li>
<li>2.5 oz Mountain Dew Baja Blast</li>
<li>Mango spear and lime wheel for garnish</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Method:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Coat the rim of a highball glass with chamoy, then roll it in Tajín.</li>
<li>Fill with fresh ice.</li>
<li>Shake tequila, mango purée, lime juice, and agave with ice, until the shaker is cold to the touch,</li>
<li>Strain into your glass and top with Baja Blast.</li>
<li>Garnish with a mango spear and lime wheel.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Drink Notes:</strong></p>
<p>Also made with 1800 Blanco, what we love about this drink is the interplay between the grassy agave flavor and the sweetness of the mango. The chamoy-Tajín rim kicks against the mango and citrus, providing a depth of flavor that keeps the palate excited.</p>
<h2>Baja Wave</h2>
<figure class="wp-caption alignnone" id="attachment_404362197"><img alt="" class="size-medium wp-image-404362197" height="1056" src="https://uproxx.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/DSC04045.jpg?w=704" width="704" /><figcaption class="wp-caption-text"><span class="wp-media-credit">UPROXX</span></figcaption></figure>
<p><strong>Ingredients:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Ice</li>
<li>1.5 oz vodka</li>
<li>1 oz fresh pineapple juice</li>
<li>½ oz fresh lime juice</li>
<li>¼ oz mint syrup</li>
<li>2.5 oz Mountain Dew Baja Blast</li>
<li>Pineapple frond, mint sprig, and lime wheel for garnish</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Method:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Combine the vodka, pineapple juice, lime, and mint syrup in a shaker with ice.</li>
<li>Shake until cold to the touch</li>
<li>strain into a highball glass over fresh ice.</li>
<li>Top with Baja Blast and garnish.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Drink Notes:</strong></p>
<p>As we stated before, the tequila drinks were our personal favorites, but the vodka-backed Baja Wave definitely earns its place on the menu. The combination of pineapple juice, fresh mint syrup, and Baja Blast pairs harmoniously with the transparent flavor of a good vodka. It&#8217;s crisp, it&#8217;s tropical, and the mint adds a whole lot of depth.</p>
