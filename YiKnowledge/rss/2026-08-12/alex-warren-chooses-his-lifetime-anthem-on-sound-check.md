<div class="upx-component upx-embed-cnx"><div class="cnx-embed-holder">&amp;&amp;&amp;&amp;</div></div><p>There&#8217;s nothing ordinary about <a href="https://uproxx.com/tag/alex-warren/" target="_blank">Alex Warren</a>. His debut album, 2025&#8217;s <em>You&#8217;ll Be Alright, Kid</em>, featured <a href="https://uproxx.com/insights/2025-top-music-videos/" target="_blank">&#8220;Ordinary,&#8221;</a> one of the biggest songs of the decade with over two billion streams and counting. Now, Warren is back with a new album, <a href="https://uproxx.com/music/alex-warren-new-album-wildchild-passenger-music-video/" target="_blank"><em>Wildchild</em></a>, out August 28 via Atlantic Records. The singles, including &#8220;Fever Dream,&#8221; &#8220;Fine Place to Die&#8221;, and &#8220;Passenger,&#8221; are in our rotation — but what are some of Warren&#8217;s favorite songs?</p>
<p>Warren sat down with <em>Uproxx</em>&#8216;s Jeremy Hecht in the latest episode of <a href="https://uproxx.com/tag/sound-check-with-jeremy-hecht/" target="_blank"><em>Sound Check</em></a>, where music meets tough decisions. The &#8220;Rescuer&#8221; singer had to pick between two different-sounding songs with the ultimate goal of Hecht guessing his lifetime anthem. The choices weren&#8217;t easy: there&#8217;s Wiz Khalifa vs. Linkin Park, Ed Sheeran vs. Bruno Mars, Hall &#038; Oates vs. Coldplay, and even Alex Warren (&#8220;You&#8217;ll Be Alright, Kid&#8221;) vs. Alex Warren (&#8220;Save You A Seat&#8221;). Along the way, Warren discussed the iconic artists who inspire his songwriting, meeting Coldplay&#8217;s Chris Martin, and dealing with loss, while also reflecting on how his experiences have shaped his creative journey.</p>
<p>Will Jeremy be able to guess Alex Warren&#8217;s lifetime anthem? You’ll have to watch him take the <em>Sound Check</em> challenge in the video above to find out.</p>
