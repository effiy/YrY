<p>While most fans have been looking forward to Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 or basically a sequel to Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine for many years, I wasn&#8217;t really aware of the first game until I played Total War: Warhammer and was looking into other Warhammer 40,000 games. Since then, I&#8217;ve played many of them, with my favorites being Boltgun and Rogue Trader. I did end up playing some of Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine on Steam Deck many months ago to see how it felt. Having played many of the Warhammer 40,000 games on PC and even console more recently, I was excited to see how Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 felt after that amazing reveal it had. </p>
<p><img alt="" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-328596" height="2160" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Warhammer-40000-Space-Marine-2-steam-deck-review-oled-settings-pc-port-.jpg" width="3840" /></p>
<p>Over the last eight days, I&#8217;ve put in about 22 hours into Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 across my Steam Deck and PS5 making use of cross progression and also testing out the online. This Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 Steam Deck review is an in progress one for two reasons. The first is I cannot score a game like this without testing out cross platform multiplayer and also the online in general with public servers. The second is because Focus and Saber <a href="https://community.focus-entmt.com/focus-entertainment/space-marine-2/blogs/68-august-community-update">have confirmed</a> that they are working on official Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 Steam Deck support and aiming to have it out by the end of the year. </p>
<p>Having seen how amazing Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine looks and plays on Steam Deck, and because Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 has cross progression, I was very curious to see how it plays on Steam Deck if at all. There&#8217;s good news and bad news right now, and I&#8217;m going to cover all of that in this Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 Steam Deck review in progress with the gameplay, online co-op, visuals, PC port features, PS5 features, and more included. Note that the screenshots in the article with the performance overlay or fps displayed are from my Steam Deck OLED while the 16:9 screenshots are from my PS5 playthrough. My testing has also been done on Proton GE 9-9 and Proton Experimental (bleeding edge).</p>
<p><img alt="" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-328585" height="2160" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/warhammer-40000-space-marine-2-ps5-review-titus.jpg" width="3840" /></p>
<p>Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 is a third person action shooter that is equal parts brutal, gorgeous, and fun, and this also applies to newcomers to the world of Warhammer 40,000. After a brief but well thought out tutorial-like intro segment that introduces you to the basics of combat and movement, you arrive at your main hub, the Battle Barge. This is where you choose your missions, game mode, adjust cosmetics, and much more. </p>
<p>The moment to moment gameplay is superb with the controls and weapons all feeling perfect. I&#8217;m sure some will gravitate to using ranged more, but I adore the melee weapons and how visceral the combat feels up close. I never got tired of the executions and just plowing through tons of fodder enemies before the more powerful foes appear. The campaign is super fun solo and with a friend (or two) in co-op, but I hate any sort of defense missions. Thankfully I wasn&#8217;t too bothered by the implementation here. </p>
<p><img alt="" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-328565" height="800" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/warhammer-40000-space-marine-2-steam-deck-review-explosion.jpg" width="1280" /></p>
<p>When playing with a friend of mine who lives in another country, I kept thinking how Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 felt like a big budget take on a co-op shooter from the Xbox 360 era that we don&#8217;t really see much of these days. It managed to get its hooks in me just like Earth Defense Force or the recent Gundam Breaker 4 have done, and I really hope Saber and Focus can somehow work with SEGA to give the original game&#8217;s campaign a modern facelift.</p>
<p>Before going further, my knowledge of Warhammer 40,000 is through the Total War Warhammer, Dawn of War, Boltgun, and Rogue Trader more or less. With that said, Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 has been refreshing to play and is one of my favorite co-op experiences in years. It is too soon to say if this is my favorite Warhammer 40,000 game, but I just want to save this review draft and get back to playing right now. I&#8217;m addicted to playing with a friend in Operations mode while trying out the different classes and slowly progressing through the missions and unlocks. </p>
<p><img alt="" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-328588" height="2160" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/warhammer-40000-space-marine-2-ps5-review-swarm-2.jpg" width="3840" /></p>
<p>I don&#8217;t want to definitively say this since I&#8217;ve not played Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 once the full game launches with randoms, but what I&#8217;ve experienced in Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 co-op so far is superb. I cannot wait to properly try out the online this week once the game launches with cross progression and cross platform online.</p>
<p>Visually, I can only comment on how Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 is on PS5 and Steam Deck, and it is definitely a looker in the 4K mode on PS5 when played on my 1440p monitor. While it has always looked good in trailers, I&#8217;m floored at how good the environments look and also how much work has clearly gone into making everything feel alive not only with the massive number of enemies in the swarms, but also the texture work and lighting. This is all complemented by stunning direction for the main character voice acting and all the gear and customization options included. You can tell a lot of care went into making it so that you can show off your creativity with the customization options and more.</p>
<p><img alt="" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-328547" height="800" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/warhammer-40000-space-marine-2-steam-deck-review-photo-mode.jpg" width="1280" /></p>
<p>There&#8217;s also a photo mode you can access during singleplayer letting you adjust frames, expressions, characters visible, FOV, and much more. One thing to note is that if you do this on Steam Deck right now, some of the frames or effects don&#8217;t look too good when using FSR 2 and a lower resolution. On PS5 though, the Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 photo mode is amazing. </p>
<p>On the audio side, I didn&#8217;t know what to expect from the music. Ideally, Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 would have shipped with Bolt Thrower&#8217;s Realm of Chaos, but that likely wasn&#8217;t an option. I know bringing this up is like when I wished God of War (2018) had some Amon Amarth music. That tangent aside, the real star of Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2&#8217;s audio is the voice acting and general sound design. This is top tier stuff. The music is very good so far, but I&#8217;ve not heard anything (so far) that I would want to listen to a lot outside the game. It works perfectly in-game though.</p>
<p><img alt="" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-328553" height="800" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/warhammer-40000-space-marine-2-steam-deck-review-graphics-settings.jpg" width="1280" /></p>
<h3><strong>Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 PC graphics options</h3>
<p></strong></p>
<p>So how is the Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 PC port? I&#8217;ve only played it on Steam Deck of course, but I can still cover what features it includes. When you boot up Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2, it installs Epic Online Services, but there is no need to link your Epic account. I have one, but didn&#8217;t bother linking it. </p>
<p>As for the Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 PC graphics options, you can adjust display, display mode (windowed, borderless, and fullscreen), resolution (800&#215;600 and above), render resolution (native, dynamic to hit the fps target), quality, balanced, performance, and ultra performance), resolution upscaling type (TAA or FSR 2 on Steam Deck), dynamic resolution target, toggle v-sync, adjust brightness, motion blur intensity, fps limit (30, 60, 90, 120, unlimited), and then change many quality-related settings. </p>
<p><img alt="" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-328557" height="800" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/warhammer-40000-space-marine-2-steam-deck-review-parry.jpg" width="1280" /></p>
<p>The Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 visual quality settings include four presets that adjust the following: texture filtering, texture resolution, shadows, screen space ambient occlusion, screen space reflections, volumetrics, effects, details, and cloth simulation. </p>
<p>As mentioned in the blog post linked above, Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 on PC ships with DLSS and FSR 2 support. FSR 3 is planned for post-launch. I imagine the game will benefit from this on Steam Deck whenever it ships. I also hope the team adds full 16:10 support when the ultrawide update ships because the game is 16:9 only now. </p>
<p><img alt="" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-328552" height="800" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/warhammer-40000-space-marine-2-steam-deck-review-adaptive-triggers-dualsense-pc-ps5-steam.jpg" width="1280" /></p>
<h3><strong>Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 PC control options</h3>
<p></strong></p>
<p>On the control side, Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 has keyboard and mouse controls in addition to full controller support. Initially, I wasn&#8217;t able to get it to display PlayStation button prompts on Steam Deck by default, but turning off Steam Input fixed this. I noticed an adaptive trigger option under control settings, and this made me try disabling Steam Input. This menu also lets you remap keyboard and mouse bindings. Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 displays PlayStation button prompts when I use my DualSense controller over Bluetooth, and it even supports Adaptive Triggers wirelessly on PC. This isn&#8217;t too common so I thought it was worth highlighting. </p>
<p><img alt="" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-328548" height="800" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/warhammer-40000-space-marine-2-steam-deck-review-solo-4.jpg" width="1280" /></p>
<h3><strong>Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 Steam Deck performance</h3>
<p></strong></p>
<p>Before getting to the visuals and performance, I want to note that I had some freezing (only while booting up the game) on the default Proton or Experimental (bleeding edge), but I did not have any freezing when using Proton GE 9-9. Aside from that, Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 is playable (technically) on Steam Deck with no config change needed. The bad news is that it is a bit too much for Valve&#8217;s handheld right now. </p>
<p>When playing at 1280&#215;800 (it still is 16:9) and using the low preset with FSR 2.0 at Ultra Performance, it still cannot hold a locked 30fps. There are regular dips to the mid 20s in tense combat, and it can even go lower. Even when playing at a lower resolution, it drops below 30fps. For a game like this, that isn&#8217;t close to ideal. I hope it can eventually reach a stage where it can run at 30fps, but it isn&#8217;t possible right now as far as I can tell across the 10 hours I played on my Steam Deck OLED. </p>
<p><img alt="" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-328576" height="800" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/warhammer-40000-space-marine-2-steam-deck-review-opening.jpg" width="1280" /></p>
<p>When using the dynamic upscaling for a 30fps target and the low preset, it can hit the 30s, but it drops to the low 20s often. This mode does still look very good on the Deck&#8217;s own screen, but as you can tell, Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 is too much for Steam Deck right now. It also sometimes doesn&#8217;t exit out correctly requiring you to manually force close the game when you hit quit.  </p>
<h3><strong>Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 Steam Deck multiplayer impressions</h3>
<p></strong></p>
<p>With all that aside, I wanted to make sure Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 is playable online on Steam Deck. We sometimes have games where the developers add some anti cheat that blocks online play on Proton or Linux. Thankfully, I can report that Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 works perfectly online. I tested with a friend in Canada and we had a blast for a few hours playing in co-op. The only issue I had was some internet related disconnection, but since these are pre-release servers, I will be waiting to test out the game in a more public environment with randoms and friends once the game launches. </p>
<p><img alt="" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-328595" height="2160" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/warhammer-40000-space-marine-2-ps5-review-activity-cards.jpg" width="3840" /></p>
<h3><strong>Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 PS5 features – DualSense, Activity Cards, and Performance Mode</h3>
<p></strong></p>
<p>Since I also played Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 on PS5, I wanted to cover how it feels on a current console for those who play on Steam Deck and consoles like I do. I&#8217;ve only tried the performance mode so far, and it feels mostly great. Don&#8217;t expect a locked 60fps though, and there seems to be some dynamic resolution or upscaling happening because I noticed it got very blurry in two big fights in the operations mode with a friend. Aside from that, I have no qualms in recommending Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 on PS5 right now based on the time I&#8217;ve put into it, but I want to make sure the online works cross platform before giving it a definite recommendation.</p>
<p>The load times are fast and Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 also has PS5 Activity Card support for different modes and getting back into your save file saving time from the PS5 dashboard. As of this writing, there is no gyro support on PS5. If this changes, I will update this section, but sadly I didn&#8217;t see anything hinting at it in the build I&#8217;ve played pre-release.</p>

<h3><strong>Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 features I want to see in updates and patches</h3>
<p></strong></p>
<p>Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 is clearly going to get a ton of post-launch support through updates and DLC, and the main thing I want to see aside from improvements to Steam Deck performance is proper HDR support. Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 is gorgeous and the texture work, materials, and lighting would pop even more with HDR. Aside from that, I&#8217;m pleased with the DualSense implementation of triggers and vibration, but haptic feedback would be great. The blog post mentions haptics won&#8217;t be in &#8220;at launch" and I hope that means it is planned for later.</p>

<p>Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 is an easy game of the year contender so far. While I still need a bit of time to test the online when cross play is available from launch day, the gameplay is sublime and Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 looks and sounds superb across the board. Having played it on PS5 and Steam Deck with cross progression, I don&#8217;t recommend playing it on Valve&#8217;s handheld right now. It gets an easy recommendation on PS5 though. I will be updating this with a full score once I&#8217;ve gotten enough time with the multiplayer and some patches.</p>
