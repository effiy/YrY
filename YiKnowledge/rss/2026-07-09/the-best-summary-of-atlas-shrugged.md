<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A brief, no-fluff, summary of <em><a href="https://www.amazon.com/Ayn-Rand-Box-Set/dp/0451947673">Atlas Shrugged</a></em> by Ayn Rand.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If you want to learn how capitalist countries become socialist, this book will help you.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Let&#8217;s dive in.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Atlas Shrugged: The Simple Version</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The world depends on a relatively small number of people who build, invent, and produce. They are the entrepreneurs, engineers, scientists, industrialists, and creators who keep civilization progressing.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Over time, mostly through the media and government, society begins to treat ability as a public resource. Need becomes a claim on achievement. The more capable you are, the more you are expected to give to those who produce less. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Ability becomes a liability. Success is increasingly resented, taxed, regulated, and morally condemned. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Gradually, the producers begin to ask why they continue to support a system that constantly punishes them both in words and policy. As they withdraw, the system begins to fail. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The government, media, and crony class blame greed and capitalism, and use that to justify more control, more regulation, and more seizures of private property.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">But the crisis was not caused by the producers. It was caused by a society that believed it could consume achievement while attacking the people who make achievement possible.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Without the producers, the system that exploited them can no longer function, and society collapses.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Nobody notices who is holding the world up until the day they stop.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What Does the Title Mean?</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Atlas is the Titan from Greek myth, sentenced to hold the sky on his shoulders forever. He&#8217;s Rand&#8217;s stand-in for the producers: carrying the world while getting blamed for its weight.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">And &#8220;Shrugged&#8221; is the whole book in a single word. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Atlas doesn&#8217;t rage. He doesn&#8217;t beg. He just shrugs his shoulders and sets the sky down.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">And the world falls.</p>
