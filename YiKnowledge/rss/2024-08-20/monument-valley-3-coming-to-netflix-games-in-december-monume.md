<p>Gamescom is kicking off in Cologne, Germany as we speak, and during their Opening Night Live event they&#8217;ve already revealed some major news across all of gaming. One of the big announcements that pertains to us in the mobile gaming world is the official unveiling of <em>Monument Valley 3</em> from developer ustwo, which will be arriving as part of the Netflix Games library on December 10th. We&#8217;ve known that <em>Monument Valley 3</em> <a href="https://toucharcade.com/2019/07/31/monument-valley-3-announced-ustwo-game-director-job/">was in the works since at least July of 2019</a> so it&#8217;s very satisfying to see some real news about when we&#8217;ll be able to get our hands on this latest entry in the legendary puzzle game series. Here&#8217;s the announcement teaser.</p>

<p>As you can see, it&#8217;s not just <em>Monument Valley 3</em> that&#8217;s heading to Netflix Game, but the previous two entries will also be making their way to the service. The original <em>Monument Valley</em> will hit Netflix next month on September 19th with the sequel <em>Monument Valley 2</em> making its way to Netflix on October 29th. It&#8217;s been clear for a while now that Netflix Games is not messing around when it comes to bringing high profile titles to their mobile library, and <em>Monument Valley 3</em> is certainly another one to add to the list. The trailer also says to expect more news about the game during the Netflix Geeked Week which is set to take place from September 16-19th, so look for some more about <em>Monument Valley 3</em> then.</p>
