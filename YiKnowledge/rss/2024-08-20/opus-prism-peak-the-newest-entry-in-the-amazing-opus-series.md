If you&#8217;ve not checked it out yet, <em>OPUS: The Day We Found Earth</em> <a href="http://toucharcade.com/link/http://itunes.apple.com/us/app/id1024488164?mt=8">(Free)</a> is a great place to start but <em>OPUS: Echo of Starsong</em>, the newest release, is brilliant as well if you want to try a newer game. Read my review of the mobile version of that release <a href="https://toucharcade.com/2022/11/18/opus-echo-of-starsong-mobile-review-iphone-ipad-pro-controller/">here</a>. Following its reveal back in January last year, SIGONO&#8217;s latest project, <em>OPUS: Prism Peak</em>, is shaping up to be another excellent narrative adventure game. With Gamescom 2024 beginning today, SIGONO has shared new screenshots for <em>OPUS: Prism Peak</em> in addition to a gameplay trailer that I missed from a few weeks ago. <p><em>OPUS: Prism Peak</em> will feature multiple endings and it has you playing as a photographer using your camera to find your way back home from an ethereal realm. The music in <em>OPUS: Prism Peak</em> will be from Triodust and Audio Textural with a story from Brian Lee who wrote <em>OPUS: Echo of Starsong</em>. While <em>OPUS: Prism Peak</em> hasn&#8217;t been announced for mobile yet, my interview <a href="https://toucharcade.com/2022/11/28/opus-echo-of-starsong-physical-release-switch-porting-interview-apple-arcade-netflix-sigono-android-playstation-release/">with Scott Chen</a>, co-founder of SIGONO and executive producer of the <em>OPUS</em> series, mentioning <em>Echo of Starsong</em> on iOS being like returning home gives me hope to see this one on mobile as well at some point. You can <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/1947910/OPUS_Prism_Peak/">wishlist <em>OPUS: Prism Peak</em> here</a> as of now. It hasn&#8217;t been officially announced for more platforms yet.
