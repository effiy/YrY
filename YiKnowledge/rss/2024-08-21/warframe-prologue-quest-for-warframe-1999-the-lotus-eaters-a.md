<p><a class="app-icon" href="/games/warframe" style="float: left; margin-top: 4px; margin-right: 10px;"><img alt="" src="https://toucharcade.com/wp-content/images/app/1520001008/100x100bb.jpg" style="border-radius: 20px;" /></a> Digital Extremes has released the next big update for <em>Warframe</em> in the form of The Lotus Eaters update. This update brings in a prologue quest for Warframe 1999 which is due this Winter. Alongside that update, <em>Warframe</em> also adds in Sevagoth Prime access from today. If you&#8217;ve not played <em>Warframe</em> in a while, The Lotus Eaters is the game&#8217;s next story beat available a free narrative chapter. As for Sevagoth Prime access, this includes cosmetics, the Epitaph Prime weapon, and much more. You can collect the required blueprints to craft Sevagoth Prime for free or buy it from the <em>Warframe</em> Marketplace. Digital Extremes also confirms new Jade Shadows TennoGen with a deluxe Ares Skin for Warframe Styanax, the Rhodora Syadana backpiece, and more.
