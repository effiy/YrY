<h3><strong>Select New Releases</strong></h3>
<h3><strong>Mika and the Witch&#8217;s Mountain ($19.99)</strong></h3>
<p><img alt="" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-327974" height="562" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/mikaandthewitchsmountain.jpg" width="1000" /></p>
<p>If you&#8217;ve ever been watching Ghibli&#8217;s classic <em>Kiki&#8217;s Delivery Service</em> and felt like playing a video game version, you might be into what <em>Mika and the Witch&#8217;s Mountain</em> is offering. I mean, it isn&#8217;t <em>Kiki</em>, but it&#8217;s clearly inspired by it. You play as an apprentice witch who has to fly around on her broom to deliver all kinds of packages to the various residents of a mountain town. The game has a nice, colorful vibe and I&#8217;m certainly going to be investigating it further to see if it lives up to its initial potential.</p>
<h3><strong>My Lovely Empress ($19.99)</strong></h3>
<p><img alt="" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-327975" height="562" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/mylovelyempress.jpg" width="1000" /></p>
<p>A lovely wife, a lovely daughter, and now a lovely empress? Phew. In this game you play as an Emperor who reigns over a massive empire. Seems like you&#8217;ve got it all, but your wife passes away and suddenly none of it matters anymore. Still, you&#8217;ve got to keep things running. This is a simulation game of sorts, but one with a heavy narrative bent that will eventually lead to you having to make a critical choice. The previous games in the series have been fascinating despite their rough edges, and I expect this will deliver in the same way.</p>
<h3><strong>Cyber Mission ($9.99)</strong></h3>
<p><img alt="" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-327976" height="562" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/cybermission.jpg" width="1000" /></p>
<p>I&#8217;d call this a run-and-gun action game, but it&#8217;s probably better classified as a fly-and-gun action game. You play as a cyber trooper who is on a mission to destroy hostile alien forces. If you don&#8217;t feel like being a one-trooper army, you can grab a friend or family member for some local co-op multiplayer. Looks like a pretty good time for those enjoy things like <em>S.C.A.T.</em> or <em>Contra</em>, and reasonably priced to boot.</p>
<h3><strong>Mangavania 2 ($4.99)</strong></h3>
<p><img alt="" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-327977" height="562" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/mangavania2.jpg" width="1000" /></p>
<p>More <em>Mangavania</em> for those who want it. A gentle reminder that these games are not Metroidvanias, just linear stage-based platformers with very light exploratory elements. If that&#8217;s your thing, this does that. Not terribly well, mind you. But it does it. That&#8217;s all I&#8217;ve got to say about that.</p>
<h3><strong>White Eternal ($6.49)</strong></h3>
<p><img alt="" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-327978" height="562" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/whiteeternal.jpg" width="1000" /></p>
<p>After his friend goes missing during the harsh winter, Simon sets out from his mountain town to try to find out what happened to them. This is a turn-based RPG where you explore a land cursed to harsh winters (<em>Winnipeg</em>?), seeking out allies and battle monsters as you go. You can actually get in battles where you have as many as sixteen party members. Neat! The description promises over twenty hours of gameplay, which would be quite a lot of game for the price.</p>
