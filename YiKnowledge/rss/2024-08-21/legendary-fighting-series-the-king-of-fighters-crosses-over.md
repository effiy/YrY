<p>I did not have <em>Another Eden</em>, beloved mobile role-playing game, having a crossover event with <em>The King of Fighters</em>, legendary fighting game series from SNK, on my Bingo card, but here we are! Actually I shouldn&#8217;t be surprised, as <em>Another Eden</em> crossing over with popular IPs from all walks of the universe is nothing new, and they always do a fantastic job with it. This latest crossover, which is titled <em>Another Bout</em>, is no exception. Aldo gets a mysterious invite to a fighting tournament, and before he knows it him and his party are transported into the world of <em>The King of Fighters</em>, where they get to fight alongside some of the series&#8217; most recognizable and beloved characters: Terry Bogard, Kyo Kusanagi, Mai Shiranui, and Kula Diamond. Together you&#8217;ll play through a brand new branching story with the ability to unlock the <em>King of Fighters</em> characters for use throughout the rest of <em>Another Eden</em>.</p>

<p>The <em>Another Bout</em> event really leans into its fighting game crossover by providing a new battle arena where you can pick a team of 3 fighters and fight against another team of 3. Skills have been replaced with actual fighting game-style input combos, and you&#8217;ll need to learn to chain your moves together in order to unleash your Special Moves. At the end of it all you&#8217;ll face off against Rugal, longtime <em>King of Fighters</em> boss and tournament mastermind. The <em>Another Bout</em> crossover event in <em>Another Eden</em> officially kicks off this Thursday, August 22nd across PC, iOS, and Android, so be sure to check it out.</p>
