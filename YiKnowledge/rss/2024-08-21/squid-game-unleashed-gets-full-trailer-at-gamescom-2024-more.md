<p>Following its <a href="https://toucharcade.com/2024/08/16/squid-game-unleashed-multiplayer-game-download-coming-this-year-gamescom-reveal-iphone-android-netflix/">announcement recently</a>, Netflix&#8217;s upcoming multiplayer game based on Squid Game, <em>Squid Game: Unleashed</em>, has gotten its first reveal gameplay trailer during Gamescom Opening Night Live 2024. <em>Squid Game: Unleashed</em> is a 32 player showdown featuring challenges from the show in addition to new ones for the game. There will be daily missions and events inspired by the brand&#8217;s universe. <em>Squid Game: Unleashed</em> is in development at Boss Fight (a Netflix Game Studio) and Season 2 of the show arrives this December.
