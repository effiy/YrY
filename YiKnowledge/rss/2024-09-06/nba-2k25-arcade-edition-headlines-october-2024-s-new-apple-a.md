Following <a href="https://toucharcade.com/2024/09/05/balatro-mobile-release-date-price-download-apple-arcade/">yesterday&#8217;s news</a> about <em>Balatro</em>, Apple confirmed that <em>NBA 2K25 Arcade Edition</em> will release on October 3rd with three App Store Greats. <em>NBA 2K25 Arcade Edition</em> is notable because it marks the debut of The Neighborhood on iOS letting you explore the immersive world, outdoor streetball courts, NBA legends recruiting, a new badge system, and more with limited-time quests planned. Check out the <em>NBA 2K25 Arcade Edition</em> gameplay screenshot below:</p>

<p>Alongside <em>NBA 2K25 Arcade Edition</em>, we have <em>Smash Hit+</em>, <em>Furistas Cat Cafe+</em>, and <em>Food Truck Pup+</em> joining Apple Arcade as App Store Greats. I&#8217;ve only played the first of those before so I will be checking out the others assuming I can stop playing <em>Balatro+</em>. If you&#8217;d like to sign up to get the new games, check out <em>NBA 2K25 Arcade Edition</em> <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nba-2k25-arcade-edition/id6504000920">here</a> on Apple Arcade.
