<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Chung Ju-yung built Hyundai because he refused to be stopped.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">He is known for turning Hyundai into an industrial force that helped transform South Korea. The company built highways, ships, cars, and entire industries. At its peak, Hyundai accounted for 16% of South Korea’s economic output.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This episode explores how Chung built Hyundai, how he helped power South Korea’s rise, and how hunger, guilt, discipline, and relentless persistence shaped a man who refused to stop when the path disappeared.</p>
