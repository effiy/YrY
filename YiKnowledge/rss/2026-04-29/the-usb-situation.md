<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I write this from the latest MacBook Pro. I&#8217;ve been running this as my primary machine for almost a month. When I&#8217;m not grinding out <a href="https://amzn.to/4twZhI8">the latest soon-to-be-released I-swear-I-am-on-schedule book</a> in a coffee shop, I sit at my desk and plug the MacBook into a Studio Display. This braided black cable does it all: video, power, and everything else plugged into the Display.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">One cable. A Thunderbolt cable.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The stakes are lower in <a href="https://randsinrepose.com/guides/usb/usb-guide.html">cable selection</a> than in <a href="https://randsinrepose.com/guides/apple-charging-guide.html">charger selection</a>, but the cables really aren&#8217;t the situation. It&#8217;s the <a href="https://randsinrepose.com/guides/usb/usb-guide.html">ports</a>.</p>
