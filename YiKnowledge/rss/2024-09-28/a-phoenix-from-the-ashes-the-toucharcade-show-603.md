<p>Hello! We are still in a transitional phase of moving the podcast entirely to our Patreon, but in the meantime the only way we can get the show&#8217;s feed pushed out to where it needs to go is to post it to the website. However, the wheels are in motion for transitioning not just the podcast but some of the content we&#8217;d normally post to TouchArcade onto Patreon. That kicks off today with <a href="https://www.patreon.com/posts/toucharcade-game-112922535">a Game of the Week post</a>. Spoiler: It&#8217;s <em>Balatro</em>. We plan on having a mixture of content available to both free and paid Patreon supporters, as well as some stuff that will be exclusive to paid supporters only. The Game of the Week will likely be one of those paid-only articles, but for this first one it is available to everyone for free. Check it out to get a taste of what the &#8220;new" incarnation of TouchArcade might end up looking like.</p>
<p>Oh yeah, and the podcast. This week&#8217;s show is episode 603 and we talk about Hurricane Helene, Eli&#8217;s Homekit woes (and resolution!), the new iPhone 16 Pro which is now in Eli&#8217;s hot little hands, the usefulness of the weird new side button, new phone cases, listener emails, and more. It&#8217;s a jam-packed XL episode clocking in at almost 90 minutes, enjoy!</p>
<p>Don&#8217;t forget to shoot us emails with any questions, feedback, or anything else relevant or irrelevant to <a href="mailto:podcast@toucharcade.com">podcast@toucharcade.com</a>. We read &#8217;em all, and <strong>love</strong> decoding messages written entirely in emoji. As always, you can listen to us with the links below&#8230; And if you like what you hear, please subscribe and/or drop us a review in iTunes. Much appreciated!</p>
<p>As a companion to this audio podcast, we also do a video version of the same show that is <a href="http://patreon.com/toucharcade">exclusive to Patreon</a> which allows you to see us playing the games we&#8217;re talking about. Backers can view the most recent <em>video</em> episodes of the TouchArcade show by <a href="https://www.patreon.com/toucharcade/posts">clicking here</a>. Be sure you&#8217;re logged in to see the latest content. For everyone else who is curious, you can check out our <a href="https://www.patreon.com/toucharcade/posts">public patreon posts</a> to see older episodes of the video podcast. If you like what you see, consider becoming a <a href="http://patreon.com/toucharcade">TouchArcade Patreon backer</a>.</p>
