It continues to both amaze and concern me that a whole other internet ecosystem appears to exist within the realm of <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/roblox">Roblox</a>. Recently, a game by the name of Steal an Egg has blown up on the UGC platform, which is more or less what it says on the tin: you need to steal an egg. This is Roblox we're talking about, however, so Steal an Egg comes with a big ole asterisk that comes in the form of a treadmill you can train on whereby you can doomscroll through TikTok videos.
