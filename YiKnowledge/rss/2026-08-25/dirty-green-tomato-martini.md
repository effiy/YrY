<p>Kevin Johnson, the chef behind <a href="https://thegrocerycharleston.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener nofollow" target="_blank">The Grocery</a> in Charleston, South Carolina, started using the brine from his pickled green tomatoes in a dirty martini when he opened the restaurant in 2011. The pickling liquid offers a great balance of salinity and savoriness from the onions and tomatoes, plus a hint of spice and a touch of sweetness. <a href="https://www.saveur.com/recipes/dirty-vodka-martini-recipe/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_self">Dirty martinis</a> also never seem to go out of style, and it was an excellent way to divert food waste, which was and continues to be a big part of The Grocery’s ethos. Fifteen years later, the mission has flipped: Now Johnson has to invent new uses for the pickled green tomatoes just to keep up with the demand for the cocktail.</p>


<div class="orgnc-Recipe" id="recipe">
        
                        <div class="yield">
                <b>Makes:</b> 1 cocktail, plus 6 pints pickled tomatoes            </div>
                            <div class="yield">
                <b>Time</b>: 1 hour 15 minutes             </div>
                
            <h3 class="ingredients">Ingredients</h3>
        <div class="ingredients_section">
                            <div class="section">
                                            <h4 class="part-title">
                            For the pickled green tomatoes:                        </h4>
                                        <ul class="ingredients" id="recipe-ingredients">
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                3 lb. green tomatoes, sliced about ¼ in. thick, halved if necessary, plus any juices                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                3  jalapeños, thinly sliced                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                1  onion, very thinly sliced                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                3 Tbsp. mustard seeds                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                2 Tbsp. celery seeds                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                2 Tbsp. black peppercorns                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                  Crushed red chile flakes                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                5  cups distilled white vinegar                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                2½  cups sugar                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                2 tsp. kosher salt                            </li>
                                            </ul>
                    <div class="chicory-order-ingredients"></div>                </div>
                            <div class="section">
                                            <h4 class="part-title">
                            For the cocktail:                        </h4>
                                        <ul class="ingredients">
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                2½ oz. gin, such as Hat Trick                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                  Green tomato wedges, for garnish                            </li>
                                            </ul>
                    <div class="chicory-order-ingredients"></div>                </div>
                    </div>
                <h3 class="instructions">Instructions</h3>
        <ol class="instructions multiple">
                            <li class="instruction">
                    Make the pickled green tomatoes: In a large pot, bring about 16 cups of water to a boil (enough to cover the canning jars).                 </li>
                            <li class="instruction">
                    Meanwhile, in a medium bowl, gently stir together the tomatoes, jalapeños, and onion. Divide the mixture among six sterilized 16-ounce jars.                </li>
                            <li class="instruction">
                    In a small skillet over medium-low heat, toast the mustard seeds, celery seeds, peppercorns, and a pinch of chile flakes, stirring occasionally, until fragrant, about 2 minutes. Set aside to cool slightly, then divide among the jars (about 1 packed tablespoon per jar).                </li>
                            <li class="instruction">
                    To a medium pot over medium-high heat, add the vinegar, sugar, and salt and bring to a boil, stirring to dissolve the sugar and salt. Carefully pour the mixture into the jars, leaving about ¼ inch of headroom at the top of each. Seal the jars, but not too snugly.                </li>
                            <li class="instruction">
                    Place the jars in the boiling water, then bring back to a boil. Boil for 10 minutes. Carefully remove the jars and set aside for about 15 minutes, then invert the jars for several hours, ensuring the seal is complete.                </li>
                            <li class="instruction">
                    Make the cocktail: To a cocktail shaker filled halfway with ice, add the gin and 1¼ ounces of the pickled green tomato brine and shake vigorously until well chilled, about 15 seconds. Strain into a chilled coupe glass. Garnish with green tomato wedges on a skewer.                </li>
                    </ol>
    </div>
