<img src="https://assetsio.gnwcdn.com/lego-skylines-announced-paradox-01.jpg?width=690&amp;quality=85&amp;format=jpg&amp;auto=webp" /> <p>You probably don't need me to recap the difficult life <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/cities-skylines-2">Cities: Skylines 2</a> has had since hitting the streets in 2023. Plagued by <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/cities-skylines-2-pc-performance-system-requirements-and-best-settings">performance issues</a> and <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/cities-skylines-2-review">prompting questions</a> over whether its additions made it a worthy sequel right out of the gate, thus followed years of tweaks aiming to undo the damage. Eventually, original developers Colossal Order <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/cities-skylines-creators-colossal-order-are-handing-the-series-over-to-the-surviving-the-aftermath-devs">handed over the game and series' reins</a> to Iceflake Studios, with Paradox Interactive staying on as publishers.</p>
<p>The latter two have now revealed the next game in the Skylines series - a brick-built city builder dubbed Lego Skylines. It's not a bad name, but surely they could have gone with Lego Cities.</p>
