<h1 class="wp-block-heading celebrity-bio__h1">Dolly Parton Movies Ranked by Tomatometer</h1>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">There are only a handful of celebrities so beloved by the general public that they transcend all boundaries, and <strong><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/dolly_parton">Dolly Parton</a></strong> was arguably the most beloved of them all. The country music legend and American icon was not only an accomplished singer-songwriter but also a celebrated philanthropist and talented actress, parlaying her multiple Grammy-winning music career into a life of humanitarian work and several notable appearances in film and television.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Parton made her TV debut in the 1960s and 1970s on variety shows, doing what she does best: performing music. On 1978’s <em>Cher… Special</em>, a TV special centered on the titular singer-actress, Parton’s performance earned her an Emmy nomination for Outstanding Performance in a Variety or Music Program. She would go on to earn three more nominations for her work on her own TV movies, finally winning one in 2021 for <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/dolly_partons_christmas_on_the_square">Dolly Parton’s Christmas on the Square</a></em>.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img alt="Dolly Parton in 1976" class="wp-image-295963" height="380" src="https://editorial.rottentomatoes.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/dolly-parton-700x380-1.jpg?w=700" width="700" /><figcaption><span class="media-credit">(Photo by David Redfern/Redferns)</span></figcaption></figure>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Her feature film debut came in 1980’s <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/9_to_5"><em>9 to 5</em>,</a> a workplace comedy in which she starred opposite <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/jane_fonda">Jane Fonda</a> and <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/lily_tomlin">Lily Tomlin</a> as a secretary who plots with her peers to bring down their company’s sexist vice president. Not only was it a breakout role for Parton, but it garnered her widespread awards attention, including a Golden Globe nod for Best Motion Picture Actress &#8211; Musical/Comedy and both Golden Globe and Oscar nominations for her song “9 to 5”, which went on to win two Grammys.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Parton went on to have memorable roles 1982’s <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/best_little_whorehouse_in_texas">The Best Little Whorehouse in Texas</a></em>, 1989’s <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/steel_magnolias">Steel Magnolias</a></em>, and 1992’s <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/straight_talk">Straight Talk</a></em>, with plenty of cameos and personal appearances across film and TV, from <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/miss_congeniality_2">Miss Congeniality 2</a></em> to <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/the_simpsons">The Simpsons</a></em>. She played longtime family friend Aunt Dolly in three episodes of <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/hannah_montana">Hannah Montana</a></em>, guest-starred on Seth McFarlane’s <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/orville">The Orville</a></em> as a simulation of herself, and played small roles in episodes of <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/reba">Reba</a></em>, <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/the_magic_school_bus">The Magic School Bus</a></em>, and <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/grace_and_frankie">Grace &amp; Frankie</a></em>, the latter of which reunited her with her <em>9 to 5</em> co-stars Jane Fonda and Lily Tomlin.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img alt="Dolly Parton and Olympia Dukakis in 1989's 'Steel Magnolias'" class="wp-image-295967" height="380" src="https://editorial.rottentomatoes.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/dolly-parton-steel-magnolias-700x380-1.jpg?w=700" width="700" /><figcaption><span class="media-credit">(Photo by TriStar Pictures/Courtesy Everett Collection)</span></figcaption></figure>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Sadly, Parton passed away after a &#8220;<a href="https://people.com/dolly-parton-died-after-a-brief-battle-with-cancer-her-team-confirms-12067930?" rel="noopener" target="_blank">brief battle with cancer</a>&#8221; on August 25, 2026 at the age of 80, just months after health concerns in late 2025 led to the cancellation of a December residency in Las Vegas. And while she will be best remembered for her staggering body of work in music, she also turned in awards-worthy performances on both the big screen and television. We celebrate the life of Dolly Parton by looking back at all of her movies ranked by Tomatometer.</p>
