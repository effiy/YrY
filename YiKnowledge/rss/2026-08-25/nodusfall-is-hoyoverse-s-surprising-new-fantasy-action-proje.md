      <p>This year's Gamescom Opening Night Live started off with a bit of a surprise swing from one big publisher. Hoyoverse, known for ongoing games like Genshin Impact, Honkai Star Rail, and Zenless Zone Zero, officially unveiled a new game called Nodusfall.</p><p>Set in a darker fantasy world than you might have expected, Nodusfall sees player avatars dubbed The Weavers taking on a host of calamitous enemies. The action looks to be a little more careful and calculated, with big swings and dangerous strikes to deal with as players try to take down their foes.</p>&nbsp;<p>There's a palpable bunch of inspirations here, to the the degree where I'd be stunned if Elden Ring wasn't among them. The action also has a bit of a Monster Hunter to it as well, with the Weavers using their thread abilities to move around the arena and leap forward onto the big bads they're fighting.</p><p>No word yet on a release date or any other news for Nodusfall, but we'll likely keep an eye on this tonal pivot for Hoyoverse.</p>
