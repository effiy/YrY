<p>The first thing you'll notice about the Hisense E7 is how thin and lightweight it is. It comes with four HDMI connections, one of which is an eARC connection so you can plug in a soundbar, and two with a native 144Hz refresh rate in game mode. Like most new TVs these days, it'll be 4K and have AI modes that improve picture quality. This will be most noticeable when watching sports; the TV will recognize the content and automatically switch the picture settings to sports mode. Because this is a Fire TV, it'll come with built-in Alexa+, which is a nice touch for those already in the Alexa ecosystem. You can also <a href="https://lifehacker.com/tech/how-to-install-kodi-on-fire-tv-stick" target="_blank">install Kodi</a> to watch virtually anything for free.</p><p>Another standout feature is the support for Dolby Vision IQ and Dolby Atmos, which can make your streaming much more immersive, making your TV adapt its picture in real time to its environment and giving you a 3D surround sound experience if you have a sound system. For its price, this is an excellent choice for someone looking to upgrade their TV to a new 2026 QLED without breaking the bank.</p><div class=" relative flex justify-center py-16 md:left-1/2 md:w-[780px] md:max-w-max md:-translate-x-1/2">
