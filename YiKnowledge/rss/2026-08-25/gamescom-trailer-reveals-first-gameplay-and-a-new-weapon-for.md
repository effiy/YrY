      <p>The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt, a game from 2015, somehow ended up being the most exciting thing at Gamescom's 2026 Opening Night Live showcase. That's partly due to&nbsp;<a href="https://gameinformer.com/2026/08/25/the-witcher-3-wild-hunt-remastered-is-a-free-huge-upgrade-out-september-29">the reveal of a new, free remaster of the game coming just next month</a>, but it's also because Wild Hunt is getting new content for the first time in years. Songs of the Past, The Witcher 3's latest expansion, was announced a few months ago, and today, we finally got to see gameplay. Check out the trailer below.</p>&nbsp;<p>In the footage, we see women dancing in flower crowns in a Midsommar-style festival (which is reminiscent of the Witcher 4 reveal, by the way), lush swamplands at sunset, and a mysterious tree creature who calls out to Geralt. "Come, sword bearer, bane of beasts," a mysterious voice calls to Geralt. "Delve into darkness and see the truth of Letten's beauty."</p><p>Letten, by the way, is the name of the game's new region. <a href="https://press.cdprojektred.com/en/news/1839/the-witcher-3-wild-hunt-remastered-announced-songs-of-the-past-gets-first-look">A press release describes it</a>, saying, "This idyllic land is rich in rural tradition and folklore, and its people enjoy a fulfilling life of bountiful harvests and lavish festivals. But a dark secret lurks just beneath the surface, and Geralt must overcome never-before-seen foes as he fights to overcome its long-dormant evil."</p><p>The footage ends with Geralt wielding a new, chain-like weapon. <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LOougswn-zg">In a follow-up interview on the Gamescom stage</a>, CD Projekt Red level design lead Miles Tost identified it as a weapon that appeared in the intro of the studio's first Witcher game. Geralt uses it to draw a foe in for an instant beheading, and it looks about as cool as it sounds.</p><p>The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt – Songs of the Past is out next year on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, Switch 2, and PC. The Switch 1 version of the game is notably absent from CD Projekt Red's messaging, however. For more Witcher, check out the recent announcement that&nbsp;<a href="https://gameinformer.com/2026/08/24/the-witcher-iv-launches-in-2028-cd-projekt-red-joint-ceo-confirms">The Witcher 4, which features a playable Ciri, drops in 2028</a>. For Witcher-adjacent news, <a href="https://gameinformer.com/2026/08/24/a-day-one-console-patch-will-make-the-blood-of-dawnwalker-run-at-60-fps">check out the latest update from the team behind The Blood of Dawnwalker</a>, a game created by former Witcher developers.</p>
