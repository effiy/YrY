      <p>Developer Daniel Mullins of Daniel Mullins Games (creator Inscryption and Pony Island) is back with their next strange experience that will undoubtedly be full of twists and play with your expectations. The previously announced Pony Island 2: Panda Circus has a release date: April 27, 2027.</p>&nbsp;<p>We also learned that the game will feature FMV performances from Sungwon Cho, Arian Moayed, Caitlin Reilly, and Kevin Saxby. The game is self-described as, "A voyage through time, myth, divinity, and video games."
