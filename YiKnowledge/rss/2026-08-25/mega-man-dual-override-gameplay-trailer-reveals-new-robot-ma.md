      <p>A new trailer for <a href="https://gameinformer.com/the-game-awards-2025/2025/12/11/mega-man-dual-override-brings-the-blue-bomber-back-in-2027">Mega Man: Dual Override</a> revealed during Gamescom Opening Night Live gave us a new look at the Blue Bomber in action, including the reveal of a new foe and a familiar ally.</p><p>The video shows Mega Man doing what he does best: blasting apart Dr. Wily’s machines, though he has a new antagonist in the form of Twinkle Girl. She’s evidently the game’s first Robot Master, and actually looks rather pleasant and whimsical despite trying to kill Mega Man.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>We also see Mega Man sporting a new suit that may either represent the power he receives from defeating Twinkle Girl, or a separate transformation in the vein of the armor set from Mega Man X. The trailer ends with the reveal of updated key art featuring Mega Man's longtime frenemy Proto Man.&nbsp;</p>&nbsp;<p>Mega Man: Dual Override is coming in 2027 to PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, Switch 2, PlayStation 4, Xbox One, Switch, and PC.&nbsp;</p>
