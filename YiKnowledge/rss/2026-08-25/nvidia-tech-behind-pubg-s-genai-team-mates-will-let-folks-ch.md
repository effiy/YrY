<img src="https://assetsio.gnwcdn.com/Nvidia-ace-ai-teammate-tech-aniimo-01.jpg?width=690&amp;quality=85&amp;format=jpg&amp;auto=webp" /> <p>Alongside releasing <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/nvidia-dlss-45-ray-reconstruction-out-now-ups-shadow-quality-and-more-accurately-illuminates-trash-bags">Ray Reconstruction for DLSS 4.5</a>, Nvidia have announced that their ACE tech best known for powering <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/playerunknowns-battlegrounds">PUBG: Battlegrounds</a>' <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/pubg-has-genai-team-mates-now-capable-of-intelligent-decision-making-and-i-must-remind-you-that-krafton-are-working-on-bots-for-the-military">generative AI team mates</a> will be coming to another game early next year. Said game is Aniimo, a creature-catching open-world RPG from Chinese devs Pawprint Studio, which looks to be following the legally distict Pokemon-like formula established by the likes of <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/palworld">Palworld</a>. With help from the Nvidia tech, it'll also let players chat with their creatures.</p>
