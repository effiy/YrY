<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a name="top"></a><strong>Want to know if your favorite television series is among the latest canceled TV shows? Consider this your all-encompassing list on renewed shows, when a series gets the axe, and which titles are being reworked as &#8220;anthologies&#8221; to squeeze every single drop of fan love (and awards) out of them.</strong></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>How it works: </strong>Renewals are obvious: new season = yay! Cancellations are more complicated. When a network or streaming service announces that they&#8217;re not making another season of a show, we count that as canceled. Sure, some shows &#8220;come to a natural end,&#8221; but someone somewhere said &#8220;enough.&#8221; So to avoid debating the semantics of &#8220;canceled&#8221; versus &#8220;retired,&#8221; we&#8217;re just going to go with the former. There are also some instances, either due to scheduling conflicts or various delays, where the answer is &#8220;I dunno.&#8221; In those instances, we will leave the series as renewed and list the <b>most recent season</b> until we&#8217;re told otherwise.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Related:</strong> <a href="https://editorial.rottentomatoes.com/article/tv-premiere-dates-2026/">TV Premiere Dates 2026</a></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Feel free to debate specific titles in the comments. But first: learn which series will live on, which were renewed and <i>then</i> canceled, which are switching channels and which have simply reached their expiration date.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Highlights</strong></h2>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Netflix Is Charmed by <em>The Gentlemen</em></strong></p>


<div class="youtube-container"></div>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">After directing the 2019 film <em>The Gentlemen</em>, Guy Ritchie was inspired to put together a loosely connected spin-off television series centering on a duke who inherits a large estate that houses a clandestine marijuana operation and runs afoul of the local criminal syndicate. Netflix&#8217;s <em><strong><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/the_gentlemen">The Gentlemen</a></strong></em>, starring Theo James as said duke, premiered in 2024 to largely positive reviews, and ahead of its second season premiere on September 3, the show has been renewed for a third season, with unconfirmed rumors that Meghan Markle may join the cast.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong><em>Legends</em> Will Return</strong></p>


<div class="youtube-container"></div>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>The Gentlemen</em> wasn&#8217;t the only show Netflix decided to bring back. One of the streamer&#8217;s more under-the-radar dramas, <em><strong><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/legends_2026">Legends</a></strong></em>, is coming back for a second season as well. Debuting in May of 2026, the crime drama follows a ragtag group of British customs officers who join a top-secret undercover task force to bring down a drug ring during the early 1990s, and it was based on true events. The second season, which will bring back the team back together for another bust, begins production in 2027.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Amazon Decides <em>Sterling Point</em> is Prime Material</strong></p>


<div class="youtube-container"></div>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A little over two weeks after Prime Video debuted the first season of <em><strong><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/sterling_point">Sterling Point</a></strong></em>, they&#8217;ve gone ahead and renewed it for a second. The drama centers on a 17-year-old who inherits her mysterious grandfather&#8217;s island in Canada and decides to move there to claim responsibility for it, building a new life for herself in the process. The show was well-received by critics, earning a Certified Fresh 90% on the Tomatometer.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>HBO Wants to Return to Derry</strong></p>


<div class="youtube-container"></div>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Last October, HBO took viewers back to the town of Derry, Maine, to further explore how Pennywise the shape-shifting clown-demon (Bill Skarsgård) terrorized the city a whole generation prior to the events of <em>It</em> and <em>It: Chapter Two</em>. Set in 1962, <em><strong><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/it_welcome_to_derry">It: Welcome to Derry</a></strong></em> followed the mysterious disappearances of a group of children and the military&#8217;s efforts to investigate the events. Now, we know not only that HBO has renewed the series for a second season, but that it will take place in 1935 and focus on an unlucky gang of bank robbers who choose the worst place to stock up on ammo.</p>
