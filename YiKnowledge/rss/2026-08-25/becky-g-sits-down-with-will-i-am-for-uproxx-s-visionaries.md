<div class="upx-component upx-embed-cnx"><div class="cnx-embed-holder">&amp;&amp;&amp;&amp;</div></div><p><a href="https://uproxx.com/tag/becky-g/" target="_blank">Becky G</a> is a global superstar. She&#8217;s sold millions of albums, her songs have been streamed tens of billions of times, and she&#8217;s collaborated with other A-listers like Bad Bunny, Peso Pluma, and Burna Boy. </p>
<p>Ahead of the release of her fire new album <em>Baraja Bendita</em>, Becky G sat down with <em>UPROXX</em> Chief Visionary Officer <a href="https://uproxx.com/tag/will-i-am/" target="_blank">will.i.am</a> for the latest episode of <a href="https://uproxx.com/tag/uproxx-visionaries/" target="_blank"><em>Visionaries</em></a>. </p>
<p>The interview begins with Becky G recalling the day she met will.i.am and how it changed her life. &#8220;I had just signed [my record deal] and one of the first-ever writing sessions that I got called into after getting signed was with will.i.am,&#8221; she said. &#8220;I pulled up, my little son had just turned 15, and that was when we wrote &#8216;Problem,&#8217; and then [it] became a soundtrack theme song for a fire-ass movie called <em>Hotel Transylvania</em>. It was also one of my first-ever official music videos.&#8221; Becky G remembers thinking, &#8220;Oh, this is what a real music video is&#8230; Wow, what is my life? This is so crazy.&#8221;</p>
<p>Becky G also discusses headlining SoFi Stadium, why she identifies as a genre-less artist, her activism, and her vision for the future. As will.i.am put it, &#8220;She became a worldwide star [by] doing things her way and representing her culture.&#8221;</p>
