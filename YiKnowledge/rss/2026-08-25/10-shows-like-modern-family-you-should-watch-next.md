<p>We may earn a commission from links on this page.</p><p>The family sitcom, once a staple, has become a bit more rare over the past decade or so&mdash;but when one hits, as <em>Modern Family</em> did over the course of its <em>eleven</em> seasons, it hits big. Gone but not forgotten, the show continues to remind us that, hey&mdash;families DO come in all shapes and sizes, and that's not just fine&mdash;it's great actually. In that spirit, here are some other shows that invite us into the lives of big ol' families living the American and/or Canadian dream.</p><h2 id="life-in-pieces-2015-2019"><em>Life in Pieces</em> (2015&ndash;2019)</h2><div class="mx-auto mt-8 w-full max-w-4xl aspect-video">

</div>


<p>A bit of a spiritual successor to Roseanne as one of our great working-class sitcoms, <em>Malcolm in the Middle</em> represented the economic reality that had firmly taken hold in the real world&mdash;the fact that an office job, once seen as a big a step up, is no longer a symbol of prosperity.
