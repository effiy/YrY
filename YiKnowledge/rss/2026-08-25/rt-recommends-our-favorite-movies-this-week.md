<h1 class="wp-block-heading celebrity-bio__h1">RT Recommends: Our Favorite Movies This Week</h1>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-full is-resized"><img alt="" class="wp-image-296150" height="250" src="https://editorial.rottentomatoes.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Steel_Mag700.jpg" style="width: 700px; height: auto;" width="700" /></figure>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If you were to ask any member of our staff for a recommendation on what to watch, chances are we&#8217;d have a slew of titles to share. We&#8217;re exposed to <em>a lot</em> of movies here at Rotten Tomatoes, and we&#8217;re not mad about it. That&#8217;s why we&#8217;ve put together this weekly guide of films, staff picks in alphabetical order, that we recommend you check out every week. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If you&#8217;re looking to honor the life and career of American icon Dolly Parton, below you&#8217;ll find a handful of her films, including <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/steel_magnolias">Steel Magnolias</a></em> (an audience favorite), where she stars alongside Sally Field, Shirley MacLaine, and Daryl Hannah. You can also find <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/9_to_5">9 to 5</a> </em>and <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/the_year_dolly_parton_was_my_mom_2011">The Year Dolly Parton was My Mom</a></em>. And speaking of iconic actors, celebrate the life of beloved actor Tim Curry by streaming a few of his movies, also listed below, including <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/rocky_horror_picture_show">The Rocky Horror Picture Show</a></em> and <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/stephen_kings_it">Stephen King&#8217;s It</a></em>, where critic Tony Atherton <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/stephen_kings_it/reviews">wrote</a>: &#8220;Curry imbues the apparition with such an alarming mix of goofiness and malevolence&#8221; as the original Pennywise. Be sure to also check out our guide to <a href="https://editorial.rottentomatoes.com/guide/dolly-parton-movies-ranked/">Dolly Parton Movies Ranked</a> and <a href="https://editorial.rottentomatoes.com/guide/tim-curry-movies-ranked/">Tim Curry Movies Ranked</a>. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">As we make our way through the 10th annual Studio Ghibli Fest, perhaps you&#8217;re looking to explore some of the studio&#8217;s most well-received films. In case you missed it, <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/my_neighbor_totoro">My Neighbor Totoro</a></em> was recently back in theaters and is now available to Stream on Fandango, along with <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/kikis_delivery_service">Kiki&#8217;s Delivery Service</a></em>, and more. &#8220;My 6-year-old twin nieces are hard to pin down; getting them to sit still for a movie, or one that they would agree to watch together, is even harder! When I suggested we watch <em>My Neighbor Totoro</em>, I was braced for failure, but was delighted when they loved it,&#8221; said Corey Arterian, Director, Data Products for RT. &#8220;One niece even insisted on eating her cucumber unsliced but rather bite by bite like Mei does in the movie. I knew <em>Kiki&#8217;s Delivery Service</em> would be a shoo-in, and indeed it was! These are beautiful films that are a joy to watch with childlike wonder, and it&#8217;s a refreshing break from the constant noise that so much kids&#8217; media seems to imbue.&#8221; </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Whatever you&#8217;re in the mood for, if you&#8217;re fresh out of ideas on what to watch this week and need a little help, the following list of staff favorites ranges from beloved classics to new releases. </p>
