The idea behind <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/humankind">Humankind</a> is a funny one, in that it pulls from real-world history to essentially let you craft historical fanfiction. I suppose that's true of many <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/the-best-strategy-games-on-pc">strategy</a> games, but this one's all about mixing and matching different cultures throughout history. And now, it's asking you to do so all over again, as a sequel in the works. Zero points for guessing what it's called, because it's very plainly titled Humankind 2.
