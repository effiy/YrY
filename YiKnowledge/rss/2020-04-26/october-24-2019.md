<h2 class="wp-block-heading">SoftBank Says It Has Now Invested&nbsp;$18.5 Billion Altogether in WeWork; &#8220;We&#8217;re Basically Betting SoftBank&#8221;<br /></h2>



<table class="wp-block-table"><tbody><tr><td>Yesterday, in addressing nervous WeWork employees at an all-hands, the company’s new chairman, SoftBank&nbsp; executive Marcelo Claure, told those gathered that their days of worrying are over,&nbsp;<a href="https://www.vox.com/2019/10/24/20929236/wework-layoffs-adam-neumann-marcelo-claure" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">says Recode</a>, which obtained a leaked recording of the meeting.&nbsp;<br /><br />In comments that may stun industry observers who haven’t done the math — and upset at least some percentage of SoftBank investors — Claure is quoted as telling employees: “We have guaranteed the future of WeWork, but more importantly is we’re putting the future back into our hands. There’s no more days needed to go fundraising. There’s no more days needed to go prove to the investor community that we’re a viable company. The size of the commitment that SoftBank has made to this company in the past and now is $18.5 billion. To put the things in context, that is bigger than the GDP of my country where I came from. That’s a country where there’s 11 million people.”&nbsp;<br /><br />Claure, a native of Bolivia who was named chairman as part of SoftBank’s rescue of the beleaguered co-working company, has been a SoftBank lieutenant for the last five years, and currently holds a variety of titles on its behalf, including COO of SoftBank Group Corp, CEO of SoftBank Group International and CEO of SoftBank Latin America.&nbsp;<br /><br />He has said he first met SoftBank founder Masayoshi Son after building up his own business, Brightstar — a&nbsp; cellphone reseller — then selling 57% of it to SoftBank in 2013 in a deal that valued the company at $2.2 billion. SoftBank later acquired more of the company before deciding to explore a sale of the low-margin business last year for&nbsp;<a href="https://www.reuters.com/article/us-brightstar-m-a/softbank-seeks-to-sell-cellphone-distributor-brightstar-sources-idUSKCN1HD2GK" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">$1 billion</a>.&nbsp;<br /><br />By then, Claure was running Sprint, a SoftBank-backed property that installed Claure as CEO in 2014, where he presided over a&nbsp;<a href="https://247wallst.com/special-report/2017/12/26/worst-ceos-in-america-2017/4/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">massive share slide</a>&nbsp;that began before he joined the company and ended only last year when T-Mobile and Sprint agreed to merge.&nbsp;<br /><br /><em><a href="https://techcrunch.com/2019/10/24/softbank-notes-it-has-now-invested-18-5-billion-in-wework-more-than-the-gdp-of-bolivia-which-has-11-5-million-people/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">More here</a></em>.</td></tr></tbody></table>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Exits</h2>



<p><strong>Amazon</strong>&nbsp;has acquired&nbsp;<strong>Health Navigator</strong>, a five-year-old startup that develops APIs for online health services. According to CNBC, Health Navigator will become part of Amazon Care, Amazon&#8217;s&nbsp;pilot healthcare service program for employees. This is the second health startup acquired by Amazon.
