<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Top News<br /></h2>



<p><strong>NordVPN</strong>, a virtual private network provider that promises to “protect your privacy online,” confirmed today that <a href="https://techcrunch.com/2019/10/21/nordvpn-confirms-it-was-hacked/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">it was hacked</a>. </p>



<p><strong>Facebook</strong> said today that it has recently found and taken down four state-backed disinformation campaigns, a sign of how <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2019/10/21/technology/facebook-disinformation-russia-iran.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">foreign interference online is increasing</a> ahead of the 2020 presidential election. </p>



<p>According to Axios, <strong>SoftBank</strong> will pay former WeWork CEO and current non-executive chairman <strong>Adam Neumann</strong> around <a href="https://www.axios.com/adam-neumann-wework-softbank-takeover-306243f9-95d5-40ef-b388-b06ef8d8bafd.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">$200 million</a> to leave the board of directors, give up his voting shares, and support SoftBank&#8217;s takeover bid, which WeWork&#8217;s board is apparently <a href="https://www.wsj.com/articles/softbank-offers-to-put-6-5b-into-wework-including-5b-loan-11571687872" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">slated to discuss tomorrow</a>. The Japanese conglomerate has already invested many billions of dollars in WeWork. The WSJ says its bid would see it lend WeWork $5 billion more and accelerate a $1.5 billion investment in the troubled company that it was scheduled to make next year. <img alt="💸" class="wp-smiley" src="https://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/16.0.1/72x72/1f4b8.png" style="height: 1em;" /><img alt="💸" class="wp-smiley" src="https://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/16.0.1/72x72/1f4b8.png" style="height: 1em;" />In the meantime, notes CNBC, if SoftBank&#8217;s deal goes through, <strong>JPMorgan</strong>, which is WeWork&#8217;s third largest external shareholder, will <a href="https://www.cnbc.com/2019/10/21/jp-morgan-is-at-risk-of-losing-on-its-wework-bet-a-blow-to-ceo-dimon.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">collect nothing</a> for months of work, along with potential hefty losses on its exiting equity and debt investments.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Massive Fundings</h2>



<p><strong>Commercetools</strong>, a 13-year-old, Munich, Germany-based company that makes cloud-based e-commerce software, has raised €130 million ($145 million) in funding from <strong>Insight Partners</strong>. TechCrunch has <a href="https://techcrunch.com/2019/10/21/commercetools-raises-145m-from-insight-for-shopify-style-e-commerce-apis-for-large-enterprises/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">more here</a>. </p>



<p><strong>Instabase</strong>, a four-year-old, San Francisco-based startup that aims to help businesses build customizable apps to automate different parts of their business, has raised $105 million in Series B funding led by <strong>Index Ventures</strong>, reportedly at a post-money valuation of more than $1 billion. The company, whose investors also include <strong>Greylock Partners</strong>, <strong>New Enterprise Associates</strong>, <strong>Andreessen Horowitz</strong>, <strong>Spark Capital</strong>, <strong>Tribe Capital</strong>, <strong>SC Ventures</strong>, and <strong>Glynn Capital</strong>, has now raised $132 million altogether, according to Crunchbase. Crunchbase News has <a href="https://news.crunchbase.com/news/instabase-raises-massive-105m-series-b-reaches-unicorn-status/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">more here</a>. </p>



<p><strong>Zoox</strong>, a five-year-old, Foster City, Ca.-based self-driving startup at work on building entire vehicles, has raised $200 million in a convertible note that will folded into an upcoming Series C round, reports Axios. The company had previously raised $800 million, per a 2018 <a href="https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2018-07-17/robot-taxi-startup-zoox-has-800-million-and-a-wild-pitch" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Bloomberg story</a> that dove deep into the company . . . right before cofounder and then-CEO <strong>Tim Kentley-Klay</strong> was elbowed out. <a href="https://www.axios.com/zoox-self-driving-car-fundraising-52aa4ef6-9b7d-4a4d-b9be-306ecf6f3511.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">More here</a>.<a href="https://news.crunchbase.com/news/instabase-raises-massive-105m-series-b-reaches-unicorn-status/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank"> </a></p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Big-But-Not-Crazy-Big Fundings</h2>



<p><strong>Athena</strong>, a two-year-old, Sydney, Australia-based online lending startup focused on home loans,  has raised the equivalent of $48 million from country’s largest industry fund, <strong>AustralianSuper</strong>, along with earlier backers <strong>Square Peg Capital</strong>, <strong>AirTree Ventures</strong> and <strong>Hostplus</strong>, as well as <strong>Salesforce Ventures</strong> and <strong>NAB Ventures</strong>. The country&#8217;s Startup Daily has <a href="https://www.startupdaily.net/2019/10/online-mortgage-lender-athena-raises-70-million-in-series-c/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">more here</a>. </p>



<p><strong>Bayzat</strong>, a six-year-old, Abu Dhabi, UAE-based company that makes a free platform for human resources, payroll and employee benefits, has raised $16 million in Series B funding. <strong>Point72 Ventures</strong> led the round, joined by <strong>Mubadala Capital</strong>, <strong>Elm</strong>, <strong>Greyhound Capital</strong>, <strong>Endeavor Catalyst</strong>, and <strong>Tech Invest Com</strong>. Wamda has <a href="https://www.wamda.com/2019/10/bayzat-raises-16-million-series-b-funding" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">more here</a>. </p>



<p><strong>Plexium</strong>, a year-old, San Diego, Ca.-based biotech focused on protein degradation, just raised $28 million in Series A funding. <strong>DCVC</strong> and <strong>The Column Group</strong> co-led the round, joined by <strong>M Ventures</strong>, <strong>CRV</strong>, and <strong>Neotribe Ventures</strong>. FierceBiotech has <a href="https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/protein-degrading-plexium-debuts-28m-to-attack-cancer-neurodegenerative-disease" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">more here</a>.  </p>



<p><strong>Thinksurance</strong>, a four-year-old, Frankfurt, Germany-based insurance distribution platform, has raised €13 million in Series B funding from <strong>Eight Roads Ventures</strong>. <a href="http://www.thinksurance.de/en" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">More here</a>.</p>



<p><strong>Upstream Security</strong>, a two-year-old, Herzliya, Israel-based cybersecurity startup focused on connected vehicles, has raised $30 million in Series B funding. <strong>Renault</strong> led the round, joined by <strong>Volvo</strong>, <strong>Hyundai</strong>, <strong>Hyundai AutoEver</strong>, <strong>Nationwide Ventures</strong>, and earlier backers <strong>CRV</strong>, <strong>Glilot Capital</strong>, and <strong>Maniv Mobility</strong>. VentureBeat has <a href="https://venturebeat.com/2019/10/21/upstream-security-raises-30-million-to-protect-connected-cars-from-cyber-attacks/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">more here</a>.</p>

The Herald has <a href="https://www.heraldscotland.com/business_hq/17984253.glasgow-private-equity-firm-backs-edinburgh-ecommerce-analytics-specialist/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">more here</a>. </p>



<p><strong>GreenPark Sports</strong>, a year-old, Calabasas, Ca.-based publisher and developer of fan-forward, digital experiences and mobile games for sports and eSports fans (it has some heavy-hitting cofounders, too, including YouTube cofounder <strong>Chad Hurley</strong>), has raised $8.5 million in funding. <strong>SignalFire</strong> led the round, joined by <strong>Sapphire Sports</strong> and <strong>Founders Fund</strong>, among others. TechCrunch has <a href="https://techcrunch.com/2019/10/21/greenpark-sports/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">more here</a>. </p>



<p><strong>Nymbl Science</strong>, a five-year-old, Denver, Co.- based startup with an app-based digital balance training program for older adults (to keep them from falling), has raised $2.8 million in seed funding led by <strong>Rockies Venture Club</strong>. <a href="https://www.nymblscience.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">More here</a>. </p>



<p><strong>Speechmatics</strong>, a 10-year-old, London-based speech recognition technology engine, has raised $8.25 million in Series A funding. <strong>AlbionVC</strong> led the round, joined by <strong>IQ Capital</strong>. <a href="https://www.speechmatics.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">More here</a>.</p>

<p>According to the WSJ, Peter Thiel’s <strong>Founders Fund</strong> is raising nearly $3 billion across two new funds, including its first dedicated fund for late-stage companies, which the firm intends to pour into existing investments like the payments business Stripe and the data-analysis company Palantir Technologies. <a href="https://www.wsj.com/articles/peter-thiels-founders-fund-building-new-war-chest-in-strategy-shift-11571672563" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">More here</a>. </p>



<p><strong>Neotribe Ventures</strong>, a two-year-old, Menlo Park, Ca.-based venture firm focused on biotech, weather tech, AI, IoT and enterprise infrastructure startups, is in the process of raising $205 million for its second fund, suggests an <a href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1787509/000178750919000001/xslFormDX01/primary_doc.xml" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">SEC filing</a>. Among Neotribe&#8217;s founders is the serial entrepreneur and longtime NEA investor <strong>Kittu Kolluri </strong>(he cofounded Healtheon/WebMD and Neoteris). <a href="https://www.neotribe.vc/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">More here</a>.  </p>



<p><strong>Refactor</strong>, the three-year-old, Burlingame, Ca.-based seed-stage investment firm that was cofounded by VCs <strong>David Lee</strong> and <strong>Zal Bilimoria</strong> and is now run by Bilimoria alone, has raised $30 million in capital commitments. Crunchbase News has <a href="https://news.crunchbase.com/news/now-a-solo-gp-firm-refactor-capital-closes-30m-for-fund-ii/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">more here</a>.</p>
