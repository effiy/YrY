	<p>You may not realize it, but over time, snacks come and go. Those small-batch, <a href="https://uproxx.com/life/best-limited-edition-bourbon-whiskeys-summer-2023/" target="_blank">limited-edition</a>, seasonal flavors sometimes turn into full-time regular SKUs, but more often than not they fade.  So don&#8217;t fall too far in love with any snack because one day you may become broken-hearted when they change the <a href="https://uproxx.com/life/best-chile-verde-recipe-super-bowl/" target="_blank">recipe</a>. Until then, stock up on your favorites and try any and every snack that strikes your mood.</p>
<p>Here at UPROXX, we have new snacks worth trying for every mood, and what good is <a href="https://uproxx.com/life/24-freeze-dried-candies-ranked/" target="_blank">snacking</a> if you can&#8217;t share with some friends? This round, we&#8217;re sharing a mix of new favorites, special editions and a bunch of great products that should hopefully be around for a long time.</p>

<p>Grown on small acreage in Virginia, Planters Special Reserve Crop 24 has an earthy nuttiness that is crunchy on the front end and creamy on the aftertaste, along with the outer salt. These nuts are no ordinary-size Planters peanuts; the Special Reserve variety may be twice as large and possibly twice the flavor, richness and complexity. Nicely salted &#8211; once you start munching on these, keep a drink nearby and maybe even some lip balm.</p>

<h3>Siete Lime Grain-Free Tortilla Chips</h3>
<p></a></p>

<p>Siete&#8217;s Lime Grain-Free Tortilla chips are more than ideal for salsa and dips. These chips aren&#8217;t super salty. Watch out, it&#8217;s pretty easy to burn through a bag of these chips without even looking down.</p>

<p>There&#8217;s something I want to know about Edley&#8217;s barbecue sauce — which pepper is it that&#8217;s gently stinging in the background?? It&#8217;s a mystery that isn&#8217;t revealed in their ingredients list but key to the addictive quality of their newly bottled and delicious sauce.  Edley&#8217;s has that signature sweetness you want in a barbecue sauce, but doesn&#8217;t go too far into saccharine territory, combining apple cider vinegar, tamarind and mustard powder to balance out the sugary notes. If you want to pick up a tasty new barbecue sauce for your ribs, wings, chicken tender dips, etc. Edley&#8217;s is definitely worth your time and taste buds.</p>
