	<p>If Maui is the dreamy vacation island everyone posts about, think turquoise ocean, swaying palms, and sunsets that stop you in your tracks, then <a href="https://www.royallahaina.com/" target="_blank">Royal Lahaina Resort &amp; Bungalows</a> is the place that <em>feels</em> like Maui. This is not a cookie-cutter <a href="https://uproxx.com/life/sls-baha-mar-bahamas-hotel-review/" target="_blank">beachfront hotel</a> trying to mimic <a href="https://uproxx.com/life/best-place-to-stay-waikiki/" target="_blank">Hawaiian charm</a>. It’s the real thing, exquisitely layered with local culture, genuine aloha spirit, and views straight out of a luxury travel magazine.</p>
<p>Read on for everything you need to know to plan the <a href="https://uproxx.com/life/adults-only-hotel-in-oahu-review/" target="_blank">perfect</a> Maui trip, including where to stay, where to go whale watching (hint: you don’t even need a boat), where to experience the best espresso martinis, and more.</p>
<h2>A Legacy on the Beach and the Perfect West Maui Home Base</h2>
<figure class="wp-caption alignnone" id="attachment_404359595"><img alt="maui" class="size-medium wp-image-404359595" height="469" src="https://uproxx.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Resort-Aerial-5.jpg?w=704" width="704" /><figcaption class="wp-caption-text"><span class="wp-media-credit">ROYAL LAHAINA RESORT &amp; BUNGALOWS</span></figcaption></figure>
<p>Set on 27 acres of oceanfront along iconic Kaʻanapali Beach, Royal Lahaina Resort &amp; Bungalows has long been one of West Maui’s most beloved places to stay. Step outside and you are greeted by a wide stretch of golden sand that feels surprisingly private, even by Maui standards, where beaches are public.</p>
<p>This is the kind of beach that invites quiet morning walks, slow afternoons with a book, and sunsets that feel personal rather than packed. The hotel is also perfectly positioned. Within minutes, you can stroll to <a href="https://www.whalersvillage.com/en/" target="_blank">Whalers Village</a> by foot or a free shuttle for shopping and dining, tee off at the <a href="https://www.kaanapaligolfcourses.com/" target="_blank">Kaʻanapali Golf Courses</a>, or hop in the car for a short drive to historic Lahaina town, Kapalua’s scenic coastline, or some of the island’s best snorkeling spots.</p>
<figure class="wp-caption alignnone" id="attachment_404359600"><img alt="maui" class="size-medium wp-image-404359600" height="469" src="https://uproxx.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Branches-1.jpg?w=704" width="704" /><figcaption class="wp-caption-text"><span class="wp-media-credit">ROYAL LAHAINA RESORT &amp; BUNGALOWS</span></figcaption></figure>
<p>Royal Lahaina Resort &amp; Bungalows&#8217; connection to the community runs deep. After the devastating Lahaina wildfires in 2023, the resort temporarily closed to guests and became a place of refuge, housing staff members and families who had lost their homes. It was a powerful reminder that this is not just a resort operating in Maui, but one that stands with Maui.</p>
<p>That sense of care is tangible throughout the property. It feels sincere, grounded, and deeply respectful. As someone who once called Hawaii home, that authenticity matters.</p>
<p>Royal Lahaina is more than a place to sleep. It is a thoughtful gateway to experiencing Maui through culture, community, and location. Oceanfront luaus, nightly live music under the Banyan Tree, easy access to world class golf, whale watching from shore, and proximity to West Maui’s top attractions all make it an exceptional home base whether this is your first visit or your fifth. Pro tip: For a nicer stay and privacy, I recommend staying in the <a href="https://www.royallahaina.com/stay/bungalows/" target="_blank">Bungalows</a> versus the tower. </p>
<h2>Golf in Maui: Courses Worth the Splurge</h2>
<figure class="wp-caption alignnone" id="attachment_404359596"><img alt="maui" class="size-medium wp-image-404359596" height="436" src="https://uproxx.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Screenshot-2026-02-11-at-3.44.24-PM.png?w=704" width="704" /><figcaption class="wp-caption-text"><span class="wp-media-credit">THE ROYAL KĀ‘ANAPALI COURSE</span></figcaption></figure>
<p>Golf in Hawaii comes at a premium, but the views and course designs make it worth every dollar. Staying at Royal Lahaina places you next to some of the best golf courses in West Maui.</p>
<p>Just steps from the resort are two championship golf courses:</p>
<p><a href="https://www.kaanapaligolfcourses.com/royal-kaanapali-course/" target="_blank">The Royal Kaʻanapali Course</a> is a classic Robert Trent Jones Sr. design known for rolling fairways, elevation changes, and sweeping ocean views. Hole 10 is a standout and should not be skipped. Arnold Palmer once praised the finishing stretch as one of his favorites anywhere, and well, golfers from all over should know that’s a big accolade.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.kaanapaligolfcourses.com/kaanapali-kai-course/" target="_blank">The Kaʻanapali Kai Course</a> offers a slightly shorter and more forgiving layout while still delivering scenic beauty and strategic play. It is a great option for golfers looking for a relaxed but rewarding round.</p>
<p>A short drive north brings you to Kapalua, home to two of Maui’s most famous courses. The Plantation Course is world renowned and hosts the PGA Tour’s Tournament of Champions each January. Expect dramatic elevation changes and panoramic coastal views.</p>
<p>The Bay Course is more approachable but equally beautiful, winding along the shoreline with several holes hugging the water. So yeah, pack extra balls. </p>
<h2>Top Dining Experiences and Cocktails in Maui</h2>
<figure class="wp-caption alignnone" id="attachment_404359597"><img alt="maui" class="size-medium wp-image-404359597" height="939" src="https://uproxx.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Photo-Jan-22-2026-8-00-51-PM-rotated.jpg?w=704" width="704" /><figcaption class="wp-caption-text"><span class="wp-media-credit">JAYME LAMM</span></figcaption></figure>
<p><a href="https://www.royallahaina.com/lahaina-noon/" target="_blank">Lahaina Noon</a>, located at Royal Lahaina Resort &amp; Bungalows, is one of the best places on property to watch the sunset, and the menu offers something for every appetite. Pro tip: get the breakfast package and prepared to be wowed every morning of your stay.</p>
<p>Don’t miss the oyster shooters, which balance briny freshness with island inspired accompaniments. The crab cakes and steak poke are popular starters that showcase local flavors. The mochi crusted mahi mahi is a top selling dish and for good reason, striking the perfect balance between crispy and tender. The steamed hamachi is light and elegant, while heartier options like the airline chicken and ribeye satisfy after a long beach day. Like I said, something for everyone here.</p>
<p>During whale season, this delicious restaurant becomes a front row seat to whale watching without having to pay anything extra. </p>
<h2>Other Favorite Restaurants in West Maui</h2>
<figure class="wp-caption alignnone" id="attachment_404359598"><img alt="maui" class="size-medium wp-image-404359598" height="939" src="https://uproxx.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Photo-Jan-21-2026-6-15-23-PM-rotated.jpg?w=704" width="704" /><figcaption class="wp-caption-text"><span class="wp-media-credit">JAYME LAMM</span></figcaption></figure>
<p><a href="https://www.monkeypodkitchen.com/" target="_blank">Monkeypod Kitchen</a> at Whalers Village is famous for its award-winning Mai Tai topped with passion fruit cold foam. It is easily one of the best (and prettiest) cocktails in Maui.</p>
<p><a href="https://malatavern.com/" target="_blank">Mala Ocean Tavern</a>, located along Front Street, is a local favorite for fresh seafood and a romantic ambiance. The seared scallops with truffle parmesan risotto are exceptional, as is the surf and turf. Any local fish such as mahi, ono, or ahi is always a great choice here. Their Espresso Martini also took the top spot out of 10 or so taste tests throughout west Maui. Make reservations here as it’s a tiny spot right on the water. </p>
<p><a href="https://www.hyatt.com/hyatt-regency/en-US/oggrm-hyatt-regency-maui-resort-and-spa/dining" target="_blank">Japengo at the Hyatt Regency</a> offers fresh sushi, sashimi, and Asian-inspired dishes right on the beach. The garlic beef noodles are a standout entree and a crowd favorite.</p>
<p>A stop at <a href="https://mamasfishhouse.com/" target="_blank">Mama’s Fish House</a> is practically a Maui rite of passage. Reservations are essential and should be made well in advance. The experience is as much about the setting as it is about the food.</p>
<h2>Fun Things To Do in Maui</h2>
<figure class="wp-caption alignnone" id="attachment_404359599"><img alt="maui" class="size-medium wp-image-404359599" height="939" src="https://uproxx.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Photo-Jan-20-2026-5-35-57-PM-rotated.jpg?w=704" width="704" /><figcaption class="wp-caption-text"><span class="wp-media-credit">JAYME LAMM</span></figcaption></figure>
<p>From December through April, Maui becomes one of the best places in the world to see humpback whales. One of the perks of staying at Royal Lahaina Resort &amp; Bungalows is that you don’t need to book a whale watching tour. You can spot whales breaching and tail slapping from the beach, pool area, or even while dining. It is one of the most magical parts of visiting Maui during the winter months. If you have poor eyesight, a dead giveaway is when everyone’s phones are pulled out trying to capture the beauty on video. </p>
<p>Since I always get a rental car when visiting Maui, I’d be remiss if I didn&#8217;t include a sunrise or sunset drive to Haleakalā, for one of Maui’s most unforgettable experiences. Offering sweeping views above the clouds as the sky shifts through dramatic shades of gold, pink, and deep blue. For a truly elevated experience, plan ahead with reservations and warm layers, as the temperatures at the top are often cooler than you expect.</p>
<p>Even if you are not staying at the resort, a visit to the recently renovated <a href="https://www.royallahaina.com/the-spa/" target="_blank">The Spa at Royal Lahaina</a> is worth the trek. Treatments include traditional Hawaiian LomiLomi massage, a flowing and rhythmic style rooted in Hawaiian healing practices. The experience feels both restorative and deeply connected to place, especially with the sound of waves nearby.</p>
<p>One of my favorite things to do in Maui is to slow down and truly relax, letting the island’s beaches and sunsets set the tone for the day and evening. Pack an extra book, take an unplanned nap, or simply sit and watch the light change over the ocean. Maui has a way of reminding you that luxury isn’t always about doing more, but about being present and appreciating the beauty that surrounds you.</p>
<p>If it sounds like I had a lazy and relaxing time in Maui with tons of good food and espresso martinis, I did. And that’s my favorite way to visit Maui.</p>
