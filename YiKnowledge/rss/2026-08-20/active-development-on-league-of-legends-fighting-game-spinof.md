Riot Games' fighting game take on the world of <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/league-of-legends">League of Legends</a>, <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/project-l">2XKO</a>, is winding down development. The developer shared word today that active development on the game is ending December this year, citing a lack of a strong enough adoption and retention rate for the <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/best-fighting-games">fighting game</a>.
