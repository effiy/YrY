<p>You also get 10-bit color, GP-Log2, and HyperSmooth stabilization, so the camera is aimed as much at creators who plan to edit their footage as it is at someone mounting it to a bike or helmet. That said, you will need to supply your own microSD card, and the highest 240Mbps recording mode requires a sufficiently fast card. On the plus side, the body of the Mission 1 Pro is waterproof up to 66 feet without an extra housing, and mounting is flexible thanks to GoPro&rsquo;s folding fingers, a standard tripod thread, and magnetic mounting support. Battery life is also a meaningful upgrade&mdash;according to this <a href="https://www.pcmag.com/reviews/gopro-mission-1-pro" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="open in a new window"><u>PCMag review</u></a>, the camera recorded 4K60 continuously for nearly two hours without overheating. This is especially useful for long rides or travel days when constantly changing batteries would get annoying.</p><p>All said, the main question is whether you actually need this much camera&mdash;at $599, the Mission 1 Pro is still expensive, and the standard <a href="https://zdcs.link/zn75DD?pageview_type=RSS&amp;template=content&amp;module=content_body&amp;element=offer&amp;item=text-link&amp;element_label=Mission%201&amp;object_type=content&amp;short_url=zn75DD&amp;u=https%3A%2F%2Flifehacker.com%2Ffeed%2Frss" rel="sponsored" target="_blank" title="open in a new window"><u>Mission 1</u></a> costs $100 less while covering most casual <a href="https://www.pcmag.com/picks/the-best-action-cameras" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="open in a new window"><u>action-camera</u></a> use. The Pro also skips the add-on lens support available on the <a href="https://lifehacker.com/tech/gopro-hero-13-black-review" target="_blank"><u>Hero13 Black</u></a>, so anyone already invested in those accessories loses some flexibility. And while GoPro&rsquo;s Quik app and cloud integration make backups and editing easier, the (optional) subscription adds another recurring cost.</p><hr /><div class=" relative flex justify-center py-16 md:left-1/2 md:w-[780px] md:max-w-max md:-translate-x-1/2">
