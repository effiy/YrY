<h2 id="hilight-studio-makes-the-pixel-11-pros-led-useful">'HiLight Studio' makes the Pixel 11 Pro's LED useful</h2><p>Enter: <a href="https://github.com/DhananjayBhosale/hilight-studio" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="open in a new window">HiLight Studio</a>, a new app from developer DhananjayBhosale. <a href="https://x.com/Dhananjay_Tech/status/2090390050181960105?s=20" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="open in a new window">DhanajayBhosale's announcement appeared on my X feed</a>, and it immediately caught my attention. The developer designed the app to take full advantage of Google's new hardware, even if Google isn't taking advantage itself. With&nbsp;HiLight Studio, you can assign colors and effects for notifications from any app on your Pixel, not just contacts. Chrome alerts can be blue, WhatsApp alerts can be green, and so on. In addition, you can effectively choose any shade of any color that Android allows, down to saturation and intensity levels. </p><p>There are also five lighting effects you can choose from: "Rainbow," "Comet," "Pulse," "Breathe," and "Wave," plus "Random" if you want to be surprised. These effects go beyond choosing a solid color: An alert could light up HiLight rainbow, or pulse in red. You can choose how long the colors and effects stay on for after each notification, and when to prevent HiLight from activating (e.g. while the screen is off, during Do Not Disturb, or when the battery hits a percentage you designate). You can save your presets and export them to other apps, and you can access your settings through a Quick Settings tile.</p><p>To be clear, this is not an app you can download from the Play Store. Instead, you need to install the APK from GitHub, which in turn needs shell-level access to your Pixel's lights service. That involves either setting up through Shizuku or ADB. DhanajayBhosale has instructions for each method, but keep in mind that this is a more technical approach than a simple app download. Luckily, there's no need to root your Pixel for this to work.</p>
