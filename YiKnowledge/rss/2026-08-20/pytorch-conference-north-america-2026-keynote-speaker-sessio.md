<p><img alt="PyTorch Conference North America 2026" class="aligncenter wp-image-119439 size-full" height="630" src="https://pytorch.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/PyTorch-Conference-North-America-2026.png" width="1200" /></p>
<p>The keynote lineup is set for <strong>PyTorch Conference North America 2026</strong>, taking place October 20–21 in San Jose, California. The program includes sessions on PyTorch updates, native PyTorch on Trainium, agent workloads, model customization, open science, and more.</p>
<p>Featured keynote speakers and their scheduled sessions include:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Mark Collier, PyTorch Foundation</strong>: <em>Welcome to PyTorch Conference North America</em> and <em>Welcome Back</em></li>
<li><strong>Alban Desmaison and Edward Yang, Meta</strong>: <em>PyTorch Updates</em></li>
<li><strong>Maen Suleiman, Amazon Web Services</strong>: <em>Sponsored Keynote: Trainium’s Journey to Native PyTorch</em></li>
<li><strong>Sara Hooker, Adaption</strong>: <em>Beyond Brute Force: The Era of Adaptive Intelligence.</em></li>
<li><strong>Jana van Greunen, Meta</strong>: <em>Sponsored Keynote: PyTorch at Meta &#8211; From Internal Workloads to Multi-Silicon Ecosystem</em></li>
<li><strong>Simon Mo, Inferact</strong>: <em>Keynote: Simon Mo, Inferact</em></li>
<li><strong>Brian Stevens, Red Hat</strong>: <em>Keynote: Brian Stevens, SVP and AI CTO, Red Hat</em></li>
<li><strong>Ujval Kapasi, NVIDIA</strong>: <em>Keynote: Ujval Kapasi, VP, AI &amp; HPC Frameworks and Libraries, NVIDIA</em></li>
<li><strong>Mazin Gilbert, Agentic AI Foundation</strong>: <em>Keynote: Mazin Gilbert, Executive Director, Agentic AI Foundation</em></li>
<li><strong>Bill Jia, Google Cloud</strong>: <em>Workload Fungibility in the Age of Agents</em></li>
<li><strong>Chris Lattner, Qualcomm</strong>: <em>Sponsored Keynote: Chris Lattner, EVP of Advanced AI Software and Platforms, Qualcomm</em></li>
<li><strong>Joelle Pineau, Cohere</strong>: <em>Open Science and AI Foundation Models: Allies in the Pursuit of Knowledge</em></li>
<li><strong>Yiwei Song, Crusoe</strong>: <em>Sponsored Keynote: AI Model Customization Platform</em></li>
<li><strong>Mark Saroufim, Core Automation</strong>: <em>Linear Algebra for the Age of Research</em></li>
</ul>
<p>Stay tuned for more.</p>
