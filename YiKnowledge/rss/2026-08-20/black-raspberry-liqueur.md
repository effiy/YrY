<p>Chambord, a liqueur flavored with black raspberries and other secret ingredients, supplies the classic pink tint in the U.S. Open’s iconic cocktail, the Honey Deuce, but homemade black raspberry liqueur, which imparts a magenta hue, really allows the blackcap flavor to shine. Make a batch, store it in a cool, dark place, and use it to add an extra layer of berry depth to cocktails like a <a href="https://www.saveur.com/recipes/basil-chambord-spritz/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_self">basil spritz</a> or <a href="https://www.saveur.com/story/recipes/french-kiss-cocktail/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_self">French Kiss</a>, or enjoy with a splash of soda water.</p>



<p><strong>Featured in “<a href="https://www.saveur.com/ingredient-spotlight/black-raspberries/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_self">Black Raspberries Are the Fleeting Summer Fruit Worth Seeking Out</a>.”</strong></p>


<div class="orgnc-Recipe" id="recipe">
        
                        <div class="yield">
                <b>Makes:</b> About 3 cups             </div>
                            <div class="yield">
                <b>Time</b>: 45 days             </div>
                
            <h3 class="ingredients">Ingredients</h3>
        <div class="ingredients_section">
                            <div class="section">
                                        <ul class="ingredients" id="recipe-ingredients">
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                4  cups black raspberries, fresh or frozen                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                1 cup sugar                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                  One 750-ml. bottle 80-proof vodka                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                  Zest of 2 small oranges, removed in large strips with a vegetable peeler                            </li>
                                                                                <li class="ingredient">
                                2  vanilla beans, split lengthwise                            </li>
                                            </ul>
                    <div class="chicory-order-ingredients"></div>                </div>
                    </div>
                <h3 class="instructions">Instructions</h3>
        <ol class="instructions multiple">
                            <li class="instruction">
                    Divide the berries and sugar evenly between two 32-ounce glass jars. Set aside to macerate for about 30 minutes, then divide the vodka evenly between the jars. Seal and gently shake to combine. Set aside in a cool, dark place, gently shaking the jars every few days to mix, for 30 days.                 </li>
                            <li class="instruction">
                    Divide the orange zest and vanilla beans evenly between the jars. Set aside in the same place, gently shaking every few days, for another 2 weeks.                </li>
                            <li class="instruction">
                    Strain the liqueur to remove the berries, orange zest, and vanilla beans. The liqueur will keep in an airtight container in a cool, dark place for up to 6 months or in the fridge for up to 1 year.                </li>
                    </ol>
    </div>
