<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In July 2025, a protester shot a cop outside an ICE detention facility in North Texas. The federal government alleged the incident was the result of an &#8220;antifa&#8221; terrorist plot involving a network of friends and acquaintances united in leftist beliefs. The case that ensued was chilling: </p>
<span class="__iawmlf-post-loop-links" hidden="hidden"></span>


<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">It didn’t matter that little evidence of a premeditated attack on law enforcement ever emerged. It didn’t matter that every defendant, except the actual shooter, was exonerated of the original attempted murder charges. It didn’t even matter that prosecutors struggled to show the defendants belonged to an “antifa cell.” In the end, they convinced the jury that Batten and most of her co-defendants were guilty of rioting, conspiracy, using explosives, and supporting terrorists.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In a Fort Worth courtroom in June, a right-wing judge&nbsp;<a href="https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/leader-antifa-cell-members-north-texas-sentenced-100-years-prison-terrorist-attack-ice">sentenced Batten</a>&nbsp;to 50 years behind bars—a stark warning to others who might challenge the president’s agenda.&nbsp;</p>
</blockquote>
