<p class="article-paragraph skip">Back in January 2025, <a href="https://www.popsci.com/gear/sennheiser-audiophile-experience-center-tullamore-ireland-he-1-headphones-factory-tour/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">we visited the Sennheiser factory and Audiophile Experience Center in Tullamore, Ireland</a>, where we explored what goes into <a href="https://www.popsci.com/category/headphones/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">headphones</a> that let us get the most out of our music. Having traveled <em>for</em> that Sennheiser sound, we can say we&#8217;re equally excited to travel <em>with</em> that Sennheiser sound. So we&#8217;re excited that Sennheiser&#8217;s natural, nuanced <a href="https://www.popsci.com/category/audio/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">sonic</a> signature and intelligent features first introduced earlier this year in the <a href="https://www.popsci.com/gear/focal-diva-alta-utopia-jl-audio-primacy-sennheiser-momentum-5-fluance-rt87-turntable-product-news/#:~:text=The%20new%20%24399%20Sennheiser%20Momentum%205%20Wireless%20headphones" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">MOMENTUM 5 Wireless headphones</a> have come to an even more pocketable active noise-cancelling counterpart: the <a href="https://us.sennheiser-hearing.com/pages/momentum-true-wireless-5-experience" rel="noreferrer noopener sponsored nofollow" target="_blank">$299.95 MOMENTUM True Wireless 5</a>.</p>

<p class="article-paragraph skip">Redesigned using data that Sennheiser says was derived from thousands of ear models, the new <a href="https://us.sennheiser-hearing.com/pages/momentum-true-wireless-5-experience" rel="noreferrer noopener sponsored nofollow" target="_blank">MOMENTUM True Wireless 5</a> design eschews the last generation&#8217;s boxier build for a finless design that is more curved and compact. This ergonomic enclosure, in turn, allows for a narrower case with a low-friction finish, meaning it&#8217;s easier to slip in and out of your pocket as you weave in and out of <a href="https://www.popsci.com/gear/best-noise-cancelling-headphones-for-airplane-travel/#:~:text=confident%2C%20in%20control.-,Best%20for%20audiophiles%3A%20Sennheiser%20HDB%20630,-Tony%20Ware" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">airport travel</a> or wherever you may need to take a call or just take a minute to regroup.</p>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">Making sure everything from playlists to conference calls sound good are a host of technologies. It starts with the 7mm TrueResponse dynamic transducer that has been adapted from far more expensive <a href="https://www.popsci.com/gear/best-iems/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">IEMs</a>. It&#8217;s fun but not fatiguing nature and 5 Hz to 21 kHz frequency range can be tweaked in the Smart Control Plus app via 8-band EQ (to get it closer to the more resolving, mids-rich <a href="https://www.popsci.com/gear/audio-awards-2025/#:~:text=a%20yearly%20tradition.-,Sennheiser%20HDB%20630%20wireless%20audiophile%20headphones,-Sennheiser" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Sennheiser HDB 630 wireless audiophile headphones</a>, for example). Trickling down from the headphones are Bluetooth 6.0 with SBC, AAC, plus Snapdragon Sound with 24-bit/96 kHz aptX Adaptive and aptX Lossless for high-resolution/bit-perfect reproduction. There&#8217;s native support for Google Fast Pair, Microsoft Swift Pair, and multipoint switching, and LE Audio/Auracast features will come in a future firmware update. On top of that, the RF antenna is said to be even more resilient with a selectable &#8220;robust mode&#8221; for crowded environments. Less dropouts FTW. </p>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">Eight sensors total feed the improved <a href="https://www.popsci.com/reviews/best-noise-cancelling-earbuds/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">noise-canceling system</a> in these IP54 earbuds: six mics and two bone-conduction accelerometers to analyze the ambient situation, filter out chaos, feed AI speech-extraction algorithms to isolate voice calls, and mitigate wind turbulence for speech and Adaptive ANC stability. In addition, <a href="https://www.popsci.com/gear/dolby-atmos-cadillac-akg-maroon-5-immersive-live-in-car-audio-feature/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Dolby Atmos spatial audio</a> with head tracking adds extra immersion to your personal bubble.</p>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">But the thing that lets you get more out of the MOMENTUM True Wireless 5 is something you can take out of it: user-serviceable batteries.</p>

<p class="article-paragraph skip">Both the batteries in the MTW5 (which are rated for 12 hours ANC off/6 hours ANC on) and in the Qi-enabled wireless charging case (which brings life total to 40 hours) are user-replaceable with a simple provided tool. This means you won&#8217;t be forced into an unplanned upgrade if those reported playback numbers start coming up short in a couple years. It all boils down to more flagship, more fun.</p>
