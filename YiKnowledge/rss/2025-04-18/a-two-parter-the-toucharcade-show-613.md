Their new game is called <em>Primrows</em>, it&#8217;s one <a href="https://toucharcade.com/2024/07/25/primrows-announcement-and-trailer/">we&#8217;ve talked about before</a>, and it&#8217;s available now on mobile and desktop. <p>As for this week&#8217;s show, well, it was a hectic one. Internet issues caused us to split the recording into two parts with the first half recorded yesterday and the second half recorded today.
