<p>Back in April we <a href="https://toucharcade.com/2024/04/02/super-farming-boy-is-harvest-moon-on-steroids-and-is-heading-to-ios-android-pc-and-consoles-soon/">took a look at the trailer</a> for <em>Super Farming Boy</em> from developer LemonChili. It took the basic idea of a cozy farming game&#8211;planting crops, harvesting, building out your own little dream property&#8211;and amped it up with lighting fast arcade-style gameplay mechanics and a devious antagonist to fight against. They dubbed it &#8220;<em>Harvest Moon</em> on steroids" and I got that same sort of vibe from watching the trailer. You play as a boy with super powers (your name is literally Super) so you&#8217;re able to zip around your farm harvesting crops, and the act of harvesting can trigger cool combos and chain reactions. Check out the trailer if you missed it before. </p>

<p>Anyway, I love the idea of a farming sim turned up to 11, and this week LemonChili has announced a more specific roadmap for the release of <em>Super Farming Boy</em> and have even put the iOS version <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/super-farming-boy/id6447262835">up for pre-order on the App Store</a>. The launch isn&#8217;t exactly close, and the plan as of now is to release in Early Access in Q2 of next year with a full launch to follow afterwards. That&#8217;s definitely a while away, but if you&#8217;re really sure this is the type of game that&#8217;s up your alley then pre-ordering the mobile version now will net you a 20% discount off the price, so there&#8217;s some incentive. You can also check out how the game is shaping up thanks to a playable Windows demo on both <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/659300/Super_Farming_Boy/">Steam</a> and <a href="https://lemonchilisoft.itch.io/super-farming-boy">Itch.io</a>. Whether you feel like taking the plunge on the pre-order or not, <em>Super Farming Boy</em> is definitely one you&#8217;ll want to keep your eye out for next year.</p>
