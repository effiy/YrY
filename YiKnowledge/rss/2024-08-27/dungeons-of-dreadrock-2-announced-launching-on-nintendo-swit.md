<p>About two and a half years ago we were treated to a lovely gaming experience titled <em>Dungeons of Dreadrock</em> from developer Christoph Minnameier. The game was a dungeon crawler inspired by the likes of classics such as <em>Dungeon Master</em> and <em>Eye of the Beholder</em>, but played from a top-down perspective rather than a first-person one. It also had a more puzzle-like focus in the designs of its 100 different levels, each one a different floor in the dungeon you were descending in order to rescue your brother. <em>Dungeons of Dreadrock</em> was quite challenging, with some levels almost feeling like logic puzzles as you sussed out when exactly to activate a trap or how to approach a certain batch of enemies. <a href="https://toucharcade.com/2022/03/08/dungeons-of-dreadrock-ios-review-iphone-ipad-mobile/">We loved the game in our review</a>, and <em>Dungeons of Dreadrock</em> went on to release on basically every other gaming platform where it was similarly well-received. And now we&#8217;ve got a sequel to look forward to. Say hello to <em>Dungeons of Dreadrock 2 &#8211; The Dead King&#8217;s Secret</em>.</p>

<p>That red background and big &#8216;ol Switch logo with the familiar finger snapping sound is an indication that this time around this new <em>Dungeons of Dreadrock</em> game is targeting Nintendo&#8217;s platform first, and according to <a href="https://dungeons-of-dreadrock.com">the game&#8217;s website</a> it&#8217;ll be launching on the Switch&#8217;s eShop on November 28th of this year. Fear not though! A PC version is also planned, and in fact can be <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/2995650/Dungeons_of_Dreadrock_2__The_Dead_Kings_Secret/">wishlisted on Steam</a> as we speak, and iOS and Android versions are also in the cards. It&#8217;s not super clear exactly when we&#8217;ll be getting <em>Dungeons of Dreadrock 2 </em>on mobile, but it makes me happy just knowing that it&#8217;s coming. Once more news on release dates for other platforms materializes we&#8217;ll be sure to update you.</p>
