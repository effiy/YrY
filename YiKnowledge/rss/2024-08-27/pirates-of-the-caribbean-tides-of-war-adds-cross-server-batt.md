<p>JOYCITY has launched an exciting new update for <em>Pirates of the Caribbean: Tides of War</em>, inviting everyone to join in on thrilling conquests against other Captains all over the world. In particular, the Empire Invasion event offers server-versus-server competitions that will have you vying for first place in exchange for valuable pirate treasure.</p>
<p>Gear up for an all-out sea brawl &#8211; can you fight to stay afloat and sink those enemy ships across servers? After the matchmaking phase, you&#8217;ll need to clear missions in the Eve Festival preliminary round to enjoy some handy buffs for the final battle. This includes server-wide perks and the ability to switch between servers &#8211; a handy trick to have up your sleeve when you&#8217;re conquering the opposing Empire&#8217;s territories.</p>
<p><img alt="" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-328282" height="1080" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/empire-invasion-1920x1080-copy.jpg" width="1920" /></p>
<p>During the Invasion Conquest, victorious Captains can switch to the opposing server and wreak havoc in Port Royal, while defenders need to hold their ground and remain in their Empire to see if they can survive the attack. Whoever wins the Empire Invasion Port Royal Conquest will not only grab bragging rights but will also nab awesome in-game goodies such as special base skins to boot.</p>
<p>If all that sounds absolutely fabulous to you, you can head on over to <em>Pirates of the Caribbean: Tides of War&#8217;s</em> <a href="https://tidesofwar.joycity.com/en">official website</a> and start your naval dominance today.</p>
