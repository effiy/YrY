<h3><strong>Select New Releases</strong></h3>
<h3><strong>Fragrant Story and Papaya&#8217;s Path ($7.99)</strong></h3>
<p><img alt="" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-328216" height="562" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/fragrantstoryandpapayaspath.jpg" width="1000" /></p>
<p>Ah, the sordid tale of <em>Fragrant Story</em>, one of the would-be final Nintendo 3DS releases. There&#8217;s a lot of confusion out there around this game, so let me sort it out. Despite being a seemingly star-studded tactical role-playing game, those who bought the game on its release were surprised to get a game that lasted all of twenty minutes. The real story? Nintendo&#8217;s deadline for 3DS releases was coming quickly, so the developer pushed an unfinished game to the eShop. It was later updated into its intended from, a far more substantial game that lasts more than ten hours. So don&#8217;t worry if you see anything about this game being ridiculously short. It&#8217;s not. This version has the updates already packed in, and is a pretty solid pick-up for eight bucks if you&#8217;re into the genre.</p>
<h3><strong>Quack Jump ($3.99)</strong></h3>
<p><img alt="" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-328217" height="562" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/quackjump.jpg" width="1000" /></p>
<p>A simple platformer, but a functional one. It tries to keep things fresh through its 40-level run by sprinkling in new gimmicks as you go. For four dollars, it&#8217;s amusing enough.</p>
<h3><strong>Underground Station ($7.90)</strong></h3>
<p><img alt="" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-328218" height="562" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/undergroundstation.jpg" width="1000" /></p>
<p>Something of an idle game where you&#8217;re trying to repay your debts by working in a dungeon. It doesn&#8217;t look very fancy, but on a day where we have to wade through Moist Editions of AI-generated bikini anime girls, we take what we can get.</p>
