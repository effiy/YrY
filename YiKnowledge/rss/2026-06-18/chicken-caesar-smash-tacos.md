<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In other words, my dream come true. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I&#8217;ve seen a few videos for this method, and I felt like I needed to give it a try, and I WAS CORRECT. What a genius little dinner invention for people who love chicken caesar salad and want to eat it in every available form (it&#8217;s me)!</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">These are crispy taco-like handhelds, golden and crispy outsides that kind of shatter when you bite into them, with seasoned ground chicken and shreddy Caesar salad + pepperoncini smashed inside?!</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Dunked in a Caesar sauce?!?!</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Holy smokes! They are so yummy. And so easy. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">These fall in the same family as the <a href="https://pinchofyum.com/crispy-black-bean-tacos-with-cilantro-lime-sauce" id="71464">black bean crispies</a> or the <a href="https://pinchofyum.com/pizza-burgers" id="141105">pizza burgers</a> &#8211; quick little hitters that require very little from you but actually end up being some of the meals you look forward to most.</p>

									</div>
				<div class="tasty-recipes-units-scale-container"><span class="tasty-recipes-convert-container">

			<div>
				<p><strong>Caesar Sauce:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><span class="nutrifox-quantity">1/2</span> <span class="nutrifox-unit">cup</span> <strong>mayo</strong></li>
<li><span class="nutrifox-quantity">1/4</span> <span class="nutrifox-unit">cup</span> <strong>plain yogurt</strong></li>
<li><span>1 tablespoon</span> <strong>Dijon</strong></li>
<li><span>1 tablespoon</span> <strong>Worcestershire sauce</strong></li>
<li><span>1 tablespoon</span> <strong>lemon juice</strong></li>
<li><span>1</span> clove <strong>garlic</strong>, grated</li>
<li><strong>salt</strong> to taste</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Caesar Salad: </strong></p>
<ul>
<li><span>1/2</span> <strong>head romaine</strong>, finely shredded <em>(<span>2</span>&#8211;<span>3</span> cups)</em></li>
<li><span class="nutrifox-quantity">1/2</span> <span class="nutrifox-unit">cup</span> <strong>pepperoncini</strong>, sliced</li>
<li><span class="nutrifox-quantity">1/4</span> <span class="nutrifox-unit">cup</span> freshly grated <strong>Parmesan cheese</strong></li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Chicken Caesar Smash Tacos:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><span class="nutrifox-quantity">1</span> <span class="nutrifox-unit">lb</span>. <strong>ground chicken</strong></li>
<li><span>1 tablespoon</span> <strong>Trader Joe&#8217;s aglio olio seasoning</strong>, or any chicken seasoning</li>
<li><strong>salt</strong> and <strong>pepper</strong> to taste</li>
<li><span>6</span>&#8211;<span>8</span> <strong>small flour tortillas</strong> <em>(I use La Banderita &#8220;Street Taco&#8221; tortillas for this)</em></li>
<li><strong>avocado oil</strong> for frying</li>
</ul>			</div>

		<div class="tasty-recipes-instructions">
		<div class="tasty-recipes-instructions-header">
			<h3>Instructions</h3>
					</div>
		<div>
			<ol>
<li id="instruction-step-1"><strong>Make Your Caesar Sauce:</strong> Whisk sauce ingredients until smooth. <img alt="Whisking caesar dressing in a bowl." class="aligncenter wp-image-170785 size-full" height="1067" src="https://pinchofyum.com/tachyon/Caesar-Sauce.jpg" width="1600" /></li>
<li id="instruction-step-2"><strong>Mix Your Salad:</strong> Toss romaine, pepperoncini, parmesan, and about half of the Caesar sauce until well-combined.<em> (For the remaining sauce &#8211; whisk it with 1-2 tablespoons of water to make it smoother for dunking.) <img alt="Tossing caesar salad in a bowl." class="aligncenter wp-image-170784 size-full" height="1067" src="https://pinchofyum.com/tachyon/Caesar-Salad.jpg" width="1600" /><br /></em></li>
<li id="instruction-step-3"><strong>Prep the Tacos:</strong> Mix the seasoning into the ground chicken. Place your tortillas on a large cutting board or tray; spread each tortilla with some of the ground chicken mixture, pushing it all the way to the edges or even slightly past. <img alt="Spreading ground chicken mixture on tortillas." class="aligncenter wp-image-170782 size-full" height="1067" src="https://pinchofyum.com/tachyon/Chicken-on-Smash-Tacos.jpg" width="1600" /></li>
<li id="instruction-step-4"><strong>Smash and Fry the Tacos:</strong> Heat a thin layer of oil over medium heat (2 tablespoons or more will give you the best crispy crust). Working in batches, place the tortilla <em>chicken-side down</em> and press down with a spatula for 15-30 seconds. Cook for another 2-3 minutes until the chicken is browned and fully cooked. Flip to the other side for another minute to get a bit of crisping on the back side of the tortilla. <em><img alt="Frying smash tacos in a skillet." class="aligncenter wp-image-170779 size-full" height="1067" src="https://pinchofyum.com/tachyon/Frying.jpg" width="1600" /><br /></em></li>
<li id="instruction-step-5"><strong>Almost Done:</strong> Remove tacos from skillet and fold in half (the tortilla may crackle a bit when you fold them &#8211; that&#8217;s fine!). If needed, place on a baking sheet and pop in a warm oven until you’re ready to serve. When you&#8217;re ready to eat, stuff the Caesar salad inside the tacos! <img alt="Adding caesar salad to smash tacos." class="aligncenter wp-image-170788 size-full" height="1707" src="https://pinchofyum.com/tachyon/crispy-chicken-caesar-smash-tacos-9-scaled.jpg" width="2560" /></li>
<li id="instruction-step-6"><strong>Serving:</strong> Serve alongside a dish of the remaining Caesar sauce for dipping. YUM! <img alt="Dipping smash tacos into caesar dressing." class="aligncenter wp-image-170780 size-full" height="1067" src="https://pinchofyum.com/tachyon/Dipping-Chicken-Caesar-Tacos.jpg" width="1600" /></li>
</ol>
