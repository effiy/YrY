	<p>We&#8217;re in the sweet spot of the year, right now. Long days, longer nights &#8212; peak festival season and the start of summer. Everyone you know is off to Europe or a rave or <a href="https://uproxx.com/music/the-best-music-festivals-still-to-come-in-2025/">both at once</a>, depending on the circles you travel in.</p>
<p>And if far-flung travel isn&#8217;t in the cards these next few months, our hope for you is that relaxation still<em> is</em>. Summer used to be lazy. But inflation and fears about AI-related job loss have killed that little fantasy. Still, the idea of curating a beautiful life and feeling chilllll during the long warm nights isn&#8217;t completely shot.</p>
<p>It&#8217;s something we all should aspire to, angst be damned.</p>
<p>That&#8217;s what this installation of our famous gift guides is about. We built the guide around pieces with vintage European energy — Italian Riviera, terry-cloth-and-Negroni, Newman-and-Redford-squinting-into-the-Mediterranean type vibes. Then we added some rave-ready lingerie and self-care tech because one or both of those directions might be your heading over the next few months, and we didn&#8217;t want to leave anyone in the cold.</p>
<p>Check the items below, then check your bank balance. Because while many of the items here draw from midcentury design cues, that&#8217;s the only thing <em>mid</em> you should tolerate about summer &#8217;26.</p>
