<p>In this week&#8217;s episode of The TouchArcade Show, we talk about the site&#8217;s closure and what that will mean moving forward. We plan to still do this here podcast, but there are a few logistical things to figure out first. Most likely we will be pivoting to have our Patreon be the new home of the podcast, so be sure to at least <a href="http://patreon.com/toucharcade">join up as a free member over on our Patreon</a> so that you&#8217;ll be sure to get any news or updates regarding the future of the show. And, just because it wouldn&#8217;t be a TouchArcade Show without off-topic banter, we weigh in on the hock tua girl&#8217;s new gig and give you our thoughts about all of Apple&#8217;s new gadgets that were launched this month. And we read emails!</p>
<p>Don&#8217;t forget to shoot us emails with any questions, feedback, or anything else relevant or irrelevant to <a href="mailto:podcast@toucharcade.com">podcast@toucharcade.com</a>. We read &#8217;em all, and <strong>love</strong> decoding messages written entirely in emoji. As always, you can listen to us with the links below&#8230; And if you like what you hear, please subscribe and/or drop us a review in iTunes. Much appreciated!</p>
<p>As a companion to this audio podcast, we also do a video version of the same show that is <a href="http://patreon.com/toucharcade">exclusive to Patreon</a> which allows you to see us playing the games we&#8217;re talking about.
