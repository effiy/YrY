<h3><strong>Select New Releases</strong></h3>
<h3><strong>Operation: Polygon Storm ($14.99)</strong></h3>
<p><img alt="" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-328071" height="562" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/operationpolygonstorm.jpg" width="1000" /></p>
<p>A relatively basic real-time base defense game, just from a slightly different point of view than the one we usually see in this genre. I wouldn&#8217;t exactly call it visually impressive, but the destructible environments do add a little spice to an otherwise straightforward take. Not my genre of choice, but if it&#8217;s yours then you might want to give it a closer look.</p>
<h3><strong>Thermonuclear ($7.99)</strong></h3>
<p><img alt="" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-328072" height="562" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/thermonuclear.jpg" width="1000" /></p>
<p>Set in a distant future where a third World War has broken out, <em>Thermonuclear</em> casts you as a member of a megacorporation out to claim victory. You have access to a powerful new technology called Thermonuclear, an advanced combat cyborg. Guide your cyborg through procedurally-generated stages, engaging in turn-based strategic combat with the enemies found within. You&#8217;ll upgrade your cyborg as you go, and if you&#8217;re lucky you might just be able to take down the Allfather AI and save the world. If not? Well, you can always try again. Seems decent from what I&#8217;ve seen so far.</p>
<h3><strong>Magnus Trilogy ($8.99)</strong></h3>
<p><img alt="" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-328073" height="591" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/magnustrilogy.jpg" width="1050" /></p>
<p>A series of visual novels in the <em>Magnus</em> series, bundled together in one simple package. These lean more towards the bizarre and psychological end of the curve. At the very least, you can&#8217;t argue with the price per title here. Not for everyone, but some will get a lot out of this.</p>
