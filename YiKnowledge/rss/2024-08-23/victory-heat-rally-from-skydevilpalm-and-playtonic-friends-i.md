<p>Playtonic Friends just announced that <em>Victory Heat Rally</em> from developer Skydevilpalm is coming to mobile through Crunchyroll and also on Steam this October. <em>Victory Heat Rally</em> is an arcade racer with stylish visuals. I aims ot be a 2.5D pixel perfect racer featuring 12 drivers, solo play, multiplayer, a world tour mode, and more. It also features tons of customization across paint jobs, parts, and more. Playtonic Friends also confirms that <em>Victory Heat Rally</em> will be playable on Switch at Gamescom.
