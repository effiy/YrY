<blockquote cite="https://jeremymorrell.dev/blog/extensible-software-in-the-age-of-llms/"><p>My hypothesis is that <strong>there is a new opportunity for Extensible Software on the web</strong>. LLMs radically lower the cost of authoring extensions, and modern sandbox primitives lower the deployment cost and provide good security boundaries. We can build our app as a solid, accountable core, and allow users to safely extend it in many directions by having LLMs fill in the missing pieces. <strong>We can give our users super powers.</strong></p></blockquote>
