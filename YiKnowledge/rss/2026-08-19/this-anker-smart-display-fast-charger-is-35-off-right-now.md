<p>The 45W output should be enough to fast-charge many phones and tablets, although the actual speed will depend on the device and cable you use. Under its test conditions, an <a href="https://zdcs.link/QWJdXl?pageview_type=RSS&amp;template=content&amp;module=content_body&amp;element=offer&amp;item=text-link&amp;element_label=iPhone%2017%20Pro&amp;object_type=content&amp;short_url=QWJdXl&amp;u=https%3A%2F%2Flifehacker.com%2Ffeed%2Frss" rel="sponsored" target="_blank" title="open in a new window"><u>iPhone 17 Pro</u></a> reached 50% in about 20 minutes (according to the manufacturer), so there is enough power here for a quick top-up before heading out. There&rsquo;s also a Care Mode, which is an interesting feature, especially if you tend to leave your phone plugged in overnight&mdash;Anker says it can keep a phone&rsquo;s battery temperature up to nine degrees Fahrenheit cooler, potentially helping extend its battery life. As for the display, it's informative but not touch-enabled&mdash;you navigate it by tapping or holding the charger itself&mdash;and it can rotate 180 degrees (not 90 degrees).</p><p>For travel, the compact design may end up being more useful than the screen. The charger is 47% smaller and 36% lighter than Anker&rsquo;s original <a href="https://zdcs.link/aXJV6x?pageview_type=RSS&amp;template=content&amp;module=content_body&amp;element=offer&amp;item=text-link&amp;element_label=30W%20model&amp;object_type=content&amp;short_url=aXJV6x&amp;u=https%3A%2F%2Flifehacker.com%2Ffeed%2Frss" rel="sponsored" target="_blank" title="open in a new window"><u>30W model</u></a>, and the folding prongs make it easier to fit into crowded outlets or power strips. You only get one USB-C port, so it is not ideal if you want to charge several devices at once, and there is no cable included in the box. Samsung users should also check their phone&rsquo;s charging requirements before buying, since some Galaxy models need 5A PPS support to reach their fastest charging speeds, and this charger does not provide that.</p><hr /><div class=" relative flex justify-center py-16 md:left-1/2 md:w-[780px] md:max-w-max md:-translate-x-1/2">
