<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The supernatural horror movie <em><strong><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/insidious_out_of_the_further">Insidious: Out of the Further</a></strong> </em>opens this weekend, and the first reviews are now online. This sixth entry in the <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/insidious">Insidious</a> </em>franchise serves as a prequel to the previous installment, <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/insidious_the_red_door">Insidious: The Red Door</a></em>, which was a direct sequel to <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/insidious_chapter_2">Insidious: Chapter 2</a></em>, and it features <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/lin_shaye">Lin Shaye</a> continuing her role as the medium Elise Rainier. While it’s not going to have the lowest Tomatometer score of the series, thanks to its general consistency for the fanbase and one noteworthy sequence, <em>Out of the Further </em>doesn’t seem to stand out in a year of more original horror releases.</p>
