<div class="upx-component upx-embed-cnx"><div class="cnx-embed-holder">&amp;&amp;&amp;&amp;</div></div><p><a href="https://uproxx.com/music/will-i-am-visionaries-grammy-event/" target="_blank">will.i.am</a> and MC Hammer have a lot in common. They&#8217;ve both sold millions of albums and <a href="https://www.instagram.com/uproxx/reel/DZ71twmupBL/" target="_blank">wracked up billions of streams combined</a>, sure, but they&#8217;ve also pushed — and <a href="https://uproxx.com/music/jarret-myer-will-i-am-fyi-podcast/" target="_blank">continue to push</a> — the boundaries of music, technology, and popular culture at large into bold new directions. </p>
<p>In the latest episode of <em>Uproxx</em>&#8216;s series Visionaires Moments, <a href="https://uproxx.com/music/will-i-am-harvard-business-school-program/" target="_blank">will.i.am</a> gives his flowers to the man known across the globe as simply Hammer.</p>
<p>&#8220;He gave us the path to cartoons, movies, MTV Music Awards, Grammys,&#8221; will.i.am said about the &#8220;U Can&#8217;t Touch This&#8221; and &#8220;2 Legit 2 Quit&#8221; rapper. &#8220;He&#8217;s the first freaking hip-hop global Michael Jackson phenomenon. You get no bigger than Hammer.&#8221;</p>
<p>Back in the day, a young MC Hammer worked for the Oakland Athletic&#8217;s baseball team and would perform at clubs while on the road with the team. He later shared what he learned from being around baseball scouts with will.i.am.</p>
<p>&#8220;He&#8217;s like, &#8216;I scout all the IT colleges. I go to Georgia Tech, Caltech.&#8217; I&#8217;m like, &#8216;Wait, what?&#8217; He&#8217;s like, &#8216;Yeah, that&#8217;s where you find the talent. Then I bring that talent up here to the Bay. That&#8217;s why I&#8217;m MC Hammer up here.&#8217; I&#8217;m like, &#8216;This dude done crack the code,'&#8221; will.i.am shared. &#8220;So, this concept of MC Hammer&#8217;s broke. Nah, n*gga, y&#8217;all caught up in the wrong hoopla. MC Hammer, and you will be rewarded. That dude.&#8221;</p>
