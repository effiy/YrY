<p>Like the technical fix-uppery of the <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/stalker-2-gets-serious-performance-and-visual-upgrades-with-its-20-update-testing-confirms-polishing-up-one-of-the-biggest-flawed-gems-in-fps-games">2.0 update</a> it&rsquo;s launching alongside, <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/stalker-2">STALKER 2: Heart of Chornobyl</a>&rsquo;s <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/stalker-2-cost-of-hope">Cost of Hope</a> expansion arrives with a determinedly furrowed brow; that kind of enthusiastic, if just slightly contrite, look of someone hellbent on righting their own past wrongs.</p>
<p>Of course, most of the open-world shooter&rsquo;s past crimes are <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/stalker-2s-broken-a-life-system-is-finally-working-as-designed-according-to-gsc-game-world">long</a> <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/stalker-2s-latest-patch-once-again-comes-with-four-figures-worth-of-fixes-and-improvements">absolved</a>, if not forgiven immediately on the grounds that they were committed amid <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/how-stalker-2-moves-the-series-forward-and-why-it-reflects-ukraines-rebellious-spirit">an actual war</a>. But Cost of Hope does serve as a second chance for this bleak, beautiful, tense, thrilling, <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/stalker-2-review">imperfectly brilliant</a> game to address certain omissions it made back in 2024 &ndash; even if some of these restorations gleam brighter than others.</p>
