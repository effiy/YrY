<h1 class="wp-block-heading">The Essential Marvel Movies to Watch Before <em>Avengers: Doomsday</em></h1>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>The Latest</strong>: Marvel has announced the official <a href="https://www.disneyplus.com/browse/page-60f4707d-19bb-4c0c-9390-ab269137be50">watchlist</a> to get you ready for <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/avengers_doomsday"><strong><em>Avengers: Doomsday</em></strong></a>. Check out the updated list below, and get ready for DOOM!</p>


<div class="youtube-container"></div>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Are you prepared for Doom?! No, not the apocalypse, but the <em>other</em> major event that’s definitely happening in 2026: <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/avengers_doomsday"><strong>Avengers: Doomsday</strong></a></em>. It’s been seven years since the Avengers finished off Thanos in <em><strong><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/avengers_endgame">Avengers: Endgame</a></strong></em>. Since then, A LOT has happened: Dr. Strange (Benedict Cumberbatch) almost destroyed the multiverse (<em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/doctor_strange_in_the_multiverse_of_madness">Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness</a></em>), the Guardians of the Galaxy appointed Rocket Raccoon as their new leader in <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/guardians_of_the_galaxy_vol_3">Vol. 3</a></em>, and a ragtag group of baddies became the <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/thunderbolts">Thunderbolts</a> (and then rebranded to become The New Avengers).</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If you want to catch up before <em>Doomsday</em>, you don&#8217;t need to watch the entire <a href="https://editorial.rottentomatoes.com/guide/marvel-movies-in-order/">MCU timeline</a> (which now spans dozens of films and television shows). We’ve narrowed it down to the essential Marvel Studios movies and shows that directly set the stage for the next Avengers saga.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Related: </strong><a href="https://editorial.rottentomatoes.com/guide/marvel-movies-in-order/"> How to Watch Marvel Movies &amp; TV Shows in Order</a></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">You won&#8217;t get every easter egg, passing reference, or surprise appearance, like the history between Tobey Maguire&#8217;s Spider-Man and Willem Dafoe&#8217;s Green Goblin in <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/spider_man_no_way_home">Spider-Man: No Way Home</a></em>, or why Jennifer Garner&#8217;s <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/elektra">Elektra</a> pops up and insults Ben Affleck&#8217;s Daredevil in <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/deadpool_and_wolverine">Deadpool &amp; Wolverine</a></em>.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">But if you follow this guide, you&#8217;ll head into theaters armed with the crucial info needed to jump right back into the MCU this December. Put these titles on your watchlist, and prepare to assemble!&nbsp;— <a href="https://editorial.rottentomatoes.com/author/bryce-marrero/"><strong>Bryce Marrero</strong></a></p>
