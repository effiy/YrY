<p class="p1"><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/tom-cruise/"><b>Tom Cruise </b></a>is ready to drive into the fast lane once again after announcing he is reprising his beloved <i>Days of Thunder </i>role in a sequel film.</p>
<p class="p1">“Days of Thunder. Summer 2028,” Cruise, 64, announced via <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/DcoZAr9sCdj/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><span class="s1">Instagram</span></a> on Saturday, August 29, beside a photo of the action star posing beside pregnant <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/anne-hathaway/"><b>Anne Hathaway </b></a>in front of a neon-green race car at Daytona International Speedway.</p><div class="mb1-wrapper" id="starter">
						<div class="htlad-mb1" id="mb1-static"></div>
						<div class="htlad-leader2" id="leader2-inline-static"></div>
					</div>
<p class="p1">In a follow-up upload, Cruise revealed that Hathaway, 43, will be his new costar in the film.</p>
<p class="p1">“I think it’s on,” he spoke into the camera in <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/DcoaubOBSlX/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><span class="s1">Instagram</span></a> footage. “Annie? It looks ‘on’ to me, don’t you think?”</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/pictures/tom-cruise-through-the-years-movies-marriages-and-more/" target="_blank">Tom Cruise Turns 63: Look Back on His Life and Movie Career</a></p>
<p class="p1">Hathaway, rocking a white cowboy hat and matching motorcycle jacket, noted that the camera did seem to be rolling.</p>
<p class="p1">“Hey,” she waved to the camera with a laugh. “What should we do?”</p>
<p class="p1">After Cruise noted that the two actors should “show ‘em,” they strolled over to the neon car.</p>
<p class="p1">“Now what?” he asked, to which Hathaway proclaimed that they should “make the movie.”</p>
<p class="p1">“I’m gonna have this baby, and then we’re gonna make this movie,” she quipped, referring to <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/stylish/pictures/anne-hathaways-stylish-baby-bump-moments-before-baby-no-3/"><span class="s1">her pregnancy</span></a> with her and husband <b>Adam Shulman</b>’s third child.</p>
<figure class="wp-caption alignnone" id="attachment_4421864" style="width: 300px;"><img alt="Anne-Hathaway-GettyImages-2286150861" class="size-medium wp-image-4421864" height="232" src="https://www.usmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Anne-Hathaway-GettyImages-2286150861.jpg?w=300" width="300" /><figcaption class="wp-caption-text"><span class="caption">Anne Hathaway.</span> <span class="credit">Jamie McCarthy/Getty Images</span></figcaption></figure>
<p class="p1">According to Cruise, it was a “good idea” for Hathaway to give birth pre-production.</p>
<p class="p1">“We’re coming out summer of 2028,” he added. “Don’t be late!”</p>
<p class="p1">Further details for the new <i>Days of Thunder </i>film have not yet been revealed at the time of publication. <a href="https://variety.com/2026/film/news/anne-hathaway-tom-cruise-days-of-thunder-sequel-1236839347/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><span class="s1"><i>Variety</i></span></a><i> </i>reports that the movie will be shot for IMAX screens and released exclusively in theaters for formats including Dolby Cinema and 4DX.</p>
<p class="p1">Per the outlet, Cruise will reprise his role as racecar driver Cole Trickle opposite Hathaway’s character as a new team owner and engineer. <b>Jonathan Levine </b>will direct the film, which Cruise is set to produce alongside <b>Jerry Bruckheimer </b>and <b>Tommy Harper.</b></p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/news/summer-movie-guide-2025-dont-miss-these-blockbusters/" target="_blank">Summer Movie Preview: A Guide to the Must-Watch Films of the Season</a></p>
<p class="p1">Cruise initially played Cole in director <b>Tony Scott</b>’s 1990 hit <i>Days of Thunder</i>, following a rookie NASCAR driver’s rivalry with veteran racer Rowdy Burns (<b>Michael Rooker</b>). After Cole and Rowdy get seriously injured in a race, they soon become fast friends. The film also starred <b>Cary Elwes </b>and <b>Nicole Kidman</b>, who <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/pictures/tom-cruise-and-nicole-kidmans-relationship-timeline/">began dating Cruise</a> after portraying an onscreen couple. Cruise and Kidman, 59, <span class="s1">got married later that year</span> before divorcing in 2001.</p>
<p class="p1">“I was 22, 23 years old and I had this huge movie star husband, but it just seemed completely natural,” Kidman <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/nicole-kidman-comments-on-marrying-tom-cruise-at-23/">told <i>British Vogue </i>in a profile</a> published earlier this month. “We just fell madly in love and it was that simple. I remember people saying to me, ‘OK, well this is really going to affect your career.’ I’m like, ‘I don’t care. I’m in love. I want to be married.’”</p>
<p class="p1">It’s not yet known whether Kidman or any other OG <i>Days of Thunder </i>stars will return for the sequel.</p>
