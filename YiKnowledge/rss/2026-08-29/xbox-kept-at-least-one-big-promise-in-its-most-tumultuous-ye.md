<p class="wp-block-paragraph">When it drastically increased the <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/game-pass-price-hike-announced-now-costs-30-month-for-ultimate/1100-6535138/">price of Game Pass Ultimate</a> on October 1, 2025, Xbox made a promise in a now-deleted Xbox Wire post: “Ultimate subscribers now get access to over 75 day-one releases a year.”&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In the year since, a lot has changed at Xbox. It has hired a new CEO, laid off thousands of developers, spun off several studios, and scaled back its multiplatform plans. This past year has been one of pivots and changes in strategy for Xbox, so I wondered if that day-one release commitment for Game Pass Ultimate fell by the wayside. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Over the past year, I have tracked every title added to Game Pass Ultimate the day it launched. As of Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy’s launch on August 27, 2026, Xbox has met the goal it set for Game Pass Ultimate content last year. Xbox looks very different now than it did a year ago, though, so reflecting on the past year of Game Pass Ultimate also means reflecting on what else has changed at Xbox over that time.&nbsp;</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Following the price-increase announcement and 75+ game commitment, Xbox came out swinging in October. This was the busiest month in terms of first-party and second-party releases on the service over the past year, as Keeper, Ninja Gaiden 4, and The Outer Worlds 2 were all published by Xbox Game Studios. The <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/game-pass-price-hike-announced-now-costs-30-month-for-ultimate/1100-6535138/">Xbox ROG Ally</a> also released this month and was positioned as a portable PC tailor-made for Game Pass. Fans and former <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/xbox-co-founder-calls-game-pass-price-hike-a-betrayal/1100-6535337/">Xbox developers did not take too kindly</a> to the Game Pass Ultimate price hike, though, which turned what could’ve been a triumphant month for Xbox into one of controversy.&nbsp;</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">More day-one games would come to Game Pass Ultimate in November 2025 than any other month. That said, the lineup was mostly made up of indies like Winter Burrow and Total Chaos. The first-party offering of the month was Call of Duty: Black Ops 7, the second Call of Duty game to come to the subscription service the day it was released. Black Ops 7 would receive <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/call-of-duty-black-ops-7-review/1900-6418441/">mixed reviews from critics</a> and the community and didn't make the grand Game Pass splash Xbox needed in the wake of the price hike.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Not many games came to the subscription service in December, though Marvel Cosmic Invasion and Routine had a fair bit of hype. Xbox also did not show anything of note at The Game Awards. The defining Xbox moment of December is when Circana sales report data pointed out <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/black-ops-7s-sales-struggles-come-into-focus/1100-6537040/">Black Ops 7 had underperformed</a> in North America. Activision announced it would no longer release Modern Warfare or Black Ops games <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/call-of-duty-will-no-longer-do-back-to-back-black-ops-or-modern-warfare-games/1100-6536815/">in back-to-back years</a>, a clear indication that the Xbox and Activision Blizzard merger was not going as planned.&nbsp;</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">January of this year would add fewer games than any other month, although MiO: Memories in Orbit is quite good. It was a busy month for Xbox, though, as it held an <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/gallery/xbox-developer-direct-2026-all-the-big-announcements/2900-7421/">Xbox Developer Direct</a> to show off Fable, Forza Horizon 5, Kiln, and Beast of Reincarnation. Previous Xbox leadership did have to answer some questions about their <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/xbox-boss-explains-why-some-games-come-to-ps5-day-one-and-others-dont/1100-6537638/">confusing multiplatform approach</a> and <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/xbox-boss-on-cancelling-games-sometimes-business-decisions-have-to-be-made/1100-6537636/">willingness to cancel games</a>, but January would ultimately prove to be the calm before the storm at Xbox.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In what was an otherwise standard month for Xbox Game Pass, some earth-shattering changes were made at Xbox. Phil Spencer, who had led the Microsoft division since 2014, <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/phil-spencer-leaving-xbox-as-microsoft-ai-boss-takes-over/1100-6538330/">stepped down as CEO of Microsoft Gaming</a> and was replaced by Asha Sharma. Sarah Bond, an executive closely tied to Xbox’s multiplatform strategy, also exited the company. At the time, Sharma wrote in a <a href="https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/02/20/asha-sharma-named-evp-and-ceo-microsoft-gaming/">Microsoft Blog post</a> that she wanted to “recommit to our core Xbox fans and players, those who have invested with us for the past 25 years,” leaving us wondering when, not if, big changes would come to Xbox.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Outside of an Xbox Partner Preview showcase highlighting third-party games, March was pretty quiet on the Xbox Game Pass front. Sharma made a big splash immediately after taking over by announcing <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/xboxs-next-console-project-helix-officially-plays-pc-and-xbox-games/1100-6538610/">Project Helix</a>, Xbox’s next-gen console. The controversial “This is an Xbox” marketing campaign, which deemphasized consoles, was also sunset <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/killing-this-is-an-xbox-campaign-was-new-ceos-decision-report/1100-6538987/">at Sharma’s </a>direction. While component shortages mean we still don’t know much about Project Helix, this month gave us our first indication of what Xbox’s recommitment to itself was.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">May 2026 was the most chaotic month for Game Pass within the past year. In terms of games, Forza Horizon 6 and Subnautica 2 both had very successful launches, while <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/please-dont-skip-this-musical-coming-of-age-story-mixtape-review/1900-6418487/">Mixtape was met with critical acclaim</a>. More importantly, Xbox also made some important changes to Game Pass by backing off its commitment to adding Call of Duty games to the service on day one. In turn, it also brought the monthly price of Ultimate <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/game-pass-gets-price-drop-new-call-of-duty-games-no-longer-included/1100-6539523/">down from $30 to $23</a> (still higher than before the previous price increase).</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">“Our players cover a wide breadth of geographies, preferences, and tastes, so while there isn’t a single model that’s best for everyone, this change responds to a lot of feedback we’ve gotten so far. We’ll continue to listen and learn,” the <a href="https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/04/21/xbox-game-pass-update/">Xbox Wire announcement</a> for this explains. No mention of the 75+ game commitment was made in that post.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">While June was as milquetoast a month as it could be in terms of new Game Pass titles, it was full of massive ups and downs for Xbox. A showcase was held during Summer Game Fest that celebrated Xbox and confirmed a recommitment to Xbox console exclusives with Gears of War: E-Day and Clockwork Revolution. At the time, <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/xbox-game-pass-lost-millions-of-subscribers-after-massive-price-hike-in-2025/">Xbox admitted</a> it had lost “millions” of Game Pass subscribers since the October price increase and had only recently begun to regain them after the price decrease in May. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Then, almost as soon as Summer Game Fest ended, an <a href="https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/06/10/next-100-days-xbox-reset/">Xbox Wire post</a> from Sharma explained that Xbox needed to “reset the business” due to a low “accountability margin,” the hardware-component crisis, and being “over extended” with the number of studios it owns. This sparked lots of speculation and rumors about layoffs, studios being shut down or spun off, and Game Pass <a href="https://playday.one/2026/06/29/xbox-has-frozen-3rd-party-game-pass-deals-per-industry-podcast/">investment disappearing</a> for third-party studios. It was a preview for what was to come in July.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Halo: Campaign Evolved, a remake of Halo: Combat Evolved, was not nearly enough to take attention away from all of the shocking moves Xbox made in July. <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/xbox-layoffs-studio-closures-and-spin-offs-everything-announced-today/">Xbox announced</a> plans to cut 3,200 jobs over the next fiscal year, sold off Ninja Theory and Undead Labs, and spun off Double Fine and Compulsion Games as independent studios.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">As for where Game Pass fits in all of this, <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/new-xbox-ceo-admits-game-pass-bet-has-not-worked-out/">Sharma admitted</a> that Game Pass and other parts of the Xbox business “did not grow at the pace we expected.” <a href="https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-07/behind-xbox-s-big-layoffs-a-streaming-strategy-that-failed">Bloomberg revealed</a> that Xbox had been spending over $1 billion a year on Game Pass-related deals. While Xbox is still committed to Xbox Game Pass, this month made it clear that the plan to center the entire Xbox business around the subscription service truly didn’t pan out as intended.&nbsp;</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-august-2026-4-games-added">August 2026: 4 Games Added</h2>



<ol class="wp-block-list">

<li>Sandustry</li>



<li>Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy</li>



<li>Blood Dungeon</li>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">As of this month, we’re still dealing with the fallout of Xbox’s July layoffs. Both Compulsion and Double Fine have reestablished themselves as independent studios and regained publishing rights to their games, although those titles remain on Xbox’s subscription service for now. Beast of Reincarnation and Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy are some of the bigger day-one releases of the year for Game Pass, although Beast of Reincarnation did not garner<a href="https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/beast-of-reincarnation-isnt-as-cinematic-as-it-thinks-it-is/"> fantastic reviews</a>. </p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-september-2026-at-least-4-games-will-be-added">September 2026: At least 4 Games will be added</h2>



<ol class="wp-block-list">
<li>Minecraft Dungeons 2</li>



<li>Dune: Awakening&nbsp;</li>



<li>Forever Ago</li>



<li>Speedrunners 2</li>
</ol>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Right now, four day-one additions are slated for Game Pass Ultimate. These games will affirm the “over” part of the “over 75 day-one releases” promise that Xbox made last year. As of August 2026, we know that at least <strong>78 games </strong>will have been added to Game Pass Ultimate on day one between October 2025 and September 2026.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Xbox has not made any particularly bold proclamations about the subscription service or its future at Gamescom. It has also neither posted online to boast that it surpassed the 75-game milestone nor publicly committed to delivering over 75 day-one releases on Game Pass Ultimate per year beyond 2026. A source tells GameSpot that the company plans to continue bringing day-one games to the subscription service, though.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A lot has changed at Xbox over the past year, as everything from Xbox Game Studios’ developer count to the price and offerings of Game Pass Ultimate looks very different from just one year ago. Still, in a year full of mayhem for Xbox as a company, it managed to deliver on a Game Pass promise made before all of this chaos ensued.</p>
