<p>Shailene Woodley shared insight into why her performance as Mary Jane Watson didn&#8217;t make it into the final cut of &#8220;The Amazing Spider-Man 2,&#8221; revealing she learned the news a year after filming her scenes with Andrew Garfield.</p>



<p>Woodley addressed being cut from the superhero movie for the “SAG-AFTRA Foundation Conversations” series, where she confirmed – 13 years after she was photographed onset as the comic book character – that she did shoot scenes with Garfield&#8217;s <a href="https://www.thewrap.com/andrew-garfield-avengers-doomsday-appearance-denial/">Peter Parker/Spider-Man</a>. Yet, she indicated that they&#8217;re unlikely to see the light of day.</p>



<p>&#8220;That was a fun phone call,&#8221; she recalled. &#8220;I was cast as Mary Jane for &#8216;Spider-Man,&#8217; which was my dream because &#8230; Spider-Man&#8217;s my favorite superhero. Mary Jane was my favorite character growing up. So, when I got it, I was like, &#8216;This is so exciting.'&#8221;</p>



<p>Per Woodley, she and Garfield filmed &#8220;three scenes&#8221; together, but that she was given the bad news a year later.</p>





<p>&#8220;And then like a year later, they were like, &#8216;So, you&#8217;re not in the movie anymore,'&#8221; she said. &#8220;And I was like, &#8216;What? What do you mean?&#8217; And they were like, &#8216;We&#8217;ve introduced a lot of new characters in this last one, and we&#8217;re going to wait for whatever happens between [Emma Stone&#8217;s Gwen Stacy] and Andrew.&#8221;</p>



<p>Woodley then poked fun at herself for trying to stay spoiler-free for a movie that came out in 2014, adding, &#8220;I&#8217;m so paranoid. All of these years of what I&#8217;m allowed to say or not allowed to say, I&#8217;m like, &#8216;It&#8217;s been out for over 10 years, Shailene.'&#8221;</p>



<p>&#8220;Whatever. They were like, &#8216;Let&#8217;s wait for her to die and then we&#8217;ll introduce Mary Jane,'&#8221; she said &#8220;[It] made sense. But then they didn&#8217;t make that film.&#8221;</p>



<p>Though, Woodley acknowledged that this was what she was told, quipping, &#8220;But again, who knows what the truth is. I could have been terrible and they&#8217;re like, &#8216;We can&#8217;t do this.'&#8221;</p>

<p>While Garfield&#8217;s planned third &#8220;Spider-Man&#8221; movies was ultimately shelved, he has since returned to the character, appearing in 2021&#8217;s &#8220;<a href="https://www.thewrap.com/creative-content/movies/mary-rivera-spider-man-no-way-home-actress-dies-at-82/">Spider-Man: No Way Home</a>&#8221; opposite Tom Holland and fellow Spider-Man/Peter Parker Tobey Maguire.</p>



<p>And he hasn&#8217;t ruled out another return, either. In August, Garfield shared that he&#8217;d be open to putting the suit back on if the script was &#8220;<a href="https://www.thewrap.com/creative-content/movies/andrew-garfield-spider-man-return-idea-road-trip-movie/">very, very odd</a>.&#8221;</p>



<p>“I would need it to be very strange, and I would need it to be completely unconventional and almost — I wouldn’t say boring — but I would want it to be just completely unexpected, like a genre of Spider-Man that you’ve never seen before, that feels very left field,” he said.</p>
