<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	In the clip, McGraw’s cowboy boot appears to get caught in a crevice onstage, sending him to the ground. But the “Humble and Kind” singer quickly got back on his feet and continued singing without missing a beat.</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	McGraw later poked fun at the onstage mishap in a social media post.</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	&#8220;Cincinnati, you started our weekend off on the right foot even if I couldn’t stay on mine,&#8221; he captioned a photo gallery from the show on <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/DcmLQ02iexu/?hl=en&#038;img_index=1" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">Instagram</a>. &#8220;We appreciate you coming out and singing along! Detroit, you’re up!&#8221;</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	Fans quickly took to the comments section to playfully react to the fall.</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	“I bet your daughters blew up the family group chat,” one person wrote. Another joked, “bubble wrap him for tonight.” A third commended the singer for his professionalism, writing, “SO GLAD YOU DIDN’T HURT YOURSELF! YOU ARE A REAL PRO YOU JUMPED RIGHT UP AND DIDN’T EVEN MISS A BEAT.”</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	The Grammy-winning superstar launched his Pawn Shop Guitar Tour in mid-July. The North American trek — featuring support from The Chicks, Lady A, 49 Winchester and Timothy Wayne on select dates — is <a href="https://www.timmcgraw.com/tour" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">scheduled to wrap</a> Sept. 29 in West Palm Beach, Florida. From there, he’ll head to Las Vegas in October for a five-night residency at The Colosseum at Caesars Palace.</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	McGraw did not address the death of iconic country singer <a href="https://www.billboard.com/artist/dolly-parton/">Dolly Parton</a> during Thursday&#8217;s Cincinnati concert, but he paid tribute to her earlier in the week on social media.</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	&#8220;She was an icon and showed us all what was possible, not only as a human but as an entertainer,&#8221; McGraw wrote on <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/Dces2yVJ5i_/?hl=en" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">Instagram</a>. &#8220;She was the blueprint, head and shoulders above anyone that came before her and all those of us that have come after.&#8221;</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	Parton <a href="https://www.billboard.com/music/country/dolly-parton-death-1236324068/">died on Aug. 25</a> following a &#8220;brief battle with cancer,&#8221; according to her team.</p>
