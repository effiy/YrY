<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Delta Air Lines has been growing its presence in Seattle for around a decade now, and has been <a href="https://onemileatatime.com/insights/alaska-delta-battle-seattle/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">trying to steal market share from Alaska Airlines</a>. Seattle is a different market than most, in the sense that many locals are fiercely loyal, and like giving business to companies that they perceive to be part of their community.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">As a result, Delta hasn’t just been growing its presence in Seattle in terms of the destinations it flies to, but has also been trying to appeal to Seattleites in a different way. The Seattle Seahawks have become increasingly popular over the years, and Alaska Airlines has even had a partnership with the team, with former Seahawks quarterback Russell Wilson being Alaska Airlines’ “Chief Football Officer.”</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Naturally Delta has also been trying to take advantage of Seattle’s football success. Since 2016 the airline has been offering “Delta 12Status,” and <a href="https://news.delta.com/first-and-10-12status-program-kicks-decade-awarding-seattle-seahawks-fans" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">it’s returning this year</a>, for the 2026-2027 NFL season.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-delta-12status-registration-amp-benefits">Delta 12Status registration &amp; benefits</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://12status.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">You can register for Delta 12Status here</a>, and it’s available exclusively to Washington state residents 18 years of age and older. Note that if you registered in past years, you’ll have to do so again this year — your registration doesn’t carry over from year to year. Registration closes on February 14, 2027.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Interestingly, this year the registration page isn’t even accessible unless you have an IP from the state of Washington, so if the page doesn’t load for you, that’s why.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img alt="" class="wp-image-371938" height="532" src="https://cdn.onemileatatime.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Delta-12-Status-1.jpg" width="1200" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Delta 12Status offers perks for free</figcaption></figure>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">What are the perks of Delta 12Status?</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>One Delta SkyMile for every yard the Seahawks throw on the field at both home and away games during the 2026-2027 NFL Season</li>



<li>Priority boarding — 12Status members receive Group 5 boarding (main priority) when departing SeaTac Airport (SEA) during the Seahawks 2026-2027 season</li>



<li>A 12% discount at Seahawks Pro Shop retail stores on the 12th of each month during the Seahawks season (September 12, 2026, October 12, 2026, November 12, 2026, December 12, 2026, and January 12, 2027)</li>
</ul>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img alt="" class="wp-image-371939" height="595" src="https://cdn.onemileatatime.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Delta-12-Status-2.jpg" width="1200" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Delta 12Status benefits</figcaption></figure>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">For those of you not familiar, you can <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/12th_man_(football)" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">read about the significance of the number 12 for the Seattle Seahawks here</a>.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-how-many-bonus-delta-skymiles-can-you-earn">How many bonus Delta SkyMiles can you earn?</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The Seahawks typically have somewhere around 4,000 passing yards every season, which would mean you’d earn around 4,000 free SkyMiles this season if results are similar.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Since the program was launched in 2016, Delta has given away over 2.4 billion miles. It’s pretty interesting how fast this program has grown. Prior to three years ago, the total was “only” around 900 million miles, so in three years, the total number of miles given away has nearly tripled.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://onemileatatime.com/guides/redeem-delta-skymiles/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Delta SkyMiles is more or less revenue based</a>, so at the rate of one cent per mile, the 2.4 billion miles are worth around $24 million.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">While 4,000 miles might not sound like that much, it’s pretty cool to think that if you’ve enrolled in this program for around a decade, you’d have around 40,000 miles. That’s actually a material number of miles (save the SkyMiles jokes!).</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-bottom-line">Bottom line</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If you’re a Washington state resident, <a href="https://12status.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">signing up</a> for Delta 12Status is a no brainer. You’ll earn thousands of bonus miles for free (hopefully), and there’s nothing you have to do other than to sign up.</p>
