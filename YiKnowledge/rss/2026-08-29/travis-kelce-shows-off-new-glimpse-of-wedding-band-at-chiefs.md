<p class="p1"><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/travis-kelce/"><b>Travis Kelce </b></a>clearly can’t forget his new hardware while suiting up on the gridiron.</p>
<p class="p1">Kelce, 36, was spotted arriving at Kansas City’s Arrowhead Stadium on Friday, August 28, dressed in a knit Nahmias jersey with camo-printed trousers and a matching baseball cap for the Kansas City Chiefs’ preseason match-up against the Seattle Seahawks. The Chiefs tight end accessorized his ‘fit with a pair of aviator sunglasses, a silver watch, a silver-clasped bracelet and his golden wedding band. (The Chiefs ultimately tied the Seahawks, 9-9.)</p><div class="mb1-wrapper" id="starter">

<p class="p1">Kelce married pop star <b>Taylor Swift</b> in a star-studded ceremony at New York City’s iconic Madison Square Garden arena in early July, <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/sports/news/travis-kelce-flashes-wedding-band-at-1st-preseason-chiefs-game/"><span class="s1">debuting his wedding ring</span></a> at a Chiefs home game earlier this month.</p>
<p class="p1">Just days earlier, Kelce had revealed that the couple’s nuptials were the “best night of my life.”</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/sports/news/travis-kelce-flashes-wedding-band-at-1st-preseason-chiefs-game/" target="_blank">Travis Kelce Flashes Wedding Band at 1st Preseason Chiefs Game</a></p>
<p class="p1">“It was a fun offseason, man,” he gushed to reporters <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/sports/news/travis-kelce-breaks-his-silence-on-taylor-swift-wedding/"><span class="s1">during an August 12 press conference</span></a> during his NFL team’s training camp. “I appreciate everybody who came out and celebrated and had fun with us. That’s about all I really got from that night. It was a crazy night. It was full of a lot of celebration.”</p>
<p class="p1">According to Kelce, it felt especially special to exchanve vows with Swift, 36, inside the legendary Big Apple-based arena.</p>
<p class="p1">“MSG, man, I always told myself I’d go for a playoff game when the [New York] Knicks were rocking, and my wife went when I was stuck in minicamp,” Kelce quipped, referring to <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/taylor-swift-jokes-she-screamed-for-entire-knicks-game/">Swift’s attendance at Game 4</a> of the 2026 NBA Finals earlier this summer. “I didn’t get the opportunity to go then, so it’s kind of cool to get to live out my childhood dream of being in that venue, the mecca of all sports venues, [by] being able to get married in there.”</p>
<p class="p1">He continued, “I can’t thank the owners of MSG [enough] for allowing us to get the opportunity to do that, knowing that we wanted a private event and everything. The A/C was cooking, baby. That was the best part.”</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/sports/news/travis-kelce-arrives-to-camp-for-1st-time-since-wedding/" target="_blank">Travis Kelce Arrives to Chiefs Camp for 1st Time After Marrying Taylor Swift</a></p>
<p class="p1">For the big day, Swift and Kelce each wore custom Christian Dior Haute Couture ensembles in white with corresponding Christian Louboutin shoes. Swift completed her bridal look with Cartier jewelry and a <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/stylish/news/taylor-swift-shows-off-wedding-ring-after-marrying-travis-kelce/"><span class="s1">golden ring stack </span></a>of her own.</p>
<p class="p1">Kelce had previously gifted Swift a custom <b>Kindred Lubeck </b>old-mine cut engagement ring when he proposed in August 2025.</p>
<p class="p1">“He designed it with this amazing jeweler [who] does all of her gold engraving by hand,” Swift recalled of her sparkler <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/stylish/news/taylor-swift-reveals-how-travis-kelce-designed-her-engagement-ring/"><span class="s1">on the U.K.’s <i>Heart</i> radio station</span></a> in October 2025. “I had shown him a video [that] I just thought was so cool. I had shown him a video a year and a half ago, and he was just paying attention to everything, it turns out! When I saw the ring, I was just, like, [gasping]. I was like, ‘I know who made that! I know who made that! And also, you listen to me.’”</p>
<p class="p1">The Grammy winner added at the time, “He did amazing; it was, like, ‘You really know me.’ I didn’t know what I would want, but he did somehow, and that’s kind of a flex.”</p>
<p>Swift is expected to share the first photos from the newlyweds’ ceremony via Instagram at a currently unknown time.</p>
