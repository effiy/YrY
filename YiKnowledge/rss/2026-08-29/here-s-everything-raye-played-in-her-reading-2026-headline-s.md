<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1270" src="https://www.nme.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/raye_reading_2026.jpg" width="2000" /></p><p><a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/raye">RAYE</a> headlined <a href="https://www.nme.com/reading-leeds-festival-2026">Reading Festival</a> for the first time in her career tonight – see the full setlist and footage below.</p>
<ul>
<li><b>READ MORE: RAYE – </b><a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/album/raye-this-music-may-contain-hope-review-3936886"><b>&#8216;This Music May Contain Hope&#8217; review: musical maximalism at its grandest</b></a></li>
</ul>
<p>The South London singer took to the festival&#8217;s main stage – now known as The Grid – at Little John&#8217;s Farm tonight (August 29), following <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/dave-joined-james-blake-central-cee-kano-reading-2026-headline-set-3965639">Dave&#8217;s co-headline performance</a>.</p>
<p>RAYE played at Reading for the first time ever two years ago, <a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/live/raye-reading-2024-review-photos-setlist-3786791">earning a five-star review from <i>NME </i>at the time</a>, and she opened her first headline set with her breakout 2022 single &#8216;Escapism&#8217;, to huge singalongs from the crowd.</p>
<p>Backed by her familiar horn and string sections and full band, she blended pop, soul, jazz and dance across a set drawn from her acclaimed albums &#8216;<a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/album/raye-my-21st-century-blues-review-3391162">My 21st Century Blues</a>&#8216; and &#8216;<a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/album/raye-this-music-may-contain-hope-review-3936886">This Music May Contain Hope</a>&#8216;.</p>
<p>An emotional performance of &#8216;I Know You&#8217;re Hurting&#8217; saw her lead the crowd in a chant of &#8220;<em>It&#8217;s gonna be alright</em>&#8220;, before tapping into the house energy of her early career with &#8216;Secrets&#8217; and &#8216;You Don&#8217;t Know Me&#8217;.</p>
<p>She brought out her sisters Amma and Absolutely for a rousing version of &#8216;Joy&#8217; before bringing the curtain down with a huge rendition of &#8216;Where Is My Husband?&#8217;</p>

<h3><strong>RAYE&#8217;s Reading 2026 setlist was: </strong></h3>
<p><strong>&#8216;Escapism&#8217;<br />
&#8216;Beware&#8230;The South London Lover Boy&#8217;<br />
&#8216;Skin &amp; Bones&#8217;<br />
&#8216;Worth It&#8217;<br />
&#8216;The WhatsApp Shakespeare&#8217;<br />
&#8216;Oscar Winning Tears&#8217;<br />
&#8216;Winter Woman&#8217;<br />
&#8216;I Know You&#8217;re Hurting&#8217;<br />
&#8216;Click Clack Symphony&#8217;<br />
&#8216;Secrets / You Don&#8217;t Know Me&#8217;<br />
&#8216;Life Boat / Prada&#8217;<br />
&#8216;Joy&#8217; (with Amma and Absolutely)<br />
&#8216;Where Is My Husband?&#8217;</strong></p>
<p>RAYE released her second album, the <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/mercury-prize-2026-check-out-this-years-shortlisted-albums-3959760">Mercury-nominated</a> ‘<a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/album/raye-this-music-may-contain-hope-review-3936886">This Music May Contain Hope</a>’ in March, and it includes contributions from <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/hans-zimmer">Hans Zimmer</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/al-green">Al Green </a>and her sisters Amma and Absolutely.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/album/raye-this-music-may-contain-hope-review-3936886">In a five-star review of the album</a>, <i>NME</i> wrote: “‘This Music May Contain Hope’ is RAYE firing on all cylinders – and then some. It’s showstopping musical maximalism at its grandest, while still being grounded in relatable experiences and unbridled emotions.</p>
<p>“Again, it’s admittedly a lot to take in – but that’s also why it’s so glorious, every theatrical flourish and stylistic detour a declaration from RAYE that she is finally making music for herself.”</p>
<p>Tonight’s headline performance came two years after RAYE <a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/live/raye-reading-2024-review-photos-setlist-3786791">made her Reading debut with a heartwarming main-stage set</a>. That appearance followed her <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/brit-awards-2024-winners-nominations-list-3595742">record-breaking success at the 2024 BRIT Awards</a>, where she became the first artist to win six prizes in a single year.</p>
<p>Earlier this summer, she <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/watch-raye-bring-out-alicia-keys-and-mark-ronson-at-montreux-jazz-festival-3955006">brought out Alicia Keys and Mark Ronson during a special performance marking the 60th anniversary of Montreux Jazz Festival</a>. Ronson joined her for ‘Uptown Funk’ and their collaboration ‘Suzanne’, while Keys performed ‘If I Ain’t Got You’ and ‘Oscar Winning Tears’ with her.</p>
<p>Outside music, RAYE is also set to <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/film/raye-to-make-acting-debut-in-yann-demange-thriller-lineage-3945207">make her acting debut in Yann Demange’s forthcoming crime thriller </a><a href="https://www.nme.com/news/film/raye-to-make-acting-debut-in-yann-demange-thriller-lineage-3945207"><i>Lineage</i></a>, which also stars Jason Bateman and Jude Law.</p>
