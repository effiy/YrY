<li><b>READ MORE:</b><b> </b><a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/live/geese-london-hammersmith-eventim-apollo-gig-review-photos-setlist-3936717"><b>Geese live in London: indie buzz band take flight as generational icons in waiting</b></a></li>

<p><a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/cameron-winter">Cameron Winter</a> and co. made their debut performance at the festival yesterday, ahead of their set at<a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/reading-leeds-2026-check-out-the-full-stage-times-3963739"> Leeds tonight (August 29)</a>. The band took to the stage against a montage video of triumphant sports moments – including a Wayne Rooney goal and Michael Smith&#8217;s perfect leg against Michael van Gerwen in the 2023 World Darts Championship.</p>
<p>They drew one of the wildest crowds of the weekend so far, in spite of the heavy rain that went on for most of the day. Winter even poked fun at the weather, warning the crowd not to “drink the water” that was seeping through The Gallery tent as they performed.</p>
<p>“It’s not good for you,” Winter added as he addressed the crowd, most of whom were damp, covered in mud, and wearing plastic ponchos.</p>
<p>They opened with &#8216;Husbands&#8217; from their most recent album &#8216;<a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/album/geese-getting-killed-review-3892821">Getting Killed</a>&#8216;, and went on to play fan favourites like &#8216;Half Real&#8217;, &#8216;2122&#8217;, and &#8216;Cobra&#8217;, before wrapping up with &#8216;Trindad&#8217;.</p>
<p>Find a full setlist and footage below, and check back at <em>NME </em>for a full review.</p>

<p>The set comes as part of their ongoing UK tour, which will include two headline shows at London’s Troxy on September 1 and 2. They will then <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/end-of-the-road-2026-geese-announced-as-final-headliner-3950117">headline the opening night of End Of The Road</a> on September 3.</p>
<p>Geese’s latest album ‘<a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/album/geese-getting-killed-review-3892821">Getting Killed</a>’ received five stars from <i>NME</i> before <a href="https://www.nme.com/lists/end-of-year/best-albums-2025-3915561#1">topping our list of the best albums of 2025</a>.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/live/geese-london-hammersmith-eventim-apollo-gig-review-photos-setlist-3936717">In a five-star review of Geese’s Hammersmith Apollo show earlier this year</a>, <i>NME</i> wrote: “While Winter’s last solo gig in these parts revealed him as a Tom Waits or Nick Cave for our time, tonight feels like one of those hallowed shows seeing Nirvana or The Strokes come into their own, or Radiohead finding their voice.</p>
<p>“In any way, it’s between Geese and Fontaines D.C. to define this decade as a band.”</p>
