<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	Musgraves, who&#8217;s on tour in support of her <em>Middle of Nowhere</em> album, took a moment on the TD Garden stage Friday to point out it&#8217;s not just Parton&#8217;s Grammys, or countless other career accolades, that people are talking about in the wake of the <a href="https://www.billboard.com/music/country/dolly-parton-death-1236324068/">country trailblazer&#8217;s death</a>.</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	&#8220;It&#8217;s what a humanitarian [she was],&#8221; Musgraves said. &#8220;Someone always looking out for someone else, looking out for the underdog, even when it probably wasn&#8217;t easy, even when it didn&#8217;t benefit her career. We can all learn a lot from someone like that.&#8221;</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	&#8220;She&#8217;s woven into the fabric of who we&#8217;ve always been, so it just feels extra sad. Let&#8217;s all sing this together for her memory, OK?&#8221; she suggested, leading the arena crowd in a sweet sing-along of &#8220;Rainbow,&#8221; the closing track from Musgraves&#8217; Grammy Award-winning 2018 album, <em>Golden Hour</em>.</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	Photos of Parton, who passed away on Aug. 25 after a <a href="https://www.billboard.com/music/country/dolly-parton-cause-of-death-1236324654/">brief battle with cancer</a>, were displayed on the big screen behind Musgraves as the room paid tribute to her, singing, &#8220;There&#8217;s always been a rainbow hangin&#8217; over your head.&#8221;</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	The next song on Musgraves&#8217; set list at Friday&#8217;s show was a performance of &#8220;Here You Come Again,&#8221; off of Parton&#8217;s 1977 studio album of the same name.</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	&#8220;Here You Come Again&#8221; was Parton&#8217;s earliest pop crossover hit, peaking at No. 3 on the Billboard Hot 100 in 1978. In fact, just two other recordings from Dolly have topped its success on the all-genre ranking: &#8220;9 to 5&#8221; and her Kenny Rogers duet &#8220;Islands in the Stream,&#8221; which both became Hot 100 No. 1s in the early 1980s. Though Parton was a prolific songwriter, &#8220;Here You Come Again&#8221; was penned by Barry Mann and Cynthia Weil.</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	Musgraves&#8217; song choice is a callback to when she performed &#8220;Here You Come Again&#8221; alongside Parton in 2019, during a special segment featuring a medley of Parton&#8217;s hits at the Grammys. That year, she also sang the tune in Parton&#8217;s honor &#8212; as a <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UWPHidlS_SY" rel="noreferrer noopener nofollow" target="_blank">duet with pop star Katy Perry</a>, in a reprise of their <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5vDodNYq1qU" rel="noreferrer noopener nofollow" target="_blank">CMT Crossroads performance</a> several years prior &#8212; at the MusiCares Person of the Year gala. Musgraves has had a solo cover of &#8220;Here You Come Again&#8221; in rotation at concerts dating back to 2014.</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	&#8220;She&#8217;s an earth angel,&#8221; she said of Parton in a <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ky_9XoKUwes" rel="noreferrer noopener nofollow" target="_blank">red carpet interview</a> at the 2019 Grammys. &#8220;She really is. She&#8217;s so cool. She&#8217;s so present. What I like about her is she has a keen attention to detail, and that&#8217;s obviously why she&#8217;s had such a long, amazing career. She really cares about those things, she can put her stamp on things without apology &#8230; She paved the way for a lot of us, especially in country music. We wouldn&#8217;t really be able to do what we do without her. She&#8217;s the first who had all of it together. The sex appeal, the brains, the humor, the songwriting, the outfits, the hair.&#8221;</p>
