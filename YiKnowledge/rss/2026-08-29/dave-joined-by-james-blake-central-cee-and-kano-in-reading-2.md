<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1270" src="https://www.nme.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/dave_live_2025.jpg" width="2000" /></p><p><a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/dave">Dave</a> welcomed guests <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/james-blake">James Blake</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/central-cee">Central Cee</a> and <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/kano">Kano</a> during his headline set at <a href="https://www.nme.com/reading-leeds-festival-2026">Reading 2026</a> &#8211; see the full setlist.</p>
<ul>
<li><b>READ MORE: Dave – </b><a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/album/dave-the-boy-who-played-the-harp-review-3902686"><b>&#8216;The Boy Who Played The Harp&#8217; review: a prophet&#8217;s pain painted in piano</b></a></li>
</ul>
<p>The South London rapper took to the festival&#8217;s main stage &#8211; now known as The Grid – at Little John&#8217;s Farm tonight (August 29).</p>
<p>It marked <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/dave-joined-by-stormzy-and-aj-tracey-at-reading-festival-headline-set-3298898">the first time Dave had headlined the event since 2022</a> and came at the end of his &#8216;The Boy Who Played The Harp&#8217; world tour, which is due to conclude at <a href="https://www.nme.com/reading-leeds-festival-2026">Leeds Festival </a>tomorrow (August 30).</p>
<p>In a rich, musically diverse set, Blake was the first guest for a version of &#8216;Both Sides Of A Smile&#8217;, from Dave&#8217;s 2021 album &#8216;<a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/album/dave-were-all-alone-in-this-together-review-3001456">We&#8217;re All Alone In This Together</a>&#8216;. The extended guitar riff from <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/the-white-stripes">The White Stripes</a>&#8216; &#8216;Seven Nation Army&#8217; later served as the intro to &#8216;Thiago Silva&#8217;.</p>
<p>Kano reunited with Dave for a high-energy rendition of the 2023 freestyle &#8216;Three P&#8217;s*&#8217; and a tender &#8216;Chapter 16&#8217; before the two artists shared an emotional embrace.</p>
<p>The rapper was joined multiple times by a string section on stage, including for a stirring performance of &#8216;The Boy Who Played The Harp&#8217;, before Central Cee raised the tempo when he came out for their 2023 smash hit single &#8216;Sprinter&#8217;.</p>
<p>Fireworks accompanied Dave&#8217;s performances of &#8216;Starlight&#8217; and &#8216;Raindance&#8217; as he brought the show to a close.</p>

<h3><b>Dave&#8217;s Reading 2026 setlist was:</b></h3>
<p><b>&#8216;Verdansk&#8217;</b><br />
<b>‘Clash’</b><br />
<b>‘Location’</b><br />
<b>‘Both Sides Of A Smile’ (with James Blake)</b><br />
<b>‘Screwface Capital’</b><br />
<b>‘Professor X’</b><br />
<b>‘Funky Friday’</b><br />
<b>‘Thiago Silva’</b><br />
<b>‘No Words&#8217;</b><br />
<b>&#8216;Three P’s*’ (with Kano)</b><br />
<b>‘Chapter 16’ (with Kano)</b><br />
<b>‘Trojan Horse’</b><br />
<b>‘The Boy Who Played The Harp’</b><br />
<b>‘Sprinter’</b> <strong>(with Central Cee)</strong><br />
<b>‘Starlight’</b><br />
<strong>&#8216;Raindance&#8217;</strong></p>
<p>Dave released his third album &#8216;The Boy Who Played The Harp&#8217; last October, which included appearances from Blake on &#8216;History&#8217; and Kano on &#8216;Chapter 16&#8217;, as well as <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/jim-legxacy">Jim Legxacy</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/tems">Tems</a> and Nicole Blakk. <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/mercury-prize-2026-check-out-this-years-shortlisted-albums-3959760">The record has been nominated for this year&#8217;s Mercury Prize</a>.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/album/dave-the-boy-who-played-the-harp-review-3902686">In a four-star review of the album</a>, <i>NME </i>wrote: &#8220;Although the record is vivid, striking and thought-provoking – with nearly every song on this album a deep, pensive sonic sulk – the south Londoner’s voice is beginning to slip further away from a generation he intended to represent: one that’s done overthinking and just wanting to feel.&#8221;</p>
<p>Dave became the youngest-ever solo artist to headline Reading in 2022, at the age of 24. That set saw him <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/dave-joined-by-stormzy-and-aj-tracey-at-reading-festival-headline-set-3298898">bring out AJ Tracey for &#8216;Thiago Silva&#8217; and Stormzy for &#8216;Clash&#8217;</a>, and <a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/live/dave-live-at-reading-festival-2022-review-3298929"><i>NME </i>labelled it a &#8220;history-making set&#8221; in a five-star review</a>, adding that it was &#8220;a special one to round out an otherwise rowdy first day&#8221;.</p>
