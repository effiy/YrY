<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/joe-amabile/"><strong>Joe Amabile</strong></a> is sharing more details of his brain cancer journey.</p>
<p>Appearing <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BDywPSId3S0" rel="noopener" target="_blank">on <em>Good Morning America</em></a> on Friday, August 28, the <em>Bachelor in Paradise</em> alum, 40, described experiencing “struggles” with his speech after undergoing surgery to remove a “blueberry size lesion” on his brain.</p><div class="mb1-wrapper" id="starter">
						<div class="htlad-mb1" id="mb1-static"></div>
						<div class="htlad-leader2" id="leader2-inline-static"></div>
					</div>
<p>Joe explained that doctors found the lesion in the area of his brain that’s responsible for “speech, motor skills and memory.”</p>
<p>He continued, “According to my doctors, I probably wouldn’t know about this [lesion] until I eventually had a seizure.”</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/bachelor-in-paradises-joe-amabile-gives-update-after-surgery/" target="_blank">Bachelor Nation's Joe Amabile Shares Update After Brain Surgery</a></p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/bachelor-in-paradises-joe-amabile-gives-update-after-surgery/">Joe underwent brain surgery</a> earlier this month and doctors warned him that he may face difficulties with his speech post-surgery, which he confirmed was the case.</p>
<p>“I would think of what to say but I would have a hard time vocalizing it,” Joe explained. “And I would say I’m about 90 per cent back but I still struggle a little bit.”</p>
<p>The <em>GMA</em> segment also reported that Joe was currently taking daily medication following the surgery, which his doctor noted had limited side effects.</p>
<p><em>The Bachelorette</em> alum announced via Instagram on July 13, <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/joe-amabile-to-undergo-brain-surgery-after-finding-lesion/">that doctors found “a blueberry size lesion”</a> on his brain which appeared to be a tumor during an MRI scan. Joe revealed at the time that he planned to undergo surgery within the next two weeks to “get it removed and get it tested.”</p>
<figure class="wp-caption "><img alt="Joe-Amabile_1786033727_3957859519358802447_7227427494" class="alignnone size-full credit-added wp-image-4399203" height="2000" src="https://www.usmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Joe-Amabile_1786033727_3957859519358802447_7227427494.jpg" width="1502" /><figcaption class="wp-caption-text"><span class="credit">Courtesy of Joe Amabile/Instagram</span></figcaption></figure>
<p>Joe went on to describe the experience as “a wild couple weeks” since he was told the news.</p>
<p>“I definitely wasn’t expecting this,” he continued. “I think it’s one of those things where you’re like, ‘Oh, something like this will never happen to me.’ And here I am.”</p>
<p>Joe added, “This past month has been a lot of ups and downs to say the least. After multiple scans and MRIs I have what looks to be an early stage brain tumor. In two weeks I’ll be undergoing a craniotomy to have it removed — yeah brain surgery. I’m doing my best to stay positive during this time and am lucky to have a lot of support from family and friends.”</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/joe-amabile-discharged-from-hospital-after-brain-surgery/" target="_blank">Joe Amabile’s Wife Gives Recovery Update After Brain Surgery</a></p>
<p>Earlier this month, Joe announced that the brain surgery to remove the tumor was a success.</p>
<p>“They got it all!” he wrote via Instagram on August 6, alongside several post-op photos at the hospital.</p>
<p>Joe’s wife, <strong>Serena Pitt</strong>, shared a follow-up post via her own social media account the same day.</p>
<p>‘The best update: they were able to remove the entire tumor,” Serena, 28, wrote via her Instagram Stories. “Joe is recovering really well. We’re feeling so grateful.”</p>
