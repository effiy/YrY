<p class="p1"><b><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/leslie-mann/">Leslie Mann</a> </b>is offering fans an update on her husband<a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/judd-apatow/"> <b>Judd Apatow</b></a>’s journey on <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-body/pictures/celebrities-whove-spoken-about-ozempic-for-weight-loss/">the controversial weight loss drug Ozempic</a>.</p>
<p class="p1">“He’s on Ozempic, he likes to talk about it, and he has gained, like, 15 pounds in four years,” Mann, 54, said on the Thursday, August 27, <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F3SeoCrMtvQ" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><span class="s1">episode</span></a> of <i>Jimmy Kimmel Live!, </i>speaking about her husband’s weight loss journey. “He’s on the highest dose.”</p><div class="mb1-wrapper" id="starter">
						<div class="htlad-mb1" id="mb1-static"></div>
						<div class="htlad-leader2" id="leader2-inline-static"></div>
					</div>
<p class="p1">She continued, “He likes to outrun the feeling that you get that Ozempic gives you to stop eating. He likes to, like, override it or outrun it. He’s very proud of the fact that the pharmaceutical companies don’t have control over him. … It’s so weird.”</p>
<p class="p1">Ozempic and other similar semaglutides are commonly prescribed to adults who struggle with weight-related conditions, including diabetes, to slim down. Doctors have <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/ozempic-not-approved-for-casual-weight-loss-despite-celeb-trend/"><span class="s1">since warned individuals </span></a>for using the medication for casual weight loss.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-body/pictures/celebrities-whove-spoken-about-ozempic-for-weight-loss/" target="_blank">Celebs Who've Spoken About Ozempic Weight Loss Trend: Brianna LaPaglia, More</a></p>
<p class="p1">As for Apatow, the 58-year-old filmmaker began taking GLP-1 medications nearly two years ago.</p>
<p class="p1">“I am on so much Ozempic right now,” the <i>Knocked Up </i>director said during a 2024<a href="https://www.facebook.com/NYCComedyCellar/videos/judd-apatow-is-on-so-much-ozempic-right-now-catch-judds-show-at-the-beacon-theat/995549448750783/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><span class="s1"> stand-up show </span></a>in New York City. “I’ve been on it for six months, I’ve gained four pounds. I don’t know how it’s supposed to work. It makes you nauseous, so you don’t want to eat, but I take it as a challenge to eat through the nausea.”</p>
<p class="p1">He added at the time, “You know, like, ‘I’m gonna f***ing prove this medicine doesn’t work.’ Sometimes I’ll eat ice cream and shoot myself with Ozempic at the same time just to see who wins the battle. It’s been a draw so far. That experiment cost me $40,000.”</p>
<p class="p1">Apatow has previously been candid about his experience <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-body/pictures/celebs-shocking-weight-loss-2014304/">trying to lose and maintain</a> his weight.</p>
<p class="p1">“[Leslie] put me on the Goop detox diet,” he revealed on<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NNrGxedHWf0" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><span class="s1"> <i>Live With Kelly and Ryan</i></span></a><i> </i>in 2017, referring to <b>Gwyneth Paltrow</b>’s lifestyle company. “She has a diet up there [on her website], and I did it, and then suddenly, I looked like<b> Ryan [Seacrest]</b> for a while. I got skinny and handsome. … I’m trying very hard to keep it together.”</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-body/pictures/celebs-shocking-weight-loss-2014304/" target="_blank">Celebrities' Weight Loss Transformations: Photos of Jelly Roll and More</a></p>
<p class="p1">At the time, Apatow noted how Mann was “cooking all this food” that he supplemented with a new exercise routine.</p>
<p class="p1">“I did a little SoulCycle <i>Hamilton</i> ride the other day, you know how it goes,” he quipped. “I’m trying to ‘cause I hate being healthy, I don’t like it, but I saw an article about ‘dad bods.’ It’s, like, when you work out a little bit and look a little crappy, and my friend sent me an article about it and I clicked the link and there was a picture showing what it was and the picture was of me. I took that as a sign.”</p>
<p class="p1">Apatow and the <i>Spa Weekend </i>actress have been married since 1997, during which they welcomed daughters Maude, 28, and Iris, 23.</p>
