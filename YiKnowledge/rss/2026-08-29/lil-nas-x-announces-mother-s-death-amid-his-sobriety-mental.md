<p class="p1"><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/lil-nas-x/"><b>Lil Nas X </b></a>is <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/pictures/celebrity-deaths-of-2026-grateful-deads-bob-weir-and-more-stars-we-lost/">mourning the death</a> of his mother, <b>Tameka Hill</b>, amid his ongoing recovery and sobriety journey.</p>
<p class="p1">“RIP TO THE BADDEST EVER! LOVE U FOREVER, MOMMY,” Lil Nas X, 27, wrote via <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/DcoGMJvm5Wj/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><span class="s1">Instagram</span></a> on Saturday, August 29, alongside throwback photos of Hill. “This one’s gonna hurt.”</p><div class="mb1-wrapper" id="starter">

<p class="p1">Further details about Hill’s death were not publicly revealed at the time of publication. <i>Us </i>has reached out to a rep for the “Old Town Road” singer for comment.</p>
<p class="p1">In addition to Lil Nas X, Hill is survived by her multiple other children. The late matriarch had a complicated relationship with the musician prior to her death.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/lil-nas-x-shares-update-on-his-life-after-rehab/" target="_blank">Lil Nas X Shares Update After Rehab as He Marks ‘10,000 Days Alive’</a></p>
<p class="p1">“I never really talk about my mom. She’s an addict, so we don’t have the closest relationship,” Lil Nas X (born Montero Lamar Hill) previously told <a href="https://variety.com/2020/music/news/lil-nas-x-grammys-old-town-road-1203469735/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><span class="s1"><i>Variety</i></span></a><i> </i>in 2020. “Even trying to get her better — things didn’t quite work out. But there’s still love.”</p>
<p class="p1">Lil Nas X is on his own sobriety journey in the wake of public mental health issues, recently reaching a new recovery milestone.</p>
<p class="p1">“So, tomorrow is my 10,000th day alive and … I made it!” he said <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/lil-nas-x-shares-update-on-his-life-after-rehab/">in a Monday, August 24, video</a> shared via his Instagram Stories. “A lot of y’all won’t make it but I made it.”</p>
<p class="p1">Lil Nas X completed<a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/lil-nas-x-seeks-treatment-after-felony-charges-and-arrest/"><span class="s1"> an in-patient treatment program</span></a> in late 2025 for undisclosed “issues.”</p>
<p class="p1">“Ever since I got out of rehab, everybody’s, like, [so] nice to me. I could be like ‘Hey’ and everybody’s going to be like ‘Oh my God, you’re so brave,’” he said in a Tuesday, August 25, <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/DccveSEyqgv/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><span class="s1">social media</span></a> update. “I can try to post a thirst trap on my Instagram Story, and then it’s going to be somebody like, ‘Keep fighting King.’”</p>
<p class="p1">Without disclosing details of his treatment, Lil Nas X returned to social media in June.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/pictures/celebrity-deaths-of-2026-grateful-deads-bob-weir-and-more-stars-we-lost/" target="_blank">Celebrity Deaths of 2026: Peter Cullen, More Stars We've Lost This Year</a></p>
<p class="p1">“I’ve been in rehab for a few months and since then, I’ve been back at home whether it’s in Atlanta with my family or in Los Angeles with myself, friends and what not, and trying to ground myself down to earth and get out of my head,” he said in a June <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/DZsWO0SyxHb/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><span class="s1">Instagram</span></a> video. “I have a therapist now and a psychiatrist, which has been really helpful when I got my bipolar disorder diagnosis.”</p>
<p class="p1">Lil Nas X continued, “I feel like I had known for like the past few years but I didn’t want to admit to it because I didn’t want to have to take medication and have people think different of me… Black, gay, bipolar. I’m living life on, like, extreme hard mode. But on a serious note, I’m doing much better. I’m doing better, I’m feeling better.”</p>
<p class="p3"><span class="s2">He was<a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/lil-nas-x-diagnosed-with-bipolar-disorder-amid-battery-case/"><span class="s1"> diagnosed with bipolar disorder</span></a> last year.</span></p>
