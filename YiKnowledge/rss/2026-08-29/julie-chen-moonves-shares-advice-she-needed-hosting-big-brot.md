<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/shows/big-brother/"><em>Big Brother</em></a>’s <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/julie-chen/"><strong>Julie Chen Moonves</strong></a> isn’t afraid to admit that she could have used some advice before hosting the reality TV show’s inaugural season.</p>
<p>“I would say don’t let those words come out of my mouth unless it’s something I would say,” Julie, 56, exclusively told <em>Us</em> on Thursday, August 27, in celebration of <a href="https://www.tvinsider.com/1284097/big-brother-2-guinness-world-records-1000th-episode/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Big Brother’s 1,000 episode</a>. “I was just so eager to, like, do a good job that [my] phrasing didn’t feel right. But you know, I didn’t argue back enough to say, ‘Let’s change the script.’ I mean, I was barely 30 years old.”</p><div class="mb1-wrapper" id="starter">
						<div class="htlad-mb1" id="mb1-static"></div>
						<div class="htlad-leader2" id="leader2-inline-static"></div>
					</div>
<p>She added, “I was just like, ‘Okay, let’s say this.’ And I’m like, ‘I would never say that. I don’t think it sounds good. But maybe, what do I know? I’m just a person from the news.’”</p>
<p>Julie, a former news anchor and producer for CBS who took on the reality TV hosting gig in 2000, said she eventually found her footing and voice in the entertainment space — so if she could she’d go back and tell herself to simply listen to her gut.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/news/julie-chen-moonves-recalls-panned-1st-big-brother-season/" target="_blank">Big Brother’s Julie Chen Moonves Feared Career Was ‘Over’ After 1st Season</a></p>
<p>“I would tell Julie Chen, season 1, ‘Don’t let them make you say it if it’s something you wouldn’t say or if it doesn’t feel right,’” she added.</p>
<figure class="wp-caption alignnone" id="attachment_4421780" style="width: 682px;"><img alt="GettyImages-2214038514JulieChenMoonvesexclusive.jpg" class="size-large wp-image-4421780" height="1024" src="https://www.usmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/GettyImages-2214038514JulieChenMoonvesexclusive.jpg?w=682" width="682" /><figcaption class="wp-caption-text"><span class="caption">Julie Chen Moonves</span> <span class="credit">Getty Images</span></figcaption></figure>
<p>Now that Chen has 1,000 episodes of <em>Big Brother </em>— and two Guinness World Records — under her proverbial belt, she told <em>Us</em> she’s simply “take one day at a time” after making television history.</p>
<p>“Most of all, I feel humble and grateful because I never would have dreamt it,” she continued, before dishing on what can still surprise her inside the drama-filled house all those seasons and episodes later.</p>
<p>“I am still surprised every day at the level of trust,” she revealed to <em>Us</em>. “You know, it’s like you’ve all watched. You should be a little bit more paranoid, but every season — I guess it’s kind of beautiful, because it shows that humans want to trust and believe other humans.”</p>
<p>As she reflected on her time hosting the series, Julie admitted she has some favorite players she would love to see return to the house.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/news/julie-chen-moonves-teases-controversial-people-in-big-brother-28/" target="_blank">Julie Chen Moonves Teases ‘Controversial People’ in 'Big Brother 28'</a></p>
<p>“I have a soft spot for Chilltown,” she said of the iconic alliance between <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/news/big-brothers-will-kirby-says-hed-definitely-lose-if-he-returned-2015239/"><strong>Dr. Will Kirby</strong></a>, <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/former-big-brother-winner-mike-boogie-malin-arrested-for-shoplifting/"><strong>Mike “Boogie” Malin</strong></a> and <strong>Shannon Dragoo</strong> during B<em>ig Brother</em> season 2. “So if, like, Mike Boogie or Dr. Little Kirby want to play again — but it has to be a package deal, and they have to deliver us this classic Chilltown.”</p>
<p>As for who is impressing the host with their <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/pictures/big-brother-28-cast-revealed-meet-the-new-houseguests/">gameplay today</a>, Julie said she’s certainly paying attention to how <strong>Taylor Brown</strong> is handling the house this season.</p>
<p>“Taylor, because she gets it,” Julie explained. “She’s a thinker, and she knows when to stay quiet. She knows when to give Lala [<strong>LaTrice Verrett</strong>] advice, and she just is entertaining me like there’s no tomorrow. She so gets this game.”</p>
<p><em>Big Brother</em> season 28 airs on CBS Wednesdays, Thursdays and Sundays at 8 p.m. ET.</p>
