<p>A new rumor connecting <a href="https://www.joblo.com/tag/eddie-murphy/">Eddie Murphy</a> and <em>Mad Men</em> creator Matthew Weiner is making the rounds. While details remain scarce, industry insider Jeff Sneider reports via his <a href="https://www.theinsneider.com/">blog</a> that the project is a brand-new series for Peacock, developed by Weiner alongside 101 Studios. Notably, the project is neither a reboot nor a continuation of Weiner&#8217;s flagship <a href="https://www.amc.com/">AMC</a> drama.</p>



<p>If realized, the show would mark Murphy&#8217;s first starring role in a scripted television series since his legendary <em>Saturday Night Live</em> run in the 1980s. For Weiner, it represents his most prominent TV endeavor since Amazon’s 2018 anthology series <em>The Romanoffs</em>.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-murphy-s-hot-streak-continues">Murphy’s Hot Streak Continues</h2>



<p id="p-rc_00a28a6b59593251-32">Landing an icon like Murphy would be a massive score for Peacock. While the comedy legend occasionally took extended hiatuses from Hollywood earlier in his career, his recent output has been remarkably steady, featuring hits like <em>Dolemite Is My Name</em>, <em>Coming 2 America</em>, and <em>Beverly Hills Cop: Axel F</em>. While his heist comedy <em>The Pickup</em>, co-starring Pete Davidson and Keke Palmer, drew mixed reactions on Prime Video, Murphy’s momentum shows no signs of slowing down, with a packed development slate spanning multiple genres.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-animation-amp-franchises-from-shrek-to-pink-panther">Animation &amp; Franchises: From Shrek to Pink Panther</h2>



<p>On the animation front, Murphy recently wrapped work on <em><a href="https://www.joblo.com/tag/shrek-5/">Shrek 5</a></em>, reuniting with Mike Myers and Cameron Diaz alongside franchise newcomers Zendaya, Skyler Gisondo, and Marcello Hernández. He is also set to headline a standalone <em>Donkey</em> spinoff feature centering on his fast-talking, waffle-loving character. Additionally, Murphy is slated to play Inspector Clouseau in director Jeff Fowler&#8217;s upcoming live-action/CG hybrid <em>The Pink Panther</em>, which takes an inventive twist on the classic franchise by giving the smooth-talking detective an imaginary pink panther companion who helps solve cases.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-packed-live-action-slate">Packed Live-Action Slate</h2>



<p>His live-action slate is equally robust. Murphy is attached to produce or star in Kyle Newacheck&#8217;s action-comedy <em>Playas Ball</em>, which carries a unique &#8220;undead&#8221; twist. He will also star opposite Eva Longoria in Julianna Baggott&#8217;s <em>Attachment Parenting</em>, a comedy following two psychologists whose child-rearing philosophies are put to the ultimate test when they are taken hostage by a crime boss&#8217;s dysfunctional family.</p>
