There is a big part of me that doesn't really believe any aspect of <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/star-citizen">Star Citizen</a> is real. It's never really a game I've given all that much of my attention to, the thing gives off pyramid scheme energy in a way I can't quite explain. Probably something to do with the numerous promises developer Cloud Imperium Games have made over the years that haven't always come to fruition in the way they said, or when they said, they would. And here's yet another example of that: <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/squadron-42">Squadron 42</a>, the story-based single-player spinoff of Star Citizen, has been delayed in 2027, all because of a pesky game called GTA 6.
