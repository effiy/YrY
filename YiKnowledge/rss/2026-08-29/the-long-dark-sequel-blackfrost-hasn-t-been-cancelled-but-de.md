Things are shifting around a bit over at <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/the-long-dark">The Long Dark</a> developer <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/hinterland">Hinterland</a> Studio. Earlier this year, The Long Dark's story mode <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/the-long-dark-comes-to-an-end-today-except-its-not-the-end-only-an-end">wrapped up with the release of Wintermute's fifth and final episode</a>. After that, you may have fairly assumed that focus would have been mostly placed on the sequel that was announced in 2024, Blackfrost. But according to Hinterland, while Blackfrost is still in the works, the main focus will actually be put on The Long Dark's <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/the-best-survival-games-on-pc">survival</a> mode for a while.
