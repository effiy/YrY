<em>"The world ended. The waiting didn't."</em> Freestyle Digital Media has debuted their official trailer for an <a href="https://www.firstshowing.net/category/indies/">indie</a> survival thriller film titled <strong><em>After Impact</em></strong>, made by filmmaker <strong>Michiel Rensen</strong> who also stars in the lead role. Another post-apocalyptic survival thriller that of course reminds me of <a href="https://www.justwatch.com/us/movie/deep-impact" rel="noopener nofollow " target="_blank"><em>Deep Impact</em></a>, also featuring a similar title, as well as last year's <a href="https://www.firstshowing.net/2026/one-final-trailer-for-greenland-2-migration-feat-butler-baccarin/" title="One Final Trailer for 'Greenland 2: Migration' Feat. Butler &amp; Baccarin"><em>Greenland 2: Migration</em></a>. As society begins to unravel, Ethan grapples with personal betrayal, overwhelming loneliness, and the growing fear that survival may come at the cost of his humanity. Seeking refuge in his family's bunker, Ethan endures the harsh aftermath of the impact, wrestling with physical injury and emotional despair. When a faint voice crackles over his radio, Ethan discovers he is not as alone as he believed. Starring <strong>Michiel Rensen</strong>, <strong>Maxie Ouyang</strong>, <strong>Will Clague</strong>, <strong>Riki Antonievich</strong>, <strong>Lee-Ann Dirks</strong>. <em>After Impact</em> is a haunting sci-fi thriller about isolation, endurance, and the fragile hope that someone else is still out there.
