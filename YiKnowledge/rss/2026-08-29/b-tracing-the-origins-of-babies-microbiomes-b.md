<p>Nature Medicine, Published online: 26 August 2026; <a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/d41591-026-00042-2">doi:10.1038/d41591-026-00042-2</a></p>A large study of mother–infant pairs shows that the maternal gut microbiome is a major reservoir for infant gut strains, which impacts early health outcomes such as eczema.
