<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">

Among the YSL vets and rookies onboard across the two-plus-hour album: Lil Keed, diamond*, Tezzus, Pz’, Sk8star, 1300SAINT, Yung Kayo, Biggs, iyrus, Yume, Lil Unky, Unfoonk and more.</p>

<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	<em>Slime Language 3</em> is the latest in a trilogy of YSL compilation albums that started with 2018&#8217;s <em>Slime Language</em> and continued with 2021&#8217;s <em>Slime Language 2</em>. The series became progressively more popular, with the original project debuting at No. 8 on the all-genre <a href="https://www.billboard.com/charts/billboard-200/">Billboard 200</a> albums chart, while the most recent entry topped that chart as well as <a href="https://www.billboard.com/charts/r-b-hip-hop-albums/">Top R&amp;B/Hip-Hop Albums</a>.</p>







<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	The two biggest songs to come from <em>Slime Language 2</em> were Young Thug and Gunna&#8217;s &#8220;Solid&#8221; (featuring Drake) and Young Thug and Gunna&#8217;s &#8220;Ski,&#8221; both top 20 hits on the <a href="https://www.billboard.com/charts/hot-100/">Billboard Hot 100</a>. Of course, Gunna is nowhere to be found on this new project, after fulfilling his contractual obligations to YSL with his 2025 album <em>The Last Wun</em> following a <a href="https://www.billboard.com/lists/young-thug-gunna-relationship-timeline/">falling-out with Young Thug</a> involving the RICO charges that plagued the label.</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	In October 2024, <a href="https://www.billboard.com/pro/young-thug-pleads-guilty-ysl-rico-case/">Young Thug was sentenced</a> to 15 years&#8217; probation and no prison time after pleading guilty in the criminal case accusing him of leading an Atlanta street gang. He released his first post-prison album, <em>UY Scuti</em>, in September 2025.</p>
