<p class="p1"><b>Patrick Mahomes</b>’ wife, <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/brittany-mahomes/"><b>Brittany Mahomes</b></a>,<b> </b>is undoubtedly ready for <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/stylish/pictures/brittany-mahomes-most-festive-game-day-outfits-at-chiefs-games/">another NFL season full of style</a>.</p>
<p class="p1">Brittany, 30, attended the Kansas City Chiefs’ preseason game at Arrowhead Stadium on Friday, August 28, in support of Patrick, also 30, and his teammates. (Patrick is the starting quarterback for the Chiefs but did not play in the game against the Seattle Seahawks, which ended in a 9-9 tie.)</p><div class="mb1-wrapper" id="starter">

<p class="p1">“Straight chaos <span class="s1">😎❤️</span>,” Brittany captioned <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/DcmrETWoA4J/?img_index=1" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><span class="s2">Instagram</span></a> footage of her family’s game day outing.</p>
<p class="p1">The mom of three — Brittany shares daughters Sterling, 5, and Golden, 19 months, as well as son Bronze, 3, with Patrick — rocked a cropped denim jacket with a pair of matching shorts. She completed her ‘fit with a white T-shirt, rose-colored sunglasses and blue Adidas sneakers.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/stylish/pictures/brittany-mahomes-most-festive-game-day-outfits-at-chiefs-games/" target="_blank">All of Brittany Mahomes' Festive Game Day Outfits at Chiefs Games</a></p>
<p class="p1">For glam, Brittany wore a full beat and styled her signature blonde tresses down, straight and side-parted.</p>
<p class="p1">Joining her in festive spirit, Brittany dressed her eldest kids in Chiefs-branded gear. While Sterling sported a gray “Chiefs Kingdom” dress with a red hair bow, Bronze donned a red polo embroidered with the team name. (Golden, for her part, did not appear to attend the game.)</p>
<p class="p1">Brittany has long been Patrick’s No. 1 cheerleader, ever since <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/pictures/patrick-mahomes-brittany-matthews-relationship-timeline/">they started dating</a> in high school.</p>
<p class="p1">“I think being the significant other to an NFL player is an incredibly hard role, and we take on a lot of important stuff behind the scenes that people don’t get to see,” Brittany told <i>Sports Illustrated</i> in February of supporting Patrick’s career. “We also get to enjoy life and do things that we love, too.”</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/pictures/patrick-mahomes-brittany-matthews-relationship-timeline/" target="_blank">Patrick Mahomes and Brittany Mahomes' Relationship Timeline</a></p>
<p class="p1">As for the three-time Super Bowl champ, he is grateful for Brittany’s tireless support.</p>
<p class="p1">“I think people don’t even realize how much she does,” he gushed <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/patrick-mahomes-says-wife-brittany-is-a-hall-of-fame-mom-and-spouse/"><span class="s2">on the “Impaulsive” podcast </span></a>in 2024. “I mean, taking care of the day-to-day stuff and make it where I can focus on football and focus on my craft and everything like that. [She’s] a Hall of Fame mom and a Hall of Fame wife, [which] makes it a lot easier.”</p>
<p class="p1">Patrick added at the time, “I mean, when you get to come home and your best friend’s there and you can just hang out, it makes you want to be there all the time and she pushes me to be great and she’s done a lot of great things herself.”</p>
