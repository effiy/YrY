<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A newly acquired Novartis drug successfully lowered signs of inflammation in a midstage study, as experts wait to hear how the company plans to advance the drug as a potential cardiovascular treatment.&#xa0;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In the Phase 2 TRANQUILITY study, the drug, pacibekitug, led to sustained decreases in biomarkers of inflammation, according to results presented Saturday at the European Society of Cardiology&#x2019;s annual meeting in Munich. Investigators reported a dose-dependent drop in biomarker levels on certain measures, meaning higher doses led to greater reductions, a sign the drug is having an effect. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Pacibekitug is an antibody that targets IL-6, or interleukin-6, a protein involved in regulating immune responses.&#xa0;</p><p><a href="https://www.statnews.com/2026/08/29/novartis-pacibekitug-inflammation-il6-tourmaline-heart-disease/?utm_campaign=rss">Continue to STAT+ to read the full story&hellip;</a></p>
