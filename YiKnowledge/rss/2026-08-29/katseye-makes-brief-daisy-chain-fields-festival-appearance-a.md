<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	Three of the group&#8217;s current active members &#8212; Yoonchae, Lara and Megan, who sustained an ankle injury during rehearsal that led to KATSEYE pulling out of performing this weekend &#8212; unexpectedly showed up on stage Saturday (Aug. 29) just before Devon Again, the act booked to fill in for what would&#8217;ve been KATSEYE&#8217;s time slot. The trio said they were honored to introduce nonprofit Baby2Baby, an organization that provides children in poverty with diapers, clothing and other necessities, ahead of Devon Again&#8217;s performance. </p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	Addressing their last-minute exit from the lineup, Megan expressed, “We&#8217;re so disappointed that we can’t perform today on stage, but my ankle is on hiatus.&#8221;</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	&#8220;But our hearts are definitely not,&#8221; she added. &#8220;We&#8217;re sending all of our love to Olivia and this beautiful community that she built at Daisy Chain Fields. We&#8217;re so happy to be here and show as much support as we can. We love you, Olivia. Thank you for letting us be a small part of this.&#8221; (Check out a video clip that was captured of the moment <a href="https://www.tiktok.com/@amplifyhervoice/video/7679555243817930014" rel="noreferrer noopener nofollow" target="_blank">here</a>.)</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	KATSEYE announced on Thursday that Megan had sprained her ankle, and that they&#8217;d no longer be performing at Rodrigo&#8217;s inaugural charity festival.</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	&#8220;My doctors have advised me to stay off it and let it heal so I can be ready for tour,&#8221; <a href="https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/katseye-megan-speaks-band-drop-out-daisy-chain-fields-rodrigo-1236327473/">Megan said</a> on Friday.</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	KATSEYE&#8217;s scheduled appearance at the Irvine, Calif., music festival curated by Rodrigo came just after the group earned its first <a href="https://www.billboard.com/music/chart-beat/katseye-wild-number-one-billboard-200-albums-chart-1236322567/">No. 1 on the Billboard 200</a> with the EP <em>WILD</em>.</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	KATSEYE&#8217;s active lineup presently consists of Yoonchae, Lara, Megan and Daniela, who wasn&#8217;t seen on stage Saturday. Fellow members Manon and Sophia are on hiatus, with Manon focusing on her wellness and Sophia focusing on her mental health.</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	On Friday, Rodrigo announced that ticket sales for Saturday&#8217;s day-long music event had <a href="https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/olivia-rodrigo-daisy-chain-fields-raises-10-million-charity-1236327985/">brought in $10 million</a> that will go directly to 10 nonprofit organizations supporting women: Planned Parenthood, Center for Reproductive Rights, Baby2Baby, Black Mamas Matter Alliance, FreeFrom, Jhpiego, Johns Hopkins Center for Indigenous Health, National Domestic Workers Alliance, National Institute for Reproductive Health and National Women’s Law Center.</p>
