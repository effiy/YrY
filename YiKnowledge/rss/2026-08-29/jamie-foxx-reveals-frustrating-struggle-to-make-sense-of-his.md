<p>Jamie Foxx admitted he struggled to make sense of his stroke while undergoing physical therapy, calling the whole ordeal &#8220;frustrating.&#8221;</p>



<p>During a recent appearance on the &#8220;Baby, This is Keke Palmer&#8221; podcast, the Oscar winner reflected on his health struggle and rehab stay after suffering <a href="https://www.thewrap.com/jamie-foxx-suffers-medical-complication-recovering/">from a stroke in April 2023</a>.</p>



<p>&#8220;Like, I was talking to him,&#8221; Foxx recalled while gesturing upwards. &#8220;I said, &#8216;Bro, what are you talking about? It&#8217;s me, bro. Like I put the smiles on people&#8217;s faces. Why me?'&#8221;</p>



<p>Foxx shared that he remembered feeling frustrated that he was struggling while there were &#8220;all these bad people out here.&#8221;</p>





<p>&#8220;And he said, &#8216;Those bad people already belong to somebody else.&#8217; He said, &#8216;This is your time.&#8217; And I said, [<em>ugh</em>],&#8221; he noted. &#8220;You just couldn&#8217;t imagine, like when I finally came to my [senses], I said, &#8216;What happened?&#8217; And they said, &#8216;You had a stroke.&#8217; I said, &#8216;What are you talking about?'&#8221;</p>



<p>He added: &#8220;I couldn&#8217;t make sense of it. And the parts I do remember, like the rehab part, was frustrating &#8217;cause &#8230; They said, &#8216;You got a second chance.&#8217; I said, &#8216;F&#8211;k the second chance. What was wrong with my first chance? This doesn&#8217;t happen to anybody. How does it happen to me?'&#8221;</p>



<p>Per Foxx, his healthcare team struggled to do &#8220;wellness checks&#8221; on him, given he utilized comedy to cope at the time.</p>



<p>&#8220;So, I was just funny. I was always joking all the time,&#8221; he said. &#8220;So, when they come and say, &#8216;Who are you today, sir?&#8217; And I was somebody different.&#8221;</p>



<p>Specifically, Foxx shared that he pulled out his Dave Chappelle impression to get through the recovery process.</p>



<p>Foxx&#8217;s daughter Corinne confirmed this to be true, sharing her dad also said his name was Denzel Washington when he was admitted to the hospital. Foxx then gave host Keke Palmer a taste of his Washington impression.</p>



<p>All jokes aside, Foxx shared that this humor was the only way he &#8220;could find myself&#8221; throughout the ordeal.</p>

<p>“Because here’s the truth of it: When this all transpired, I went back to meet the 60 nurses that kept me alive,” he noted. Per Foxx, his healthcare team told him that his recovery was &#8220;a miracle,&#8221; adding, &#8220;It makes you want to go get it.&#8221;</p>



<p>Watch Foxx&#8217;s comments above.</p>



<p>The &#8220;Ray&#8221; star previously opened up <a href="https://www.thewrap.com/jamie-foxx-hospitalized-instagram-post/">about his health crisis</a> in his 2024 Netflix comedy special, titled &#8220;<a href="https://www.thewrap.com/jamie-foxx-netflix-special-review-what-had-happened-was/">What Had Happened Was</a>.&#8221;</p>



<p>&#8220;Before I could get the Aspirin, I went out,&#8221; he said in the special. &#8220;I don&#8217;t remember 20 days, what I was told &#8230; was that they [took] me in to operate on me and my sister knelt down outside the operating room and prayed the whole time.&#8221;</p>



<p>Prior to this tell-all special, Foxx had shared a video update on Instagram, where he told fans: &#8220;I went to hell and back &#8230; And my road to recovery had some potholes as well. But I’m coming back. And I’m able to work.&#8221;</p>
