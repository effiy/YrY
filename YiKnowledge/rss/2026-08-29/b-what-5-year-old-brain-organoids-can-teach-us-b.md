<p>Nature Medicine, Published online: 28 August 2026; <a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/d41591-026-00044-0">doi:10.1038/d41591-026-00044-0</a></p>Human brain organoids cultured for 5 years exhibit epigenetic aging dynamics that mirror those seen in vivo, which makes them a powerful tool for studying postnatal brain development.
