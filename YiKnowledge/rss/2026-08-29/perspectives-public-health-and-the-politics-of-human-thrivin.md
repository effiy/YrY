How do we enable populations to thrive? In a world increasingly marked by polarisation, precarity, and the retreat of the USA from global public health leadership, public health is on the defensive. Against this backdrop, Michelle A Williams’ The Cure for Everything: The Epic Struggle for Public Health and a Radical Vision for Human Thriving, written with Linda Marsa, offers a sweeping account of public health as a struggle over who is afforded the power to intervene and whose interests are served.
