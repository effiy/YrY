<li><b>READ MORE: </b><a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/sombr-brits-2026-interview-verve-oasis-uk-music-jack-whitehall-3932360"><b>Sombr at the BRITs 2026: “I love America, but I think music from the UK beats them”</b></a></li>

<p>The US pop singer performed on the festival’s main stage – now known as The Grid – at Little John’s Farm earlier today (August 29), ahead of headline performances from <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/dave">Dave </a>and <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/raye">RAYE</a>.</p>
<p>His performance attracted one of the biggest crowds of the weekend so far, and began with an elaborate entrance broadcast on the stage’s screens.</p>
<p>The footage showed the back doors of an ambulance opening to reveal Sombr lying on a stretcher, with Slayyyter sitting alongside him. After climbing down, the pair took each other’s hands and began walking through the backstage area towards The Grid.</p>
<p>Sombr stopped along the route to drop to the ground for a set of press-ups, with Slayyyter doing the same. They then emerged together at the side of the stage, where Slayyyter briefly hyped up the crowd and waved to fans before leaving Sombr to begin his set.</p>

<p>Slayyyter had played her own set on The Grid earlier in the afternoon, and <a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/live/slayyyter-reading-2026-review-setlist-photos-3965580"><i>NME</i></a><a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/live/slayyyter-reading-2026-review-setlist-photos-3965580"> awarded the performance four-and-a-half stars</a>, praising her “hangover-demolishing, hype-worthy performance” and writing: “That Slayyyter might have given up music before she got to this point makes today’s set feel even more special – a star who’s grinded for her breakthrough and is now enjoying the fruits of her labour, big time.”</p>
<p>Her set drew heavily from her third album ‘<a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/album/slayyyter-worst-girl-in-america-review-3936891">WOR$T GIRL IN AMERICA</a>’, which arrived in March. <i>NME</i> awarded the record four stars and described it as a “new strain of brash, sleazy pop”.</p>
<p>Sombr, meanwhile, arrived at Reading less than 24 hours after performing at Electric Picnic in Ireland, where he <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/watch-sombr-team-up-with-the-cranberries-to-cover-zombie-at-electric-picnic-in-ireland-3965543">brought out Noel and Mike Hogan of The Cranberries to cover ‘Zombie’</a>. It was his second recent collaboration with the band, having previously <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/watch-sombr-try-to-split-the-g-and-perform-linger-with-the-cranberries-on-stage-in-dublin-3935235">performed ‘Linger’ with the Hogan brothers at Dublin’s 3Arena</a> in March.</p>
<p>Sombr released his debut album ‘I Barely Know Her’ in August last year. <a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/album/sombr-i-barely-know-her-review-3888251">In a four-star review</a>, <i>NME</i> noted: &#8220;‘I Barely Know Her’ is an album driven by heartache but that hasn’t stopped Sombr from creating a gloriously dynamic record that’s less about misery and more about catharsis. It’s sad boy indie with a smirk.&#8221;</p>
<p>Both Sombr and Slayyyter are due to perform at <a href="https://www.nme.com/reading-leeds-festival-2026">Leeds Festival</a> tomorrow (August 30).</p>
