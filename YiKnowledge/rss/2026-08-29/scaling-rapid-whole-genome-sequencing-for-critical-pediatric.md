<p>Nature Medicine, Published online: 27 August 2026; <a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04627-9">doi:10.1038/s41591-026-04627-9</a></p>We describe the large-scale implementation of Little Falcon, a rapid whole-genome sequencing (rWGS) program that enables improved diagnosis and management of critically ill pediatric patients in Dubai. Our study demonstrates the clinical utility of rWGS in populations of Middle Eastern and Asian ancestries.
