<p class="p1"><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/shows/dancing-with-the-stars/"><i>Dancing With the Stars: The Next Pro </i></a>judge <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/news/shirley-ballas-defends-son-mark-ballas-dwts-samba-routine/"><b>Shirley Ballas </b></a>is weighing in on <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/rylee-arnold/"><b>Rylee Arnold </b></a>and <b>AJ Pritchard</b>’s off-the-floor drama.</p>
<p class="p1">In the comments of her latest <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/Dcl9MfOuHac/?hl=en" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><span class="s1">Instagram</span></a> post promoting the upcoming <i>Next Pro </i>season finale, Shirley, 65, was asked for her “opinion” after AJ’s fiancée, <b>Zara Zoffany</b>, openly criticized Rylee, 21, for calling out his alleged behavior during rehearsals.</p><div class="mb1-wrapper" id="starter">
						<div class="htlad-mb1" id="mb1-static"></div>
						<div class="htlad-leader2" id="leader2-inline-static"></div>
					</div>
<p class="p1">“@ryleearnold1 has earned her place on <i>DWTS</i> through hard work,” Shirley, fellow pro <b>Mark Ballas</b>’ mom, replied via Instagram comment. “Dedicating her time to all styles.”</p>
<p class="p1">She continued, “I have a lot of respect for her. Shame others do not. <span class="s2">🔥🔥🔥</span>.”</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/news/ezra-sosa-defends-rylee-arnold-amid-dwts-next-pro-drama-with-aj/" target="_blank">DWTS' Ezra Defends Rylee After Viral Dance Drama With Next Pro's AJ</a></p>
<p class="p1">Rylee, who was named a pro beginning in 2023’s season 32, made a cameo on the <i>Next Pro </i>semifinals for a routine <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/pictures/dancing-with-the-stars-the-next-pro-trailer-introduces-12-contestants-meet-the-cast/">with competitor AJ</a>, 31. In post-rehearsal footage with Shirley, Mark, 40, and guest judge <b>Kym Johnson</b>, Rylee admitted that AJ appeared overly critical without providing any positive feedback.</p>
<p class="p1">In response, Zara, 32, slammed Rylee via social media.</p>
<p class="p1">“[Rylee] — @aj11ace wouldn’t have had to critique you in the first place if everything had been right. Dancing with <b>Bailey [McKnight]</b> last week showed how he danced with an amateur & their dance was amazing,” Zara alleged, referring to AJ’s <i>Next Pro </i>dance with a newbie dancer. “This week was suppose [sic] to be dancing with a pro … very different dynamics & what people didn’t see in rehearsals was that you injured him when you hit him in the head, leaving with a slight concussion and needing to see a [doctor].”</p>
<figure class="wp-caption alignnone" id="attachment_4421785" style="width: 240px;"><img alt="Rylee-Arnold-and-AJ-Pritchard-181639_3852" class="size-medium wp-image-4421785" height="300" src="https://www.usmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Rylee-Arnold-and-AJ-Pritchard-181639_3852.jpg?w=240" width="240" /><figcaption class="wp-caption-text"><span class="caption">Rylee Arnold and AJ Pritchard.</span> <span class="credit">Disney/Vince Valitutti</span></figcaption></figure>
<p class="p1">She added at the time, “He wouldn’t have needed to give it if something hadn’t been wrong. I hope you were able to take something positive and useful from his teaching and feedback.”</p>
<p class="p1">Neither Rylee nor AJ, who previously served as pro dancer on the U.K.’s <i>Strictly Come Dancing</i>, have publicly addressed the social media drama. Rylee’s best friend and fellow pro, <b>Ezra Sosa</b>, meanwhile,<a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/news/ezra-sosa-defends-rylee-arnold-amid-dwts-next-pro-drama-with-aj/"><span class="s1"> has clapped back</span></a> at Zara’s apparent diss in defense of his colleague.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/news/rylee-arnold-addresses-rumors-about-dancing-with-the-stars-return/" target="_blank">Rylee Arnold Addresses Rumor She Won't Be Asked Back to 'DWTS' Next Season</a></p>
<p class="p1">“I usually stay out of stuff like this, but this genuinely made me sick to my stomach,” Ezra, 25, replied via Instagram comment. “Rylee is my best friend, I love her and I will speak up for her. Rylee is more than qualified to be a pro, and one dance with a completely different partnership does not erase the years of training, talent and work that earned her that title.”</p>
<p class="p1">While Ezra acknowledged that “everyone gets critiqued” in the dance world, Rylee is “one of the most honest and trustworthy” people he knows.</p>
<p class="p1">“She reads people and their energy incredibly well, and she is a fanatic pro and [an] INCREDIBLE DANCER,” he added in his statement. “Watching a woman go out of her way to publicly question another woman’s qualifications and tear her down like this is so sad to see.”</p>
<p class="p1">AJ is among the finalists to become the next <i>DWTS</i> pro for <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/news/what-to-know-about-season-35-of-dancing-with-the-stars-so-far/">the upcoming season 35</a> alongside <b>Selena Hamilton</b> and<b> Adele Zaikman</b>.</p>
<p class="p1">The <i>Dancing With the Stars: The Next Pro</i> finale airs on ABC Monday, August 31 at 8 p.m. ET.</p>
