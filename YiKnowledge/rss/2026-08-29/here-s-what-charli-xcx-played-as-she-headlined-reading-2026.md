<ul>
<li><strong>READ MORE: <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/reading-leeds-boss-talks-biggest-ever-arena-overhaul-and-strength-of-uk-and-irish-music-on-line-up-were-buzzing-with-ideas-for-the-future-3955730"><b>Reading &amp; Leeds boss talks biggest-ever arena overhaul and “strength of UK and Irish music” on line-up: “We’re buzzing with ideas for the future”</b></a></strong></li>
</ul>
<p>The singer made <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/reading-leeds-2026-line-up-headliners-announced-poster-tickets-3914124">her headline debut appearance on the festival&#8217;s main stage</a> – now <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/reading-leeds-boss-talks-biggest-ever-arena-overhaul-and-strength-of-uk-and-irish-music-on-line-up-were-buzzing-with-ideas-for-the-future-3955730">known as The Grid</a> – last night (August 28). She&#8217;ll play another set tonight in Leeds, marking her first time playing the dual festivals since 2022.</p>
<p>The performances come on the heels of her latest album ‘<a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/album/charli-xcx-music-fashion-film-review-3959104">Music, Fashion, Film</a>’, and ahead of <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/charli-xcx-announces-2026-north-america-music-fashion-film-tour-3949755">a North American arena tour with Underscores</a> in September. After a day of heavy rain on site, Charli took to the main stage to kick off her first full UK set of her &#8216;Music, Fashion, Film&#8217; era, opening with <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/watch-charli-xcxs-mosh-heavy-video-for-new-single-rock-music-3944492">the album&#8217;s first single &#8216;Rock Music&#8217;</a>.</p>
<p>Unlike the &#8216;Brat&#8217; era, which often saw her alone onstage, the set featured a full band and a host of dancers, all dressed in black. Charli sailed through a setlist predominantly made up of songs from her latest album, though it also drew from &#8216;Brat&#8217;, with high-octane performances of &#8216;360&#8217;, &#8216;Von Dutch&#8217; and &#8216;Guess&#8217;, as well as her &#8216;Wuthering Heights&#8217; album, which came out back in February.</p>
<p>Before &#8216;Apple&#8217;, Charli told the audience: “Here’s a classic for you, Reading&#8221;, while &#8216;2007&#8217; was preceded by her being handed a cigarette by a dancer.</p>
<p>The set wrapped up with &#8216;Nothing Lasts Forever&#8217;, the closing track from &#8216;Music, Fashion, Film&#8217;, with the final words of David Cronenberg’s monologue: “They are dead, they are gone, nothing lasts forever” repeating over the main stage.</p>
<p>Find the full setlist below, and check back at <em>NME</em> for our full review.</p>

<p>Also headlining the 2026 festival are <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/florence-and-the-machine">Florence + The Machine</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/fontaines-d-c">Fontaines D.C.</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/dave">Dave</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/raye">RAYE</a> and <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/chase-status">Chase &amp; Status</a>, and other names on the line-up include <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/skepta">Skepta</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/sombr">Sombr</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/role-model">Role Model</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/jade-thirlwall">JADE</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/kneecap">Kneecap</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/geese">Geese</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/skye-newman">Skye Newman</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/holly-humberstone">Holly Humberstone</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/gurriers">Gurriers</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/westside-cowboy">Westside Cowboy</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/declan-mckenna">Declan McKenna</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/maisie-peters">Maisie Peters</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/paris-paloma">Paris Paloma</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/overpass">Overpass</a> and <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/duke-dumont">Duke Dumont</a>.</p>
<p>Earlier this summer, Charli <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/watch-charli-xcx-join-the-strokes-for-take-it-or-leave-it-at-outside-lands-2026-3961552">made a surprise appearance during The Strokes‘ set</a> at Outside Lands, and also <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/watch-charli-xcx-leave-djo-a-voicemail-during-charlies-garden-at-outside-lands-2026-3961679">made a virtual cameo during Djo’s set at the festival</a>.</p>
<p>Before then, she<a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/charli-xcx-records-intimate-performance-and-interview-from-maida-vale-says-2007-is-about-making-tiktoks-3960850"> recorded an intimate live performance from Maida Vale</a>,<a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/watch-charli-xcx-deliver-first-full-music-fashion-film-headline-show-at-lollapalooza-2026-3960441"> performed her first full headline show of the ‘Music, Fashion, Film’ era at Lollapalooza</a>, and also <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/watch-lorde-bring-out-charli-xcx-for-girl-so-confusing-at-lollapalooza-3959964">joined Lorde on stage at the Chicago festival for ‘Girl, So Confusing’</a>.</p>
<p>Since her latest album dropped, the singer<a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/charli-xcx-begrudgingly-releases-music-fashion-film-b-sides-edition-on-itunes-3959109"> has “begrudgingly” released the ‘B-sides Edition’ </a>and <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/watch-charli-xcxs-full-music-fashion-film-album-film-3958934">shared an accompanying film for the original album</a>.&lt;</p>
