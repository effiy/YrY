You can see more than 11 minutes of exclusive footage in the player below, and get a deeper look into just how the game will work. </p><p></p><p>Earlier this week, developer Wolf Haus games announced that the darkly satirical survival/action cult simulator will be available on PS5 at launch alongside the PC and Xbox Series X/S versions in March 2027.
