<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-who-is-eligible-for-the-chase-ink-cash-card-bonus">Who is eligible for the Chase Ink Cash Card bonus?</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">When it comes to getting approved for the <a href="https://onemileatatime.com/go/cc-chase-inkcash-1k8k/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Chase Ink Cash Card</a> and earning the welcome bonus, what rules should you be aware of? Well, there are the card specific policies, and then also the more generic Chase policies. When you pull up the application, you’ll see the following terms:</p>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The new cardmember bonus may not be available to you if you have ever had this card or any other Chase for Business card without an annual fee. We may also consider factors pertinent to your business in determining your bonus eligibility.</p>
</blockquote>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">As you can see, officially the welcome bonus isn’t available to you if you’ve had this exact card, or another Chase business card without an annual fee, including the <a href="https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/chase/chase-ink-business-unlimited/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Ink Business Unlimited® Credit Card</a> (<a href="https://onemileatatime.com/go/cc-chase-inkunlimited-bo/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">learn more</a>). So there is an element of <a href="https://onemileatatime.com/guides/chase-ink-cash-vs-ink-unlimited/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">cards being mutually exclusive</a>.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">However, the key here is that you “may” not be eligible, and not that you absolutely won’t be eligible. I don’t love that level of vagueness, though the good news is that if you apply for the card and aren’t eligible for the bonus, you should receive a pop-up message informing you of this, before there’s a hard pull. So that at least means the downside of applying is limited.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The good news is that eligibility for the bonus is completely unrelated to whether you have a Chase business card with an annual fee, like the <a href="https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/chase/chase-ink-business-preferred/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Ink Business Preferred® Credit Card</a> (<a href="https://onemileatatime.com/go/cc-chase-inkpreferred-bo/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">learn more</a>) or <a href="https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/chase/chase-sapphire-reserve-business/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Sapphire Reserve for Business℠</a> (<a href="https://onemileatatime.com/go/cc-chase-csrbiz-bo/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">learn more</a>).</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">It’s fine to apply for Chase business cards as a sole proprietorship, corporation, etc. If you apply as a sole proprietorship, you can just enter your Social Security Number in the section where you’d otherwise put your EIN.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Beyond that, there are <a href="https://onemileatatime.com/guides/credit-card-application-rules/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">some general Chase rules to be aware of</a>:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>This card may be subjected to <a href="https://onemileatatime.com/guides/chase-5-24-rule/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Chase’s 5/24 rule</a>, which is to say that you may not be approved if you’ve opened five or more new card accounts in the past 24 months (this <a href="https://onemileatatime.com/guides/chase-sapphire-preferred-vs-ink-business-preferred/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">excludes many business cards</a>); however, <a href="https://onemileatatime.com/guides/chase-5-24-rule/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">there are increasingly data points</a> that this rule is no longer consistently enforced</li>



<li>It’s generally considered a best practice to not apply for more than one Chase business credit card every 30 days, so I’d recommend observing that limit when applying</li>
</ul>
