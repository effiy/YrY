<p>Nature, Published online: 26 August 2026; <a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10577-6">doi:10.1038/s41586-026-10577-6</a></p>Single-cell RNA-sequencing shows that cell-type-specific expression quantitative trait loci drive much of complex trait heritability, highlighting cell-type-specific gene regulation as key to linking genetic variants with traits.
