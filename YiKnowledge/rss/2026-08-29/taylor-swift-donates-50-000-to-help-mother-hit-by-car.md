<p>The pop star donated $50,000 (£37,000) to a <a href="https://www.gofundme.com/f/support-ashley-tauntons-brave-recovery" rel="noopener" target="_blank">GoFundMe</a> created to assist Ashley Taunton, her husband, and their three children with daily expenses while Taunton recovers from the accident.</p>
<p>According to the fundraising page, Taunton saw someone in need after a car accident and pulled over during a heavy rainstorm to help. As her husband, Joe, writes, it was “Ashley doing Ashley things&#8221;.</p>
<p>While Taunton was assisting, another vehicle hydroplaned toward them, and Taunton pushed a person at the scene out of the way before being struck herself. The GoFundMe says that Taunton is &#8220;now facing a very long and difficult road to recovery&#8221;, having already undergone two major surgeries with more scheduled in the days ahead.</p>
<p>Fans soon noticed that, in the comment section of the page, Swift had left a message for Taunton and her family, alongside her donation, that read: “Wishing you the best recovery possible and sending love to your family!”</p>
<p>Taunton has since responded, saying she is &#8220;sobbing with so much gratitude&#8221;.</p>
