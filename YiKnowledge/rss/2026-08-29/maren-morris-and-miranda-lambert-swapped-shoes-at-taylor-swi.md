<p class="p1"><b><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/maren-morris/">Maren Morris</a> </b>apparently walked away with an unexpected souvenir from <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/taylor-swift/"><b>Taylor Swift </b></a>and <b>Travis Kelce</b>’s star-studded Madison Square Garden wedding.</p>
<p class="p1">“<a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/miranda-lambert/"><b>Miranda Lambert</b> </a>and I accidentally took each other’s shoes home,” Morris, 36, claimed during a recent <a href="https://www.tiktok.com/@alanathegreat/video/7679164655150910751?_r=1&amp;_t=ZT-99GyOe7DYth" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><span class="s1">radio interview</span></a>. “We don’t wear the same shoe size, and the shoes did not look the same at all. They were both just, like, black heels.”</p><div class="mb1-wrapper" id="starter">

						<div class="htlad-leader2" id="leader2-inline-static"></div>
					</div>
<p class="p1">Morris and Lambert, 42, were <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/celebrities-who-attended-taylor-swift-travis-kelce-wedding/"><span class="s1">among the many famous faces present</span></a> when Swift and Kelce, both 36, said “I do” on July 3.</p>
<p class="p1">The bride and groom each wore bespoke, white creations from Christian Dior Haute Couture, while Morris and Lambert <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/maren-morris-reveals-taylor-swift-wedding-handkerchief/"><span class="s1">unintentionally coordinated in black</span></a>. While the “Bones” singer donned a sheath dress with a midriff cutout, Lambert rocked a lacy halter gown to match their respective charcoal-colored shoes.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/stars-marvel-over-taylor-swift-travis-kelce-wedding-details/" target="_blank">What Was Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce’s Wedding Like? Stars Detail Ceremony</a></p>
<p class="p1">Upon returning home the next morning, Morris soon realized that she brought home a different pair of pumps than the ones she had arrived at MSG in.</p>
<p class="p1">“The next day I was, like, ‘Whose shoes are these?’” she recalled. “I had to wear them because I was, like, ‘I can’t walk out of this wedding barefoot, that’s crazy.’”</p>
<p class="p1">Morris further noted that Lambert, similarly, realized that she <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/maren-morris-got-spam-text-invite-to-taylor-swifts-wedding/">also left the NYC arena</a> with someone else’s stilettos the following day.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/inside-taylor-swift-and-travis-kelces-emotional-wedding/" target="_blank">Inside Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce’s ‘Incredibly Emotional’ MSG Wedding</a></p>
<p class="p1">“When we realized we had taken each other’s shoes home, of course I’m like dying laughing, but I’m also relieved,” Morris said. “I was like, ‘I don’t want whoever’s shoes I took to think that I stole them, but someone took mine.’ I guess [the] lesson is, don’t take your shoes off. At a wedding you need to commit to the bit.”</p>
<p class="p1">According to Morris, she only found out about the shoe swap with Lambert when the latter’s stylist shared that the “Little Red Wagon” songstress walked away with a pair of “size 6 shoes” that apparently didn’t fit.</p>
<p class="p1">“[So], I texted her,” Morris added, referring to the fellow country star. “I was like, ‘That has to be the sign of a really good time.’”</p>
