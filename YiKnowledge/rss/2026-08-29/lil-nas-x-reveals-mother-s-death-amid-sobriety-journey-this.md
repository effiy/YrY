<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	“RIP TO THE BADDEST EVER! LOVE U FOREVER MOMMY,&#8221; Nas wrote on <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/DcoGMJvm5Wj/?hl=en&#038;img_index=1" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">Instagram</a> alongside throwback photos. &#8220;This one’s gonna hurt.&#8221;</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	Hill’s age and cause of death were unknown at press time. <em>Billboard</em> has reached out to Nas’ representatives for further information.</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	The “Old Town Road” hitmaker had a complicated relationship with his mother but revealed in 2020 that she struggled with addiction.</p>





<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	“I never really talk about my mom,” the Grammy winner told <em><a href="https://variety.com/2020/music/news/lil-nas-x-grammys-old-town-road-1203469735/" target="_blank">Variety</a></em> at the time. “She’s an addict, so we don’t have the closest relationship. Even trying to get her better — things didn’t quite work out. But there’s still love.&#8221;</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	The news of Hill’s passing comes days after Lil Nas X — who recently revealed that he had been diagnosed with bipolar disorder and had entered rehab for mental health support — <a href="https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/lil-nas-x-after-rehab-fans-nice-1236324464/">celebrated his “10,000th day alive.”</a></p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	“So, tomorrow is my 10,000th day alive and I made it,” he said in a video shared to his Instagram Stories. “A lot of you won’t make it, but I made it.”</p>





<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	In mid-June, he <a href="https://www.instagram.com/lilnasx/reel/DZsWO0SyxHb/" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">revealed on Instagram</a> that he had been in rehab “for a few months.” He later spent time with family in Atlanta and friends in Los Angeles to “ground myself down to Earth and get out of my head.” He added, “I have a therapist now and a psychiatrist, which is really helpful.”</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	Nas, whose real name is Montero Hill, has also <a href="https://www.billboard.com/pro/lil-nas-x-court-arrest-mental-health-treatment/">faced legal trouble</a>. In August 2025, he was charged with four felonies after allegedly attacking police officers while walking naked through the streets of Los Angeles. The charges will be dismissed if he continues with mental health treatment, <em>Variety</em> <a href="https://variety.com/2026/music/news/lil-nas-x-assault-charges-dismissed-mental-health-program-1236709714/" target="_blank">reports</a>.</p>
