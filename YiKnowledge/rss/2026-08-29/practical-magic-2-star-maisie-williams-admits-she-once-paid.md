<p>Maisie Williams knows a thing or two about witchcraft beyond her work in “Practical Magic 2,” admitting she once hired a digital mystic.</p>



<p>Specifically, at Wednesday&#8217;s European Premiere for &#8220;<a href="https://www.thewrap.com/creative-content/movies/practical-magic-2-trailer-nicole-kidman-sandra-bullock/">Practical Magic 2</a>,&#8221; the &#8220;<a href="https://www.thewrap.com/game-of-thrones-arya-stark-queer-maisie-williams/">Game of Thrones</a>&#8221; alum shared that she once hired an &#8220;Etsy witch&#8221; to assist in her career.</p>



<p>&#8220;I paid an Etsy witch to give me a role. To help me manifest a role in a movie,” she said to British Vogue during a red carpet interview. &#8220;And she told me some things that I had to say every night.&#8221;</p>



<p>Per Williams, she followed the online spellcaster&#8217;s instruction, as she said the words &#8220;every night.&#8221; However, the spell didn&#8217;t work, with Williams quipping, &#8220;It didn&#8217;t happen!&#8221;</p>





<p>While Williams may not be sold on the Etsy witch industry, the actress confessed that she does partake in superstitions.</p>



<p>&#8220;I don’t like to walk over the three consecutive drains,&#8221; she said, referring to the British superstition that it’s bad luck. &#8220;I don’t swear by it. I’ve done it. Listen, I’ve partaken. But it makes me feel uncomfortable every time, even if I sometimes end up walking into ongoing traffic. I just have to. I stick with that one.&#8221;</p>



<p>She added: &#8220;It makes me feel like everything is going to be fine. It makes me feel like I have control over the uncontrollable, which I don’t.&#8221;</p>

<p>Williams stars opposite Sandra Bullock, Nicole Kidman and Joey King in the sequel to the 1998 romantic fantasy &#8220;Practical Magic.&#8221; She steps into the role of Antonia Owens, one of Bullock&#8217;s onscreen daughters, taking over the part from the original film&#8217;s child star, Alexandra Artrip. </p>



<p>In an August <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/DcBdshHDmXd/?img_index=1">Instagram post</a>, Artrip shared that she &#8220;retired from acting after the film,&#8221; but voiced her support for her onscreen sister, Evan Rachel Wood, <a href="https://www.thewrap.com/evan-rachel-wood-why-not-appear-practical-magic-sequel-recasting/">who did not return to &#8220;Practical Magic 2&#8221;</a> to reprise her role of Kylie Owens.</p>
