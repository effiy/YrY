Continuing its commitment to high-quality <a href="https://www.ign.com/wikis/apple-tv/Original_Series">Apple TV Originals</a>, the upcoming release schedule is absolutely star-studded with talent.</p><p>You can check out what's premiering on the service in September below, or <a href="https://www.ign.com/articles/everything-coming-to-apple-tv-in-september#full-september-2026-schedule">jump ahead to the full release schedule</a> by day.</p><h2>What's New on Apple TV in September</h2><h3>Mayday - September 4</h3><section></section><p>Mayday is described as an action comedy film set during the height of the Cold War. It stars Ryan Reynolds as a a hot-shot U.S. Navy Pilot known as Lieutenant Troy &quot;Assassin&quot; Kelly who gets stranded behind enemy lines. He's discovered by ex-KGB agent Nikolai Ustinov (Kenneth Branagh), who just so happens to hold a certain reverence for American culture. The result is an unlikely buddy spy movie that looks to mix action with comedy.</p><p>If the trailer isn't enough of a preview for you, you can also check out <a href="https://www.ign.com/videos/mayday-exclusive-ryan-reynolds-and-kenneth-branagh-should-have-waited-in-this-action-packed-clip">IGN's exclusive clip from the film</a> for additional details.</p><h3>Last Seen - Premieres September 9</h3><p>The first new Apple TV series of September is a six-part adaptation of <a href="https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/11785893-the-dispatcher">Ryan David Jahn's novel The Dispatcher</a>. The story follows former detective Ian Ridley (Patrick Brammall), who is still pretty messed up since his daughter Maggie disappeared over a decade ago. Now Ridley is working as a police dispatcher when he receives a distress call from a teenage girl that he thinks could be his long lost daughter.</p><p>You'll likely recognize Brammall as the Australian love interest in this year's <a href="https://www.ign.com/articles/the-devil-wears-prada-2-review">Devil Wear's Prada 2</a></p><h3>Slow Horses Season 6 - Premieres September 16</h3><a href="https://assets-prd.ignimgs.com/2024/08/14/slowhorse-thumb-1723596945722.jpg"><img class="article-image-full-size" src="https://assets-prd.ignimgs.com/2024/08/14/slowhorse-thumb-1723596945722.jpg" title="undefined" /></a><p>Slow Horses is entering its sixth season this month, but it's already been officially renewed for Season 7. If you're not already deep into this series, there's never a wrong time to dive right in. With Gary Oldman pretty much extending his terrific performance in Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy into this series. Here's <a href="https://www.ign.com/articles/slow-horses-premiere-review-gary-oldman">what we said about the Season 1 premiere</a> back in 2022:</p><p>&quot;The premiere of Apple TV's Slow Horses sets up a sharp spy thriller centering on an excellent performance by Gary Oldman. The timely drama blends dark humor and tense action for a novel ensemble spin on the genre.&quot; - <a href="https://www.ign.com/person/SamanthaNelson">Samantha Nelson</a></p><p>For those who are caught up, the latest Season 6 trailer features the return of <a href="https://www.ign.com/articles/olivia-cooke-makes-a-surprise-return-in-the-trailer-for-slow-horses-season-6">Olivia Cooke's fan favorite character</a>.</p><h3>Brothers - Premieres September 23</h3><section></section><p>You may have seen the rumors floating around over the years that Matthew McConaughey and Woody Harrelson <a href="https://www.reddit.com/r/popculturechat/comments/1v4k7zz/matthew_mcconaughey_wishes_his_maybe_brother_from/">are actually secretly related</a>. While the pair has never publicly confirmed this to be true, they are seemingly taking advantage of those rumors with this new series. Brothers is a comedy in which McConaughey and Harrelson play fictional versions of themselves. The series explores a friendship between the two and what happens when the secret that they might actually be brothers is revealed.</p><p>The series premiere will include the first two episodes, followed by new episodes every Wednesday.</p><h2>Apple TV Full September 2026 Schedule</h2><p>Alongside the new releases for September, Apple TV is continuing to put out new episodes of <a href="https://www.ign.com/articles/ted-lasso-season-4-premiere-review">Ted Lasso Season 4</a>, Dark Matter Season 2, and Women in Blue.
