<p class="p1"><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/scott-wolf/"><b>Scott Wolf</b></a>’s estranged wife, <b>Kelley Wolf</b>, is speaking out about the pair’s ongoing divorce and legal drama.</p>
<p class="p1">“Wow, I have taken about 100 deep breaths to write this. How do I even start? It’s been a year since I recognized what everyone around me could see: I was not well, and I needed help,” Kelley, 49, began a lengthy <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/Dcm6ch6lK19/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><span class="s1">Instagram</span></a> statement shared Friday, August 28. “I felt like my brain had been hijacked, and the world was moving at the speed of a freight train.”</p><div class="mb1-wrapper" id="starter">

						<div class="htlad-leader2" id="leader2-inline-static"></div>

<p class="p1">Scott, 58, and Kelley announced in June 2025 that <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/scott-and-kelley-wolf-divorce-timeline-police-drama-explained/">they had separated after 21 years of marriage</a>. After filing for divorce that month, the <i>Party of Five </i>alum was granted temporary sole custody of the pair’s three children while Kelley was placed on involuntary psychiatric hold. Months later, Scott was granted a temporary protective order after Kelley allegedly shared his phone number via social media. (The order was dismissed earlier this year.)</p>
<p class="p1">According to Kelley, she had “no awareness” of any of her actions.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/kelley-wolf-responds-to-misinformation-amid-sobriety-and-divorce/" target="_blank">Kelley Wolf Talks Mental Health, Sobriety During 'Dark' Chapter</a></p>
<p class="p1">“I was completely delusional, and it was hitting critical mass,” she alleged on Friday. “After a year of working with incredible doctors, therapists, psychologists and healers, I have an answer.”</p>
<p class="p1">Kelley revealed that she was “diagnosed as breakthrough drug-induced mania with psychosis.”</p>
<p class="p1">“[It] means it had never happened before, and I pray never again — caused by stress, past trauma, hormonal shifts and hallucinogens mixed with prescribed medications. A perfect storm,” she said. “I won’t recount all of the chaos, but what has burdened my soul is my need to say … I am so sorry.”</p>
<p class="p1">Kelley, who tagged Scott’s account in her upload, also issued a heartfelt message to the actor directly.</p>
<p class="p1">“<a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/scott-wolf-and-kelley-wolfs-relationship-timeline-amid-divorce/">My husband of 20 years</a> was taken through a nightmare that wouldn’t even fly in a movie script; it was so brutal,” the life coach recalled. “The things I did, said, wrote and posted caused profound harm and were not based in truth or reality, and they caused pain I could never imagine to the people I love the most.”</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/kelley-wolf-claims-heinous-allegations-keep-her-from-kids/" target="_blank">Kelley Wolf Says 'Heinous' Allegations Are Keeping Her From Seeing Kids</a></p>
<p class="p1">She continued, “My brain turned on me. In my illness, I made accusations and did things that make me shudder. I have spent my life believing in love, hope, kindness and compassion. What I became was the opposite: cruel, vicious [and] mean. … In mania and psychosis, people can attack the ones they love the most and believe grand and delusional ideas.”</p>
<p class="p1">Kelley further stressed that she will “never be the same” after acknowledging that her alleged actions “caused enormous pain” to her family.</p>
<p class="p1">“[To] Scott and my kids, there won’t be a day that I don’t thank God for you. Causing you pain and being the reason you suffered will haunt me for the rest of my life,” she stated. “It was my job to protect you, honor you and keep you safe, and I became the cause of your heartbreak. I am sorry. You never gave up on me, and you fought like hell until you landed me in safe harbor. I owe you my life.”</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/kelley-wolf-claims-shes-malnourished-after-leaving-rehab/" target="_blank">Scott Wolf’s Ex Kelley Wolf Claims She Is 'Malnourished' After Exiting Rehab</a></p>
<p class="p1">Kelley pledged to “work hard” on herself moving forward.</p>
<p class="p1">“I will work hard to use this life to leave it better, to pay it forward and to never give up on the power of love,” she added. “To all of you who offered support, love, encouragement and hope … you gave us strength and showed us that we are all more loving than cruel. Thank you.”</p>
<p class="p1">Kelley also offered apologies to her family, friends and the Wolfs’ “community” in Park City, Utah.</p>
<p class="p1">“This has been our home for 20 years, and hurting you was like burning down my own house,” she concluded. “Thank you for loving our family and continuing to prove why this is a place where we found the meaning of home. I am sorry. Your grace has humbled me.”</p>
<p class="p1">Scott, who has yet to publicly address Kelley’s apology and diagnosis, <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/scott-wolf-addresses-reunion-with-ex-kelley-wolf-and-kids/"><span class="s1">reunited with his estranged wife</span></a> in February.</p>
<p class="p1">“Healing. <span class="s2">💙💙💙💙💙</span>,” he captioned a family photo via Instagram at the time. “Thank you @unistudios for an epic day at the park! So much fun! <span class="s2">🙏🏻💛</span>.”</p>
