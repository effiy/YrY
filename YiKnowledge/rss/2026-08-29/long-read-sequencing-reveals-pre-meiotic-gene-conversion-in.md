<p>Nature, Published online: 26 August 2026; <a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10901-0">doi:10.1038/s41586-026-10901-0</a></p>Single-molecule long-read sequencing of human sperm reveals variation in recombination across donors and that a substantial fraction of non-crossover gene conversions arises before meiosis, distinct from PRDM9-driven recombination and associated with fragile sites.
