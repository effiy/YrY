<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1270" src="https://www.nme.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Skepta-at-Reading-Festival-2026.-CREDIT_-Jahnay-Tennai.jpg" width="2000" /></p><p><a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/skepta">Skepta</a> played new songs from his upcoming album &#8216;Fork &amp; Knife&#8217; at <a href="https://www.nme.com/reading-leeds-festival-2025">Reading Festival 2026</a> &#8211; check them out below.</p>
<ul>
<li><b>READ MORE: </b><a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/skepta-ignorance-is-bliss-review-2503032"><b>Skepta – ‘Ignorance Is Bliss’ review</b></a></li>
</ul>
<p>The rapper played last night (August 28) ahead of his set at Leeds today. His set saw an appearance from Nigerian artist Slawn, who live spray-painted throughout the performance.</p>
<p>During his set, he debuted three new tracks from his long-awaited, forthcoming album &#8216;Fork And Knife&#8217;, which <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/skepta-releases-new-ep-mas-tiempo-vol-2-says-hes-adding-the-finishing-touches-to-celebratory-new-album-fork-and-knife-3942534">he said he was putting “finishing touches” on back in April</a>.</p>
<p>The album will serve as the follow-up to 2019&#8217;s ‘<a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/skepta-ignorance-is-bliss-review-2503032">Ignorance Is Bliss</a>‘, as well as <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/skepta-releases-new-ep-mas-tiempo-vol-2-says-hes-adding-the-finishing-touches-to-celebratory-new-album-fork-and-knife-3942534">his recent house-influenced EP ‘Más Tiempo Vol. 2’</a>. It still doesn&#8217;t have a release date, but fans in the crowd at Reading got to be the first to hear &#8216;Pandora&#8217;s Box&#8217;, &#8216;Aloe Vera&#8217; and &#8216;Innocent Smile&#8217;.</p>
<p>While he played &#8216;Toxic&#8217; his 2025 collaboration with <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/playboi-carti">Playboi Carti</a>, Skepta invited two fans onto the stage who had recently gone viral for dancing to the track while working as security at Carti&#8217;s recent London show. They subsequently lost their jobs.</p>
<p>&#8220;These are my two security guard brothers,&#8221; Skepta said, welcoming them onto the stage. &#8220;They got fired from their jobs for dancing to Playboi Carti &#8216;Toxic&#8217;, so we had to bring them here live and do this.&#8221;</p>

<p>Earlier this month, we caught Skepta&#8217;s set at Sziget Festival in Budapest. <a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/live/bring-me-the-horizon-rose-gray-sziget-2026-day-four-round-up-3962845">In <i>NME</i>‘s round-up</a>, we wrote: “Whenever an artist starts their set with one of their biggest songs, you know you’re probably in for a stacked performance. That certainly was true of Skepta’s main stage show last night, which kicked off on an instant high with ‘Shutdown’.”</p>
<p><a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/skepta-ignorance-is-bliss-review-2503032"><i>NME </i>also gave his last album ‘Ignorance Is Bliss’ four stars</a>, writing: “So although album five lacks the narrative that made ‘Konnichiwa’ so compelling (a victory lap for grime’s commercial renaissance, it also reasserted his DIY credentials), this sounds like a record from a rapper with gallons of creative juice in the tank.”</p>
