<ul>
<li><b>READ MORE: </b><a href="https://www.nme.com/features/music-features/turnstile-never-enough-album-interview-3866778"><b>Turnstile embarked on an odyssey of their own to make new album ‘Never Enough’</b></a></li>
</ul>
<p>The Baltimore hardcore band performed on the West Stage at Victoria Park last night (August 28), ahead of the first of <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/tyler-the-creator">Tyler, The Creator</a>’s two headline sets at the festival.</p>
<p>Webster, who was not on Friday’s advertised line-up but is due to play her own All Points East set today (August 29), appeared four songs into Turnstile’s performance to join them for ‘I Care’.</p>
<p>The appearance coincided with the release of <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/turnstile-announce-surprise-reimagined-never-enough-versions-album-with-elton-john-hayley-williams-blood-orange-a-g-cook-and-more-3964012">the band’s reimagined album ‘Never Enough: Versions’</a>, on which Webster&#8217;s reworked version of &#8216;I Care&#8217; appears.</p>
<p>Another notable moment came when Turnstile played their version of <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/the-stone-roses">The Stone Roses</a>’ ‘I Wanna Be Adored’, a cover they had <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/watch-turnstile-play-cover-of-the-stone-roses-i-wanna-be-adored-3964973">given its live debut at The Piece Hall in Halifax</a> two days earlier. The band originally recorded their rendition for Australian radio station Triple J’s <i>Like A Version</i> series in March, describing it as a tribute to the band&#8217;s late bassist Mani.</p>
<p>Elsewhere, Turnstile paid tribute to <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/dolly-parton">Dolly Parton</a> by walking out to her <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/kenny-rogers">Kenny Rogers</a> duet ‘Islands In The Stream&#8217;, while frontman Brendan Yates acknowledged the band’s relationship with London, telling the audience: “It’s a second home for us. Meg [Mills, guitarist] is from here. Thank you for always making it feel like home.”</p>

<li><b>READ MORE: </b><a href="https://www.nme.com/features/music-interviews/faye-webster-undressed-at-the-symphony-3598620"><b>Faye Webster is more than a sad girl indie star</b></a></li>

<p>‘Never Enough: Versions’ contains 22 new interpretations of tracks from <a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/album/turnstile-never-enough-review-3867668">Turnstile’s fourth album ‘Never Enough’</a>. As well as Webster, the project includes contributions from <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/elton-john">Elton John</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/hayley-williams">Hayley Williams</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/blood-orange">Blood Orange</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/four-tet">Four Tet</a> and <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/a-g-cook">A.G. Cook</a>.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/slayyyter">Slayyyter</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/dying-fetus">Dying Fetus</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/oklou">Oklou</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/floating-points">Floating Points</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/panda-bear">Panda Bear</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/alex-g">Alex G</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/julien-baker">Julien Baker </a>and <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/mustafa">Mustafa </a>are also among those involved.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.nme.com/reviews/album/turnstile-never-enough-review-3867668"><i>NME</i> awarded the original ‘Never Enough’ five stars</a>, praising Turnstile for “furthering their unique, pop-facing take on hardcore”. It subsequently <a href="https://www.nme.com/lists/end-of-year/best-albums-2025-3915561">landed at Number Eight on <i>NME</i>’s list of the best albums of 2025</a>, while ‘Look Out For Me’ was Number 11 on <a href="https://www.nme.com/lists/end-of-year/best-songs-2025-3912937">the best songs of the year</a>.</p>
<p>Earlier this year, Turnstile <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/turnstile-speak-out-on-heightened-state-violence-in-america-after-grammy-wins-we-are-watching-people-be-pushed-out-of-their-homes-3927668">won Best Rock Album and Best Metal Performance at the Grammy Awards</a>, with the latter prize recognising ‘Birds’.</p>
<p>The second of Tyler’s <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/tyler-the-creator-to-headline-all-points-east-2026-with-two-day-takeover-3898216">two All Points East headline shows</a> takes place today with a different supporting line-up. <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/daniel-caesar">Daniel Caesar</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/baby-keem">Baby Keem</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/dijon">Dijon</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/ghostface-killah">Ghostface Killah</a>, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/syd">Syd</a>, Webster, <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/danny-brown">Danny Brown</a> and <a href="https://www.nme.com/artists/jim-legxacy">Jim Legxacy</a> are among those due to appear.</p>
<p>Turnstile, meanwhile, are set to play at <a href="https://www.nme.com/festivals/rock-en-seine-festival">Rock En Seine</a> in France today (August 29), before heading to Lisbon&#8217;s MEO Kalorama tomorrow. Their North American tour begins in Seattle next week – see all of the dates and ticket information <a href="https://www.nme.com/news/music/turnstile-announce-surprise-reimagined-never-enough-versions-album-with-elton-john-hayley-williams-blood-orange-a-g-cook-and-more-3964012">here</a>.</p>
