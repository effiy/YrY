<p>Nature, Published online: 26 August 2026; <a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10941-6">doi:10.1038/s41586-026-10941-6</a></p>Experiments have shown that spontaneously charged water drops can damage coatings on metal surfaces, leading to corrosion of the metal.
