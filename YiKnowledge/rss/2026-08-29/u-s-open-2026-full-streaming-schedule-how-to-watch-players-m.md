<h2>When Is the 2026 U.S. Open?</h2>
<p>The 2026 edition of the U.S. Open singles tournament starts <strong>Sunday, August 30</strong>, and will last approximately two weeks until <strong>Sunday, September 13</strong>, when the Men’s Championship match takes place.</p>
<h2>How Can I Watch the 2026 U.S. Open on Cable?</h2>
<p><a href="https://www.espn.com/tennis/story/_/id/49743842/2026-us-open-where-watch-tennis-schedule">ESPN will air the entirety of the 2026 U.S. Open</a>, with day-to-day coverage of all 16 courts that will air across ESPN, ESPN2, and ESPN Deportes (Spanish).</p>
<p>In addition, ABC will broadcast all three Sundays of the tournament, including first round coverage, Round of 16 matches and the Men’s Championship Preview and Championship.</p>
<h2>How Can I Stream the 2026 U.S. Open?</h2>
<p>You can stream the 2026 U.S. Open on the ESPN App for ESPN Unlimited subscribers. <a href="https://www.disneyplus.com/browse/page-8f61ffe0-e01b-4fcd-8be7-7f42db4e331a">Disney+</a> subscribers can watch select Day 1 matches directly within the app, even without a bundle subscription to ESPN.</p>
<h2>Where Is the 2026 U.S. Open Taking Place?</h2>
<p>As usual, the 2026 U.S. Open is taking place at the USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center, Queens, NY.</p>
