<p>A new movie starring the late Power Rangers legend <a href="https://www.comingsoon.net/tag/jason-david-frank"><strong>Jason David Frank</strong></a> is heading to theaters, and will see him reunited with former Power Rangers stars.</p>



<p>The Legend of the White Dragon, a new action movie starring Frank, is out in theaters now, and sees Frank appearing with several of his Power Rangers stars, including Jason Faunt, Cerina Vincent, and Ciara Hanna. Other stars in the movie include Aaron Schoenke, Rachele Brooke Smith, Michael Madsen, Andrew Bachelor, David Ramsey, and Mark Dacascos.</p>



<p>The Legend of the White Dragon is available now as part of a special three-day theatrical event, tied to the anniversary of the premiere of the original Mighty Morphin Power Rangers TV series.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-what-is-jason-david-frank-s-legend-of-the-white-dragon-movie-about">What is Jason David Frank&#8217;s Legend of the White Dragon movie about?</h2>



<p>The Legend of the White Dragon follows Frank as a mysterious, fugitive hero who must try and clear his name after being thrust into chaos.</p>



<p>&#8220;Jason David Frank is the White Dragon. Forced to hide in the shadows, fugitive hero Erik Reed must clear his name to reunite with his family. Standing in his way is a mysterious villain obsessed with destroying the White Dragon for good,&#8221; reads the film&#8217;s official synopsis.</p>



<p>Frank is best known for his role as Tommy Oliver in the Power Rangers franchise, and was a fan-favorite over the years on the convention circuit and in sparse cameo roles elsewhere. Tragically, Frank passed away in 2022, at the age of 49.</p>
