<p>Nature, Published online: 26 August 2026; <a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10893-x">doi:10.1038/s41586-026-10893-x</a></p>Analysis of tracking data from Mars orbiters reveals time-variable gravity field behaviour reflecting the structure underlying the Martian crustal dichotomy and indicates the preservation of a 200–400 °C thermal anomaly in the present-day mantle below the southern highlands.
