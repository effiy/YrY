<p>Fans tuning into <strong>Leanne Morgan</strong>’s sitcom, <em>Leanne</em>, should gear up for a lot of jokes surrounding weight and body image.</p>
<p>The first season <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/news/fall-2025-tv-schedule-premiere-dates-for-new-returning-shows/">follows a woman from Tennessee</a> who finds herself starting over after her husband leaves her for another woman. The show is inspired by Morgan’s stand-up comedy.</p><div class="mb1-wrapper" id="starter">
						<div class="htlad-mb1" id="mb1-static"></div>
						<div class="htlad-leader2" id="leader2-inline-static"></div>
					</div>
<p>“Leanne uses humor to cope,” Morgan told Netflix’s <em>Tudum</em> in July 2025. “Sometimes you just have to laugh to keep from crying. She leans on her support system — her family, close friends and church community — to get through it.”</p>
<p>Morgan, who is the star, cocreator and executive producer, <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/news/summer-tv-preview-2025-love-island-the-bear-and-more/">was thrilled to bring her comedic</a> material to a scripted format, adding, “This is something I’ve dreamed of since I was a little girl, and it was just as exciting as I imagined.”</p>
<p><a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/news/fall-2025-tv-schedule-premiere-dates-for-new-returning-shows/" target="_blank">Fall 2025 TV Schedule: See When Your Favorite Shows Will Air</a></p>
<p>She continued: “Learning a new medium was definitely a challenge — memorizing new scripts every week was totally new for me — but I loved it. As a stand-up, the comedic timing felt natural, and filming in front of a live audience was especially comfortable. By the end of the season, it really felt like home.”</p>
<p>While there is some heart to the show, it’s hard to miss the amount of jokes surrounding eating disorders and physical appearance. Keep scrolling for a breakdown:</p>
<h3>Episode 1</h3>
<figure class="wp-caption "><img alt="Leanne Sitcom" class="alignnone size-large credit-added wp-image-4057563" height="600" src="https://www.usmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/LEANNE_101_241014_PM_00070_RC2.jpg?w=900" width="900" /><figcaption class="wp-caption-text"><span class="credit">Patrick McElhenney/Netflix</span></figcaption></figure>
<p>In the series premiere, Leanne made a comment about how she “will always take a laxative” as she deals with her split from her husband. She later said she would “carry on and go back to Weight Watchers.”</p>
<p>Before the first episode was over, Leanne made a joke about the differences between her and her daughter. “I took a buttload of Dexatrim before I knew I was pregnant with her,” she added. “And I kept wondering why I wasn’t losing weight.”</p>
<h3>Episode 2</h3>
<p>Leanne’s father <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/pictures/most-memorable-tv-siblings-through-the-years/">claimed her sister, Carol (<strong>Kristen Johnston</strong>)</a>, is the one who “should be counting calories” because she’s single.</p>
<h3>Episode 3</h3>
<p>After going out with Carol, Leanne told her sister she was trying to hide her stomach at the restaurant.</p>
<p>“Stop with the body-shaming,” Carol said, to which Leanne fired back, “Well I am ashamed of my body.”</p>
<h3>Episode 4</h3>
<p>Leanne <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-body/pictures/body-positive-celebrities-share-wisdom-inspiring-quotes-video/">recalled starring in her estranged</a> husband’s first late night TV commercial “before the babies — when eating disorders weren’t frowned on like they are now.” Later in the episode, Leanne compared herself to a “pelican with a mahi-mahi in my throat.” Carol then suggested a “nip tuck” to help Leanne feel better about herself.</p>
<h3>Episode 5</h3>
<p>Once Leanne found out that Bill’s (<strong>Ryan Stiles</strong>) mistress was expecting a baby, she said she had “seven months to get skinny” while her ex’s new girlfriend “gets fat.” This inspired Leanne to go on a diet where she was only sipping bone broth and watching her calories.</p>
<p>“If I am going to build up my self-esteem, starvation is my foundation,” she told Carol.</p>
<h3>Episode 7</h3>
<p>During a visit to the gynecologist, Leanne asked her doctor to “shave a couple of pounds” off her final weight. Her OBGYN argued that her weight was “normal for a woman her age” but Leanne still wanted to check her weight again to see if it was lower.</p>
<h3>Episode 8</h3>
<p>Leanne accidentally chugged Carol’s drink — which turned out to be Metamucil — and later said the appetite suppressant helped her go “down a size.”</p>
<h3>Episode 9</h3>
<p>In the second half of the season, Leanne called “hitting your goal weight” a natural high before later discussing how she has “given up carbs.” Mary (<strong>Jayma Mays</strong>), meanwhile, mentioned how she weighed 97 pounds.</p>
<h3>Episode 10</h3>
<p><img alt="" class="size-large wp-image-4057564" height="600" src="https://www.usmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/LEANNE_102_241114_PM_00896_R2.jpg?w=900" width="900" /></p>
<p>Bill poked fun at Carol by asking if she “put on weight,” adding, “Mainly around your neck.”</p>

<h3>Episode 11</h3>
<p>There was a dig made about one character’s ankles looking too big.</p>
<h3>Episode 12</h3>
<p>Carol was told to “tilt up more” in a photo or put a book in front of her neck as an insult.</p>
<h3>Episode 14</h3>
<p>While reflecting on the past, Leanne recalled being on “diet pills” and drinking Tab.</p>
<h3>Episode 15</h3>
<p>Leanne <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-body/pictures/stars-who-battled-eating-disorders-201128/">talked about her eating habits</a> — specifically why she was eating like it was “the last supper.”</p>
<h3>Episode 16</h3>
<figure class="wp-caption "><img alt="Leanne Morgan" class="alignnone size-large credit-added wp-image-4057565" height="600" src="https://www.usmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/LEANNE_106_250117_PM_00775_R.jpg?w=900" width="900" /><figcaption class="wp-caption-text"><span class="credit">Patrick McElhenney/Netflix</span></figcaption></figure>
<p>In the season finale, Leanne was worried about going away to the beach with Andrew, which led to jokes about wearing chunky sweaters instead of a bathing suit — and how she needed “six months prep.” After sending her food back at the restaurant, Leanne told Carol her concerns about “flopping around” in front of her boyfriend.</p>
