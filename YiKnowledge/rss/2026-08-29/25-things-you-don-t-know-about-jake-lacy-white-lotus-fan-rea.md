<p>“That, or they throw some family member under the bus,” he continued. “‘You are just like my brother-in-law…’”</p>
<p>Lacy’s latest movie is<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5jwTOyEyXq4"><em> A Mosquito in the Ear</em></a>, in theaters now.</p>
<p>Keep scrolling for more fun facts from Lacy – including an iconic email from <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/diane-keaton/"><strong>Diane Keaton</strong></a> he’ll always treasure.</p>
<p>1. When it’s my turn to cook, it’s definitely steak on the grill.</p>
<p>2. When people talk about <em>The White Lotus</em> to me, they typically start [with], “We couldn’t stand your character!” That, or they throw some family member under the bus: “You are just like my brother-in-law…”</p>
<p>3. I just finished [bingeing] <em>Task</em>. I have always loved <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/mark-ruffalo/"><strong>Mark Ruffalo</strong></a>, but my God, is he good on that show.</p>
<p>4. My celebrity crush growing up was <strong>Chilli</strong> from TLC.</p>
<p>5. My dad lost his wallet once when we were at the beach. I was probably 6 or 8. A couple found it and knew my grandparents and called. My dad and I drove together to get it, just [us two]… I think about that small moment often.</p>
<p>6. What I remember from my time on <em>Girls</em> was thinking everyone was so nice and talented on a whole other level. I felt like I was trying to keep up!</p>
<p>7. I used to read reviews when I was younger. But that search for acceptance from outside sources leads to insanity.</p>
<p>8. I have two little boys, so most Saturdays are filled with sports and activities and visits to the library.</p>
<p>9. Working on <em>All Her Fault</em> was wonderful. <a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrities/sarah-snook/"><strong>Sarah [Snook]</strong></a> would bring her ukulele, and I would bring this little guitar, and we would pass the time playing together.</p>
<p>10. It’s summer, so the MLB app is getting a lot of use at the moment. I can get the NESN broadcast for all the Sox games.</p>
<p>11. Idon’t do a ton of karaoke, but I go <strong>Black Sabbath</strong>. Specifically, “War Pigs.” I like it because there’s nothing cute about it. You have to go for it.</p>
<p>12. Everyone was so kind when I started on <em>The Office</em>. It was my favorite show, and I was so nervous to join and looked up to all of them.</p>
<p>13. My favorite comfort meal is Detroit-style pepperoni pizza.</p>
<p>14. My first car was a black ’98 Honda Accord coupe. Stick.</p>
<p>15. I have been so fortunate to travel the world for this job. I got to work in Havana a year and a half ago; my first time in Cuba. It was incredible.</p>
<p>16. I was in <strong>William Friedkin</strong>’s final movie, <em>The Caine Mutiny Court-Martial</em>. He was an amazing guy and an absolute legend.</p>
<p>17. I think I quote <em>Arrested Development</em> more than any movie.</p>
<p>18. I had a meeting with <strong>Ivan Reitman</strong> about 15 years ago. <em>Ghostbusters</em> was, and is, my favorite movie. And he was so incredibly kind. I didn’t mention <em>Ghostbusters</em> or <em>Stripes</em> or <em>Animal House</em> in the meeting. But before I left, I asked for a piece of paper and wrote him a note thanking him for everything.</p>
<p>19. The book I come back to again and again is<em> Nine Stories</em> by J.D. Salinger.</p>
<p>20. If I wasn’t acting, I’d like to work in historic preservation in coastal New England.</p>
<p>21. “Astral Weeks” by<strong> Van Morrison</strong> brings back great memories. Some of those songs played during my wedding and it reminds me of being back home.</p>
<p>22. Diane Keaton sent me a very kind email after she saw a cut of <em>Love the Coopers</em>. She was so cool and generous. It meant so much for her to reach out like that.</p>
<p>23. When I was 17, I was hired to rebuild a mini-golf course at a marina. I had zero experience and zero skills… I smoked a lot of cigs, got a hell of a tan and completed one and a half holes in 10 weeks. 24. I still want to learn Italian. And direct a film — in English.</p>
<p>25. If I’m in a [funk], I’ve got a few friends I call. Guys that can offer up compassion and reality in equal measure.</p>
