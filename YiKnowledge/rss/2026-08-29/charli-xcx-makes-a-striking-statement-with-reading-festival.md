<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	Earlier this week, <a href="https://www.billboard.com/artist/charli-xcx">Charli xcx</a> took a trip down memory lane. Sitting with BBC Radio 1’s Jack Saunders, she reflected on her <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HY3Xv5fjPkI" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">previous appearances as a performer</a> at Reading Festival over the years. She was hard on herself in the way you can be when confronted with your younger, more idealistic, carefree self. “It’s cringing me out, if I’m honest,” she said of her 2017 performance. Her review of her 2019 show? She didn’t like her fringe much, she added. “This is a collection of some of the worst hairstyles and hair dyes ever throughout Reading &amp; Leeds, it seems.”</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	This is typical Charli, though. Few acts in modern times are as achingly aware of their presentation, their overarching narrative as a creator, or how their music or eras are received. This mindset fueled <em>Brat</em>, an unapologetic riposte that had, indeed, buckets of intentionality behind it. <em><a href="https://www.billboard.com/lists/charli-xcx-music-fashion-film-best-songs/">Music, Fashion, Film</a></em>, her new LP, finds her ruminating deeply on the direction of her career and if being a recording artist is even something she wants to do anymore, particularly as her head is turned by acting roles.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	So how will Charli xcx look back on her headline set at Reading Festival on Friday (Aug. 28)? It seems safe to say she will look back on the set with bullish pride. This set is loaded with additional subtext: It’s the first U.K. performance since the release of <em>Music, Fashion, Film</em>, her second No. 1 on the Official Albums Chart in 2026 following <em>Wuthering Heights</em> soundtrack in February 2026. It was also her first time headlining the iconic festival she grew up attending, as mentioned in her BBC Radio 1 interview. The stakes were high, not least because of Charli’s own expectations.</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	The striking new stage production — <a href="https://www.billboard.com/music/pop/charli-xcx-lorde-lollapalooza-girl-so-confusing-special-1236307408/">first debuted at Lollapalooza last month</a> — brings a thrilling edge to Charli’s performance and the songs sound fuller, less bitty than they do on record. She commanded the stage with a singular focus and was bolstered by a live band in places and a group of dancers; the result was a statement of epic proportions.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="paragraph larva // lrv-u-margin-lr-auto  lrv-a-font-body-m   ">
	These were the five best moments from Charli’s headline debut at Reading Festival.</p>
