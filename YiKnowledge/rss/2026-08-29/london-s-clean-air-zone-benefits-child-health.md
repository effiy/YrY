<p>Nature Medicine, Published online: 28 August 2026; <a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/d41591-026-00043-1">doi:10.1038/d41591-026-00043-1</a></p>Children’s lung function growth has accelerated since the introduction of London’s ultra-low-emission zone, which addresses a critical evidence gap and supports wider adoption of this approach.
