There's a running joke in the world of THEA-TAH that Shakespeare's Hamlet is a play made up entirely of quotations. I often get the same feeling when I play a fantasy RPG, and especially a fantasy RPG created with toolkits such as EasyRPG or <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/rpg-maker">RPG Maker</a> that come with their own freely reusable assets, resulting in a slew of homebrew RPGs that can seem interchangeable, on the surface.
