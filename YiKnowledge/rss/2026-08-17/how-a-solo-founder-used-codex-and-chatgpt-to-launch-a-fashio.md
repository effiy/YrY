<div class="youtube-wrap" id="youtube2-P03ZNceXe2A"><div class="youtube-inner"></div></div><p><strong><span>Yana Welinder</span></strong><span> is the solo founder of Yana Bana, an AI-native fashion brand built  with AI as her technical co-founder, starting from hand-drawn sketches and ending with runway photos, CAD files for 3D printing, and a live Stripe-connected pre-order site&#8212;no engineers required. A former product leader, she brings an operator&#8217;s rigor to her creative process: her &#8220;fashion prompt&#8221; is a detailed spec covering silhouette, volume, fabric beavior, movement, and sound, and watching her use Codex plus computer use to navigate 3D design software that&#8217;s entirely new to her is a clarifying demo of what today&#8217;s toolset actually makes possible.</span></p><div class="pullquote"><p><strong>Listen or watch on <a href="https://youtu.be/P03ZNceXe2A">YouTube</a>, <a
