<h2>Household Names<br /><span>And The Ones About To Be</span></h2>
<figure><img alt="Charli XCX at Lollapalooza 2026" src="https://mcusercontent.com/46d0fa24d2473a4222c769631/images/df857b89-6b51-509c-9ffd-351ed34ffd6e.jpg" style="width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; border-radius: 8px; margin-top: 20px; border: 0;" /><figcaption>Charli XCX at Lollapalooza 2026</figcaption></figure>
<p style="font-size: 16px; line-height: 1.6; color: #dddddd; margin: 20px 0 0;">While it might seem like there&#8217;s a massive music event on a weekly basis, the reality is that only a handful are loud enough to permeate culture and make sure everyone is paying attention. Lollapalooza is firmly in this group, taking over Grant Park in Chicago every summer while people at home catch the biggest artists on the livestream. This year, it&#8217;s where <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/Dbf7aT0Dnhg/?img_index=1" style="color: #F0FE69; text-decoration: underline;">Charli XCX chose to make her first big-stage live performance of the <em>Music, Fashion, Film</em> era</a>. For those wondering what comes after <em>Brat</em>, Charli proved her peak may still be ahead.</p>
<p style="font-size: 16px; line-height: 1.6; color: #dddddd; margin: 14px 0 0;">But Lollapalooza isn&#8217;t just about the household names. Artists like <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/Dbd-13RFVPc/?img_index=1" style="color: #F0FE69; text-decoration: underline;">Bella Kay</a> and Stella Lefty broke through for the first time, with full audiences shouting their songs back at them. sombr solidified his place in pop-rock with a 5 Seconds of Summer collab, <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/Dbe6JgujRe0/?img_index=1" style="color: #F0FE69; text-decoration: underline;">Olivia Rodrigo crossed generations by popping out with The Smashing Pumpkins</a>, and <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/DblIhAADrg0/?img_index=1" style="color: #F0FE69; text-decoration: underline;">the Turnstile mosh pit was as chaotic and joyful as it gets</a>.</p>
<p style="font-size: 16px; line-height: 1.6; color: #dddddd; margin: 14px 0 0;">There are still a few of these events remaining on this year&#8217;s calendar, and next year is approaching fast.
