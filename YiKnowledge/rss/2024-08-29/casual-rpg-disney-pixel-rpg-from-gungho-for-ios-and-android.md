<p><a class="app-icon" href="/games/disney-pixel-rpg" style="float: left; margin-top: 4px; margin-right: 10px;"><img alt="" src="https://toucharcade.com/wp-content/images/app/6502584695/100x100bb.jpg" style="border-radius: 20px;" /></a> Last month, <a href="https://toucharcade.com/2024/07/08/disney-pixel-rpg-game-mobile-iphone-android-gameplay-release-date/">GungHo announced</a> a new casual RPG for mobile in the form of <em>Disney Pixel RPG</em> <a href="http://toucharcade.com/link/http://itunes.apple.com/us/app/id6502584695?mt=8">(Free)</a>, due later this year. Today, Gungho has released (<a href="https://www.gematsu.com/2024/08/disney-pixel-rpg-debut-trailer">via Gematsu</a>) the first trailer for <em>Disney Pixel RPG</em>. <em>Disney Pixel RPG</em> aims to let players explore with pixel versions of Disney characters across multiple worlds with battles, action, rhythm, and more challenges. It features an original story taking you on a journey with Mickey Mouse and other characters with many customization options. Watch the first gameplay trailer for <em>Disney Pixel RPG</em> below:</p>
