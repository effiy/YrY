<p><a class="app-icon" href="/games/final-fantasy-brave-exvius" style="float: left; margin-top: 4px; margin-right: 10px;"><img alt="" src="https://toucharcade.com/wp-content/images/app/1078553808/100x100bb.jpg" style="border-radius: 20px;" /></a> Square Enix just announced that <em>Final Fantasy Brave Exvius</em> <a href="http://toucharcade.com/link/http://itunes.apple.com/us/app/id1078553808?mt=8">(Free)</a> is shutting down on October 30th (this year) following its debut back in June 2016. Since launching on mobile, <em>Final Fantasy Brave Exvius</em> has seen many collaborations including ones with some of Square Enix&#8217;s best games like <a href="http://toucharcade.com/2017/08/08/final-fantasy-brave-exvius-nier-automata-event/">NieR: Automata</a> and also its other titles like <em>Final Fantasy XV</em>. It is being shut down because <a href="https://www.facebook.com/ffbeen/posts/pfbid0qoNuQBi72nxmQBpRsd8MzmEhN72gvzupDzMoovEWvY341oefC1heYgdU4UT1XuDyl">Square Enix says</a> that it will be &#8220;difficult to maintain the app experience." Following this announcement, in app purchases will no longer be offered but you will be able to use anything you&#8217;ve bought until service ends. Bundles will no longer be sold as well.</p>
