<p><a class="app-icon" href="/games/animal-crossing-pocket-camp" style="float: left; margin-top: 4px; margin-right: 10px;"><img alt="" src="https://toucharcade.com/wp-content/images/app/1179915619/100x100bb.jpg" style="border-radius: 20px;" /></a> Nintendo <a href="https://twitter.com/Pocket_Camp/status/1826502311830860094/">just announced</a> that <em>Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp</em> <a href="http://toucharcade.com/link/http://itunes.apple.com/us/app/id1179915619?mt=8">(Free)</a> is shutting down on November 28th at 3 PM UTC. <em>Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp</em> debuted on iOS and Android nearly seven years ago, and it has since received multiple content and feature updates. From November 28th, you will no longer be able to play this version of <em>Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp</em>, but the team is developing a version of <em>Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp</em> for mobile with save carrying over. This will be a flat-fee paid version of <em>Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp</em>. Social aspects from the original game will not be available here. Details for the new paid app are <a href="https://faq.ac-pocketcamp.com/hc/en-us/articles/36353725150489-What-is-the-paid-version-of-the-app">here.</a> A full release date announcement for the paid app will be made around October. Check out the full announcement below:</p>

That game deserved so much better. At least I still have the music from it. Either way, you can play <em>Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp</em> on iOS and Android right now ahead of it shutting down in November.
