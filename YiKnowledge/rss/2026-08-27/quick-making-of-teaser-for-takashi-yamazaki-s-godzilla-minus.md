<em>"I believe this team can create something greater than the last film. By doing things we've never done before and taking on new challenges."</em> Toho debuted a new behind-the-scenes promo teaser for <strong><em>Godzilla Minus Zero</em></strong>, the highly anticipated sequel to the global smash hit <em>Godzilla Minus One</em> <a href="https://www.firstshowing.net/2023/glorious-final-trailer-for-japans-godzilla-minus-one/" title="Glorious Final Trailer for Japan's 'Godzilla Minus One' Now In Theaters">from 2023</a>. Director <strong>Takashi Yamazaki</strong> has been hard at work on this sequel ever since that first one roared its way to Oscar <a href="https://www.firstshowing.net/2024/academy-awards-winners-2024/">glory</a>. This next one is releasing in theaters worldwide starting <a href="https://www.firstshowing.net/schedule2026/#nov">in November</a> – all of this footage shows the director on set but doesn't reveal any new hints or show too much yet. It's 1949 just after the war, when the Americans were still all over Japan helping them rebuild. But they must also deal with Gojira. This story continues with the Shikishima family as they face an all-new calamity. With both <strong>Ryunosuke Kamiki</strong> as Koichi Shikishima and <strong>Minami Hamabe</strong> as Noriko Oishi returning in the same roles. Also starring <strong>Miou Tanaka</strong>, <strong>Sakura Ando</strong>, <strong>Hidetaka Yoshioka</strong>, <strong>Yuki Yamada</strong>, & <strong>Kuranosuke Sasaki</strong>. Having survived the previous deadly struggle alongside Shikishima, these beloved characters stand ready to face Godzilla once again. A few more glimpses beyond the <a href="https://www.firstshowing.net/2026/must-see-teaser-for-takashi-yamazakis-godzilla-minus-zero-sequel/" title="Must See Teaser for Takashi Yamazaki's 'Godzilla Minus Zero' Sequel">first teaser trailer in April</a>. I'm digging the way they're hyping this up! And I definitely do think it might just end up better than <em>Minus Zero</em>.
