      <p>Today, Rockstar finally raised the curtain on Grand Theft Auto VI, and we got our <a href="https://gameinformer.com/2026/08/27/grand-theft-auto-vi-trailer-provides-lengthy-first-look-at-gameplay-including-story">first good look at what the game will actually play like</a>. While we saw footage of GTA staples like shootouts and police chases, anyone who's played one of these massive open-world games knows that many of the most memorable moments are the optional side activities. Today's trailer showed a lot of them, and while we don't yet know if all of them are actually playable, we've gathered every activity we could spot in one article.</p>
