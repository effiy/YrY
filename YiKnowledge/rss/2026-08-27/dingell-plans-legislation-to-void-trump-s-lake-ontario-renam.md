Rep. Debbie Dingell (D-Mich.) plans to introduce a bill Monday that would nullify President Trump&#8217;s executive order renaming Lake Ontario to “Lake America.” The one-page Hands Off Our Great Lakes Act would ensure the executive order “shall have no force or effect” and “restore the name of Lake Ontario,” according to documents obtained by Politico.&#8230;
