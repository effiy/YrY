<p>Earlier this year at the <a href="https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/">Cannes Film Festival</a>, international cinema enthusiasts were shaken by one of the year&#8217;s most profoundly challenging dramas: Cristian Mungiu’s <em>Fjord</em>. Today, the impactful film is set to make waves online with a brand-new trailer ahead of its anticipated <strong>October 9</strong> theatrical debut. The intense new promo offers a chilling look into the private lives of a devout Romanian family who relocate to Norway, only to find their domestic foundation torn apart when local authorities accuse the parents of child abuse.</p>



<p>Filmed on location in Ålesund and the breathtaking, isolated expanses of Hjørundfjorden in Western Norway, the trailer exudes a naturalistic, bone-chilling atmosphere that perfectly complements <a href="https://www.joblo.com/tag/sebastian-stan/">Sebastian Stan</a>’s cold and deeply unnerving performance. Throughout the footage, Stan’s character devolves from a strict, deeply religious patriarch into someone completely unrecognizable, a descent captured in a terrifying shot that directly evokes Jack Nicholson’s iconic turn as Jack Torrance in <em>The Shining</em>.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-a-cold-isolated-backdrop-for-a-devastating-family-breakdown">A Cold, Isolated Backdrop for a Devastating Family Breakdown</h2>



<p>Here is the official synopsis for <em><a href="https://www.neonrated.com/">Fjord</a></em>:</p>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p>In <em>Fjord</em>, a Norwegian woman and her devout Romanian husband relocate with their five children to a remote, close-knit, coastal town. Their teenage children form a fast bond with a neighboring family as unease simmers over their rigid beliefs. When allegations of abuse emerge, they are forced to confront a system of judgment that blurs the lines between concern and prejudice.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-our-thoughts-on-fjord">Our Thoughts on <em>Fjord</em></h2>
</blockquote>



<p>The film has already garnered immense critical acclaim ahead of its fall release. In <strong><a href="https://www.joblo.com/fjord-cannes-review/">Chris Bumbray&#8217;s review from Cannes</a></strong>, he praised the movie’s stark, realistic aesthetic, noting that <em>Fjord</em> is:</p>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p>&#8220;&#8230;gorgeously shot on location in Norway, with the widescreen lensing capturing the winter avalanches that happen with alarming regularity but are just a fact of life for the residents. The film also notably has no musical score, with only a bit of source music used to enhance the mood at times.&#8221;</p>
</blockquote>



<p>With Neon handling domestic distribution following its triumphant festival debut, <em>Fjord</em> is positioning itself as a serious contender for the upcoming awards season. Expect it to be a frontrunner for a Best International Feature Oscar nomination, with Stan standing out as a formidable dark-horse candidate for Best Actor given his multilingual, transformative performance.</p>
