<img src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2024/10/31121939/russo-brothers-production-company-ai-expert-agbo-feature.jpeg" /><p><i>Electric State</i> director Joe Russo has a new assignment for moviegoers looking to enjoy Marvel&#8217;s latest crossover event: Paying $20 to see a new cut of his last hit movie, <i>Avengers: Endgame</i>. In celebration of Marvel&#8217;s upcoming epic <em>Avengers: Doomsday</em>, 2019&#8217;s <i>Avengers: Endgame</i> returns to theaters this fall for <i>Avengers: Endgame Encore</i>. The new version has been retrofitted for Disney&#8217;s proprietary Imax competitor, Infinity Vision, and includes a &#8220;custom introduction, additional new footage, and a special end tag exclusive to Infinity Vision and IMAX releases,&#8221; <a href="https://deadline.com/2026/06/avengers-endgame-re-release-retitle-disney-infinity-vision-1236966128/">according to <i>Deadline</i></a>. While the re-release might seem like a chance for lapsed viewers who haven&#8217;t checked in with the non-<i>Spider-Man</i> end of the MCU in seven years to catch up, Russo wants to make something clear: If you don&#8217;t see this custom intro, new footage, and premium large format-exclusive post-credits scene, you won&#8217;t understand <i>Doomsday</i>.&nbsp;</p><!-- RevContent --> <div id="revcontent-hidden"> <!-- revisit -->
<p>&#8220;It&#8217;s going to connect the story of the two movies,&#8221; <a href="https://x.com/Fandango/status/2092288506735284245">Russo tells Fandango</a>. &#8220;I think [<i>Endgame Encore</i>] is required viewing for following the story in <i>Doomsday</i>.&#8221; His brother, Anthony, agrees: &#8220;If you saw <i>Endgame</i> when it originally was released, you couldn&#8217;t see this coming.&#8221; All that tracks, considering <i>Doomsday</i> star Sebastian Stan says the <a href="https://people.com/sebastian-stan-on-avengers-doomsday-script-returning-as-bucky-12062350">Russos directed the film without a script</a>.</p><!-- inlinecontent_1 --> </p><!-- admarker --> 
<p>We suppose it&#8217;s easier to watch one slightly longer three-hour superhero movie than the apparently skippable titles Marvel&#8217;s released in the interim. However, anyone hoping Marvel would stop assigning homework to innocent moviegoers looking for a fun night out might want to stick with <i>Dune: Part Three</i> this winter. <i>Avengers: Doomsday</i> has already made $35 million in pre-sale tickets, making it the highest-grossing pre-sale ever, and it&#8217;s expected to make $300 million in its opening weekend. Let&#8217;s hope those lucky ticket buyers got the fall reading list.</p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p>
