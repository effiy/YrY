<p><strong>Not such a Cowabummer.</strong></p><p>It's been a while since we've had a TMNT game on Switch, huh? Well, next month, we'll be back to business as usual, as Trailmark Games has announced <a href="https://www.nintendolife.com/games/nintendo-switch-2/teenage_mutant_ninja_turtles_arcade_wrath_of_the_mutants_-_ninja_edition">Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles Arcade: Wrath of the Mutants - Ninja Edition</a>, a Switch 2 re-release of the 2024 console port.</p><p>You'd be forgiven for not remembering <a href="https://www.nintendolife.com/games/nintendo-switch/teenage_mutant_ninja_turtles_arcade_wrath_of_the_mutants">Wrath of the Mutants</a>. This GameMill-published beat 'em up was rather forgettable when it broke free of its arcade cabinet origins, with its short playtime and repetitive gameplay pushing it to the wayside as it launched in the midst of something of a TMNT renaissance. Fortunately, the upcoming Ninja Edition hopes to make things slightly better.</p><p>Read the <a href="https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/08/beat-em-up-tmnt-wrath-of-the-mutants-gets-a-switch-2-ninja-edition-next-month">full article on nintendolife.com</a></p>
