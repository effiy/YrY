<img src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2019/10/14173333/wvsouqs5kohx2rz0nzg4.jpg" /><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">While the world mourns <a href="https://www.avclub.com/dolly-parton-dies-age-80">the passing of superstar of country music and film Dolly Parton</a>, we should take comfort in knowing that Dolly, sweetheart that she was, didn&#8217;t leave us without a parting gift—it might just take a minute until we can appreciate it.&nbsp;</span></p><!-- RevContent --> <div id="revcontent-hidden"> <!-- revisit -->
<p><a href="https://variety.com/2026/music/news/dolly-parton-song-buried-time-capsule-my-place-in-history-1236844669/"><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Variety</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> reports</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> that a time capsule was buried by the Parton team with her blessing in 2015, containing cassette and CD recordings of a new song—previously </span><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=INHV3lvyBfM&amp;t=165s"><span style="font-weight: 400;">confirmed to exist by Parton herself in 2022</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> on </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">The</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Kelly</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Clarkson</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Show</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">—that has gone unheard by us. </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Variety </span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">also notes that the recordings are packed alongside &#8220;their corresponding players,&#8221; because Dolly was good like that, making sure everyone had what they needed. </span><a href="https://x.com/BuzzingPop/status/2092416846712410603?s=20"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The chestnut &#8220;Dream Box&#8221;</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> has been buried under the famous Dollywood DreamMore Resort And Spa, which is to remain unopened until the resort&#8217;s &#8220;30th anniversary in 2045,&#8221; just before Parton&#8217;s 100th birthday on January 19.&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></p><!-- inlinecontent_1 --> </p><!-- admarker --> 
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“There is a song,&#8221; Parton told Clarkson. &#8220;I figure it’ll probably disintegrate, probably nobody ever hears it. That’s what bothers me, to think that it’s gonna be a song that nobody’s ever gonna hear if it rots in there before they open it. It’s a really good song. But yes, I have a song that’s buried that nobody’s gonna hear—I don’t know whose damn idea that was.”</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“The chestnut ‘Dream Box’ is a repository of Dolly’s wishes and memories for the future,&#8221; a placard reads on the box. &#8220;It will not be opened until Dolly’s birthday on January 19, 2046. The Dream Box itself is tribute to her original mentor and uncle, Bill Owens, who has worked tirelessly to preserve the American Chestnut tree. As a final tribute, Dolly placed a song which she has written to be released as her last. Entitled ‘My Place In History,’ only Dolly will know its melody and content until 2046.”&nbsp;</span></p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p><!-- admarker --> 
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Dolly&#8217;s fans can expect more posthumous Parton goodness as they eagerly await the artist&#8217;s seemingly final unreleased recording. </span><a href="https://www.dollyslifeofmanycolors.com/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Her &#8220;Life Of Many Colors&#8221; museum and hotel</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is set to open in Nashville later this year; she&#8217;s endorsed a line of Purina dog treats planned for 2027; a coffee brand called &#8220;Cup Of Ambition&#8221; is </span><a href="https://www.cupofambition.com/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">expected to launch later this year</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">; and </span><a href="https://www.avclub.com/dolly-parton-announces-original-broadway-musical-1851524484"><span style="font-weight: 400;">a Broadway musical about her life</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Dolly:</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">A</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">True</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Original</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Musical</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">, opens on January 19, 2027. All this comes amid an energetic campaign by Nashville comedian Lydia Popovich </span><a href="https://www.billboard.com/music/country/change-org-petition-rename-nashville-airport-dolly-parton-1236326222/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">to rename the Nashville International Airport to &#8220;Dolly Parton International Airport,&#8221;</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> an ambitious yet modest tribute to an entertainer who kept busy giving so much that her generosity continues to impress us even now that she&#8217;s at rest. </span></p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p>
