I also found myself there between errands or whenever I wanted something quick that didn&#x27;t feel like a typical fast-food meal.</p><p>Cava was even launched in my hometown in Maryland, and there&#x27;s a restaurant there today. I don&#x27;t add avocado or premium proteins, so my total typically comes out to around $12 or $12.50, depending on tax. That is a dessert like a cookie or brownie. So, maybe skip adding a sweet treat to your meal.</p><h2 class="rssH2">The underrated perks of Cava Rewards</h2><p>The opportunity to earn points (and redeem them for food) is what initially caught my attention, but Cava Rewards offers several other worthwhile perks.</p><p>Here are a few of my favorites:</p><ul><li><strong>You get a birthday gift</strong>: Sea and Sand members can choose a drink, pita chips or dessert, while Sun members get 50% off a bowl or pita. Considering Cava Rewards is free to join, this alone is a nice reason to sign up if you eat at Cava occasionally.</li><li><strong>You can earn through challenges and multiplier events</strong>: Cava offers personalized challenges and multiplier events, with Sea members eligible to earn double the usual points, Sand members triple and Sun members quadruple.</li><li><strong>Catering earns rewards</strong>: Catering orders earn 10 points per dollar spent at every status level, so a large order for a work event, party or other gathering can be an easy way to build your balance.</li><li><strong>Higher status comes with more perks</strong>: Sand and Sun members get opportunities for free delivery, while Sun members also receive access to exclusive events.</li></ul><figure class="rssFigure"><img alt="bag of Cava pita chips and dip" src="https://runway-media-production.global.ssl.fastly.net/us/originals/2026/08/20260827_Cava-1.jpg" /><figcaption>CAVA</figcaption></figure><p>Since Cava Rewards is free to join, there&#x27;s little downside to signing up. Just remember that points expire if you don&#x27;t make a qualifying purchase at least once every 180 days, and rewards redeemed with points generally expire after 60 days. Also keep in mind that gift card purchases and orders placed through third-party delivery services, such as Uber Eats and DoorDash, do not earn points; you&#x27;ll need to order in person or through Cava&#x27;s app to get points for your purchase.</p><p><strong>Related: <a href="https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/inkind-dining/?utm_campaign=the-points-guy&amp;utm_content=396967&amp;utm_medium=syndication&amp;utm_source=The+Points+Guy" target="_blank">This overlooked dining program is one of the most generous and gets you $25 off your very first meal</a></strong></p><h2 class="rssH2">Who is the Cava Rewards program best for?</h2><p>I&#x27;d argue that anyone who eats at Cava should join Cava Rewards.</p><p>If you&#x27;re a weekly or monthly customer like I was before moving to Northern California, your regular purchases can quickly add up to free food and drinks, while having a higher status helps you earn rewards even faster.</p><figure class="rssFigure"><img alt="outdoor view of Cava" src="https://runway-media-production.global.ssl.fastly.net/us/originals/2026/08/20260827_Cava-3.jpg" /><figcaption>CAVA</figcaption></figure><p>Even if you only eat at Cava a few times a year, there&#x27;s little downside to signing up. You can still take advantage of the birthday reward and any surprise offers or promotions that come your way.</p><h2 class="rssH2">Bottom line</h2><p>I&#x27;ve worked hard for some of my travel status. I may earn rewards more slowly these days, but I still like adding to my points balance and keeping my account active.</p><p>And now that Cava is making its way toward the Bay Area, I&#x27;m looking forward to getting back to a weekly bowl — and earning some of my tastiest rewards along the way.</p><p><strong>Related: <a href="https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/taco-bell-rewards/?utm_campaign=the-points-guy&amp;utm_content=396967&amp;utm_medium=syndication&amp;utm_source=The+Points+Guy" target="_blank">Gen Z is right.
