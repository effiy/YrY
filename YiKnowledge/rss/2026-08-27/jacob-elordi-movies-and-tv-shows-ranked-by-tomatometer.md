<h1 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Jacob Elordi Movies and TV Shows Ranked by Tomatometer</strong></h1>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>The Latest:</strong> <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/jacob_elordi">Jacob Elordi</a> stars in Ridley Scott&#8217;s <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/the_dog_stars"><em><strong>The Dog Stars</strong></em></a>. Buy your tickets on <a href="https://www.fandango.com/the-dog-stars-2026-245569/movie-overview">Fandango</a>!</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Hailing from Australia, famous tall man and Academy Award nominee <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/jacob_elordi"><strong>Jacob Elordi</strong></a> got his first taste of Hollywood as an extra on&nbsp;<em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/pirates_of_the_caribbean_dead_men_tell_no_tales">Pirates of the Caribbean: Dead Men Tell No Tales</a>,</em> playing one of the redcoats. He then traded in his military uniform for a kissing booth when he starred in the Netflix <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/the_kissing_booth">Kissing Booth</a></em> trilogy, cementing Elordi as a bona fide Gen Z heartthrob.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Most actors would&#8217;ve taken that heartthrob status and cash it in for a string of romantic comedies, like how <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/mathew_mcconaughey">Matthew McConaughey</a>, <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/ben_affleck">Ben Affleck</a>, and <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/hugh_grant">Hugh Grant</a> did early in their careers. Instead, Elordi jumped into the hit TV show <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/euphoria"><em>Euphoria</em></a> and subverted that heartthrob archetype by playing a jock who was as sexy as he was terrifying and abusive.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Afterwards, much like his co-stars <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/zendaya">Zendaya</a>, <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/sydney_sweeney">Sydney Sweeney</a>, and <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/hunter_schafer">Hunter Schafer</a>, Elordi lobbied the success of <em>Euphoria</em> to star in a string of critically acclaimed movies, including <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/priscilla">Priscilla</a></em>, <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/the_sweet_east"><em>The Sweet East</em></a>, and <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/oh_canada"><em>Oh, Canada</em></a>. And in 2025, he would be nominated for his work as Frankenstein’s monster in <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/guillermo_del_toro">Guillermo Del Toro</a>’s <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/frankenstein_2025"><em>Frankenstein</em></a>.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Elordi is becoming one of the biggest stars in Hollywood, so we’ve collected all of his movies and television shows and ranked them by Tomatometer. Check out the lists below! — <a href="https://editorial.rottentomatoes.com/author/bryce-marrero/">Bryce Marrero</a></p>
