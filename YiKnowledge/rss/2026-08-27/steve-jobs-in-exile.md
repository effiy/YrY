<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We all know the Steve Jobs who came back in the late 1990s and saved Apple from becoming a footnote. And we&#8217;ve all heard the story of how, in 1985, he was pushed out of the company he&#8217;d created.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">What we don&#8217;t know is what happened in between.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-text-align-center is-style-default has-black-color has-secondary-background-color has-text-color has-background" id="h-public-release-may-20-members-have-access-now-join-us">Public Release: September 1. <br />Members have <a href="https://fs.blog/prime/">access now</a>.<br /><a href="https://fs.blog/membership/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank"><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Join us</span></a>.</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Geoffrey Cain spent years reconstructing the decade Jobs spent outside Apple. NeXT spent years losing money. His co-founders left. His investors lost faith. His fortune nearly disappeared. At one point, he was a weekend away from shutting the company down.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Somewhere in those failures, Jobs learned how to become the leader Apple would eventually need.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This is Steve Jobs before he became Steve Jobs.</p>
