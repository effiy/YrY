<em>"Remember this eternal truth: there is no such thing as death."</em> Vertical has revealed the eerie teaser trailer for an <a href="https://www.firstshowing.net/category/indies/">indie</a> <a href="https://www.firstshowing.net/category/horror/">horror film</a> coming up titled <strong><em>Pendulum</em></strong>, arriving to watch right the start of <a href="https://www.firstshowing.net/schedule2027/#jan">2027</a>. A very early tease for this! <em>Pendulum</em> is produced by filmmaker Darren Aronofsky, because it's the feature directorial debut of screenwriter Mark Heyman – who (co-)wrote the script for <em>Black Swan</em> which earned him a BAFTA Award nomination. After a traumatic event a couple journeys to a retreat in New Mexico seeking healing. Patrick grows skeptical of the retreat's leader as Abigail falls under her influence. They start to wonder if the group's practices offer genuine healing – or conceal a terrifying truth that threatens to consume them both. I'm pretty sure it's the latter. Starring <strong>Joseph Gordon-Levitt</strong> & <strong>Phoebe Dynevor</strong>, along with <strong>Norman Reedus</strong> & <strong>Jacki Weaver</strong>. Heyman also states: "<em>Pendulum</em> is a deeply personal film, inspired by real, and really scary, experiences." Vertical will be sending this out into theaters <a href="https://www.firstshowing.net/schedule2027/#jan">in January</a> for anyone brave enough to venture with them into this retreat. What a clever, creepy horror trailer!
