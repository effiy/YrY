<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-how-we-chose-the-best-headphones-for-working-out"><strong>How we chose the best headphones for working out</strong></h2>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">The best headphones for working out are wireless, and <a href="https://www.popsci.com/reviews/how-does-bluetooth-work/">Bluetooth</a> requires power. And, in all honesty, the best headphones for working out are earphones, which is why they make up the majority of our selections. We picked pairs that offer at least five hours of single-charge battery life, and all of the earbuds include charging cases. Considering the battery life of your audio device is extremely important for keeping the flow going—you wouldn’t want the music to stop mid-workout. All of the selections on our list fit securely around the head or in the ear to ensure that they move very little or not at all during workouts. Everyday headphones made for walking or when you&#8217;re stationary aren’t typically designed with extreme movement in mind. </p>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">Sound quality was also a big factor in considering headphones for this list, and many of the earbuds we picked either come with a full and balanced sound profile or offer the option to adjust their sound via an app.&nbsp;Staying aware of the activity around you is always important while wearing headphones, especially if you’re on the go. For our list, we picked headphones/earphones that either feature a transparency mode or are completely open-ear.</p>

<p class="article-paragraph skip"><strong>Why it made the cut:</strong> The Apple Beats Fit Pro offers a snug fit, balanced sound, and seamless iOS connectivity, making them easy to add to any workout routine.</p>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">We’ve thoroughly evaluated the Apple Beats Fit Pro, and they take the best overall award on this list thanks to their sound quality, workout-friendly design, and fantastic iOS compatibility. They contain the same proprietary H1 chip found in some Apple AirPods, which enables near-instantaneous pairing and steady, reliable connections to iOS devices along with battery life optimization. Like AirPods, the Beats Fit Pro earbuds are also fully compatible with iOS head-tracking and spatial audio features for simulated surround sound and immersive media listening. Their inclusion of <a href="https://www.popsci.com/reviews/what-is-active-noise-canceling/">active noise cancellation (ANC)</a> and transparency modes make them equally useful on the road and at the gym. We also love the range of colors these buds come in.</p>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">The Beats Fit Pro earbuds are designed to sit securely in your ears using small rubber wings that rest comfortably against your outer ear along with three sets of variously sized ear tips. A quick optional setup step uses the earbuds’ onboard microphones to test your fit for sound leaks. Once your optimal fit is achieved, the Beats Fit Pro can endure almost every exercise imaginable, including <a href="https://www.popsci.com/reviews/best-rowing-machine/">rowing</a>, without budging. They&#8217;re some of the <a href="https://www.popsci.com/gear/best-running-headphones/">best running headphones</a> around. The earbuds’ conveniently pocket-sized charging case brings their total battery life to around 24 hours. While it would be nice to see wireless charging in a product with this price tag, we appreciate that the case is chargeable via USB-C rather than Apple’s proprietary Lightning connector.</p>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">While the Beats Fit Pro earbuds are unmistakably great for iPhone users, it’s unfortunate that the instant pairing and steady connectivity of the earbuds’ H1 chip aren&#8217;t extended to Android users. It would also be nice to see features like multipoint pairing and more robust weatherproofing rather than the same IPX4 rating found in older AirPods. Still, if you work out regularly and are an iPhone user, you’d be seriously hard-pressed to find a better in-ear option under $150 (a newer $199 <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FPGQMMGN?tag=psc519976-20" rel="noreferrer noopener sponsored nofollow" target="_blank">Powerbeats Fit</a> model expands the sound features, but we think the Fit Pro is a bargain while still available). </p>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">That doesn&#8217;t mean AirPods haven&#8217;t improved their workout-friendly features. For instance, <a href="https://www.popsci.com/gear/apple-airpods-pro-3-true-wireless-anc-earbuds-first-impressions/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">the AirPods Pro 3 introduced in 2025</a> are now rated IP57, so sweat (and sand) is no longer a concern. On top of that, they come with an integrated in-ear PPG heart-rate sensor, which has proven quite accurate in third-party tests. So, if you&#8217;re into metrics-based training, the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FQFB8FMG" rel="noreferrer noopener sponsored nofollow" target="_blank">AirPods Pro 3</a> are worth a consideration. The main drawback is the stemmed form factor, which doesn&#8217;t offer as secure a fit as earbuds with wings/fins/hooks. If you’re sold on a heart-rate sensor but want an option with earhooks, the Powerbeats Pro 2 earbuds below are a solid choice.</p>

<p class="article-paragraph skip">When it comes to earbuds, nothing keeps them in place during a workout better than a full-on hook. <p class="article-paragraph skip"><strong>Why it made the cut:</strong> The unique design of the SHOKZ OpenRun Pro 2 keeps your ears fully open so that you can stay aware of your surroundings and your motivating sounds.</p>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">The SHOKZ OpenRun Pro 2 is an open-ear headphone that sports a unique design that allows you to listen to music and podcasts while maintaining full situational awareness. Unlike traditional headphone designs, bone-conduction headphones employ a clever hack of human anatomy to transfer sounds directly to the inner ear by gently vibrating your facial bones from the outside in, leaving the entire ear canal unobstructed.</p>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">The typical drawbacks to this design include that they provide no passive or active noise cancellation, and the vibrations may be audible to others, so they’re not the best choice for quiet environments or taking public transit. Bone-conduction headphones also produce markedly less bass frequencies than traditional designs, making them less ideal for immersive music listening. However, the SHOKZ OpenRun Pro 2 addresses this with a dual-driver setup it calls DualPitch—using bone-conduction on your temples for the highs and air-conduction (aka an 18mmx11mm racetrack woofer) pointed into the ear for the lows. </p>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">On the functionality side of things, the SHOKZ OpenRun Pro 2 packs about 12 hours of battery life on a single charge (now via USB-C for cord convenience) and boasts a substantial IP55 rating—protected from dust and water but not impervious, so don&#8217;t submerge them (if you&#8217;re looking for the best headphones for swimming, the company makes the IP68 <a href="https://www.amazon.com/SHOKZ-OpenSwim-Pro-Headphones-Built/dp/B0CS3JBP67?tag=psc519976-20" rel="noreferrer noopener sponsored nofollow" target="_blank">OpenSwim Pro</a> with onboard storage for that). They have a comfy 30g Ni-Ti headband design with easy-to-use control buttons that make them particularly suited for running and cycling in traffic. <a href="https://www.popsci.com/gear/shokz-openfit-air-2-bone-conduction-sports-headphone-openrun-air-2-fitness-earbuds-workout-product-news/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Shokz has introduced lighter, cheaper options</a>, but these remain the flagship thanks to higher battery life and more drivers (aka more bass). </p>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">The end result is a set that is pretty comfortable, even if you have a helmet, wear a snood, or have a ponytail. You&#8217;re not going to be bumping massive bass, but you can hear the beat and your surroundings. These aren&#8217;t for you if you want to be drowning in music, but they&#8217;re great if you want to keep an ear out for traffic. And you don&#8217;t have to worry about them flying off if you hit a bump or turn your head really fast (a genuine traditional earbud issue). Oh, and Bluetooth 5.3 with multipoint pairing lets you use them at a computer and easily pick up where you left off on your phone when it&#8217;s time to leave the house and hit the trails. </p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-what-to-consider-before-buying-the-best-headphones-for-working-out"><strong>What to consider before buying the best headphones for working out</strong></h2>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">You could take the <a href="https://www.popsci.com/gear/best-headphones/">best headphones</a> to the gym, but you would probably have very sweaty ears and potentially ruin your precious investment. Heck, even some of the <a href="https://www.popsci.com/reviews/best-noise-cancelling-earbuds/">best Bluetooth earbuds</a> might not be suited for more than a brisk stroll. So here is what to think about before clicking &#8220;add to cart&#8221; on the best headphones for working out and going all in on that next set:</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Sweat level</h3>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">Sweating is a normal part of any workout, but if you tend to sweat more than the average person while you exercise, you should consider a pair of headphones with an <a href="https://www.iec.ch/ip-ratings" rel="noreferrer noopener nofollow" target="_blank">Ingress Protection rating</a> of IPX7. Most of the headphones on our list are designed with at least IP55 level of sweatproofing. If you sweat a lot, you’ll probably also want to steer clear of over-ear designs, which can heat up your ears and exacerbate the problem (plus they&#8217;re less secure and less durable in general when it comes to high-output exercise).</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Workout intensity</h3>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">If you do CrossFit or other forms of intense workouts, choose earbuds that use an ear hook or another type of external fastener to ensure that they don’t go flying. We like the <a href="https://goto.walmart.com/c/6430275/565706/9383?veh=aff&#038;sourceid=imp_000011112222333344&#038;u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.walmart.com%2Fip%2FBeats-Powerbeats-Pro-2-High-Performance-Earbuds-Electric-Orange%2F15075506141&#038;subId1=PS-RU" rel="noreferrer noopener sponsored nofollow" target="_blank">Powerbeats Pro 2</a> earbuds for heavy exertion, though the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CRT6HQ82" rel="noreferrer noopener sponsored nofollow" target="_blank">soundcore by Anker Sport X20</a> is an inexpensive alternative.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Charging capacity</h3>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">If you use your headphones frequently, have limited access to charging capabilities, or just find yourself constantly running devices on low battery, picking a set of headphones with a high battery capacity can increase the likelihood that they’ll be ready come workout time. The 10-hour single charge (45 hours with charging case) of the <a href="https://goto.walmart.com/c/6430275/565706/9383?veh=aff&#038;sourceid=imp_000011112222333344&#038;u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.walmart.com%2Fip%2FBeats-Powerbeats-Pro-2-High-Performance-Earbuds-Electric-Orange%2F15075506141&#038;subId1=PS-RU" rel="noreferrer noopener sponsored nofollow" target="_blank">Apple Powerbeats Pro 2</a> is the best we’ve tested in the battery life department, though our budget pick, the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CRT6HQ82" rel="noreferrer noopener sponsored nofollow" target="_blank">soundcore by Anker Sport X20</a>, also performs well.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Situational awareness</h3>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">If you spend lots of time exercising near traffic or in busy locations, it’s important to maintain a good level of situational awareness for your own safety. Many headphones include a transparency mode that allows you to hear your surroundings in addition to your music, including the <a href="https://goto.walmart.com/c/6430275/565706/9383?veh=aff&#038;sourceid=imp_000011112222333344&#038;u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.walmart.com%2Fip%2FBeats-Fit-Pro-Noise-Cancelling-Wireless-Earbuds-Apple-Android-Compatible-Sage-Gray%2F232379973&#038;subId1=PS-RU" rel="noreferrer noopener sponsored nofollow" target="_blank">Apple Beats Fit Pro</a>. For maximum situational awareness, nothing beats the open-ear design of bone-conduction headphones, and we prefer the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/SHOKZ-New-OpenRun-Pro-Comfortable/dp/B0D2HKCMBP/" rel="noreferrer noopener sponsored nofollow" target="_blank">SHOKZ OpenRun Pro 2</a>.</p>
