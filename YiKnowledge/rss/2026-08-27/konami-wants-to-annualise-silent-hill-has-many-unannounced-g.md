<p><strong>Much more to come from horror IP.</strong></p><p>Alongside a new <a href="https://www.pushsquare.com/games/ps5/call_of_duty_modern_warfare_4">Call of Duty</a> and <a href="https://www.pushsquare.com/games/ps5/ea_sports_fc_27">EA Sports FC</a> every year, how do you fancy a new <a href="https://www.pushsquare.com/games/ps1/silent_hill">Silent Hill</a> game every 12 months?</p><p>That's the release cadence Konami is now working towards as it brings the horror franchise back in a big way.
