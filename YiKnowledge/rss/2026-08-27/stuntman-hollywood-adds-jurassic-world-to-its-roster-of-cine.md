      <p><a href="https://gameinformer.com/state-of-play/2026/06/02/stuntman-hollywood-brings-the-big-name-cars">Stuntman: Hollywood</a>, first revealed during June's PlayStation State of Play, resurrects the dormant franchise with a new entry that lets players perform wild vehicular stunts across the sets of licensed franchises, including Back to the Future, Mad Max, and Knight Rider. A new trailer reveals Jurassic World as Stuntman Hollywood's next IP.</p><p>The trailer showcases both cinematic and in-game footage of the stuntman driving the Jurassic World park jeep and a motorcycle across familiar setpieces from the modern series of films born out of Jurassic Park. This includes riding a motorcycle alongside a pack of raptors in a forest à la the first <em>Jurassic World</em> to escaping the volcanic eruption from its sequel,<em> Jurassic World:</em> <em>Fallen Kingdom</em>. &nbsp;The trailer also shows players performing backflipping a motorcycle over the gaping maw of an angry T. rex, which is pretty awesome.&nbsp;</p>&nbsp;<p>Stuntman: Hollywood has no release window, but it’s coming to PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, and PC.&nbsp;</p>
