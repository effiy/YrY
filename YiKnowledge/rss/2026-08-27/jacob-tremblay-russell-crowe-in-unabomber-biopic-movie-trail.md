<em>"When the battle starts, keep up the fight."</em> Netflix has revealed the first official trailer for the <strong><em>Unabomber</em></strong> movie, a biopic about the life and times and infamous turn to terrorism for American math genius <strong>Ted K</strong>. Yep they made another film about him. Not just a series, this one is an actual 97 minute feature arriving on Netflix. A smart Harvard student becomes the Unabomber after his professor's psychological experiments. Years later, an FBI agent's pursuit reveals how his past shaped his deadly campaign that killed 3 and injured 23 from 1978-1995. <em>Unabomber</em> flashes back & forth between Ted Kaczynski's struggle to fit in as a young man, and the future investigation by Joanne Miller, the FBI agent tasked with catching this "Unabomber". Between these dual timelines, a portrait of Ted K takes shape. "Ideas are like fire," Crowe says in the trailer. "They burn from man to man." Starring <strong>Jacob Tremblay</strong> as Ted K, <strong>Russell Crowe</strong> as Professor Murray, <strong>Shailene Woodley</strong> as Joanne Miller, with <strong>Annabelle Wallis</strong>, <strong>Alexander Ludwig</strong>, <strong>Steven Ogg</strong>, and <strong>Marc Menchaca</strong>.
