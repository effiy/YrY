<p><strong>Can't argue with freebies.</strong></p><p>As if <a href="https://www.purexbox.com/games/xbox-one/witcher_3_wild_hunt">The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt's</a> current-gen upgrade wasn't already awesome enough, CD Projekt Red has announced a free <a href="https://www.purexbox.com/news/2026/08/the-witcher-3-remastered-is-a-major-free-upgrade-heading-to-xbox-this-september">Witcher 3 Remaster</a> coming next month, with an <a href="https://www.purexbox.com/news/2026/05/the-witcher-3-is-getting-a-brand-new-expansion-on-xbox-12-years-after-release">all-new DLC Songs of the Past</a> scheduled for 2027. But to top it all off, both <a href="https://www.purexbox.com/games/xbox-one/witcher_3_wild_hunt_-_hearts_of_stone">Hearts of Stone</a> and <a href="https://www.purexbox.com/news/2024/06/the-witcher-3-dev-concedes-best-rated-rpg-dlc-congratulates-fromsoftware-in-the-process"><strong>Blood and Wine</strong></a> DLC expansions are now completely free for all!</p><p>Until now, if you fancied picking up The Witcher 3's stellar DLC expansions, you'd have had to fork out around $30 for the pair. It's funny, because free is free - which is great - but it's a little awkward when you consider existing owners of the RPG will get all of this included in next month's remaster.
