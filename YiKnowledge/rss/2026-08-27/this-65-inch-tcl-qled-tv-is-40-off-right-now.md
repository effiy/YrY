<p>The QM7L sits between the more budget <a href="https://zdcs.link/avAAPq?pageview_type=RSS&amp;template=content&amp;module=content_body&amp;element=offer&amp;item=text-link&amp;element_label=QM6K&amp;object_type=content&amp;short_url=avAAPq&amp;u=https%3A%2F%2Flifehacker.com%2Ffeed%2Frss" rel="sponsored" target="_blank" title="open in a new window">QM6K</a> and the more premium QM8L. It is available from 55 inches up to 98 inches. All sizes will have a different number of dimming zones; the 65-inch version has 1,152, offering strong contrast and deep black levels, even when scenes have bright areas (especially noticeable around subtitles).  </p><p>This TV advertises a maximum brightness of 3,000 nits, but <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nuWe7w3Qeiw" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="open in a new window">reviews suggest</a> it's actually higher, up to 3,500. Its glossy display could help lessen reflections from sunlight, but you'll notice rainbow smearing if you have a direct light in front of the TV. The biggest con of the QM7L is the color accuracy. <a href="https://www.rtings.com/tv/reviews/tcl/qm7l" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="open in a new window">Reviews like this find</a> the image will look warmer out of the box than intended, and that HDR content will have a blue tint. However, the TV can always be <a href="https://www.cnet.com/tech/home-entertainment/should-you-professionally-calibrate-your-tv/" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="open in a new window">calibrated</a>.</p><p>The biggest selling point of the QM7L is the <a href="https://www.cnet.com/tech/home-entertainment/tcl-super-quantum-dot-qm8l-qm7l-micro-rgb-tvs/" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="open in a new window">value it offers for the price</a>. Coming in at over $600 less than the <a href="https://zdcs.link/zEKKR2?pageview_type=RSS&amp;template=content&amp;module=content_body&amp;element=offer&amp;item=text-link&amp;element_label=QM8L&amp;object_type=content&amp;short_url=zEKKR2&amp;u=https%3A%2F%2Flifehacker.com%2Ffeed%2Frss" rel="sponsored" target="_blank" title="open in a new window">QM8L</a> for the 65-inch version, it is a smart pick for those who want premium features and specs without breaking the bank.
