<em>"Do you honestly think you'd have done anything different?"</em> Briarcliff Entertainment has debuted the first official trailer for <strong><em>Trust Me, I'm a Doctor</em></strong>, a dark comedy film based on a true story from the early 2000s. Written and directed by filmmaker Thane Economou, this film is based <a href="https://amzn.to/4iyghek" rel="noopener" target="_blank">on the memoirs</a> of and focused on Dr. Sandeep Kapoor, as played by <strong>Kal Penn</strong>. It's also about the infamous death of American model, actress, TV personality <strong>Anna Nicole Smith</strong> - who passed at age 39 in 2007 due to combined drug intoxication. Led by <strong>Kal Penn</strong> as Dr. Sandeep Kapoor, <strong>Linda Hamilton</strong> as renowned defense attorney Ellyn Garofalo, and <strong>Abbie Cornish</strong> as <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Nicole_Smith" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Anna Nicole Smith</a>, the film explores the very intense legal battle and media frenzy that followed one of the most sensational celebrity cases of the early 2000s. Was it really his fault? Or was it all her fault? Or is there something more going on here? <em>Trust Me, I'm a Doctor</em> hits theaters in October this fall. Starring Penn, Hamilton, Cornish, along with <strong>David Julian Hirsh</strong>, <strong>Dhirendra</strong>, and <strong>Dan Willmott</strong>.
