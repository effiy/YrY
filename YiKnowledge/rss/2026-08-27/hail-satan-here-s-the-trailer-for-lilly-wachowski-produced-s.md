<img src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2026/08/27165818/cassie-workman-feature.jpg" /><p>As the world spirals in its right-wing haze of hate speech and bigoted legislation, only one Australian transgender comedian has the guts to make a show about the occult featuring an Owen Wilson puppet. Enter: <i>Cassie Workman is Witchy AF</i>, a new YouTube series executive produced by Lilly Wachowski. Wachowski, <a href="https://avclub.com/lilly-wachowski-hollywood-wont-make-movie-all-trans-cast">who recently opined about the difficulties of getting her trans Weather Underground script produced because of its &#8220;wall-to-wall trans cast,&#8221;</a> has found yet another way to terrify the world’s transphobes: Black magic infotainment.&nbsp;</p><!-- RevContent --> <div id="revcontent-hidden"> <!-- revisit -->
<p>The eight-part comedy documentary series takes viewers through the occult’s history, mythology, and impact on culture at large. Set in a fictional occult bookstore that&#8217;s loaded with puppets, the series dives deep on everything occult, from Aleister Crowley to tarot cards. With the help of guest stars, including Garbage&#8217;s Shirley Manson, and a demon raven named King Grimskwawk to whom Workman may or may not have sold her soul, Workman guides viewers through the absurd underworld with a drink firmly in hand.&nbsp;</p><!-- inlinecontent_1 --> </p>
<p>A joint production with ETCH,<i>&nbsp;Cassie Workman Is Witchy AF</i> is the first from Wachowski’s production company, Anarchists United Studios, which &#8220;focuses on elevated genre projects created by artists from underrepresented communities.&#8221; We imagine transgender immigrant TV show hosts &#8220;created in a lab specifically to make Ron DeSantis angry&#8221; fall under that category.&nbsp;</p>
<p>The series premieres on YouTube on September 16.</p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p>
