The issue I have is, if you put a charming and slightly rotund biplane (or similar such vehicle) in a game, I'm just simply going to have to take a look at it at minimum. At minimum! Porco Rosso is my favourite of Hayao Miyazaki's outings, after all, in no small part thanks to ever iconic "I'd rather be a pig than a fascist" line from the titular porky pilot. What I'm saying is, obviously I'm curious about Lou's Lagoon, a crafty little exploration game that comes complete with a seaplane you can customise to your heart's delight, which is out today.
