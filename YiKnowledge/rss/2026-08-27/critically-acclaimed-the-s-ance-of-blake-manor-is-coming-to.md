<p><strong>The spirits heard us.</strong></p><p>If you're a fan of detective games, then chances are you've caught a peek at The Séance of Blake Manor. Released in 2025 from developer Spooky Doorway (<a href="https://www.nintendolife.com/games/switch-eshop/darkside_detective">The Darkside Detective</a>), it was critically-acclaimed for its haunting atmosphere and expertly-crafted mystery. Oh, and it's now coming to Switch 2 – huzzah!</p><p>Launching on <strong>28th October 2026</strong> for <strong>£16.75</strong>, the game is available for pre-order now via the Switch 2 eShop (thanks for the spot, <a href="https://nintendoeverything.com/the-seance-of-blake-manor-coming-to-nintendo-switch-2/">Nintendo Everything</a>). There's unfortunately no indication that Switch 2 players will benefit from mouse mode or anything, but that's not to say it definitely won't be supported – it's just not mentioned on the eShop page.</p><p>Read the <a href="https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/08/critically-acclaimed-the-seance-of-blake-manor-is-coming-to-switch-2">full article on nintendolife.com</a></p>
