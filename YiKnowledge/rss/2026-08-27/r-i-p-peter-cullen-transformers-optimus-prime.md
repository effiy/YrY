<img src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2026/08/27131743/cullen-header.jpg" /><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Peter Cullen, the legendary voice actor known for his rumbling performance as Autobot leader Optimus Prime in </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">The</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Transformers</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> animated series and live-action franchise, died at his Los Angeles home on Wednesday, </span><a href="https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/peter-cullen-optimus-prime-transformers-eeyore-1236683075/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">according to his manager.</span></a></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The fatherly Montreal-born actor was known for his extensive vocal credits across television, film, and video games, becoming the face and rallying voice behind the </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Transformers </span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">franchise across its many incarnations, as well as an enduring presence as the gentle-and-drowsy Eeyore in various </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Winnie The Pooh</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> series and films for Disney. The actor bridged the gap between pop culture fandom and STEM academia as the ambassador for </span><a href="https://www.nasa.gov/centers-and-facilities/goddard/nasa-goddard-rolls-out-10-opsparc-winners-and-optimus-prime-himself/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">the NASA OPTIMUS PRIME Spinoff Promotion and Research Challenge</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> (OPSPARC), which awards young students who excel in math, science, technology, and space.</span></p><!-- inlinecontent_1 --> </p>
<p><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">Space would be a recurring motif throughout his life. Cullen began his five-decade career in 1969 as Québécois astronaut Commander Bi Bi Latuque on&nbsp;<em>The Buddies</em> for CFCF (now CTV Montreal). That role led to a gig announcing <em>The</em>&nbsp;<em>Smothers</em>&nbsp;<em>Brothers</em>&nbsp;<em>Comedy</em>&nbsp;<em>Hour</em>, the variety show that also launched the careers of Steve Martin, Rob Reiner, and fellow voice actor Lorenzo Music (<em>The</em>&nbsp;<em>Real</em>&nbsp;<em>Ghostbusters</em>,&nbsp;<em>Garfield</em>&nbsp;<em>And</em>&nbsp;<em>Friends</em>). From there, Cullen appeared in&nbsp;<em>The</em>&nbsp;<em>Sonny</em>&nbsp;<em>&amp;</em>&nbsp;<em>Cher</em>&nbsp;<em>Comedy</em>&nbsp;<em>Hour</em>&nbsp;alongside&nbsp;<em>Buddies</em>&nbsp;co-star Ted Zeigler beginning in 1971. &#8220;It ended up being a series and for four years I, you know, I could make the house payments and lived happily ever after,&#8221;&nbsp;<a href="https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/326214943/behind-optimus-prime-and-eeyore-one-man-s-signature-voice" rel="noopener" target="_blank">he told NPR&#8217;s&nbsp;</a><a href="https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/326214943/behind-optimus-prime-and-eeyore-one-man-s-signature-voice" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><em>All</em></a><a href="https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/326214943/behind-optimus-prime-and-eeyore-one-man-s-signature-voice" rel="noopener" target="_blank">&nbsp;</a><a href="https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/326214943/behind-optimus-prime-and-eeyore-one-man-s-signature-voice" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><em>Things</em></a><a href="https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/326214943/behind-optimus-prime-and-eeyore-one-man-s-signature-voice" rel="noopener" target="_blank">&nbsp;</a><a href="https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/326214943/behind-optimus-prime-and-eeyore-one-man-s-signature-voice" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><em>Considered</em></a><a href="https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/326214943/behind-optimus-prime-and-eeyore-one-man-s-signature-voice" rel="noopener" target="_blank"> in 2014.</a></span></p><!-- admarker --> 
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">After the </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Sonny</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">&amp;</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Cher</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> TV run ended in 1974, Cullen&#8217;s career consisted of supporting voice credits in animated series such as </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Scooby-Doo</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">And</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Scrappy-Doo</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">, </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">The</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Smurfs</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">, and </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Spider-Man</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">And</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">His</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Amazing</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Friends</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> (where he voiced characters such as Mysterio, Red Skull, and the Hulk). His willingness to lend his voice to vivid cartoon characters led to an audition for a role that would define his career. &#8220;I was living with my brother Larry at the time,&#8221; he told NPR. &#8220;He had returned from service as a Marine in Vietnam. And I told him one day I was going out to audition for a truck. And he says, &#8216;a truck?&#8217; And I said, &#8216;Yeah, but he&#8217;s a hero truck.'&#8221;</span></p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Beginning in 1984, Cullen became the leading voice behind </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">The</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Transformers</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">, the popular animated series based on the Hasbro toy line, voicing Optimus Prime until the hero&#8217;s untimely demise in 1986&#8217;s </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">The</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Transformers:</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">The</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Movie</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">. That moment proved controversial, sparking public outcry that led to an almost immediate reversal in &#8220;The Return Of Optimus Prime,&#8221; a two-part epic in </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">The</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Transformers</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">&#8216; third season. After seemingly retiring the character, Cullen kept busy voicing Eeyore in various </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Disney Winnie</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">The</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Pooh</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> TV and film entries from the late 80s through 2002, reprising the role in 2017&#8217;s Disney Junior series, </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Doc</span></i> <i><span style="font-weight: 400;">McStuffins</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">.&nbsp;</span></p><!-- admarker --> <!-- RevContent --> <div id="revcontent-hidden"> <!-- revisit -->
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Naturally, given the lasting mark he left on the franchise and the 80s nostalgia reaching a new apex in the mid-Aughts, Cullen&#8217;s time away from </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Transformers</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> wouldn&#8217;t last. He was the only choice to voice the Autobot when Michael Bay launched a live-action version of the franchise in 2007 with </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Transformers</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">, leading to his reprising the role in five sequels and the 2018 spin-off, </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Bumblebee</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Cullen also became a cult favorite as the voice of Cartoon Network&#8217;s late-night animation block </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Toonami</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> from 1999 to 2008, lending his gravitas to announcements and interstitials for upcoming episodes and series. His voice was a lulling comfort to night-owl animation enthusiasts, offering a warm-blanket boom to spots ranging from the adorable anime series </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Hamtaro</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> (</span><a href="https://x.com/TheRealSwood/status/2093006185217638732"><span style="font-weight: 400;">&#8220;Big things come in small packages!&#8221;</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">) to making </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Batman &amp; Mr. Freeze: SubZero</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> feel like appointment television.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p></p><!-- admarker --> 
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Among his many animation credits, Cullen also left a mark on monster movies, delivering a guttural performance as the eponymous furry beast in the 1976 </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">King Kong</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">, starring Jeff Bridges and Jessica Lange, and providing </span><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FciHXIcebVo"><span style="font-weight: 400;">the eerie alien clicking noises</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> that put Arnold Schwarzenegger on edge in </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Predator</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Cullen&#8217;s family said in a statement that the </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Transformers</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> actor “passed away peacefully, surrounded by his loving family—and held in the hearts of his many friends and countless fans worldwide.” The statement went on to ask “that you honor his remarkable legacy by serving your communities and leading others with compassion, integrity and loyalty and always remember ‘be strong enough to be gentle’.”</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">That phrase—&#8221;strong enough to be gentle,&#8221; taught to him at a critical stage in his career by his older brother—was Cullen&#8217;s governing philosophy throughout his life. Its origin is an anecdote </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Transformers</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> fans know well, as Cullen was happy to share it at conventions and in interviews. From his 2014 interview with NPR: &#8220;[Larry] says, well, if you&#8217;re going to be a hero, be a real hero. Don&#8217;t be a Hollywood stereotypical thing, you know, the yelling and screaming. He said be strong enough to be gentle. And Larry was that way. I had no idea what the script was going to be. But [the] lines just came out, and I just did my brother Larry.&#8221;</span></p>
