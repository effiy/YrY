<p><em>Update: As of 5 p.m. EDT, the problem seems to have largely been resolved, as reports on Downdetector have dropped from a high of around 10,000 to under 500. Twitch still has not commented on the issue beyond its initial status message.</em></p><p>If you're having some trouble logging into Twitch for a gaming session right now, it's not just you: According to Downdetector, <a href="https://downdetector.com/status/twitch/" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="open in a new window">thousands of users are reporting issues with the online gaming platform</a>. </p><p>It looks like the problems began around 2:30 p.m. EDT, when reports of access issues started to trickle in. By around 4 p.m., those numbers spiked to almost 10,000 and remain elevated as of this writing. Twitch has yet to reveal what might be causing the problem or offer a timeframe for a fix, with <a href="https://status.twitch.com/history?page=1" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="open in a new window">its status page</a> merely sharing the following:</p><blockquote><p>Service Negatively Impacted: We have received reports of impacted services. This could manifest in chat not working, login difficulties, search impaired, etc. We're looking into the issue.</p></blockquote><p>No additional updates have been shared on the company's social media profiles, <a href="https://x.com/Twitch" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="open in a new window">including X</a>. According to Twitch, the platform <a href="https://status.twitch.com/history?page=9" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="open in a new window">experienced its last major outage two years ago</a>, in August 2024.<br /></p>
