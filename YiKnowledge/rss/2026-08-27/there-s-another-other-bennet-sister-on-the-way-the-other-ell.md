<img src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2026/03/31164157/other-bennet-sister-feature.jpg" /><p>Earlier this year, the British Broadcasting Company had a surprise hit with <a href="https://www.avclub.com/other-bennet-sister-review-britbox-ella-bruccoleri"><i>The Other Bennet Sister</i></a>, an adaptation of Jane Austen&#8217;s <i>Pride &amp; Prejudice</i> from the perspective of Mary (Ella Bruccoleri), the Jan Brady of literature&#8217;s Bennet bunch. Overlooked by Austen for her more beautiful and charismatic sisters, Mary provided the series with a unique point of view, one that couldn&#8217;t be carried into a second season. Faced with the challenge of continuing the series, which has since become BritBox&#8217;s biggest-ever hit, <i>Bad Wolf</i> founder Jane Tranter announced a follow-up, <i>The Other Elliot Sister</i>, at the 2026 Edinburgh TV Festival. Based on Austen&#8217;s <i>Persuasion</i>, <i>The Other Elliot Sister</i> aims to do for one of Anne&#8217;s sisters what <i>Bennet Sister</i> did for Mary.</p><!-- RevContent --> <div id="revcontent-hidden"> <!-- revisit --><!-- admarker --> 
<p>Based on Janice Hadlow&#8217;s novel, <i>The Other Bennet Sister</i> follows the events of Austen&#8217;s novel from Mary&#8217;s eyes as well as her own search for love. Hadlow is currently working on the follow-up novel about the Elliots, which the BBC picked up the rights to. <a href="https://www.tellyvisions.org/the-other-bennet-sister-sequel-the-other-elliot-sister-what-to-know/">As <i>Telly Visions</i></a> noted, <i>Persuasion</i> doesn&#8217;t really have a clear analogue to Mary. Speaking to Jane Austen&#8217;s affinity for the names Elizabeth and Mary, <i>Persuasion</i> also follows three daughters, Anne, Elizabeth, and Mary, who are not to be confused with <i>Pride &amp; Prejudice</i>&#8216;s Elizabeth and Mary. In <i>Persuasion</i>, Elizabeth is the eldest and favorite daughter, while Mary is the nightmare baby of the family. However, the BBC has yet to announce which Elliot sister will be designated <i>“The Other</i>.”</p><!-- inlinecontent_1 --> </p>
<p><i>The Other Bennet Sister</i> wraps its run with a three-part Christmas special airing later this year.</p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p>
