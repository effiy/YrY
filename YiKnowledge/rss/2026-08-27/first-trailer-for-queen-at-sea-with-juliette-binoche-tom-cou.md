<em>"In this regard, I know her better than you do."</em> Greenwich Ent. has unveiled the official trailer for the indie film <strong><em>Queen at Sea</em></strong>, a London drama from filmmaker <strong>Lance Hammer</strong> - his first since his original debut <em>Ballast</em> in 2008. This premiered at the 2026 Berlin Film Festival earlier this year and played at a number of other fests throughout the year. Now set for a US release in limited theaters this fall after its festival run. As dementia erodes an elderly woman's ability to communicate her inner life, her new husband and daughter struggle to act in her best interest, navigating love and the fragile boundaries between care, protection, and autonomy. An intimate tale of morality and consent. Starring <strong>Juliette Binoche</strong>, <strong>Tom Courtenay</strong>, <strong>Anna Calder-Marshall</strong>, and <strong>Florence Hunt</strong>. Featuring cinematography by Oscar-nominee <strong>Adolpho Veloso</strong> of <a href="https://www.firstshowing.net/2025/one-final-trailer-for-train-dreams-still-one-of-the-best-films-of-2025/" title="One Final Trailer for 'Train Dreams' - Still One of the Best Films of 2025"><em>Train Dreams</em></a>.
