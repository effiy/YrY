<img src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2026/08/27104149/MixCollage-27-Aug-2026-10-41-AM-8071.jpg" /><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Between his work with the Talking Heads and his solo material, David Byrne already has a pretty impressive concert filmography. That streak seems poised to continue with the concert film for Byrne&#8217;s still-ongoing Who Is The Sky Tour?, which will be released as a concert film shot by Brady Corbet, director of </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">The Brutalist</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> (and, perhaps more relevantly here, </span><a href="https://www.avclub.com/natalie-portman-plays-a-cynical-pop-star-for-an-oversti-1830885877"><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Vox Lux</span></i></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">). The film is currently in post-production, so there&#8217;s not yet any confirmed information about a release date or distributor (or even an official title). But we do know that the film was shot over four nights in Byrne&#8217;s hometown of Baltimore and was shot on 70 mm film.&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></p><!-- RevContent --> <div id="revcontent-hidden"> <!-- revisit -->
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">&#8220;When Brady told me he wanted to shoot our show in 70 millimeter (70 millimeter!) I didn&#8217;t realize what a difference it would make until I saw some frames he sent me,&#8221; says Byrne in a press release. &#8220;He was right, it really does capture the wide screen visual scope of the show. I&#8217;ve never seen anything like it.&#8221; Adds Corbet, &#8220;I am so grateful to David Byrne and his entire team for entrusting us to capture his hometown show on 8-perf and 15-perf 70mm. The band’s performance is an inclusive celebration of community, and of Baltimore in particular—one of America’s most underappreciated historic cities.”</span></p><!-- inlinecontent_1 --> </p><!-- admarker --> 
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The Who Is The Sky? show is a hopeful and ultimately very moving couple of hours with Byrne. (This writer </span><a href="https://www.avclub.com/david-byrne-who-is-the-sky-maya-rudolph-loot"><span style="font-weight: 400;">caught a performance at Radio City Music Hall last year</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">.) The show consists of both Byrne&#8217;s newer solo material along with some classics from the Talking Heads era. There is still a handful of shows left at the Hollywood Bowl and in Mexico City before the tour concludes on September 19 at Forest Hills Stadium in Queens.&nbsp;</span></p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p>
