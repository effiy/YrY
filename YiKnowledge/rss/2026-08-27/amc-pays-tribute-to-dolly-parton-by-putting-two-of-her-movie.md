<img src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2026/08/27190232/steel-magnolias.jpg" /><p>Dolly Parton didn&#8217;t star in a&nbsp;<em>lot&nbsp;</em>of movies after she got seriously into acting in the 1980s, but the ones she did make left a strong impact. (Give or take a <em>Frank McKlusky, C.I.&nbsp;</em>here or there, anyway.) In light of that, and in the wake of <a href="https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESZwHrOzAVydIMwxTOfR24lLID_bWq17c6ox5NAW8T58wAwAWt3X6cigcDkWHx55TQswheXkwim7QIPcGxT8ujRVPn2-4slwi0n0UxZq-8hRmlJwijacC7ppapHyEJI0CefdV9SyhznhA">Parton&#8217;s passing on Tuesday</a>, AMC Theaters <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/DcjisfcH4R1/?img_index=1">has announced</a> that it&#8217;s launching a tribute to the late country superstar and <a href="https://www.avclub.com/dolly-parton-retrospective-9-to-5-sabrina-carpenter-johnny-carson">enduring screen presence</a>, stating that it&#8217;ll be putting two of Parton&#8217;s films—<em>Steel Magnolias&nbsp;</em>and&nbsp;<em>The Best Little Whorehouse In Texas</em>—back into theaters starting this weekend.</p><!-- RevContent --> <div id="revcontent-hidden"> <!-- revisit --><!-- admarker --> 
<p>Specifically, AMC CEO Adam Aron announced on social media on Thursday that both movies will be rolling out to 200 AMC locations starting tomorrow, Friday, August 27, where they&#8217;ll stay until the following Wednesday. For those wondering which Dolly film to take the kids to, and who have some kind of persistent mental block that keeps them from understanding the words that pop up in movie titles, we&#8217;ll note that <em>Steel Magnolias&nbsp;</em>is rated PG, while&nbsp;<em>Whorehouse</em>—despite being significantly tamer/more musical than its name might lead you (<a href="https://www.instagram.com/reel/DTVc-AHjKjS/">or Rusty Venture</a>) to imagine—does carry an R. (Although it&#8217;s probably actually a lot less emotionally devastating than <em>Magnolias</em>, especially for those hit hard by Parton&#8217;s death this week.) And while you might be forgiven for asking where the heck <em>9 To 5</em>—Parton&#8217;s first major film role, and a persistent favorite—is in this little line-up, on the other hand, you&#8217;re also not being expected to show your love of Dolly by making it through a screening of <em>Rhinestone</em>, yeah?</p><!-- inlinecontent_1 --> </p>
<p>AMC&#8217;s move is just one of a pretty huge number of tributes that have been made to Parton this week, as people from all walks of life mourn the death of one of the last people that pretty much every American could agree was fantastic; there are not a <em>lot&nbsp;</em>of people in our current reality who would garner simultaneous tributes from Beyoncé, Andrew Lloyd Weber, and the Trump White House—but Parton was, clearly, that woman.</p>
