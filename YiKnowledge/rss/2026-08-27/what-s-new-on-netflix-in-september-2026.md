<p>Netflix's September slate is heavy on documentaries. The newest installments of sports doc series <em>Untold</em> tell the story of Australian Olympic breakdancer Raygun (<em>Untold Raygun: Breaking Badly</em>, Sept. 1) and pro wrestler&mdash;and 80s icon&mdash;Mr. T (<em>Untold Mr. T: I Pity The Fool</em>, Sept. 8). The <em>Turning Point</em> series, which has covered geopolitical conflicts like the War on Terror, the Cold War, and the Vietnam War, returns with <em>Turning Point: Generation 9/11</em> (Sept. 2), examining the impact of 9/11 on children who lost parents, journalists, and those who experienced the Afghanistan War. On the docudrama side, <em>UNABOMBER</em> (Sept. 25), inspired by the true story of Ted Kaczynski, stars Russell Crowe, Jacob Tremblay, and Shailene Woodley.</p><p>The second installment of <em>Hot Ones: Extra Heat</em>&mdash;which <a href="https://lifehacker.com/entertainment/whats-new-on-netflix-in-july-2026" target="_blank">first premiered in July</a>&mdash;drops on Sept. 14 at 10 p.m. ET. Host Sean Evans will be bringing spicy wings to WWE Raw. Netflix will also be hosting a live NFL matchup, played in Melbourne, Australia, between the San Francisco 49ers and the Los Angeles Rams. The game kicks off at 8:35 p.m. ET on Sept. 10. </p><p>New reality TV options include <em>Earle Meets World</em> (Sept. 4), an unscripted series following TikTok star Alix Earle, and <em>Wonka's The Golden Ticket</em> (Sept. 23), a competition set in a recreation of Willy Wonka's chocolate factory. Finally, there are new comedy specials from Jared Freid (<em>The Family Plan</em>, Sept. 1), Stavros Halkias (<em>Uncle Stav</em>, Sept. 8), and Jo Koy (<em>Blue in the Face</em>, Sept. 15).
