<div class="upx-component upx-embed-url"><div class="embed-holder"></div></div><p><a href="https://uproxx.com/pop/alex-warren-ordinary-no-1-hot-100-billboard/" target="_blank">Alex Warren</a> was one of the biggest breakout stars of 2025. His debut album <a href="https://uproxx.com/pop/alex-warren-youll-be-alright-kid-album/" target="_blank"><em>You&#8217;ll Be Alright, Kid</em></a> reached the top 5 of the Billboard 200; <a href="https://uproxx.com/insights/2025-top-music-videos/" target="_blank">&#8220;Ordinary&#8221;</a> has been streamed over 2 billion times (with an additional 377 million views for the music video); and he was nominated for Best New Artist at the Grammys. Now Warren has revealed his follow-up album, and based on the latest single, it&#8217;s going to be another mega-hit.</p>
<p>Warren announced that <em>Wildchild</em> is coming out on August 28 via Atlantic Records. The album will feature endearing new song &#8220;Passenger,&#8221; as well as previously singles <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vd60BO4uE54" target="_blank">“Fine Place to Die”</a> and <a href="https://uproxx.com/music/alex-warren-fever-dream/" target="_blank">“Fever Dream.&#8221;</a></p>
<p>“Growing up, my dad used to call me <em>Wildchild</em> so it’s always had a special meaning and I even have it tattooed on me,” Warren said. “Creating this album was really healing, therapeutic and a way for me to continue finding out more about myself. This album really shows where I am in my life today and compared to my first album which feels like a man who is broken, this one is a man who&#8217;s trying to heal. It’s been really nice to be able to confront those feelings and also be able to share them with the world to give everyone a transparent view into my life.”</p>
