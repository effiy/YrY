<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-how-to-make-chicken-fajita-quesadillas">How to make Chicken Fajita Quesadillas</h2>



<ol class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Cook the onion and bell peppers.</strong> Cook until tender and lightly charred in a&nbsp;<a href="https://amzn.to/3UqkJfr" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">large cast iron skillet</a>. Stir in the garlic, chili powder, cumin, salt, and pepper.</li>



<li><strong>Combine the cheeses.</strong> Toss the sharp cheddar cheese, pepper jack cheese, and cilantro until well combined.</li>



<li><strong>Cook the quesadillas using the half moon method.</strong> Add 1 tortilla to the skillet, sprinkling with the cheese mixture, chicken, and bell pepper mixture on one half of the tortilla. Cook until the cheeses melt, folding in half.</li>



<li><strong>Keep warm.</strong> Keep the quesadillas warm in a low oven (200°F) up to 20 minutes while the rest of the quesadillas are being cooked.</li>



<li><strong>Serve.</strong> Let the quesadillas rest 1-2 minutes before serving warm with toppings.</li>
</ol>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-pro-tip">PRO TIP</h2>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-leave-a-small-border">Leave a small border.</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Leave a small 1/4 or 1/2-inch border around the edge of the quesadilla – this will help prevent the filling from spilling out, sealing much better and keeping the filling contained for a crisp, golden brown quesadilla.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading faq-title">Chicken Fajita Quesadillas: Frequently Asked Questions</h2>



<div class="schema-faq wp-block-yoast-faq-block"><div class="schema-faq-section" id="faq-question-1781372304308"><strong class="schema-faq-question">What is the half moon method?</strong> <p class="schema-faq-answer">Instead of fighting to flip a two-tortilla quesadilla, place the filling on one half of a single tortilla, folding the empty half over to create a half moon shape. </p> </div> <div class="schema-faq-section" id="faq-question-1775052502936"><strong class="schema-faq-question">Can I make these in the oven?</strong> <p class="schema-faq-answer">Absolutely! The quesadillas can be baked on a <a href="https://amzn.to/3G4qThB" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">baking sheet</a> at 425°F for 10-15 minutes (brushing the tops with oil or butter for maximum crispness) until the tortillas are golden brown and cheeses have melted.</p> </div> <div class="schema-faq-section" id="faq-question-1715620917648"><strong class="schema-faq-question">What quesadilla toppings are best?</strong> <p class="schema-faq-answer"><a href="https://damndelicious.net/2026/04/17/the-best-guacamole-simple-fresh-restaurant-quality/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Guacamole</a>, pico de gallo, diced avocado, sour cream, cilantro, and lime wedges are all classic options. Serve with <a href="https://damndelicious.net/2014/03/12/mexican-rice/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Mexican rice</a> for a heartier meal.</p> </div> <div class="schema-faq-section" id="faq-question-1715620969548"><strong class="schema-faq-question">How can I store and reheat leftovers?</strong> <p class="schema-faq-answer">Leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the fridge for 3-4 days, reheating in the microwave, oven, or air fryer. For a quick reheat, the microwave is best, whereas the <a href="https://amzn.to/41981rq" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">air fryer</a> will be more suitable to achieve that favorited light exterior crispness. </p> </div> <div class="schema-faq-section" id="faq-question-1774982615197"><strong class="schema-faq-question">Are quesadillas freezer-friendly?</strong> <p class="schema-faq-answer">Yes! Let the quesadillas cool completely, wrapping individually in plastic wrap. Place on a <a href="https://amzn.to/3G4qThB" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">baking sheet</a> in a single layer, freezing for 1 hour until solid. Transfer to an <a href="https://amzn.to/3YOHZbg" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">airtight, resealable freezer bag</a>. Label, date, and freeze up to 2 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge and reheat in the oven or air fryer until warmed through.</p> </div> </div>

<div class="wprm-recipe-notes-container wprm-block-text-normal" id="recipe-46962-notes"><h3 class="wprm-recipe-header wprm-recipe-notes-header wprm-block-text-bold wprm-align-left wprm-header-decoration-none">Notes</h3><div class="wprm-recipe-notes"><ul>
<li><strong>Make them mini.</strong> Use smaller flour tortillas to make them bite-sized, perfect to serve as a party appetizer or after school snack.</li>
<li><strong>Use flour tortillas.</strong> While corn tortillas can certainly be used, flour tortillas is generally preferred for flexibility and ease of handling without splitting.</li>
<li><strong>Pre-cooked or leftover chicken is a huge time saver.</strong> Using pre-cooked chicken will cut down on prep time even further, making this dinner a perfect candidate for those busy, hectic weeknights.</li>
<li><strong>Do not overstuff.</strong> Size matters! Overstuffing may sound like a good idea since the filling is the best part, but this can result in ripped tortilla and overspill. Always spread the filling over half the quesadilla – this will ensure easy folding (think half moon shape) and handling without spillage.</li>
<li><strong>Freshly grated cheese goes a long way.</strong> Pre-packaged shredded cheese contains anti-caking agents that can hinder their ability to melt smoothly. Freshly grated cheese is best, melting more smoothly and evenly, and creating a far superior ooey gooey quesadilla filling.</li>
<li><strong>Coat the pan.</strong> Start with 1 tsp oil in the pan. Adding too much oil will result in soggy, greasy quesadillas.</li>
<li><strong>Kick up the heat.</strong> Add jalapeno slices or your favorite hot sauce for a kick of heat.</li>
<li><strong>Keep warm in a low oven.</strong> The quesadillas can be transferred to a <a href="https://amzn.to/3G4qThB" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">baking sheet</a> and kept warm in a low oven at 200°F while additional quesadillas are cooked or until you are ready to serve.</li>
<li><strong>Reheat in the oven or air fryer.</strong> For best results with leftovers, reheat in the oven at 350°F until warmed through, about 5-8 minutes, or in the <a href="https://amzn.to/41981rq" id="https://amzn.to/41981rq" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank" type="link">air fryer</a> at 350°F until warmed though and crisp, about 10 minutes, flipping halfway.</li>
</ul></div></div>
