She was in Italy for a bit, and in the upcoming final season of the series, Emily is going to be spending some time in Greece. &#8220;I wanted the final season to feel like the biggest </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Emily In Paris </span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">experience yet. We went to multiple locations this season, and Greece was somewhere we really wanted to showcase in a spectacular way,&#8221; says series creator Darren Star in a press statement. </span><a href="https://www.avclub.com/emily-in-paris-macron-comments-staying-in-france"><span style="font-weight: 400;">But fear not, President Macron</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">—Emily will be back in Paris by the season&#8217;s end. Netflix confirmed that the upcoming season is currently in production in France while also sharing some photos from Greece.&nbsp;</span></p><!-- RevContent --> <div id="revcontent-hidden"> <!-- revisit -->
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The official synopsis for </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Emily In Paris</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">&#8216; final season reads:</span></p><!-- inlinecontent_1 --> </p>
<blockquote><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Stepping into a new era of ambition and romance, Emily faces defining choices that test her ultimate loyalties. But as unexpected career conflicts and shifting relationships force her to reevaluate where her true future lies, she must make one final choice between her career, her heart, and the life she’s built in Paris.</span></p></blockquote>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The sixth and final season of </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Emily In Paris</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> premieres on Netflix on December 24.
