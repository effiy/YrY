<p>These wireless ANC headphones, which earned a <a href="https://ca.pcmag.com/headphones/15513/nothing-headphone-a" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="open in a new window"><u>PCMag Editors' Choice Award</u></a>, feature class-leading battery life, excellent sound, and effective noise cancellation. They last up to 135 hours with ANC and AAC codec off, and 75 hours with ANC on. A quick five-minute charge gives you up to eight minutes of playback, and the headphones completely recharge in two hours. The app lets you switch listening modes, adjust how much high- and low-frequency noise gets filtered out, and enable Transparency mode.&nbsp;</p><p>PCMag describes the sound as &ldquo;rich, bright, and bass-heavy.&rdquo;&nbsp; The bass-boosted sound is courtesy of the More Bass EQ preset, and unlike some headphone brands, the bass is pronounced but doesn&rsquo;t overpower the mids and highs, remaining fairly balanced. They have 40mm dynamic drivers and a 20-40kHz frequency response, earning them Hi-Res Audio certification.</p><p>The headphones use four HD mics to capture voices clearly, and they do a good job of blocking out ambient noise. While this is a small step down from the six mics on the <a href="https://zdcs.link/aD8E2o?pageview_type=RSS&amp;template=content&amp;module=content_body&amp;element=offer&amp;item=text-link&amp;element_label=Headphone%20%281%29&amp;object_type=content&amp;short_url=aD8E2o&amp;u=https%3A%2F%2Flifehacker.com%2Ffeed%2Frss" rel="sponsored" target="_blank" title="open in a new window"><u>Headphone (1)</u></a>, they&rsquo;re not a stripped-down budget version. They share many of the same features, including ANC, spatial audio with head tracking, LDAC high-res codec support, and USB-C lossless audio. The main trade-offs are in material, appearance, and subtle differences in sound tuning. While still plush and comfy, the earcups aren&rsquo;t quite as supple as those on the flagship pair.</p><p>That said, you might actually prefer the Nothing Headphone (a) if you want a more low-key look. They also have better battery life, and the lighter materials make them more comfortable and compact. If you&rsquo;re looking for a long-lasting, feature-rich pair of over-ear ANC headphones that back up the aesthetics with genuinely strong sound, the <a href="https://zdcs.link/aA08jb?pageview_type=RSS&amp;template=content&amp;module=content_body&amp;element=offer&amp;item=text-link&amp;element_label=Nothing%20Headphone%20%28a%29&amp;object_type=content&amp;short_url=aA08jb&amp;u=https%3A%2F%2Flifehacker.com%2Ffeed%2Frss" rel="sponsored" target="_blank" title="open in a new window"><u>Nothing Headphone (a)</u></a> is a solid option.</p><div class=" relative flex justify-center py-16 md:left-1/2 md:w-[780px] md:max-w-max md:-translate-x-1/2">
