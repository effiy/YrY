<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-mediterranean-cucumber-salad">Mediterranean Cucumber Salad</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Cucumber might be my favourite vegetable. (By &ldquo;vegetable&rdquo;, I mean the fresh, virtuous non-starchy kind. Potatoes are in a separate category &ndash; my family doesn&rsquo;t call me Potato Girl for nothing.)</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I think I&rsquo;ve done more iterations of cucumber salads than any other vegetable &ndash; from <a href="https://www.recipetineats.com/creamy-cucumber-salad-lemon-yogurt-dressing/" id="40025" type="post">tangy creamy ones</a> to <a href="https://www.recipetineats.com/spicy-asian-cucumber-salad/" id="96425" type="post">spicy Asian ones</a>, <a href="https://www.recipetineats.com/smashed-cucumber-salad/" id="40623" type="post">smashed</a>, <a href="https://www.recipetineats.com/beetroot-cured-salmon-gin-or-vodka/" id="60227" type="post">pickled</a>, <a href="https://www.recipetineats.com/cucumber-salad-with-herb-garlic-vinaigrette/" id="40229" type="post">herby</a>, and everything in between. And here I am today with another one. I just love them!</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This one is full of bright Mediterranean flavours. I adore the combination of mint and dill, they make cucumbers taste even more refreshing, especially with a fresh lemon dressing. Add a crumble of feta, and watch how quickly this salad disappears!</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="Mediterranean Cucumber Salad" class="wp-image-202233" height="1500" src="https://www.recipetineats.com/tachyon/2026/08/Favourite-Cucumber-Salad-4.jpg" width="1200" /></figure>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-ingredients-in-cucumber-salad">Ingredients in Cucumber Salad</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">There&rsquo;s not much that goes in this. But I&rsquo;m sharing my swap-out options too, because I don&rsquo;t always have fresh herbs, or a lemon. Though obviously if you change everything, you&rsquo;ll end up with a completely different salad! </p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-1-for-the-salad">1. For the salad</h3>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="Mediterranean Cucumber Salad" class="wp-image-202240" height="857" src="https://www.recipetineats.com/tachyon/2026/08/Ingredients-in-my-Favourite-Cucumber-Salad-1.jpg" width="1200" /></figure>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><p><strong>Cucumbers</strong> &ndash; I prefer using Lebanese cucumbers in this salad because it&rsquo;s juicier and the skin is soft enough to eat without peeling. If you use the long ones (telegraph / continental / English cucumbers), the skin can be a little tough so you might want to do zebra stripe peels or completely peel.</p></li>



<li><p><strong>Greek feta &ndash;</strong> The recipe only calls for a little bit, just 50g (about 1/4 cup crumbled). It&rsquo;s not much but makes a difference! I can&rsquo;t think of a substitute. Instead, add an extra 1/4 teaspoon of salt into the dressing (essential, as it&rsquo;s light on dressing because there&rsquo;s salt in the feta) and if you want, give the salad another point of interest, like lemon zest (add this into the dressing) or chopped olives.</p></li>



<li><p><strong>Fresh dill and mint &ndash; </strong>This combination is just everything to bring bright freshness to salads! Dill is a natural friend of cucumber while mint makes the whole salad taste extra fresh. <strong>If you don&rsquo;t have fresh herbs</strong>, add dried herbs into the dressing instead &ndash; dill, mint or oregano. All these have Mediterranean vibes! </p></li>



<li><p><strong>Red onion </strong>&ndash; Just one quarter, finely sliced, to bring a little sharpness and crunch without taking over. Substitute with finely sliced green onion.</p></li>
</ul>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-2-lemon-garlic-dressing">2. lemon garlic dressing</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Lemon, olive oil, garlic, salt and pepper is all you need &ndash; in the right proportions!</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="Mediterranean Cucumber Salad" class="wp-image-202241" height="857" src="https://www.recipetineats.com/tachyon/2026/08/Ingredients-in-my-Favourite-Cucumber-Salad-2.jpg" width="1200" /></figure>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><p><strong>Lemon juice </strong>&ndash; You can&rsquo;t beat fresh lemon juice in a lemon dressing, though if you don&rsquo;t have any, I&rsquo;d rather use a vinegar than bottled lemon juice. Red or white wine vinegar or apple cider vinegar are my go-to choices for dressings, and all will work beautifully here.</p></li>



<li><p><strong>Extra virgin olive oil </strong>&ndash; The better your oil, the better your dressing! So pull out your good stuff here. 🙂</p></li>



<li><p><strong>Garlic &ndash; </strong>Just one small garlic clove, crushed using a garlic crush or finely grated. Don&rsquo;t go too wild with the garlic else it will dominate and be too harsh.</p></li>



<li><p><strong>Salt and pepper</strong> &ndash; To season the salad. I only use 1/4 heaped teaspoon of salt, not much for the mound of cucumbers. But you get plenty of salt from the feta. </p></li>
</ul>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="Mediterranean Cucumber Salad" class="wp-image-202236" height="960" src="https://www.recipetineats.com/tachyon/2026/08/Favourite-Cucumber-Salad-8.jpg" width="1200" /></figure>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-how-to-make-mediterranean-cucumber-salad">How to make Mediterranean Cucumber Salad</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The jar-shaking method is my favourite way to make dressings because it&rsquo;s quick and mixes everything together really well so the oil and vinegar don&rsquo;t separate. Plus, the jar doubles as storage when you make extra.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="Mediterranean Cucumber Salad" class="wp-image-202239" height="327" src="https://www.recipetineats.com/tachyon/2026/08/How-to-make-my-Faourite-Cucumber-Salad-2.jpg" width="1200" /></figure>



<ol class="wp-block-list">
<li><p><strong>Dressing</strong> &ndash; Shake the dressing ingredients in a jar until they are well combined and a creamy yellow colour. (Or whisk in a small bowl). <strong>Set aside</strong> &ndash; Put the dressing aside while you chop all the ingredients. This will give the garlic a chance to meld into the dressing (it only needs a few minutes).</p></li>



<li><p><strong>Salad </strong>&ndash; Put all the salad ingredients in a bowl except a bit of feta, save it for sprinkling on at the end. Then pour the dressing over.</p></li>
</ol>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="Mediterranean Cucumber Salad" class="wp-image-202238" height="327" src="https://www.recipetineats.com/tachyon/2026/08/How-to-make-my-Faourite-Cucumber-Salad-1.jpg" width="1200" /></figure>



<ol class="wp-block-list" start="3">
<li><p><strong>Toss</strong>, toss, toss!</p></li>



<li><p><strong>Garnish and serve</strong> &ndash; Pour the cucumber salad into a serving bowl then sprinkle with remaining feta. Ready to serve!</p></li>
</ol>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="Mediterranean Cucumber Salad" class="wp-image-202237" height="1500" src="https://www.recipetineats.com/tachyon/2026/08/Favourite-Cucumber-Salad-9.jpg" width="1200" /></figure>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Hand on heart, I had a big bowl of this for lunch yesterday, standing at the kitchen bench as I mentally added up the list of meaty and chocolatey and cheesy and noodly things I&rsquo;d been making that morning and were to come that afternoon.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This thing I do that I call a job &ndash; being surrounded by outrageously good food all day &ndash; means I need to find absurdly delicious ways to eat wholesome food, just to balance it all out! But if you&rsquo;re a normal person making this as a side dish, try it alongside a simple seasoned <a href="https://www.recipetineats.com/chicken-breast-recipe/" id="181805" type="post">chicken breast</a> (that&rsquo;s my go-to recipe) a pan seared <a href="https://www.recipetineats.com/crispy-pan-fried-fish/" id="21797" type="post">fish fillet</a> or <a href="https://www.recipetineats.com/lemon-garlic-marinated-pork-chops/" id="26934" type="post">Lemon Garlic Pork Chops</a>.</p>
