<img src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2026/03/27184614/gosling-soap-feature.jpg" /><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">It&#8217;s been over 25 years since the last live-action </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Flintstones </span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">movie, so why not give another followup a try? This at least seems to be what producer Ryan Gosling, hot off the success of </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Project Hail Mary</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">, is thinking. </span><a href="https://deadline.com/2026/08/ryan-gosling-flintstones-bamm-bamm-movie-1237049958/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">According to </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Deadline</span></i></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, Gosling and Open Invite producing partner Jessie Henderson are in the early stages of developing a new </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Flintstones</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> movie centered on an adult Bamm-Bamm. It&#8217;s not clear at this point whether Gosling intends to act in the film—it&#8217;s still looking for a writer—but Bamm Bamm could have a certain Kenergy, if they age the character up enough.&nbsp;</span></p><!-- RevContent --> <div id="revcontent-hidden"> <!-- revisit --><!-- admarker --> 
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Bamm-Bamm, who is the adopted son of Barney and Betty Rubble, was previously the focus of an animated series in the early 1970s with Fred and Wilma Flintstone&#8217;s daughter Pebbles. That was called, naturally, </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">The Pebbles And Bamm-Bamm Show</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">. Bamm-Bamm&#8217;s adoption was also featured in the 1994 live-action </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Flintstones</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> film, where he generally behaves like a menace and helps plunge his parents into financial trouble. He wasn&#8217;t featured in the 2000 prequel film </span><a href="https://www.avclub.com/the-flintstones-in-viva-rock-vegas-1798192363"><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">The Flintstones In Viva Rock Vegas</span></i></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, which bombed hard enough to effectively close the door on the notion of a live action </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Flintstones </span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">film for the past 26 years. But perhaps Gosling has enough goodwill for a more modern take on a modern Stone Age family.&nbsp;</span></p><!-- inlinecontent_1 --> </p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p>
