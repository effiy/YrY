"How powerful is this music?"</em> HBO Max has revealed their full official trailer for a new <a href="https://www.firstshowing.net/category/docs/">documentary</a> series streaming this fall titled <strong><em>JAŸ-Z in 8</em></strong>, although the actual title features the infinity symbol: <strong><em>JAŸ-Z in ∞</em></strong>. The 8-part doc series features artist JAŸ-Z in conversation with music guru <strong>Rick Rubin</strong>, and it's also directed by Rick Rubin (you can learn more about him <a href="https://www.firstshowing.net/2020/first-trailer-for-paul-mccartney-x-rick-rubin-documentary-event/" title="First Trailer for 'Paul McCartney x Rick Rubin' Documentary Series">in this doc</a>). In Rick Rubin’s directorial documentary debut, the series unfolds over many hours of candid conversation between two creative superstars. The career-spanning series closely studies the ingenuity of JAŸ-Z’s lyrics as he breaks down his writing process and traces how his words reflect his childhood, the experiences that shaped him, and the evolving world he navigated as his career grew. As JAŸ-Z guides us through the words and music that informed the work, he shows the deep inextricable connection between art and life. This doc series is executive produced by <strong>Daniel Kaluuya</strong>, and features cinematography by acclaimed DP <strong>Bradford Young</strong> (of <em>Selma, Arrival, Solo: A Star Wars Story, BLKNWS</em>). This actually does look quite fascinating to watch, covering all of Jay-Z's career and many of his most famous songs.
