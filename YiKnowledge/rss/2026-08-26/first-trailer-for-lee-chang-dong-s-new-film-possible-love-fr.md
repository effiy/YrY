<em>"I thought it'd be nice to go on a vacation together."</em> Netflix has revealed the first official trailer for one of the fall's most anticipated new films – titled <strong><em>Possible Love</em></strong>, the latest feature made by acclaimed Korean filmmaker <strong>Lee Chang-dong</strong>. This is a must watch! Of course. This is his first new film in 8 years since last making <a href="https://www.firstshowing.net/2018/new-full-length-us-trailer-for-lee-chang-dongs-burning-from-korea/" title="New Full-Length US Trailer for Lee Chang-dong's 'Burning' from Korea"><em>Burning</em></a>, and he's ready to premiere it soon – <a href="https://www.firstshowing.net/2026/83rd-venice-film-festival-selection-affleck-herzog-gibney-zeller/" title="83rd Venice Film Festival Selection - Affleck, Herzog, Gibney, Zeller">debuting at the</a> 2026 Venice Film Festival starting up next week. It's a tale of two couples in Korea. The first reviews will be arriving soon as it plays at the major festivals this fall. The story follows two married couples — a laid-off worker and his wife, and a documentary filmmaker and her husband — whose paths cross when the filmmaker sets out to film them, bringing both marriages face to face with their different lives and hidden desires. These complex emotional entanglements challenge their existing relationships and force the group to confront uncomfortable truths about love and commitment. Starring <strong>Jeon Do-yeon</strong> as Mi-ok, <strong>Sul Kyung-gu</strong> as Ho-seok, <strong>Zo In-sung</strong> as Sang-woo, and <strong>Cho Yeo-jeong</strong> as Ye-ji. What an intriguing trailer! It ends on a very strange note, with many fascinating moments before setting up the characters.
