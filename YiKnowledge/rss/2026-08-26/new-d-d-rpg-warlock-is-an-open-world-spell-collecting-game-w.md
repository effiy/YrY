<img src="https://assetsio.gnwcdn.com/warlock_p6x8U0n.jpg?width=690&amp;quality=85&amp;format=jpg&amp;auto=webp" /> This week we got our first proper look at Warlock: Dungeons & Dragons, Wizard of the Coast's new RPG-tinted action game. In theory, it's the story of a grizzled weapon master who is searching a lawless pocket dimension for somebody precious to her, but I disapprove massively of this objective, because it sounds like it's going to get in the way of unearthing weird spells and making a glorious mess.
<p>Created by a team of former <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/far-cry">Far Cry</a>, <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/watch-dogs">Watch Dogs</a> and <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/splinter-cell">Splinter Cell</a> devs, Warlock gives you an open world full of charms, cantrips and conjurations to gather and fool around with. I'm really hoping it's as open-ended as they're making it sound. Here's a video, followed by some commentary from creators Invoke.
