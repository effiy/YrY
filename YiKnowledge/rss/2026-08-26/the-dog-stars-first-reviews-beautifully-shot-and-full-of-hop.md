<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/ridley_scott">Ridley Scott’</a>s <strong><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/the_dog_stars"><em>The Dog Stars</em> </a></strong>opens this weekend, and the first reviews are now online, giving the veteran filmmaker one of his lower Tomatometer scores. Based on the 2012 novel of the same name by Peter Heller, the familiar post-apocalyptic sci-fi movie is set after a devastating pandemic and stars Oscar-nominated actors <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/josh_brolin">Josh Brolin</a>, <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/jacob_elordi">Jacob Elordi</a>, and <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/guy_pearce">Guy Pearce</a>, alongside <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/margaret_qualley">Margaret Qualley</a>. While the cinematography is being praised abundantly, and the cast is expectedly strong, the script is reportedly not on the level that Scott deserves.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Here’s what critics are saying about <em>The Dog Stars</em>:</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Has Ridley Scott made another must-see classic?</strong></h2>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>The Dog Stars</em> seems unlikely to land near the top of anyone’s Ridley Scott rankings. But it’s an appealingly mellow watch.<br />&#8211; <a href="https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-reviews/the-dog-stars-review-jacob-elordi-ridley-scott-1236680319/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">David Rooney, The Hollywood Reporter</a></p>
</blockquote>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If not quite a return to form…[it] is at least a return to baseline competency after the gilded train wreck that was <em>Gladiator II</em>.<br />&#8211; <a href="https://www.slantmagazine.com/film/the-dog-stars-review-jacob-elordi-ridley-scott/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Eli Friedberg, Slant Magazine</a></p>
</blockquote>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>The Dog Stars</em> is still pretty good as far as middle-tier Ridley Scott movies go. While not one of his classics, he’s still a pro at creating atmosphere.<br />&#8211; <a href="https://www.joblo.com/the-dog-stars-review-ridley-scotts-latest-is-uneven-but-entertaining/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Chris Bumbray, JoBlo’s Movie Network</a></p>
</blockquote>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">[It’s] the kind of movie Hollywood used to make a lot more often than they do now. Hopefully, audiences find it.<br />&#8211; <a href="https://deadline.com/2026/08/the-dog-stars-review-jacob-elordi-ridley-scott-1237049569/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Pete Hammond, Deadline Hollywood Daily</a></p>
</blockquote>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>The Dog Stars</em> isn’t a total misfire — it’s just the first Ridley Scott joint in some years that feels totally unnecessary.<br />&#8211; <a href="https://www.indiewire.com/criticism/movies/the-dog-stars-review-jacob-elordi-ridley-scott-movie-1235212620/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Ryan Lattanzio, IndieWire</a></p>
</blockquote>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">It is rare that Scott releases an actively boring film, but here we are.<br />&#8211; <a href="https://fictionmachine.com/2026/08/26/review-the-dog-stars-2026/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Grant Watson, Fiction Machine</a></p>
</blockquote>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>The Dog Stars</em> is unfortunately Scott’s first in a long while where the haphazard nature of the film isn’t even fascinating to watch.<br />&#8211; <a href="https://geekvibesnation.com/the-dog-stars-review/#google_vignette" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Joshua Mbonu, Geek Vibes Nation</a></p>
</blockquote>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>The Dog Stars</em>… continues Scott’s late-stage slide into mediocrity.<br />&#8211; <a href="https://www.rogerebert.com/reviews/the-dog-stars-jacob-elordi-josh-brolin-margaret-qualley-ridley-scott-film-review-2026" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Robert Daniels, RogerEbert.com</a></p>
</blockquote>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>How does it compare to his other movies?</strong></h2>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">It’s arguably Ridley Scott’s best since <em>The Martian</em> 11 years ago.<br />&#8211; <a href="https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-reviews/the-dog-stars-review-jacob-elordi-ridley-scott-1236680319/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">David Rooney, The Hollywood Reporter</a></p>
</blockquote>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">It’s nowhere near Sir Ridley’s finest, but at least it’s another subtype of speculative fiction filmmaking he can check off his bucket list.<br />&#8211; <a href="https://www.slantmagazine.com/film/the-dog-stars-review-jacob-elordi-ridley-scott/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Eli Friedberg, Slant Magazine</a></p>
</blockquote>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>The Dog Stars</em> cements itself as both the weakest and most prodigiously empty of Scott’s most recent works.<br />&#8211; <a href="https://geekvibesnation.com/the-dog-stars-review/#google_vignette" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Joshua Mbonu, Geek Vibes Nation</a></p>
</blockquote>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">It&#8217;s a movie that doesn&#8217;t feel like Scott at either his most bombastic or thought-provoking, but it&#8217;s certainly one of his most basic ones.<br />&#8211; <a href="https://thefilmmaven.com/the-dog-stars-review-jacob-elordi/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Kristen Lopez, The Film Maven</a></p>
</blockquote>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">It’s his most lackluster offering in over a decade.<br />&#8211; <a href="https://www.thedailybeast.com/obsessed/the-dog-stars-this-star-studded-post-apocalyptic-drama-is-dog-awful/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Nick Schager, The Daily Beast</a></p>
</blockquote>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This wasteland narrative is the most tedious film of Scott’s career.<br />&#8211; <a href="https://www.rogerebert.com/reviews/the-dog-stars-jacob-elordi-josh-brolin-margaret-qualley-ridley-scott-film-review-2026" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Robert Daniels, RogerEbert.com</a></p>
</blockquote>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Is it more hopeful than he&#8217;s typically known for?</strong></h2>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Some might wonder if Scott is going soft in his senior years in some of the concluding developments, which provide hope for humanity’s resilience.<br />&#8211; <a href="https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-reviews/the-dog-stars-review-jacob-elordi-ridley-scott-1236680319/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">David Rooney, The Hollywood Reporter</a></p>
</blockquote>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The film&#8217;s optimism ultimately keeps it from ever turning into a slog.<br />&#8211; <a href="https://collider.com/the-dog-stars-review-jacob-elordi-ridley-scott/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Gabrielle Moss, Collider</a></p>
</blockquote>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Occasionally, the director has permitted himself a little more faith in people. His new picture lacks the ingenuity and confidence of his 2015 picture <em>The Martian</em>, but the two films share a stubborn belief that humans’ inherent decency and resourcefulness can, every once in a while, save the day.<br />&#8211; <a href="https://www.screendaily.com/reviews/the-dog-stars-review-jacob-elordi-and-margaret-qualley-navigate-the-apocalypse-in-muted-tender-ridley-scott-drama/5219711.article" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Tim Grierson, Screen International</a></p>

<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Scott goes almost laughably soft in the film’s closing stanzas, not to mention cuts a couple of giant corners in order to reach his pat and unconvincing happily-ever-after.<br />&#8211; <a href="https://www.thedailybeast.com/obsessed/the-dog-stars-this-star-studded-post-apocalyptic-drama-is-dog-awful/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Nick Schager, The Daily Beast</a></p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>How is the action?</strong></h2>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Scott’s flair for tactile world-building is most excitingly felt in the tense, elaborate, and bloody battle sequences.<br />&#8211; <a href="https://www.slantmagazine.com/film/the-dog-stars-review-jacob-elordi-ridley-scott/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Eli Friedberg, Slant Magazine</a></p>
</blockquote>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In its most climactic moments, <em>The Dog Stars</em> resembles the explosive warfare of <em>Black Hawk Down</em>.<br />&#8211; <a href="https://www.polygon.com/the-dog-stars-review-ridley-scott/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Jake Kleinman, Polygon</a></p>
</blockquote>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">When the action is present, it yields some of the best moments found in the movie.<br />&#8211; <a href="https://thefilmmaven.com/the-dog-stars-review-jacob-elordi/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Kristen Lopez, The Film Maven</a></p>
</blockquote>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">[It’s] a long shot to satisfy short-attention-span consumers of brawny action.<br />&#8211; <a href="https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-reviews/the-dog-stars-review-jacob-elordi-ridley-scott-1236680319/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">David Rooney, The Hollywood Reporter</a></p>
</blockquote>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">First and foremost, despite a pair of crackerjack action sequences, this is a movie where character development takes the front seat.<br />&#8211; <a href="https://deadline.com/2026/08/the-dog-stars-review-jacob-elordi-ridley-scott-1237049569/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Pete Hammond, Deadline Hollywood Daily</a></p>
</blockquote>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>The Dog Stars</em> may disappoint viewers wanting an explosive action-horror comparable to <em>I Am Legend</em>, <em>The Last Of Us</em> or <em>28 Years Later</em>.<br />&#8211; <a href="https://www.screendaily.com/reviews/the-dog-stars-review-jacob-elordi-and-margaret-qualley-navigate-the-apocalypse-in-muted-tender-ridley-scott-drama/5219711.article" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Tim Grierson, Screen International</a></p>
</blockquote>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Ridley Scott’s attempt at a post-apocalyptic thriller never fully commits to whether it wants to be an action movie or horror, settling for minute bits of each.<br />&#8211; <a href="https://edwarddouglas.substack.com/p/the-dog-stars-review" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Edward Douglas, The Weekend Warrior</a></p>
</blockquote>

<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
