<em>"I want the big story, and then I want the bigger story." "I promised these sources they were going to be safe."</em> Sony Pictures has revealed the official full trailer for <strong><em>The Social Reckoning</em></strong>, the highly anticipated follow-up to David Fincher's 2010 film <em>The Social Network</em> about Facebook. The big reveal trailer following the <a href="https://www.firstshowing.net/2026/first-look-teaser-for-the-social-reckoning-sorkins-facebook-sequel/" title="First Look Teaser for 'The Social Reckoning' Sorkin's Facebook Sequel">teaser a few months ago</a> featuring way more footage. Screenwriter <strong>Aaron Sorkin</strong> returns steering the ship, also directing in addition to writing. "The <a href="https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/aug/26/meta-social-media-addiction-trial-settlement" rel="noopener nofollow " target="_blank">verdict is in</a>. The reckoning is coming." This is essentially the sequel to <em>The Social Network</em>, arriving in theaters <a href="https://www.firstshowing.net/schedule2026/#oct">this October</a>. "Every revolution begins with a reckoning." This sequel follows the story of Frances Haugen, a young Facebook engineer, who enlists the help of Jeff Horwitz, a Wall Street Journal reporter, to blow the whistle on the social network's most guarded secrets - soon to be revealed. Starring <strong>Mikey Madison</strong>, <strong>Jeremy Allen White</strong>, <strong>Wunmi Mosaku</strong>, <strong>Betty Gilpin</strong>, <strong>Billy Magnussen</strong>, with <strong>Bill Burr</strong>, and <strong>Jeremy Strong</strong> as the slimy Mark Zuckerberg trying to push back against all the harm that his website has caused. A much better trailer than before, but still a tad hesitant about going all in on it. Though it does look like it's going to hit back hard against Zuckerberg.
