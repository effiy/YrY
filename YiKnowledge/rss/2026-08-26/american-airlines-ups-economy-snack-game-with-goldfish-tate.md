<p class="wp-block-paragraph">American Airlines has <a href="https://news.aa.com/news/news-details/2026/Boarding-soon-American-Airlines-to-elevate-the-inflight-experience-with-new-lineup-of-complimentary-snacks-MKG-OB-08/default.aspx" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">announced that it will update</a> its complimentary economy snack offerings. While nothing major, it’s a reminder of how little, positive changes can impact the perception passengers have…</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-american-introducing-more-economy-snack-options">American introducing more economy snack options</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">American serves complimentary snacks to travelers in economy, offered with the beverage service. Up until now, the choice has been either Biscoff cookies or unbranded pretzels. That will soon be changing, with a sweet and savory option always available:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>On morning flights, passengers will have the option of Biscoff cookies or Goldfish</li>



<li>For the rest of the day, passengers will have the option of Tate’s Bake Shop chocolate chip cookies or Peterson’s savory and sweet snack mix</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The new offerings are expected to be available starting in early October 2026. Here’s how Heather Garboden, American’s Chief Customer Officer, describes this change:</p>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">“Small details can make a meaningful difference in the travel experience, especially the familiar touches customers look forward to throughout their journey. As we looked at our snack offerings, we wanted to take the time to get this refresh right by listening to customer feedback and understanding what customers value most. This updated lineup brings together beloved favorites and new choices in a way that feels familiar, elevated and enjoyable.”</p>
</blockquote>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img alt="" class="wp-image-371591" height="675" src="https://cdn.onemileatatime.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/American-Snacks-Economy.jpg" width="1200" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">American is rolling out new complimentary snacks in economy</figcaption></figure>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-kudos-this-is-a-minor-but-positive-change-for-american">Kudos, this is a minor but positive change for American</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Obviously this individual change isn’t going to get anyone to book American. However, this is a minor update that a vast majority of customers will appreciate, since it’s not something just reserved for a limited number of routes, for a few lounges, etc. It’s great to see the pace at which American is rolling out positive changes across cabins. American management of course finally realizes how far behind it has fallen in recent years, and the extent to which customers care.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I commend American management for these changes. Of course it has to acknowledged that the reason we’re seeing so many positive changes as of late is because management was asleep at the wheel for many years when it comes to customer experience, and most of what we’re seeing is just a copy and paste of what the competition is doing.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">So I don’t want to be too nice for simply doing the obvious, when it’s most of the same people who have been there all along, and were also responsible for things going in the opposite direction. But hey, a positive change is a positive change, and I’m happy to see this.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-bottom-line">Bottom line</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">As of October 2026, American will be improving the snacks it serves in economy. Rather than just serving Biscoffs and unbranded pretzels all day, we’ll see the option of Biscoffs of Goldfish in the morning, and of Tate’s Bake Shop chocolate chip cookies and Peterson’s savory and sweet snack mix the rest of the day. Keep it coming, American!</p>
