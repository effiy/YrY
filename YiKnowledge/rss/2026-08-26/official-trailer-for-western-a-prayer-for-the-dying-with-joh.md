The whole town!"</em> Quiver Distribution has debuted the latest official trailer for the indie western film titled <strong><em>A Prayer for the Dying</em></strong>, marking the feature debut of Norwegian-American filmmaker Dara Van Dusen. This first premiered at the 2026 Berlin Film Festival earlier this year (<a href="https://www.firstshowing.net/2026/john-c-reilly-in-western-film-a-prayer-for-the-dying-first-look-trailer/" title="John C. Not a remake, based on the book of the same name, and not the same as the 1987 British film also titled <a href="https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0093771/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><em>A Prayer for the Dying</em></a> with Mickey Rourke. This new film is set in 1870 in Friendship – a small Wisconsin town, made up of Scandinavian settlers, is still suffering from the repercussions of the recent Civil War. So when faced with a new and even deadlier threat, one man is forced to make a harrowing choice: save his young family or defend the community that gave him a second chance at life and meaning. <em>A Prayer for the Dying</em> is described as "powerful account of the choices we must make when disaster strikes and everything we hold dear, including faith, family, community, is shaken to its core." With <strong>Johnny Flynn</strong> as Jacob Hansen, <strong>John C. Reilly</strong> as Doc, <strong>Kristine Kujath Thorp</strong>, <strong>Gustav Lindh</strong>, <strong>Hilton Pelser</strong>, <strong>Andrew Whipp</strong>. Based on Stewart O'Nan's <a href="https://amzn.to/4qu5yCh" rel="noopener" target="_blank">novel</a>.
