<img src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2026/08/26112820/MixCollage-26-Aug-2026-11-28-AM-6081.jpg" /><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">It appears there are an endless number of opportunities to join a </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Harry Potter</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> project. While </span><a href="https://www.avclub.com/kit-harrington-nicholas-hoult-harry-potter"><span style="font-weight: 400;">HBO has been hard at work casting and recasting</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> its upcoming series, actors continue to join the Broadway stage show, </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Harry Potter And The Cursed Child</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">. The latest of these is Rupert Grint, who will reprise his role of Ron Weasley from the </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Harry Potter</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> films on stage. Grint will perform the role starting on February 2 until May 30, 2027.&nbsp;</span></p><!-- RevContent --> <div id="revcontent-hidden"> <!-- revisit -->
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">&#8220;Ron Weasley has been a part of me since I was 11 years old and I can’t wait to meet him again in this stage of life,&#8221; says Grint in a press release. &#8220;There is something so full-circle and so special about stepping back into Ron’s shoes now when we are both fathers; it is somehow both very familiar and completely new. And the joy of being back on Broadway, this time in a role that defined so much of my life is truly exciting.&#8221;&nbsp;</span></p><!-- inlinecontent_1 --> </p><!-- admarker --> 
<p><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Harry Potter And The Cursed Child </span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">focuses mostly on the children of the characters from the original stories and, other than Harry himself, most of the adult characters have pretty limited stage time. But Grint isn&#8217;t the first original cast member to join the play; Tom Felton, who portrayed Draco Malfoy in the films, has performed off and on in </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Cursed Child </span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">since November 2025, extending his run multiple times. The play has credited his participation as a major factor in its current success; since he joined, the play has &#8220;consistently held the #1 spot on the Broadway grosses,&#8221; according to a press release from April. Felton isn&#8217;t scheduled to overlap with Grint (though he wouldn&#8217;t be surprised to see him extend his stay at Hogwarts yet again), so the Ron actor&#8217;s inclusion in the show will likely be an even bigger boon to the play. Back in 2022, Grint </span><a href="https://www.thetimes.com/life-style/article/rupert-grint-i-liken-jk-rowling-to-an-auntie-i-dont-agree-with-everything-my-auntie-says-qslq3qh3x"><span style="font-weight: 400;">described his relationship</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> with </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Harry Potter </span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">author J.K. Rowling as &#8220;tricky,&#8221; likening her to an &#8220;auntie&#8221; that he doesn&#8217;t completely agree with but is still willing to line the pockets of. </span></p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p>
