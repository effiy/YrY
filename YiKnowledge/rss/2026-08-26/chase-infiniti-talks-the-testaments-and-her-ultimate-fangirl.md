<p class="wp-block-paragraph">After the work that she put in during the awards season for <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/one_battle_after_another">One Battle After Another</a></em>, we all would understand if <strong><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/chase_infiniti">Chase Infiniti</a></strong> took a much-needed break from the grind. However just a few months later, she is back at it and Emmy nominated for her work in <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/the_handmaids_tale">The Handmaid&#8217;s Tale</a></em> sequel series <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/the_testaments"><strong>The Testaments</strong></a></em>. Playing Agnes, the daughter of revolutionary June Osborn, she gives a reserved and captivating performance. The Hulu series, which attempts to fills in the gaps <em>The Handmaid&#8217;s Tale </em>didn&#8217;t cover, also serves as an incredible showcase for a crop of talented young actresses and an exciting peek into the new Gilead.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In their chat, Infiniti shares when she auditioned for the role, why the return of crew members from the original series made the world of Gilead feel so lived-in and real, and how she mentally prepared for the emotionally demanding scene in which Agnes is interrogated by the Commanders about her suitability for marriage.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img alt="" class="wp-image-295951" height="380" src="https://editorial.rottentomatoes.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Chase1.jpg" width="700" /></figure>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Later, Infiniti reflects on her most precious memories from <em>One Battle After Another</em> and how it felt to live every fangirl&#8217;s dream starring in the music video for her favorite K-pop band Ateez, during which she marries every member.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Read on for a preview of the chat with Chase Infiniti for</strong><em><strong>The Testaments.</strong></em></p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Jacqueline Coley: You finished <em>One Battle After Another </em>before this, correct? What was the order of filming these two? And talk about the scripts and the audition process, because obviously <em>The Handmaid&#8217;s Tale </em>was such a huge show, so there&#8217;s a lot of pressure.</strong></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Chase Infiniti:</strong> Our first day of filming was April of 2025, and I had wrapped <em>One Battle After Another </em>maybe July of 2024, I believe. I found out I had the job around December or January, so there was a good amount of time between the two. But that whole audition process&#8230; I remember getting an email in my inbox saying &#8220;confidential project.&#8221; Well, this is interesting. But you can kind of get an idea when you start to read the script, and you&#8217;re like, &#8220;OK, they&#8217;re speaking very differently to how we&#8217;re speaking.&#8221; The names and everything were hidden, but I could kind of get a sense. I was like, &#8220;Okay, this is <em>The Handmaid&#8217;s Tale </em>sequel,<em> The Testaments,</em>&#8221; because it&#8217;s very distinct in the way that people talk and the way that they behave.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I got an email in my inbox — I want to say beginning of November or something — asking me to audition for it. I read the first script and was like, &#8220;Oh, definitely.&#8221; I loved the idea of getting to dive into a world that was so well built out and was very different from the other projects I&#8217;ve done, compared to <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/presumed_innocent">Presumed Innocent</a></em>, compared to <em>One Battle After Another</em>, especially. <em>The Testaments</em> is very different from both of those. So the idea of diving into a world that was distinctly different in that way, I found very interesting.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I think I sent in my first audition via self-tape, and then I met with Bruce Miller, the showrunner, twice after that, for two separate callbacks. I found out maybe in December or January, and then we were filming in April. I flew out maybe a month before we started filming to do a bunch of fittings, get to know the cast, and meet everybody. So it was a good amount of time between <em>OBAA</em> and this, but they came out so perfectly after each other. I&#8217;m still like, how did that happen so perfectly?</p>
