<img src="https://img.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/avuploads/2025/10/01122117/92_TW_1199.jpg" /><p>Last week, actor and activist Mark Ruffalo <a href="https://www.avclub.com/paramount-accuses-mark-ruffalo-antisemitic-rhetoric-oracle-ellison-merger">kicked up a firestorm</a> when he brought together two different topics he&#8217;s vocally passionate about on social media: His opposition to Israel&#8217;s current conduct in Gaza, and Paramount&#8217;s attempts to swallow up Warner Bros. in a $111 billion merger. Ruffalo did so via an Instagram Stories post noting that massive tech company Oracle—the source of both high-end tech to the Israeli government, and the cash Paramount&#8217;s David Ellison is using to manage the sale, courtesy of his dad, Larry—is connected to both operations, writing that, &#8220;The way you do one thing is the way you do everything.” Paramount pushed back, hard, against these statements, accusing Ruffalo of employing &#8220;antisemitic tropes&#8221; by accusing Oracle of abetting &#8220;apartheid&#8221; and &#8220;genocide&#8221; in the same post where he talked about its Hollywood-focused corporate business practices. Now, one of Ruffalo&#8217;s long-time allies has waded into <a href="https://www.avclub.com/mark-ruffalo-paramount-antisemitic-fundamentally-dishonest">the fight</a>, with fellow actor-activist Jane Fonda stepping forward to slam Paramount for its attempts to &#8220;smear&#8221; Ruffalo with accusations of antisemitism.</p><!-- RevContent --> <div id="revcontent-hidden"> <!-- revisit --><!-- admarker --> 
<p>This is per <a href="https://www.committeeforthefirstamendment.com/news-updated/statement-on-paramount-smearing-mark-ruffalo">a statement put out today by The 1A Committee</a>, a free-speech advocacy group that Fonda founded last year, and which Ruffalo is a member of. In the statement, Fonda defended Ruffalo&#8217;s &#8220;right to speak out about politics, powerful corporations and this dangerous merger,&#8221; writing, &#8220;Calling his criticism antisemitic is a smear to delegitimize criticism of the merger and to distract from the question Paramount would rather not answer: should one company be allowed to accumulate this much power over our movies, television and news?&#8221; Fonda was joined in her statement by 1A Committee Steering Committee member and Oscar-winning film producer Bruce Cohen, who wrote that, &#8220;Especially as a Jewish American, I find it deeply troubling to see false accusations of antisemitism used to silence legitimate political criticism in this country. Antisemitism is real and dangerous, which is precisely why the charge should never be used as a cudgel against people whose political views you dislike, in this case as dedicated a crusader for democracy as Mark, whom we are proud to have as a Committee member and to be in this fight with.&#8221;&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><!-- inlinecontent_1 --> </p>
<p>Fonda—who <a href="https://www.avclub.com/9-to-5-stars-lily-tomlin-and-jane-fonda-mourn-dolly-parton">recently spoke, in less politically heavy waters, in tribute of her friend and former co-star Dolly Parton</a>—and her group have been loud and early critics of the Paramount/Warner Bros. merger. Although, to be fair, Fonda was also publicly critical back when it was Netflix, not the Trump-adjacent Ellisons, trying to buy the studio. In <a href="https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/business/business-news/jane-fonda-condemns-netflix-warner-bros-deal-1236443899/">a previous statement</a>, the group called that earlier purchase attempt &#8220;an alarming escalation of the consolidation that threatens the entire entertainment industry, the democratic public it serves, and the First Amendment.”</p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p><!-- inlinecontent_2 --> </p>
