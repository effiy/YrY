<p>Unlike the Google Pixel Watch 4, which includes emergency-only satellite messaging, the Fenix 8 Pro takes things further with two-way satellite communications, allowing you to check in with friends and family off the grid. Its inReach technology provides two-way satellite and LTE connectivity, though it requires an active subscription. Plans cost $8 to $49.99 per month and include a free 30-day trial.</p><p>Another major benefit is the ability to trigger an interactive SOS message for 24/7 assistance from Garmin&rsquo;s global response center. The 1.4-inch AMOLED display is bright and durable, with a scratch-resistant sapphire lens, and LTE lets you access key communication features without relying on your phone. Battery life lasts up to 27 days in smartwatch mode.</p><p>The aesthetic is familiar, with Garmin&rsquo;s signature rugged look, a sapphire crystal display, titanium bezel, and easy-to-navigate action buttons, even if you&rsquo;re wearing gloves or have sweaty hands. It has a built-in speaker and mic and is water-resistant up to 100 meters, making it suitable for dives, while its built-in flashlight is a useful extra for low-light adventures. The watch has the usual sensors, including a pulse oximeter and barometer, plus ECG support and advanced insights such as endurance and hill score. It also suggests daily workouts for runners.</p><p>If you&rsquo;ll get solid use out of the upgraded cellular and satellite connectivity (and don&rsquo;t mind investing in a subscription to activate those features), the <a href="https://zdcs.link/zEK8PM?pageview_type=RSS&amp;template=content&amp;module=content_body&amp;element=offer&amp;item=text-link&amp;element_label=Garmin%20Fenix%208%20Pro&amp;object_type=content&amp;short_url=zEK8PM&amp;u=https%3A%2F%2Flifehacker.com%2Ffeed%2Frss" rel="sponsored" target="_blank" title="open in a new window"><u>Garmin Fenix 8 Pro</u></a> is a smart investment for outdoorsy people and athletes. If you don&rsquo;t need the connectivity, the <a href="https://lifehacker.com/tech/garmin-fenix-8-smartwatch-sale-august-2026" target="_blank">standard Garmin Fenix 8</a> may suffice, and it is also seeing price drops following the release of the Fenix 9.</p><div class=" relative flex justify-center py-16 md:left-1/2 md:w-[780px] md:max-w-max md:-translate-x-1/2">
