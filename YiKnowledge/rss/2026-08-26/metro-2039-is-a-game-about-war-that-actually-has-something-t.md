<img src="https://assetsio.gnwcdn.com/metro-2039-header.png?width=690&amp;quality=85&amp;format=jpg&amp;auto=webp" /> <p>Earlier this year, publisher Deep Silver and developer 4A Studios published a blog post outlining its vision for Metro 2039. The studio revealed the game - being developed primarily in Ukraine amid the continued Russian invasion - is darker <a href="https://www.eurogamer.net/metro-2039-much-darker-out-this-winter">and focuses on the "cost of silence, the horrors of tyranny, and the price of freedom"</a>. How could it not be? The developers turn up to work every day not knowing if the power will go out, if they will have to take cover from a drone strike, if they will be able to see their loved ones that evening, or eat hot food when they get home. This is a game being developed in the middle of a warzone, and you better believe the final product is going to reflect that.</p> <p><a href="https://www.eurogamer.net/metro-2039-preview-interview-war-ukraine-bloch-ulmer">Read more</a></p>
