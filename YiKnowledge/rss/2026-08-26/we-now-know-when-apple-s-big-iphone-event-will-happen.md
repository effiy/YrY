<h2 id="what-will-apple-announce-at-its-iphone-event">What will Apple announce at its iPhone event?</h2><p>We don't know for <em>sure</em> what Apple will reveal on Sept. 9, but leaks and rumors give us some good guesses. The math tells us that Apple will go from the iPhone 17 to the iPhone 18 this year, while <a href="https://mashable.com/tech/apple-iphone-18-latest-rumors-release-date-leaks-august-2026" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="open in a new window">the leaks suggest</a> that we'll see the iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max, and the foldable "iPhone Ultra."(Apple may delay the standard iPhone 18 to 2027, per rumors.) </p><p>The iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max will likely be standard upgrades from the iPhone 17 Pro series: Expect better cameras, bigger batteries, new colors (including the new "light blue" and "dark cherry"), the new A20 chip, and higher price tags. The star of the show will be <a href="https://mashable.com/tech/foldable-apple-iphone-ultra-specifications-rumors-leaks" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="open in a new window">Apple's first foldable iPhone</a>, which&nbsp;will come with many of the same internals as the iPhone 18 Pros, but may ship with Touch ID instead of Face ID, and, of course, with two display types: a smaller outer display, which opens up to reveal a larger inner display. This might be Apple's most expensive iPhone yet, fetching over $2,000, but we'll have to wait and see once Apple officially announces its price tag.   </p>
