<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-why-you-ll-love-these-honey-bbq-chicken-bites">Why you&#8217;ll love these honey BBQ chicken bites</h2>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>The irresistible sticky glaze.</strong> The combination of the sweet, smoky, tangy, honey BBQ glaze is just a guaranteed hit for both kids and grown-ups. Indeed, it&#8217;s really all about the finger-licking sauce.</li>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-key-ingredients-for-the-chicken">Key ingredients for the chicken</h2>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-chicken">Chicken</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Use boneless, skinless, chicken breasts, tenderloins or thighs, cut into 1-inch chunks.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-bbq-seasoning">BBQ seasoning</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Choose your favorite brand of BBQ seasoning. I love&nbsp;<a href="https://amzn.to/3xieogG" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Yellowstone Cowboy BBQ seasoning</a>.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-buttermilk-and-egg">Buttermilk and egg</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">These two ingredients will ultimately create the wet coating for the chicken, helping the cornflake crumbs adhere without falling off. The buttermilk will also tenderize the chicken while adding a rich, tangy flavor.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-cornflakes">Cornflakes</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Using cornflake crumbs is an excellent way to achieve that deep-fried crunch without actually frying the chicken. They also hold up incredibly well in the oven!</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-vegetable-oil">Vegetable oil</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Use a neutral oil to avoid altering the flavor profile (for example &#8211; canola, avocado, or vegetable oil can all be used here).</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-all-purpose-flour">All-purpose flour</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The flour serves as a primer, absorbing all of the surface moisture and allowing the buttermilk-egg mixture to successfully adhere to the chicken without sliding off. Without the flour, the final crust can fall off during the baking process.</p>
</div></div>



<div class="wp-block-group is-style-qna block-ingredients"><div class="wp-block-group__inner-container is-layout-constrained wp-block-group-is-layout-constrained">
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-key-ingredients-for-the-honey-bbq-sauce">Key ingredients for the honey bbq sauce</h2>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-bbq-sauce">BBQ sauce</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Choose your favorite store-bought BBQ sauce here (<a href="https://amzn.to/3IxGXcn" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Stubb&#8217;s</a> is one of my personal favorites). If you prefer a sweeter BBQ sauce, skip the honey.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-honey">Honey</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Use a mild, neutral honey to balance the tangy, smoky BBQ sauce. </p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-apple-cider-vinegar">Apple cider vinegar</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This will add a tangy, bright kick to cut through the sweetness. White vinegar can be used as well.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-hot-sauce-optional">Hot sauce (optional)</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Tabasco, Frank&#8217;s, or Sriracha are all excellent options.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-how-to-make-honey-bbq-chicken-bites">How to make honey BBQ chicken bites</h2>



<ol class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Prep the baking sheet.</strong> Coat a&nbsp;<a href="https://amzn.to/4gyRanQ" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">cooling rack</a>&nbsp;with nonstick spray and place it inside a&nbsp;<a href="https://amzn.to/3G4qThB" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">baking sheet</a>. <strong>Pro tip: line the baking sheet with foil first for easier clean up.</strong></li>



<li><strong>Season the chicken.</strong> Cut the chicken into uniform 1-inch chunks and season with BBQ seasoning, salt and pepper.</li>



<li><strong>Crush the cornflakes.</strong> Place the cornflakes in a&nbsp;<a href="https://amzn.to/49YRcD7" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">gallon Ziploc bag</a>&nbsp;and crush (using a&nbsp;<a href="https://amzn.to/41OynAA" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">rolling pin</a> or meat mallet) until the crumbs resemble coarse breadcrumbs. Pour the crumbs into a shallow bowl, stirring in the vegetable oil until evenly coated.</li>



<li><strong>Set up a coating station.</strong> Line up 3 shallow bowls in this order &#8211; (1) flour, (2) buttermilk mixture, and (3) cornflakes mixture.</li>



<li><strong>Coat the chicken.</strong> Working one piece at a time, dredge the chicken in flour, shaking off any excess, dip into the buttermilk mixture, then press firmly into the cornflakes mixture.</li>



<li><strong>Bake.</strong> Arrange the chicken in a single layer onto the baking sheet, generously spraying and coating with cooking oil (olive oil or avocado oil). Bake until golden and crispy.</li>



<li><strong>Make the honey BBQ sauce.</strong> Combine the BBQ sauce, honey, apple cider vinegar, and hot sauce in a small <a href="https://amzn.to/3KYLfdS" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">saucepan</a> over medium heat. This can also be microwaved until warmed through, about 1 minute.</li>



<li><strong>Sauce the chicken.</strong> Combine half of the warm honey BBQ sauce with the cooked chicken, gently tossing to combine until the chicken is thoroughly coated and glossed.</li>



<li><strong>Serve.</strong> Serve warm with the remaining sauce on the side.</li>
</ol>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-pro-tip">PRO TIP</h2>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-use-a-wire-rack">Use a wire rack.</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The <a href="https://amzn.to/4gyRanQ" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">wire rack</a> is one of the best kept secrets for baking chicken. This helps elevate the chicken pieces, allowing for better air circulation, resulting in even cooking and a more consistent golden brown crispy, crunchy coating.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-how-to-serve-honey-bbq-chicken-bites">How to serve honey bbq chicken bites</h2>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Appetizer:</strong> Arrange the chicken bites on a platter with carrot and celery sticks alongside various dipping sauces such as ranch or blue cheese dip.</li>



<li><strong>Entree:</strong> Serve with classic cookout sides such as <a href="https://damndelicious.net/2022/06/10/boiled-corn-on-the-cob/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">corn</a>, <a href="https://damndelicious.net/2022/07/29/the-best-potato-salad-ever/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">potato salad</a>, coleslaw, and baked beans.</li>



<li><strong>Salad:</strong> Dice the chicken into bite-sized pieces for a <a href="https://damndelicious.net/2014/01/03/bbq-chicken-salad/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">BBQ-style chopped salad</a>.</li>



<li><strong>Sandwich:</strong> Transform this into a quick, easy sandwich, adding 3-4 chicken bites to a hot dog bun or <a href="https://damndelicious.net/2021/03/02/homemade-hamburger-buns/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">burger bun</a>.</li>
</ul>

<div class="wprm-recipe-notes-container wprm-block-text-normal" id="recipe-51387-notes"><h3 class="wprm-recipe-header wprm-recipe-notes-header wprm-block-text-bold wprm-align-left wprm-header-decoration-none">Notes</h3><div class="wprm-recipe-notes"><ul>
<li><strong>Use white or dark meat.</strong> For example, chicken breast, thighs and tenderloins will all work very well here. Always cut the chicken into uniform and equal 1-inch pieces to ensure even cooking throughout.</li>
<li><strong>Cornflake crumbs are key.</strong> Cornflake crumbs are not only easy to make but also give the chicken bites a far more crispy, crunchy and flavorful crust than traditional breadcrumbs.</li>
<li><strong>Crush the cornflakes properly.</strong> Place the cornflakes in a <a href="https://amzn.to/49YRcD7" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">gallon Ziploc bag</a> and crush (using a <a href="https://amzn.to/41OynAA" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">rolling pin</a>, if desired) the cornflakes until they resemble coarse breadcrumbs for texture &#8211; avoid large, whole flakes.</li>
<li><strong>Set up a breading station.</strong> Set up a shallow bowl of flour, a bowl of the buttermilk mixture and finally a bowl of the crushed cornflakes mixture.</li>
<li><strong>Don’t skip the cooling rack.</strong> This will elevate the chicken pieces, allowing for even cooking and maximum crispness.</li>
<li><strong>Simmer for longer.</strong> For a thicker honey BBQ sauce, simmer on low heat until reduced by half, about 10-15 minutes. For a quicker heat, microwave until warmed through, about 1 minute.</li>
<li><strong>Toss just before serving.</strong> Toss the chicken bites with the warm honey BBQ sauce right before serving to maintain maximum crispness.</li>
<li><strong>Serve the leftover sauce with the chicken.</strong> Serve the remaining sauce right alongside the chicken, giving the saucy folks a chance for additional dipping.</li>
</ul></div></div>
