<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Citing "significant increases in memory and storage component costs," in <a href="https://fortune.com/2026/08/21/exclusive-amazon-quietly-hiked-prices-echo-fire-tv-kindle-eero-significant-increases-memory-costs">a statement given to <em>Fortune</em></a>, Amazon has raised prices on Echos, Kindles, <a href="https://www.pocket-lint.com/amazon-just-quietly-raised-the-price-of-all-its-fire-sticks/">Fire TVs</a>, and Eeros by up to 60 percent. </p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The cheapest products in Amazon's lineup were hit hardest, with the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09B94RL1R?ref=amzdv_ucc_dp_lod__B09B94RL1R">Echo Dot</a> smart speaker jumping from $49.99 to $79.99.
