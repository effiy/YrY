<p class="article-paragraph skip">Android and iOS have competed against each other since 2008, and have borrowed a lot from each other over that time—so a lot of the differences that did exist between these two operating systems have now disappeared.</p>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">However, <a href="https://www.popsci.com/diy/clipboard-android-phone/">Android</a> and <a href="https://www.popsci.com/diy/ios-27-iphone-features/">iOS</a> are still unique in some ways. It&#8217;s worth knowing these differences so you can make the most of the handset you have, and perhaps make a more informed decision as to whether you want to <a href="https://www.popsci.com/diy/switching-between-android-iphone/">switch OS</a> the next time you upgrade your phone.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-put-two-apps-on-screen-together">Put two apps on screen together</h2>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">Want to see a video alongside a social media feed? Or a text message next to a map? If you&#8217;ve got an Android phone, you can get two apps together on the display in a split-screen mode, but it&#8217;s not something that you&#8217;re able to do on an iPhone.</p>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">On a Pixel phone, swipe up from the bottom of the screen to the center to see your recently used apps. Swipe left and right to find the first app you want to put in split-screen mode, then tap its icon and choose <strong>Split screen</strong>. You can then select a second app to go alongside the first one.</p>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">On a Samsung phone, the process is largely the same: Swipe up from the bottom of the display to the center, tap the icon of the first app you want to use, and select <strong>Open in split screen view</strong>. You&#8217;ll then be asked to choose a second app as well. Tap and hold on the border between the apps to change where the split is.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-set-up-multiple-user-profiles">Set up multiple user profiles</h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="A screenshot of setting up multiple users on a Pixel phone" class="wp-image-781193" height="1333" src="https://www.popsci.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/01-users.jpg?strip=all&#038;quality=85&#038;w=2000" width="2000" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Multiple users can be set up on a Pixel phone. David Nield</figcaption></figure>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">If you&#8217;ve got two Google accounts, you can set up two profiles on a Pixel phone, each with their own data, settings, and apps. From Settings, tap <strong>System &gt; Users</strong> to manage different accounts and switch between them.</p>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">The most obvious way to use this is to have one account for personal use and another for work. You can also use Guest mode if someone else needs to borrow your phone, but you don&#8217;t want to give them access to all your apps and data.</p>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">Unfortunately, this isn&#8217;t a trick you can do on an iPhone with your Apple accounts. It&#8217;s also a feature that&#8217;s now missing on Samsung Galaxy phones, though it used to be there.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-take-more-control-over-your-vpn">Take more control over your VPN</h2>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">If you&#8217;re <a href="https://www.popsci.com/diy/vpn-tips/">using a VPN</a> on your phone, you get extra layers of security and privacy when connected to the web, but there&#8217;s an interesting difference in how Android and iOS handle these apps.</p>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">On Android, VPNs can make use of a feature called split-tunneling. This essentially means some apps use the VPN and some don&#8217;t (maybe the apps where raw speed is more important than security). It&#8217;s a useful option to have, and virtually every VPN app on the market offers it.</p>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">This isn&#8217;t an option on iOS, though—Apple only lets VPNs run or not run, and there&#8217;s no way to configure protection on an app-by-app basis.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-browse-through-your-old-notifications">Browse through your old notifications</h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="A screenshot of setting up your notification history on Android so you can look at past alerts" class="wp-image-781194" height="1333" src="https://www.popsci.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/02-history.jpg?strip=all&#038;quality=85&#038;w=2000" width="2000" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Notification history lets you look back at past alerts on your phone. David Nield</figcaption></figure>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">Ever dismissed a notification that&#8217;s popped up on screen too hastily, and then wished you could bring it back to see what it said? Android phones let you do this, but iPhones don’t—sorry, Apple users.</p>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">On your Android phone, open Settings and tap <strong>Notifications</strong>, then choose <strong>Notification history</strong> (Pixel phones) or <strong>Advanced settings &gt; Notification history</strong> (Galaxy phones). The next screen lets you set up the feature and access the recent alerts you&#8217;ve missed.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-use-your-phone-through-a-desktop-interface">Use your phone through a desktop interface</h2>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">For years, Samsung <a href="https://www.popsci.com/story/diy/samsung-dex-guide/">has included a feature</a> called DeX on its Galaxy phones, which lets you connect a monitor, keyboard, and mouse to use Android through a desktop-style interface, complete with a taskbar and floating windows.</p>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">It&#8217;s helpful for getting work done at home or the office, and as phones and apps have become more powerful, DeX has become more useful. It&#8217;s also something available on recent Pixel phones, though Google&#8217;s version isn&#8217;t quite as sophisticated as Samsung&#8217;s at the moment.</p>



<p class="article-paragraph skip">All you need to set this up is to plug your phone into a compatible monitor and go from there. However, this isn&#8217;t something Apple offers through iOS.</p>
