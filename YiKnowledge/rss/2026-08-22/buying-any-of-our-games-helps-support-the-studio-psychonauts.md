Earlier this week, <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/south-of-midnight">South of Midnight</a> developer Compulsion Games shared word that they <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/were-going-to-take-things-slow-south-of-midnight-devs-now-own-the-rights-to-all-their-games-again-after-quitting-xbox-and-are-working-on-a-new-game">now own all of the publishing rights to their games</a>, a welcome update in light of the <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/microsoft-confirm-theyve-supplied-ai-tech-to-the-israeli-military-for-use-in-gaza-following-bds-xbox-boycott">ongoing BDS boycott against Microsoft and Xbox</a>. And now, another studio is joining the realm of boycott freedom, this time <a href="https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/psychonauts">Psychonauts</a> studio Double Fine, as CEO Tim Schafer has confirmed that they too now own the publishing rights to their own catalogue.
