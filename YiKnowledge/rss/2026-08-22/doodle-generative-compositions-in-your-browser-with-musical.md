<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I remember having a <a href="https://www.theverge.com/2014/5/26/5752028/turntable-with-robotic-arms-draws-beautiful-spirographs">Spirograph</a> as a kid and being obsessed with it. Its geometric patterns are hypnotic and gorgeous. I also love <a href="https://www.theverge.com/report/879819/laurie-spiegel-is-celebrating-40-of-music-mouse-with-a-modern-revival">generative music</a> composition. So bringing those two things together in a browser tab, I'm hooked. <a href="https://musical.toys/toys/spirograph/">Musical Spirograph</a> is a relatively simple concept. A set of points dash around the screen according to mathematical equations, generating hypotrochoids and epitrochoids. Those points pass through a series of concentric rings which trigger specific notes.</p>
