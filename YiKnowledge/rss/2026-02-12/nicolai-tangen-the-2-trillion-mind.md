<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Tiny Lessons</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">1. The faster you reply, the less you need to say. If you reply in a minute, you can say two words. If you wait a day, it&#8217;s a paragraph.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">2. Overanalyzing doesn&#8217;t improve the outcome. It just makes you more confident about the outcome.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">3. &#8220;If you have really high ambitions, you achieve great things even if you fail. If you have low ambitions, you achieve nothing even if you succeed.&#8221;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">4. &#8220;You call it gut feel, nobody believes in it. You call it pattern recognition, a lot of people believe in it.&#8221;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">5. Repetition is persuasive.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">6. &#8220;Why make the same mistakes when there are so many to choose from? Find some new ones.&#8221;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">7. Take the same amount of risk after a win and a loss.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">8. &#8220;Things shouldn&#8217;t take longer than they need to take.&#8221;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">9. Doing nothing is often the best option.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">10. Speed and agility are the only hedge in a world you can&#8217;t predict.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">11. Be impenetrable against social pressure and instant in responding to evidence.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">12. The people who feel weird, different, and misunderstood are the ones who change the world.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">13. Changing a culture is a ten-year project. If you think you&#8217;re halfway done in two, you haven&#8217;t started.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">14. Asking for advice makes people think you’re smart because you are clever enough to recognize how clever they are.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">15. &#8220;If you’re not curious, you’re not going to listen.&#8221;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">16. &#8220;I would inject AI everywhere. I would just go all in.&#8221;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">17. &#8220;It’s difficult to find people who are really contrarian now because you need to live in a space where people don’t agree with you.&#8221;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">18. &#8220;You don’t have to be disliked even though people disagree with you.&#8221;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">19. You can&#8217;t please everyone. Even if you are right, 10% of people will disagree with you.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">20. The source of most of our poor decisions is blind spots.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">21. &#8220;I don’t think you should profit from people who have a tough time.&#8221;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">22. &#8220;Wealth is basically created by owning one or two really good assets, and then you just hold onto it for the very long term.&#8221;</p>
