<p>WHO and Switzerland have announced the renewal of their cooperation strategy for the period 2024-2028. In 2013, Switzerland became the first high-income country in the WHO European Region to pilot a cooperation strategy with WHO (hereafter referred to as the WHO-Switzerland Cooperation Strategy). In 2022, WHO and Switzerland jointly decided to update and renew this cooperation strategy, which was done on 4 March 2025 at the Regional Office of the WHO in Copenhagen, Denmark.</p><p>Visit the <a href="https://www.who.int/europe/news/item/04-03-2025-who-and-switzerland-cement-cooperation-until-2028">WHO/Europe website</a>&nbsp;to read the news release and learn more about the <a href="https://www.who.int/europe/publications/i/item/WHO-EURO-2025-11274-51046-77639"></a><a href="https://www.who.int/europe/publications/i/item/WHO-EURO-2025-11274-51046-77639">WHO&mdash;Switzerland country cooperation strategy 2024&mdash;2028</a>.</p><p>Read more about the <a href="https://www.who.int/news/item/13-06-2024-who-and-switzerland-strengthen-partnership-for-global-biohub-system">WHO-Switzerland partnership for the global BioHub system</a>.</p><p style="text-align: center;"><img alt="Regional Director of EURO, Dr Hans Kluge holds WHO's Country Cooperation Strategy document with Swiss Ambassador for Global Health, Barbara Schedler" height="1280" src="https://cdn.who.int/media/images/default-source/funding/who-euro-and-switzerland-cement-cooperation-until-2028.jpg?sfvrsn=24f9f687_3" style="height: auto;" title="WHO EURO and Switzerland cement cooperation until 2028" width="1920" /><em><sup>Barbara Schedler, Ambassador for Global Health at the Swiss Federal Office of Public Health, and Dr Henri P.
