<p><img alt="Constellating Wonder: Celebrating 20 Years of The Marginalian (October 23, 2026)" class="cover alignright size-medium" height="270" src="https://i0.wp.com/www.themarginalian.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/monogram_round.png?fit=270%2C270&amp;ssl=1" width="270" /></p><p>At the dawn of my twenties, lonely, destitute, and disoriented by the demands of making a new life in an alien world, I started keeping notes on my search for meaning while working four jobs to pay my way through college. On October 23, 2006, I began sharing my discoveries &#8212; passages from books, links to neuroscience studies, reproductions of 16th-century Japanese woodcuts &#8212; in an office email to seven colleagues. They in turn began sharing it with friends, who began sharing it with friends. The fractal spread and, within a few years, the Library of Congress had included it in its permanent digital archive of culturally valuable materials. It changed shape (the essays grew longer, the questions deeper) and name (<a href="https://www.themarginalian.org/2021/10/22/brain-pickings-becoming-the-marginalian/"><em>Brain Pickings</em> became <em>The Marginalian</em></a>) but continued beating with the same heart, reckoning with the ongoing bewilderment of being alive &#8212; sometimes through science and philosophy, sometimes through poetry and children&#8217;s books, always through the lens of wonder, always offered freely: no advertising, no AI, not even an assistant, a thoroughly one-woman labor of love sustained by the love of strangers. </p>
<p>To <a href="https://spac.org/events/constellating-wonder/" target="_blank">celebrate</a> twenty years of it, I am inviting friends from various walks of life &#8212; poets and physicists, musicians and actors, artists and historians of science &#8212; to read fragments of the writings, poetry and prose, that have most touched me over the years as I introduce each with a short reflection on its resonance to the eternal questions we live with: what do we make of our suffering, why did the world have to be beautiful, how do we see more clearly and love more purely. </p>
<p><a href="https://spac.org/events/constellating-wonder/" target="_blank"><img alt="" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-87687" height="680" src="https://i0.wp.com/www.themarginalian.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/marginalian20_1325x1325_SPAC.jpg?resize=680%2C680&#038;ssl=1" width="680" /></a></p>
<p>With the format of <a href="https://www.themarginalian.org/the-universe-in-verse/"><em>The Universe in Verse</em></a> and the feeling-tone of my <em>Marginalian</em> essays, the evening will feature live appearances and special contributions across spacetime by a constellation of beloved humans who have made my own life more livable over the years, among them Krista Tippett, Marie Howe, Diane Ackerman, James Gleick, Natascha McElhone, Debbie Millman, Sophie Blackall, Uma Thurman, Stephon Alexander, Natalie Batalha, Willow Defebaugh, Paola Prestini, Wendy MacNaughton, Zoë Keating, Joan As Police Woman, Nick Cave, and surprise guests. <a href="https://spac.org/events/constellating-wonder/" target="_blank">Come join us</a> at Saratoga Performing Arts Center &#8212; a state-of-the-art outdoor amphitheater in Upstate New York, surrounded by waterfalls and hot springs, awned by old-growth trees in full autumnal glory. </p>
<p><a href="https://spac.org/events/constellating-wonder/" target="_blank"><img alt="" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-87711" height="425" src="https://i0.wp.com/www.themarginalian.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/spac2.jpg?resize=680%2C425&#038;ssl=1" width="680" /></a></p>
<p><a href="https://spac.org/events/constellating-wonder/" target="_blank"><img alt="" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-87710" height="453" src="https://i0.wp.com/www.themarginalian.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/spac1.jpg?resize=680%2C453&#038;ssl=1" width="680" /></a></p>
<p>Accompanying the show will be a curated bookstore, featuring my twenty favorite books of all time and signed copies of my own, as well as a pop-up Museum of Delights (housed in an airstream from the 1950s), where some of my <a href="https://www.themarginalian.org/2025/07/25/urns/">ceramics</a> and <a href="https://www.themarginalian.org/2026/07/27/serpentine-birdworlds">serpentine creations</a> will be given away by the impartial hand of chance in a raffle.</p>
<p><a href="https://spac.org/events/constellating-wonder/" target="_blank"><img alt="" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-87690" height="680" src="https://i0.wp.com/www.themarginalian.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/museumofdelights.jpg?resize=680%2C680&#038;ssl=1" width="680" /></a></p>
<p>To honor the spirit by which <em>The Marginalian</em> has endured over the years, <a href="https://spac.org/events/constellating-wonder/" target="_blank">tickets</a> are offered on a pay-what-you-please basis, ranging from basic operating costs to the extremes of appreciation. To those who need them, free tickets are available <a href="mailto:boxoffice@spac.org">by request</a>.</p>

It has remained free and ad-free and alive thanks to patronage from readers. I have no staff, no interns, no assistant — a thoroughly one-woman labor of love that is also my life and my livelihood. If this labor makes your own life more livable in any way, please consider lending a helping hand with a <a href="https://www.themarginalian.org/donate/">donation</a>. Your support makes all the difference.</p>
