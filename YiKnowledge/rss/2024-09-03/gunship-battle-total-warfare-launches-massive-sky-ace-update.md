<p>Summer holidays might be over, but fans of <em>Gunship Battle: Total Warfare</em> are still in for a treat as Joycity released one of the game’s most impressive updates that introduces Sky Ace alongside numerous QOL enhancements and a special event to keep things as exciting as possible this fall. </p>
<p><img alt="" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-328626" height="1037" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Sky-Ace-Stage-Selection-Page.jpg" width="1920" /></p>
<p>Sky Ace is a classic 2D puzzle shooter that pays homage to the legendary shooters of console gaming with an unquestionably high level of polish and prestige. It also has its own storyline. With the help of a famous cast of fighter jets, players will be able to engage in aerial battle against enemy troops in Sky Ace and retrieve lost allies and locations. Players control their fighter jets left and right against a barrage of guided missiles and fast-moving enemy incursions as they shoot and strafe their way through simple calculation-based tasks. This vibrant, engaging feature combines the thrill of solving puzzles with traditional shooter action.</p>
<p>But Sky Ace is not the only thing to look forward to and we are sure that players will enjoy the enhanced unit, the inventory navigations, rings that provide fast access to more easily control essential features at log in. Additionally, a new statistics tool has been implemented to let players confirm, use, and increase things more quickly. Together with input from both new and experienced players, these improvements significantly improve player convenience on all fronts.</p>
<p><img alt="" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-328628" height="480" src="//cdn.toucharcade.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Sky-Ace-Key-Art-2-1010x505.jpg" width="960" /></p>
<p>And to mark this special occasion, Joycity also decided to treat the GBTW with a special event that awards all players with an exclusive Jet, the &#8216;F-35 Sky Pro’ when they successfully complete specific Sky Ace stages.</p>
<p>You can find out more about this major update on the official website or on the game’s <a href="https://www.facebook.com/gbtw.eng/">Facebook</a> page for the latest developments.</p>
<p><em>Gunship Battle: Total Warfare</em> is available for free on the <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.joycity.gw&#038;hl=en&#038;gl=US">Google Play Store</a> or <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gunship-battle-total-warfare/id1397714781">App Store</a>. </p>
