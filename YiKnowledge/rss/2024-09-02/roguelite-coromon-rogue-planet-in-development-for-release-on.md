<p><a class="app-icon" href="/games/coromon" style="float: left; margin-top: 4px; margin-right: 10px;"><img alt="" src="https://toucharcade.com/wp-content/images/app/6451449431/100x100bb.jpg" style="border-radius: 20px;" /></a> Following developer TRAGsoft bringing monster collecting game <em>Coromon</em> to mobile after it debuted on PC and Switch, we are getting a roguelite spin-off in the form of <em>Coromon: Rogue Planet</em> <a href="http://toucharcade.com/link/http://itunes.apple.com/us/app/id6451449431?mt=8">(Free)</a> due next year for not only Steam and Switch, but also iOS and Android. <em>Coromon: Rogue Planet</em> aims to blend in the turn-based combat of the main game with roguelite gameplay to deliver an endlessly replayable monster collecting gameplay loop. The Steam page mentions &#8220;10 ever changing biomes", 7 different playable characters, over 130 monsters, and more.
