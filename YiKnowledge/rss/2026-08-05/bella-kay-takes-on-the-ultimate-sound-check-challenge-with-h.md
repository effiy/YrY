<div class="upx-component upx-embed-cnx"><div class="cnx-embed-holder">&amp;&amp;&amp;&amp;</div></div><p>Bella Kay is having <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/DXo41ScDYQM/" target="_blank">a massive 2026</a>. The singer-songwriter released her debut album <em>My Reckless Abandon</em> on Atlantic Records in July; &#8220;Iloveitiloveitiloveit&#8221; has spent over 20 weeks on the Hot 100; and she just played <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/Dbd-13RFVPc/" target="_blank">her biggest show ever</a> at <a href="https://uproxx.com/life/lollapalooza-2024-essentials-guide-from-perry-ferrell/" target="_blank">Lollapalooza</a>. </p>
<p>But Kay found time in her busy schedule to be the latest guest on <a href="https://uproxx.com/tag/sound-check-with-jeremy-hecht/" target="_blank"><em>Sound Check</em></a>, the series hosted by <em>Uproxx</em>&#8216;s <a href="https://uproxx.com/tag/Jeremy-Hecht/" target="_blank">Jeremy Hecht</a> where music makes tough decisions. And they don&#8217;t get much tougher than Tate McRae vs. Sabrina Carpenter.</p>
<p>Here&#8217;s how it works: Kay picks between a pair of different-sounding songs with the ultimate goal of Hecht guessing her lifetime anthem. This episode&#8217;s difficult choices include Gigi Perez vs. Rihanna, Olivia Rodrigo vs. Lizzy McAlpine, Beyoncé vs. Megan Thee Stallion. Hecht even had her choose between two of her own songs: &#8220;The Sick&#8221; or &#8220;21st Street.&#8221; Along the way, Kay also opened up about her favorite artists, listening to music in the morning, complicated love songs, her songwriting process, her new era, and more. The quick-fire format led to some interesting and surprising revelations. </p>
<p>Knowing her answers, will Jeremy be able to guess Bella Kay&#8217;s lifetime anthem? You&#8217;ll have to watch her take the <em>Sound Check</em> challenge in the video above to find out.</p>
