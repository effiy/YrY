<p>Oh hello there. Yes, we ARE still alive, thank you for asking. We took an unintended month-long hiatus due to me starting my new job as well as the holidays being as hectic as they always are, but we&#8217;re back and with any luck we should be able to settle into a mostly regular recording schedule from here on out. I know I&#8217;ve said that before, but here&#8217;s to hoping! At any rate, this week&#8217;s show is mostly just me and Eli catching up after not really talking for an entire month, but we do keep things slightly on topic by talking about some Apple Intelligence stuff and the latest games that I&#8217;ve been playing on Steam Deck lately. It&#8217;s good to be back, enjoy the show!</p>
<p>Don&#8217;t forget to shoot us emails with any questions, feedback, or anything else relevant or irrelevant to <a href="mailto:podcast@toucharcade.com">podcast@toucharcade.com</a>.
