# network / People

> **As a** knowledge curator, **I want to** track team members, experts, and stakeholders, **so that** I can route knowledge requests to the right people.

Archives of people relevant to the work: team members, stakeholders, industry experts. **Especially for high-value tacit knowledge gaps**. 

## Subdirectories

| Leaf | Archived content | File count |
|---|---|---|
| [team/](./team/) | Team member archive and collaboration conventions | 2 (team-overview, roster)  |
| [stakeholders/](./stakeholders/) | Business stakeholders (partners, approver roles)  | 2 (stakeholder-map, communication-cadence)  |
| [experts/](./experts/) | Industry experts and tech authorities | 1 (external-experts-roster)  |

## Privacy and compliance

- Only archive public information (public talks, articles, blogs, official bios) 
- Do not archive private contact methods (phone, personal email, address) 
- Changes to team member content require the person's own confirmation
- Expert archives must mark "last verified date" (`last_verified`) 

## Tacit knowledge capture

Team topology (T006), expert network (T007), customer insight (T008) are all high-value tacit knowledge, to be extracted through workshops, see [lifecycle/tacit-knowledge-backlog.md](../governance/tacit-knowledge-backlog.md).
