## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: baseline + decomposition + action + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by GRR improvement + revenue + renewal + retention + NRR; not sloganeering
3. **Baseline**: historical / industry / cohort / segment / ACV; do not omit
4. **Decomposition**: churn / contraction / cohort / segment / ACV; do not omit
5. **Action**: playbook / training / product / pricing / health score; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: GRR improvement + revenue + renewal + retention + NRR; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from baseline -> decomposition -> action -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Interplay with nrr**: GRR + NRR co-built
13. **Interplay with arr**: GRR + ARR co-built
14. **Interplay with retention**: GRR + retention co-built
15. **Interplay with churn-prediction**: GRR + churn prediction co-built
16. **Interplay with customer-health**: GRR + health score co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Snowflake / ChartMogul / Baremetrics / Gainsight / Catalyst / Salesforce
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why GRR is required; the worst consequence of not having it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by renewal rate; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: the simpler the GRR the better; cut redundant dimensions
