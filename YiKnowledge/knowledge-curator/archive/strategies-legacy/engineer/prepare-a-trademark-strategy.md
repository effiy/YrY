## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: design + search + register + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Design**: target / class / distinctiveness / no conflict; do not omit
4. **Search**: domestic / international / similarity / risk; do not omit
5. **Register**: application / response / publication / renewal; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from design → search → register → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: registration is only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with ip**: trademark + intellectual property co-built
13. **Link with patent**: trademark + patent co-built
14. **Link with copyright**: trademark + copyright co-built
15. **Link with brand-identity**: trademark + brand identity co-built
16. **Link with legal**: trademark + legal co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Anaqua / CPA Global / Clarivate / MarkMonitor / Corsearch
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must trademark strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: the simpler the trademark the better; cut redundant layers
