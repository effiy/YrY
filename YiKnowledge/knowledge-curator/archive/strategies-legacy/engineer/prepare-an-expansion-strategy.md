## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: opportunity + content + channel + cadence + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by NRR + expansion-ARR + LTV + retention + CAC; not sloganeering
3. **Opportunity**: upsell / cross-sell / land-expand / multi-product / multi-year; do not omit
4. **Content**: case / ROI / demo / proposal / business value; do not omit
5. **Channel**: CS / AM / self-service / channel / executive; do not omit
6. **Cadence**: daily / monthly / quarterly / milestone / renewal; do not omit
7. **Measure**: NRR + expansion-ARR + LTV + retention + CAC; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from opportunity → content → channel → cadence → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with customer-lifecycle**: expansion + customer life cadence co-built
13. **Link with customer-success**: expansion + CS co-built
14. **Link with account-management**: expansion + account manager co-built
15. **Link with revenue-operations**: expansion + revenue operations co-built
16. **Link with self-serve-growth**: expansion + self-serve growth co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Gainsight / Catalyst / Churn-Zero / Planhat / Salesforce-CPQ / Clari / Boost-UP
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must expand; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by relying on new customers; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: expansion the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
