## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: structure + incentives + compliance + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **structure Structure**: group / cross-border / shareholding / entity; do not omit
4. **incentives Incentive**: exemptions / deductions / preferences / credits; do not omit
5. **compliance Compliance**: filing / trace / review / disputes; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from structure -> incentives -> compliance -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: tax avoidance is only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **linked with tax**: planning + tax co-built
13. **linked with tax-management**: planning + tax management co-built
14. **linked with transfer-pricing**: planning + transfer pricing co-built
15. **linked with corporate-finance**: planning + corporate finance co-built
16. **linked with regulatory-reporting**: planning + regulatory reporting co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Thomson Reuters ONESOURCE / Vertex / Sovos / Longview / Bloomberg Tax
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why tax planning strategy is required; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: tax planning the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
