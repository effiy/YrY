## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: positioning + trade-offs + fit + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Positioning**: customer / value / price / channel; do not omit
4. **Trade-off**: do / do not do / prioritize / exit; do not omit
5. **Fit**: activities / alignment / reinforce / sustain; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from positioning → trade-offs → fit → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: slogans only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Links with corporate-strategy**: business + corporate strategy co-build
13. **Links with business-architecture**: business + business architecture co-build
14. **Links with business-capability**: business + business capability co-build
15. **Links with value-chain**: business + value chain co-build
16. **Links with strategic-planning**: business + strategic planning co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Porter / Blue Ocean / McKinsey 7S / BCG Growth-Share / Strategyzer
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must business strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: business strategy the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
