## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: facts + analysis + action + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **facts Fact**: timeline / data / closed loop; do not omit
4. **analysis Analysis**: root cause / 5 whys / closed loop; do not omit
5. **action Action**: owner / follow-up / closed loop; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from facts → analysis → action → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with scrum**: Retrospective + Scrum co-build
13. **link with kanban**: Retrospective + Kanban co-build
14. **link with extreme-programming**: Retrospective + ExtremeProgramming co-build
15. **link with postmortem**: Retrospective + Postmortem co-build
16. **link with pair-programming**: Retrospective + PairProgramming co-build
17. **Toolchain**: FunRetro / Retromat / Miro / EasyRetro / Conceptboard
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must Retrospective; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can self-reflection solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: Retrospective the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
