## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identify + analyze + engage + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by adoption + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Identify**: internal / external / decision / impact / regulator; do not omit
4. **Analyze**: power / interest / stance / impact / priority; do not omit
5. **Engage**: communication / enablement / negotiation / escalation / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: adoption + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from identify → analyze → engage → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Linked with stakeholder-engagement**: SM + Engagement co-build
13. **Linked with change-management**: SM + CM co-build
14. **Linked with change-communications**: SM + Comms co-build
15. **Linked with communication-cadence**: SM + Cadence co-build
16. **Linked with leadership**: SM + Leadership co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Smartsheet / Monday / Asana / Trello / Miro
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why SM is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can intuition solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (adoption / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler SM is, the better; cut redundant layers
