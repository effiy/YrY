## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: standards + process + data + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Standards**: cabin / lodging / meals / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Process**: approval / booking / reimbursement / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Data**: travel / expense / safety / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from standards -> process -> data -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with procurement**: TravelPolicy + Procurement co-built
13. **Link with contract-management**: TravelPolicy + ContractManagement co-built
14. **Link with vendor-operations**: TravelPolicy + VendorOperations co-built
15. **Link with supplier-relationship**: TravelPolicy + SupplierRelationship co-built
16. **Link with expense-management**: TravelPolicy + ExpenseManagement co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Concur / TripActions / Navan / Amex GBT / SAP Fioneer
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must TravelPolicy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can personal booking solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: TravelPolicy the simpler the better; cut redundant clauses
