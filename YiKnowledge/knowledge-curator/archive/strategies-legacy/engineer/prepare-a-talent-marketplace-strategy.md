## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: post + match + apply + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Post**: opportunity / project / gig / role / duration; do not omit
4. **Match**: skill / interest / experience / capacity / referral; do not omit
5. **Apply**: intent / approval / transfer / guarantee; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from post → match → apply → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: project count is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with internal-mobility**: talent marketplace + internal mobility co-build
13. **Link with talent-acquisition**: talent marketplace + talent acquisition co-build
14. **Link with talent-pipeline**: talent marketplace + talent pipeline co-build
15. **Link with career-pathing**: talent marketplace + career pathing co-build
16. **Link with workforce-planning**: talent marketplace + workforce planning co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Gloat / Workday Talent Marketplace / Eightfold AI / Fuel50 / SAP SuccessFactors Talent Intelligence Hub
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must talent marketplace strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: talent marketplace the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
