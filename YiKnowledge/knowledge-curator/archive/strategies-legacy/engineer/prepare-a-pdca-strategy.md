## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: plan + do + check + act + governance; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Plan Plan**: goals / assumptions / resources / cadence / traceability; do not omit
4. **Do Do**: implementation / data / exceptions / traceability / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Check Check**: comparison / deviation / root cause / retrospective / closed loop; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Act Act**: standardize / copy / revoke / evolve / closed loop; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from plan → do → check → act → governance; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link to continuous-improvement**: PDCA + CI co-build
13. **Link to kaizen**: PDCA + Kaizen co-build
14. **Link to lean**: PDCA + Lean co-build
15. **Link to six-sigma**: PDCA + SixSigma co-build
16. **Link to quality-management**: PDCA + QM co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Minitab / EngineRoom / KaiNexus / Power BI / Notion
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must we have PDCA; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by relying on intuition; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: PDCA the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
