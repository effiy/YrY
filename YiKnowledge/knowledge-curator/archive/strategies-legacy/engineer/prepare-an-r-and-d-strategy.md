## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: direction + portfolio + execution + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **direction Direction**: forward-looking / trend / strategy / tech; do not omit
4. **portfolio Portfolio**: basic / applied / development / experiment; do not omit
5. **execution Execute**: project initiation / review / stage / exit; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from direction → portfolio → execution → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: tech only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with innovation**: R&D + innovation co-build
13. **link with innovation-portfolio**: R&D + portfolio co-build
14. **link with product**: R&D + product co-build
15. **link with product-innovation**: R&D + product innovation co-build
16. **link with research-and-development**: R&D + R&D co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Itonics / Hype Innovation / Brightidea / Planbox / Spira
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must R&D strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: R&D the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
