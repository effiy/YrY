## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: role + cadence + switch + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **role Role**: driver / navigator / mob / closed loop; do not omit
4. **cadence Rhythm**: pomodoro / switch / closed loop; do not omit
5. **switch Switch**: keyboard / train of thought / closed loop; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progress from role → cadence → switch → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with pair-programming**: MobProgramming + PairProgramming co-build
13. **Link with extreme-programming**: MobProgramming + ExtremeProgramming co-build
14. **Link with retrospective**: MobProgramming + Retrospective co-build
15. **Link with coding-style-guide**: MobProgramming + StyleGuide co-build
16. **Link with code-review**: MobProgramming + CodeReview co-build
17. **Toolchain**: VS Live Share / Tuple / Zoom / Miro / JetBrains CodeWithMe
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must MobProgramming; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by single-person solo coding; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: MobProgramming the simpler the better; cut redundant rules
