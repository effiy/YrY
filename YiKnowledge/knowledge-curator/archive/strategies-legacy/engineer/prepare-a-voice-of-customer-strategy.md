## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Source + collection + analysis + action + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: Prioritize by retention + NPS + conversion + repeat purchase + revenue share; not sloganeering
3. **Source**: Direct + indirect + inferred; do not omit
4. **Collect**: Qualitative + quantitative + timing + channel + frequency; do not omit
5. **Analyze**: Categorization + themes + sentiment + quantification + tracking; do not omit
6. **Act**: Product + process + customer service + training + closed loop; do not omit
7. **Measure**: Action rate + closure rate + NPS + retention + revenue; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: From source → collection → analysis → action → measurement progressively; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: Reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: Every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: Strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Links with customer-feedback**: VoC + feedback co-built
13. **Links with customer-insights**: VoC + insights co-built
14. **Links with customer-journey**: VoC + journey co-built
15. **Links with product-strategy**: VoC + product strategy co-built
16. **Links with product-roadmap**: VoC + roadmap co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Qualtrics / Medallia / InMoment / Confirmit / Clarabridge / Chattermill / Kapost / Productboard / Dovetail
18. **Publicly queryable**: Strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: Evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: Why must VoC strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: How much can ad-hoc surveys solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: Second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / retention / business)
23. **Occam**: VoC the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
