## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: supply + demand + transaction + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by transaction + speed + trust + cost + risk; not sloganeering
3. **supply Supply**: internal / external / third-party / partner / public; do not omit
4. **demand Demand**: business / data / analytics / AI / partner; do not omit
5. **transaction Transaction**: paid / free / trial / freemium / subscription; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: transaction + speed + trust + cost + risk; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from supply -> demand -> transaction -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with data-product**: Marketplace + DataProduct co-build
13. **link with data-governance**: Marketplace + Governance co-build
14. **link with data-catalog**: Marketplace + Catalog co-build
15. **link with data-monetization**: Marketplace + Monetization co-build
16. **link with data-strategy**: Marketplace + Data co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Snowflake / Databricks / AWS Data Exchange / Google / Narrative
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must Marketplace; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can relying on a directory solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (transaction / trust / cost / risk)
23. **Occam**: Marketplace the simpler the better; cut redundant modes
