## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: recruitment + agenda + feedback + governance + measurement; no missing dimension.
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering.
3. **Recruit**: profile / invitation / tenure / incentives; do not omit.
4. **Agenda**: topics / cadence / executives / resolutions; do not omit.
5. **Feedback**: closed loop / follow-up / landing / retrospective; do not omit.
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit.
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit.
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from recruitment → agenda → feedback → governance → measurement; no skipping.
9. **Not report-ized**: meetings are only the start; not the end.
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague.
11. **Versioned**: the strategy has versions; evolution is traceable.
12. **Link with customer-council**: advisory + council co-build.
13. **Link with customer-advocacy**: advisory + advocacy co-build.
14. **Link with customer-feedback**: advisory + feedback co-build.
15. **Link with customer-success**: advisory + success co-build.
16. **Link with executive-briefing**: advisory + executive briefing co-build.
17. **Toolchain**: Gainsight / Salesforce CAB Workspace / Slack / Notion / Convene.
18. **Publicly queryable**: the strategy is look-up-able by everyone; not hidden.
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot.
20. **First principles**: why we must do a customer-advisory-board strategy; the worst consequence of not doing it.
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy.
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk).
23. **Occam**: simpler advisory is better; cut redundant layers.
