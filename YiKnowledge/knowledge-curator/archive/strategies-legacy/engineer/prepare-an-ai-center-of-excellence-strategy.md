## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: mission + capability + service + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + innovation; not sloganeering
3. **Mission**: direction / ambition / boundary / KPI; do not omit
4. **Capability**: platform / engineering / education / governance; do not omit
5. **Service**: consulting / co-built / managed / promotion; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress gradually from mission -> capability -> service -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: team is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Work with ai-strategy**: CoE + AI co-built
13. **Work with it-strategy**: CoE + IT co-built
14. **Work with data-strategy**: CoE + data co-built
15. **Work with ml-strategy**: CoE + ML co-built
16. **Work with digital-transformation**: CoE + digital co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Microsoft AI / McKinsey / BCG / Deloitte / Anthropic
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why an AI center-of-excellence strategy is needed; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: AI center of excellence the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
