## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: role + decision + investment + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Role**: sponsor / champion / owner; do not omit
4. **Decision**: priority / resources / escalation; do not omit
5. **Investment**: budget / resources / time; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: decision count + cadence + unblocks + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from role → decision → investment → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: name count only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Links with data-product-management**: sponsor + product management co-build
13. **Links with data-strategy**: sponsor + data strategy co-build
14. **Links with data-governance**: sponsor + data governance co-build
15. **Links with data-stewardship**: sponsor + data stewardship co-build
16. **Links with executive-sponsorship**: sponsor + executive sponsorship co-build
17. **Toolchain**: OKR / RACI / decision log / stakeholder map / exec briefing
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must data sponsor; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by self-organization; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: data sponsor the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
