## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: mapping + assessment + mitigation + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by resilience + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Map**: tier-1 / tier-2 / tier-n / nodes / single points; do not omit
4. **Assess**: geopolitical / climate / financial / compliance / concentration; do not omit
5. **Mitigate**: diversification / inventory / substitutes / contracts / monitoring; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: resilience + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from mapping → assessment → mitigation → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with vendor-risk**: SCRM + VRM co-build
13. **Link with third-party-risk**: SCRM + TPRM co-build
14. **Link with risk**: SCRM + Risk co-build
15. **Link with business-continuity**: SCRM + BC co-build
16. **Link with procurement**: SCRM + Procurement co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Resilinc / Everstream / Riskmethods / Interos / Craft
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must SCRM; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can inventory alone solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (resilience / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: SCRM the simpler the better; cut redundant metrics
