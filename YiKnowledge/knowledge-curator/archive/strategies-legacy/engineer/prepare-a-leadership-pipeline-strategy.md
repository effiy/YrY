## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Identify + develop + transition + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: Prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Identify Identify**: high-potential / turning points / signals / assessment; do not omit
4. **Develop Develop**: experience / coaching / training / feedback; do not omit
5. **Transition Transition**: skills / values / time / responsibility; do not omit
6. **Governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: Progress from identify → develop → transition → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: headcount is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with leadership-team**: leadership pipeline + leadership team co-built
13. **Link with leadership-succession**: leadership pipeline + leadership succession co-built
14. **Link with leadership**: leadership pipeline + leadership co-built
15. **Link with leadership-development**: leadership pipeline + leadership development co-built
16. **Link with high-potential**: leadership pipeline + high-potential co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Korn Ferry / Hogan / Lattice / 15Five / Workday
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must the leadership-pipeline strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by defaults; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: leadership pipeline — the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
