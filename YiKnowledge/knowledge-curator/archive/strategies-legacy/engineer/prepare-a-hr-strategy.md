## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: attract + recruit + develop + retain + measurement; none can be missing
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by revenue per head + retention rate + recruiting cost + candidate NPS + business supply; not sloganeering
3. **Attract**: EPP + ED + brand + transparency + culture; do not omit
4. **Recruit**: persona + channel + process + interview + offer; do not omit
5. **Develop**: path + L&D + certification + high-potential + succession; do not omit
6. **Retain**: compensation + benefits + culture + transparency + retirement; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: revenue per head + retention + recruiting cost + candidate NPS + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from attract → recruit → develop → retain → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with talent**: HR + talent co-built
13. **Link with compensation**: HR + compensation co-built
14. **Link with benefits**: HR + benefits co-built
15. **Link with learning-and-development**: HR + L&D co-built
16. **Link with culture**: HR + culture co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Workday / BambooHR / Rippling / Gusto / Personio / SAP SuccessFactors / Oracle HCM / Eightfold / Greenhouse / Lever
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why HR strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can outsourcing solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / retention / business)
23. **Occam**: HR — the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
