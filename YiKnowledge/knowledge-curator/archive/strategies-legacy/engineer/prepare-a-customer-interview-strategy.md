## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: recruit + guide + conduct + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Recruit**: persona / invitation / incentive / quota; do not omit
4. **Guide**: goal / prompts / follow-ups / recording; do not omit
5. **Conduct**: environment / cadence / notes / retrospective; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from recruit → guide → conduct → governance → measurement progressively; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: a chat is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with user-research**: interview + user research co-built
13. **Link with customer-feedback**: interview + feedback co-built
14. **Link with customer-survey**: interview + survey co-built
15. **Link with customer-research**: interview + research co-built
16. **Link with customer-insight**: interview + insight co-built
17. **Toolchain**: User Interviews / UserTesting / Zoom + Otter.ai / Dovetail / Reframer
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why customer-interview strategy must exist; the worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the interview the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
