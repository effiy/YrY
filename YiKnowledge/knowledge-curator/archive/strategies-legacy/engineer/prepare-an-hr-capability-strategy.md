## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: model + assessment + development + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Model**: strategy / business / data / digital / culture; do not omit
4. **Assessment**: self-assessment + 360 + assessment center + retrospective; do not omit
5. **Development**: learning / experience / relationships / coaching; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from model → assessment → development → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: training is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with hr-competency**: capability + competency co-built
13. **Link with hr-operating-model**: capability + operating model co-built
14. **Link with hr-excellence**: capability + excellence co-built
15. **Link with hr-transformation**: capability + transformation co-built
16. **Link with talent-management**: HR + talent management co-built
17. **Toolchain**: DDI / Korn Ferry / McKinsey / Josh Bersin / HBR
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must HR capability strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: HR capability simpler is better; cut redundant layers
