## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: responsibility + budget + allocation + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Responsibility**: owner / boundary / KPI / authorization; do not omit
4. **Budget**: preparation / approval / adjustment / audit trail; do not omit
5. **Allocation**: drivers / tiers / rules / reporting; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: gradual from responsibility -> budget -> allocation -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: aggregation is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with cost-management**: cost center + cost management co-built
13. **Link with cost-allocation**: cost center + cost allocation co-built
14. **Link with cost-accounting**: cost center + cost accounting co-built
15. **Link with cost-reduction**: cost center + cost reduction co-built
16. **Link with fp-and-a**: cost center + FP&A co-built
17. **Toolchain**: SAP CO / Oracle Cost Accounting / Anaplan / IBM Cognos / Workday Adaptive Planning
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must cost center strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: cost center the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
