## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: collect + organize + share + apply + evolve; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by business scenario + decision point + reuse value; not sloganeering
3. **Collect**: documentation + wiki + code + tickets + meetings; do not omit
4. **Organize**: classify + tag + search + lineage; do not omit
5. **Share**: permission + collaboration + push + training; do not omit
6. **Apply**: search + recommend + context + decision support; do not omit
7. **Evolve**: version + review + archive + retire; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from documentation → classify → search → recommend → full automation; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with docs**: KM + documentation co-built
13. **Link with KT**: KM + knowledge transfer co-built
14. **Link with audit**: KM + audit co-built
15. **Link with developer-portal**: KM + portal co-built
16. **Link with contribute**: KM + contribution co-built
17. **Link with evolve**: KM + evolution co-built
18. **Link with training**: KM + training co-built
19. **Toolchain**: Notion / Confluence / GitBook / Docusaurus / Outline / Obsidian
20. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
21. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
22. **First principles**: why must KM; worst consequence of not doing
23. **Inversion thinking**: how much can wiki + manual maintenance solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
24. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / complexity / reuse / business)
25. **Occam**: KM the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
