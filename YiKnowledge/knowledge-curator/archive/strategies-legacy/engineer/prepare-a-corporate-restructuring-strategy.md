## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: diagnosis + design + execution + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Diagnosis**: root cause / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Design**: plan / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Execution**: cadence / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from diagnosis -> design -> execution -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Interplay with mergers-and-acquisitions**: CorporateRestructuring + MergersAndAcquisitions co-built
13. **Interplay with divestiture**: CorporateRestructuring + Divestiture co-built
14. **Interplay with joint-venture**: CorporateRestructuring + JointVenture co-built
15. **Interplay with strategic-alliance**: CorporateRestructuring + StrategicAlliance co-built
16. **Interplay with corporate-governance**: CorporateRestructuring + CorporateGovernance co-built
17. **Toolchain**: FTI Consulting / Alvarez & Marsal / Huron / Bain / McKinsey
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why CorporateRestructuring is required; the worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by gradual adjustment; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler the CorporateRestructuring the better; cut redundant actions
