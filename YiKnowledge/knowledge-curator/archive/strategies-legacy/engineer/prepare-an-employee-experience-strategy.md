## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: touchpoints + cadence + channels + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by eNPS + retention + performance + referral + decision speed; not sloganeering
3. **Touchpoints**: recruiting / offer / onboarding / 1:1 / review / offboarding + upgrade; do not omit
4. **Cadence**: daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly / yearly + upgrade; do not omit
5. **Channels**: 1:1 / Slack / Teams / intranet / all-hands / EX platform; do not omit
6. **Governance**: charter + owner + budget + guidelines + retirement; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: eNPS + retention + performance + referral + decision speed; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from touchpoints -> cadence -> channels -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Interplay with employee-engagement**: EX + engagement co-built
13. **Interplay with employer-branding**: EX + EB co-built
14. **Interplay with culture**: EX + culture co-built
15. **Interplay with performance-management**: EX + PM co-built
16. **Interplay with internal-communications**: EX + internal comms co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Qualtrics-EX / Culture-Amp / Peakon / Glint / Lattice / 15Five / Workday-HXM / Microsoft-Viva / ServiceNow-HRSD
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why an EX strategy is required; the worst consequence of not having one
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by ad-hoc benefits; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / retention / business)
23. **Occam**: the simpler the EX the better; cut redundant steps
