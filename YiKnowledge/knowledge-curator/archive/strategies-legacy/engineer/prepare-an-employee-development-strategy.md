## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identify + plan + enable + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Identify**: capability / interest / values / strengths; do not omit
4. **Plan**: goal / path / resources / cadence; do not omit
5. **Enable**: training / experience / rotation / mentor; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from identify → plan → enable → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: course hours are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with career-development**: employee development + career development co-built
13. **Link with learning-and-development**: employee development + learning and development co-built
14. **Link with training-and-enablement**: employee development + training and enablement co-built
15. **Link with leadership-development**: employee development + leadership development co-built
16. **Link with employee-retention**: employee development + employee retention co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Workday Learning / Cornerstone / Degreed / LinkedIn Learning / Udemy
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why an employee development strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: employee development — the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
