## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identify + predict + retain + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Identify**: signal / stage / type / cause; do not omit
4. **Predict**: model / threshold / upgrade / action; do not omit
5. **Save**: talk track / incentive / repair / retrospective; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from identify → predict → retain → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: numbers are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with customer-retention**: churn + retention co-built
13. **Link with customer-reactivation**: churn + reactivation co-built
14. **Link with customer-lifetime**: churn + lifetime co-built
15. **Link with customer-health**: churn + health co-built
16. **Link with customer-success**: churn + success co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Gainsight / ChurnZero / Totango / Pendo / Amplitude
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must a customer-churn strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: churn the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
