## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: hypothesis + design + collect + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; no empty slogans
3. **hypothesis Hypothesis**: problem / variable / metric / threshold; none missing
4. **design Design**: sampling / quantitative / experiment / bias; none missing
5. **collect Collect**: channel / time series / cleaning / weighting; none missing
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / docs / drift; none missing
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; none missing
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from hypothesis → design → collect → governance → measurement; no skipping levels
9. **Not report-only**: numbers are only the starting point; not the endpoint
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must have landed evidence; no ambiguity
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **links with qualitative-research**: quantitative + qualitative co-build
13. **links with customer-survey**: quantitative + survey co-build
14. **links with market-research**: quantitative + market co-build
15. **links with customer-research**: quantitative + customer co-build
16. **links with product-metrics**: quantitative + metric co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Qualtrics / SurveyMonkey / SPSS / R / Stata
18. **Publicly accessible**: strategy accessible to everyone; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why quantitative research strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: quantitative research — the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
