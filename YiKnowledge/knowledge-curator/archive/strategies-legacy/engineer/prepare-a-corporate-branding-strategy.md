## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: positioning + identity + experience + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Positioning**: vision / mission / differentiation / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Identity**: visual / language / behavior / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Experience**: touchpoints / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from positioning → identity → experience → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with brand-positioning**: CorporateBranding + BrandPositioning co-built
13. **Link with corporate-identity**: CorporateBranding + CorporateIdentity co-built
14. **Link with employer-branding**: CorporateBranding + EmployerBranding co-built
15. **Link with brand-architecture**: CorporateBranding + BrandArchitecture co-built
16. **Link with corporate-communications**: CorporateBranding + CorporateCommunications co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Interbrand / Landor / Siegel+Gale / Lippincott / FutureBrand
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must CorporateBranding; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can word-of-mouth solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: CorporateBranding the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
