## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: journey + device + collaboration + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Journey**: onboarding / active / offboarding / return; do not omit
4. **Device**: endpoint / mobile / collaboration / self-service; do not omit
5. **Collaborate**: communication / documentation / knowledge / automation; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from journey → device → collaboration → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: tool count is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with digital-workplace**: experience + digital workplace co-build
13. **Link with workplace**: experience + workplace co-build
14. **Link with employee-experience**: experience + employee experience co-build
15. **Link with hybrid-work**: experience + hybrid work co-build
16. **Link with future-of-work**: experience + future of work co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Microsoft Viva / Workplace from Meta / VMware Workspace ONE / Stravito / Nexthink
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a digital employee experience strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by defaults; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: digital employee experience, the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
