## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identify + assess + mitigate + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Identify**: safety / fairness / privacy / transparency / social; do not omit
4. **Assess**: probability / impact / severity / priority; do not omit
5. **Mitigate**: avoid / reduce / transfer / accept; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from identify → assess → mitigate → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: item count is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with ai-impact-assessment**: risk assessment + AI impact assessment co-build
13. **Link with ai-audit**: risk assessment + AI audit co-build
14. **Link with ai-responsibility**: risk assessment + AI responsibility co-build
15. **Link with ai-governance**: risk assessment + AI governance co-build
16. **Link with ai-compliance**: risk assessment + AI compliance co-build
17. **Toolchain**: NIST AI RMF / EU AI Act / ISO/IEC 23894 / Custom / Custom
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must AI risk assessment strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: AI risk assessment the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
