## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: planning + compliance + transfer + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by tax savings + compliance + trust + speed + risk; no empty slogans
3. **Planning**: income tax / sales tax / VAT / transfer pricing / international; none missing
4. **Compliance**: filing / payment / record-keeping / recheck / audit; none missing
5. **Transfer**: profit / price / risk / asset / IP; none missing
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / docs / drift; none missing
7. **Measurement**: tax savings + compliance + trust + speed + risk; none missing
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from planning -> compliance -> transfer -> governance -> measurement; no skipping levels
9. **Not report-only**: reports are only the starting point; not the endpoint
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must have landed evidence; no ambiguity
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Links with cfo**: Tax + CFO co-build
13. **Links with treasury**: Tax + Treasury co-build
14. **Links with financial-reporting**: Tax + Reporting co-build
15. **Links with compliance**: Tax + Compliance co-build
16. **Links with risk**: Tax + risk co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Vertex / ONESOURCE / Avalara / Sovos / H&R Block
18. **Publicly accessible**: strategy accessible to everyone; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why Tax is required; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion**: how much can be solved by outsourcing to an accounting firm; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (tax savings / compliance / trust / speed)
23. **Occam**: the simpler the Tax strategy the better; cut redundant structures
