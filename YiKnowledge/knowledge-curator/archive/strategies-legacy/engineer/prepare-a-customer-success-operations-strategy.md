## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: data + process + tools + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by NRR + retention + expansion + revenue share + NPS; not sloganeering
3. **Data**: customer profile + health + adoption + renewal + value; do not omit
4. **Process**: onboarding / adoption / expansion / advocacy / renewal; do not omit
5. **Tooling**: CS platform / health score / play / integration / data; do not omit
6. **Governance**: charter + owner + budget + guidelines + retirement; do not omit
7. **Measure**: NRR + retention + expansion + revenue share + NPS; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from data -> process -> tools -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with customer-success**: operations + CS co-built
13. **link with customer-health**: operations + health co-built
14. **link with revenue-ops**: operations + RevOps co-built
15. **link with customer-lifetime-value**: operations + CLV co-built
16. **link with customer-journey**: operations + customer journey co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Gainsight / Totango / Catalyst / ChurnZero / CustomerSuccessBox / Planhat / Churnify / Vitally
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must CS operations strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can CSM manual solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / retention / business) 
23. **Occam**: CS operations the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
