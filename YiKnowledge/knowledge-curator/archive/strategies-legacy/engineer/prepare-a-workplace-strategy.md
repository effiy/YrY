## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Space + Experience + Technology + Governance + Measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by talent + trust + speed + cost + risk; not sloganeering
3. **Space**: HQ / satellite / home / co-work / virtual; do not omit
4. **Experience**: welcome / collaboration / focus / rest / farewell; do not omit
5. **Technology**: booking / video / access / network / smart; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: talent + trust + speed + cost + risk; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from Space → Experience → Technology → Governance → Measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Future-of-work linkage**: Workplace + FoW co-built
13. **Employee-experience linkage**: Workplace + EX co-built
14. **Remote-work-policy linkage**: Workplace + Remote co-built
15. **Facilities linkage**: Workplace + Facilities co-built
16. **Culture linkage**: Workplace + Culture co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Envoy / Robin / Microsoft Places / CBRE Host / Nxmod
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must Workplace exist; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can traditional office work solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (talent / trust / speed / cost)
23. **Occam**: Workplace the simpler the better; cut redundant spaces
