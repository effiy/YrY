## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: intelligence + analysis + choice + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Intelligence**: target / movement / advantage / weakness; do not omit
4. **Analyze**: SWOT / five forces / value chain / strategic group; do not omit
5. **Choose**: leader / challenger / follower / niche; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from intelligence → analysis → choice → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: rivals are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with market**: competition + market co-build
13. **Link with business**: competition + business strategy co-build
14. **Link with positioning**: competition + positioning co-build
15. **Link with pricing**: competition + pricing co-build
16. **Link with messaging**: competition + messaging co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Klue / Crayon / Kompyte / Contify / AlphaSense
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must competition strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by relying on defaults; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: competition — the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
