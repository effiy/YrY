## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: baseline + growth + profit + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by Rule-of-40 passing + growth + profit + efficiency + renewal; not sloganeering
3. **baseline Baseline**: history / industry / cohort / segment / stage; do not omit
4. **growth Growth**: ARR / NRR / new customer / expansion / channel; do not omit
5. **profit Profit**: gross / operating profit / FCF / EBITDA / unit economics; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: Rule-of-40 passing + growth + profit + efficiency + renewal; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from baseline → growth → profit → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **and magic-number link**: Rule-of-40 + Magic Number co-build
13. **and saas-metrics link**: Rule-of-40 + SaaS metrics co-build
14. **and arr link**: Rule-of-40 + ARR co-build
15. **and budget-planning link**: Rule-of-40 + budget co-build
16. **and marketing-attribution link**: Rule-of-40 + marketing attribution co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Snowflake / ChartMogul / Baremetrics / Salesforce / Marketo / 6sense
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must Rule-of-40; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can growth or profit alone solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / growth / retention)
23. **Occam**: Rule-of-40 the simpler the better; cut redundant assumptions
