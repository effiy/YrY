## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: framework + data + disclosure + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: set priority by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Framework**: GRI / SASB / TCFD / CSRD; do not omit
4. **Data**: environment / social / governance / value chain; do not omit
5. **Disclose**: report / assurance / platform / grievance; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from framework -> data -> disclosure -> governance -> measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: disclosure is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with esg**: ESG reporting + ESG co-build
13. **Link with sustainability**: ESG reporting + sustainability co-build
14. **Link with carbon-accounting**: ESG reporting + carbon accounting co-build
15. **Link with regulatory-reporting**: ESG reporting + regulatory reporting co-build
16. **Link with compliance-management**: ESG reporting + compliance management co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Workiva / Enablon / Sphera / Diligent / Novata
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must ESG reporting strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequence after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: ESG reporting, the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
