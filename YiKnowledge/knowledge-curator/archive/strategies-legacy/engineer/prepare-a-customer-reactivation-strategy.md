## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Identification + segmentation + reach + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Identification Identify**: churned / dormant / downgraded / trial; do not omit
4. **Segmentation Segment**: value / cause / time / profile; do not omit
5. **Reach**: channel / offer / copy / timing; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from identification → segmentation → reach → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: recall is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with customer-churn**: reactivation + churn co-built
13. **Link with customer-retention**: reactivation + retention co-built
14. **Link with customer-lifetime**: reactivation + lifetime co-built
15. **Link with customer-loyalty**: reactivation + loyalty co-built
16. **Link with customer-feedback**: reactivation + feedback co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Braze / Customer.io / Iterable / Optimove / Epsilon
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must customer reactivation strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: reactivation the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
