## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: signal + trigger + intervention + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: set priority by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Signal**: behavior / node / value / model; do not omit
4. **Trigger**: time / event / stage / smart; do not omit
5. **Intervene**: email / push / in-app / human; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from signal → trigger → intervention → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: activation rate is only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with customer-acquisition**: customer activation + customer acquisition co-build
13. **link with onboarding-flow**: customer activation + onboarding process co-build
14. **link with product-adoption**: customer activation + product adoption co-build
15. **link with customer-retention**: customer activation + customer retention co-build
16. **link with growth-marketing**: customer activation + growth marketing co-build
17. **toolchain**: Braze / Customer.io / Intercom / Appcues / Pendo
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must customer activation strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable don't introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: customer activation the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
