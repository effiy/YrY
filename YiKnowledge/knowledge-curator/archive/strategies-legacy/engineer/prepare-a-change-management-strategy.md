## Action recommendations

1. **five dimensions**: need + blueprint + enablement + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritise by adoption + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **need Need**: current state / pain points / expectations / benefits / urgency; do not omit
4. **blueprint Blueprint**: vision / roadmap / resources / resistance / timing; do not omit
5. **enablement Enable**: training / communication / incentive / support / feedback; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: adoption + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from need → blueprint → enablement → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with organizational-change**: CM + OC co-built
13. **link with change-communications**: CM + Comms co-built
14. **link with stakeholder-management**: CM + Stakeholder co-built
15. **link with training-and-enablement**: CM + Training co-built
16. **link with culture**: CM + Culture co-built
17. **toolchain**: Prosci / Whatfix / WalkMe / Appcues / Pendo
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must CM; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by mandatory command; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (adoption / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: CM the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
