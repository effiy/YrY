## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: acquisition + activation + retention + revenue + referral; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by business growth + CAC + LTV + ROAS; not sloganeering
3. **Acquisition**: channel + traffic + CAC + conversion + trend; do not omit
4. **Activation**: aha moment + time window + steps + conversion + optimize; do not omit
5. **Retention**: D1 / D7 / D30 / D90 + cohort + trend + churn; do not omit
6. **Revenue**: ARPU + ARPPU + LTV + MRR + ARR + churn rate; do not omit
7. **Referral**: K factor + invite rate + acceptance rate + viral coefficient + NPS; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from acquisition → activation → retention → revenue → referral progressive; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with heart-aarrr**: pirate funnel + HEART+AARRR co-build
13. **link with kpi**: pirate funnel + KPI co-build
14. **link with north-star-metric**: pirate funnel + north star co-build
15. **link with product-analytics**: pirate funnel + measurement co-build
16. **link with growth**: pirate funnel + growth co-build
17. **link with go-to-market**: pirate funnel + GTM co-build
18. **Toolchain**: AARRR Framework / Amplitude / Mixpanel / Heap / Google Analytics / Adjust / AppsFlyer / Branch / Looker
19. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
20. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
21. **first principles**: why must pirate funnel; worst consequence of not doing
22. **inversion thinking**: how much can a single metric solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
23. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / growth / business)
24. **Occam**: pirate funnel the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
