## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + content + cadence + channel + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by subscription + open rate + click + revenue + retention; not sloganeering
3. **Audience**: ICP / role / subscription source / cohort / segment; do not omit
4. **Content**: column / template / personalization / original / repost; do not omit
5. **Cadence**: weekly / daily / monthly / thematic / trigger; do not omit
6. **Channel**: email / web / RSS / social / app; do not omit
7. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
8. **Measure**: subscription + open rate + click + revenue + retention; do not omit
9. **Not one-shot**: from audience → content → cadence → channel → measurement gradual; no skipping
10. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
11. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
12. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
13. **Link with content**: Newsletter + Content co-built
14. **Link with lifecycle-email**: Newsletter + Lifecycle Email co-built
15. **Link with seo**: Newsletter + SEO co-built
16. **Link with brand-identity**: Newsletter + Brand co-built
17. **Link with marketing-attribution**: Newsletter + Attribution co-built
18. **Toolchain**: Mailchimp / Substack / Beehiiv / ConvertKit / HubSpot / Marketo
19. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
20. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
21. **First principles**: why must Newsletter; worst consequence of not doing
22. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by social; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
23. **Occam**: Newsletter the simpler the better; cut redundant columns
