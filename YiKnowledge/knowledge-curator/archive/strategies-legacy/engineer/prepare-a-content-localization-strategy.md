## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: language + culture + regulation + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Language Language**: languages / terminology / style / translation review; do not omit
4. **Culture Culture**: colors / metaphors / taboos / cases; do not omit
5. **Regulation Regulation**: disclosure / privacy / compliance / labeling; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from language -> culture -> regulation -> governance -> measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: translation is only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with content**: localization + content co-build
13. **Link with content-operations**: localization + operations co-build
14. **Link with content-accessibility**: localization + accessible co-build
15. **Link with content-distribution**: localization + distribution co-build
16. **Link with frontend-i18n**: localization + frontend i18n co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Lokalise / Crowdin / Phrase / Transifex / Smartling
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must content localization strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: localization simpler is better; cut redundant layers
