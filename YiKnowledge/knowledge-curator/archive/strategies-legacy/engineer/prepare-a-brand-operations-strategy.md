## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: assets + guidelines + rollout + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Asset**: logo / visual / audio / content; do not omit
4. **Guideline**: basics / application / digital / assets; do not omit
5. **Rollout**: publish + training + monitoring + enforcement; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from assets → guidelines → rollout → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: logo count is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with creative-operations**: brand + creative operations co-build
13. **Link with content-operations**: brand + content operations co-build
14. **Link with brand-management**: operations + brand management co-build
15. **Link with brand-equity**: operations + brand equity co-build
16. **Link with brand-experience**: operations + brand experience co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Brandfolder / Frontify / Bynder / Widen / Hootsuite
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a brand operations strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by defaults; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: brand operations, the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
