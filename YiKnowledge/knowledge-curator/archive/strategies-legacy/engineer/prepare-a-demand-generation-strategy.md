## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + channel + content + conversion + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by MQL + SQL + pipeline + CAC + ROI; not sloganeering
3. **Audience**: ICP + persona + pain points + buying-committee + intent; do not omit
4. **Channel**: SEO + SEM + social + email + events + partners; do not omit
5. **Content**: top + middle + bottom + evergreen + viral + interactive; do not omit
6. **Conversion**: TOFU + MOFU + BOFU + lead-score + nurture; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: MQL + SQL + pipeline + CAC + LTV + attribution; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from audience → channel → content → conversion → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with product-marketing**: demand + product marketing co-build
13. **Link with lead-generation**: demand + leads co-build
14. **Link with growth**: demand + growth co-build
15. **Link with go-to-market**: demand + GTM co-build
16. **Link with content**: demand + content co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Demand-Gen-Framework / Marketo / Pardot / HubSpot / 6sense / Demandbase / Drift / Clearbit / ZoomInfo / Outreach
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must demand generation; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can natural traffic solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / growth / business)
23. **Occam**: demand generation the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
