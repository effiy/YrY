## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: channel + region + SLA + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Channel**: direct / dealer / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Region**: tier / boundary / closed loop; do not omit
5. **SLA**: standard / expedited / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from channel → region → SLA → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Linked with logistics-network**: Distribution + LogisticsNetwork co-built
13. **Linked with transportation**: Distribution + Transportation co-built
14. **Linked with warehouse-management**: Distribution + WarehouseManagement co-built
15. **Linked with supply-chain-management**: Distribution + SCM co-built
16. **Linked with inventory-management**: Distribution + InventoryManagement co-built
17. **Toolchain**: SAP S/4HANA / Oracle / Manhattan / Blue Yonder / Kinaxis
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why Distribution is needed; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by a single channel; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: Distribution, the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
