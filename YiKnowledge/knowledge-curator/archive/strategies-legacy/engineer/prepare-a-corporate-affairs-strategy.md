## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Government + PR + issues + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: Prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Government Government**: legislation / regulation / closed loop; do not omit
4. **PR PublicAffairs**: issues / position / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Issues Issues**: monitoring / response / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: Progress from government → PR → issues → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with government-relations**: CorporateAffairs + GovernmentRelations co-built
13. **Link with regulatory-affairs**: CorporateAffairs + RegulatoryAffairs co-built
14. **Link with corporate-communications**: CorporateAffairs + CorporateCommunications co-built
15. **Link with public-relations**: CorporateAffairs + PublicRelations co-built
16. **Link with stakeholder-engagement**: CorporateAffairs + StakeholderEngagement co-built
17. **Toolchain**: FiscalNote / Bloomberg Government / Quorum / RAP / Edelman
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must CorporateAffairs; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by PR; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: CorporateAffairs — the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
