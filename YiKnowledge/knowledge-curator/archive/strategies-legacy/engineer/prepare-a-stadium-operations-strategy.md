1. **Five dimensions**: pre-match + in-match + post-match + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **pre-match Pre-match**: ticketing / security / closed-loop; do not omit
4. **in-match In-match**: crowd / f&b / closed-loop; do not omit
5. **post-match Post-match**: clearing / retrospective / closed-loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from pre-match → in-match → post-match → governance → measurement; no skipping
