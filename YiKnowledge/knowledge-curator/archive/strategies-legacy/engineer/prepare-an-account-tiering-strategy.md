## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: dimension + threshold + resource + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by ARR + retention + NRR + strategic value + growth potential; not sloganeering
3. **dimension Dimension**: ARR / industry / region / strategy / potential; do not omit
4. **threshold Threshold**: T1 / T2 / T3 / T4 tiering; do not omit
5. **resource Resource**: AE / CSM / SE / executive / marketing; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: cadence / owner / upgrade / re-tier / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: tier revenue contribution + retention + NRR + win-rate + coverage efficiency; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from dimension -> threshold -> resource -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with account-mapping**: tiering + account mapping co-built
13. **link with account-engagement**: tiering + account manager engagement co-built
14. **link with account-management**: tiering + account management co-built
15. **link with sales-territory**: tiering + sales territory co-built
16. **link with customer-success**: tiering + CS co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce / Clari / Gainsight / Boost-UP / People-AI / 6sense
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must account tiering; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can relying on freeform coverage solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: tiering the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
