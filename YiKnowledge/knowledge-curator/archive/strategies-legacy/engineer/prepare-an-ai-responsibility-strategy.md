## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: responsibility + accountability + transparency + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **responsibility Responsible**: role / responsibility / boundary; do not omit
4. **accountability Accountable**: traceability / evidence / retrospective; do not omit
5. **transparency Transparent**: decision / data / model / limitations; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from responsibility → accountability → transparency → governance → measurement progressive; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: role count is only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with ai-governance**: responsibility + AI governance co-build
13. **link with ai-accountability**: responsibility + AI accountability co-build
14. **link with ai-transparency**: responsibility + AI transparency co-build
15. **link with ai-fairness**: responsibility + AI fairness co-build
16. **link with ai-bias**: responsibility + AI bias co-build
17. **Toolchain**: AI Responsibility Frameworks / EU AI Act / NIST AI RMF / ISO/IEC 42001 / Custom
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must AI responsibility strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can default solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: AI responsibility the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
