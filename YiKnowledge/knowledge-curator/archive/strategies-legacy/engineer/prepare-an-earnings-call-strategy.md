## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + content + cadence + Q&A + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by accuracy + transparency + confidence + share price + coverage; not sloganeering
3. **audience Audience**: analyst / investor / media / employee / public; do not omit
4. **content Content**: remarks / guidance / KPI / risk / outlook; do not omit
5. **cadence Cadence**: quarterly / preparation / rehearsal / day-of / follow-up; do not omit
6. **Q&A Q&A**: rehearsal / topic selection / drill / on-site / recording; do not omit
7. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
8. **measurement Measure**: accuracy + transparency + confidence + share price + coverage; do not omit
9. **not one-shot**: progress from audience → content → cadence → Q&A → measurement; no skipping
10. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
11. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
12. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
13. **Link with investor-relations**: Earnings Call + IR co-built
14. **Link with financial-reporting**: Earnings Call + Reporting co-built
15. **Link with business-model**: Earnings Call + Business Model co-built
16. **Link with narrative**: Earnings Call + Narrative co-built
17. **Link with positioning**: Earnings Call + Positioning co-built
18. **Toolchain**: Q4inc / Nasdaq / Virmaca / S&P / FactSet
19. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
20. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
21. **first principles**: why must Earnings Call; worst consequence of not doing it
22. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by a press release; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
23. **Occam**: Earnings Call the simpler the better; cut redundant segments
