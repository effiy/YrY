## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: baseline + decomposition + action + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by NRR lift + ARR + renewal + retention + NRR; not sloganeering
3. **baseline Baseline**: history / industry / cohort / segment / ACV; do not omit
4. **decomposition Decompose**: expansion / churn / contraction / reactivation / cohort; do not omit
5. **action Action**: playbook / training / product / pricing / positioning; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: NRR lift + ARR + renewal + retention + NRR; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from baseline → decomposition → action → governance → measurement progressive; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with arr**: NRR + ARR co-build
13. **link with grr**: NRR + GRR co-build
14. **link with expansion**: NRR + expansion co-build
15. **link with retention**: NRR + retention co-build
16. **link with churn-prediction**: NRR + churn prediction co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Snowflake / ChartMogul / Baremetrics / Gainsight / Catalyst / Salesforce
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must NRR; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can renewal rate solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: NRR the simpler the better; cut redundant dimensions
