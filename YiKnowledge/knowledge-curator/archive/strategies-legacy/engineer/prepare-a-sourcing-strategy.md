## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: source + bid + assess + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **source Source**: candidates / qualifications / closed loop; do not omit
4. **bid Bid**: RFP / quote / closed loop; do not omit
5. **assess Assess**: TCO / risk / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from source -> bid -> assess -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with procurement**: Sourcing + Procurement co-built
13. **link with vendor-risk**: Sourcing + VendorRisk co-built
14. **link with supplier-relationship**: Sourcing + SupplierRelationship co-built
15. **link with contract-management**: Sourcing + ContractManagement co-built
16. **link with third-party-risk**: Sourcing + ThirdPartyRisk co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Coupa / Ariba / Scout RFP / Jaggaer / Zip
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must Sourcing; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can relying on direct ordering solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: Sourcing the simpler the better; cut redundant processes
