## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: structure + level + flexibility + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by retention + recruiting + satisfaction + health cost + culture; not sloganeering
3. **Structure**: health + dental + vision + retirement + wellness + family + time + perq; do not omit
4. **Level**: benchmark + percentile + range + deviation + assessment; do not omit
5. **Flexibility**: cafeteria + option + personalization + flex time + flex location; do not omit
6. **Governance**: committee + transparency + audit + compliance + exception; do not omit
7. **Measure**: retention + satisfaction + health cost + ROI + retirement savings; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from structure -> level -> flexibility -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with hr**: benefits + HR co-built
13. **Link with talent**: benefits + talent co-built
14. **Link with compensation**: benefits + compensation co-built
15. **Link with culture**: benefits + culture co-built
16. **Link with learning-and-development**: benefits + L&D co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Carta / Pave / Benefitfocus / Businessolver / Gusto / Justworks / Rippling / Personio / Sequoia / Aon
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must benefits strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can statutory minimums solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / retention / culture) 
23. **Occam**: benefits the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
