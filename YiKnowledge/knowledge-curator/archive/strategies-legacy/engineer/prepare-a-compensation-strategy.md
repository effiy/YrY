## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: structure + leveling + adjustment + governance + measurement; no missing dimension.
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by revenue per capita + retention + key-role supply + perceived fairness; no empty slogans.
3. **Structure**: base + bonus + equity + incentive + commission + benefits; none missing.
4. **Leveling**: benchmark + bands + ranges + deviation + assessment; none missing.
5. **Adjustment**: promotion + merit + demotion + escalation + exception; none missing.
6. **Governance**: compensation committee + transparency + audit + compliance + exception approval; none missing.
7. **Measure**: revenue per capita + retention + comp ratio + attrition + satisfaction; none missing.
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from structure → leveling → adjustment → governance → measurement; no skipping levels.
9. **Not report-only**: reports are only the starting point; not the endpoint.
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must have landed evidence; no ambiguity.
11. **Versioned**: the strategy has versions; evolution is traceable.
12. **Link with hr**: compensation + HR co-build.
13. **Link with talent**: compensation + talent co-build.
14. **Link with benefits**: compensation + benefits co-build.
15. **Link with sales-compensation**: compensation + sales comp co-build.
16. **Link with learning-and-development**: compensation + L&D co-build.
17. **Toolchain**: Carta Total Comp / Pave / Radford / Mercer / PayScale / Salary.com / Korn Ferry / Aon Radford.
18. **Publicly accessible**: the strategy is accessible to everyone; not hidden.
19. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot.
20. **First principles**: why we must do a compensation strategy; the worst consequence of not doing it.
21. **Inversion**: how much can gut-call merit increases solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy.
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / retention / business).
23. **Occam**: simpler compensation is better; cut redundant steps.
