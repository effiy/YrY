## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + information + channel + governance + measurement; no missing dimension.
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by adoption + trust + speed + risk + cost; no empty slogans.
3. **Audience**: internal / external / executives / middle management / frontline; no omissions.
4. **Information**: why / what / how / who / when; no omissions.
5. **Channel**: email / meeting / video / intranet / one-on-one; no omissions.
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; no omissions.
7. **Measurement**: adoption + trust + speed + risk + cost; no omissions.
8. **Not one-shot**: gradual from audience → information → channel → governance → measurement; no skipping levels.
9. **No report-ism**: a report is just the start; not the end.
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must mark implementation evidence; no vagueness.
11. **Versioned**: the strategy is versioned; evolution is traceable.
12. **Link with change-management**: CC + CM co-build.
13. **Link with organizational-change**: CC + OC co-build.
14. **Link with stakeholder-management**: CC + SM co-build.
15. **Link with communication-cadence**: CC + Cadence co-build.
16. **Link with internal-communications**: CC + Internal co-build.
17. **Toolchain**: PoliteMail / ContactMonkey / Workshop / Simpplr / Staffbase.
18. **Publicly discoverable**: the strategy is publicly discoverable; not hidden.
19. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot.
20. **First principles**: why must we do CC; the worst consequence of not doing it.
21. **Inversion**: how much can a mass email solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy.
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (adoption / trust / speed / risk).
23. **Occam's razor**: simpler CC is better; cut redundant channels.
