## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identification + enablement + incentives + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Identify**: advocates / themes / channels / audiences; do not omit
4. **Enable**: content / tools / training / templates; do not omit
5. **Incentivize**: rewards / leaderboards / recognition / experience; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: gradual from identification -> enablement -> incentives -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: share counts are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with corporate-storytelling**: employee advocacy + corporate storytelling co-build
13. **Link with brand-journalism**: employee advocacy + brand journalism co-build
14. **Link with employer-branding**: employee advocacy + employer branding co-build
15. **Link with internal-communications**: employee advocacy + internal communications co-build
16. **Link with social-media**: employee advocacy + social media co-build
17. **Toolchain**: EveryoneSocial / Hootsuite Amplify / Sprout Social Employee Advocacy / GaggleAMP / Smarp
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must employee advocacy strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by relying on defaults; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: employee advocacy the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
