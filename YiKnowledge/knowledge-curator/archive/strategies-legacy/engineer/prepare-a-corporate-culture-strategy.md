## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: values + behaviors + embedding + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Values**: core / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Behaviors**: observable / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Embedding**: recruiting / assessment / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from values → behaviors → embedding → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with culture**: CorporateCulture + Culture co-built
13. **Link with corporate-communications**: CorporateCulture + CorporateCommunications co-built
14. **Link with employee-engagement**: CorporateCulture + EmployeeEngagement co-built
15. **Link with employee-experience**: CorporateCulture + EmployeeExperience co-built
16. **Link with leadership**: CorporateCulture + Leadership co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Culture Amp / Glint / Qualtrics / Officevibe / Waggl
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why CorporateCulture is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by slogans; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: CorporateCulture — the simpler the better; cut redundant dimensions
