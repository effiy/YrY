## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: purpose + cadence + facilitation + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **purpose Purpose**: goal / decision / alignment / feedback / cancel; do not omit
4. **cadence Cadence**: frequency / duration / buffer / reschedule / cancel; do not omit
5. **facilitation Facilitate**: agenda / role / notes / actions / closed-loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from purpose → cadence → facilitation → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with async-work**: MeetingCulture + Async co-built
13. **link with all-hands**: MeetingCulture + AllHands co-built
14. **link with one-on-one**: MeetingCulture + 1:1 co-built

17. **Toolchain**: Notion / Loom / Miro / Slido / Calendly
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must MeetingCulture; worst consequence of not doing it
