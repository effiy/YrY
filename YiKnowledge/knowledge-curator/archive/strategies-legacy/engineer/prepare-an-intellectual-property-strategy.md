## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: ownership + protection + operations + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Ownership**: invention / transfer / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Protect**: application / enforcement / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Monetize**: license / transfer / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from ownership → protection → operations → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with legal-operations**: IntellectualProperty + LegalOperations co-build
13. **link with contract-management**: IntellectualProperty + ContractManagement co-build
14. **link with legal-tech**: IntellectualProperty + LegalTech co-build
15. **link with corporate-secretary**: IntellectualProperty + CorporateSecretary co-build
16. **link with litigation**: IntellectualProperty + Litigation co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Anaqua / CPA Global / PatSnap / Clarivate / Innography
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must IntellectualProperty; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: rely on outsourcing agent how much can be solved; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: IntellectualProperty the simpler the better; cut redundant registrations
