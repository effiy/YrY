## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identify + enable + incentivize + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Identify**: evangelist / story / value / impact; do not omit
4. **Enable**: content / platform / data / tools; do not omit
5. **Incentivize**: reward / recognition / priority / experience; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from identify → enable → incentivize → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: evangelism counts are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with customer-advocacy**: evangelism + customer advocacy co-built
13. **Link with customer-marketing**: evangelism + customer marketing co-built
14. **Link with product-evangelism**: evangelism + product evangelism co-built
15. **Link with thought-leadership**: evangelism + thought leadership co-built
16. **Link with developer-advocacy**: evangelism + developer advocacy co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Influitive / AdvocacyHub / ReferralCandy / Ambassadory / Salesforce
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must a customer-evangelism strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: customer evangelism the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
