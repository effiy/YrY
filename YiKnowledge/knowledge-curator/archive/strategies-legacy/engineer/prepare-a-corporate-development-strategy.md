## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identify + assess + execute + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; no empty slogans
3. **Source identification**: target / market / strategy / value; none missing
4. **Assess**: finance / business / legal / integration; none missing
5. **Execute**: negotiation / signing / closing / integration; none missing
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / docs / drift; none missing
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; none missing
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from identify -> assess -> execute -> governance -> measurement; no skipping levels
9. **Not report-only**: M&A is only the starting point; not the endpoint
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must have landed evidence; no ambiguity
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with corporate-strategy**: development + corporate strategy co-build
13. **Link with strategic-management**: development + strategy management co-build
14. **Link with governance**: development + governance co-build
15. **Link with business-strategy**: development + business strategy co-build
16. **Link with corporate-development-function**: development + corporate development function co-build
17. **Toolchain**: DealCloud / SourceScrub / PitchBook / Grata / Affinity
18. **Publicly accessible**: strategy accessible to everyone; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must corporate development strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: corporate development the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
