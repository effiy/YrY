## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: visibility + optimization + attribution + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Visibility**: bill / tag / unit / trend; do not omit
4. **Optimize**: spec / idle / reservations / automation; do not omit
5. **Attribution**: business / team / product / customer; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from visibility -> optimization -> attribution -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ism**: bill is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **linked with cloud**: cost + cloud co-built
13. **linked with it-financial-management**: cost + IT finance co-built
14. **linked with it-strategy**: cost + IT co-built
15. **linked with enterprise-architecture**: cost + architecture co-built
16. **linked with technology**: cost + technology co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Apptio Cloudability / CloudHealth / Flexera / Spot.io / Nutanix Xi Beam
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must cloud-cost strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by defaults; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: cloud cost the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
