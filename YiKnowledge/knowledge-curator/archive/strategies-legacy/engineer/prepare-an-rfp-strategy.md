## Action recommendations

1. **five dimensions**: identify + evaluate + respond + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by win-rate + revenue + speed + cost + experience; not sloganeering
3. **identify Identify**: source / channel / qualification / timeliness / priority; do not omit
4. **evaluate Evaluate**: fit / strategy / resource / risk / win-rate; do not omit
5. **respond Respond**: questions / template / collaboration / review / submit; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: win-rate + revenue + speed + cost + experience; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from identify -> evaluate -> respond -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **links with rfi**: RFP + RFI co-build
13. **links with proposal**: RFP + Proposal co-build
14. **links with vendor-analysis**: RFP + Vendor Analysis co-build
15. **links with deal-desk**: RFP + Deal Desk co-build
16. **links with sales-enablement**: RFP + Enablement co-build
17. **Toolchain**: RFP360 / Loopio / RFPIO / Qvident / Highspot
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must RFP; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can sales individuals solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / speed / win-rate / revenue)
23. **Occam**: RFP the simpler the better; cut redundant process
