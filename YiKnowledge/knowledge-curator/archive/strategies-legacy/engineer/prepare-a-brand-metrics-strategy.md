## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: awareness + equity + performance + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Awareness Awareness**: recognition / association / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Equity Equity**: preference / loyalty / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Performance Performance**: share / growth / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from awareness → equity → performance → governance → measurement progressive; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with brand-strategy**: BrandMetrics + BrandStrategy co-build
13. **Link with brand-equity**: BrandMetrics + BrandEquity co-build
14. **Link with brand-management**: BrandMetrics + BrandManagement co-build
15. **Link with brand-portfolio**: BrandMetrics + BrandPortfolio co-build
16. **Link with corporate-branding**: BrandMetrics + CorporateBranding co-build
17. **Toolchain**: BrandZ / Kantar / Millward Brown / Brand Finance / Nielsen
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must BrandMetrics; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by sales data; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: BrandMetrics the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
