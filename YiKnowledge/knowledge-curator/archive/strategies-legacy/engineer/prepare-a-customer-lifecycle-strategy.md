## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: stages + touchpoints + cadence + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by NRR + LTV + retention + expansion + NPS; not sloganeering
3. **Stages**: acquire / activate / adopt / expand / advocate / renew; do not omit
4. **Touchpoints**: email / product / sales / CS / self-serve / channel; do not omit
5. **Cadence**: T+0 / +7 / +30 / +90 / quarterly / yearly; do not omit
6. **Governance**: charter + owner + budget + guidelines + retirement; do not omit
7. **Measure**: NRR + LTV + retention + expansion + NPS; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from stages -> touchpoints -> cadence -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with customer-journey**: lifecycle + journey co-built
13. **Link with customer-onboarding**: lifecycle + onboarding co-built
14. **Link with customer-success**: lifecycle + CS co-built
15. **Link with churn-reduction**: lifecycle + churn reduction co-built
16. **Link with CLV**: lifecycle + CLV co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Gainsight / Totango / Catalyst / ChurnZero / Planhat / Amplitude / Mixpanel / Salesforce / HubSpot
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must customer lifecycle strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by scattered touchpoints; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / retention / business) 
23. **Occam**: customer lifecycle the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
