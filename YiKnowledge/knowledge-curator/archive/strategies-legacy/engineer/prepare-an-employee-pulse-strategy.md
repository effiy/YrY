## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: design + collect + act + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Design Design**: questions / frequency / audience / anonymous; do not omit
4. **Collect Collect**: distribution / reminders / channels / return rate; do not omit
5. **Act Act**: analysis / prioritization / plan / follow-up; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: return rate + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from design -> collect -> act -> governance -> measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: scores are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with employee-feedback**: pulse + employee feedback co-build
13. **Link with employee-survey**: pulse + employee survey co-build
14. **Link with employee-engagement**: pulse + employee engagement co-build
15. **Link with employee-net-promoter**: pulse + eNPS co-build
16. **Link with employee-experience**: pulse + employee experience co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Culture Amp / Glint / Peakon / Lattice / 15Five
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must employee pulse strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: employee pulse simpler is better; cut redundant layers
