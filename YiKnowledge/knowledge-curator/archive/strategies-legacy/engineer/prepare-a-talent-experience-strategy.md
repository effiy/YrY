## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: journey + touchpoints + emotion + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by attraction + retention + engagement + trust + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Journey**: attract / onboarding / in-role / offboarding / alumni; do not omit
4. **Touchpoints**: channels / moments / tools / people; do not omit
5. **Emotion**: perception / interaction / accumulation / peak; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: satisfaction + engagement + retention + referral + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from journey -> touchpoints -> emotion -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: satisfaction is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Interplay with employee-experience**: talent + employee experience co-built
13. **Interplay with onboarding-experience**: talent + onboarding experience co-built
14. **Interplay with candidate-experience**: talent + candidate experience co-built
15. **Interplay with workplace-experience**: talent + workplace experience co-built
16. **Interplay with employer-value**: talent + employer value co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Qualtrics / Medallia / Glint / Culture Amp / Microsoft Viva
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a talent experience strategy is required; the worst consequence of not having one
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (attraction / retention / engagement / trust)
23. **Occam**: the simpler the talent experience the better; cut redundant layers
