## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: method + data + analysis + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Method Method**: perception map / competitive map / mind / differentiation; do not omit
4. **Data Data**: customer / competitor / own / market; do not omit
5. **Analyze Analyze**: dimensions / clustering / whitespace / opportunities; do not omit
6. **Governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from method → data → analysis → governance → measurement progressive; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: coordinates only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with messaging-research**: positioning + messaging co-build
13. **Link with brand-research**: positioning + brand co-build
14. **Link with competitive-research**: positioning + competitor co-build
15. **Link with market-research**: positioning + market co-build
16. **Link with customer-research**: positioning + customer co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Qualtrics / Perceptual Map / BrandMap / Positioning/R / SnapStrategist
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must positioning research strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: positioning research the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
