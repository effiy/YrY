## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: stages + conversion + health + forecast + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by pipeline coverage + revenue + quota attainment + conversion rate + cycle-time; no sloganeering
3. **Stages**: lead / MQL / SQL / opp / close / win; do not omit
4. **Conversion**: stage-to-stage + rate + effort + exit + upgrade; do not omit
5. **Health**: 3x / 4x coverage + aging + stagnation + quality score; do not omit
6. **Forecast**: commit / best-case / worst-case / weighted / AI; do not omit
7. **Measure**: pipeline coverage + revenue + quota attainment + conversion rate + cycle-time; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from stages → conversion → health → forecast → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **No sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with pipeline-management**: sales pipeline + pipeline management co-build
13. **Link with sales-forecast**: sales pipeline + sales forecast co-build
14. **Link with deal-review**: sales pipeline + deal-review co-build
15. **Link with quota**: sales pipeline + quota co-build
16. **Link with sales-ops**: sales pipeline + sales ops co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce / Clari / Gong-Insight / Outreach / Salesloft / People-AI / Insight-Square / Boost-UP
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must have sales pipeline; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by stages developing freely; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: sales pipeline the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
