## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: asset + valuation + investment + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Asset**: awareness / association / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Valuation**: measurement / valuation / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Investment**: building / maintenance / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from asset → valuation → investment → governance → measurement progressively; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with brand-strategy**: BrandEquity + BrandStrategy co-built
13. **Link with brand-portfolio**: BrandEquity + BrandPortfolio co-built
14. **Link with brand-management**: BrandEquity + BrandManagement co-built
15. **Link with brand-metrics**: BrandEquity + BrandMetrics co-built
16. **Link with corporate-branding**: BrandEquity + CorporateBranding co-built
17. **Toolchain**: BrandZ / Interbrand / Kantar / Millward Brown / Brand Finance
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must BrandEquity; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by sales; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: BrandEquity the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
