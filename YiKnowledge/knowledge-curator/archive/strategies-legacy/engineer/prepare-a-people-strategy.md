## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: value + structure + development + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **value Value**: philosophy / expectation / behavior / measurement; do not omit
4. **structure Structure**: organization / team / role / complementary; do not omit
5. **development Develop**: learning / growth / path / transition; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from value → structure → development → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: headcount only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **and talent link**: people + talent co-build
13. **and culture link**: people + culture co-build
14. **and leadership link**: people + leadership co-build
15. **and human-resources link**: people + HR co-build
16. **and organization link**: people + organization co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Workday / SuccessFactors / Lattice / Culture Amp / 15Five
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must people strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can default solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: people the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
