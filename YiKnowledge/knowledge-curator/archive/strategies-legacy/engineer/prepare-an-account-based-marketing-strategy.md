## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: account + team + content + channel + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by ACV + win rate + pipeline + customer LTV; not sloganeering
3. **Account Account**: ICP + tier + buying-committee + intent + tech-stack; do not omit
4. **Team Team**: marketing + SDR + AE + CSM + SE + RACI; do not omit
5. **Content Content**: customized + case + data + stories + interactive; do not omit
6. **Channel Channel**: email + direct mail + ads + social + events + 1:1 conversation; do not omit
7. **Measurement Measure**: account penetration + reach + engagement + pipeline + won-ACV; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from account → team → content → channel → measurement progressive; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with demand-generation**: ABM + demand co-build
13. **Link with customer-segmentation**: ABM + segmentation co-build
14. **Link with sales**: ABM + sales co-build
15. **Link with content**: ABM + content co-build
16. **Link with revenue-ops**: ABM + revenue co-build
17. **Toolchain**: ABM-Framework / 6sense / Demandbase / Engagio / Terminus / RollWorks / LinkedIn / Bombora / ZoomInfo / Clearbit
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must ABM; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can inbound solve; if solvable do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / sales cadence / business) 
23. **Occam**: ABM the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
