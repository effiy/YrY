## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: process + data + resources + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by risk + trust + speed + cost + compliance; not sloganeering
3. **Process**: contract / dispute / compliance; do not omit
4. **Data**: knowledge base + template + benchmark + measurement; do not omit
5. **Resources**: internal + external + budget + evaluation; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from process → data → resources → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: contract review is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with legal-operations**: ops + legal-ops co-built
13. **Link with contract-operations**: legal + contract ops co-built
14. **Link with ip-operations**: legal + IP ops co-built
15. **Link with litigation**: legal + litigation co-built
16. **Link with legal-tech**: legal + legal-tech co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Clio / MyCase / Smokeball / NetDocuments / iManage
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must a legal-ops strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (risk / trust / speed / cost)
23. **Occam**: legal ops the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
