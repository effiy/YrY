## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: design + procurement + communication + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Design**: health / retirement / family / flexible / financial; do not omit
4. **Procure**: vendor / negotiation / contract / renewal; do not omit
5. **Communicate**: advocacy / onboarding / option / assessment; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from design → procurement → communication → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: program count is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with benefits**: employee benefits + benefits co-build
13. **Link with employee-wellbeing**: employee benefits + employee wellbeing co-build
14. **Link with employee-experience**: employee benefits + employee experience co-build
15. **Link with compensation**: employee benefits + compensation co-build
16. **Link with employee-retention**: employee benefits + employee retention co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Workday / Dayforce / Benefitfocus / PlanSource / Gusto
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why an employee benefits strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by defaults; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: employee benefits, the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
