## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: agenda + resolutions + reports + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Agenda**: meetings / proposals / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Resolution**: voting / minutes / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Report**: operations / risk / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from agenda -> resolutions -> reports -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with corporate-secretary**: BoardGovernance + CorporateSecretary co-build
13. **Link with corporate-governance**: BoardGovernance + CorporateGovernance co-build
14. **Link with legal-entity-management**: BoardGovernance + LegalEntityManagement co-build
15. **Link with compliance-management**: BoardGovernance + ComplianceManagement co-build
16. **Link with risk**: BoardGovernance + Risk co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Diligent / BoardEffect / OnBoard / Nasdaq Boardvantage / Aprio
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why BoardGovernance is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by ad-hoc meetings; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler BoardGovernance is the better; cut redundant agenda items
