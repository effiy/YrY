## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: rules + routes + escalation + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by routing speed + conversion + revenue + experience + efficiency; not sloganeering
3. **Rules**: score / ICP / region / size / industry; do not omit
4. **Routes**: SDR / AE / partner / self-service / channel; do not omit
5. **Escalation**: SLA / timeout / escalation / handoff / recycle; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: routing speed + conversion + revenue + experience + efficiency; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from rules → routes → escalation → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Links to lead-scoring**: Routing + Scoring co-build
13. **Links to territory-plan**: Routing + Territory co-build
14. **Links to sales-process**: Routing + Sales Process co-build
15. **Links to account-tiering**: Routing + Tiering co-build
16. **Links to marketing-attribution**: Routing + Attribution co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce / Marketo / LeanData / Chili Piper / 6sense / Outreach
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why Lead Routing is a must; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can sales self-claim solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / speed / experience)
23. **Occam**: Routing — the simpler the better; cut redundant rules
