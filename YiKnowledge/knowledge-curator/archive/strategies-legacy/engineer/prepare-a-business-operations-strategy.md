## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: alignment + process + data + tooling + measurement; none missing
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by revenue + speed + retention + cost + risk; not sloganeering
3. **Alignment**: strategy + budget + KPI + OKR + cadence; do not omit
4. **Process**: budget / revenue / titles / process / retirement; do not omit
5. **Data**: revenue / cost / people / contracts / projects; do not omit
6. **Tooling**: BI / FP&A / HR / IT / integration; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: revenue + speed + retention + cost + risk; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from alignment → process → data → tooling → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with revenue-operations**: BizOps + RevOps co-built
13. **Link with marketing-ops**: BizOps + MOps co-built
14. **Link with sales-ops**: BizOps + SOps co-built
15. **Link with CS-ops**: BizOps + CS-ops co-built
16. **Link with product-ops**: BizOps + POps co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Anaplan / Adaptive / NetSuite / Workday / Coupa / DocuSign / Tableau / Looker / Snowflake / dbt
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a BizOps strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can department autonomy solve; if solvable, don't introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / speed / business) 
23. **Occam**: BizOps the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
