## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: booking + safety + expense + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + cost + risk; not sloganeering
3. **Booking**: flight / hotel / car / visa / itinerary; do not omit
4. **Safety**: alert / tracking / emergency / insurance / reporting; do not omit
5. **Expense**: standard / reimbursement / audit / refund / corporate account; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + cost + risk; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from booking → safety → expense → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with employee-experience**: Travel + EX co-built
13. **Link with remote-work-policy**: Travel + Remote co-built
14. **Link with vendor-management**: Travel + Vendor co-built
15. **Link with budget-planning**: Travel + Budget co-built
16. **Link with sustainability**: Travel + Sustainability co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Concur / TripIt / TravelPerk / Navan / Expensify
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must Travel; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can video meetings solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / cost)
23. **Occam**: Travel the simpler the better; cut redundant clauses
