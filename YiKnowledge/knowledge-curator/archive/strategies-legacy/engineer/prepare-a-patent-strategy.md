## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: invention + assessment + filing + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **invention Invent**: idea / disclosure / assessment / priority; do not omit
4. **assessment Assess**: novelty / inventiveness / utility / risk; do not omit
5. **filing File**: drafting / response / enforcement / overseas; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from invention → assessment → filing → governance → measurement progressive; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: filing is only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with ip**: patent + IP co-build
13. **link with trademark**: patent + trademark co-build
14. **link with copyright**: patent + copyright co-build
15. **link with trade-secret**: patent + trade secret co-build
16. **link with legal**: patent + legal co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Anaqua / CPA Global / PatSnap / Innography / LexisNexis PatentSight
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must patent strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can default solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: patent the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
