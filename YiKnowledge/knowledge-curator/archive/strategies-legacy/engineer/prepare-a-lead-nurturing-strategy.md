## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + content + cadence + trigger + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by nurture → MQL + MQL + revenue + CAC + timeliness; not sloganeering
3. **Audience**: ICP + stage + behavior + interest + intent; do not omit
4. **Content**: email / landing page / resources / case study / retire; do not omit
5. **Cadence**: daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly / yearly; do not omit
6. **Trigger**: behavior / time / score / stage / hand-raise; do not omit
7. **Measure**: nurture → MQL + MQL + revenue + CAC + timeliness; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: gradual from audience → content → cadence → trigger → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with lead-scoring**: nurture + scoring co-built
13. **Link with mql-to-sql**: nurture + MQL→SQL co-built
14. **Link with demand-generation**: nurture + funnel co-built
15. **Link with content-marketing**: nurture + content co-built
16. **Link with marketing-operations**: nurture + marketing operations co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Marketo / Hubspot / Pardot / Eloqua / Customer.io / Iterable / ActiveCampaign / Klaviyo
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must lead nurturing; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by one-off campaign; if solvable don't introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / CAC)
23. **Occam**: lead nurturing simpler is better; cut redundant steps
