## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: cadence + data + decision + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by alignment + speed + risk + decision + trust; not sloganeering
3. **cadence Cadence**: month start / mid / end / quarter start / end; do not omit
4. **data Data**: finance / customer / operations / talent / strategy; do not omit
5. **decision Decision**: exception / resource / priority / risk / action; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: alignment + speed + risk + decision + trust; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from cadence → data → decision → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: process has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **and okr link**: MBR + OKR co-build
13. **and quarterly-okr-review link**: MBR + QBR co-build
14. **and kpi link**: MBR + KPI co-build
15. **and leadership link**: MBR + Leadership co-build
16. **and communication-cadence link**: MBR + Cadence co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Polaris / Quantive / Stragetic / Asana / Notion
18. **publicly queryable**: process everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must MBR; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can a one-off retrospective solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy process
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after process (alignment / speed / risk / trust)
23. **Occam**: MBR the simpler the better; cut redundant agenda items
