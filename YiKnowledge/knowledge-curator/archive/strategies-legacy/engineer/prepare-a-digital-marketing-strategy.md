## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: channel + content + automation + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by adoption + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Channel**: SEO / SEM / social / email / content; do not omit
4. **Content**: theme / format / cadence / personalization / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Automation**: trigger / journey / scoring / route / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: adoption + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from channel -> content -> automation -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with marketing-operations**: DM + MOps co-build
13. **Link with content-strategy**: DM + Content co-build
14. **Link with demand-generation**: DM + Demand co-build
15. **Link with growth-strategy**: DM + Growth co-build
16. **Link with brand-identity**: DM + Brand co-build
17. **Toolchain**: HubSpot / Marketo / Eloqua / Pardot / Adobe Experience Cloud
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must DM; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: see how much organic can solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (adoption / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: DM the simpler the better; cut redundant channels
