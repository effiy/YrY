## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: store + ecom + omnichannel + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **store Store**: operations / revenue / closed-loop; do not omit
4. **ecom Ecom**: traffic / conversion / closed-loop; do not omit
5. **omnichannel Omnichannel**: inventory / fulfillment / closed-loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from store → ecom → omnichannel → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with store-operations**: RetailOperations + StoreOperations co-built
13. **link with merchandising**: RetailOperations + Merchandising co-built
14. **link with store-format**: RetailOperations + StoreFormat co-built
15. **link with order-fulfillment**: RetailOperations + OrderFulfillment co-built
16. **link with customer-experience**: RetailOperations + CustomerExperience co-built
17. **toolchain**: Salesforce Commerce / Shopify / SAP S/4HANA / Oracle / Manhattan
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must RetailOperations; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by single-store single-channel; if solvable don't introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: RetailOperations the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
