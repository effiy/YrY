## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: collection + cleansing + analysis + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Collect**: ERP / bank / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Cleanse**: classify / standardize / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Analyze**: classify / opportunity / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: gradual from collection -> cleansing -> analysis -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with category**: SpendAnalysis + Category co-build
13. **Link with strategic-sourcing**: SpendAnalysis + StrategicSourcing co-build
14. **Link with procurement**: SpendAnalysis + Procurement co-build
15. **Link with vendor-performance**: SpendAnalysis + VendorPerformance co-build
16. **Link with supplier-diversity**: SpendAnalysis + SupplierDiversity co-build
17. **Toolchain**: SAP Ariba Spend Analysis / Coupa Spend Guard / Sievo / Spend360 / Rosslyn Analytics
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must SpendAnalysis; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by relying on Excel; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler SpendAnalysis the better; cut redundant dimensions
