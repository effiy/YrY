## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + content + cadence + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by decision + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Audience**: executives / HRB / business / employees; do not omit
4. **Content**: metrics + interpretation + action + risk; do not omit
5. **Cadence**: daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly / annual; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from audience -> content -> cadence -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: tables are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with hr-metrics**: reporting + metrics co-built
13. **Link with hr-analytics**: reporting + analytics co-built
14. **Link with hr-data**: reporting + data co-built
15. **Link with hr-operations**: reporting + operations co-built
16. **Link with hr-shared-services**: reporting + shared services co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Workday Reporting / SAP SuccessFactors / Visier / OneModel / Tableau
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must HR reporting strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (decision / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: HR reporting the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
