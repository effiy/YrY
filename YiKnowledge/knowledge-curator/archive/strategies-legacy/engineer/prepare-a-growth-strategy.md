## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: acquisition + activation + retention + revenue + referral; no missing dimension
2. **User-value driven**: prioritize by user scenario + LTV + CAC + conversion funnel; not sloganeering
3. **Acquisition**: channels + ads + SEO + viral; do not omit
4. **Activation**: onboarding + Aha moment + time-to-value; do not omit
5. **Retention**: core loop + notifications + recall; do not omit
6. **Revenue**: pricing + upgrade + churn prevention; do not omit
7. **Referral**: invites + viral coefficient + NPS; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from north-star → core loop → funnel → experiment → scale; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with roadmap**: growth + roadmap co-built
13. **Link with metrics**: growth + metrics co-built
14. **Link with A/B**: growth + experiment co-built
15. **Link with experiment**: growth + validation co-built
16. **Link with content**: growth + content co-built
17. **Link with dev-marketing**: growth + marketing co-built
18. **Link with tracking**: growth + instrumentation co-built
19. **Toolchain**: Amplitude / Mixpanel / PostHog / GrowthBook / Optimizely
20. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
21. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
22. **First principles**: why must growth strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
23. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by pure product strength; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
24. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / complexity / experience / business)
25. **Occam**: growth the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
