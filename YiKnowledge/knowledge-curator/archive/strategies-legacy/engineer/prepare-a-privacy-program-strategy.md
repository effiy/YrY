## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: notice + consent + exercise + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **notice Notice**: collection / purpose / closure; do not omit
4. **consent Consent**: mechanism / trace / closure; do not omit
5. **exercise Exercise**: access / delete / closure; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from notice → consent → exercise → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **and privacy-management link**: PrivacyProgram + PrivacyManagement co-build
13. **and data-protection link**: PrivacyProgram + DataProtection co-build
14. **and compliance-management link**: PrivacyProgram + ComplianceManagement co-build
15. **and data-governance link**: PrivacyProgram + DataGovernance co-build
16. **and regulatory-compliance link**: PrivacyProgram + RegulatoryCompliance co-build
17. **Toolchain**: OneTrust / TrustArc / Securiti / BigID / PrivacyEngine
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must PrivacyProgram; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can privacy notice solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: PrivacyProgram the simpler the better; cut redundant nodes
