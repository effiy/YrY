## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identify + prepare + deploy + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Identify**: role / gap / quantity / cadence / boundary; do not omit
4. **Prepare**: training / tools / process / resources / validation; do not omit
5. **Deploy**: scheduling / onboarding / monitoring / feedback / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from identify → prepare → deploy → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with workforce-planning**: WR + Planning co-built
13. **Link with onboarding**: WR + Onboarding co-built
14. **Link with training-and-enablement**: WR + T&E co-built
15. **Link with change-management**: WR + Change co-built
16. **Link with learning-and-development**: WR + L&D co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Workday HCM / Gloat / Eightfold / Visier / SAP SuccessFactors
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why WR is a must; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can onboarding solve; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler WR is the better; cut redundant steps
