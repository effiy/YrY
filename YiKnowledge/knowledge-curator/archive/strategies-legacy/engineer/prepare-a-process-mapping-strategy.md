## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: scope + notation + publishing + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Scope**: end-to-end / cross-functional / scenario / role; do not omit
4. **Notation**: BPMN / UML / process diagrams / value stream; do not omit
5. **Publishing**: versioning / access / embedding / training; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from scope -> notation -> publishing -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: diagramming is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with process-design**: mapping + design co-built
13. **Link with process-improvement**: mapping + improvement co-built
14. **Link with business-process**: mapping + business process co-built
15. **Link with process-mining**: mapping + process mining co-built
16. **Link with business-architecture**: mapping + business architecture co-built
17. **Toolchain**: SAP Signavio / ARIS / IBM Blueworks / Lucid / Miro
18. **Publicly queryable**: everyone can look up the strategy; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a mapping strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: mapping the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
