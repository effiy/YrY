## Action recommendations

1. **five dimensions**: requirement + supply + budget + Governance + Measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by people efficiency + speed + quality + cost + retention; no empty slogans
3. **requirement Demand**: business / role / skills / time / geography; no leakage
4. **supply Supply**: internal / external / inflow / outflow / inventory; no leakage
5. **budget Budget**: headcount / compensation / benefits / training / temporary; no leakage
6. **Governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; no leakage
7. **Measurement Measure**: people efficiency + speed + quality + cost + retention; no leakage
8. **Not one-shot**: from requirement → supply → budget → Governance → Measurement gradual; no skipping levels
9. **no report-ism**: report is just the start; not the end
10. **no empty slogans**: every principle must mark implementation evidence; no vagueness
11. **Versioned**: strategy is versioned; evolution is traceable
12. **link with talent-acquisition**: Workforce + TA Co-build
13. **link with org-design**: Workforce + Org Design Co-build
14. **link with recruiting**: Workforce + Recruiting Co-build
15. **link with budget-planning**: Workforce + Budget Co-build
16. **link with DEI**: Workforce + DEI Co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Workday / Anaplan / SAP SuccessFactors / Lattice / ChartHop
18. **Publicly discoverable**: strategy is publicly discoverable; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: Evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must Workforce Planning; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion**: how much can reactive recruitment solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequence after strategy (cost / Risk / retention / culture)
