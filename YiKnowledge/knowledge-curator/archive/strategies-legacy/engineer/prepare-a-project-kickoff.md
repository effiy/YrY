## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: background + goal + scope + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by alignment + speed + risk + trust + cost; not sloganeering
3. **background Context**: business pain / strategic anchor / stakeholders / assumption / constraint; do not omit
4. **goal Objective**: business / measurement / milestone / success / failure; do not omit
5. **scope Scope**: in / out / dependencies / assumption / constraint; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: alignment + speed + risk + trust + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from background → goal → scope → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: process has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **links with handoff**: Kickoff + Handoff co-build
13. **links with okr**: Kickoff + OKR co-build
14. **links with communication-cadence**: Kickoff + Cadence co-build
15. **links with rfc**: Kickoff + RFC co-build
16. **links with stakeholder-engagement**: Kickoff + Stakeholder co-build
17. **toolchain**: Notion / Asana / Linear / Confluence / Jira
18. **publicly queryable**: process everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why Kickoff is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can word-of-mouth solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy process
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the process (alignment / speed / risk / trust)
23. **Occam**: Kickoff — the simpler the better; cut redundant agenda items
