## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: demand + persona + sourcing + development + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by revenue-per-capita + key-role supply + retention + business supply; not sloganeering
3. **Demand**: business + strategy + gap + seasonality + retirement; do not omit
4. **Persona**: capability + experience + culture + motivation + values; do not omit
5. **Sourcing**: referral + campus + social + talent pool + talent map; do not omit
6. **Develop**: path + high-potential + succession + certification + transfer; do not omit
7. **Measure**: revenue-per-capita + retention + recruiting cost + candidate NPS + key-role fill rate; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from demand -> persona -> sourcing -> development -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with hr**: talent + HR co-built
13. **Link with learning-and-development**: talent + L&D co-built
14. **Link with compensation**: talent + compensation co-built
15. **Link with culture**: talent + culture co-built
16. **Link with product-strategy**: talent + product strategy co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Eightfold / Gloat / Seekout / LinkedIn Talent Insights / Beamery / Phenom / Greenhouse / Lever / Ashby
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must talent strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by passive recruiting; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / retention / business) 
23. **Occam**: talent strategy the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
