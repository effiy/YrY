## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Metrics + retrospective + improvement + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Metrics KPI**: delivery / quality / cost / service; do not omit
4. **retrospective Review**: monthly / quarterly / yearly / incident; do not omit
5. **improvement Improve**: CAPA / upgrade / exit; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from metrics → retrospective → improvement → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: scoring is only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with supplier-management**: performance + supplier management co-built
13. **Link with supplier-qualification**: performance + qualification co-built
14. **Link with supplier-audit**: performance + audit co-built
15. **Link with procurement**: performance + procurement co-built
16. **Link with supplier-relationship**: performance + relationship co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Coupa SPM / Aravo / HICX / supplier-IQ / SAP Ariba
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must performance strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: performance the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
