## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: penetration + frequency + depth + path + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by adoption rate + retention + LTV + expansion; not sloganeering
3. **Penetration Penetration**: account + team + feature + workflow + role; do not omit
4. **Frequency Frequency**: DAU + WAU + MAU + frequency distribution + habit; do not omit
5. **Depth Depth**: core + advanced + integration + data + custom; do not omit
6. **Path Path**: first + second + third + workflow + conversion; do not omit
7. **Measurement Measure**: adoption rate + frequency + depth + path + funnel; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from penetration → frequency → depth → path → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with activation**: adoption + activation co-built
13. **Link with onboarding**: adoption + onboarding co-built
14. **Link with customer-success**: adoption + customer success co-built
15. **Link with product-analytics**: adoption + measurement co-built
16. **Link with customer-education**: adoption + education co-built
17. **Tooling**: Adoption-Framework / Pendo / Appcues / Userflow / Whatfix / WalkMe / Gainsight PX / Mixpanel / Amplitude / Heap
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why an adoption strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by natural usage; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / retention / business)
23. **Occam**: adoption the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
