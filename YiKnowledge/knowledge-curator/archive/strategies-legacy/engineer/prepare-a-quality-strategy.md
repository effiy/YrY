## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: design + control + improvement + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Design**: requirements / standards / failure / mistake-proofing; do not omit
4. **Control**: inspection / sampling / SPC / blocking; do not omit
5. **Improve**: root cause / actions / verification / standardization; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from design → control → improvement → governance → measurement progressive; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: inspection is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with quality-management**: quality + system co-built
13. **Link with quality-control**: quality + control co-built
14. **Link with quality-assurance**: quality + assurance co-built
15. **Link with continuous-improvement**: quality + continuous improvement co-built
16. **Link with iso-9001**: quality + ISO co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Minitab / JMP / InfinityQS / SPC XL / TrackWise
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a quality strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: quality the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
