## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: stage + conversion + governance + entry + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by win-rate + cycle-time + ACV + revenue + renewal; not sloganeering
3. **Stage**: prospect / qualify / discover / propose / negotiate / close; do not omit
4. **Conversion**: inter-stage conversion rate + speed + funnel + cohort; do not omit
5. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
6. **Entry**: per-stage definition + entry criteria + exit criteria + measurement; do not omit
7. **Measure**: win-rate + cycle-time + ACV + revenue + renewal; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from stage -> conversion -> governance -> entry -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with sales-pipeline**: sales process + sales pipeline co-built
13. **Link with sales-methodology**: sales process + sales methodology co-built
14. **Link with sales-playbook**: sales process + sales playbook co-built
15. **Link with opportunity-management**: sales process + opportunity management co-built
16. **Link with deal-review**: sales process + deal-review co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce / Clari / Gong / Outreach / Salesloft / Boost-UP
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must sales process; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: see how much free-form can solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: sales process the simpler the better; cut redundant stages
