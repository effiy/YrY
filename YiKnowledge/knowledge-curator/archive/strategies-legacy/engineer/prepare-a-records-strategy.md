## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: classification + retention + disposition + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Classify**: type / value / risk / period; do not omit
4. **Retain**: years / trigger / storage / backup; do not omit
5. **Dispose**: expire / destroy / convert / forensics; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: gradual from classification → retention → disposition → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: archives are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with information-governance**: records + information governance co-built
13. **Link with e-discovery**: records + e-discovery co-built
14. **Link with compliance**: records + compliance co-built
15. **Link with legal**: records + legal co-built
16. **Link with data-lifecycle**: records + data lifecycle co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Microsoft Purview / OpenText Records / Records365 / Iron Mountain / Hyland
18. **Publicly queryable**: everyone can look up the strategy; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a records strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: simpler records are better; cut redundant layers
