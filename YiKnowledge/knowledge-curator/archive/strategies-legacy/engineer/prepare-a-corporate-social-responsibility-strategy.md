## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: environment + social + governance + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; no empty slogans
3. **Environment Environmental**: emissions / resources / closed loop; none missing
4. **Social Social**: employees / community / closed loop; none missing
5. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / docs / drift; none missing
6. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; none missing
7. **Not one-shot**: progressive from environment → social → governance → governance → measurement; no skipping levels
8. **Not report-only**: reports are only the starting point; not the endpoint
9. **No empty slogans**: every principle must have landed evidence; no ambiguity
10. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
11. **Link to corporate-citizenship**: CSR + Citizenship co-build
12. **Link to corporate-sustainability**: CSR + Sustainability co-build
13. **Link to esg**: CSR + ESG co-build
14. **Link to stakeholder-engagement**: CSR + StakeholderEngagement co-build
15. **Link to corporate-philanthropy**: CSR + Philanthropy co-build
16. **Link to corporate-foundation**: CSR + Foundation co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Workiva / Enablon / Diligent / Sphera / CSRware
18. **Publicly accessible**: strategy accessible to everyone; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must we have CSR; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion**: how much can be solved by relying on PR; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: CSR the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
