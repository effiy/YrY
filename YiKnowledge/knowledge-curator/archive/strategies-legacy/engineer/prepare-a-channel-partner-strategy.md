## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: recruit + enable + empower + co-sell + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by joint ARR + revenue share + partner NPS + retention + speed; not sloganeering
3. **recruit Recruit**: profile + source + qualification + agreement + onboarding; do not omit
4. **enable Onboard**: training + certification + tooling + contact + milestones; do not omit
5. **empower Enable**: marketing kit / sales kit / demo / pricing / quote; do not omit
6. **co-sell Co-sell**: lead registration + routing + joint deal + revenue share + speed; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: joint ARR + revenue share + partner NPS + retention + speed; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from recruit → enable → empower → co-sell → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with partnership**: channel + partnership co-built
13. **link with partner-program**: channel + program co-built
14. **link with partner-success**: channel + success co-built
15. **link with co-marketing**: channel + joint marketing co-built
16. **link with channel**: channel + full-channel co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Impartner / Allbound / PartnerStack / Channeltapest / Crossbeam / Reveal / Salesforce-PRM / Zinfi
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must channel partner strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can direct sales solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / channel conflict / business) 
23. **Occam**: channel partners the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
