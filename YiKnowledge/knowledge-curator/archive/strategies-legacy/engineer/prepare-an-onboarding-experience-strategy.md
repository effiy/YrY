## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: pre-onboard + day 1 + integrate + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **pre-onboard Pre-onboard**: welcome / documentation / equipment / systems; do not omit
4. **day 1 Day 1**: check-in / introduction / buddy / tools; do not omit
5. **integrate Integrate**: 30/60/90 / goal / feedback / probation conversion; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from pre-onboard → day 1 → integrate → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: process count only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Linked with employee-onboarding**: onboarding experience + employee onboarding co-built
13. **Linked with employee-experience**: onboarding experience + employee experience co-built
14. **Linked with candidate-experience**: onboarding experience + candidate experience co-built
15. **Linked with employee-engagement**: onboarding experience + employee engagement co-built
16. **Linked with employee-retention**: onboarding experience + employee retention co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Enboarder / Sapling / BambooHR / Workday / Microsoft Viva
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must onboarding experience strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: onboarding experience the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
