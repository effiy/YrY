## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: recruit + enable + conflict + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Recruit**: profile / qualification / agreement / onboarding; do not omit
4. **Enable**: training / materials / commission / systems; do not omit
5. **Conflict**: registration / segmentation / arbitration / escalation; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from recruit -> enable -> conflict -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: revenue share only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with partner-sales**: channel + partner sales co-build
13. **Link with channel-partner**: channel + partner co-build
14. **Link with reseller**: channel + reseller co-build
15. **Link with distributor**: channel + distributor co-build
16. **Link with channel-marketing**: channel + channel marketing co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce PRM / Channel360 / Impartner / Allbound / Zift Solutions
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must channel sales strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: see how much defaults can solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: channel sales the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
