## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: booking + scheduling + operations + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Booking**: venue / equipment / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Scheduling**: schedule / conflicts / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Servicing**: personnel / catering / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from booking → scheduling → operations → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with stadium-operations**: VenueManagement + StadiumOperations co-build
13. **Link with facility-management**: VenueManagement + FacilityManagement co-build
14. **Link with event-marketing**: VenueManagement + EventMarketing co-build
15. **Link with property-management**: VenueManagement + PropertyManagement co-build
16. **Link with customer-experience**: VenueManagement + CustomerExperience co-build
17. **Toolchain**: VenueOps / Venuetize / Eventbrite / Bizzabo / Cvent
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy accessible to everyone; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must VenueManagement; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can paper contracts solve; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler VenueManagement is, the better; cut redundant process
