## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Identify + analyze + gap + trend + response; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: Prioritize by business growth + market share + risk + opportunity; not sloganeering
3. **Identify**: direct + indirect + potential + substitute multiple types; do not omit
4. **Analyze**: product + pricing + channel + marketing + user + finance + tech + organization; do not omit
5. **Gap**: feature gap + experience gap + price gap + channel gap + brand gap; do not omit
6. **Trend**: industry trend + tech trend + user trend + policy trend + business-model trend; do not omit
7. **Response**: differentiation + follow + acquisition + partnership + defense + offense; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: Progress from identify → analyze → gap → trend → response; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with product-strategy**: competitors + product strategy co-built
13. **Link with market-research**: competitors + market co-built
14. **Link with understand-competitors**: competitors + industry co-built
15. **Link with go-to-market**: competitors + GTM co-built
16. **Link with product-analytics**: competitors + measurement co-built
17. **Link with pitch**: competitors + fundraising co-built
18. **Toolchain**: Crunchbase / SimilarWeb / App Annie / Sensor Tower / Ahrefs / Semrush / PitchBook / public financials / user interviews / mystery shopping
19. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
20. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
21. **First principles**: why must competitive analysis; worst consequence of not doing it
22. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by building products on intuition; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
23. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / growth / business)
24. **Occam**: competitive analysis — the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
