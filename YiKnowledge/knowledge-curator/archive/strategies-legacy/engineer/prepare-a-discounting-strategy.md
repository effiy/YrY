## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: policy + thresholds + approval + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by win-rate + ACV + margin + cycle-time + renewal; not sloganeering
3. **Policy**: volume / strategic / competitive / renewal / startup; do not omit
4. **Threshold**: 5% / 10% / 20% / 30% / custom; do not omit
5. **Approval**: AE / manager / deal-desk / VP / CFO; do not omit
6. **Governance**: cadence / owner / escalation / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: win-rate + ACV + margin + cycle-time + renewal; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from policy → thresholds → approval → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with pricing**: discounting + pricing co-built
13. **Link with deal-desk**: discounting + deal-desk co-built
14. **Link with proposal**: discounting + proposal documentation co-built
15. **Link with deal-review**: discounting + deal-review co-built
16. **Link with cpq**: discounting + CPQ co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce-CPQ / Conga / PandaDoc / Clari / Boost-UP
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must discounting strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can AE freehand solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: discounting the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
