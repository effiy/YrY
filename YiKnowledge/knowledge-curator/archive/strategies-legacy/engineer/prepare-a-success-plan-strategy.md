## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Goal + milestone + action + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by value realization + renewal + expansion + retention + experience; not sloganeering
3. **Goal Goals**: business / quantified / stage / time / owner; do not omit
4. **Milestone Milestones**: stage / time / validation / evidence / risk; do not omit
5. **Action Actions**: playbook / training / integration / review / upgrade; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: value realization + renewal + expansion + retention + experience; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from Goal → milestone → action → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Links with customer-success**: Success Plan + CS co-built
13. **Links with customer-health**: Success Plan + Health Score co-built
14. **Links with account-tiering**: Success Plan + Tiering co-built
15. **Links with customer-lifecycle**: Success Plan + Lifecycle co-built
16. **Links with customer-onboarding**: Success Plan + Onboarding co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Gainsight / Catalyst / ChurnZero / Salesforce / ClientSuccess
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must Success Plan; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by relying on reactive support; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / renewal / retention)
23. **Occam**: Success Plan the simpler the better; cut redundant sections
