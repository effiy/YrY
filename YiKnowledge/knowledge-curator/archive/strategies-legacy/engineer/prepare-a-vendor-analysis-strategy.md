## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: capability + finance + strategy + risk + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by analysis-impact revenue + win-rate + renewal + NRR + decision efficiency; not sloganeering
3. **Capability**: product / tech / service / integration / roadmap; none missing
4. **Financial**: revenue / valuation / growth / profitability / cash; none missing
5. **Strategic**: direction / investment / M&A / partnerships / risk; none missing
6. **Risk**: finance / product / strategy / compliance / operations; none missing
7. **Measure**: analysis-impact revenue + win-rate + renewal + NRR + decision efficiency; none missing
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from capability → finance → strategy → risk → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with competitor-analysis**: vendor + competitor analysis co-build
13. **Link with competitive-intelligence**: vendor + competitive intelligence co-build
14. **Link with competitive-positioning**: vendor + competitive positioning co-build
15. **Link with vendor-operations**: vendor + vendor operations co-build
16. **Link with buy-vs-build**: vendor + build co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Gartner / Forrester / G2 / Capterra / Klue / Crayon / Salesforce
18. **Publicly accessible**: strategy accessible to everyone; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must vendor analysis; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion**: how much can sales intuition solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention) 
23. **Occam**: vendor analysis the simpler the better; cut redundant dimensions
