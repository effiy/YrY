## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Channel + conversion + measurement + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Channel**: organic / paid / referral / direct / channel; do not omit
4. **Convert**: landing / register / activate / pay; do not omit
5. **Funnel**: CAC / LTV / conversion / speed; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from channel → conversion → measurement → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: traffic numbers are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Links with customer-activation**: customer acquisition + customer activation co-build
13. **Links with growth-marketing**: customer acquisition + growth marketing co-build
14. **Links with demand-generation**: customer acquisition + demand generation co-build
15. **Links with lead-generation**: customer acquisition + lead generation co-build
16. **Links with customer-ltv**: customer acquisition + customer lifetime value co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Google Ads / Meta Ads / LinkedIn Ads / HubSpot / Salesforce
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must customer acquisition strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by relying on defaults; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: customer acquisition the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
