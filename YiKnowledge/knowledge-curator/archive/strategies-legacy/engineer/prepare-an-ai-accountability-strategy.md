## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: role + decision + impact + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Role**: owner / approver / operator / auditor / audit trail; do not omit
4. **Decision**: admission / launch / modify / decommission / closed-loop; do not omit
5. **Impact**: business / user / society / legal / closed-loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress gradually from role -> decision -> impact -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Work with ai-transparency**: AIAccountability + AITransparency co-built
13. **Work with ai-governance**: AIAccountability + AIGovernance co-built
14. **Work with ai-ethics**: AIAccountability + AIEthics co-built
15. **Work with ai-safety**: AIAccountability + AISafety co-built
16. **Work with ai-explainability**: AIAccountability + XAI co-built
17. **Toolchain**: RACI Matrix / Conform / Credo AI / Holistic AI / Saidot
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why AIAccountability is needed; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by after-the-fact blame; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: AIAccountability the simpler the better; cut redundant roles
