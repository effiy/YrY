## Action recommendations

1. **five dimensions**: channel + Process + team + Governance + Measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: set priority by efficiency + trust + speed + Risk + cost; no empty slogans
3. **channel Channel**: phone / email / closed loop; no leakage
4. **Process Process**: SLA / escalation / closed loop; no leakage
5. **team Team**: people / training / closed loop; no leakage
6. **Governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; no leakage
7. **Measurement Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + Risk + cost; no leakage
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from channel → Process → team → Governance → Measurement; no skipping levels
9. **no report-ism**: report is just the start; not the end
10. **no empty slogans**: every principle must mark implementation evidence; no vagueness
11. **Versioned**: strategy is versioned; evolution is traceable
12. **link with customer-care**: Service + Care co-build
13. **link with customer-support-ops**: Service + SupportOps co-build
14. **link with customer-success**: Service + CS co-build
15. **link with customer-experience**: Service + Experience co-build
16. **link with customer-contact**: Service + Contact co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Zendesk / Salesforce Service Cloud / Freshdesk / Intercom / ServiceNow
18. **Publicly discoverable**: strategy is publicly discoverable; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must CustomerService; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion**: how much can FAQ solve; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequence after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / Risk)
23. **Occam's razor**: CustomerService simpler is better; cut redundant layers
