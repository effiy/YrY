## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + content + cadence + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by community revenue contribution + retention + acquisition + NPS + UGC; not sloganeering
3. **Audience**: customer / developer / partner / product / fan; do not omit
4. **Content**: posts / cases / Q&A / UGC / events; do not omit
5. **Cadence**: daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly / events; do not omit
6. **Governance**: rules + moderators + upgrade + retirement + incentives; do not omit
7. **Measure**: community revenue contribution + retention + acquisition + NPS + UGC; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from audience → content → cadence → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with community**: community management + community co-built
13. **Link with customer-advocacy-program**: community management + customer advocacy co-built
14. **Link with customer-engagement**: community management + customer engagement co-built
15. **Link with developer-advocacy**: community management + developer advocacy co-built
16. **Link with social-media**: community management + social media co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Discourse / Slack / Discord / Circle / Vanilla / Khoros / Salesforce / Sprout-Social
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must community management; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can social media solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / revenue / brand)
23. **Occam**: community management the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
