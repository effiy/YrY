## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: environment + social + governance + measurement + disclosure; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by trust + compliance + risk + speed + cost; no empty slogans
3. **Environment**: carbon / water / waste / energy / biodiversity; no leakage
4. **Social**: DEI / human rights / labor / community / customer; no leakage
5. **Governance**: board / ethics / executive compensation / anti-corruption / information security; no leakage
6. **Measurement**: trust + compliance + risk + speed + cost; no leakage
7. **Disclosure**: TCFD / GRI / SASB / CDP / CSRD; no leakage
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from environment -> social -> governance -> measurement -> disclosure; no skipping levels
9. **No report-ism**: report is just the start; not the end
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must mark implementation evidence; no vagueness
11. **Versioned**: strategy is versioned; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with sustainability**: ESG + Sustainability co-build
13. **Link with corporate-governance**: ESG + Governance co-build
14. **Link with compliance**: ESG + Compliance co-build
15. **Link with risk**: ESG + Risk co-build
16. **Link with investor-relations**: ESG + IR co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Watershed / Persefoni / Sinai / Sweep / Workiva
18. **Publicly discoverable**: strategy is publicly discoverable; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: Evolve and update; Not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must ESG; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion**: see how much PR can solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequence after strategy (trust / compliance / risk / speed)
23. **Occam's razor**: ESG simpler is better; cut redundant metrics
