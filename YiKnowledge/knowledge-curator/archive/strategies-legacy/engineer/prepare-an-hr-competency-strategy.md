## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: model + assessment + certification + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Model**: behaviors + levels + evidence; do not omit
4. **Assess**: self-assessment + 360 + assessment center + retrospective; do not omit
5. **Certify**: levels + standards + certification + maintenance; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from model → assessment → certification → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: job roles are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with hr-capability**: competency + capability co-built
13. **Link with competency-model**: HR + general competency co-built
14. **Link with hr-operating-model**: competency + operations co-built
15. **Link with hr-excellence**: competency + excellence co-built
16. **Link with leader-development**: HR + leadership development co-built
17. **Toolchain**: DDI / Korn Ferry / SHL / Hogan / Lominger
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why an HR competency strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: HR competency — the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
