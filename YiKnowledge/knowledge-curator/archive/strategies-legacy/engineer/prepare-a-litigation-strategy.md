## Action recommendations

1. **five dimensions**: prevention + litigation + discovery + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by risk + trust + speed + compliance + cost; not sloganeering
3. **prevention Prevent**: contract / policy / training / monitoring / evaluation; do not omit
4. **litigation Litigate**: strategy / team / documents / trial / settlement; do not omit
5. **discovery Discovery**: preservation / search / review / privilege / production; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: risk + trust + speed + compliance + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from prevention → litigation → discovery → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **and legal-operations linkage**: LT + LegalOps co-built
13. **and investigations linkage**: LT + Investigations co-built
14. **and compliance linkage**: LT + Compliance co-built
15. **and risk linkage**: LT + Risk co-built
16. **and internal-audit linkage**: LT + Audit co-built
17. **toolchain**: Relativity / Logikcull / Everlaw / DISCO / Casepoint
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must LT; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: rely on outsourcing how much can be solved; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (risk / trust / speed / compliance) 
23. **Occam**: LT the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
