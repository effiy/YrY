## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: process + control + system + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by accuracy + timeliness + compliance + cost + decision; not sloganeering
3. **process Process**: O2C / P2P / R2R / FP&A / treasury; do not omit
4. **Control**: SOX / approval / credential / audit trail / audit; do not omit
5. **System**: ERP / billing / reimbursement / tax / BI; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: accuracy + timeliness + compliance + cost + decision; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from process → control → system → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with financial-reporting**: FinOps + Reporting co-built
13. **Link with budget-planning**: FinOps + Budget co-built
14. **Link with deal-desk**: FinOps + Deal Desk co-built
15. **Link with clm**: FinOps + CLM co-built
16. **Link with revenue-operations**: FinOps + RevOps co-built
17. **Toolchain**: NetSuite / Workday / Anaplan / BlackLine / Tipalti
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why FinOps is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by Excel manual; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / compliance / decision)
23. **Occam**: FinOps — the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
