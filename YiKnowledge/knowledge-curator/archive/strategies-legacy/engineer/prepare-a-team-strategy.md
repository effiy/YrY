## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: goal + structure + operations + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Goal**: mission / value / OKR / priority; do not omit
4. **Structure**: role / boundary / collaboration / autonomy; do not omit
5. **Operate**: cadence / decision / communication / retrospective; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from goal -> structure -> operations -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ism**: headcount is only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **linked with team-design**: team + team design co-build
13. **linked with team-development**: team + team development co-build
14. **linked with team-enablement**: team + team enablement co-build
15. **linked with team-topology**: team + team topology co-build
16. **linked with team-communications**: team + team communications co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Lattice / 15Five / Workday / Microsoft Teams / Slack
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must team strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by defaults; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: team the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
