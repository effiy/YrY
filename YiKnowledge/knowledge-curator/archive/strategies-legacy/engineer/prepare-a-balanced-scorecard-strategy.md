## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: perspective + goal + measurement + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by strategy + alignment + speed + risk + trust; not sloganeering
3. **perspective Perspective**: financial / customer / internal / learning & growth; do not omit
4. **goal Objective**: strategic goals / action goals / measurement goals / actions / risk; do not omit
5. **measurement Measure**: leading / lagging / quantitative / qualitative / threshold; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: strategy + alignment + speed + risk + trust; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from perspective → goal → measurement → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **links with okr**: BSC + OKR co-build
13. **links with kpi**: BSC + KPI co-build
14. **links with business-model**: BSC + Business co-build
15. **links with operating-model**: BSC + Operating co-build
16. **links with leadership**: BSC + Leadership co-build
17. **toolchain**: Quantive / ClearPoint / BSC Designer / Power BI / Tableau
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why BSC is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can a single financial KPI solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (strategy / alignment / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: BSC — the simpler the better; cut redundant perspectives
