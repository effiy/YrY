## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: ask + calculate + act + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Ask**: questions / frequency / audience / anonymity; do not omit
4. **Calculate**: promoters / passives / detractors / net score; do not omit
5. **Act**: analysis / prioritization / plan / follow-up; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: response rate + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progress gradually from ask -> calculate -> act -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: score is only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with employee-survey**: eNPS + employee survey co-built
13. **Link with employee-pulse**: eNPS + employee pulse co-built
14. **Link with employee-engagement**: eNPS + employee engagement co-built
15. **Link with employee-feedback**: eNPS + employee feedback co-built
16. **Link with employer-branding**: eNPS + employer branding co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Culture Amp / Glint / Peakon / Lattice / 15Five
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must employee net promoter strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: employee net promoter the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
