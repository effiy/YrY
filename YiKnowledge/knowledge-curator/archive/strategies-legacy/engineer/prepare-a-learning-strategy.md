## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: need + content + delivery + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Need Need**: skills / role / business / strategy; do not omit
4. **Content Content**: courses / resources / path / certification; do not omit
5. **Delivery Deliver**: online / offline / blended / embedded; do not omit
6. **Governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from need → content → delivery → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: courses are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link to talent-development**: learning + development co-build
13. **Link to development**: learning + personal development co-build
14. **Link to people**: learning + people co-build
15. **Link to competency**: learning + competency co-build
16. **Link to organizational-culture**: learning + culture co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Cornerstone / Degreed / LinkedIn Learning / Coursera / Udemy Business
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must we have a learning strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by relying on defaults; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: learning the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
