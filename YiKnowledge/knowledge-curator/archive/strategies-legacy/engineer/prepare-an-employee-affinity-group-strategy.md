## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: theme + empower + impact + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **theme Theme**: sports / arts / tech / reading / public service; do not omit
4. **empower Enable**: budget / tooling / platform / time; do not omit
5. **impact Influence**: business / culture / public service / innovation; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from theme → empower → impact → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: group count only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with employee-resource-group**: affinity group + resource group co-built
13. **link with employee-resource-network**: affinity group + resource network co-built
14. **link with community-engagement**: affinity group + community engagement co-built
15. **link with employee-engagement**: affinity group + employee engagement co-built
16. **link with corporate-culture**: affinity group + corporate culture co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Disco / ERGify / Teambuild / Bonusly / Donut
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must employee affinity group strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: employee affinity groups the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
