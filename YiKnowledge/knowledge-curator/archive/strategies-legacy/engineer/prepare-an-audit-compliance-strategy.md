## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: internal audit + external audit + remediation + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Internal audit**: plan / execute / closed loop; do not omit
4. **External audit**: materials / communication / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Remediation**: defects / tracking / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress gradually from internal audit → external audit → remediation → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with internal-controls**: AuditCompliance + InternalControls co-built
13. **Link with financial-reporting**: AuditCompliance + FinancialReporting co-built
14. **Link with financial-close**: AuditCompliance + FinancialClose co-built
15. **Link with general-ledger**: AuditCompliance + GeneralLedger co-built
16. **Link with tax-management**: AuditCompliance + TaxManagement co-built
17. **Toolchain**: AuditBoard / Workiva / Diligent / TeamMate / LogicGate
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why AuditCompliance is necessary; the worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved with ad-hoc responses; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler AuditCompliance is, the better; cut redundant processes
