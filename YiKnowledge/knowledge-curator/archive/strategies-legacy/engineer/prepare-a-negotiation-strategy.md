## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: bottom line + leverage + cadence + escalation + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by win-rate + ACV + margin + cycle-time + renewal; not sloganeering
3. **Bottom line (BATNA)**: price / terms / time / scope / fallback; do not omit
4. **Leverage**: value / term / volume / case / urgency; do not omit
5. **Cadence**: proposal / counter / mutual / close / sign; do not omit
6. **Escalation**: trigger / path / owner / time window / backup; do not omit
7. **Measure**: win-rate + ACV + margin + cycle-time + renewal; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from bottom line → leverage → cadence → escalation → measurement progressive; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with closing**: negotiation + closing co-built
13. **Link with discounting**: negotiation + discount co-built
14. **Link with deal-review**: negotiation + deal-review co-built
15. **Link with mutual-action-plan**: negotiation + MAP co-built
16. **Link with sales-playbook**: negotiation + sales playbook co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce / Clari / Gong / Boost-UP / People-AI / PandaDoc
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a negotiation strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can AE free-form solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: negotiation the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
