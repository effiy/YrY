## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: environment + social + economic + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Environmental**: emission reduction / resources / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Social**: employees / community / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Economic**: growth / resilience / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from environment → social → economic → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Linked with corporate-social-responsibility**: Sustainability + CSR co-built
13. **Linked with environmental**: Sustainability + Environmental co-built
14. **Linked with carbon-management**: Sustainability + CarbonManagement co-built
15. **Linked with esg**: Sustainability + ESG co-built
16. **Linked with corporate-citizenship**: Sustainability + Citizenship co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Workiva / Enablon / Diligent / Sphera / CSRware
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why Sustainability is needed; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by compliance; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: Sustainability, the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
