## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: liquidity + capital + risk + governance + measurement; no missing dimension.
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by cash + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering.
3. **Liquidity**: cash / credit / forecast / dispatch / contingency; do not omit.
4. **Capital**: equity / debt / hybrid / dividends / buybacks; do not omit.
5. **Risk**: FX / interest rate / credit / liquidity / operational; do not omit.
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit.
7. **Measure**: cash + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit.
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from liquidity → capital → risk → governance → measurement; no skipping.
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end.
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague.
11. **Versioned**: the strategy has versions; evolution is traceable.
12. **Link with cfo**: Treasury + CFO co-build.
13. **Link with financial-reporting**: Treasury + Reporting co-build.
14. **Link with risk**: Treasury + Risk co-build.
15. **Link with budget-planning**: Treasury + Budget co-build.
16. **Link with saas-metrics**: Treasury + Metrics co-build.
17. **Toolchain**: Kyriba / GTreasury / Salo / ION Treasury / FIS Quantum.
18. **Publicly queryable**: the strategy is look-up-able by everyone; not hidden.
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot.
20. **First principles**: why we must do Treasury; the worst consequence of not doing it.
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can bank balances solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy.
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cash / trust / speed / risk).
23. **Occam**: simpler Treasury is better; cut redundant tools.
