## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: mission + team + method + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by speed + trust + risk + cost + differentiation; not sloganeering
3. **Mission**: positioning / ambition / boundary / KPI; do not omit
4. **Team**: internal / external / cross-discipline / rotation; do not omit
5. **Method**: design thinking / lean startup / agile / OKR; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from mission → team → method → governance → measurement progressive; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: space is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with innovation**: lab + innovation co-built
13. **Link with innovation-portfolio**: lab + portfolio co-built
14. **Link with open-innovation**: lab + open co-built
15. **Link with product-innovation**: lab + product co-built
16. **Link with research-and-development**: lab + R&D co-built
17. **Toolchain**: IDEO / Microsoft / Google / Plug and Play / BCG Digital Ventures
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why an innovation lab strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (speed / trust / risk / cost)
23. **Occam**: the innovation lab the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
