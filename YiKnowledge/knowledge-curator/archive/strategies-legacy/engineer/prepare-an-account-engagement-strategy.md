## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + cadence + channel + content + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by ARR + NRR + retention + expansion + satisfaction; no sloganeering
3. **Audience**: named / tier / pursuit / multi-thread / executive; do not omit
4. **Cadence**: daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly / yearly; do not omit
5. **Channel**: meetings / email / phone / digital / executive; do not omit
6. **Content**: industry / customer / case / value / roadmap; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: ARR + NRR + retention + expansion + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from audience → cadence → channel → content → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **No sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with account-management**: engagement + account management co-built
13. **Link with account-based-experience**: engagement + ABX co-built
14. **Link with account-based-marketing**: engagement + ABM co-built
15. **Link with customer-success**: engagement + CS co-built
16. **Link with mutual-close-plan**: engagement + mutual close co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce / Outreach / Salesloft / Apollo / 6sense / Demandbase / LinkedIn-Sales-Nav / Clari
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must account engagement; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can one-off visits solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: account engagement the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
