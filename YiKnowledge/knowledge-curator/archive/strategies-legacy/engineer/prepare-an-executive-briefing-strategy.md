## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + objective + content + cadence + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by executive NPS + revenue + renewal + strategy consensus + actions; no empty slogans
3. **Audience**: CEO / CFO / CIO / CTO / business line / board members; none missing
4. **Objective**: strategy consensus + investment + risk / opportunity / retirement; none missing
5. **Content**: vision / value / case study / roadmap / ROI; none missing
6. **Cadence**: kick-off / quarterly / semi-annual / annual / milestone; none missing
7. **Measure**: executive NPS + revenue + renewal + strategy consensus + actions; none missing
8. **Not one-shot**: from audience → objective → content → cadence → measurement progressive; no skipping levels
9. **Not report-only**: reports are only the starting point; not the endpoint
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must have landed evidence; no ambiguity
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with executive-sponsorship**: executive briefing + executive sponsorship co-build
13. **Link with account-engagement**: executive briefing + account manager engagement co-build
14. **Link with deal-review**: executive briefing + deal-review co-build
15. **Link with pitch-deck**: executive briefing + proposal co-build
16. **Link with account-based-selling**: executive briefing + ABS co-build
17. **Toolchain**: PowerPoint / Google-Slides / Beautiful.ai / Pitch / Prezent / Salesloft / Gong
18. **Publicly accessible**: strategy accessible to everyone; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: Evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must executive briefing; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion**: how much can be solved by email; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: executive briefing the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
