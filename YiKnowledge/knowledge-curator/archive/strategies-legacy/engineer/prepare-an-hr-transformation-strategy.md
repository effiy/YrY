## Action recommendations

1. **five dimensions**: diagnosis + blueprint + journey + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritise by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **diagnosis Diagnose**: maturity / pain points / opportunities / priority; do not omit
4. **blueprint Blueprint**: goal / operating model / service model / path; do not omit
5. **journey Journey**: pilot + rollout + consolidation + evolution; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from diagnosis → blueprint → journey → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: system count is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with hr-operations**: HR transformation + HR operations co-built
13. **link with hr-service-delivery**: HR transformation + HR service delivery co-built
14. **link with hr-shared-services**: HR transformation + HR shared services co-built
15. **link with hr-business-partner**: HR transformation + HR business partner co-built
16. **link with workforce-transformation**: HR transformation + workforce transformation co-built
17. **toolchain**: Workday / SAP SuccessFactors / Oracle HCM / ServiceNow HR / Eightfold AI
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must HR transformation strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: HR transformation the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
