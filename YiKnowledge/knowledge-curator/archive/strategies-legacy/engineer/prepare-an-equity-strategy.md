## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: design + grant + management + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Design**: type / pool / vesting / value; do not omit
4. **Grant**: instrument / quantity / exercise / filing; do not omit
5. **Admin**: account / exercise / transfer / tax; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from design → grant → management → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: options only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with incentive**: equity + incentive co-built
13. **Link with compensation**: equity + compensation co-built
14. **Link with rewards**: equity + total rewards co-built
15. **Link with esop**: equity + employee stock ownership co-built
16. **Link with finance**: equity + finance co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Carta / Shareworks / Solium / Equity Edge / Computershare
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must equity strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: equity the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
