## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: preservation + search + review + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Preserve**: litigation / hold / close loop; do not omit
4. **Search**: keywords / models / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Review**: relevance / privilege / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from preservation -> search -> review -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with litigation**: E-Discovery + Litigation co-build
13. **Link with records-management**: E-Discovery + RecordsManagement co-build
14. **Link with information-governance**: E-Discovery + InformationGovernance co-build
15. **Link with data-retention**: E-Discovery + DataRetention co-build
16. **Link with legal-operations**: E-Discovery + LegalOperations co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Relativity / Logikcull / Everlaw / DISCO / Reveal
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must E-Discovery; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: see how much manual review can solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: E-Discovery the simpler the better; cut redundant processes
