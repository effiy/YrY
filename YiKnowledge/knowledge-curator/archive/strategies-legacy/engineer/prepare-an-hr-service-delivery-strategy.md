## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: channel + knowledge + case + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Channel**: self-service / portal / ticket / chat / consultant; do not omit
4. **Knowledge**: FAQ / policy / process / case / smart; do not omit
5. **Case**: intake / triage / route / escalate / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from channel → knowledge → case → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with hr-operations**: HSD + HROps co-built
13. **Link with people-analytics**: HSD + PA co-built
14. **Link with employee-experience**: HSD + EX co-built
15. **Link with chro**: HSD + CHRO co-built
16. **Link with internal-communications**: HSD + Internal co-built
17. **Toolchain**: ServiceNow HR / Workday Service / Zendesk / Freshworks / Espressive
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must HSD; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by email; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: HSD the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
