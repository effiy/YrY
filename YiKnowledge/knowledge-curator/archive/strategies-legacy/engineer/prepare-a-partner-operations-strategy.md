## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: data + process + tooling + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by influenced ARR + revenue share + partner NPS + retention + speed; not sloganeering
3. **Data**: partner profile + tier + performance + lead + payout; do not omit
4. **Process**: recruit / onboard / co-sell / co-marketing / retire; do not omit
5. **Tooling**: PRM / Crossbeam / Reveal / portal / data integration; do not omit
6. **Governance**: charter + owner + budget + guidelines + retire; do not omit
7. **Measure**: influenced ARR + revenue share + partner NPS + retention + speed; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from data → process → tooling → governance → measurement progressive; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with partner-program**: operations + program co-build
13. **Link with partner-success**: operations + success co-build
14. **Link with revenue-ops**: operations + RevOps co-build
15. **Link with sales-ops**: operations + sales operations co-build
16. **Link with channel-partner**: operations + channel co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Impartner / Allbound / PartnerStack / Channeltapest / Crossbeam / Reveal / Salesforce-PRM / Zinfi / 6sense / Demandbase
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a partner operations strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved with manual spreadsheets; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / channel conflict / business)
23. **Occam**: simpler partner operations is better; cut redundant steps
