## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identify + stage + routes + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by win-rate + cycle-time + revenue + pipeline + retention; not sloganeering
3. **Identify**: signals / behavior / intent / triggers / pain points; do not omit
4. **Stage**: qualify / discover / propose / negotiate / close; do not omit
5. **Routes**: playbook / next-step / milestones / retire / escalate; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: win-rate + cycle-time + revenue + pipeline + retention; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from identify → stage → routes → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Links to sales-pipeline**: opportunity management + sales pipeline co-build
13. **Links to deal-review**: opportunity management + deal-review co-build
14. **Links to mutual-action-plan**: opportunity management + MAP co-build
15. **Links to sales-playbook**: opportunity management + sales playbook co-build
16. **Links to sales-ops**: opportunity management + sales operations co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce / Microsoft-Dynamics / Hubspot / Clari / Gong / Outreach / Salesloft
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why opportunity management is a must; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can free development solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: opportunity management — the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
