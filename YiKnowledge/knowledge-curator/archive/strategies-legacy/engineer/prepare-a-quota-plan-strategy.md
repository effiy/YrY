## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: baseline + allocation + adjustment + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by revenue + attainment + retention + AE satisfaction + fairness; no sloganeering
3. **Baseline**: history / market / potential / capacity / strategy; do not omit
4. **Allocation**: top-down / bottom-up / hybrid / cohort / capacity; do not omit
5. **Adjustment**: seasonality / reorg / onboarding / retirement / special; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: revenue + attainment + retention + AE satisfaction + fairness; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from baseline → allocation → adjustment → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **No sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with territory-plan**: quota + territory plan co-build
13. **Link with commission-plan**: quota + commission co-build
14. **Link with sales-territory**: quota + sales territory co-build
15. **Link with sales-forecast**: quota + sales forecast co-build
16. **Link with sales-ops**: quota + sales ops co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Xactly / Varicent / Salesforce / Clari / Boost-UP / Captivate
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must a quota plan; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can gut calls solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: quota the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
