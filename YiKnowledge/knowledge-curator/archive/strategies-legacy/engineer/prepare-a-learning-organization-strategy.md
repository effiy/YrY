## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: systems + shared vision + mental models + team + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Systems thinking**: feedback / delay / leverage / patterns; do not omit
4. **Shared vision**: mission / values / co-creation / propagation; do not omit
5. **Team learning**: dialogue / shared / inquiry / mental models; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from systems → shared vision → mental models → team → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: training counts are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with learning-culture**: learning organization + learning culture co-built
13. **Link with employee-learning**: learning organization + employee learning co-built
14. **Link with corporate-culture**: learning organization + corporate culture co-built
15. **Link with organizational-leadership**: learning organization + organizational leadership co-built
16. **Link with knowledge-management**: learning organization + knowledge management co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Degreed / EdCast / Coursera for Business / Udemy Business / Notion
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must a learning-organization strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: learning organization the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
