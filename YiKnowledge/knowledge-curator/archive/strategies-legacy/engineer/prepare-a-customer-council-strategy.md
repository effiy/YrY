## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: recruit + agenda + output + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Recruit**: persona / invite / tenure / incentive; do not omit
4. **Agenda**: topics / cadence / executives / resolutions; do not omit
5. **Output**: white paper / roadmap / case / charter; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from recruit → agenda → output → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: meeting is only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with customer-advisory**: council + advisory co-built
13. **Link with customer-advocacy**: council + advocacy co-built
14. **Link with customer-feedback**: council + feedback co-built
15. **Link with customer-success**: council + success co-built
16. **Link with customer-program**: council + program co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Gainsight / Salesforce Council Workspace / Notion / Convene / Slack
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must customer council strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: the simpler the council the better; cut redundant layers
