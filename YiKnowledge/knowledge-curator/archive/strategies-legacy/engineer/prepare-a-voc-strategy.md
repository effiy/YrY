## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Collection + analysis + action + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: Prioritize by retention + trust + experience + conversion + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Collect**: Survey / interview / feedback / review / social; do not omit
4. **Analyze**: Qualitative / quantitative / theme / sentiment / priority; do not omit
5. **Act**: Distribute / closed loop / product / process / culture; do not omit
6. **Governance**: Owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: Retention + trust + experience + conversion + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: Progressive from collection → analysis → action → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: Reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: Every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: Strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with customer-experience**: VoC + CX co-build
13. **Link with customer-success**: VoC + CS co-build
14. **Link with customer-journey**: VoC + CJ co-build
15. **Link with customer-research**: VoC + Research co-build
16. **Link with cco**: VoC + CCO co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Qualtrics / Medallia / InMoment / Sprinklr / Gong
18. **Publicly queryable**: Strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: Evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: Why must VoC; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: How much can be solved by intuition; if solvable do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: Second-order consequences after strategy (retention / trust / experience / conversion) 
23. **Occam**: VoC the simpler the better; cut redundant surveys
