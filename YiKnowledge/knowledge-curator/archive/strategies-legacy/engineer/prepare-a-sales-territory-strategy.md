## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Design + allocation + balance + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: Prioritize by TAM + revenue + quota attainment + coverage + retention; not sloganeering
3. **Design**: Geography / vertical / named / round-robin / pod; do not omit
4. **Allocation**: Account + AE / SDR + named + capacity + conflict; do not omit
5. **Balance**: Potential + workload + historical disputes + rebalancing + gradual rollout; do not omit
6. **Governance**: Approval + change + transparency + dispute + retirement; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: TAM + revenue + quota attainment + coverage + retention; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: Progressive from design → allocation → balance → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: Reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: Every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: Strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with territory**: Sales territory + territory co-build
13. **Link with quota**: Sales territory + quota co-build
14. **Link with sales-ops**: Sales territory + sales operations co-build
15. **Link with account-management**: Sales territory + account manager co-build
16. **Link with sales-development**: Sales territory + sales development co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce / Anaplan / Clari / ZoomInfo / Outreach / 6sense / Demandbase / Full- insights
18. **Publicly queryable**: Strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: Evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: Why must sales territory; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: How much can be solved by free competition; if solvable do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: Second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention) 
23. **Occam**: Sales territory the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
