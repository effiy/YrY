## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: procurement + usage + optimization + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; no sloganeering
3. **Procurement**: contracts / PPA / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Usage**: monitoring / peak-shaving / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Optimization**: efficiency / storage / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from procurement → usage → optimization → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **No sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with environmental**: EnergyManagement + Environmental co-build
13. **Link with carbon-management**: EnergyManagement + CarbonManagement co-build
14. **Link with sustainability**: EnergyManagement + Sustainability co-build
15. **Link with circular-economy**: EnergyManagement + CircularEconomy co-build
16. **Link with manufacturing**: EnergyManagement + Manufacturing co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Schneider EcoStruxure / GE Digital / ABB Ability / Siemens Energy / ENERGY STAR Portfolio Manager
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must have EnergyManagement; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by electricity bills; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: EnergyManagement the simpler the better; cut redundant monitoring
