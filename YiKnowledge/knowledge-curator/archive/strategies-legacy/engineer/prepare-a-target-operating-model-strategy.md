## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: vision + structure + capability + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Vision**: business / value / boundaries / principles; do not omit
4. **Structure**: org / roles / decisions / collaboration; do not omit
5. **Capability**: people / process / technology / data; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: gradual from vision → structure → capability → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ism**: an org chart is only the start; not the end
10. **No sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with operating-model**: TOM + operating model co-built
13. **Link with business-architecture**: TOM + business architecture co-built
14. **Link with business-capability**: TOM + business capability co-built
15. **Link with business-process**: TOM + business process co-built
16. **Link with organizational-change**: TOM + organizational change co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Deloitte / McKinsey / BCG / Accenture / Capgemini TOM Toolkit
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a target operating model strategy is needed; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: target operating model the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
