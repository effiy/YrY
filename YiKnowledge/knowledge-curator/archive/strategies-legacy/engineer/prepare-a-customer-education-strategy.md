## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + content + channel + measurement + iteration; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by adoption rate + retention + LTV + support reduction; not sloganeering
3. **Audience**: role + industry + tier + maturity + learning style; do not omit
4. **Content**: basics + workflow + advanced + industry + case + certification; do not omit
5. **Channel**: academy + LMS + documentation + video + webinar + 1:1; do not omit
6. **Measurement**: completion rate + certification rate + adoption + retention + support reduction; do not omit
7. **Iterate**: feedback + update + version + roadmap + retire; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from audience → content → channel → measurement → iteration; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with onboarding**: education + onboarding co-built
13. **Link with customer-success**: education + customer success co-built
14. **Link with adoption**: education + adoption co-built
15. **Link with content**: education + content co-built
16. **Link with community**: education + community co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Customer-Education-Framework / Skilljar / Thinkific / Teachable / Docebo / Canvas / Moodle / Articulate / Lessonly / Northpass
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must customer education strategy; the worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by the product itself; if solvable do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / adoption / business)
23. **Occam**: the simpler education is, the better; cut redundant steps
