## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: insight + positioning + narrative + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; no sloganeering
3. **Insight**: customer / pain points / value / differentiation; do not omit
4. **Positioning**: one sentence / story / evidence / rebuttal; do not omit
5. **Narrative**: theme / arguments / scenario / evidence; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from insight → positioning → narrative → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: slogans are only the start; not the end
10. **No sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with positioning**: messaging + positioning co-build
13. **Link with brand-identity**: messaging + brand identity co-build
14. **Link with marketing**: messaging + marketing co-build
15. **Link with competitive**: messaging + competitive co-build
16. **Link with content-marketing**: messaging + content co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Pitch / Messaging Studio / Gong / Crystal / Lusha
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must have messaging strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by defaults; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: messaging the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
