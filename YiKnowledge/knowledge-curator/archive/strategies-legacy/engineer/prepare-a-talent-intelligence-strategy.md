## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: source + inference + application + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Source**: internal / external / market / skills / behavior; do not omit
4. **Infer**: ML / models / recommendation / matching; do not omit
5. **Apply**: recruiting / mobility / development / retention; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from source → inference → application → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: model count is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with people-analytics**: talent intelligence + people analytics co-built
13. **Link with skills-based**: talent intelligence + skills-based co-built
14. **Link with talent-acquisition**: talent intelligence + talent acquisition co-built
15. **Link with hr-data**: talent intelligence + HR data co-built
16. **Link with talent-marketplace**: talent intelligence + talent marketplace co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Eightfold AI / Beamery / SAP SuccessFactors Talent Intelligence Hub / Workday Talent Intelligence / SeekOut
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a talent intelligence strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: talent intelligence — the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
