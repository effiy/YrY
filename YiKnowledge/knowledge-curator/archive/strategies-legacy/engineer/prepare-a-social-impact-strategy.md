## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: issues + investment + execution + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by trust + speed + risk + compliance + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Issues**: community / education / health / equity / disaster relief; do not omit
4. **Investment**: funds / goods / time / expertise / network; do not omit
5. **Execution**: partners / projects / volunteering / measurement / reporting; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: trust + speed + risk + compliance + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from issues → investment → execution → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with sustainability**: SI + Sustainability co-build
13. **Link with esg**: SI + ESG co-build
14. **Link with dei**: SI + DEI co-build
15. **Link with culture**: SI + Culture co-build
16. **Link with stakeholder-management**: SI + Stakeholder co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce Foundation / Submittable / Bright Funds / Groundswell / Millie
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must SI; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can donations alone solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (trust / speed / risk / compliance)
23. **Occam**: the simpler SI is, the better; cut redundant projects
