## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: growth + conversion + retention + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by revenue + growth + trust + speed + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Growth**: new customer / expansion / retention / churn / repeat purchase; do not omit
4. **Convert**: MQL / SQL / opportunity / closed-won / renewal; do not omit
5. **Retain**: renewal / health / value / upgrade / referral; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: revenue + growth + trust + speed + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: gradual from growth → conversion → retention → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with cmo**: CRO + CMO co-built
13. **Link with revenue-operations**: CRO + RevOps co-built
14. **Link with sales-operations**: CRO + SalesOps co-built
15. **Link with saas-metrics**: CRO + SaaS co-built
16. **Link with leadership**: CRO + Leadership co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce / Clari / Gong / Outreach / Salesloft
18. **Publicly queryable**: everyone can look up the strategy; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why CRO is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can a sales director solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (revenue / growth / trust / cost)
23. **Occam**: simpler CRO is better; cut redundant layers
