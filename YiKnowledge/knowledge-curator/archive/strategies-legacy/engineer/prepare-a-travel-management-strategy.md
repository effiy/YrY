## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: policy + booking + service + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Policy**: tier / standard / approval / exception; do not omit
4. **Booking**: flight / hotel / car / meal; do not omit
5. **Service**: visa / change / emergency / risk; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from policy → booking → service → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: ticket booking is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with travel-policy**: travel + travel policy co-built
13. **Link with expense-management**: travel + expense management co-built
14. **Link with spend-management**: travel + spend management co-built
15. **Link with procurement**: travel + procurement co-built
16. **Link with finance**: travel + finance co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Concur / TripActions / Navan / Amex GBT / SAP Fioneer
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a travel strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: travel, the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
