## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identify + assess + control + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Identify Identify**: hazards / exposure / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Assess Assess**: risk / priority / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Control Control**: hierarchy / measures / closed loop; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from identify -> assess -> control -> governance -> measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with process-safety**: HealthAndSafety + ProcessSafety co-build
13. **Link with environmental**: HealthAndSafety + Environmental co-build
14. **Link with continuous-improvement**: HealthAndSafety + ContinuousImprovement co-build
15. **Link with operations**: HealthAndSafety + Operations co-build
16. **Link with manufacturing**: HealthAndSafety + Manufacturing co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Cority / Intelex / VelocityEHS / EHS Insight / Sphera
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must HealthAndSafety; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can PPE solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: HealthAndSafety simpler is better; cut redundant procedures
