## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: alignment + process + data + tools + measurement; no missing dimension.
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by ARR + funnel + win-rate + NRR + sales cadence; not sloganeering.
3. **Alignment**: marketing + sales + CS co-build goals + funnel + SLA + escalation; do not omit.
4. **Process**: lead → MQL → SQL → opp → order → onboard → expand; do not omit.
5. **Data**: single source of truth + account + funnel + forecast + commission; do not omit.
6. **Tools**: CRM + MAP + CS platform + BI + enrichment; do not omit.
7. **Measurement**: ARR + funnel + win-rate + NRR + sales cadence; do not omit.
8. **Not one-shot**: from alignment → process → data → tools → measurement, gradual; no skipping.
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end.
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague.
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable.
12. **Link with sales-ops**: RevOps + sales ops co-build.
13. **Link with sales-strategy**: RevOps + sales strategy co-build.
14. **Link with demand-generation**: RevOps + demand generation co-build.
15. **Link with customer-success**: RevOps + CS co-build.
16. **Link with sales-forecast**: RevOps + forecast co-build.
17. **Toolchain**: Clari / InsightSquared / Salesforce Revenue Cloud / Gong / BoostUp / Aviso / People.ai / Salesloft / Outreach / Ambition.
18. **Publicly queryable**: anyone can look up the strategy; not hidden.
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot.
20. **First principles**: why must a RevOps strategy; worst consequence of not doing it.
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can relying on department autonomy solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy.
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / organization / business).
23. **Occam**: simpler RevOps is better; cut redundant steps.
