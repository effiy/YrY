## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identify + enable + embed + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **identify Identify**: audience / readiness / value / obstacles; do not omit
4. **enable Enable**: training / resources / pilot / support; do not omit
5. **embed Embed**: integration / workflow / habits / data; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from identify → enable → embed → governance → measurement progressive; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: deployment only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Linked with customer-activation**: adoption + activation co-built
13. **Linked with customer-success**: adoption + customer success co-built
14. **Linked with customer-onboarding**: adoption + onboarding co-built
15. **Linked with customer-training**: adoption + training co-built
16. **Linked with product-adoption**: adoption + product adoption co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Pendo / Gainsight PX / Appcues / WalkMe / Whatfix
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must customer adoption strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: customer adoption the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
