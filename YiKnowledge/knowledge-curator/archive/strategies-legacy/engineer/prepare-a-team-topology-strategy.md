## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: type + boundary + interaction + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by deploy frequency + lead time + retention + business supply; not sloganeering
3. **Team Types**: stream-aligned + platform + enabling + complicated-subsystem; do not omit
4. **Boundaries**: mission + team size + cognitive load + ownership + API; do not omit
5. **Interaction**: collaboration / x-as-a-service / facilitating; do not omit
6. **Governance**: committee + transparency + decision rights + upgrade + retirement; do not omit
7. **Measure**: DORA + retention + cognitive load + business supply; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from type -> boundary -> interaction -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with org-design**: team topology + OD co-built
13. **Link with hr**: team topology + HR co-built
14. **Link with product-strategy**: team topology + product strategy co-built
15. **Link with platform-engineering**: team topology + platform co-built
16. **Link with leadership**: team topology + leadership co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Team Topologies Canvas / Backstage / Port / Humanitec / Spotify Squad Health / Atlassian Team Playbook
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must team topology strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can a functional org solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / retention / business) 
23. **Occam**: team topology the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
