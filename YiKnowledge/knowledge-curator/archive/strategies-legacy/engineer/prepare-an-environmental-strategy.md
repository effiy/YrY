## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: assessment + targets + actions + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by risk + trust + speed + compliance + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Assess**: baseline / boundary / data / priority / gap; do not omit
4. **Targets**: SBT / Net Zero / short-term / long-term / cadence; do not omit
5. **Actions**: emission reduction / water saving / waste reduction / energy saving / ecology; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: risk + trust + speed + compliance + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from assessment → targets → actions → governance → measurement progressively; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with sustainability**: EN + Sustainability co-built
13. **Link with esg**: EN + ESG co-built
14. **Link with compliance**: EN + Compliance co-built
15. **Link with risk**: EN + Risk co-built
16. **Link with facilities**: EN + Facilities co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Watershed / Persefoni / Sinai / Sweep / Sphera
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must EN; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by offsets; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (risk / trust / speed / compliance)
23. **Occam**: EN the simpler the better; cut redundant metrics
