## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: family + hierarchy + rhythm + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Family**: sans / serif / mono / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Hierarchy**: h1-h6 / body / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Rhythm**: baseline / line-height / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from family → hierarchy → rhythm → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Links with design-system**: Typography + DesignSystem co-build
13. **Links with animation**: Typography + Animation co-build
14. **Links with micro-interaction**: Typography + MicroInteraction co-build
15. **Links with accessibility**: Typography + Accessibility co-build
16. **Links with internationalization**: Typography + I18n co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Google Fonts / Adobe Fonts / Fontshare / Typography.js / Fluid Type Scale
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must Typography; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default fonts; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: Typography the simpler the better; cut redundant weights
