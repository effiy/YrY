## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: data + interviews + patterns + actions + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by win-rate lift + revenue + cycle time + renewal + pattern; not sloganeering
3. **Data**: CRM / calls / emails / health / renewal; do not omit
4. **Interviews**: buyers / executives / champion / AM / retrospective; do not omit
5. **Patterns**: win / loss / no-decision / displacement / multi-case; do not omit
6. **Actions**: playbook / training / product / pricing / positioning; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: win-rate lift + revenue + cycle time + renewal + pattern; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress gradually from data → interviews → patterns → actions → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with deal-review**: win/loss + deal-review co-built
13. **Link with sales-pipeline**: win/loss + sales pipeline co-built
14. **Link with competitive-intelligence**: win/loss + competitive intelligence co-built
15. **Link with sales-playbook**: win/loss + sales playbook co-built
16. **Link with product-positioning**: win/loss + product positioning co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Gong / Clari / Salesforce / Boost-UP / People-AI / Insight-Square / Klue / Crayon
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why win/loss analysis is necessary; the worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved with intuition alone; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: the simpler win/loss analysis is, the better; cut redundant steps
