## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + content + channel + cadence + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by NRR + LTV + retention + expansion + NPS; not sloganeering
3. **Audience**: activate / adopt / expand / advocate / win-back; do not omit
4. **Content**: education / case / product / resource / retirement; do not omit
5. **Channel**: email / in-app / push / community / sales; do not omit
6. **Cadence**: daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly / milestone; do not omit
7. **Measure**: NRR + LTV + retention + expansion + NPS; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from audience → content → channel → cadence → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with customer-lifecycle**: engagement + customer lifecycle co-build
13. **link with customer-success**: engagement + CS co-build
14. **link with account-engagement**: engagement + account manager engagement co-build
15. **link with in-app-messaging**: engagement + in-app messaging co-build
16. **link with lifecycle-marketing**: engagement + lifecycle marketing co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Intercom / Pendo / Customer.io / Iterable / Braze / Appcues / Gainsight / Hubspot
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must customer engagement; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: rely on one-time push how much can be solved; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention) 
23. **Occam**: customer engagement the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
