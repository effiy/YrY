## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + content + channel + cadence + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by MQL + SQL + revenue + CAC + ROAS; not sloganeering
3. **Audience**: ICP + retargeting + lookalike + interest + custom; do not omit
4. **Content**: copy / image / video / carousel / story / retirement; do not omit
5. **Channel**: Meta / LinkedIn / X / TikTok / Reddit; do not omit
6. **Cadence**: daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly / yearly; do not omit
7. **Measure**: MQL + SQL + revenue + CAC + ROAS; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from audience → content → channel → cadence → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with paid-media**: paid social + paid media co-built
13. **Link with social-media**: paid social + social media co-built
14. **Link with demand-generation**: paid social + funnel co-built
15. **Link with content**: paid social + content co-built
16. **Link with campaign-planning**: paid social + campaign co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Meta-Ads / LinkedIn-Campaign-Manager / X-Ads / TikTok-Ads / Reddit-Ads / Sprout / Skai / WordStream
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must paid social strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can natural social solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / CAC)
23. **Occam**: paid social the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
