## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: psychological safety + skills + cadence + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Psychological safety**: trust / inclusion / vulnerability / no retaliation; do not omit
4. **Skills**: SBI / non-violent / listening / receiving; do not omit
5. **Cadence**: real-time / weekly-monthly / quarterly / 360 / retrospective; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from psychological safety → skills → cadence → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: feedback counts are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with employee-feedback**: feedback culture + employee feedback co-build
13. **Link with employee-pulse**: feedback culture + employee pulse co-build
14. **Link with performance-management**: feedback culture + performance management co-build
15. **Link with corporate-culture**: feedback culture + corporate culture co-build
16. **Link with employee-experience**: feedback culture + employee experience co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Lattice / 15Five / Culture Amp / Small Improvements / Reflektive
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must a feedback culture strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can the default solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler feedback culture is, the better; cut redundant layers
