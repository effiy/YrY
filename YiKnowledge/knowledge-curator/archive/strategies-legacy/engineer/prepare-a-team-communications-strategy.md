## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: cadence + content + channel + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Cadence**: daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly / event-driven; do not omit
4. **Content**: goals / progress / blockers / decisions / retrospective; do not omit
5. **Channel**: meeting / documentation / chat / email / dashboard; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: reach + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from cadence → content → channel → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: meeting count is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with partner-communications**: team + partner communications co-build
13. **Link with internal-communications**: team + internal communications co-build
14. **Link with customer-communications**: team + customer communications co-build
15. **Link with collaboration**: team communications + collaboration co-build
16. **Link with corporate-culture**: team communications + corporate culture co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Slack / Microsoft Teams / Notion / Confluence / Loom
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a team communications strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by defaults; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: team communications, the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
