## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: values + behavior + spread + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Values**: core / expected / opposed / boundary; do not omit
4. **Behavior**: exemplars / counter-examples / blockers / incentives; do not omit
5. **Spread**: leadership / rituals / stories / symbols; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from values → behavior → spread → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: slogans are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Linked with culture**: organizational culture + culture co-build
13. **Linked with organization**: organizational culture + organization co-build
14. **Linked with people**: organizational culture + people co-build
15. **Linked with leadership**: organizational culture + leadership co-build
16. **Linked with employee-engagement**: organizational culture + employee engagement co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Culture Amp / Glint / Lattice / 15Five / Workday Peakon
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why an organizational culture strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler organizational culture is, the better; cut redundant layers
