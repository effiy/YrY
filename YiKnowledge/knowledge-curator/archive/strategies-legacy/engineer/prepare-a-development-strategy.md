## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Goals + experience + relationships + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: Prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **goals Goal**: Short-term / long-term / role / behavior; do not omit
4. **experience Experience**: Project / rotation / challenge / failure; do not omit
5. **relationships Relationship**: Mentor / coach / peer / network; do not omit
6. **Governance**: Owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: Coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: From goals -> experience -> relationships -> governance -> measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: Promotion is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: Every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: Strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Linked with learning**: Development + learning co-built
13. **Linked with talent-development**: Development + talent development co-built
14. **Linked with career**: Development + career co-built
15. **Linked with people**: Development + people co-built
16. **Linked with competency**: Development + competency co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Lattice / 15Five / Fuel50 / Gloat / Eightfold
18. **Publicly queryable**: Strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: Evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: Why must development strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: How much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: Second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: Development the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
