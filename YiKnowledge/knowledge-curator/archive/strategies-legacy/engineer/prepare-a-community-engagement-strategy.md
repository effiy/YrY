## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: members + content + cadence + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by linked ARR + retention + referrals + revenue share + satisfaction; not sloganeering
3. **Members**: dev / user / champion / ambassador + tiering + upgrade; do not omit
4. **Content**: forum + case + tutorial + video + resource library; do not omit
5. **Cadence**: daily / monthly / quarterly / events + upgrade; do not omit
6. **Governance**: charter + moderators + guidelines + retirement + upgrade; do not omit
7. **Measure**: linked ARR + retention + referrals + revenue share + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from members → content → cadence → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with community-strategy**: engagement + community co-built
13. **Link with devrel**: engagement + DevRel co-built
14. **Link with customer-advocacy**: engagement + advocacy co-built
15. **Link with customer-success**: engagement + CS co-built
16. **Link with content**: engagement + content co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Discourse / Vanilla / Khoros / Bevy / Socious / Circle / Mighty Networks / Slack / Discord
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must community engagement strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can natural word-of-mouth solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / retention / business)
23. **Occam**: community engagement the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
