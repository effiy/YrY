## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: standards + process + improvement + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by adoption + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Standards**: ISO / industry / internal / customer / regulatory; do not omit
4. **Process**: QMS / documentation / training / audit / CAPA; do not omit
5. **Improvement**: PDCA / 8D / Kaizen / Six Sigma / Lean; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: adoption + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from standards → process → improvement → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with process-excellence**: QM + Process co-build
13. **Link with six-sigma**: QM + SixSigma co-build
14. **Link with lean**: QM + Lean co-build
15. **Link with operations**: QM + Ops co-build
16. **Link with compliance**: QM + Compliance co-build
17. **Toolchain**: MasterControl / Veeva Quality / Sparta Systems / ETQ / Intellex
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must QM; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can inspection alone solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (adoption / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler QM is, the better; cut redundant rules
