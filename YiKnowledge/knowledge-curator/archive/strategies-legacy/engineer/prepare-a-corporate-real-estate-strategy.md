## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Site selection + Portfolio + Finance + Governance + Measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Site selection**: location / population / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Portfolio**: owned / leased / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Finance**: capex / opex / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from Site selection → Portfolio → Finance → Governance → Measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Property-management linkage**: CorporateRealEstate + PropertyManagement co-build
13. **Facility-management linkage**: CorporateRealEstate + FacilityManagement co-build
14. **Asset-management linkage**: CorporateRealEstate + AssetManagement co-build
15. **Workplace linkage**: CorporateRealEstate + Workplace co-build
16. **Sustainability linkage**: CorporateRealEstate + Sustainability co-build
17. **Toolchain**: JLL / CBRE / CoStar / Yardi / MRI
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must CorporateRealEstate exist; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can temporary site selection solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: CorporateRealEstate the simpler the better; cut redundant portfolios
