## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: goal + structure + landing + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **goal Goal**: business / team / individual / behavior; do not omit
4. **structure Structure**: short / long / cash / equity; do not omit
5. **landing Execute**: calculation / communication / payout / retrospective; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from goal → structure → landing → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: bonus is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with compensation**: incentive + compensation co-built
13. **Link with bonus**: incentive + bonus co-built
14. **Link with equity**: incentive + equity co-built
15. **Link with rewards**: incentive + total rewards co-built
16. **Link with performance-management**: incentive + performance co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Workday / Anaplan / Xactly / beqom · Varicent
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must incentive strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler incentive the better; cut redundant layers
