## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: build + enable + impact + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Build**: group / charter / sponsor / launch; do not omit
4. **Enable**: budget / tools / training / platform; do not omit
5. **Influence**: business / product / policy / culture; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from build → enable → impact → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: group count is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with employee-resource-network**: ERG + ERN co-built
13. **Link with employee-affinity-group**: ERG + EAG co-built
14. **Link with dei**: ERG + DEI co-built
15. **Link with employee-engagement**: ERG + engagement co-built
16. **Link with inclusion-belonging**: ERG + inclusion-belonging co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Disco / ERGify / Teambuild / Bonusly / Workday
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must ERG strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: ERG the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
