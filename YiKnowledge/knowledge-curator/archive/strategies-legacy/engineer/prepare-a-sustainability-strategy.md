## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: environment + social + governance + measurement + governance; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by brand + compliance + trust + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **environment Environment**: carbon / water / waste / energy / biodiversity; do not omit
4. **social Social**: employee / community / customer / diversity / human rights; do not omit
5. **governance Governance**: board / executive compensation / anti-corruption / disclosure / risk; do not omit
6. **measurement Measure**: carbon emissions + water use + waste + diversity + compliance; do not omit
7. **not one-shot**: from environment → social → governance → measurement → governance gradual; no skipping
8. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
9. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
10. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
11. **link with compliance**: Sustainability + Compliance co-built
12. **link with risk**: Sustainability + Risk co-built
13. **link with brand**: Sustainability + Brand co-built
14. **link with investor-relations**: Sustainability + IR co-built
15. **link with supply-chain**: Sustainability + Supply co-built
16. **toolchain**: Watershed / Persefoni / Sinai / Sweep / Normative
17. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
18. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
19. **first principles**: why must Sustainability; worst consequence of not doing
20. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by CSR report; if solvable don't introduce heavy strategy
21. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (brand / compliance / trust / risk) 
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / capital / growth / risk) 
23. **Occam**: Sustainability the simpler the better; cut redundant metrics
