## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: situation + problem + implication + need + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: set priority by win-rate + ACV + cycle-time + renewal + NRR; no sloganeering
3. **Situation**: business / role / decision-maker / budget / timeline; do not omit
4. **Problem**: business / technical / financial / strategic / risk; do not omit
5. **Implication**: cost / risk / opportunity / strategy / measurement; do not omit
6. **Need-Payoff**: value / ROI / business / strategy / measurement; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: win-rate + ACV + cycle-time + renewal + NRR; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: gradual from situation → problem → implication → need → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ism**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **No sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with solution-selling**: SPIN + solution co-build
13. **Link with consultative-selling**: SPIN + consultative co-build
14. **Link with challenger-sale**: SPIN + challenger co-build
15. **Link with discovery-call**: SPIN + discovery call co-build
16. **Link with sales-methodology**: SPIN + methodology co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Gong / Chorus / Salesforce / Outreach / Salesloft / 6sense
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why SPIN selling is needed; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: rely on free-form for how much can be solved; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: SPIN the simpler the better; cut redundant questions
