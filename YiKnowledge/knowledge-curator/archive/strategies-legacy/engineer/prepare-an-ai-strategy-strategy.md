## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: vision + use cases + capabilities + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Vision**: north star / value proposition; do not omit
4. **UseCase**: efficiency / innovation / experience; do not omit
5. **Capability**: data / models / platform / talent; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: adoption + value + risk + cost + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from vision → use cases → capabilities → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: use case count is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with ai-governance**: AI strategy + governance co-built
13. **Link with llm-ops**: AI strategy + LLM Ops co-built
14. **Link with data-strategy**: AI strategy + data strategy co-built
15. **Link with ai-safety**: AI strategy + safety co-built
16. **Link with model-risk-management**: AI strategy + model risk co-built
17. **Toolchain**: OKR / strategy map / balanced scorecard / AI radar / internal ai-review
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must there be an AI strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by traditional automation; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler the AI strategy the better; cut redundant layers
