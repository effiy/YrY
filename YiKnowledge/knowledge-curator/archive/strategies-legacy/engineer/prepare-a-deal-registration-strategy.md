## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: rules + process + conflict + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by protection + conversion + revenue + speed + experience; not sloganeering
3. **Rules**: eligibility / validity / protection / exclusivity / renewal; do not omit
4. **Process**: apply / approve / reject / escalate / exit; do not omit
5. **Conflict**: overlap / both parties / escalation / arbitration / record; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: protection + conversion + revenue + speed + experience; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progress gradually from rules -> process -> conflict -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with partner-program**: Deal Reg + Partner Program co-built
13. **Link with partner-compensation**: Deal Reg + Compensation co-built
14. **Link with opportunity-management**: Deal Reg + Opp Mgmt co-built
15. **Link with reseller**: Deal Reg + Reseller co-built
16. **Link with isv**: Deal Reg + ISV co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce PRM / Crossbeam / Impartner / Allbound / Tackle
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must Deal Reg; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by relying on channel self-discipline; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / channel conflict / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: Deal Reg the simpler the better; cut redundant rules
