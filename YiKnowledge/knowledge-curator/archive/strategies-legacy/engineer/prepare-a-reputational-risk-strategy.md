## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: sense + respond + repair + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost set priority; not sloganeering
3. **sense Sense**: sentiment / social / closed loop; do not omit
4. **respond Respond**: statement / action / closed loop; do not omit
5. **repair Repair**: long-term / closed loop; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from sense → respond → repair → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **not report-ism**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **and risk-management link**: ReputationalRisk + RiskManagement co-build
13. **and enterprise-risk link**: ReputationalRisk + EnterpriseRisk co-build
14. **and crisis-management link**: ReputationalRisk + CrisisManagement co-build
15. **and corporate-communications link**: ReputationalRisk + CorporateCommunications co-build
16. **and public-relations link**: ReputationalRisk + PublicRelations co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Meltwater / Cision / Brandwatch / Sprinklr / Talkwalker
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must ReputationalRisk; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: rely on PR itself how much can be solved; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
