## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: onboarding + delivery + evaluation + offboarding + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by SLA + cost + risk + renewal + ROI; not sloganeering
3. **Onboarding**: RFI / RFP / due diligence / contract / access; do not omit
4. **Delivery**: SLA + orders + delivery + billing + after-sales; do not omit
5. **Performance**: quality + timeliness + cost + compliance + satisfaction; do not omit
6. **Offboarding**: migration + data recovery + replacement + renewal / retirement; do not omit
7. **Measure**: SLA + cost + risk + renewal + ROI; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from onboarding → delivery → evaluation → offboarding → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with procurement**: vendor operations + procurement co-built
13. **Link with contract-lifecycle**: vendor operations + contract co-built
14. **Link with revenue-operations**: vendor operations + revenue operations co-built
15. **Link with business-operations**: vendor operations + business operations co-built
16. **Link with partner-operations**: vendor operations + partner operations co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Coupa / SAP-Ariba / Vendorflow / Zip / Tropic / Vendr / Sirion / Coupa-Risk
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must vendor operations; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can one-time procurement solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / compliance / renewal) 
23. **Occam**: vendor operations the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
