## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: acquire + activate + retain + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Acquire**: funnel / channel / cost / conversion; do not omit
4. **Activate**: Aha / first value / time / path; do not omit
5. **Retain**: habit / renewal / extension / referral; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progress from acquire → activate → retain → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: stages are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with customer-retention**: lifetime + retention co-build
13. **Link with customer-ltv**: lifetime + LTV co-build
14. **Link with customer-onboarding**: lifetime + Onboarding co-build
15. **Link with customer-success**: lifetime + customer success co-build
16. **Link with customer-health**: lifetime + health co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Amplitude / Mixpanel / Heap / Pendo / Gainsight / Totango / ChurnZero
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must customer lifetime strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by defaults; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: lifetime the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
