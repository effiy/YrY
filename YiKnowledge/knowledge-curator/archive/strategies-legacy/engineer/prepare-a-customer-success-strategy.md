## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: health + intervention + measurement + cadence + iteration; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by NRR + renewal rate + expansion + win-back + NPS; not sloganeering
3. **Health**: adoption + depth + coverage + outcome + sentiment; do not omit
4. **Intervention**: high-touch / tech-touch / scaled + trigger + cadence + upgrade; do not omit
5. **Measure**: NRR / GRR / renewal rate / expansion rate / win-back rate / CSAT / NPS; do not omit
6. **Cadence**: kickoff / quarterly exec review / monthly operations / weekly report; do not omit
7. **Iterate**: feedback + health score threshold tuning + playbook update + sunset; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progress gradually from health -> intervention -> measurement -> cadence -> iteration; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: health score is only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with customer-journey**: CS + journey co-built
13. **Link with customer-onboarding**: CS + onboarding co-built
14. **Link with retention**: CS + retention co-built
15. **Link with customer-education**: CS + education co-built
16. **Link with customer-success-plan**: CS + CSP co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Gainsight / Totango / ChurnZero / Catalyst / Planhat / HubSpot Service Hub / Intercom Flatly
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must CS strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by relying on reactive customer support; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / retention / business)
23. **Occam**: CS the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
