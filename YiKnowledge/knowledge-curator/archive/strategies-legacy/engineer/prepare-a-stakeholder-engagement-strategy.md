## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identify + prioritize + engage + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by engagement revenue contribution + win-rate + renewal + NRR + retention; not sloganeering
3. **Identify**: champion / decision / influence / blocker / user; do not omit
4. **Prioritize**: tier / power / interest / relationship / stage; do not omit
5. **Engage**: meeting / content / demo / trial / case study; do not omit
6. **Governance**: cadence + owner + escalation + documentation + drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: engagement revenue contribution + win-rate + renewal + NRR + retention; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from identify -> prioritize -> engage -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with account-mapping**: stakeholder + account mapping co-built
13. **Link with account-engagement**: stakeholder + account-manager engagement co-built
14. **Link with executive-sponsorship**: stakeholder + executive sponsorship co-built
15. **Link with mutual-action-plan**: stakeholder + MAP co-built
16. **Link with customer-success**: stakeholder + CS co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce / Outreach / Salesloft / Apollo / 6sense / LinkedIn-Sales-Nav / Gong
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must stakeholder engagement; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by CRM contact; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention) 
23. **Occam**: stakeholder engagement the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
