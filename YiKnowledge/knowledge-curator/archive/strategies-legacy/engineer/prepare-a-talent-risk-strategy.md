## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: key + attrition + succession + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by risk + trust + speed + cost + compliance; not sloganeering
3. **Key**: position / person / knowledge / relationships; do not omit
4. **Attrition**: identify / predict / intervene / retrospective; do not omit
5. **Succession**: identify / develop / succeed / handover; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + remediation + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: gradual from key → attrition → succession → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: shortage is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with hr-risk**: talent + HR risk co-build
13. **Link with workforce-risk**: talent + workforce risk co-build
14. **Link with talent-pipeline**: risk + pipeline co-build
15. **Link with succession-management**: risk + succession co-build
16. **Link with employee-retention**: risk + retention co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Korn Ferry / Hogan / Eightfold AI / Visier / Lattice
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must talent risk strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable don't introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (risk / trust / speed / cost)
23. **Occam**: talent risk simpler is better; cut redundant layers
