## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: criteria + assessment + documentation + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **criteria Criteria**: finance / quality / ESG / security; do not omit
4. **assessment Assess**: questionnaire / on-site / third-party / rating; do not omit
5. **documentation Document**: certificate / report / renewal / blocklist; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from criteria → assessment → documentation → governance → measurement progressively; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: review is only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with supplier-management**: qualification + supplier management co-build
13. **link with supplier-onboarding**: qualification + supplier onboarding co-build
14. **link with supplier-audit**: qualification + supplier audit co-build
15. **link with sourcing**: qualification + sourcing co-build
16. **link with procurement**: qualification + procurement co-build
17. **toolchain**: Coupa Risk Aware / Aravo / HiBob / EcoVadis / RapidRatings
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why qualification strategy must exist; the worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can default solve; if solvable don't introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: qualification the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
