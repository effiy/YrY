## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identify + assess + respond + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by risk + trust + cost + speed + compliance; not sloganeering
3. **Identify Identify**: compliance / talent / culture / data / workplace injury; do not omit
4. **Assess Assess**: probability + impact + level + trend; do not omit
5. **Respond Respond**: avoid / reduce / transfer / accept; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement Measure**: coverage + remediation + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from identify → assess → respond → governance → measurement progressive; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: compliance only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with hr-compliance**: risk + compliance co-built
13. **Link with workforce-risk**: HR + workforce risk co-built
14. **Link with talent-risk**: HR + talent risk co-built
15. **Link with risk-management**: HR + enterprise risk co-built
16. **Link with hr-operations**: risk + operations co-built
17. **Toolchain**: RSA Archer / ServiceNow GRC / LogicGate / NAVEX / Convercent
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must HR risk strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (risk / trust / cost / speed)
23. **Occam**: HR risk the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
