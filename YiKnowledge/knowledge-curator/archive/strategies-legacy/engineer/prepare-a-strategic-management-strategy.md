## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: formulation + execution + retrospective + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Formulate**: analysis / choice / route / decision; do not omit
4. **Execute**: decomposition / resource / action / monitoring; do not omit
5. **Review**: gap / cause / adjust / learning; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from formulation -> execution -> retrospective -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: planning is only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with strategic-planning**: management + planning co-built
13. **link with corporate-strategy**: management + corporate strategy co-built
14. **link with business-performance**: management + business performance co-built
15. **link with governance**: management + governance co-built
16. **link with corporate-development**: management + corporate development co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Balanced Scorecard / OKR / McKinsey / BCG / Hoshin Kanri
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must strategic management strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: strategic management the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
