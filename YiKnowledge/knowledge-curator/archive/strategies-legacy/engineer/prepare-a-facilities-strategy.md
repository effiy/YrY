## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: assets + service + safety + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + cost + risk; not sloganeering
3. **Assets**: building / equipment / furniture / systems / renovation; do not omit
4. **Service**: HVAC / power / security / cleaning / catering; do not omit
5. **Safety**: fire / emergency / inspection / compliance / drill; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + cost + risk; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from assets → service → safety → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with workplace**: Facilities + Workplace co-build
13. **Link with future-of-work**: Facilities + FoW co-build
14. **Link with real-estate**: Facilities + RealEstate co-build
15. **Link with vendor-management**: Facilities + Vendor co-build
16. **Link with sustainability**: Facilities + Sustainability co-build
17. **Toolchain**: IBM Tririga / Planon / ServiceChannel / Facilio / Corfix
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must Facilities; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can property backup solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / cost)
23. **Occam**: Facilities the simpler the better; cut redundant outsourcing
