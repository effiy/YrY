## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: planning + acquisition + development + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Plan**: staffing / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Acquire**: recruiting / onboarding / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Develop**: training / promotion / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from planning → acquire → develop → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with talent-management**: HumanResources + TalentManagement co-build
13. **Link with hr-operations**: HumanResources + HROperations co-build
14. **Link with compensation**: HumanResources + Compensation co-build
15. **Link with benefits**: HumanResources + Benefits co-build
16. **Link with performance-management**: HumanResources + PerformanceManagement co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Workday / SAP SuccessFactors / Oracle HCM / BambooHR / ADP
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why HumanResources is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by letting each manager self-manage; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: HumanResources, the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
