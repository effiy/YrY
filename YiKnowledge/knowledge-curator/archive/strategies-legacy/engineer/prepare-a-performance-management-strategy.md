## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: goals + feedback + evaluation + development + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by revenue-per-capita + retention + key-role supply + strategy landing + satisfaction; not sloganeering
3. **Goals**: OKR / KPI + individual + team + company + alignment; do not omit
4. **Feedback**: 1:1 + real-time + 360 + context + transparency; do not omit
5. **Evaluate**: cadence + rating + calibration + fairness + transparency; do not omit
6. **Develop**: IDP + high-potential + succession + transfer + upgrade; do not omit
7. **Measure**: revenue-per-capita + retention + key-roles + satisfaction + calibration distribution; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from goals -> feedback -> evaluation -> development -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with hr**: PM + HR co-built
13. **Link with talent**: PM + talent co-built
14. **Link with compensation**: PM + compensation co-built
15. **Link with learning-and-development**: PM + L&D co-built
16. **Link with culture**: PM + culture co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Lattice / 15Five / Leapsome / Reflektive / Betterworks / Workday Talent / Culture Amp / Small Improvements
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must PM strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by gut-call assessment; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / retention / business)
23. **Occam**: PM the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
