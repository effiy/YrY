## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: budget + forecast + analysis + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Budget Budget**: annual / rolling / zero-based / flexible; do not omit
4. **Forecast Forecast**: revenue / cost / cash / risk; do not omit
5. **Analysis Analyze**: variance / drivers / scenarios / decisions; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from budget → forecast → analysis → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: making tables is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link to corporate-finance**: FP&A + corporate finance co-build
13. **Link to finance**: FP&A + finance co-build
14. **Link to budgeting**: FP&A + budgeting co-build
15. **Link to cost-management**: FP&A + cost management co-build
16. **Link to profit-margin**: FP&A + profit margin co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Anaplan / Workday Adaptive Planning / Oracle EPM / SAP BPC / IBM Cognos
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must we have an FP&A strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: FP&A the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
