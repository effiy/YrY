## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identify + layered + prioritization + governance + measurement; no missing dimension.
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; no empty slogans.
3. **Identify**: functional / emotional / social / value; no omissions.
4. **Segment**: must / expects / excited / indifferent; no omissions.
5. **Prioritize**: impact / urgency / cost / risk; no omissions.
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; no omissions.
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; no omissions.
8. **Not one-shot**: gradual from identify → layered → prioritization → governance → measurement; no skipping levels.
9. **No report-ism**: lists are only the start; not the end.
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must mark implementation evidence; no vagueness.
11. **Versioned**: the strategy is versioned; evolution is traceable.
12. **Link with persona-research**: needs + role co-build.
13. **Link with user-persona**: needs + persona co-build.
14. **Link with customer-research**: needs + customer co-build.
15. **Link with customer-interview**: needs + interview co-build.
16. **Link with jobs-to-be-done**: needs + JTBD co-build.
17. **Toolchain**: Productboard / Aha! / Dovetail / Notion / Miro.
18. **Publicly discoverable**: the strategy is publicly discoverable; not hidden.
19. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot.
20. **First principles**: why we must do a customer-needs strategy; the worst consequence of not doing it.
21. **Inversion**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy.
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk).
23. **Occam's razor**: simpler customer needs is better; cut redundant layers.
