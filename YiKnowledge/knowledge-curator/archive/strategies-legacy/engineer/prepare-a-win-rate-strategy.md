## Action recommendations

1. **five dimensions**: baseline + decomposition + action + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by win-rate improvement + revenue + ACV + cycle-time + renewal; not sloganeering
3. **baseline Baseline**: historical / industry / cohort / segment / stage; do not omit
4. **decomposition Decompose**: segment / stage / cohort / competitor / deal-type; do not omit
5. **action Action**: playbook / training / product / pricing / positioning; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: win-rate improvement + revenue + ACV + cycle-time + renewal; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from baseline → decomposition → action → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **and win-loss-analysis linkage**: win-rate + win-loss co-build
13. **and deal-review linkage**: win-rate + deal-review co-build
14. **and sales-pipeline linkage**: win-rate + sales pipeline co-build
15. **and forecast-accuracy linkage**: win-rate + forecast accuracy co-build
16. **and competitor-analysis linkage**: win-rate + competitor analysis co-build
17. **toolchain**: Clari / Salesforce / Gong / Boost-UP / Insight-Square / People-AI
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must win-rate; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: rely on intuition how much can be solved; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention) 
23. **Occam**: win-rate the simpler the better; cut redundant dimensions
