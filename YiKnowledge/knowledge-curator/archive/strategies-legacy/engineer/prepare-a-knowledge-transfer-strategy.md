## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identify + capture + transfer + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **identify Identify**: knowledge / holder / priority / risk / cadence; do not omit
4. **capture Capture**: documentation / video / template / checklist / case library; do not omit
5. **transfer Transfer**: cadence / channel / verification / feedback / loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from identify → capture → transfer → governance → measurement progressive; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with knowledge-management**: KT + KM co-built
13. **link with onboarding**: KT + Onboarding co-built
14. **link with offboarding**: KT + Offboarding co-built
15. **link with mentoring**: KT + Mentoring co-built
16. **link with lessons-learned**: KT + Lessons co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Notion / Confluence / Guru / Tettra / Bloomfire
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must KT; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can documentation alone solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: KT the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
