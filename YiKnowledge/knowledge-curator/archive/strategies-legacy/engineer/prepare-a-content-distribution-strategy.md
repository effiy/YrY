## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: channel + timing + format + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Channel**: owned / earned / paid / shared; do not omit
4. **Timing**: calendar / cadence / event / experiment; do not omit
5. **Format**: long / short / video / audio / interactive; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from channel → timing → format → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: posting is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with content**: distribution + content co-built
13. **Link with content-operations**: distribution + operations co-built
14. **Link with seo-content**: distribution + SEO co-built
15. **Link with content-repurposing**: distribution + repurposing co-built
16. **Link with influencer-marketing**: distribution + influencer co-built
17. **Toolchain**: HubSpot / Buffer / Sprout Social / Hootsuite / Mailchimp
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must content distribution strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, don't introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: distribution the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
