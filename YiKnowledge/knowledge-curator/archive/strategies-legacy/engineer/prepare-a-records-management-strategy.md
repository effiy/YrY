## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: create + retain + dispose + governance + measurement; no missing dimension.
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering.
3. **Create**: classify / archive / closed loop; do not omit.
4. **Retain**: storage / retrieval / closed loop; do not omit.
5. **Dispose**: retention period / destruction / closed loop; do not omit.
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit.
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit.
8. **Not one-shot**: from create → retain → dispose → governance → measurement, gradual; no skipping.
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end.
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague.
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable.
12. **Link with information-governance**: RecordsManagement + InformationGovernance co-build.
13. **Link with compliance-management**: RecordsManagement + ComplianceManagement co-build.
14. **Link with data-retention**: RecordsManagement + DataRetention co-build.
15. **Link with policy-management**: RecordsManagement + PolicyManagement co-build.
16. **Link with audit-compliance**: RecordsManagement + AuditCompliance co-build.
17. **Toolchain**: SharePoint / OpenText / M-Files / Laserfiche / NetDocuments.
18. **Publicly queryable**: anyone can look up the strategy; not hidden.
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot.
20. **First principles**: why must RecordsManagement; worst consequence of not doing it.
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can relying on paper records solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy.
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk).
23. **Occam**: simpler RecordsManagement is better; cut redundant classifications.
