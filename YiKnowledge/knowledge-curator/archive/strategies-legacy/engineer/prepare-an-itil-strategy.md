## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: service + lifecycle + continual + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Service**: strategy / directory / tier; do not omit
4. **Lifecycle**: design / transition / operations / continual; do not omit
5. **Continual**: measurement + improvement + retrospective; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from service → lifecycle → continual → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: process is only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with it-service-management**: ITIL + ITSM co-build
13. **link with it-service-catalog**: ITIL + service directory co-build
14. **link with cmdb**: ITIL + CMDB co-build
15. **link with it-asset-management**: ITIL + asset co-build
16. **link with incident-management**: ITIL + incident co-build
17. **Toolchain**: ServiceNow / BMC Helix / Jira Service Management / Freshworks / Cherwell
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must ITIL strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: rely on default how much can be solved; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: ITIL the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
