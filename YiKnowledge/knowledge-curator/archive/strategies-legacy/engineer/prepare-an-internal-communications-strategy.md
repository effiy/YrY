## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + content + channel + cadence + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by eNPS + retention + strategy landing + decision speed + satisfaction; not sloganeering
3. **Audience**: exec + team + individual + new hires + departing employees; do not omit
4. **Content**: strategy + decisions + performance + culture + education + crisis; do not omit
5. **Channel**: email / Slack / Teams / intranet / all-hands / 1:1; do not omit
6. **Cadence**: daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly / annual + upgrades; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: eNPS + retention + strategy landing + decision speed + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from audience → content → channel → cadence → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Linked with corporate-communications**: internal + corporate co-built
13. **Linked with culture**: internal + culture co-built
14. **Linked with customer-communications**: internal + customer co-built
15. **Linked with employer-branding**: internal + EB co-built
16. **Linked with employee-engagement**: internal + engagement co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Workplace / Staffbase / Simpplr / Happeo / Poppulo / Smarp / ContactMonkey / EmailAnalytics
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why internal communications strategy is needed; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by email blasts; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / retention / business)
23. **Occam**: internal communications, the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
