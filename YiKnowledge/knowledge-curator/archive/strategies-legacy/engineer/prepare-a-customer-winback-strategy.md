## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identification + reach + conversion + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Identify**: churn / signal / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Reach**: channel / content / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Convert**: offer / process / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from identification -> reach -> conversion -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with customer-retention**: Winback + Retention co-built
13. **link with churn-prediction**: Winback + ChurnPrediction co-built
14. **link with customer-recovery**: Winback + Recovery co-built
15. **link with customer-success**: Winback + CS co-built
16. **link with customer-lifecycle**: Winback + Lifecycle co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Winback / ReSci / Optimove / Braze / Iterable
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must Winback; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can new customers solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: Winback the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
