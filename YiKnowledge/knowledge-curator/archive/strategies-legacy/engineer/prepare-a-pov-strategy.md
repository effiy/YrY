## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: value + measurement + validation + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by win-rate + cycle-time + POV-to-close + revenue + renewal; not sloganeering
3. **Value**: ROI / payback / business / strategic / risk; do not omit
4. **Measure**: baseline / threshold / validation / measurement / decision; do not omit
5. **Validate**: data / time / scope / responsibility / exit path; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure (external)**: win-rate + cycle-time + POV-to-close + revenue + renewal; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from value → measurement → validation → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with poc**: POV + POC co-built
13. **Link with pilot**: POV + pilot co-built
14. **Link with roi-calculator**: POV + ROI co-built
15. **Link with value-selling**: POV + value selling co-built
16. **Link with business-case**: POV + business case co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce / Clari / Gong / Excel / Google-Sheets / Tableau
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why POV is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by POC; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention) 
23. **Occam**: POV: the simpler the better; cut redundant dimensions
