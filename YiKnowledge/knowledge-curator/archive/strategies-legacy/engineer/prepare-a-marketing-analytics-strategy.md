## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: metrics + models + insights + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Metric Metric**: funnel / conversion / LTV / CAC / retention; do not omit
4. **Model Model**: attribution / segmentation / prediction / prescription; do not omit
5. **Insight Insight**: reports / dashboards / alerts / actions; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from metrics → models → insights → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: number of reports is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link to marketing-operations**: marketing analytics + marketing operations co-build
13. **Link to growth-marketing**: marketing analytics + growth marketing co-build
14. **Link to marketing-attribution**: marketing analytics + marketing attribution co-build
15. **Link to customer-insights**: marketing analytics + customer insights co-build
16. **Link to marketing-data**: marketing analytics + marketing data co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Google Analytics 4 / Adobe Analytics / Amplitude / Mixpanel / Tableau
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must we have a marketing analytics strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: marketing analytics the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
