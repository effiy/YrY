## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Analysis + choice + roadmap + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: Prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Analysis**: External / internal / customer / trends; do not omit
4. **Choice**: Strategy / option / decision / trade-off; do not omit
5. **Roadmap**: Phases / resources / milestones / exit; do not omit
6. **Governance**: Owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: Coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: Progressive from analysis → choice → roadmap → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: PPT only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: Every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: Strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with strategic-management**: Planning + management co-build
13. **Link with corporate-strategy**: Planning + corporate strategy co-build
14. **Link with business-strategy**: Planning + business strategy co-build
15. **Link with capability-based-planning**: Planning + capability planning co-build
16. **Link with business-architecture**: Planning + business architecture co-build
17. **Toolchain**: McKinsey 7S / Balanced Scorecard / OKR / BCG / Strategyzer
18. **Publicly queryable**: Strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: Evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: Why must strategic planning strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: Rely on default how much can be solved; if solvable don't introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: Second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: Strategic planning the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
