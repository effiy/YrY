## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Partner + content + event + co-built + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: Prioritize by joint deals + registered opportunities + MQL + SQL + ARR; not sloganeering
3. **Partner**: Tier + head + long-tail + profile + qualification; do not omit
4. **Content**: Co-brand + case + webinar + landing + asset library; do not omit
5. **Event**: Tradeshow / webinar / joint-launch / roadshow / conference; do not omit
6. **Co-built**: MDF + reimbursement + reporting + joint deal + revenue share; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: Joint deal ARR + registered opportunities + conversion rate + MDF ROI + brand exposure; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: Progressive from partner → content → event → co-built → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: Reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: Every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: Strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with partnership**: PM + partnership strategy co-built
13. **Link with partner-success**: PM + partner success co-built
14. **Link with demand-generation**: PM + demand generation co-built
15. **Link with customer-journey**: PM + journey co-built
16. **Link with content**: PM + content co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Crossbeam / Reveal / Allbound / Impartner / PartnerStack / 6sense / Demandbase / Mutiny
18. **Publicly queryable**: Strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: Evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: Why must PM strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: Rely on single-sided marketing how much can be solved; if solvable don't introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: Second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / channel conflict / business) 
23. **Occam**: PM the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
