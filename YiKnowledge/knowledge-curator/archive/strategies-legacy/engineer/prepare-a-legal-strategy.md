## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: transactions + support + risk control + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Transact**: contract / document / filing / seal; do not omit
4. **Support**: consult / train / review / accompany; do not omit
5. **Risk**: identify / assess / mitigate / respond; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from transactions -> support -> risk control -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: litigation is only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with regulatory**: legal + regulatory co-built
13. **link with contract**: legal + contract co-built
14. **link with ip**: legal + IP co-built
15. **link with compliance**: legal + compliance co-built
16. **link with policy**: legal + policy co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Ironclad / ContractWorks / DocuSign CLM / Thomson Reuters / LexisNexis
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must legal strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: legal the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
