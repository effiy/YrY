## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: vision + scenarios + tech + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Vision Vision**: mission / north star / value; do not omit
4. **Scenario Scenario**: business / use case / priority; do not omit
5. **Tech Tech**: model / platform / data; do not omit
6. **Governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement Measure**: value + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from vision → scenarios → tech → governance → measurement progressive; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: scenario count only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with ml-strategy**: AI + ML strategy co-build
13. **Link with llm-strategy**: AI + LLM strategy co-build
14. **Link with ai-platform**: strategy + AI platform co-build
15. **Link with digital-transformation**: AI + digital transformation co-build
16. **Link with ai-governance**: AI + AI governance co-build
17. **Toolchain**: HuggingFace / OpenAI / Anthropic / Vertex AI / SageMaker
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must AI strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: AI strategy the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
