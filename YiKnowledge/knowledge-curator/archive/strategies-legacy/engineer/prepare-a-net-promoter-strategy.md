## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: ask + classify + analyze + improve + governance; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **ask Ask**: question / timing / channel / frequency; do not omit
4. **classify Classify**: promoters / passive / detractors; do not omit
5. **analyze Analyze**: root cause / theme / closed loop / tracking; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from ask -> classify -> analyze -> improve -> governance; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: score is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **linked with customer-satisfaction**: NPS + satisfaction co-built
13. **linked with customer-feedback**: NPS + feedback co-built
14. **linked with customer-pulse**: NPS + pulse co-built
15. **linked with customer-effort**: NPS + CES co-built
16. **linked with customer-loyalty**: NPS + loyalty co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Delighted / Promoter.io / Qualtrics NPS / SurveyMonkey / Typeform
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why NPS strategy is required; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: NPS the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
