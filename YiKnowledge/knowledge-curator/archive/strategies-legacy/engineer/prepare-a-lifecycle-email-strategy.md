## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: stage + trigger + content + cadence + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by email → conversion + LTV + retention + revenue + churn; not sloganeering
3. **Stage**: onboarding / activation / adoption / expansion / win-back; do not omit
4. **Trigger**: behavior / time / score / stage / hand-raise; do not omit
5. **Content**: welcome / educate / case / resources / retirement; do not omit
6. **Cadence**: daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly / milestone; do not omit
7. **Measure**: email → conversion + LTV + retention + revenue + churn; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from stage → trigger → content → cadence → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with email-marketing**: lifecycle email + email marketing co-built
13. **Link with lifecycle-marketing**: lifecycle email + lifecycle marketing co-built
14. **Link with customer-lifecycle**: lifecycle email + customer lifecycle co-built
15. **Link with lead-nurturing**: lifecycle email + lead nurturing co-built
16. **Link with customer-engagement**: lifecycle email + customer engagement co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Customer.io / Iterable / Braze / Marketo / Hubspot / Klaviyo / ActiveCampaign / Postmark
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must lifecycle email; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can newsletter solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: lifecycle email the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
