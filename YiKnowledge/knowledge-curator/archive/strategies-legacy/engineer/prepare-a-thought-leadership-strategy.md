## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: POV + channel + cadence + influence + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by references + forwards + leads + revenue + brand; not sloganeering
3. **POV**: original / industry / contrarian / trend / framework; do not omit
4. **Channel**: blog / podcast / speaking / media / social; do not omit
5. **Cadence**: weekly / monthly / quarterly / adjust / reuse; do not omit
6. **Influence**: followers / references / speaking / media / ranking; do not omit
7. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
8. **Measure**: references + forwards + leads + revenue + brand; do not omit
9. **Not one-shot**: progressive from POV → channel → cadence → influence → measurement; no skipping
10. **Not report-only**: reports are only the start; not the end
11. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
12. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
13. **Link with content**: Thought Leadership + Content co-built
14. **Link with personal-branding**: Thought Leadership + Personal Branding co-built
15. **Link with blog**: Thought Leadership + Blog co-built
16. **Link with podcast**: Thought Leadership + Podcast co-built
17. **Link with pr**: Thought Leadership + PR co-built
18. **Toolchain**: LinkedIn / Twitter / Substack / Medium / Notion
19. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
20. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
21. **First principles**: why must Thought Leadership; worst consequence of not doing it
22. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by ads; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
23. **Occam**: Thought Leadership the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
