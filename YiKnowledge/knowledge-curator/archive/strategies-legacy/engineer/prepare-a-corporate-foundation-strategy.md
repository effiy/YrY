## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Charter + governance + grantmaking + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: Prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Charter**: Vision / funding scope / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Board**: Directors / governance / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Grantmaking**: Application / review / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: Owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: Efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: Progressive from charter → governance → grantmaking → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: Reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: Every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: Strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with corporate-philanthropy**: Foundation + Philanthropy co-build
13. **Link with corporate-citizenship**: Foundation + Citizenship co-build
14. **Link with corporate-social-responsibility**: Foundation + CSR co-build
15. **Link with corporate-sustainability**: Foundation + Sustainability co-build
16. **Link with stakeholder-engagement**: Foundation + StakeholderEngagement co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Foundation Source / Blackbaud / CyberGrants / Fidelity Charitable / Schwab Charitable
18. **Publicly queryable**: Strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: Evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: Why must Foundation; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: How much can be solved by direct donation; if solvable do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: Second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: Foundation the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
