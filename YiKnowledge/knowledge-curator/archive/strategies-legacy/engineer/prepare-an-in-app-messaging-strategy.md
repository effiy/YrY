## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + content + trigger + experiment + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by adoption + conversion + retention + NPS + revenue; not sloganeering
3. **Audience**: new / mid / advanced / variant / cohort; do not omit
4. **Content**: tour / tooltip / modal / banner / coach-mark + retirement; do not omit
5. **Triggers**: behavior + time + attribute + state + upgrade; do not omit
6. **Experiments**: A/B + multi-variant + priority + learning + upgrade; do not omit
7. **Measure**: adoption + conversion + retention + NPS + revenue; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from audience → content → trigger → experiment → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with feature-adoption**: in-app + adoption co-built
13. **Link with onboarding-flow**: in-app + onboarding co-built
14. **Link with activation**: in-app + activation co-built
15. **Link with customer-communications**: in-app + customer comms co-built
16. **Link with content**: in-app + content co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Appcues / Userpilot / Pendo / Kentvita / Chameleon / Userflow / Klaviyo
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must in-app messaging strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by in-product embedded copy; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / experience / business)
23. **Occam**: in-app messaging the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
