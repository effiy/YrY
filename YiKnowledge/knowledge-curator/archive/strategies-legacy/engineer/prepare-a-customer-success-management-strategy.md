## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: planning + execution + monitoring + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Planning**: segmentation / resources / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Execution**: playbook / cadence / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Monitoring**: health / risk / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from planning → execution → monitoring → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with customer-success**: CSM + CustomerSuccess co-build
13. **Link with customer-success-operations**: CSM + CSOps co-build
14. **Link with account-management**: CSM + AccountManagement co-build
15. **Link with customer-lifecycle**: CSM + CustomerLifecycle co-build
16. **Link with customer-retention**: CSM + Retention co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Gainsight / Totango / Catalyst / ChurnZero / ClientSuccess
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must CSM; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can CSM alone solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler CSM is, the better; cut redundant layers
