## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: dimensions + signals + segments + application + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by LTV + CAC + retention + ARR + win-rate; no sloganeering
3. **Dimensions**: firmographic + technographic + behavioral + needs + value; do not omit
4. **Signals**: fit + intent + opportunity + engagement + trigger; do not omit
5. **Segments**: best-fit / good-fit / expansion / churn-risk; do not omit
6. **Apply**: marketing + sales + product + customer service + strategy; do not omit
7. **Measure**: LTV + CAC + retention + ARR + win-rate; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: gradual from dimensions → signals → segments → application → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with market-segmentation**: ICP + market segmentation co-build
13. **Link with customer-segmentation**: ICP + customer segmentation co-build
14. **Link with positioning**: ICP + positioning co-build
15. **Link with product-strategy**: ICP + product strategy co-build
16. **Link with go-to-market**: ICP + GTM co-build
17. **Toolchain**: 6sense / Demandbase / Bombora / Clearbit / ZoomInfo / Apollo / Cognism / LinkedIn Sales Navigator
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy is queryable by everyone; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must an ICP strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by relying on mass marketing; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / channel / business) 
23. **Occam**: ICP simpler is better; cut redundant steps
