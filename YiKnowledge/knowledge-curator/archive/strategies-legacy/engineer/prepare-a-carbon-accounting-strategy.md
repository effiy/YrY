## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: scope + data + method + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Scope**: scope 1 / scope 2 / scope 3; do not omit
4. **Data**: activity / emission factors / units / sources; do not omit
5. **Method**: GHG Protocol / ISO 14064 / SBTi / CDP; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from scope -> data -> method -> governance -> measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: accounting is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with carbon-management**: carbon accounting + carbon management co-built
13. **Link with esg**: carbon accounting + ESG co-built
14. **Link with esg-reporting**: carbon accounting + ESG reporting co-built
15. **Link with sustainability**: carbon accounting + sustainability co-built
16. **Link with supply-chain-risk**: carbon accounting + supply chain risk co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Persefoni / Watershed / Sweep / Plan A / Microsoft Sustainability Cloud
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must a carbon accounting strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: carbon accounting, the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
