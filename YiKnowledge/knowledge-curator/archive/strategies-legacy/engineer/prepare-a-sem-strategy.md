## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: keywords + landing + bid + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Keyword**: intent / groups / long-tail / negative; do not omit
4. **Landing**: match / conversion / speed / test; do not omit
5. **Bid**: manual / smart / goal / ROAS; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from keywords -> landing -> bid -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: click counts are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Interplay with seo**: search marketing + SEO co-built
13. **Interplay with paid-search**: search marketing + paid search co-built
14. **Interplay with paid-media**: search marketing + paid media co-built
15. **Interplay with content**: search marketing + content co-built
16. **Interplay with marketing-analytics**: search marketing + marketing analytics co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Google Ads / Microsoft Ads / SEMrush / Ahrefs / WordStream
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a search marketing strategy is required; the worst consequence of not having one
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler the search marketing the better; cut redundant layers
