## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: baseline + decomposition + action + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by ACV lift + ARR + revenue + cycle-time + renewal; not sloganeering
3. **Baseline**: history / industry / cohort / segment / deal-size; do not omit
4. **Decompose**: new / expansion / segment / cohort / deal-size; do not omit
5. **Action**: playbook / training / product / price / positioning; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: ACV lift + ARR + revenue + cycle-time + renewal; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from baseline → decomposition → action → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with arr**: ACV + ARR co-built
13. **Link with nrr**: ACV + NRR co-built
14. **Link with pricing**: ACV + Pricing co-built
15. **Link with discounting**: ACV + Discounting co-built
16. **Link with sales-pipeline**: ACV + Sales Pipeline co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce / Clari / ChartMogul / Baremetrics / Snowflake / Boost-UP
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must ACV; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by revenue totals; if solvable, don't introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: ACV the simpler the better; cut redundant dimensions
