## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: eligibility + structure + plan + settlement + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by partner-sourced revenue + CAC + renewal + ROAS; no sloganeering
3. **Eligibility**: tiers + certification + performance / role + tier; do not omit
4. **Structure**: front-end / back-end / MDF / SPIF / rebate / tier; do not omit
5. **Plan**: quota + coefficients + accelerators + cliffs + cliff; do not omit
6. **Settlement**: deal-reg / eligibility verification / invoices / payment / audit; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: partner-sourced revenue + CAC + renewal + ROAS + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from eligibility → structure → plan → settlement → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **No sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with partner-program**: partner compensation + partner program co-build
13. **Link with partner-operations**: partner compensation + partner operations co-build
14. **Link with sales-compensation**: partner compensation + sales compensation co-build
15. **Link with quota**: partner compensation + quota co-build
16. **Link with revenue-operations**: partner compensation + revenue operations co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce-PRM / Impartner / Channeltivity / ZayZay / Allbound / Tackle / Metronome
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must partner compensation; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can one-off rebates solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / channel conflict / renewal)
23. **Occam**: partner compensation the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
