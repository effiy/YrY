## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: mind + differentiation + value + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **mind Mind**: category / keywords / associations / positioning / loop; do not omit
4. **differentiation Differentiate**: competition / advantage / weakness / loop / loop; do not omit
5. **value Value**: functional / emotional / symbolic / experience / loop; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from mind → differentiation → value → governance → measurement progressive; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with brand-identity**: BrandPositioning + BrandIdentity co-build
13. **link with brand-architecture**: BrandPositioning + BrandArch co-build
14. **link with positioning-statement**: BrandPositioning + PositioningStmt co-build
15. **link with messaging-framework**: BrandPositioning + MessagingFramework co-build
16. **link with product-positioning**: BrandPositioning + ProductPositioning co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Brandwatch / Sprout Social / Qualtrics / BrandZ / Kantar
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must BrandPositioning; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can advertising solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: BrandPositioning the simpler the better; cut redundant dimensions
