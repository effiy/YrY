## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: scope + gradient + alignment + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Scope**: level / band / grade / scope; do not omit
4. **Gradient**: promotion / level / standard / cadence; do not omit
5. **Alignment**: internal / external / role / value; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from scope -> gradient -> alignment -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: level numbers only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with job-architecture**: Job leveling + Job architecture co-build
13. **Link with employee-compensation**: Job leveling + Employee compensation co-build
14. **Link with career-architecture**: Job leveling + Career architecture co-build
15. **Link with career-pathing**: Job leveling + Career path co-build
16. **Link with job-evaluation**: Job leveling + Job evaluation co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Mercer / Korn Ferry / Radford / Salary.com / Payscale
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must job leveling strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: Job leveling the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
