## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: line + layer + touchpoint + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Line**: interaction / visible / internal / failure / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Layer**: customer / front-stage / back-stage / support / management; do not omit
5. **Touchpoint**: physical / digital / channel / human / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from line -> layer -> touchpoint -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with service-design**: ServiceBlueprint + ServiceDesign co-built
13. **Link with customer-experience**: ServiceBlueprint + CX co-built
14. **Link with customer-journey**: ServiceBlueprint + CustomerJourney co-built
15. **Link with journey-orchestration**: ServiceBlueprint + JourneyOrchestration co-built
16. **Link with process-excellence**: ServiceBlueprint + ProcessExcellence co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Miro / Smaply / UXPressia / Lucidchart / Figma
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must ServiceBlueprint; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can a process diagram solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: ServiceBlueprint the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
