## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: procurement + logistics + closed loop + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; no empty slogans
3. **Sourcing**: compliance / closed loop; no leakage
4. **Logistics**: carbon / closed loop; no leakage
5. **Closed loop**: traceability / closed loop; no leakage
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; no leakage
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; no leakage
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from procurement → logistics → closed loop → governance → measurement; no skipping levels
9. **No report-ism**: report is just the start; not the end
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must mark implementation evidence; no vagueness
11. **Versioned**: strategy is versioned; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with supply-chain**: SustainableSupplyChain + SupplyChain co-build
13. **Link with sustainability**: SustainableSupplyChain + Sustainability co-build
14. **Link with supplier-diversity**: SustainableSupplyChain + SupplierDiversity co-build
15. **Link with carbon-management**: SustainableSupplyChain + CarbonManagement co-build
16. **Link with circular-economy**: SustainableSupplyChain + CircularEconomy co-build
17. **Toolchain**: SAP Ariba / Ecovadis / Sourcemap / IBM Supply Chain Intelligence / Resilinc
18. **Publicly discoverable**: strategy is publicly discoverable; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: Evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must SustainableSupplyChain; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion**: how much can compliance solve; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequence after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam's razor**: simpler SustainableSupplyChain is better; cut redundant steps
