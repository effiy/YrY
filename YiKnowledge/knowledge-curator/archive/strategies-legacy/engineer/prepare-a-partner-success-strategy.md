## Action recommendations

1. **five dimensions**: Recruit + enablement + co-sell + Measurement + iteration; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by partner revenue share + renewal + co-deal + referral + satisfaction; no empty slogans
3. **Recruit**: persona + qualifications + onboarding + trial fit + retire; no leakage
4. **enablement Enablement**: training + certification + materials + demo + quote; no leakage
5. **Co-sell**: opportunities + reporting + registration + co-deal + revenue share; no leakage
6. **Measurement Measure**: partner revenue + registration opportunities + co-deal rate + certification rate + NPS; no leakage
7. **iteration Iterate**: feedback + policy update + retire + upgrade; no leakage
8. **Not one-shot**: from recruit → enablement → co-sell → Measurement → iteration gradual; no skipping levels
9. **no report-ism**: report is just the start; not the end
10. **no empty slogans**: every principle must mark implementation evidence; no vagueness
11. **Versioned**: strategy is versioned; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with partnership**: PS + partnership strategy Co-build
13. **Link with partner-marketing**: PS + joint marketing Co-build
14. **Link with customer-success**: PS + CS Co-build
15. **Link with customer-education**: PS + education Co-build
16. **Toolchain**: Crossbeam / Reveal / Allbound / Impartner / PartnerStack / Tackle / Zift / Maggioli PRM
17. **Publicly discoverable**: strategy is publicly discoverable; not hidden
18. **Regular review**: Evolve and update; not one-shot
19. **First principles**: why must PS strategy; worst consequence of not doing
20. **Inversion**: how much can be solved by relying on natural referral; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
21. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequence after strategy (cost / risk / channel conflict / business)
22. **Occam's razor**: PS simpler is better; redundant step cut
