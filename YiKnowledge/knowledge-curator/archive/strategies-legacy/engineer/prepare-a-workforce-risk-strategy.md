## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: supply + skills + compliance + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by risk + trust + speed + cost + compliance; not sloganeering
3. **Supply**: quantity / structure / turnover / gap; do not omit
4. **Skill**: gap / stagnation / obsolescence / transformation; do not omit
5. **Compliance**: working hours / workplace injury / labor unrest / labor law; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + remediation + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from supply -> skills -> compliance -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ism**: headcount shortage is only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **linked with hr-risk**: workforce + HR risk co-build
13. **linked with talent-risk**: workforce + talent risk co-build
14. **linked with workforce-planning**: risk + planning co-build
15. **linked with workforce-management**: risk + management co-build
16. **linked with risk-management**: workforce + enterprise risk co-build
17. **Toolchain**: RSA Archer / ServiceNow GRC / Visier / OneModel / Korn Ferry
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must workforce-risk strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by defaults; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (risk / trust / speed / cost) 
23. **Occam**: workforce risk the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
