## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: time + tasks + compliance + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + compliance + cost + experience + risk; no sloganeering
3. **Time**: scheduling / attendance / leave / hours; do not omit
4. **Tasks**: allocation / dispatch / tracking / completion; do not omit
5. **Compliance**: labor law / hours / overtime / leave; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + compliance + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from time → tasks → compliance → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: scheduling is only the start; not the end
10. **No sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with workforce-planning**: management + planning co-built
13. **Link with workforce-optimization**: management + optimization co-built
14. **Link with workforce-transformation**: management + transformation co-built
15. **Link with hr-shared-services**: management + shared services co-built
16. **Link with hr-operations**: management + operations co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Kronos / UKG Pro Workforce / Workday Time / ADP Workforce Now / Deputy
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must a workforce-management strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / compliance / cost / experience)
23. **Occam**: workforce management the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
