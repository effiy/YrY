## Action recommendations

1. **six dimensions**: requirement + budget + time + channel + interview + offer; no missing dimension
2. **requirement driven**: by business cadence + team gap; not gut call
3. **budget explicit**: each role must tag budget; no vagueness
4. **time window**: each role must tag window; not unlimited
5. **channel classification**: referral + headhunter + recruitment platform + campus hire; not mixed
6. **interview rubric**: each role must tag rubric; no vagueness
7. **interviewer training**: interviewers must train + calibration; not by word of mouth
8. **structured interview**: each round must tag assessment points; not chat
9. **offer cadence**: issue + negotiation + accept + onboarding; not drag
10. **diversity**: each role must tag diversity target; not deviate
11. **links with budget**: recruitment goes through budget approval
12. **links with ladder**: role level aligns with ladder
13. **links with onboarding**: offer acceptance goes through onboarding process
14. **links with succession**: key role recruitment aligns with succession
15. **links with team charter**: recruitment aligns with team charter
16. **links with diagnosis**: org diagnosis finds gap and goes to recruitment to fill
17. **Publicly discoverable**: everyone can look up the plan; not hidden
18. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot
19. **First principles**: why must plan; worst consequence of not doing it
20. **Inversion**: how much can referrals solve; if solvable, don't introduce plan
21. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequence after plan (coherence / retention / hiring / trust)
22. **Occam's razor**: plan simpler is better; cut redundant steps
