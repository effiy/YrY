## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Hazard + risk + barriers + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: Prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **hazard Hazard**: Identification / assessment / closed loop; do not omit
4. **risk Risk**: Scenario / frequency / consequence / closed loop; do not omit
5. **barrier Barrier**: Prevention / mitigation / emergency / closed loop; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: Owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: Efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: From hazard -> risk -> barriers -> governance -> measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: Reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: Every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: Strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Linked with manufacturing**: ProcessSafety + Manufacturing co-build
13. **Linked with quality-management**: ProcessSafety + QualityManagement co-build
14. **Linked with environmental**: ProcessSafety + Environmental co-build
15. **Linked with continuous-improvement**: ProcessSafety + ContinuousImprovement co-build
16. **Linked with operations**: ProcessSafety + Operations co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Sphera / DNV PHAST / ProcessMap / bowtie XP / Efficy
18. **publicly queryable**: Strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: Evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: Why must ProcessSafety; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: How much can PPE solve; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: Second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: ProcessSafety the simpler the better; cut redundant barriers
