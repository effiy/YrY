## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: key roles + persona + development + measurement + iteration; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: set priority by key role supply + succession rate + retention + business continuity + strategy landing; not sloganeering
3. **key roles Critical Roles**: CEO / CFO / CTO / CPO + key technical + region + business line; do not omit
4. **persona Persona**: capability + experience + culture + motivation + risk; do not omit
5. **development Develop**: IDP + high-potential + experience + rotation + coaching; do not omit
6. **measurement Measure**: succession rate + key role fill + retention + upgrade rate + business continuity; do not omit
7. **iteration Iterate**: feedback + update + version + retire + upgrade; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from key roles → persona → development → measurement → iteration; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with talent**: SP + talent co-built
13. **link with hr**: SP + HR co-built
14. **link with performance-management**: SP + performance co-built
15. **link with leadership**: SP + leadership co-built
16. **link with learning-and-development**: SP + L&D co-built
17. **toolchain**: Gloat / Eightfold / Lattice / Cornerstone / SAP SuccessFactors / Workday Talent / Saba / SumTotal
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must SP strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can temporary hiring solve; if solvable don't introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / retention / business)
23. **Occam**: SP the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
