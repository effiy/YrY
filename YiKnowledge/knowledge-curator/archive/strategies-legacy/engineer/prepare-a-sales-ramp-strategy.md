## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: baseline + path + cadence + support + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by ramp-time + ramp-revenue + quota attainment + retention + NPS; not sloganeering
3. **baseline Baseline**: industry + role + history + capacity + hours; do not omit
4. **path Path**: day-30 / day-60 / day-90 / quarter / year; do not omit
5. **cadence Cadence**: daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly / milestone; do not omit
6. **support Support**: mentor + resources + system + tools + upgrade; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: ramp-time + ramp-revenue + quota attainment + retention + NPS; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from baseline → path → cadence → support → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **and sales-readiness link**: ramp + sales readiness co-build
13. **and sales-enablement link**: ramp + sales enablement co-build
14. **and sales-coaching link**: ramp + sales coaching co-build
15. **and quota link**: ramp + quota co-build
16. **and field-enablement link**: ramp + field enablement co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Mindtackle / Second-Nature / Highspot / Seismic / Showpad / Gong-Enablement / Lessonly / Saleshood
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must ramp strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can self-study solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: ramp the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
