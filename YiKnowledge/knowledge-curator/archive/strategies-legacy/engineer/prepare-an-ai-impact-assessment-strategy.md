## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: risk + impact + assessment + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Risk**: identification / analysis / prioritization; do not omit
4. **Impact**: individual / society / economy / environment / human rights; do not omit
5. **Assess**: method / evidence / report / retrospective; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from risk -> impact -> assessment -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: item counts are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with ai-risk-assessment**: impact assessment + AI risk assessment co-built
13. **Link with ai-audit**: impact assessment + AI audit co-built
14. **Link with ai-responsibility**: impact assessment + AI responsibility co-built
15. **Link with ai-governance**: impact assessment + AI governance co-built
16. **Link with ai-compliance**: impact assessment + AI compliance co-built
17. **Toolchain**: EU AIA / Canada AIA / NIST AI RMF / Custom / Custom
18. **Publicly queryable**: everyone can look up the strategy; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why an AI impact assessment strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: AI impact assessment the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
