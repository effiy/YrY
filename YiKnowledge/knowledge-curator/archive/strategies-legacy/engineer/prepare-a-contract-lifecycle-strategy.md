## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: drafting + negotiation + fulfillment + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Drafting**: template / clauses / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Negotiation**: red lines / versions / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Fulfillment**: signature / obligations / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from drafting → negotiation → fulfillment → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with contract-management**: ContractLifecycle + ContractManagement co-build
13. **Link with legal-operations**: ContractLifecycle + LegalOperations co-build
14. **Link with sourcing**: ContractLifecycle + Sourcing co-build
15. **Link with vendor-management**: ContractLifecycle + VendorManagement co-build
16. **Link with procurement-operations**: ContractLifecycle + ProcurementOperations co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Icertis / DocuSign CLM / Agiloft / Conga / SAP Ariba
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must ContractLifecycle; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can Word + email solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: ContractLifecycle the simpler the better; cut redundant phases
