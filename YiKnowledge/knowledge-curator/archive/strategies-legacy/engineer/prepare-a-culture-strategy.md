## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: values + behaviors + rituals + measurement + iteration; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by retention + candidate NPS + business supply + decision speed + satisfaction; not sloganeering
3. **Values**: mission + vision + values + principles + boundaries; do not omit
4. **Behaviors**: do / don't + case + counter-example + feedback + evaluation; do not omit
5. **Rituals**: all-hands / retro / 1:1 / decision meeting + onboarding + retirement + milestones; do not omit
6. **Measure**: eNPS + retention + candidate NPS + culture survey + decision speed; do not omit
7. **Iterate**: feedback + update + version + retirement + upgrade; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from values -> behaviors -> rituals -> measurement -> iteration; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with hr**: culture + HR co-built
13. **link with talent**: culture + talent co-built
14. **link with learning-and-development**: culture + L&D co-built
15. **link with leadership**: culture + leadership co-built
16. **link with product-strategy**: culture + product strategy co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Culture Amp / Lattice / Bonusly / 15Five / Officevibe / Qualtrics / TinyPulse / Motivis
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must culture strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can natural evolution solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / retention / business)
23. **Occam**: culture the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
