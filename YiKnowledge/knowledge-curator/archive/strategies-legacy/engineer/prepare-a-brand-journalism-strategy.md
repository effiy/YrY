## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Editorial + content + channel + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: Prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Editorial Editorial**: newsroom / editor-in-chief / calendar / standards; do not omit
4. **Content Content**: news / deep-dive / video / podcast / report; do not omit
5. **Channel Channel**: official site / social / internal / email / cross-region; do not omit
6. **Governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: Progress from editorial → content → channel → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: press-release count is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with corporate-storytelling**: brand journalism + corporate storytelling co-built
13. **Link with content**: brand journalism + content co-built
14. **Link with corporate-communications**: brand journalism + corporate communications co-built
15. **Link with employee-advocacy**: brand journalism + employee advocacy co-built
16. **Link with thought-leadership**: brand journalism + thought leadership co-built
17. **Toolchain**: NewsCred / Contently / Brandwatch / Pitcher / Sharethrough
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must the brand-journalism strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by defaults; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: brand journalism — the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
