## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: accounting + budgeting + control + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Account**: direct / indirect / fixed / variable; do not omit
4. **Budget**: rolling / zero-based / elastic / business-finance; do not omit
5. **Control**: threshold / approval / variance / retrospective; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from accounting → budgeting → control → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: compression is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with cost-reduction**: cost + cost reduction co-built
13. **Link with spend-management**: cost + spend management co-built
14. **Link with finance**: cost + finance co-built
15. **Link with profit-margin**: cost + profit margin co-built
16. **Link with procurement**: cost + procurement co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Anaplan / Oracle EPM / SAP BPC / Workday Adaptive Planning / IBM Cognos
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must cost strategy; the worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler cost is, the better; cut redundant layers
