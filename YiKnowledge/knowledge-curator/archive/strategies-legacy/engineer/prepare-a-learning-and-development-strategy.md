## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: competency + pathway + content + measurement + iteration; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by business impact + retention + promotion + business metrics; not sloganeering
3. **Competency**: core + technical + soft skills + leadership + business + role; do not omit
4. **Pathway**: career + learning path + level + promotion + certification; do not omit
5. **Content**: foundation + work-stream + advanced + case + template + assessment; do not omit
6. **Measurement**: completion rate + certification rate + business impact + retention + satisfaction; do not omit
7. **Iteration**: feedback + update + version + roadmap + retirement; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from competency → pathway → content → measurement → iteration; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with product-strategy**: L&D + strategy co-built
13. **Link with customer-education**: L&D + education co-built
14. **Link with customer-success**: L&D + customer success co-built
15. **Link with product-roadmap**: L&D + roadmap co-built
16. **Toolchain**: L-D-Framework / Docebo / Cornerstone / Workday Learning / Degreed / Coursera / Udemy Business / LinkedIn Learning / Skillshare
17. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
18. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
19. **first principles**: why must L&D strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
20. **inversion thinking**: how much can natural learning solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
21. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / retention / business) 
22. **Occam**: L&D the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
