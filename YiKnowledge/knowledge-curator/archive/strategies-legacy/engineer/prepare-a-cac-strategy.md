## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: marketing + sales + tools + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by CAC reduction + revenue + speed + conversion + efficiency; not sloganeering
3. **Marketing**: channels / campaigns / content / events / SEO; do not omit
4. **Sales**: SDR / AE / salary / commission / tools; do not omit
5. **Tools**: CRM / MAP / automation / data / analytics; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: CAC reduction + revenue + speed + conversion + efficiency; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from marketing → sales → tools → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with ltv-cac**: CAC + LTV/CAC co-build
13. **Link with marketing-attribution**: CAC + marketing attribution co-build
14. **Link with budget-planning**: CAC + budget co-build
15. **Link with lead-scoring**: CAC + lead scoring co-build
16. **Link with customer-lifecycle**: CAC + lifecycle cadence co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Snowflake / Salesforce / Marketo / 6sense / HubSpot / Outreach
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy accessible to everyone; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must CAC; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can LTV growth solve; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / speed / retention)
23. **Occam**: the simpler CAC is, the better; cut redundant assumptions
