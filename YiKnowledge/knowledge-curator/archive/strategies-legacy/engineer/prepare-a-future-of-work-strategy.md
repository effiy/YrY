## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: people + place + time + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by talent + trust + speed + cost + risk; not sloganeering
3. **People**: skills / role / team / collaboration / culture; do not omit
4. **Place**: office / home / third-party / shared / metaverse; do not omit
5. **Time**: sync / async / flexible / cross-region / extreme; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: talent + trust + speed + cost + risk; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from people → place → time → governance → measurement progressive; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with employee-experience**: FoW + EX co-build
13. **Link with remote-work-policy**: FoW + Remote co-build
14. **Link with culture**: FoW + Culture co-build
15. **Link with org-design**: FoW + Org co-build
16. **Link with leadership**: FoW + Leadership co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Microsoft Viva / Workday / Lattice / Culture Amp / Slack
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why FoW is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by the traditional model; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (talent / trust / speed / cost)
23. **Occam**: simpler FoW is better; cut redundant policies
