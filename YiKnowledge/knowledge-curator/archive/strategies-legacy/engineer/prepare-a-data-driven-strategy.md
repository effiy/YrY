## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: decision + experiment + measurement + governance + culture; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Decision**: problem / assumption / action; do not omit
4. **Experiment**: ab / multi-variable / online; do not omit
5. **Measure**: metrics / north star / dashboard; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: adoption + value + risk + cost + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from decision -> experiment -> measurement -> governance -> culture; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: decision numbers are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with data-culture**: data-driven + culture co-built
13. **Link with data-literacy**: data-driven + literacy co-built
14. **Link with data-democratization**: data-driven + democratization co-built
15. **Link with data-analytics**: data-driven + analytics co-built
16. **Link with data-strategy**: data-driven + data strategy co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Optimizely / Statsig / GrowthBook / Amplitude / Mixpanel
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must data-driven; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by intuition; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: data-driven the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
