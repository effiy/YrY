## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: regulations + process + evidence + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by trust + risk + compliance + speed + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Regulations**: GDPR / CCPA / SOX / HIPAA / ISO; do not omit
4. **Process**: identify / assess / control / supervise / close loop; do not omit
5. **Evidence**: logs / audit / reports / trail / archive; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: trust + risk + compliance + speed + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from regulations → process → evidence → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Linked with corporate-governance**: Compliance + Governance co-built
13. **Linked with risk**: Compliance + Risk co-built
14. **Linked with audit**: Compliance + Audit co-built
15. **Linked with regulatory-affairs**: Compliance + Regulatory co-built
16. **Linked with information-security**: Compliance + Security co-built
17. **Toolchain**: OneTrust / LogicGate / Compliance.ai / Vanta / Drata
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why Compliance is needed; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by verbal compliance; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (trust / risk / compliance / speed)
23. **Occam**: Compliance, the simpler the better; cut redundant process
