## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: assessment + planning + intervention + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Assess**: stage / capability / trust / conflict; do not omit
4. **Plan**: goal / path / resources / cadence; do not omit
5. **Intervene**: training / workshop / coaching / retrospective; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from assessment → planning → intervention → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: days are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with team**: team development + team co-built
13. **Link with team-design**: team development + team design co-built
14. **Link with team-enablement**: team development + team enablement co-built
15. **Link with employee-development**: team development + employee development co-built
16. **Link with team-communications**: team development + team communications co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Lattice / 15Five / Workday / Range / FridayPulse
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must team development strategy; the worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler team development is, the better; cut redundant layers
