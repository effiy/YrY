## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + content + channel + cadence + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by ARR + win-rate + velocity + retention + revenue share; not sloganeering
3. **Audience**: named + tier + target + pursuit + switching; do not omit
4. **Content**: account profile / business case / co-creation plan / demo / reviews; do not omit
5. **Channel**: 1:1 / email / video / on-site / owned / ABM ads; do not omit
6. **Cadence**: T+0 / +7 / +30 / +90 + upgrade; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: ARR + win-rate + velocity + retention + revenue share; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress gradually from audience → content → channel → cadence → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with ABM**: ABS + ABM co-built
13. **Link with ABX**: ABS + ABX co-built
14. **Link with sales-playbook**: ABS + playbook co-built
15. **Link with mutual-close-plan**: ABS + co-creation co-built
16. **Link with sales-development**: ABS + sales development co-built
17. **Toolchain**: 6sense / Demandbase / Bombora / Clearbit / ZoomInfo / Apollo / Cognism / LinkedIn-Sales-Nav / Outreach
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why an ABS strategy is necessary; the worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved with general sales alone; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / revenue / brand)
23. **Occam**: the simpler ABS is, the better; cut redundant steps
