## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: account + role + relationship + pain point + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by mapped account revenue + win-rate + cycle-time + renewal + NRR; not sloganeering
3. **Account**: industry / revenue / size / tier / multiple business lines; do not omit
4. **Stakeholder**: champion / decision / influence / blocker / user; do not omit
5. **Relationship**: strength / stance / history / trust / upgrade; do not omit
6. **Pain point**: business + value + ROI + priority + validation; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: mapped account revenue + win-rate + cycle-time + renewal + NRR; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from account → role → relationship → pain point → measurement progressively; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with account-based-marketing**: account mapping + ABM co-built
13. **Link with account-based-selling**: account mapping + ABS co-built
14. **Link with sales-territory**: account mapping + sales territory co-built
15. **Link with ideal-customer-profile**: account mapping + ICP co-built
16. **Link with stakeholder-engagement**: account mapping + stakeholder co-built
17. **Toolchain**: ZoomInfo / Apollo / Clearbit / Cognism / LinkedIn-Sales-Nav / 6sense / Demandbase / OrgChart
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must account mapping; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by CRM contact; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: account mapping the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
