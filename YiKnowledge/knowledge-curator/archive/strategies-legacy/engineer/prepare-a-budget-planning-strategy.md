## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: goal + allocation + control + measurement + governance; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by budget contribution to revenue + ROI + CAC + LTV + cash; not sloganeering
3. **Goal**: OKR + north star + capacity + revenue + cash; do not omit
4. **Allocation**: team / channel / initiative / quarter / project; do not omit
5. **Control**: threshold + approval + adjustment + reallocation + block; do not omit
6. **Measure**: budget contribution to revenue + ROI + CAC + LTV + cash; do not omit
7. **Governance**: cadence + approval + transparency + accountability + drift; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from goal → allocation → control → measurement → governance; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with budget**: budget planning + budget co-build
13. **Link with business-operations**: budget planning + business operations co-build
14. **Link with revenue-operations**: budget planning + revenue operations co-build
15. **Link with marketing-attribution**: budget planning + attribution co-build
16. **Link with okr-cycle**: budget planning + OKR co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Anaplan / Adaptive / NetSuite / Workday / Pigment / Cube / Vena / Mosaic
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must budget planning; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can one-shot budgeting solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / cash)
23. **Occam**: budget planning the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
