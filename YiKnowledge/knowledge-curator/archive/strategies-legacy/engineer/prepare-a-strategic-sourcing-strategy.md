## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: need + market + negotiation + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **need Need**: spec / SLA / closed loop; do not omit
4. **market Market**: supply / risk / closed loop; do not omit
5. **negotiate Negotiate**: tco / closed loop; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from need → market → negotiation → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **links with procurement**: StrategicSourcing + Procurement co-build
13. **links with category**: StrategicSourcing + Category co-build
14. **links with spend-analysis**: StrategicSourcing + SpendAnalysis co-build
15. **links with vendor-performance**: StrategicSourcing + VendorPerformance co-build
16. **links with supplier-diversity**: StrategicSourcing + SupplierDiversity co-build
17. **toolchain**: SAP Ariba Sourcing / Coupa Sourcing / Ivalua / Jaggaer / Keelvar
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why StrategicSourcing is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can price comparison solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: StrategicSourcing — the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
