## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: strategy + structure + execution + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by focus + value + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Strategy**: why sell / what to sell / to whom / cadence / value; do not omit
4. **Structure**: legal / tax / financial / operations / governance; do not omit
5. **Execution**: TS / SPA / transition / separation / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: focus + value + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from strategy → structure → execution → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with m-and-a**: Divestiture + M&A co-build
13. **Link with corporate-governance**: Divestiture + Governance co-build
14. **Link with change-management**: Divestiture + Change co-build
15. **Link with legal-operations**: Divestiture + LegalOps co-build
16. **Link with risk**: Divestiture + Risk co-build
17. **Toolchain**: DealRoom / Transcript / PitchBook / Crunchbase / Affinity
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy accessible to everyone; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must Divestiture; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can retention solve; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (focus / value / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler Divestiture is, the better; cut redundant structure
