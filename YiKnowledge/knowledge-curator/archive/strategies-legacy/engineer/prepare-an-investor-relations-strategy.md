## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + message + cadence + channel + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by valuation + confidence + liquidity + conversion + experience; not sloganeering
3. **Audience Audience**: institution / retail / analyst / media / internal; do not omit
4. **Message Message**: performance / guidance / strategy / risk / events; do not omit
5. **Cadence Cadence**: quarterly report / roadshow / 1on1 / conference / analyst; do not omit
6. **Channel Channel**: phone / email / meeting / website / regulatory; do not omit
7. **Governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
8. **Measurement Measure**: valuation + confidence + liquidity + conversion + experience; do not omit
9. **Not one-shot**: from audience → message → cadence → channel → measurement progressive; no skipping
10. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
11. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
12. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
13. **Link with analyst-relations**: IR + AR co-built
14. **Link with brand-identity**: IR + Brand co-built
15. **Link with public-relations**: IR + PR co-built
16. **Link with financial-planning**: IR + FP co-built
17. **Link with corporate-communications**: IR + Corp Comms co-built
18. **Toolchain**: Q4 / Notified / FactSet / Bloomberg / Salesforce
19. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
20. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
21. **First principles**: why must IR; worst consequence of not doing it
22. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by disclosure; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
23. **Occam**: IR the simpler the better; cut redundant actions
