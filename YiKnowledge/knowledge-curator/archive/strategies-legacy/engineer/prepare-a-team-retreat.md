## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: goals + agenda + logistics + communication + retrospective; no missing dimension
2. **Clear goals**: strategy + relationships + retrospective + learning; not vague
3. **Have outputs**: every retreat must have outputs; not a vacation
4. **Agenda**: tag every segment; not vague
5. **Logistics**: venue + food + lodging + transport; do not omit
6. **Budget**: must tag the budget; not vague
7. **Communication**: before + during + after; not silent
8. **Retrospective**: after the retreat must run a retrospective; not one-shot
9. **Team co-built**: agenda co-built; not arranged
10. **Diversity**: activities consider diversity; not single-type
11. **Duration**: 1-3 days; not over-long
12. **Frequency**: quarterly / half-year; not one-shot
13. **Link with team charter**: retreat aligns with team charter
14. **Link with quarterly retrospective**: retreat follows the quarterly retrospective
15. **Link with retrospective**: retreat follows the retrospective process
16. **Link with all-hands**: retreat outputs feed into all-hands
17. **Link with hackathon**: retreat + hackathon co-built
18. **Link with budget**: retreat goes through budget approval
19. **Publicly queryable**: everyone can look up the plan; not hidden
20. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
21. **First principles**: why a retreat is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
22. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved with all-hands; if solvable do not introduce a retreat
23. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the retreat (relationships / trust / retention / hiring) 
24. **Occam**: retreat the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
