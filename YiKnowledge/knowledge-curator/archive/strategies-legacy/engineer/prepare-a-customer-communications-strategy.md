## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: channels + cadence + personalization + content + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by open rate + click rate + conversion + retention + unsubscribe; not sloganeering
3. **Channel**: email / push / sms / in-app / call + preference + consent; do not omit
4. **Cadence**: welcome + habit + milestone + risk + reactivation; do not omit
5. **Personalization**: user + context + behavior + time + AI generation; do not omit
6. **Content**: template + brand voice + multi-language + multi-region + compliance; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: open / click / convert / unsubscribe / complaint / reach; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: gradual from channels -> cadence -> personalization -> content -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with customer-journey**: communications + journey co-build
13. **Link with lifecycle-marketing**: communications + LCM co-build
14. **Link with content**: communications + content co-build
15. **Link with customer-success**: communications + CS co-build
16. **Link with customer-onboarding**: communications + onboarding co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Customer.io / Braze / Iterable / Intercom / OneSignal / Twilio / SendGrid / HubSpot / Marketo / AppFlow
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must communications strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by relying on product's built-in notifications; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / unsubscribe / business)
23. **Occam**: the simpler communications the better; cut redundant steps
