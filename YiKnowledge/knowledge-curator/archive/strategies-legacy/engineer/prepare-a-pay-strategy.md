## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: structure + benchmark + adjustment + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **structure Structure**: base / variable / benefits / long-term; do not omit
4. **benchmark Benchmark**: role / grade / market / percentile; do not omit
5. **adjustment Adjust**: general / individual / promotion / transfer; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from structure → benchmark → adjustment → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: payroll only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **and salary link**: pay + salary co-build
13. **and compensation link**: pay + total compensation co-build
14. **and incentive link**: pay + incentive co-build
15. **and rewards link**: pay + total rewards co-build
16. **and job-evaluation link**: pay + job evaluation co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Mercer / Radford / Payscale / Salary.com / Korn Ferry
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must pay strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can default solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: pay the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
