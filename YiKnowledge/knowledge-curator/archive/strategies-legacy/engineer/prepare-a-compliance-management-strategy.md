## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: policy + execution + monitoring + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Policy**: identify / write / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Execute**: communicate / land / closed loop; do not omit
5. **monitoring Monitor**: metrics / thresholds / closed loop; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from policy → execution → monitoring → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with regulatory-compliance**: ComplianceManagement + RegulatoryCompliance co-built
13. **Link with policy-management**: ComplianceManagement + PolicyManagement co-built
14. **Link with audit-compliance**: ComplianceManagement + AuditCompliance co-built
15. **Link with risk**: ComplianceManagement + Risk co-built
16. **Link with records-management**: ComplianceManagement + RecordsManagement co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Compliance.ai / NAVEX / SAI Global / Convercent / LogicGate
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must ComplianceManagement; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by post-hoc accountability; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: the simpler ComplianceManagement the better; cut redundant process
