## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: accounting + reduction + offset + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Accounting**: scope1 / scope2 / scope3 / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Reduction**: goal / path / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Offset**: carbon credits / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from accounting → reduction → offset → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with environmental**: CarbonManagement + Environmental co-build
13. **Link with sustainability**: CarbonManagement + Sustainability co-build
14. **Link with energy-management**: CarbonManagement + EnergyManagement co-build
15. **Link with circular-economy**: CarbonManagement + CircularEconomy co-build
16. **Link with supplier-diversity**: CarbonManagement + SupplierDiversity co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Persefoni / Watershed / Sweep / Plan A / Salesforce Net Zero Cloud
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must CarbonManagement; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can buying offsets solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: CarbonManagement the simpler the better; cut redundant accounting
