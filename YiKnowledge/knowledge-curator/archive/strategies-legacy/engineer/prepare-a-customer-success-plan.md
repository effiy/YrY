## Action recommendations

1. **Five stages**: onboarding + adoption + retention + value-add + renewal; no missing stage
2. **Proactive**: proactive contact + proactive discovery + proactive drive; not passive waiting
3. **Not just problem-solving**: drive business value; not just tickets
4. **Customer tiering**: assign CSM by ARR / strategic level; not averaged
5. **Health score**: every customer must have a health score; not vague
6. **Risk early warning**: low score triggers intervention; not relying on humans
7. **QBR**: quarterly business review; do not skip
8. **Value-add**: upsell + cross-sell; not just retention
9. **Renewal**: renewal plan + N months ahead; not last-minute
10. **Churn analysis**: every lost account must retrospective; do not omit
11. **Link with customer feedback**: feedback goes through CS
12. **Link with escalation**: CS escalations follow escalation path
13. **Link with metrics**: NRR / GRR / churn / health
14. **Link with GTM**: CS + GTM co-built
15. **Link with advisory board**: key customers join advisory board
16. **Link with release notes**: CS syncs release notes
17. **Toolchain**: Gainsight / Totango / in-house
18. **Publicly queryable**: process + tools everyone can find; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must CS; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by support; if solvable, do not introduce CS
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after CS (retention / revenue / trust / expansion)
23. **Occam**: CS the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
