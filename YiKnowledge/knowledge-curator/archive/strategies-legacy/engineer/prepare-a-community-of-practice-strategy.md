## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: domain + member + practice + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by adoption + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Domain**: theme / boundary / goal / value / cadence; do not omit
4. **Members**: sponsor / lead / core / active / lurker; do not omit
5. **Practice**: sharing / learning / projects / tools / output; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: adoption + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from domain → member → practice → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with mentoring**: CoP + Mentoring co-build
13. **Link with knowledge-management**: CoP + KM co-build
14. **Link with learning-and-development**: CoP + L&D co-build
15. **Link with career-development**: CoP + Career co-build
16. **Link with culture**: CoP + Culture co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Slack / Confluence / Notion / Circle / Mighty Networks
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must CoP; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can spontaneous effort solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (adoption / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: CoP the simpler the better; cut redundant communities
