## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + content + relations + cadence + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by brand equity + trust + revenue + retention + SOV; no sloganeering
3. **Audience**: media + analysts + KOL + influencer + government + customer; do not omit
4. **Content**: press release + speech + whitepaper + case + crisis + commentary; do not omit
5. **Relations**: tier 1/2/3 + beat journalist + briefing + tour + embargo; do not omit
6. **Cadence**: daily + quarterly + annual + crisis + milestone; do not omit
7. **Measure**: SOV + sentiment + trust + revenue + retention; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from audience → content → relations → cadence → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **No sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with corporate-communications**: PR + corporate communications co-build
13. **Link with brand**: PR + brand co-build
14. **Link with analyst-relations**: PR + AR co-build
15. **Link with content**: PR + content co-build
16. **Link with crisis-communications**: PR + crisis co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Cision / Meltwater / Muck Rack / PR Newswire / Business Wire / Critical Mention / Mention / Brand24 / Prowly
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must have PR strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by ad-hoc media relations; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / brand / business)
23. **Occam**: PR the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
