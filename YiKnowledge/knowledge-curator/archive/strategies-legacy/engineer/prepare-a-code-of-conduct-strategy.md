## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: principle + behavior + training + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by trust + speed + risk + compliance + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Principle**: honesty / responsibility / fairness / transparency / care; do not omit
4. **Behavior**: must do / forbidden / gray area / escalation / disposition; do not omit
5. **Training**: new hire / annual / high-risk / leadership / acknowledgment; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: trust + speed + risk + compliance + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: gradual from principle → behavior → training → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with ethics**: CoC + Ethics co-build
13. **Link with whistleblower**: CoC + Whistleblower co-build
14. **Link with compliance**: CoC + Compliance co-build
15. **Link with corporate-governance**: CoC + Governance co-build
16. **Link with culture**: CoC + Culture co-build
17. **Toolchain**: LRN / NAVEX / Convercent / SAI360 / EthicsPoint
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must CoC; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by compliance; if solvable don't introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (trust / speed / risk / compliance)
23. **Occam**: CoC simpler is better; cut redundant rules
