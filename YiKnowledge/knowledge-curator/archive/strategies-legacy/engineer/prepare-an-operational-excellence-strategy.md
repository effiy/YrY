## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: culture + method + enablement + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Culture**: customer / transparency / kaizen / respect; do not omit
4. **Method**: DMAIC / value-stream / 5S / Kaizen; do not omit
5. **Enablement**: training / tools / projects / certification; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from culture -> method -> enablement -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: projects are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with continuous-improvement**: operational excellence + continuous improvement co-built
13. **Link with lean**: operational excellence + Lean co-built
14. **Link with six-sigma**: operational excellence + Six Sigma co-built
15. **Link with process-optimization**: operational excellence + process optimization co-built
16. **Link with quality**: operational excellence + quality co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Kainexus / PowerSteering / Trello / iObeya / KaiNexus
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must operational excellence strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: operational excellence the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
