## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: process + self-service + center of excellence + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; no sloganeering
3. **Process**: standardization / automation / SLA / escalation; do not omit
4. **Self-Service**: employee portal / manager portal / mobile / AI assistant; do not omit
5. **Center of Excellence**: payroll / benefits / talent / learning; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from process → self-service → center of excellence → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: ticket count is only the start; not the end
10. **No sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with hr-operations**: HR shared services + HR operations co-build
13. **Link with hr-service-delivery**: HR shared services + HR service delivery co-build
14. **Link with hr-business-partner**: HR shared services + HR business partner co-build
15. **Link with hr-transformation**: HR shared services + HR transformation co-build
16. **Link with employee-experience**: HR shared services + employee experience co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Workday / ServiceNow HR / SAP SuccessFactors / Oracle HCM / UKG Pro
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must have HR shared services strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by defaults; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: HR shared services the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
