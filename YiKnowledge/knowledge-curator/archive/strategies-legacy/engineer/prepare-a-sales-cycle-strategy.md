## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: baseline + stages + acceleration + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by cycle-time reduction + revenue + win-rate + renewal + NRR; not sloganeering
3. **Baseline**: historical / industry / segment / cohort / deal-size; do not omit
4. **Stages**: qualify / discover / propose / negotiate / close; do not omit
5. **Acceleration**: playbook / training / tools / path / automation; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: cycle-time reduction + revenue + win-rate + renewal + NRR; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from baseline → stages → acceleration → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with sales-pipeline**: sales cycle + sales pipeline co-build
13. **Link with win-rate**: sales cycle + win-rate co-build
14. **Link with deal-review**: sales cycle + deal-review co-build
15. **Link with forecast-accuracy**: sales cycle + forecast accuracy co-build
16. **Link with opportunity-management**: sales cycle + opportunity management co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Clari / Salesforce / Gong / Boost-UP / Insight-Square / People-AI
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a sales cycle strategy is a must; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can intuition solve; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: the simpler the sales cycle the better; cut redundant stages
