## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: structure + decision + process + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + compliance; not sloganeering
3. **Structure**: hierarchy / committees / roles / responsibilities; do not omit
4. **Decision**: type / authority / cadence / escalation; do not omit
5. **Process**: agenda / input / output / follow-up; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from structure → decision → process → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: meetings are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with corporate-strategy**: governance + company strategy co-built
13. **Link with enterprise-architecture**: governance + enterprise architecture co-built
14. **Link with business-architecture**: governance + business architecture co-built
15. **Link with decision-rights**: governance + decision rights co-built
16. **Link with strategic-planning**: governance + strategic planning co-built
17. **Toolchain**: RSA Archer / ServiceNow GRC / Diligent / LogicGate / NAVEX
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must there be a governance strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler the governance the better; cut redundant layers
