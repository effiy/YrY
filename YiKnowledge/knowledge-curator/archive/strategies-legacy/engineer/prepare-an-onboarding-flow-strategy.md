## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: steps + triggers + channels + experiments + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by activation + retention + revenue + conversion + NPS; not sloganeering
3. **Steps**: account / setup / first-value / habit / habit-lock; do not omit
4. **Triggers**: behavior + time + attribute + state + upgrade; do not omit
5. **Channels**: in-app / email / push / sales / partner; do not omit
6. **Experiments**: A/B + multi-variant + prioritization + learning + upgrade; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: activation + retention + revenue + conversion + NPS; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from steps → triggers → channels → experiments → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with customer-onboarding**: flow + customer onboarding co-built
13. **Link with activation**: flow + activation co-built
14. **Link with trial**: flow + trial co-built
15. **Link with PLG**: flow + PLG co-built
16. **Link with feature-adoption**: flow + adoption co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Appcues / Userpilot / Pendo / Mixpanel / Amplitude / PostHog / Kochava / LaunchDarkly / Customer.io
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why an onboarding flow strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by in-tool embedding; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / retention / business)
23. **Occam**: onboarding flow — the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
