## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: matching + accountability + cadence + communication + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by sponsorship-attributed revenue + renewal + NRR + strategic alignment + retention; not sloganeering
3. **Match**: ICP + industry + role + relationship + capacity; do not omit
4. **Accountability**: goal + executive + AM / CS + milestones + escalation; do not omit
5. **Cadence**: kick-off / monthly / quarterly / milestone / annual; do not omit
6. **Communication**: meeting / email / phone / digital / executive; do not omit
7. **Measure**: sponsorship-attributed revenue + renewal + NRR + strategic alignment + retention; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from match → accountability → cadence → communication → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with executive-briefing**: executive sponsorship + executive briefing co-build
13. **Link with account-engagement**: executive sponsorship + account manager engagement co-build
14. **Link with account-based-selling**: executive sponsorship + ABS co-build
15. **Link with mutual-action-plan**: executive sponsorship + MAP co-build
16. **Link with ideal-customer-profile**: executive sponsorship + ICP co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce / Outreach / Salesloft / Clari / Gong / 6sense / Demandbase / LinkedIn-Sales-Nav
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why executive sponsorship is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by AM alone; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention) 
23. **Occam**: executive sponsorship, the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
