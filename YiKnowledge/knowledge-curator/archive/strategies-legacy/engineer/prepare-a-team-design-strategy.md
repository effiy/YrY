## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: role + boundary + collaboration + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **role Role**: mission / responsibility / skills / capacity; none missing
4. **boundary Boundary**: interface / scope / ownership / cross-boundary; none missing
5. **collaboration Collaborate**: cadence / decision / communicate / tool; none missing
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / docs / drift; none missing
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; none missing
8. **Not one-shot**: from role → boundary → collaboration → governance → measurement progressively; no skipping levels
9. **Not report-only**: role count is only the starting point; not the endpoint
10. **No sloganeering**: every principle must have landed evidence; no ambiguity
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with team**: team design + team co-build
13. **link with team-topology**: team design + team topology co-build
14. **link with org-design**: team design + org design co-build
15. **link with team-development**: team design + team development co-build
16. **link with team-enablement**: team design + team enablement co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Miro / Lucidchart / TeamTopologies / Notion / Mural
18. **Publicly accessible**: strategy accessible to everyone; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why team-design strategy must exist; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: team design the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
