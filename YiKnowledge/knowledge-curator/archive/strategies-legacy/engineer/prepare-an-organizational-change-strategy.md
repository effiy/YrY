## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: vision + structure + talent + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: set priority by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Vision**: why / what / how / who / when; do not omit
4. **Structure**: architecture / process / role / decision / boundary; do not omit
5. **Talent**: select / use / develop / retain / leave; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from vision -> structure -> talent -> governance -> measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with change-management**: OC + CM co-build
13. **Link with org-design**: OC + OrgDesign co-build
14. **Link with change-communications**: OC + Comms co-build
15. **Link with stakeholder-management**: OC + Stakeholder co-build
16. **Link with leadership**: OC + Leadership co-build
17. **Toolchain**: McKinsey Influence Model / Kotter 8-Step / Prosci ADKAR / Lewin 3-Stage / HiRoom
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must OC; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by natural evolution; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequence after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: OC, the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
