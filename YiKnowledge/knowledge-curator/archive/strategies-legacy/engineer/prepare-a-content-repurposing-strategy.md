## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: decompose + reform + republish + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Decompose**: atom / theme / slice / assets; do not omit
4. **Reform**: format / audience / channel / style; do not omit
5. **Republish**: calendar / frequency / interlink / experiment; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from decompose → reform → republish → governance → measurement progressively; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: moving is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with content**: repurposing + content co-built
13. **Link with content-distribution**: repurposing + distribution co-built
14. **Link with content-operations**: repurposing + operations co-built
15. **Link with editorial-calendar**: repurposing + calendar co-built
16. **Link with content-audit**: repurposing + audit co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Repurpose / Castmagic / Opus Clip / Pictory / Descript
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must content repurposing strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: repurposing the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
