## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: strategy + investment + operations + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + innovation + trust + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Strategy**: apps / infra / data / security / digital; do not omit
4. **Investment**: budget / projects / capital / operations / innovation; do not omit
5. **Operations**: service / infra / network / endpoints / cloud; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + innovation + trust + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress gradually from strategy → investment → operations → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with cto**: CIO + CTO co-built
13. **Link with cdo**: CIO + CDO co-built
14. **Link with it-operations**: CIO + ITOps co-built
15. **Link with dev-ops**: CIO + DevOps co-built
16. **Link with leadership**: CIO + Leadership co-built
17. **Toolchain**: ServiceNow / Jira / Confluence / Smartsheet / Asana
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why CIO is necessary; the worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved with department autonomy; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / innovation / risk / cost)
23. **Occam**: the simpler CIO is, the better; cut redundant systems
