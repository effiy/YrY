## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: intake + plan + execute + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by trust + speed + risk + compliance + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Intake Intake**: channels / tiering / priority / route / paper trail; do not omit
4. **Plan Plan**: scope / team / evidence / legal / cadence; do not omit
5. **Execute Execute**: interviews / evidence / analysis / report / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: trust + speed + risk + compliance + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from intake -> plan -> execute -> governance -> measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with whistleblower**: IX + Whistleblower co-built
13. **Link with ethics**: IX + Ethics co-built
14. **Link with compliance**: IX + Compliance co-built
15. **Link with litigation**: IX + Litigation co-built
16. **Link with internal-audit**: IX + Audit co-built
17. **Toolchain**: NAVEX Case IQ / Convercent / SAI360 / Ethico / i-Sight
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must IX; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can legal solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (trust / speed / risk / compliance)
23. **Occam**: IX simpler is better; cut redundant steps
