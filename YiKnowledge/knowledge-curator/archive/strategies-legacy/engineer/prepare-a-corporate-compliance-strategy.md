## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Regulations + Policies + Training + Governance + Measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Regulatory**: identify / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Policies**: draft / publish / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Training**: courses / communicate / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from Regulations → Policies → Training → Governance → Measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Compliance-management linkage**: CorporateCompliance + ComplianceManagement co-build
13. **Regulatory-compliance linkage**: CorporateCompliance + RegulatoryCompliance co-build
14. **Corporate-governance linkage**: CorporateCompliance + CorporateGovernance co-build
15. **Internal-controls linkage**: CorporateCompliance + InternalControls co-build
16. **Audit-compliance linkage**: CorporateCompliance + AuditCompliance co-build
17. **Toolchain**: NAVEX / Convercent / MetricStream / LogicGate / SAI Global
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must CorporateCompliance exist; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can legal solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: CorporateCompliance the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
