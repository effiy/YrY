## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: goals + methodology + process + team + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by ARR + ACV + CAC + LTV + win rate; not sloganeering
3. **Target**: ICP + TAM + SAM + SOM + account tiering; do not omit
4. **Methodology**: inbound + outbound + ABM + solution-selling + challenger + SPIN; do not omit
5. **Process**: lead -> MQL -> SQL -> opp -> close + stage gate + forecast; do not omit
6. **Team**: SDR + AE + CSM + SE + manager + RACI; do not omit
7. **Measure**: pipeline + win-rate + ACV + sales-cycle + quota-attainment; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: gradual from goals -> methodology -> process -> team -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with go-to-market**: sales + GTM co-build
13. **Link with revenue-ops**: sales + RevOps co-build
14. **Link with partnership**: sales + partnership co-build
15. **Link with pricing**: sales + pricing co-build
16. **Link with pitch**: sales + fundraising co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Sales-Framework / Salesforce / HubSpot / Outreach / Salesloft / Gong / Apollo / Clari / ZoomInfo / LinkedIn Sales Navigator
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must sales strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by relying on PLG; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / CAC / business) 
23. **Occam**: sales the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
