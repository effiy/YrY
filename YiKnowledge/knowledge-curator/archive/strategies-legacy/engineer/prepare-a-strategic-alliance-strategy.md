## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: partner selection + structure + operations + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Partner**: strategy / culture / complementary / credit / long-term; do not omit
4. **Structure**: technology / marketing / supply / R&D / licensing; do not omit
5. **Operations**: people / finance / production / sales / IT; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from partner selection → structure → operations → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with joint-venture**: Alliance + JV co-built
13. **Link with partnership**: Alliance + Partnership co-built
14. **Link with partner-program**: Alliance + Program co-built
15. **Link with co-marketing**: Alliance + CoMarketing co-built
16. **Link with m-and-a**: Alliance + M&A co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Crossbeam / Reveal / PartnerStack / Allbound / Impartner
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must Alliance; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by self-build; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: Alliance the simpler the better; cut redundant clauses
