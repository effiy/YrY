## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: standards + roles + process + measurement + archive; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by business growth + user value + risk + compliance; not sloganeering
3. **Standards**: style + tone + terminology + forbidden + compliance + multilingual; do not omit
4. **Roles**: edit + review + legal + compliance + translation + operations + RACI; do not omit
5. **Process**: submission + review + publish + re-review + takedown + appeal; do not omit
6. **Measure**: submissions + pass rate + reject rate + appeal rate + timeliness + quality; do not omit
7. **Archive**: version + history + audit trail + audit + retention period; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from standards -> roles -> process -> measurement -> archive; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with content-strategy**: governance + content co-built
13. **Link with content-moderation**: governance + review co-built
14. **Link with product-strategy**: governance + strategy co-built
15. **Link with customer-experience**: governance + experience co-built
16. **Link with brand-strategy**: governance + brand co-built
17. **Link with product-marketing**: governance + marketing co-built
18. **Toolchain**: Content-Governance Framework / Content Style Guide / Brand Voice / Acrolinx / Grammarly Business / Vale / YaCDL / Contentful / Strapi / Sanity / Algolia / Elasticsearch
19. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
20. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
21. **First principles**: why must content governance; worst consequence of not doing it
22. **Inversion thinking**: how much can intuition-based publishing solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
23. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / growth / business) 
24. **Occam**: content governance the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
