## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: stage + touchpoint + closed loop + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Stage**: awareness / conversion / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Touchpoint**: channel / content / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Closed loop**: feedback / optimize / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: gradual from stage → touchpoint → closed loop → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with customer-journey-mapping**: CJDesign + CJM co-build
13. **Link with customer-experience-design**: CJDesign + CXDesign co-build
14. **Link with customer-lifecycle**: CJDesign + Lifecycle co-build
15. **Link with service-blueprint**: CJDesign + ServiceBlueprint co-build
16. **Link with customer-onboarding**: CJDesign + Onboarding co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Miro / Lucidchart / Smaply / UXPressia / Custellix
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must CJDesign; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by funnel; if solvable don't introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: CJDesign simpler is better; cut redundant layers
