## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + scope + cadence + feedback + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by defect count + adoption rate + NPS + retention + revenue; not sloganeering
3. **Audience**: champion + design partner + friendly customer + internal + public; do not omit
4. **Scope**: feature + platform + data + quota + retirement; do not omit
5. **Cadence**: recruitment / T0 / T+2 / T+4 / GA + upgrade; do not omit
6. **Feedback**: channel + classification + priority + follow-up + transparency; do not omit
7. **Measure**: defect count + adoption rate + NPS + retention + revenue; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from audience -> scope -> cadence -> feedback -> measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ism**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **linked with product-launch**: beta + launch co-build
13. **linked with user-group**: beta + user group co-build
14. **linked with customer-research**: beta + customer research co-build
15. **linked with feature-adoption**: beta + adoption co-build
16. **linked with customer-feedback**: beta + feedback co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Centercode / UserTesting / Betabound /-launchnotes / Productboard / Aha! / Pendo / Mixpanel
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must beta strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved relying on internal QA; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / trust / business) 
23. **Occam**: beta the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
