## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: dimension + factor + verification + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Dimension**: people / machine / material / method / measurement / environment; do not omit
4. **Factor**: enumerate / prioritize / related / leave evidence / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Verify**: data / experiment / counter-example / retrospective / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from dimension -> factor -> verification -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with root-cause-analysis**: Ishikawa + RCA co-build
13. **Link with five-whys**: Ishikawa + 5Whys co-build
14. **Link with blameless-postmortem**: Ishikawa + Postmortem co-build
15. **Link with continuous-improvement**: Ishikawa + CI co-build
16. **Link with quality-management**: Ishikawa + QM co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Miro / Lucidchart / Ishikawa Online / Confluence / MindManager
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must Ishikawa; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can 5Whys solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: Ishikawa the simpler the better; cut redundant dimensions
