## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Deal count + ACV + win rate + cadence + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: Prioritize by velocity lift + revenue + efficiency + speed + conversion; not sloganeering
3. **Deal count Count**: Pipeline / stage / cohort / segment / channel; do not omit
4. **ACV Value**: Unit price / upgrade / discount / cohort / deal-size; do not omit
5. **Win rate Win**: Stage / coaching / competition / cohort / segment; do not omit
6. **cadence Cycle**: Stage / acceleration / delay / cohort / channel; do not omit
7. **governance Governance**: Owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
8. **measurement Measure**: Velocity lift + revenue + efficiency + speed + conversion; do not omit
9. **Not one-shot**: From deal count -> ACV -> win rate -> cadence -> measurement gradual; no skipping
10. **Not report-ized**: Reports are only the start; not the end
11. **Not sloganeering**: Every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
12. **Versioned**: Strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
13. **Linked with win-rate**: Velocity + Win Rate co-build
14. **Linked with sales-cycle**: Velocity + Cycle co-build
15. **Linked with deal-coaching**: Velocity + Coaching co-build
16. **Linked with acv**: Velocity + ACV co-build
17. **Linked with sales-pipeline**: Velocity + Pipeline co-build
18. **Toolchain**: Snowflake / Salesforce / Clari / Gong / InsightSquared
19. **Publicly queryable**: Strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
20. **Periodic review**: Evolution updates; not one-shot
21. **First principles**: Why must Velocity; worst consequence of not doing
22. **Inversion thinking**: How much can total revenue growth solve; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
23. **Occam**: Velocity the simpler the better; cut redundant assumptions
