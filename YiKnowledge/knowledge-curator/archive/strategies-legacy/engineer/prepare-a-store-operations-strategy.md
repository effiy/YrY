## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: staffing + inventory + process + governance + measurement; none can be missing
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Staffing**: scheduling / training / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Inventory**: receiving / display / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Process**: opening / closing / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from staffing → inventory → process → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with retail-operations**: StoreOperations + RetailOperations co-build
13. **Link with merchandising**: StoreOperations + Merchandising co-build
14. **Link with store-format**: StoreOperations + StoreFormat co-build
15. **Link with employee-experience**: StoreOperations + EmployeeExperience co-build
16. **Link with customer-experience**: StoreOperations + CustomerExperience co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Retail Pro / SAP S/4HANA / Salesforce / Workday / NCR
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why StoreOperations is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can store manager experience solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: StoreOperations — the simpler the better; cut redundant reports
