## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + message + channel + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **audience Audience**: tiering / layering / role / persona; do not omit
4. **message Message**: product / policy / training / incentive / event; do not omit
5. **channel Channel**: email / portal / community / event / newsletter; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: reach + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from audience → message → channel → governance → measurement progressive; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: send count is only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with customer-communications**: partner + customer communications co-build
13. **link with team-communications**: partner + team communications co-build
14. **link with internal-communications**: partner + internal communications co-build
15. **link with partner-program**: partner communications + partner program co-build
16. **link with partner-operations**: partner communications + partner operations co-build
17. **Toolchain**: PartnerStack / Allbound / Impartner / PRM / Salesforce Communities
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must partner communications strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can default solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: partner communications the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
