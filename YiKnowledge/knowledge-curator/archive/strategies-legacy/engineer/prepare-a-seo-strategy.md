## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + keywords + technical + content + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by organic + MQL + revenue + ranking + retention; not sloganeering
3. **Audience**: ICP + stage + intent + channel + competition; do not omit
4. **Keywords**: seed / long-tail / intent / SERP / gap; do not omit
5. **Technical**: crawl / index / speed / mobile / schema; do not omit
6. **Content**: pillar / cluster / retire / update / link; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: organic + MQL + revenue + ranking + retention; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: gradual from audience -> keywords -> technical -> content -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with content-marketing**: SEO + content marketing co-build
13. **Link with content**: SEO + content co-build
14. **Link with organic-search**: SEO + organic search co-build
15. **Link with demand-generation**: SEO + funnel co-build
16. **Link with web**: SEO + website co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Ahrefs / Semrush / Moz / Search Console / Botify / -krudo / Screaming-Frog / Sitebulb / Clearscope
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must SEO strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by relying on paid ads; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / brand)
23. **Occam**: the simpler SEO the better; cut redundant steps
