## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: prevention + support + intervention + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: set priority by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **prevention Prevent**: education / culture / workload / flexibility; do not omit
4. **support Support**: EAP / counseling / resources / peer; do not omit
5. **intervention Intervene**: identify / assess / refer / follow-up; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from prevention → support → intervention → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: project count is only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with employee-wellbeing**: mental health + employee wellbeing co-build
13. **link with employee-engagement**: mental health + employee engagement co-build
14. **link with employee-experience**: mental health + employee experience co-build
15. **link with corporate-culture**: mental health + corporate culture co-build
16. **link with employee-retention**: mental health + employee retention co-build
17. **toolchain**: Modern Health / Lyra Health / Ginger / Headspace / Calm
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must employee mental health strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable don't introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: employee mental health the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
