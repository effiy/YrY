## Action recommendations

1. **five dimensions**: role + rhythm + switching + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **role Role**: driver / navigator / closed loop; do not omit
4. **rhythm Rhythm**: pomodoro / switching / closed loop; do not omit
5. **switching Switch**: keyboard / thinking / closed loop; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from role → rhythm → switching → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **and mob-programming linkage**: PairProgramming + MobProgramming co-build
13. **and extreme-programming linkage**: PairProgramming + ExtremeProgramming co-build
14. **and code-review linkage**: PairProgramming + CodeReview co-build

17. **toolchain**: VS Live Share / Tuple / Zoom / Miro / JetBrains Code With Me
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must PairProgramming; worst consequence of not doing
