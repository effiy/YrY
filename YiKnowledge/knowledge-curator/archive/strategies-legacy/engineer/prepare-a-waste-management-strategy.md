## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: source + processing + disposal + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Source**: identify / classify / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Process**: reduce / reuse / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Dispose**: compliance / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from source → processing → disposal → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with environmental**: WasteManagement + Environmental co-built
13. **Link with circular-economy**: WasteManagement + CircularEconomy co-built
14. **Link with sustainability**: WasteManagement + Sustainability co-built
15. **Link with carbon-management**: WasteManagement + CarbonManagement co-built
16. **Link with water-management**: WasteManagement + WaterManagement co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Rubicon / WasteLogics / ISB Global / AMCS / Trux
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why WasteManagement is a must; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by relying on outsourced hauling; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: WasteManagement the simpler the better; cut redundant processes
