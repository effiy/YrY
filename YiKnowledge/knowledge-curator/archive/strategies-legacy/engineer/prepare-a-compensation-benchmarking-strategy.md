## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: benchmark + compare + decision + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Benchmark**: data / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Compare**: gap / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Decide**: salary adjustment / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from benchmark → compare → decision → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with compensation**: CompensationBenchmarking + Compensation co-built
13. **Link with job-architecture**: CompensationBenchmarking + JobArchitecture co-built
14. **Link with job-evaluation**: CompensationBenchmarking + JobEvaluation co-built
15. **Link with benefits**: CompensationBenchmarking + Benefits co-built
16. **Link with sales-compensation**: CompensationBenchmarking + SalesCompensation co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Radford / Mercer / Aon / PayScale / Salary.com
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must CompensationBenchmarking; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can gut feel solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
