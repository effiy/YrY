## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: planning + execution + monitoring + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Plan**: annual / quarterly / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Execute**: projects / tasks / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Monitor**: metrics / deviation / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: gradual from planning -> execution -> monitoring -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with brand-strategy**: BrandManagement + BrandStrategy co-built
13. **Link with brand-equity**: BrandManagement + BrandEquity co-built
14. **Link with brand-portfolio**: BrandManagement + BrandPortfolio co-built
15. **Link with brand-metrics**: BrandManagement + BrandMetrics co-built
16. **Link with corporate-branding**: BrandManagement + CorporateBranding co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Brandfolder / Frontify / Bynder / Widen / Hootsuite
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must BrandManagement; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by relying on agencies; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler BrandManagement the better; cut redundant layers
