## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: resolutions + filing + disclosure + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Resolutions**: meetings / minutes / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Filing**: business registration / regulator / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Disclosure**: periodic / ad-hoc / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progress gradually from resolutions -> filing -> disclosure -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with board-governance**: CorporateSecretary + BoardGovernance co-built
13. **Link with legal-entity-management**: CorporateSecretary + LegalEntityManagement co-built
14. **Link with legal-operations**: CorporateSecretary + LegalOperations co-built
15. **Link with intellectual-property**: CorporateSecretary + IntellectualProperty co-built
16. **Link with compliance-management**: CorporateSecretary + ComplianceManagement co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Diligent / BoardEffect / Aprio / Azeus / BoardPad
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must CorporateSecretary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by relying on manual minutes; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: CorporateSecretary the simpler the better; cut redundant disclosures
