## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: matching + cadence + content + escalation + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by executive coverage + win-rate + revenue + renewal + NRR; not sloganeering
3. **Matching**: level / industry / relationship / interest / role; do not omit
4. **Cadence**: kickoff / milestone / QBR / EBR / renewal; do not omit
5. **Content**: strategy / business / risk / investment / roadmap; do not omit
6. **Escalation**: trigger / path / owner / time window / alternative; do not omit
7. **Measure**: executive coverage + win-rate + revenue + renewal + NRR; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from matching → cadence → content → escalation → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with executive-sponsorship**: executive engagement + executive sponsorship co-built
13. **Link with executive-briefing**: executive engagement + executive briefing co-built
14. **Link with stakeholder-engagement**: executive engagement + stakeholder engagement co-built
15. **Link with account-mapping**: executive engagement + account mapping co-built
16. **Link with qbr**: executive engagement + QBR co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce / Clari / Gong / Outreach / Brightwheel / Nural
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must executive engagement; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can AE free-form solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: executive engagement the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
