## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: recruit + qualify + launch + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by adoption + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Recruit**: profile / sourcing / evaluation / signing / enrollment; do not omit
4. **Qualify**: certification / assessment / verification / tiering / backup; do not omit
5. **Launch**: training / tools / connection / launch / handoff; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: adoption + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from recruit → qualify → launch → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with partner-strategy**: PO + Strategy co-built
13. **Link with partner-program**: PO + Program co-built
14. **Link with partner-success**: PO + Success co-built
15. **Link with partner-enablement**: PO + Enablement co-built
16. **Link with partner-compensation**: PO + Comp co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Allbound / Channeltivity / Impartner / Partnerstack / Zift Solutions
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why PO is a must; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by relying on self-onboarding; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (adoption / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: PO the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
