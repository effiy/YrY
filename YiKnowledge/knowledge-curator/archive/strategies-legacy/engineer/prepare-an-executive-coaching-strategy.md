## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: assessment + matching + journey + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; no sloganeering
3. **Assess**: 360 / personality / strengths / challenges; do not omit
4. **Match**: coach / style / experience / chemistry; do not omit
5. **Journey**: goals / cadence / feedback / transition; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from assessment → matching → journey → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: coaching hours are only the start; not the end
10. **No sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with coaching-program**: executive coaching + coaching program co-build
13. **Link with mentoring-program**: executive coaching + mentoring program co-build
14. **Link with leader-development**: executive coaching + leader development co-build
15. **Link with executive-development**: executive coaching + executive development co-build
16. **Link with leadership-development**: executive coaching + leadership development co-build
17. **Toolchain**: CoachHub / BetterUp / Bravely / Sounding Board / Korn Ferry
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must an executive coaching strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: executive coaching the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
