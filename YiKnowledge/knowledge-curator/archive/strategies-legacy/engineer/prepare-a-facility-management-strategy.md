## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: space + service + maintenance + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Space**: building / workstation / closed-loop; do not omit
4. **Service**: cleaning / security / catering / closed-loop; do not omit
5. **Maintenance**: preventive / corrective / closed-loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from space → service → maintenance → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with property-management**: FacilityManagement + PropertyManagement co-built
13. **Link with asset-management**: FacilityManagement + AssetManagement co-built
14. **Link with workplace**: FacilityManagement + Workplace co-built
15. **Link with sustainability**: FacilityManagement + Sustainability co-built
16. **Link with workplace**: FacilityManagement + Workplace co-built
17. **Toolchain**: IBM TRIRIGA / Planon / Archibus / ServiceChannel / FM:Systems
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why FacilityManagement is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by a property manager; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: FacilityManagement: the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
