## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Design + assignment + balance + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by coverage + revenue + win-rate + retention + AE efficiency; not sloganeering
3. **Design**: geography / vertical / named / round-robin / pod; do not omit
4. **Assignment**: accounts / AE / priority / balance / retirement; do not omit
5. **Balance**: potential / workload / history / fairness / adjustment; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + revenue + win-rate + retention + AE efficiency; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from design → assignment → balance → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with sales-territory**: territory plan + sales territory co-built
13. **Link with quota-plan**: territory plan + quota co-built
14. **Link with commission-plan**: territory plan + commission co-built
15. **Link with account-plan**: territory plan + account plan co-built
16. **Link with sales-ops**: territory plan + sales operations co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce / Clari / Boost-UP / People-AI / Xactly / Varicent
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must territory plan; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by free assignment; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: territory plan the simpler the better; cut redundant rules
