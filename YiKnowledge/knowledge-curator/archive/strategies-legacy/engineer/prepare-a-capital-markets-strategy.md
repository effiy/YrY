## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: equity + debt + structure + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **equity Equity**: ipo / spo / closed loop; do not omit
4. **debt Debt**: bond / loan / closed loop; do not omit
5. **structure Structure**: capital structure / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from equity -> debt -> structure -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **linked with investor-relations**: CapitalMarkets + InvestorRelations co-built
13. **linked with treasury**: CapitalMarkets + Treasury co-built
