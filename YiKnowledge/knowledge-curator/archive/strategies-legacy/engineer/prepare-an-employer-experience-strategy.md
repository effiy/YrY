## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Attraction + experience + retention + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Attraction Attraction**: channel / conversion / closed loop; do not omit
4. **experience Experience**: onboarding / in-service / closed loop; do not omit
5. **retention Retention**: development / departure / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from attraction → experience → retention → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Links with employer-branding**: EmployerExperience + EmployerBranding co-built
13. **Links with employer-value-proposition**: EmployerExperience + EVP co-built
14. **Links with employee-experience**: EmployerExperience + EmployeeExperience co-built
15. **Links with employee-engagement**: EmployerExperience + EmployeeEngagement co-built
16. **Links with talent-management**: EmployerExperience + TalentManagement co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Glassdoor / LinkedIn / Indeed / Kununu / Comparably
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must EmployerExperience; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by relying on recruiting funnel; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
