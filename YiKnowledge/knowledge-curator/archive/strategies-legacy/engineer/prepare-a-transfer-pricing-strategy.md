## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: principles + documentation + implementation + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; no empty slogans
3. **Principles**: arm's length / economic substance / risk assumption; none missing
4. **Documentation**: entities / jurisdictions / country-by-country / value chain; none missing
5. **Implementation**: policy / contracts / systems / data; none missing
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / docs / drift; none missing
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; none missing
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from principles → documentation → implementation → governance → measurement; no skipping levels
9. **Not report-only**: allocation is only the starting point; not the endpoint
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must have landed evidence; no ambiguity
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with tax**: Transfer pricing + Tax co-build
13. **Link with tax-management**: Transfer pricing + Tax Management co-build
14. **Link with tax-planning**: Transfer pricing + Tax Planning co-build
15. **Link with compliance-management**: Transfer pricing + Compliance Management co-build
16. **Link with regulatory-reporting**: Transfer pricing + Regulatory Reporting co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Thomson Reuters ONESOURCE / Vertex / Sovos / Longview / Aon Transfer Pricing
18. **Publicly accessible**: strategy accessible to everyone; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must Transfer Pricing strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: Transfer pricing the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
