## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: investment + benefit + time + risk + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: set priority by payback time + revenue + renewal + NRR + decision efficiency; no empty slogans
3. **Investment**: license / implementation / integration / training / resources; no leakage
4. **Benefit**: revenue / cost / efficiency / risk / strategy; no leakage
5. **Time**: simple / discounted / risk-adjusted / scenario / multi-year; no leakage
6. **Risk**: cost / time / organization / technology / finance; no leakage
7. **Measurement**: payback time + revenue + renewal + NRR + decision efficiency; no leakage
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from investment → benefit → time → risk → measurement; no skipping levels
9. **No report-ism**: reports are just the start; not the end
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must mark implementation evidence; no vagueness
11. **Versioned**: strategy is versioned; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with roi-calculator**: payback + ROI co-build
13. **Link with tco**: payback + TCO co-build
14. **Link with business-case**: payback + business case co-build
15. **Link with value-selling**: payback + value selling co-build
16. **Link with pov**: payback + POV co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Excel / Google-Sheets / Salesforce / Clari / Tableau / 6sense
18. **Publicly discoverable**: strategy is publicly discoverable; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must payback; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion**: how much can ROI solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam's razor**: payback simpler is better; cut redundant assumptions
