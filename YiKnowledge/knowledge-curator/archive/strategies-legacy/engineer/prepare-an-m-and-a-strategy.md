## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: strategy + valuation + integration + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + synergy + trust + risk + cost; no sloganeering
3. **Strategy**: buy / sell / merge / JV / carve-out; do not omit
4. **Valuation**: DCF / comparable / precedent / asset / option; do not omit
5. **Integration**: people / systems / culture / brand / customer; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: growth + synergy + trust + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from strategy → valuation → integration → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **No sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with business-model**: M&A + Business co-built
13. **Link with investor-relations**: M&A + IR co-built
14. **Link with financial-reporting**: M&A + Finance co-built
15. **Link with vendor-analysis**: M&A + Vendor co-built
16. **Link with change-management**: M&A + Change co-built
17. **Toolchain**: DealRoom / PitchBook / Crunchbase / SourceScrub / Affinity
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must have M&A; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by building in-house; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / synergy / risk / trust)
23. **Occam**: M&A the simpler the better; cut redundant deals
