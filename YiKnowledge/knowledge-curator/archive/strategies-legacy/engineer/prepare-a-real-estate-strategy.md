## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: portfolio + site selection + operations + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + cost + risk; not sloganeering
3. **Portfolio**: own / lease / shared / sale-leaseback / JV; do not omit
4. **Site**: city / region / transport / policy / talent; do not omit
5. **Operations**: lease / property / maintenance / renewal / exit; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + cost + risk; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from portfolio → site selection → operations → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with facilities**: RealEstate + Facilities co-built
13. **Link with workplace**: RealEstate + Workplace co-built
14. **Link with vendor-management**: RealEstate + Vendor co-built
15. **Link with sustainability**: RealEstate + Sustainability co-built
16. **Link with budget-planning**: RealEstate + Budget co-built
17. **Toolchain**: JLL / CBRE / CoStar / Prologis / VTS
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must RealEstate; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by temporary lease; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / cost)
23. **Occam**: RealEstate the simpler the better; cut redundant holdings
