## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + path + conversion + experiments + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by trial-to-paid + ARR + CAC + LTV + NPS; not sloganeering
3. **Audience**: ICP + individual + SMB + mid + enterprise; do not omit
4. **Path**: landing / sign-up / first-value / habit / upgrade; do not omit
5. **Conversion**: triggers + tips + offers + sales + upgrade; do not omit
6. **Experiments**: A/B + multi-variant + prioritization + learning + upgrade; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: trial-to-paid + ARR + CAC + LTV + NPS; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from audience -> path -> conversion -> experiments -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Interplay with PLG**: self-serve + PLG co-built
13. **Interplay with self-serve-analytics**: self-serve + analytics co-built
14. **Interplay with onboarding-flow**: self-serve + onboarding co-built
15. **Interplay with trial**: self-serve + trial co-built
16. **Interplay with pricing**: self-serve + pricing co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Amplitude / Mixpanel / Pendo / PostHog / GrowthBook / LaunchDarkly / Customer.io
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a self-serve growth strategy is required; the worst consequence of not having one
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by sales pushing; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / conversion / business)
23. **Occam**: the simpler the self-serve growth the better; cut redundant steps
