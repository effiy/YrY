## Action recommendations

1. **five dimensions**: representation + equity + inclusion + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritise by innovation + retention + revenue per capita + candidate NPS + compliance; no empty slogans
3. **representation Representation**: gender / race / age / background / identity; no leakage
4. **equity Equity**: pay / promotion / opportunity / resource / transparency; no leakage
5. **inclusion Inclusion**: belonging + psychological safety + voice + decision + culture; no leakage
6. **Governance Governance**: committee + policy + audit + compliance + retire; no leakage
7. **Measurement Measure**: representation ratio + pay ratio + promotion ratio + retention + eNPS; no leakage
8. **Not one-shot**: from representation → equity → inclusion → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping levels
9. **no report-ism**: report is just the start; not the end
10. **no empty slogans**: every principle must mark implementation evidence; no vagueness
11. **Versioned**: strategy is versioned; evolution is traceable
12. **link with hr**: DEI + HR co-build
13. **link with talent**: DEI + talent co-build
14. **link with culture**: DEI + culture co-build
15. **link with compensation**: DEI + pay co-build
16. **link with benefits**: DEI + benefits co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Culture Amp / Syndio / Visier / ChartHop / Pave / BCG / Korn Ferry / Aon
18. **Publicly discoverable**: strategy is publicly discoverable; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must DEI strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion**: how much can be solved by natural evolution; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequence after strategy (cost / risk / retention / business)
23. **Occam's razor**: DEI simpler is better; cut redundant steps
