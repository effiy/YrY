## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + content + channel + cadence + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by brand asset + revenue + retention + trust + risk; no empty slogans
3. **Audience**: media + analysts + investors + government + employees + customers; none missing
4. **Content**: press releases + speeches + white papers + earnings reports + crisis + case studies; none missing
5. **Channel**: media + social + owned + events + email + delivery; none missing
6. **Cadence**: daily + quarterly + annual + crisis + milestones; none missing
7. **Measure**: brand asset + media coverage + trust + retention + revenue; none missing
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from audience -> content -> channel -> cadence -> measurement; no skipping levels
9. **Not report-only**: reports are only the starting point; not the endpoint
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must have landed evidence; no ambiguity
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with brand-strategy**: corporate communications + brand co-build
13. **Link with internal-communications**: corporate communications + internal co-build
14. **Link with customer-communications**: corporate communications + customer co-build
15. **Link with content**: corporate communications + content co-build
16. **Link with public-relations**: corporate communications + PR co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Cision / Meltwater / Muck Rack / PR Newswire / Business Wire / Critical Mention / Mention / Brand24
18. **Publicly accessible**: strategy accessible to everyone; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must corporate communications strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion**: how much can be solved by relying on scattered media relationships; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / brand / business) 
23. **Occam**: corporate communications the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
