## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Channel + content + conversion + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by MQL + SQL + revenue + CAC + retention; not sloganeering
3. **Channel**: SEO / content / social / email / referral; do not omit
4. **Content**: theme / keywords / format / personalization / stage; do not omit
5. **Conversion**: CTA / form / LP / routing / nurture; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: MQL + SQL + revenue + CAC + retention; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from channel → content → conversion → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with content**: Inbound + Content co-built
13. **Link with seo**: Inbound + SEO co-built
14. **Link with lead-scoring**: Inbound + Scoring co-built
15. **Link with lead-nurturing**: Inbound + Nurturing co-built
16. **Link with outbound**: Inbound + Outbound co-built
17. **Toolchain**: HubSpot / Marketo / WordPress / Semrush / Ahrefs / Salesforce
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must Inbound; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by outbound; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: Inbound the simpler the better; cut redundant channels
