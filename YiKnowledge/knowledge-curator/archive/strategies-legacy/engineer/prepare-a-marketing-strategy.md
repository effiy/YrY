## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Audience + message + channel + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: Prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **audience Audience**: Segmentation / persona / pain point / path; do not omit
4. **message Message**: Value / story / evidence / CTA; do not omit
5. **channel Channel**: Owned / paid / shared / partnership; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: Owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: Coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: From audience -> message -> channel -> governance -> measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: Ads are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: Every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: Strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Linked with market**: Marketing + market co-build
13. **Linked with brand**: Marketing + brand co-build
14. **Linked with content-marketing**: Marketing + content co-build
15. **Linked with digital-marketing**: Marketing + digital co-build
16. **Linked with growth**: Marketing + growth co-build
17. **Toolchain**: HubSpot / Marketo / Pardot / Salesforce Marketing Cloud / Adobe Experience Cloud
18. **publicly queryable**: Strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: Evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: Why must marketing strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: How much can defaults solve; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: Second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: Marketing the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
