## Action recommendations

1. **five dimensions**: dimension + cadence + channel + Governance + Measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by eNPS + retention + performance + Decision speed + referral; no empty slogans
3. **dimension Dimensions**: cognitive + emotional + behavioral + social + value; no leakage
4. **cadence Cadence**: daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly / annual + upgrade; no leakage
5. **channel Channel**: all-hands / 1:1 / Slack / Teams / intranet / survey; no leakage
6. **Governance Governance**: charter + owner + budget + guidelines + retire; no leakage
7. **Measurement Measure**: eNPS + retention + performance + Decision speed + referral; no leakage
8. **Not one-shot**: from dimension → cadence → channel → Governance → Measurement gradual; no skipping levels
9. **no report-ism**: report is just the start; not the end
10. **no empty slogans**: every principle must mark implementation evidence; no vagueness
11. **Versioned**: strategy is versioned; evolution is traceable
12. **link with internal-communications**: engagement + internal communication Co-build
13. **link with culture**: engagement + culture Co-build
14. **link with employer-branding**: engagement + EB Co-build
15. **link with performance-management**: engagement + PM Co-build
16. **link with learning-and-development**: engagement + L&D Co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Culture Amp / Peakon / Glint / Qualtrics-EX / Lattice / 15Five / Bonusly / Workhuman / Kudos
18. **Publicly discoverable**: strategy is publicly discoverable; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: Evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must employee engagement strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion**: how much can sending emails solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequence after strategy (cost / Risk / retention / business)
23. **Occam's razor**: employee engagement simpler is better; redundant step cut
