## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: diagnose + feedback + action + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by win-rate + cycle-time + ACV + revenue + renewal; not sloganeering
3. **diagnose Diagnose**: stage / role / pain point / value / risk; do not omit
4. **feedback Feedback**: strengths / weaknesses / action / resources / upgrade; do not omit
5. **action Action**: next-step / owner / time window / resources / measurement; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: win-rate + cycle-time + ACV + revenue + renewal; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from diagnose → feedback → action → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with sales-coaching**: deal coaching + sales coaching co-built
13. **link with deal-review**: deal coaching + deal-review co-built
14. **link with pipeline-review**: deal coaching + pipeline review co-built
15. **link with call-coaching**: deal coaching + call coaching co-built
16. **link with win-plan**: deal coaching + win plan co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Gong / Clari / Salesforce / Boost-UP / People-AI / Mindtickle
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must deal coaching; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can free-form play solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: deal coaching the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
