## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: membership + agenda + feedback + closed loop + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by cross-sell + renewal + NPS + strategy co-build + product impact; not sloganeering
3. **Members**: tiering + top accounts + industry + persona + tenure; do not omit
4. **Agenda**: strategy + trends + product + feedback + joint motion; do not omit
5. **Feedback**: listening + categorization + prioritization + follow-up + transparency; do not omit
6. **Loop**: response + responsibility + timeframe + retrospective + public; do not omit
7. **Measure**: cross-sell + renewal + NPS + impact + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from membership → agenda → feedback → closed loop → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with customer-success**: CAB + CS co-built
13. **Link with customer-advocacy**: CAB + advocacy co-built
14. **Link with product-strategy**: CAB + strategy co-built
15. **Link with product-roadmap**: CAB + roadmap co-built
16. **Link with customer-journey**: CAB + journey co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Gainsight CS / Zapnito / Bevy / Socious / Cabin / AWS / Salesforce Experience Cloud
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why CAB strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by one-sided research alone; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / relationship / business)
23. **Occam**: the simpler CAB is, the better; cut redundant steps
