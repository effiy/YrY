## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + cadence + channel + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by alignment + trust + speed + risk + decision; not sloganeering
3. **audience Audience**: executives / team / cross-department / partner / customer; do not omit
4. **cadence Cadence**: daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly / ad-hoc; do not omit
5. **channel Channel**: email / IM / meeting / documentation / dashboard; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: alignment + trust + speed + risk + decision; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from audience → cadence → channel → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Linked with stakeholder**: Cadence + Stakeholder co-built
13. **Linked with executive-sponsorship**: Cadence + Sponsor co-built
14. **Linked with okr**: Cadence + OKR co-built
15. **Linked with leadership**: Cadence + Leadership co-built
16. **Linked with change-management**: Cadence + Change co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Slack / Notion / Confluence / Loom / Gamma
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must Cadence; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by a one-time email; if solvable do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (alignment / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: Cadence the simpler the better; redundant meetings cut
