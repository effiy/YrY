## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: trigger + path + cadence + risk + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by win-rate + cycle-time + ACV + revenue + renewal; not sloganeering
3. **Trigger**: verbal / pain point / budget / decision / time; do not omit
4. **Path**: proposal / negotiate / counter / verbal-won / signed; do not omit
5. **Cadence**: daily / weekly / milestone / escalation / close; do not omit
6. **Risk**: competition / budget / decision / time / implementation; do not omit
7. **Measure**: win-rate + cycle-time + ACV + revenue + renewal; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from trigger -> path -> cadence -> risk -> measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with negotiation**: closing + negotiation co-built
13. **link with deal-review**: closing + deal-review co-built
14. **link with mutual-close-plan**: closing + mutual action plan co-built
15. **link with sales-playbook**: closing + sales playbook co-built
16. **link with contract-lifecycle-management**: closing + contract co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce / Clari / Gong / DocuSign / PandaDoc / Conga
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must closing strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can AE free-form solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention) 
23. **Occam**: closing the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
