## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: goal + model + execution + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Goal**: company / team / individual / behavior; do not omit
4. **Model**: pool / multiplier / coefficient / boundary; do not omit
5. **Execute**: rules / communication / payout / appeal; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from goal → model → execution → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: paying is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with incentive**: bonus + incentive co-built
13. **Link with compensation**: bonus + compensation co-built
14. **Link with rewards**: bonus + total rewards co-built
15. **Link with commission-plan**: bonus + sales commission co-built
16. **Link with performance-management**: bonus + performance management co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Workday / Xactly / Anaplan / beqom / Varicent
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must bonus strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by defaults; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: bonus the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
