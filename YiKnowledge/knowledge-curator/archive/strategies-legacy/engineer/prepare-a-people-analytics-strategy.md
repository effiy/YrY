## Action recommendations

1. **five dimensions**: question + data + insight + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by decision + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **question Question**: business / talent / process / prediction / causality; do not omit
4. **data Data**: HR / business / listening / external / privacy; do not omit
5. **insight Insight**: description / diagnosis / prediction / prescription / generation; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: decision + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from question → data → insight → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **and hr-operations linkage**: PA + HROps co-built
13. **and employee-listening linkage**: PA + Listening co-built
14. **and talent-review linkage**: PA + Talent co-built
15. **and workforce-planning linkage**: PA + Workforce co-built
16. **and chro linkage**: PA + CHRO co-built
17. **toolchain**: Visier / One Model / ChartHop / Crunchr / Workday People Analytics
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must PA; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: rely on intuition how much can be solved; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (decision / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: PA the simpler the better; cut redundant metrics
