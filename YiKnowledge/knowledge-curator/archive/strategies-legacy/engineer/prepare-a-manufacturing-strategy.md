## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: process + capacity + quality + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Process**: process / takt / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Capacity**: bottleneck / cadence / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Quality**: standards / SPC / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from process → capacity → quality → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with production-planning**: Manufacturing + ProductionPlanning co-built
13. **Link with quality-management**: Manufacturing + QualityManagement co-built
14. **Link with lean**: Manufacturing + Lean co-built
15. **Link with supply-chain**: Manufacturing + SupplyChain co-built
16. **Link with operations**: Manufacturing + Operations co-built
17. **Toolchain**: SAP S/4HANA / Oracle MES / Siemens Opcenter / Apriso / Camstar
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must Manufacturing; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by outsourcing; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: Manufacturing the simpler the better; cut redundant processes
