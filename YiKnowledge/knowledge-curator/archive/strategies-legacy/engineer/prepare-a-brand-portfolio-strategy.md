## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: architecture + role + optimization + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **architecture Architecture**: layering / relationship / closed loop; do not omit
4. **role Role**: master brand / sub-brand / closed loop; do not omit
5. **optimization Optimize**: add-remove / migration / closed loop; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progress from architecture → role → optimization → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with brand-architecture**: BrandPortfolio + BrandArchitecture co-build
13. **Link with brand-strategy**: BrandPortfolio + BrandStrategy co-build
14. **Link with brand-equity**: BrandPortfolio + BrandEquity co-build
15. **Link with corporate-branding**: BrandPortfolio + CorporateBranding co-build
16. **Link with brand-management**: BrandPortfolio + BrandManagement co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Interbrand / Landor / McKinsey / BCG / Siegel+Gale
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must BrandPortfolio; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by a single brand; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: BrandPortfolio the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
