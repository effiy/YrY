## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: scan + assess + interpret + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Scan**: sources / signals / cadence / tools; do not omit
4. **Assess**: maturity / impact / probability / timing; do not omit
5. **Interpret**: scenarios / paths / actions / rehearsals; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: gradual from scan → assess → interpret → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: news is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with futures-research**: trend + future co-build
13. **Link with innovation-research**: trend + innovation co-build
14. **Link with market-intelligence**: trend + market intelligence co-build
15. **Link with market-research**: trend + market co-build
16. **Link with competitive-research**: trend + competitor co-build
17. **Toolchain**: CB Insights / Gartner / McKinsey / WGSN / TrendWatching
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must trend research strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable don't introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: trend research simpler is better; cut redundant layers
