## Action recommendations

1. **five dimensions**: physical + mental + financial + Governance + Measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; no empty slogans
3. **physical Physical**: health / exercise / diet / sleep; no leakage
4. **mental Mental**: stress / emotion / support / resources; no leakage
5. **financial Financial**: savings / investment / retirement / emergency; no leakage
6. **Governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; no leakage
7. **Measurement Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; no leakage
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from physical -> mental -> financial -> Governance -> Measurement; no skipping levels
9. **no report-ism**: numbers are only the start; not the end
10. **no empty slogans**: every principle must have implementation evidence; no vagueness
11. **Versioned**: strategy is versioned; evolution is traceable
12. **link with employee-mental-health**: Wellbeing + Employee mental health co-build
13. **link with employee-engagement**: Wellbeing + Employee engagement co-build
14. **link with employee-experience**: Wellbeing + Employee experience co-build
15. **link with benefits**: Wellbeing + Benefits co-build
16. **link with employee-retention**: Wellbeing + Employee retention co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Virgin Pulse / Limeade / Wellable / Gympass / Modern Health
18. **Publicly discoverable**: strategy is publicly discoverable; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: Evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must employee wellbeing strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion**: how much can relying on defaults solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam's razor**: employee wellbeing simpler is better; cut redundant layers
