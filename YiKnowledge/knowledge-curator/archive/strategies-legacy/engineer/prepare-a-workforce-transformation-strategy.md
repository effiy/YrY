## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: vision + capability + journey + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by speed + cost + experience + risk + trust; not sloganeering
3. **Vision Vision**: future + paradigm + value; do not omit
4. **Capability Capability**: skills / structure / culture / tools; do not omit
5. **Journey Journey**: diagnosis / blueprint / migration / retrospective; do not omit
6. **Governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement Measure**: progress + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from vision → capability → journey → governance → measurement progressive; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: replacing people is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with workforce-management**: transformation + management co-build
13. **Link with workforce-optimization**: transformation + optimize co-build
14. **Link with hr-transformation**: workforce + HR transformation co-build
15. **Link with workforce-planning**: transformation + planning co-build
16. **Link with skills-based**: transformation + skills-oriented co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Workday / Eightfold AI / Gloat / Korn Ferry / Mercer
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must workforce transformation strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (speed / cost / experience / risk)
23. **Occam**: workforce transformation the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
