## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: front-stage + back-stage + linkage + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Front-stage**: touchpoints / roles / paper trail / closed loop / experience; do not omit
4. **Back-stage**: process / systems / data / collaboration / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Linkage**: physical / digital / channels / policy / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from front-stage -> back-stage -> linkage -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with service-blueprint**: ServiceDesign + ServiceBlueprint co-build
13. **Link with customer-experience**: ServiceDesign + CX co-build
14. **Link with design-system**: ServiceDesign + DesignSystem co-build
15. **Link with customer-journey**: ServiceDesign + CustomerJourney co-build
16. **Link with journey-orchestration**: ServiceDesign + JourneyOrchestration co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Miro / Figma / FigJam / Mural / Smaply
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must ServiceDesign; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by the product; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: ServiceDesign the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
