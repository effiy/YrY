## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: visualize + limit + flow + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Visualize**: board / columns / cards / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Limit**: wip / bottleneck / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Flow**: lead / cycle / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from visualize -> limit -> flow -> governance -> measurement progressive; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with scrum**: Kanban + Scrum co-built
13. **Link with retrospective**: Kanban + Retrospective co-built
14. **Link with extreme-programming**: Kanban + ExtremeProgramming co-built
15. **Link with pair-programming**: Kanban + PairProgramming co-built
16. **Link with mob-programming**: Kanban + MobProgramming co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Jira / Linear / Trello / WeKan / LeanKit
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must Kanban; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by a todo list; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: Kanban the simpler the better; cut redundant columns
