## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: method + data + analysis + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **method Method**: qualitative / quantitative / A/B / neuro; do not omit
4. **data Data**: audience / message / channel / assumption; do not omit
5. **analysis Analyze**: attention / memory / persuasion / conversion; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from method → data → analysis → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: copy is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **links with positioning-research**: messaging + positioning co-built
13. **links with brand-research**: messaging + brand co-built
14. **links with content**: messaging + content co-built
15. **links with customer-research**: messaging + customer co-built
16. **links with market-research**: messaging + market co-built
17. **toolchain**: Qualtrics / SurveyMonkey / Message Tested / Neural Insight / Madonn
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why a messaging research strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: messaging research — the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
