## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: framework + scenarios + training + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by win-rate + cycle-time + ACV + revenue + renewal; not sloganeering
3. **Framework**: SPIN / Challenger / MEDDPICC / Solution / Consultative; do not omit
4. **Scenarios**: industry / role / stage / pain points / value; do not omit
5. **Training**: new hires / on-the-job / retrospective / certification / upgrade; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: win-rate + cycle-time + ACV + revenue + renewal; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from framework -> scenarios -> training -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Interplay with sales-process**: methodology + sales process co-built
13. **Interplay with sales-playbook**: methodology + sales playbook co-built
14. **Interplay with sales-coaching**: methodology + sales coaching co-built
15. **Interplay with sales-readiness**: methodology + sales readiness co-built
16. **Interplay with value-selling**: methodology + value selling co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Gong / Clari / Salesforce / Outreach / Salesloft / Highspot
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a sales methodology is required; the worst consequence of not having one
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by free-form selling; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: the simpler the methodology the better; cut redundant frameworks
