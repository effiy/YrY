## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: vision + strategy + roadmap + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by innovation + growth + trust + speed + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Vision**: user / market / technology / long-term / short-term; do not omit
4. **Strategy**: positioning / differentiation / roadmap / resources / measurement; do not omit
5. **Roadmap**: theme / milestones / now / next quarter / future; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: innovation + growth + trust + speed + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from vision → strategy → roadmap → governance → measurement progressive; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with cto**: CPO + CTO co-build
13. **Link with product**: CPO + Product co-build
14. **Link with product-operations**: CPO + ProductOps co-build
15. **Link with product-research**: CPO + Research co-build
16. **Link with leadership**: CPO + Leadership co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Aha / Productboard / Pendo / Amplitude / Mixpanel
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why CPO is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved with a CEO covering the role; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (innovation / growth / trust / cost)
23. **Occam**: simpler CPO is better; cut redundant layers
