## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: pain point + solution + value + risk + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by win-rate + ACV + cycle-time + renewal + NRR; not sloganeering
3. **Pain**: business / technical / financial / strategy / risk; do not omit
4. **Solution**: function / implementation / integration / service / support; do not omit
5. **Value**: ROI / TCO / payback / business / strategy; do not omit
6. **risk Risk**: cost / time / organizational / technical / financial; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: win-rate + ACV + cycle-time + renewal + NRR; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from pain point → solution → value → risk → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with roi-calculator**: business case + ROI co-built
13. **Link with value-selling**: business case + value selling co-built
14. **Link with deal-presentation**: business case + proposal co-built
15. **Link with proposal**: business case + proposal documentation co-built
16. **Link with budget-planning**: business case + budget co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Excel / Google-Sheets / Salesforce / Clari / Gong / 6sense
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must business case; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by feature demos; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: the simpler the business case the better; cut redundant sections
