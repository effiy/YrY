## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: theme + cadence + channel + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by traffic + conversion + retention + trust + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Theme**: pillar / cluster / seasonal / event / pain point; do not omit
4. **Cadence**: daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly / ad-hoc; do not omit
5. **Channel**: blog / social / email / video / podcast; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: traffic + conversion + retention + trust + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from theme -> cadence -> channel -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with content**: Calendar + Content co-built
13. **Link with blog**: Calendar + Blog co-built
14. **Link with social-selling**: Calendar + Social co-built
15. **Link with campaign**: Calendar + Campaign co-built
16. **Link with content-governance**: Calendar + Governance co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Notion / Airtable / Trello / CoSchedule / ContentCal
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must Calendar; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can ad hoc solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (traffic / conversion / retention / cost) 
23. **Occam**: Calendar the simpler the better; cut redundant columns
