## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: access + observation + thick description + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; no empty slogans
3. **Access**: recruitment / trust / consent / role; none missing
4. **Observe**: on-site / shadowing / ritual / documentation; none missing
5. **Thick Description**: scenario / interaction / meaning / context; none missing
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / docs / drift; none missing
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; none missing
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from access → observation → thick description → governance → measurement; no skipping levels
9. **Not report-only**: observation is only the starting point; not the endpoint
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must have landed evidence; no ambiguity
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with contextual-inquiry**: ethnographic research + context co-build
13. **Link with diary-study**: ethnographic research + diary co-build
14. **Link with customer-interview**: ethnographic research + interview co-build
15. **Link with user-research**: ethnographic research + users co-build
16. **Link with design-research**: ethnographic research + design co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Dovetail / Dscout / Evernote / Notion / Otter.ai
18. **Publicly accessible**: strategy accessible to everyone; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why ethnographic research strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler ethnographic research is, the better; cut redundant layers
