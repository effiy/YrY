## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Role + process + responsibility + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: Prioritize by delivery + speed + quality + satisfaction + revenue; not sloganeering
3. **Role Role**: lead / co-deliver / sub-contract / staff-aug / joint; do not omit
4. **Process Process**: discovery / scoping / kickoff / delivery / closure; do not omit
5. **Responsibility Responsibility**: SOW / RACI / SLA / risk / upgrade; do not omit
6. **Governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement Measure**: delivery + speed + quality + satisfaction + revenue; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: Progress from role → process → responsibility → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with si**: Co-Delivery + SI co-built
13. **Link with partner-program**: Co-Delivery + Partner Program co-built
14. **Link with partner-enablement**: Co-Delivery + Enablement co-built
15. **Link with partner-compensation**: Co-Delivery + Compensation co-built
16. **Link with opportunity-management**: Co-Delivery + Opp Mgmt co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce PRM / Impartner / Crossbeam / Allbound / Asana
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must Co-Delivery; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by direct sales; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / satisfaction / revenue)
23. **Occam**: Co-Delivery — the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
