## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: value + customer + channel + revenue + cost; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by ARR + gross margin + LTV / CAC + growth rate + retention; not sloganeering
3. **Value**: JTBD + value proposition + differentiation + evidence + counter-example; do not omit
4. **Customer**: ICP + segment + profile + channel + relationship; do not omit
5. **Channel**: direct / self-service / partner / channel + cost + conversion; do not omit
6. **Revenue**: subscription / usage / one-time / marketplace / hybrid + pricing + packaging; do not omit
7. **Cost**: COGS + OPEX + CAC + channel + retirement; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from value -> customer -> channel -> revenue -> cost; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with product-strategy**: business model + product strategy co-built
13. **Link with pricing**: business model + pricing co-built
14. **Link with packaging**: business model + packaging co-built
15. **Link with go-to-market**: business model + GTM co-built
16. **Link with unit-economics**: business model + unit economics co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Strategyzer / Lean Canvas / BMG / ChartMogul / ProfitWell / Baremetrics / Geckoboard / Klipfolio
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must business model strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: see how much gut-call pricing can solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / channel / business)
23. **Occam**: business model the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
