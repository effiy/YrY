## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: empathy + response + closed loop + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Empathy**: listen / empathize / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Respond**: tiering / escalate / closed loop; do not omit
5. **FollowUp**: revisit / care / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from empathy -> response -> closed loop -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with customer-service**: Care + Service co-built
13. **Link with customer-success**: Care + CS co-built
14. **Link with customer-experience**: Care + Experience co-built
15. **Link with customer-advocacy**: Care + Advocacy co-built
16. **Link with customer-recovery**: Care + Recovery co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Zendesk / Salesforce Service Cloud / Gladly / Five9 / Genesys
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why CustomerCare is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by scripts; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler CustomerCare is the better; cut redundant layers
