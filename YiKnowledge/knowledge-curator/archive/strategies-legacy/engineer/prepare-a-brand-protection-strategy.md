## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: monitoring + enforcement + legal + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Monitor**: scan goods / domains / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Enforce**: send letters / complaints / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Legal**: litigation / seizure / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from monitoring → enforcement → legal → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with brand-licensing**: BrandProtection + BrandLicensing co-build
13. **Link with intellectual-property**: BrandProtection + IP co-build
14. **Link with brand-management**: BrandProtection + BrandManagement co-build
15. **Link with corporate-branding**: BrandProtection + CorporateBranding co-build
16. **Link with brand-equity**: BrandProtection + BrandEquity co-build
17. **Toolchain**: MarkMonitor / CSC / Corsearch / OpSec / Appdetex
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must BrandProtection; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can legal alone solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler BrandProtection is, the better; cut redundant layers
