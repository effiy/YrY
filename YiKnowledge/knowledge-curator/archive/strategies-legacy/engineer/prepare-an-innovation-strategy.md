## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: pipeline + portfolio + investment + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by innovation + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Pipeline**: collect / filter / validate / incubate / exit; do not omit
4. **Portfolio**: incremental / adjacent / transformational / disruptive / open; do not omit
5. **Investment**: people / money / time / facilities / ecosystem; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: innovation + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from pipeline → portfolio → investment → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with r-and-d**: Innovation + R&D co-built
13. **Link with product**: Innovation + Product co-built
14. **Link with technology**: Innovation + Technology co-built
15. **Link with cto**: Innovation + CTO co-built
16. **Link with corporate-venture**: Innovation + CVC co-built
17. **Toolchain**: ITONIC / Hype Innovation / Spigit / Brightidea / Qmarkets
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must Innovation; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by following; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (innovation / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: the simpler Innovation the better; cut redundant process
