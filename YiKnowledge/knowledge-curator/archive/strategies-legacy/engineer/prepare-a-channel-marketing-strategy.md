## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + content + cadence + channel + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by leads + conversion + revenue + retention + satisfaction; not sloganeering
3. **Audience**: partner / end-user / segment / region / role; do not omit
4. **Content**: collateral / campaign / ABM / event / digital; do not omit
5. **Cadence**: quarterly / monthly / weekly / adjust / reuse; do not omit
6. **Channel**: portal / email / social / event / PR; do not omit
7. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
8. **Measure**: leads + conversion + revenue + retention + satisfaction; do not omit
9. **not one-shot**: progressive from audience → content → cadence → channel → measurement; no skipping
10. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
11. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
12. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
13. **Link with partner-marketing**: Channel Marketing + Partner Marketing co-built
14. **Link with co-marketing**: Channel Marketing + Co-Marketing co-built
15. **Link with channel**: Channel Marketing + Channel co-built
16. **Link with mdf**: Channel Marketing + MDF co-built
17. **Link with partner-program**: Channel Marketing + Partner Program co-built
18. **Toolchain**: Imparter / Allbound / Salesforce PRM / Marketo / Highspot
19. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
20. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
21. **first principles**: why must Channel Marketing; worst consequence of not doing it
22. **inversion thinking**: how much can direct sales marketing solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
23. **Occam**: Channel Marketing the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
