## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: goal + accountability + cadence + risk + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by win-rate + cycle-time + revenue + renewal + NPS; not sloganeering
3. **Goal**: business + value + milestones + validation + endpoint; do not omit
4. **Accountability**: buyer / seller + executive + champion + owner + deadline; do not omit
5. **Cadence**: kick-off / weekly / monthly / milestone / quarterly; do not omit
6. **Risk**: blockers + assumptions + fallback + escalation + alternatives; do not omit
7. **Measure**: win-rate + cycle-time + revenue + renewal + NPS; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: gradual from goal → accountability → cadence → risk → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with mutual-close-plan**: MAP + mutual-close-plan co-build
13. **Link with account-engagement**: MAP + customer manager engagement co-build
14. **Link with deal-review**: MAP + deal-review co-build
15. **Link with sales-playbook**: MAP + sales playbook co-build
16. **Link with account-based-selling**: MAP + ABS co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Mutual-Action-Plan / Recapped / Salesloft / Outreach / Clari / Gong / Salesforce
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must MAP; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by one-off follow-up; if solvable don't introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: MAP simpler is better; cut redundant steps
