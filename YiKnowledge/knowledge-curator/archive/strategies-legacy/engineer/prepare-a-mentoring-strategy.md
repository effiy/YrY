## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identify + contract + deliver + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by talent + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Identify**: needs / mentor / mentee / matching / cadence; do not omit
4. **Contract**: goal / trust / confidentiality / cadence / exit; do not omit
5. **Deliver**: 1:1 / peer / group / reverse / flash; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: talent + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from identify → contract → deliver → governance → measurement progressive; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with career-development**: MT + CD co-built
13. **Link with coaching**: MT + Coaching co-built
14. **Link with leadership-development**: MT + LD co-built
15. **Link with community-of-practice**: MT + CoP co-built
16. **Link with talent-review**: MT + Talent co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Chronus / Together / Qooper / Guider / MentorcliQ
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why MT is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can self-study solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (talent / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: MT the simpler the better; cut redundant pairings
