## Action recommendations

1. **Six dimensions**: audience + goal + theme + channel + cadence + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Audience driven**: developer / user / decision-maker / media; do not mix
3. **goal clear**: brand / education / conversion / retention; not vague
4. **theme list**: must tag theme; not vague
5. **Channel tiering**: blog / social / video / email / conference; do not mix
6. **Cadence**: weekly / biweekly / monthly; not one-shot
7. **Measurement**: traffic / conversion / engagement / retention; not by gut feel
8. **Continuous**: not one-shot; by cadence
9. **editorial calendar**: calendar driven; not ad-hoc
10. **SEO**: every post must tag key words; do not omit
11. **reusable**: every post must be reusable; not one-shot
12. **Link with blog**: content strategy + blog co-built
13. **Link with tech talk**: content strategy + tech talk co-built
14. **Link with GTM**: content strategy + GTM co-built
15. **Link with pitch**: content strategy + pitch co-built
16. **Link with documentation**: content strategy + documentation co-built
17. **Link with knowledge base**: content strategy + knowledge base co-built
18. **Toolchain**: CMS / editor / analytics / SEO; do not mix
19. **publicly queryable**: everyone can look up the strategy; not hidden
20. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
21. **First principles**: why must strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
22. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved ad-hoc; if solvable, do not introduce a strategy
23. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (brand / hiring / expansion / trust) 
24. **Occam**: the simpler the strategy the better; cut redundant steps
