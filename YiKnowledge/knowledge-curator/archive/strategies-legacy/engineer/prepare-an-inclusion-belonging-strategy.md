## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identify + intervene + evaluate + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Identify**: groups / experiences / signals / data; do not omit
4. **Intervene**: policy / training / communities / leadership; do not omit
5. **Evaluate**: belonging / safety / fairness / satisfaction; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from identify -> intervene -> evaluate -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: scores are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with dei**: inclusion + diversity-equity co-built
13. **Link with employee-engagement**: inclusion + employee engagement co-built
14. **Link with corporate-culture**: inclusion + corporate culture co-built
15. **Link with employee-experience**: inclusion + employee experience co-built
16. **Link with internal-communications**: inclusion + internal communications co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Culture Amp / Glint / Peakon / Lattice / SurveyMonkey
18. **Publicly queryable**: everyone can look up the strategy; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why an inclusion-belonging strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: inclusion-belonging the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
