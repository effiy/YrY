## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: narrative + channels + cadence + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by adoption + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Narrative Narrative**: vision / strategy / value / culture / cadence; do not omit
4. **Channels Channel**: internal / external / social / media / stage; do not omit
5. **Cadence Cadence**: daily / quarterly / annual / crisis / event; do not omit
6. **Governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement Measure**: adoption + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from narrative → channels → cadence → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link to corporate-communications**: EC + CorpComms co-build
13. **Link to internal-communications**: EC + Internal co-build
14. **Link to public-relations**: EC + PR co-build
15. **Link to leadership**: EC + Leadership co-build
16. **Link to thought-leadership**: EC + ThoughtLeadership co-build
17. **Toolchain**: PoliteMail / ContactMonkey / Staffbase / Simpplr / Workshop
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must we have EC; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by relying on a spokesperson; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (adoption / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: EC the simpler the better; cut redundant channels
