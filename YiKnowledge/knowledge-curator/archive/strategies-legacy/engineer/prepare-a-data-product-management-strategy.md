## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: product + roadmap + value + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Product**: vision / definition / acceptance; do not omit
4. **Roadmap**: milestones / dependencies / prioritization; do not omit
5. **Value**: north star / okr / roi; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: adoption + active rate + satisfaction + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from product → roadmap → value → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: product count is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Linked with data-product**: product management + data product co-built
13. **Linked with data-strategy**: product management + data strategy co-built
14. **Linked with data-governance**: product management + data governance co-built
15. **Linked with data-mesh**: product management + data mesh co-built
16. **Linked with analytics-engineering**: product management + analytics engineering co-built
17. **Toolchain**: data.world / Atlan / Collibra / Alation / Acceldata
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why data product management is needed; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by project delivery; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: data product management, the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
