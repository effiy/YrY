## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identify + alert + intervene + verify + retrospective; no missing dimension
2. **Customer-value driven**: prioritize by customer scenario + LTV + churn risk; not sloganeering
3. **Identify**: health score + behavior signal + NPS + ticket; do not omit
4. **Alert**: threshold + trend + ML prediction + multi-layer; do not omit
5. **Intervene**: CSM outreach + automated message + offer + win-back; do not omit
6. **Verify**: A/B + retention comparison + ROI quantification; do not omit
7. **Retrospective**: churn cause + commonality + improvement + landing; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress gradually from rules -> health score -> ML -> fully automated; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Work with growth**: churn + growth co-built
13. **Work with customer-success**: churn + success co-built
14. **Work with onboarding**: churn + onboarding co-built
15. **Work with metrics**: churn + metrics co-built
16. **Work with feedback**: churn + feedback co-built
17. **Work with advisory**: churn + advisory co-built
18. **Work with tracking**: churn + tracking co-built
19. **Toolchain**: Gainsight / Totango / Catalyst / ChurnZero / Amplitude
20. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
21. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
22. **First principles**: why churn reduction is needed; worst consequence of not doing it
23. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by intuition + manual win-back; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
24. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / complexity / experience / business) 
25. **Occam**: churn reduction the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
