## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: recruit + enable + co-sell + Governance + Measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; no empty slogans
3. **recruit Recruit**: profile / qualification / agreement / onboarding; none missing
4. **enable Enable**: training / certification / materials / system; none missing
5. **co-sell Co-sell**: registration / joint visit / joint pitch / revenue share; none missing
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / docs / drift; none missing
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; none missing
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from recruit → enable → co-sell → Governance → Measurement; no skipping levels
9. **Not report-only**: commission is only the starting point; not the endpoint
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must have landed evidence; no ambiguity
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with channel-sales**: partner sales + channel co-build
13. **Link with partner-program**: partner sales + partner program co-build
14. **Link with deal-registration**: partner sales + registration co-build
15. **Link with co-marketing**: partner sales + co-marketing co-build
16. **Link with partner-onboarding**: partner sales + onboarding co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce PRM / Impartner / Allbound / Zift / Partnerstack
18. **Publicly accessible**: strategy accessible to everyone; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must partner-sales strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: partner sales the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
