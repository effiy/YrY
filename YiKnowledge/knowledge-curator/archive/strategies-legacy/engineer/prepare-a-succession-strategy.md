## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identify + develop + transition + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Identify**: key positions / high potential / risk / readiness; do not omit
4. **Develop**: experience / relationships / skills / challenges; do not omit
5. **Transition**: handover / pilot / retrospective / documentation; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from identify → develop → transition → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: backup is only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with talent**: succession + talent co-build
13. **Link with talent-development**: succession + development co-build
14. **Link with performance-review**: succession + review co-build
15. **Link with people**: succession + people co-build
16. **Link with career**: succession + career co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Lattice / Cornerstone / SuccessFactors / Workday / Ninebox
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy accessible to everyone; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must succession strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler succession is, the better; cut redundant layers
