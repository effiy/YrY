2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **accumulate Accumulate**: direct / indirect / closed loop; do not omit
4. **allocate Allocate**: cost center / activity / closed loop; do not omit
5. **analyze Analyze**: variance / trend / closed loop; do not omit

7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from accumulate -> allocate -> analyze -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
