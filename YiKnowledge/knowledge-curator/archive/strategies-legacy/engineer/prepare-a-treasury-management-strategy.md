## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: strategy + liquidity + risk + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Strategy**: goals + policy + risk preference; do not omit
4. **Liquidity**: cash flow + forecast + emergency + optimization; do not omit
5. **Risk**: FX / interest rate / credit / liquidity; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from strategy → liquidity → risk → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: managing money is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with treasury-operations**: management + operations co-build
13. **Link with cash-management**: treasury + cash co-build
14. **Link with financial-planning**: treasury + financial planning co-build
15. **Link with capital-markets**: treasury + capital markets co-build
16. **Link with working-capital**: treasury + working capital co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Kyriba / FIS / ION / Wall Street Systems / Bloomberg
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why treasury management strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler treasury management is, the better; cut redundant layers
