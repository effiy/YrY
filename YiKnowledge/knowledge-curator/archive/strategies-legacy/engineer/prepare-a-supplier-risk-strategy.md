## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identify + assess + mitigate + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Identify**: target / concentration / substitute / geopolitical; do not omit
4. **Assess**: financial / operations / compliance / ESG / cyber; do not omit
5. **Mitigate**: backup / inventory / contract / monitoring / exit; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from identify → assess → mitigate → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: assessment is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with supplier-management**: risk + management co-built
13. **Link with supplier-relationship**: risk + relationship co-built
14. **Link with vendor-risk**: risk + vendor risk co-built
15. **Link with third-party-risk**: risk + third-party risk co-built
16. **Link with supplier-innovation**: risk + innovation co-built
17. **Toolchain**: RSAM / BitSight / RiskRecon / Interos / Resilinc
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must a supplier-risk strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: supplier risk the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
