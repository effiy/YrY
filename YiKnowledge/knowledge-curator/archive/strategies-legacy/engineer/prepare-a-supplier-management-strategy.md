## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: onboarding + tiering + performance + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by cost + trust + speed + risk + quality; not sloganeering
3. **Onboarding**: qualifications / finance / compliance / risk; do not omit
4. **Tiering**: strategic / preferred / standard / exit; do not omit
5. **Performance**: indicators / scoring / assessment / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from onboarding → tiering → performance → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: roster is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with supplier-relationship**: management + relationship co-built
13. **Link with vendor-performance**: vendor + performance co-built
14. **Link with supplier-diversity**: management + diversity co-built
15. **Link with sourcing-operations**: vendor + sourcing co-built
16. **Link with vendor-risk**: management + risk co-built
17. **Toolchain**: SAP Ariba Supplier Mgmt / Coupa SPM / Ivalua / HICX / Aravo
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a supplier management strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: supplier management — the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
