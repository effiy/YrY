## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: business + advisory + advocacy + Governance + Measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; no empty slogans
3. **Business**: alignment + prioritization + priority + investment; none missing
4. **Advisory**: leadership + organization design + personnel + documentation; none missing
5. **Advocacy**: employee voice + experience + integrity; none missing
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / docs / drift; none missing
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; none missing
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from business -> advisory -> advocacy -> Governance -> Measurement; no skipping levels
9. **Not report-only**: headcount supported is only the starting point; not the endpoint
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must have landed evidence; no ambiguity
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **and hr-operations links**: HR business partner + HR Operations co-build
13. **and hr-shared-services links**: HR business partner + HR shared services co-build
14. **and hr-service-delivery links**: HR business partner + HR service delivery co-build
15. **and hr-transformation links**: HR business partner + HR transformation co-build
16. **and organizational-leadership links**: HR business partner + organizational leadership co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Workday / SAP SuccessFactors / ServiceNow HR / OrgVitality / Lattice
18. **Publicly accessible**: strategy accessible to everyone; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: Evolve and update; Not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must HR business partner strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: HR business partner the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
