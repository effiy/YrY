## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: positioning + content + channel + cadence + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by impact + leads + revenue + recruiting + experience; not sloganeering
3. **Positioning**: role / pain points / value / differentiation / tag; do not omit
4. **Content**: original / repost / video / live / topic selection; do not omit
5. **Channel**: LinkedIn / Twitter / GitHub / Speaking / Podcast; do not omit
6. **Cadence**: daily / weekly / monthly / theme / quarterly; do not omit
7. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
8. **Measurement**: impact + leads + revenue + recruiting + experience; do not omit
9. **Not one-shot**: progress from positioning → content → channel → cadence → measurement; no skipping
10. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
11. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
12. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
13. **Link with brand-identity**: Personal Brand + Brand Identity co-built
14. **Link with content**: Personal Brand + Content co-built
15. **Link with social-selling**: Personal Brand + Social Selling co-built
16. **Link with employer-branding**: Personal Brand + Employer Brand co-built
17. **Link with thought-leadership**: Personal Brand + Thought Leadership co-built
18. **Toolchain**: LinkedIn / Hootsuite / Buffer / Sprout / RSS / Notion
19. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
20. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
21. **First principles**: why must Personal Branding; the worst consequence of not doing it
22. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by the company brand; if solvable do not introduce a heavy strategy
23. **Occam**: the simpler Personal Branding is, the better; cut redundant channels
