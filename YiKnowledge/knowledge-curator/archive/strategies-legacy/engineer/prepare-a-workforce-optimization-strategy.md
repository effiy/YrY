## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Efficiency + experience + compliance + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: Prioritize by cost + speed + experience + compliance + risk; not sloganeering
3. **Efficiency**: Process / hours / automation; do not omit
4. **Experience**: Scheduling / leave / flexibility; do not omit
5. **Compliance**: Hours / overtime / labor law; do not omit
6. **Governance**: Owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: Efficiency + cost + compliance + satisfaction + risk; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: Progressive from efficiency → experience → compliance → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: Cuts are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: Every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: Strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with workforce-management**: Optimization + management co-built
13. **Link with workforce-planning**: Optimization + planning co-built
14. **Link with workforce-transformation**: Optimization + transformation co-built
15. **Link with resource-allocation**: Optimization + allocation co-built
16. **Link with capacity-planning**: Optimization + capacity co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Kronos / UKG / Workday / Deputy / Reflexis
18. **Publicly queryable**: Strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: Evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: Why must workforce optimization strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: How much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: Second-order consequences after strategy (cost / speed / experience / compliance) 
23. **Occam**: Workforce optimization the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
