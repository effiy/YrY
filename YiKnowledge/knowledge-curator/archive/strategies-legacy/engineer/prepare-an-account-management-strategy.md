## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: customer + cadence + tooling + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by NRR + expansion + retention + NPS + revenue share; not sloganeering
3. **Accounts**: named / tier / multi-tenant + switching; do not omit
4. **Cadence**: daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly / annual; do not omit
5. **Tooling**: CRM / CS platform / play / communication / integration; do not omit
6. **Governance**: charter + owner + budget + guidelines + retirement; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: NRR + expansion + retention + NPS + revenue share; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from customer → cadence → tooling → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Links with customer-success**: account manager + CS co-build
13. **Links with sales-playbook**: account manager + sales playbook co-build
14. **Links with ABX**: account manager + ABX co-build
15. **Links with CS-ops**: account manager + CS operations co-build
16. **Links with customer-health**: account manager + health co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce / HubSpot / Gainsight / Catalyst / ChurnZero / Outreach / Salesloft / Clari / Clari-crowd
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must account manager strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by part-time CSM; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / retention / business)
23. **Occam**: account manager the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
