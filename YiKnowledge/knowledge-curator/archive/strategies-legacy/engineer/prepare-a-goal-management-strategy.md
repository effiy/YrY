## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: alignment + setting + tracking + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Align**: vision / strategy / up-down / left-right; do not omit
4. **Set**: OKR / KPI / SMART / boundary; do not omit
5. **Track**: cadence / scoring / retrospective / adjustment; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from alignment -> setting -> tracking -> governance -> measurement progressive; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: goal count is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with okr**: goal-management + OKR co-built
13. **Link with performance-management**: goal-management + performance management co-built
14. **Link with feedback-culture**: goal-management + feedback culture co-built
15. **Link with leadership**: goal-management + leadership co-built
16. **Link with employee-development**: goal-management + employee development co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Workday Goals / Lattice / 15Five / Ally.io / Perdoo
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must goal-management strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: goal-management the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
