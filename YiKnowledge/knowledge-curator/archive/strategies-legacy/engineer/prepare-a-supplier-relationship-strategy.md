## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: tiering + co-creation + governance + measurement; no missing dimension.
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by cost + trust + speed + risk + innovation; no empty slogans.
3. **Segment**: strategic / preferred / ordinary / risk; none missing.
4. **Co-create**: joint planning / joint innovation / information sharing; none missing.
5. **Govern**: cadence / agenda / action items / retrospective; none missing.
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / docs / drift; none missing.
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; none missing.
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from tiering → co-creation → governance → measurement; no skipping levels.
9. **Not report-only**: banquets are only the starting point; not the endpoint.
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must have landed evidence; no ambiguity.
11. **Versioned**: the strategy has versions; evolution is traceable.
12. **Link with supplier-management**: relationship + management co-build.
13. **Link with vendor-performance**: relationship + performance co-build.
14. **Link with sourcing-operations**: relationship + sourcing co-build.
15. **Link with vendor-risk**: relationship + risk co-build.
16. **Link with supplier-diversity**: relationship + diversification co-build.
17. **Toolchain**: SAP Ariba / Coupa / Ivalua / HICX / Aravo.
18. **Publicly accessible**: the strategy is accessible to everyone; not hidden.
19. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot.
20. **First principles**: why we must do a supplier-relationship strategy; the worst consequence of not doing it.
21. **Inversion**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy.
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / trust / speed / risk).
23. **Occam**: simpler supplier relationship is better; cut redundant layers.
