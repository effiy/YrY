## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: value + differentiation + evidence + communication + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by candidate NPS + retention + recruiting cost + revenue per head + brand exposure; no sloganeering
3. **Value**: compensation + benefits + development + purpose + culture; do not omit
4. **Differentiation**: unique + defensible + evidence + story + counter-examples; do not omit
5. **Evidence**: data + cases + video + transparency + authenticity; do not omit
6. **Communicate**: channels + copy + timing + personalization + transparency; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: candidate NPS + retention + recruiting cost + eNPS + brand exposure; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from value → differentiation → evidence → communication → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **No sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with employer-branding**: EVP + EB co-built
13. **Link with hr**: EVP + HR co-built
14. **Link with talent**: EVP + talent co-built
15. **Link with compensation**: EVP + compensation co-built
16. **Link with benefits**: EVP + benefits co-built
17. **Toolchain**: LinkedIn Talent Insights / Glassdoor / Qualtrics Employee EX / Korn Ferry / Gartner / Radford / Pave
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must an EVP strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can a comp package solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / retention / business)
23. **Occam**: EVP the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
