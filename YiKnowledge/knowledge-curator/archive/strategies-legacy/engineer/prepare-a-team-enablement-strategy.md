## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identify + tools + training + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Identify**: capability / role / gap / priority; do not omit
4. **Tools**: collaboration / automation / self-service / standards; do not omit
5. **Train**: onboarding / advanced / retrospective / sharing; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from identify → tools → training → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: tool count only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with team**: team enablement + team co-build
13. **Link with team-design**: team enablement + team design co-build
14. **Link with team-development**: team enablement + team development co-build
15. **Link with training-and-enablement**: team enablement + training enablement co-build
16. **Link with employee-development**: team enablement + employee development co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Notion / Confluence / Range / Lattice / Workday
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must team enablement strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: team enablement the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
