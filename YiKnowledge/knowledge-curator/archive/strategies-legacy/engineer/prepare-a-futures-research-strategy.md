## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: framework + scenario + rehearsal + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: set priority by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Framework Framework**: STEEP / drivers / uncertainty / time; do not omit
4. **Scenario Scenario**: baseline / optimistic / pessimistic / disruptive; do not omit
5. **Rehearsal Wind-Tunnel**: strategy / rehearsal / roadmap / option; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from framework → scenario → rehearsal → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: prophecy is only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with trend-research**: future + trend co-build
13. **link with innovation-research**: future + innovation co-build
14. **link with strategic-foresight**: future + strategic foresight co-build
15. **link with market-forecast**: future + forecast co-build
16. **link with market-research**: future + market co-build
17. **toolchain**: IftF / Sohail Inayatullah / Scenario.io / Futures Toolkit / Hawkewood
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must futures research strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable don't introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: futures research the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
