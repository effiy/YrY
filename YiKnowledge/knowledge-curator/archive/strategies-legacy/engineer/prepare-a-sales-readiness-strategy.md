## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: scope + training + certification + launch + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by ramp + quota attainment + revenue + retention + NPS; not sloganeering
3. **Scope**: new-hire / new-feature / new-market / re-cert / competitive; do not omit
4. **Training**: path / module / accompany practice / demo / role-play; do not omit
5. **Certification**: exam / assessment / badge / sunset / re-certification; do not omit
6. **Go-live**: checklist / readiness-score / approval / cutoff / rollback; do not omit
7. **Measure**: ramp + quota attainment + revenue + retention + NPS; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progress gradually from scope -> training -> certification -> launch -> measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with sales-enablement**: sales readiness + sales enablement co-built
13. **Link with sales-coaching**: sales readiness + sales coaching co-built
14. **Link with sales-playbook**: sales readiness + sales playbook co-built
15. **Link with product-launch**: sales readiness + product launch co-built
16. **Link with field-enablement**: sales readiness + field enablement co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Mindtickle / Second-Nature / Highspot / Seismic / Showpad / Gong-Enablement / Lessonly / Allego
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must sales readiness; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by self-study; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: sales readiness the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
