## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: model + pricing + packaging + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **model Model**: one-time / subscription / usage / channel; do not omit
4. **pricing Price**: cost / value / competition / tiering; do not omit
5. **packaging Package**: edition / module / add-on / discount; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progress from model → pricing → packaging → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: bills are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with pricing**: revenue + pricing co-build
13. **Link with packaging**: revenue + packaging co-build
14. **Link with business**: revenue + business strategy co-build
15. **Link with sales**: revenue + sales co-build
16. **Link with growth**: revenue + growth co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce CPQ / Conga CPQ / Zoura / Chargebee / Stripe Billing
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must revenue strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by defaults; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: revenue the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
