## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: cash + financing + risk + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Cash**: receipts + reconciliation + allocation + pooling; do not omit
4. **Financing**: limit + cost + term + rating; do not omit
5. **Risk**: FX / interest rate / liquidity / credit; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from cash → financing → risk → governance → measurement progressively; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: payments are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with treasury-management**: operations + management co-built
13. **Link with financial-operations**: treasury + financial operations co-built
14. **Link with cash-management**: treasury + cash management co-built
15. **Link with working-capital**: treasury + working capital co-built
16. **Link with capital-planning**: treasury + capital planning co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Kyriba / FIS / ION / Wall Street Systems / SAP S/4HANA
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must treasury operations strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: treasury operations the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
