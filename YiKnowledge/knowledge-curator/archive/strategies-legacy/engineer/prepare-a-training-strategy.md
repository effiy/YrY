## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: needs + design + delivery + evaluation + iteration; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by completion rate + business impact + retention + satisfaction + compliance; not sloganeering
3. **Demand**: business + role + gap + compliance + strategy; do not omit
4. **Design**: goal + outline + pedagogy + cases + evaluation; do not omit
5. **Delivery**: online / cohort / instructor-led / on-job / blended; do not omit
6. **Evaluate**: Kirkpatrick L1-L4 + ROI + business impact + satisfaction; do not omit
7. **Iterate**: feedback + update + version + retire + upgrade; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress gradually from needs -> design -> delivery -> evaluation -> iteration; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Work with learning-and-development**: training + L&D co-built
13. **Work with customer-education**: training + education co-built
14. **Work with hr**: training + HR co-built
15. **Work with talent**: training + talent co-built
16. **Work with product-strategy**: training + product strategy co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Docebo / Cornerstone / Workday Learning / Degreed / Coursera / Udemy Business / LinkedIn Learning / Skillshare / 360Learning / TalentLMS
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a training strategy is needed; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by self-study; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / retention / business) 
23. **Occam**: training the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
