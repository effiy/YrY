## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: principle + Process + accountability + Governance + Measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: set priority by efficiency + trust + speed + Risk + cost; no empty slogans
3. **Principle**: fairness / transparency / privacy / security; no leakage
4. **Process**: Review / Launch / Monitoring / decommission; no leakage
5. **Accountability**: Role / authority-responsibility / traceability; no leakage
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; no leakage
7. **Measurement**: use-case coverage rate + Risk rate + accountability coverage rate + Risk + cost; no leakage
8. **Not one-shot**: from principle → Process → accountability → Governance → Measurement gradually; no skipping levels
9. **No report-ism**: use cases are only the start; not the end
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must mark implementation evidence; no vagueness
11. **Versioned**: strategy is versioned; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with model-risk-management**: AI Governance + ModelRisk co-build
13. **Link with ai-ethics**: AI Governance + ethics co-build
14. **Link with ai-safety**: AI Governance + security co-build
15. **Link with model-governance**: AI Governance + model governance co-build
16. **Link with ai-accountability**: AI Governance + accountability co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Credo AI / Holistic AI / IBM AI Fairness / Microsoft Responsible AI / Google PAIR
18. **Publicly discoverable**: strategy is publicly discoverable; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must AI Governance; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion**: how much can be solved by relying on after-the-fact correction; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequence after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / Risk)
23. **Occam's razor**: AI Governance simpler is better; cut redundant layers
