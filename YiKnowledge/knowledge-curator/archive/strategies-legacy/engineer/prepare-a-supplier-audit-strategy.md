## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: plan + onsite + findings + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; no sloganeering
3. **Plan**: scope / frequency / team / standards; do not omit
4. **Onsite**: opening / onsite / closing / report; do not omit
5. **Findings**: grading / remediation / tracking / closure; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from plan → onsite → findings → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: inspection is only the start; not the end
10. **No sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with supplier-management**: audit + vendor management co-build
13. **Link with supplier-qualification**: audit + qualification co-build
14. **Link with supplier-performance**: audit + performance co-build
15. **Link with procurement**: audit + procurement co-build
16. **Link with quality-assurance**: audit + quality assurance co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Aravo / HICX / Coupa SPM / EcoVadis / Navex
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must have an audit strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by defaults; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler the audit the better; cut redundant layers
