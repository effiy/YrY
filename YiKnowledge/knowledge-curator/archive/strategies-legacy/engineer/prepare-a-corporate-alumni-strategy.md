## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: build + connect + value + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Build**: roster / platform / charter / launch; do not omit
4. **Connect**: events / content / community / greetings; do not omit
5. **Value**: recruiting / business / brand / charity; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from build → connect → value → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: member count is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with alumni-program**: corporate alumni + alumni program co-built
13. **Link with employee-offboarding**: corporate alumni + employee offboarding co-built
14. **Link with employer-branding**: corporate alumni + employer branding co-built
15. **Link with employee-referral**: corporate alumni + employee referral co-built
16. **Link with employee-retention**: corporate alumni + employee retention co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Alumni Space / Enterprise Alumni / Graduway / Salesforce + Alumni / PeopleGrove
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a corporate alumni strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: corporate alumni — the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
