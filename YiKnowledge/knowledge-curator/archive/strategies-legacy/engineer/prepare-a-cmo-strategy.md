## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: brand + demand + content + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + conversion + speed + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Brand**: positioning / experience / assets / consistency / measurement; do not omit
4. **Demand**: MQL / SQL / pipeline / scoring / attribution; do not omit
5. **Content**: strategy / audience / theme / channel / measurement; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: growth + trust + conversion + speed + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from brand -> demand -> content -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with cro**: CMO + CRO co-build
13. **Link with brand**: CMO + Brand co-build
14. **Link with content**: CMO + Content co-build
15. **Link with demand-gen**: CMO + Demand co-build
16. **Link with leadership**: CMO + Leadership co-build
17. **Toolchain**: HubSpot / Marketo / Salesforce / 6sense / Drift
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must CMO; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can sales self-marketing solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / conversion / cost)
23. **Occam**: CMO the simpler the better; cut redundant channels
