## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: process + data + systems + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by accuracy + timeliness + compliance + experience + cost; no sloganeering
3. **Process**: hire-to-retire / payroll / benefits / compliance / employee; do not omit
4. **Data**: employee / compensation / performance / training / exit; do not omit
5. **Systems**: HRIS / payroll / benefits / ATS / LMS; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: accuracy + timeliness + compliance + experience + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from process → data → systems → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **No sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with talent-acquisition**: HROps + TA co-built
13. **Link with workforce-planning**: HROps + Workforce co-built
14. **Link with compensation**: HROps + Compensation co-built
15. **Link with benefits**: HROps + Benefits co-built
16. **Link with performance-management**: HROps + Performance co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Workday / SAP SuccessFactors / BambooHR / Gusto / ADP
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must have HROps; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by Excel manual work; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / compliance / experience / retention)
23. **Occam**: HROps the simpler the better; cut redundant processes
