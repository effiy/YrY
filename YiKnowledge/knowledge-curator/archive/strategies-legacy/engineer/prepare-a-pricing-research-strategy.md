## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: method + data + analysis + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Method**: Van Westendorp / Gabor-Granger / conjoint / A/B; do not omit
4. **Data**: sample / preference / price / assumption; do not omit
5. **Analyze**: curve / segment / elasticity / optimize; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from method → data → analysis → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: quotation only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Links with product-pricing**: research + pricing co-build
13. **Links with product-monetization**: research + monetization co-build
14. **Links with customer-research**: research + customer research co-build
15. **Links with competitive-research**: research + competitor co-build
16. **Links with market-research**: research + market co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Qualtrics conjoint / PriceBeam / Vivaldi / PRX / Van Westendorp tool
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must pricing research strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: research the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
