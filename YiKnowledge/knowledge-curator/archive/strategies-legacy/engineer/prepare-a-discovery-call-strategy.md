## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: objective + pain points + value + cadence + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by win-rate + cycle-time + ACV + renewal + NRR; not sloganeering
3. **Objective**: business / role / pain points / value / decision; do not omit
4. **Pain points**: business / technical / financial / strategic / risk; do not omit
5. **Value**: ROI / TCO / business / strategic / risk; do not omit
6. **Cadence**: first / deep / executive / technical / champion; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: win-rate + cycle-time + ACV + renewal + NRR; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from objective → pain points → value → cadence → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Links with call-coaching**: discovery call + call coaching co-build
13. **Links with value-selling**: discovery call + value selling co-build
14. **Links with sales-methodology**: discovery call + sales methodology co-build
15. **Links with opportunity-management**: discovery call + opportunity management co-build
16. **Links with mutual-action-plan**: discovery call + MAP co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Gong / Chorus / Salesforce / Outreach / Salesloft / 6sense
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must discovery call; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by free-form; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: discovery call the simpler the better; cut redundant questions
