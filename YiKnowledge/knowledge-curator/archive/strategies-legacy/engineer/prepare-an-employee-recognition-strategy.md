## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: design + trigger + redeem + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Design Design**: tier / standard / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Trigger Trigger**: event / automatic / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Redeem Redeem**: points / gift / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from design → trigger → redeem → governance → measurement progressive; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with employee-engagement**: EmployeeRecognition + EmployeeEngagement co-build
13. **Link with performance-management**: EmployeeRecognition + PerformanceManagement co-build
14. **Link with compensation**: EmployeeRecognition + Compensation co-build
15. **Link with culture**: EmployeeRecognition + Culture co-build

17. **Toolchain**: Bonusly / Kazoo / Achievers / Awardco / Reward Gateway
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must EmployeeRecognition; worst consequence of not doing it
