## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identify + assess + communicate + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Identify Identify**: capability / limitation / risk / incident / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Assess Assess**: severity / scope / audience / priority / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Communicate Communicate**: channel / cadence / audience / feedback / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from identify → assess → communicate → governance → measurement progressive; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with ai-transparency**: AIRD + AITransparency co-build
13. **Link with ai-ethics**: AIRD + AIEthics co-build
14. **Link with ai-governance**: AIRD + AIGovernance co-build
15. **Link with ai-safety**: AIRD + AISafety co-build
16. **Link with ai-incident-response**: AIRD + AIIR co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Model Card Toolkit / Datasheet / AI Disclosure Framework / AIPreamble / Disclosure.studio
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must AIRD; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can announcements solve; if solvable do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: AIRD the simpler the better; cut redundant sections
