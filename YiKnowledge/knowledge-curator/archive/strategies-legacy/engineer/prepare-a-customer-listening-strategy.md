## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: channel + collect + closed loop + governance + measurement; no missing dimension.
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; no empty slogans.
3. **Channel**: survey / social media / closed loop; none missing.
4. **Collect**: quantitative / qualitative / closed loop; none missing.
5. **Closed loop**: distribute / actions / closed loop; none missing.
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / docs / drift; none missing.
7. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; none missing.
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from channel → collect → closed loop → governance → measurement; no skipping levels.
9. **Not report-only**: reports are only the starting point; not the endpoint.
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must have landed evidence; no ambiguity.
11. **Versioned**: the strategy has versions; evolution is traceable.
12. **Link with voice-of-customer**: Listening + VoC co-build.
13. **Link with customer-feedback**: Listening + Feedback co-build.
14. **Link with customer-insight**: Listening + Insight co-build.
15. **Link with customer-experience**: Listening + CX co-build.
16. **Link with nps**: Listening + NPS co-build.
17. **Toolchain**: Qualtrics / Medallia / SurveyMonkey / Sprinklr / Brandwatch.
18. **Publicly accessible**: the strategy is accessible to everyone; not hidden.
19. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot.
20. **First principles**: why we must do Listening; the worst consequence of not doing it.
21. **Inversion**: how much can a survey alone solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy.
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk).
23. **Occam**: simpler Listening is better; cut redundant layers.
