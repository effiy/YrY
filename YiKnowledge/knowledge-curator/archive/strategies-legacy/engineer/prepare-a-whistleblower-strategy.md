## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: channels + protection + investigation + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by trust + speed + risk + compliance + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Channels Channel**: hotline / portal / grievance / external / regulator; do not omit
4. **Protection Protection**: confidentiality / anti-retaliation / anonymity / legal / psychological; do not omit
5. **Investigation Investigation**: intake / triage / routing / closed loop / report; do not omit
6. **Governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement Measure**: trust + speed + risk + compliance + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from channels → protection → investigation → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link to ethics**: WB + Ethics co-build
13. **Link to code-of-conduct**: WB + Conduct co-build
14. **Link to investigations**: WB + Investigations co-build
15. **Link to compliance**: WB + Compliance co-build
16. **Link to corporate-governance**: WB + Governance co-build
17. **Toolchain**: NAVEX EthicsPoint / Convercent / SAI360 / WhistleB / Expolab
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must we have WB; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by relying on open-door management; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (trust / speed / risk / compliance) 
23. **Occam**: WB the simpler the better; cut redundant channels
