## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: account + breakdown + action + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by ARPA lift + revenue + expansion + efficiency + renewal; not sloganeering
3. **Account**: total / active / paying / cohort / segment; do not omit
4. **Breakdown**: new / expansion / segment / cohort / deal-size; do not omit
5. **Action**: playbook / training / product / price / positioning; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: ARPA lift + revenue + expansion + efficiency + renewal; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from account -> breakdown -> action -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with arr**: ARPA + ARR co-built
13. **Link with acv**: ARPA + ACV co-built
14. **Link with pricing**: ARPA + Pricing co-built
15. **Link with expansion**: ARPA + Expansion co-built
16. **Link with upsell-cross-sell**: ARPA + cross/up-sell co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Snowflake / ChartMogul / Baremetrics / Salesforce / Chartio / Amplitude
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must ARPA; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can account totals solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention) 
23. **Occam**: ARPA the simpler the better; cut redundant assumptions
