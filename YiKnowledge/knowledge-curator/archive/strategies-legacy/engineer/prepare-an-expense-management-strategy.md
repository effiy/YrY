## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: submission + approval + reimbursement + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Submission**: forms / receipts / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Approval**: layered approval / thresholds / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Reimbursement**: accounts / reconciliation / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from submission → approval → reimbursement → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Links with accounts-payable**: ExpenseManagement + AccountsPayable co-build
13. **Links with budgeting**: ExpenseManagement + Budgeting co-build
14. **Links with travel-policy**: ExpenseManagement + TravelPolicy co-build
15. **Links with cash-management**: ExpenseManagement + CashManagement co-build
16. **Links with general-ledger**: ExpenseManagement + GeneralLedger co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Concur / Expensify / Coupa / Ramp / Brex
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must ExpenseManagement; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by paper receipts; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: ExpenseManagement the simpler the better; cut redundant rules
