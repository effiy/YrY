## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: physical + digital + service + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Physical**: space / furniture / equipment / environment; do not omit
4. **Digital**: collaboration / tools / self-service / experience; do not omit
5. **Service**: reception / catering / health / support; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from physical → digital → service → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: scores are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with employee-experience**: workplace + employee experience co-built
13. **Link with workplace**: workplace experience + workplace co-built
14. **Link with facilities**: workplace experience + facilities co-built
15. **Link with employee-wellbeing**: workplace experience + employee wellbeing co-built
16. **Link with corporate-culture**: workplace experience + corporate culture co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Envoy / Sine / Eptura / Tribeloo / Microsoft Places
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why workplace experience strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler workplace experience is, the better; cut redundant layers
