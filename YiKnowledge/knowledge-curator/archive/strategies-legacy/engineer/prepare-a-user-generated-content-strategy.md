## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: incentive + moderation + repurpose + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Incentive**: reward / campaign / tier / case; do not omit
4. **Moderate**: policy / risk control / review / removal; do not omit
5. **Repurpose**: license / marketing / product / sales; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress gradually from incentive -> moderation -> repurpose -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: photo sharing is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Work with content**: UGC + content co-built
13. **Work with customer-advocacy**: UGC + advocacy co-built
14. **Work with customer-program**: UGC + program co-built
15. **Work with community-engagement**: UGC + community co-built
16. **Work with content-distribution**: UGC + distribution co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Bazaarvoice / Yotpo / TurnTo / Tagboard / TINT
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a UGC strategy is needed; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by defaults; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: UGC the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
