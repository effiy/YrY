## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: assets + leases + maintenance + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Asset**: buildings / equipment / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Lease**: contracts / tenants / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Maintenance**: repair requests / work orders / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: gradual from assets → leases → maintenance → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with corporate-real-estate**: PropertyManagement + CorporateRealEstate co-build
13. **Link with facility-management**: PropertyManagement + FacilityManagement co-build
14. **Link with asset-management**: PropertyManagement + AssetManagement co-build
15. **Link with workplace**: PropertyManagement + Workplace co-build
16. **Link with sustainability**: PropertyManagement + Sustainability co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Yardi / MRI / RealPage / AppFolio / Buildium
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must PropertyManagement; worst consequence of not doing
