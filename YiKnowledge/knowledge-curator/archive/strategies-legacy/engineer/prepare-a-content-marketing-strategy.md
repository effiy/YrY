## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + theme + channel + cadence + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by MQL + SQL + revenue + retention + SOV; not sloganeering
3. **Audience**: ICP + persona + stage + channel + intent; do not omit
4. **Topics**: education + thought leadership + product + case + comparison; do not omit
5. **Channel**: blog / video / podcast / webinar / retirement; do not omit
6. **Cadence**: daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly / yearly; do not omit
7. **Measure**: MQL + SQL + revenue + retention + SOV; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from audience -> theme -> channel -> cadence -> measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ism**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **linked with content**: content marketing + content co-build
13. **linked with SEO**: content marketing + SEO co-build
14. **linked with demand-generation**: content marketing + funnel co-build
15. **linked with brand**: content marketing + brand co-build
16. **linked with social-media**: content marketing + social co-build
17. **Toolchain**: WordPress / Webflow / Contentful / Sanity / HubSpot / Marketo / -kalyviyo / Pardot / Newscred
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must content marketing strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved relying on single-point content; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / brand) 
23. **Occam**: content marketing the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
