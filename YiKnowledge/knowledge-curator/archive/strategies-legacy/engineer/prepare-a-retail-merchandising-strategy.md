## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Visual + product + data + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: Prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Visual**: Display / lighting / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Product**: SKU / inventory / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Data**: Sales / conversion / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: Owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: Efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: From visual → product → data → governance → measurement progressively; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: Reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: Every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: Strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Links with merchandising**: Retail Merchandising + Merchandising co-build
13. **Links with assortment**: Retail Merchandising + Assortment co-build

16. **Links with promotion**: Retail Merchandising + Promotion co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Blue Yonder / JDA / Spaceman / SAP S/4HANA / SymphonyAI
18. **Publicly queryable**: Strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: Evolution updates; not one-shot
