## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Role + Responsibility + Decision + Governance + Measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Role**: product / domain / platform; do not omit
4. **Responsibility**: definition / SLA / evolution; do not omit
5. **Decision**: change / priority / risk; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: product count + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from Role → Responsibility → Decision → Governance → Measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: owner count is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Data-product-management linkage**: owner + data product co-build
13. **Data-mesh linkage**: owner + data mesh co-build
14. **Data-ownership linkage**: owner + data ownership co-build
15. **Data-stewardship linkage**: owner + data stewardship co-build
16. **Data-governance linkage**: owner + data governance co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Collibra / Alation / DataHub / Amundsen / Custom
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must a data product owner exist; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: data product owner the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
