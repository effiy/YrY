## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: demand + channel + experience + data + governance; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by fill-rate + quality + retention + experience + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Demand**: JD / role / headcount / priority / SLA; do not omit
4. **Channel**: inbound / outbound / referral / agency / alumni; do not omit
5. **experience Experience**: communication / feedback / process / offer / rejection; do not omit
6. **data Data**: source / quality / time-to-hire / cost-per-hire / funnel; do not omit
7. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
8. **measurement Measure**: fill-rate + quality + retention + experience + cost; do not omit
9. **not one-shot**: progressive from demand → channel → experience → data → governance; no skipping
10. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
11. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
12. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
13. **Link with talent**: TA + Talent co-built
14. **Link with recruiting**: TA + Recruiting co-built
15. **Link with hiring-plan**: TA + Hiring Plan co-built
16. **Link with employer-branding**: TA + Employer Brand co-built
17. **Link with onboarding-flow**: TA + Onboarding co-built
18. **Toolchain**: Greenhouse / Lever / Ashby / LinkedIn Recruiter / Gem / SeekOut
19. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
20. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
21. **First principles**: why must TA; worst consequence of not doing it
22. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by internal promotion; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
23. **Occam**: the simpler TA the better; cut redundant process
