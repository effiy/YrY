## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Theme + pattern + channel + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: Prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Theme**: Mission / values / strategy / culture; do not omit
4. **Pattern**: Hero / transformation / customer / employee / data; do not omit
5. **Channel**: Internal / external / social / video / self-service; do not omit
6. **Governance**: Owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: Coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: Progressive from theme → pattern → channel → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: Story count only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: Every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: Strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with brand-journalism**: Corporate storytelling + brand journalism co-build
13. **Link with leadership-communications**: Corporate storytelling + leadership communications co-build
14. **Link with employee-advocacy**: Corporate storytelling + employee advocacy co-build
15. **Link with internal-communications**: Corporate storytelling + internal communications co-build
16. **Link with corporate-communications**: Corporate storytelling + corporate communications co-build
17. **Toolchain**: NewsCred / Contently / Pitcher / SocialChorus / Firstup
18. **Publicly queryable**: Strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: Evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: Why must corporate storytelling strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: Rely on default how much can be solved; if solvable don't introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: Second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: Corporate storytelling the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
