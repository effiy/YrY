## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: channel + orchestration + experience + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Channel**: voice / email / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Orchestration**: route / context / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Experience**: wait / transfer / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from channel → orchestration → experience → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with customer-service**: Contact + Service co-built
13. **Link with customer-care**: Contact + Care co-built
14. **Link with customer-experience**: Contact + Experience co-built
15. **Link with customer-communications**: Contact + Communications co-built
16. **Link with customer-support-ops**: Contact + SupportOps co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Genesys / Five9 / Talkdesk / NICE / Avaya
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must CustomerContact; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by phone; if solvable, don't introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: CustomerContact the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
