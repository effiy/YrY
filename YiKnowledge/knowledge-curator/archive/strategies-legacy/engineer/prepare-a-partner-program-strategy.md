## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: tiering + qualification + benefits + cadence + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by co-sell ARR + renewal + attach rate + certification rate + ROI; no sloganeering
3. **Tiers**: authorized / premier / elite + promotion / demotion / retirement; do not omit
4. **Qualification**: certification + revenue + customers + team + case studies; do not omit
5. **Benefits**: discount + MDF + leads + training + demos + co-sell; do not omit
6. **Cadence**: onboarding / quarterly / monthly / weekly report + upgrade; do not omit
7. **Measure**: co-sell ARR + renewal + attach rate + certification rate + ROI; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: gradual from tiering → qualification → benefits → cadence → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with partnership**: partner program + partnership strategy co-build
13. **Link with partner-success**: partner program + partner success co-build
14. **Link with partner-marketing**: partner program + co-marketing co-build
15. **Link with affiliate-program**: partner program + affiliate co-build
16. **Link with channel**: partner program + channel co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Crossbeam / Reveal / Allbound / Impartner / PartnerStack / Tackle / Zift / Maggioli PRM / Channeltivity
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy is queryable by everyone; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a partner-program strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by relying on organic referrals; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / channel conflict / business) 
23. **Occam**: partner program simpler is better; cut redundant steps
