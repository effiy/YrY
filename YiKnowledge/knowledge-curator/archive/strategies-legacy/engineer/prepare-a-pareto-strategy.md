## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identification + measurement + prioritization + governance + measurement; no missing dimension.
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering.
3. **Identify**: factors / categories / boundaries / data / traces; do not omit.
4. **Measure**: frequency / impact / cumulative / sort / visualize; do not omit.
5. **Prioritize**: 80/20 / high impact / resources / cadence / closed loop; do not omit.
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit.
7. **Outcome measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit.
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from identification → measurement → prioritization → governance → measurement; no skipping.
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end.
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague.
11. **Versioned**: the strategy has versions; evolution is traceable.
12. **Link with quality-management**: Pareto + QM co-build.
13. **Link with root-cause-analysis**: Pareto + RCA co-build.
14. **Link with continuous-improvement**: Pareto + CI co-build.
15. **Link with six-sigma**: Pareto + SixSigma co-build.
16. **Link with lean**: Pareto + Lean co-build.
17. **Toolchain**: Minitab / Excel / Power BI / Tableau / JMP.
18. **Publicly queryable**: the strategy is look-up-able by everyone; not hidden.
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot.
20. **First principles**: why we must do Pareto; the worst consequence of not doing it.
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can even allocation solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy.
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk).
23. **Occam**: simpler Pareto is better; cut redundant dimensions.
