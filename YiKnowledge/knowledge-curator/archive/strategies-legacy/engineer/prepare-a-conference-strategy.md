## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: theme + agenda + experience + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Theme**: positioning / topics / key visual; do not omit
4. **Agenda**: keynote / tracks / speakers / process; do not omit
5. **Experience**: registration / check-in / on-site / retrospective; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from theme → agenda → experience → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: gathering only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Links with event-management**: conference + event co-built
13. **Links with webinar**: conference + webinar co-built
14. **Links with trade-show**: conference + trade show co-built
15. **Links with event-marketing**: conference + marketing co-built
16. **Links with content**: conference + content co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Cvent / Bizzabo / Aventri / Hopin / Swapcard
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must conference strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: conference the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
