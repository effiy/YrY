## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identify + assess + mitigate + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by trust + compliance + risk + speed + cost; not sloganeering
3. **identify Identify**: inventory / classification / priority / key / cascade; do not omit
4. **assess Assess**: financial / security / compliance / operations / concentration; do not omit
5. **mitigate Mitigate**: contract / control / monitoring / emergency / exit; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: trust + compliance + risk + speed + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from identify → assess → mitigate → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with vendor-risk**: TPRM + VRM co-build
13. **link with supply-chain-risk**: TPRM + SCRM co-build
14. **link with risk**: TPRM + Risk co-build
15. **link with compliance**: TPRM + Compliance co-build
16. **link with cybersecurity**: TPRM + Cyber co-build
17. **Toolchain**: ProcessUnity / Aravo / OneTrust / SecurityScorecard / BitSight
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must TPRM; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can contract clauses solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (trust / compliance / risk / speed) 
23. **Occam**: TPRM the simpler the better; cut redundant process
