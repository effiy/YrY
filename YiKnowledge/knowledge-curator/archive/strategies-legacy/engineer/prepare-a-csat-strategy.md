## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: touchpoint + survey + segmentation + closed loop + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by CSAT + retention + repurchase + churn; not sloganeering
3. **Touchpoint**: purchase + onboarding + support + renewal + pre-churn; do not omit
4. **Survey**: post-interaction + post-purchase + post-onboarding + timing + frequency; do not omit
5. **Segmentation**: satisfied + neutral + unsatisfied + industry + tier; do not omit
6. **Closed loop**: follow-up + win-back + upgrade + root-cause + system improvement; do not omit
7. **Measure**: CSAT + response-rate + trend + touchpoint comparison + retention related; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from touchpoint → survey → segmentation → closed loop → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with nps**: CSAT + NPS co-built
13. **Link with customer-experience**: CSAT + experience co-built
14. **Link with customer-support**: CSAT + support co-built
15. **Link with feedback-loop**: CSAT + feedback co-built
16. **Link with customer-success**: CSAT + customer success co-built
17. **Toolchain**: CSAT-Framework / Qualtrics / SurveyMonkey / Typeform / Delighted / Zendesk / Intercom / Freshdesk / Hotjar
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must CSAT strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can intuition solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / retention / business)
23. **Occam**: CSAT the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
