## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + pain points + behavior + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by precision + conversion + retention + experience + revenue; not sloganeering
3. **Audience**: role / responsibility / KPI / budget / channel; do not omit
4. **Pain**: scenario / problem / consequence / expectation / evidence; do not omit
5. **Behavior**: information / decision / purchase / usage / feedback; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: precision + conversion + retention + experience + revenue; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from audience -> pain points -> behavior -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with ICP**: Persona + ICP co-built
13. **Link with customer-segmentation**: Persona + Segmentation co-built
14. **Link with customer-journey**: Persona + Journey co-built
15. **Link with positioning**: Persona + Positioning co-built
16. **Link with messaging-house**: Persona + Messaging House co-built
17. **Toolchain**: User Interviews / Gong / Crystal / LinkedIn / Salesforce
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must Persona; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can ICP solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / precision / conversion / retention)
23. **Occam**: Persona the simpler the better; cut redundant fields
