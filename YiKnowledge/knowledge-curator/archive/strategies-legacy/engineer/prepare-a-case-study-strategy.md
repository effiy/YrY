## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: customer + pain points + solution + value + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by case study impact on revenue + win-rate + cycle-time + renewal + NRR; not sloganeering
3. **customer Customer**: industry / scale / region / use case / consent; do not omit
4. **pain points Pain**: business / technology / financial / strategy / risk; do not omit
5. **solution Solution**: product / implementation / integration / service / support; do not omit
6. **value Value**: ROI / TCO / business / strategy / measurement; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: case study impact on revenue + win-rate + cycle-time + renewal + NRR; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from customer -> pain points -> solution -> value -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **linked with reference-program**: case study + reference program co-built
13. **linked with testimonial**: case study + testimonial co-built
14. **linked with customer-advocacy**: case study + customer advocacy co-built
15. **linked with customer-marketing**: case study + customer marketing co-built
16. **linked with content-marketing**: case study + content marketing co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce / Influitive / Vidyard / WordPress / Drift / Case-Study-Interactive
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why case studies are required; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by free-form improvisation; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: case study the simpler the better; cut redundant sections
