## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: framework + governance + culture + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Framework**: COSO / ISO / closure; do not omit
4. **Governance**: three-tier / closure; do not omit
5. **Culture**: risk awareness / closure; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from framework -> governance -> culture -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with risk-management**: EnterpriseRisk + RiskManagement co-build
13. **Link with operational-risk**: EnterpriseRisk + OperationalRisk co-build
14. **Link with financial-risk**: EnterpriseRisk + FinancialRisk co-build
15. **Link with strategic-risk**: EnterpriseRisk + StrategicRisk co-build
16. **Link with reputational-risk**: EnterpriseRisk + ReputationalRisk co-build
17. **Toolchain**: RSA Archer / ServiceNow GRC / MetricStream / IBM OpenPages / SAP GRC
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must EnterpriseRisk; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by each department managing its own; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: EnterpriseRisk the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
