## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: drafting + negotiation + execution + retirement + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by revenue + profit + speed + risk + retention; not sloganeering
3. **Drafting**: template + clauses + AI drafting + version + review; do not omit
4. **Negotiation**: redline + routing + thresholds + SLA + escalation; do not omit
5. **Execution**: e-sign + integration + repository / library + reminders; do not omit
6. **Retirement**: expiry + renewal + termination + conversion + archive; do not omit
7. **Measure**: revenue + profit + speed + risk + retention; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from drafting → negotiation → execution → retirement → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with contract-management**: CLM + contract management co-built
13. **Link with quote-to-cash**: CLM + Q2C co-built
14. **Link with vendor-risk**: CLM + VRM co-built
15. **Link with renewal**: CLM + renewal co-built
16. **Link with procurement**: CLM + procurement co-built
17. **Toolchain**: DocuSign-CLM / Icertis / Ironclad / ContractWorks / -linksvr / PandaDoc / Adobe-Acrobat-Sign
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must CLM strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can manual management solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / speed / business)
23. **Occam**: CLM the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
