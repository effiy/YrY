## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: relationship + value + channel + measurement + iteration; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by boomerang + referral + brand + opportunity + candidate NPS; not sloganeering
3. **Relationship**: tiered + head + long-tail + profile + tenure; do not omit
4. **Value**: community + resources + training + perks + experience; do not omit
5. **Channel**: Slack / LinkedIn / events / email / alumni portal; do not omit
6. **Measure**: boomerang + referral + opportunity + brand exposure + satisfaction; do not omit
7. **Iterate**: feedback + update + version + retire + upgrade; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from relationship → value → channel → measurement → iteration gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with hr**: alumni + HR co-build
13. **Link with talent**: alumni + talent co-build
14. **Link with employer-branding**: alumni + EB co-build
15. **Link with culture**: alumni + culture co-build
16. **Link with customer-advocacy**: alumni + advocacy co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Hivebrite / Almabees / Graduway / PeopleGrove / Salesforce Experience Cloud / LinkedIn / Slack
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must alumni strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can natural word-of-mouth solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / brand / business)
23. **Occam**: alumni the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
