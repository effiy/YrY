## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Identification + assessment + response + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Identification Identify**: source / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Assessment Assess**: probability / impact / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Response Respond**: avoid / mitigate / transfer / accept / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from identification → assessment → response → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Links with enterprise-risk**: RiskManagement + EnterpriseRisk co-build
13. **Links with operational-risk**: RiskManagement + OperationalRisk co-build
14. **Links with financial-risk**: RiskManagement + FinancialRisk co-build
15. **Links with strategic-risk**: RiskManagement + StrategicRisk co-build
16. **Links with reputational-risk**: RiskManagement + ReputationalRisk co-build
17. **Toolchain**: ServiceNow GRC / RSA Archer / MetricStream / IBM OpenPages / LogicGate
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must RiskManagement; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by relying on experience; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: RiskManagement the simpler the better; cut redundant process
