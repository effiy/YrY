## Action recommendations

1. **five dimensions**: identify + assess + mitigate + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by trust + risk + compliance + speed + cost; not sloganeering
3. **identify Identify**: strategy / financial / operational / compliance / reputation; do not omit
4. **assess Assess**: probability / impact / speed / related / cumulative; do not omit
5. **mitigate Mitigate**: avoid / reduce / transfer / accept / monitor; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: trust + risk + compliance + speed + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from identify → assess → mitigate → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **and corporate-governance linkage**: Risk + Governance co-built
13. **and compliance linkage**: Risk + Compliance co-built
14. **and audit linkage**: Risk + Audit co-built
15. **and information-security linkage**: Risk + Security co-built
16. **and business-continuity linkage**: Risk + BC co-built
17. **toolchain**: LogicManager / Riskonnect / RSA Archer / ServiceNow GRC / MetricStream
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must Risk; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: rely on luck how much can be solved; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (trust / risk / compliance / speed) 
23. **Occam**: Risk the simpler the better; cut redundant committees
