## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: strategy + experience + measurement + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Strategy Strategy**: vision / promise / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Experience Experience**: journey / touchpoints / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Measurement Measure**: NPS / CSAT / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from strategy → experience → measurement → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with customer-experience**: CEM + CX co-built
13. **Link with customer-listening**: CEM + Listening co-built
14. **Link with customer-insight**: CEM + Insight co-built
15. **Link with customer-journey-mapping**: CEM + CJM co-built
16. **Link with service-design**: CEM + ServiceDesign co-built
17. **Tooling**: Qualtrics / Adobe Experience Cloud / Salesforce Experience Cloud / Medallia / Sprinklr
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why CEM is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by the CX team; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: CEM the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
