## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: source + topic + processing + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Source**: feeds / subscriptions / trust / diversity; do not omit
4. **Topic**: relevance / timeliness / value / preference; do not omit
5. **Curate**: excerpt / annotate / revise / cite; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from source → topic → processing → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reposting is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with content**: curation + content co-built
13. **Link with content-distribution**: curation + distribution co-built
14. **Link with influencer-marketing**: curation + influencer co-built
15. **Link with thought-leadership**: curation + thought leadership co-built
16. **Link with newsletter**: curation + Newsletter co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Feedly / Inoreader / Pocket / Curata / Scoop.it
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must content-curation strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by defaults; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: curation the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
