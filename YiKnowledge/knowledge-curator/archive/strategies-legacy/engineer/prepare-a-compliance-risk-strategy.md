## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identify + assess + mitigate + Governance + Measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; no empty slogans
3. **identify Identify**: regulations / business / Process / third party; none missing
4. **assess Assess**: likelihood / impact / rating / matrix; none missing
5. **mitigate Mitigate**: policy / Process / control / training; none missing
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / docs / drift; none missing
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; none missing
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from identify → assess → mitigate → Governance → Measurement; no skipping levels
9. **Not report-only**: checklist only the starting point; not the endpoint
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must have landed evidence; no ambiguity
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **and compliance-management links**: compliance risk + compliance management co-build
13. **links with compliance**: compliance risk + compliance co-build
14. **and regulatory-compliance links**: compliance risk + regulatory compliance co-build
15. **and operational-risk links**: compliance risk + operational risk co-build
16. **and third-party-risk links**: compliance risk + third party risk co-build
17. **Toolchain**: MetricStream / IBM Open Pages / LogicGate / NAVEX / OneTrust
18. **Publicly accessible**: strategy accessible to everyone; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: Evolve and update; Not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must compliance risk strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: compliance risk the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
