## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: goal + milestone + responsibility + risk + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by win-rate + cycle-time + revenue + renewal + NRR; not sloganeering
3. **Objective**: business + value + validation + endpoint + measurement; do not omit
4. **Milestone**: discover / propose / negotiate / close / implementation; do not omit
5. **Accountability**: AE / SE / CS / executive / champion; do not omit
6. **Risk**: blocker + assumption + fallback + upgrade + alternative; do not omit
7. **Measure**: win-rate + cycle-time + revenue + renewal + NRR; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from goal -> milestone -> responsibility -> risk -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with mutual-action-plan**: Win plan + MAP co-build
13. **Link with mutual-close-plan**: Win plan + consensus co-build
14. **Link with deal-review**: Win plan + deal-review co-build
15. **Link with sales-playbook**: Win plan + sales playbook co-build
16. **Link with opportunity-management**: Win plan + opportunity management co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce / Clari / Gong / Outreach / Salesloft / Boost-UP / People-AI
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must win plan; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can AM free-form solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: Win plan the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
