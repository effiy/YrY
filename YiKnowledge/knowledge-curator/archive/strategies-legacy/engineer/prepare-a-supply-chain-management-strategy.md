## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: planning + sourcing + fulfillment + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **planning Plan**: demand / capacity / inventory / cadence / gap; do not omit
4. **sourcing Source**: vendor / contract / negotiation / admission / relationship; do not omit
5. **fulfillment Fulfill**: order / production / logistics / warehousing / delivery; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from planning → sourcing → fulfillment → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **and procurement link**: SCM + Procurement co-build
13. **and supply-chain-risk link**: SCM + Risk co-build
14. **and logistics link**: SCM + Logistics co-build
15. **and vendor-management link**: SCM + Vendor co-build
16. **and sustainability link**: SCM + Sustainability co-build
17. **Toolchain**: SAP / Oracle SCM / Kinaxis / Blue Yonder / Coupa
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must SCM; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can inventory solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: SCM the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
