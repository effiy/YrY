## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: culture + behavior + measurement + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Culture**: beliefs / values / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Behavior**: start from the customer / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Measure**: customer metrics / employee metrics / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from culture → behavior → measurement → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with customer-first**: Obsession + First co-built
13. **Link with customer-centered**: Obsession + Centered co-built
14. **Link with customer-experience**: Obsession + CX co-built
15. **Link with corporate-culture**: Obsession + Culture co-built
16. **Link with leadership**: Obsession + Leadership co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Culture Amp / Qualtrics / Glint / Satmetrix / Zendesk
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must Obsession; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by mission; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
