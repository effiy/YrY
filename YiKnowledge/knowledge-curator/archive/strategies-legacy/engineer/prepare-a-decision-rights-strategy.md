## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: authority + boundary + escalation + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Authority**: role / decision / scope / paper trail / accountability; do not omit
4. **Boundary**: amount / risk / time validity / compliance / exceptions; do not omit
5. **Escalation**: triggers / path / cadence / closed loop / retrospective; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from authority -> boundary -> escalation -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with corporate-governance**: DR + Governance co-built
13. **Link with raci-matrix**: DR + RACI co-built
14. **Link with organizational-design**: DR + OrgDesign co-built
15. **Link with leadership**: DR + Leadership co-built
16. **Link with delegation-of-authority**: DR + Delegation co-built
17. **Toolchain**: RACI matrix / Decision Matrix / Notion / Lucidchart / Miro
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must DR; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can managers solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: DR the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
