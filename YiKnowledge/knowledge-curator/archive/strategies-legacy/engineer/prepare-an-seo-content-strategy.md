## Action recommendations

1. **five dimensions**: intent + topic + tech + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **intent Intent**: informational / commercial / navigational / transactional; do not omit
4. **topic Topic**: cluster / pillar / sub-page / interlink; do not omit
5. **tech Tech**: crawl / speed / structured / mobile; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from intent -> topic -> tech -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: keywords are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **links with content**: SEO + content co-build
13. **links with organic-search**: SEO + organic search co-build
14. **links with content-distribution**: SEO + distribution co-build
15. **links with content-audit**: SEO + audit co-build
16. **links with content-measurement**: SEO + measurement co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Ahrefs / SEMrush / Clearscope / MarketMuse / Surfer SEO
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must SEO content strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: SEO the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
