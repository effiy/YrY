## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: collection + visualization + insight + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Collect**: SDK / events / privacy / sampling; do not omit
4. **Visualize**: click / scroll / move / conversion; do not omit
5. **Insight**: obstacles / priority / assumption / landing; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress gradually from collection -> visualization -> insight -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: color is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Work with eye-tracking**: heat map + eye tracking co-built
13. **Work with session-replay**: heat map + replay co-built
14. **Work with ux-research**: heat map + UX co-built
15. **Work with content-performance**: heat map + content performance co-built
16. **Work with usability-testing**: heat map + usability co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Hotjar / Crazy Egg / Microsoft Clarity / ContentSquare / Mouseflow
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a heat-map strategy is needed; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by defaults; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: heat map the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
