## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: demand + market + negotiation + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by cost + trust + speed + risk + quality; not sloganeering
3. **Demand**: spec / quantity / time / service; do not omit
4. **Market**: supply / price / trend / risk; do not omit
5. **Negotiate**: strategy / red line / concession / record; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from demand → market → negotiation → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: bidding is only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with procurement-operations**: sourcing + procurement operations co-build
13. **link with strategic-sourcing**: operations + strategic sourcing co-build
14. **link with sourcing**: operations + sourcing co-build
15. **link with supplier-management**: sourcing + vendor management co-build
16. **link with supplier-relationship**: sourcing + vendor relationship co-build
17. **Toolchain**: SAP Ariba Sourcing / Coupa Sourcing / Ivalua / Jaggaer / Keelvar
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must sourcing operations strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: rely on default how much can be solved; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: sourcing operations the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
