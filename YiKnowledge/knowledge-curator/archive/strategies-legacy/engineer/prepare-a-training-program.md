## Action recommendations

1. **Six dimensions**: needs + path + resources + practice + verification + retrospective; no missing dimension
2. **Needs-driven**: by business + individual gap; not gut call
3. **Tiered learning paths**: junior / mid / senior; do not mix
4. **Resources**: internal training + external training + books + videos + practice projects; not single
5. **Practice projects**: every path must pair with practice; not just listening
6. **Verification**: every path must pair with verification; not by gut feel
7. **Retrospective**: every training must have a retrospective; not one-shot
8. **Continuous**: not one-shot; by cadence
9. **Budget**: every training must tag a budget; not vague
10. **Time**: every training must tag time; not unbounded
11. **Link with onboarding**: first month runs training
12. **Link with ladder**: training aligns with ladder growth
13. **Link with mentor**: mentor guides training
14. **Link with knowledge transfer**: knowledge transfer runs via training
15. **Link with 1on1**: 1on1 tracks training progress
16. **Link with tech talk**: tech talk co-builds internal training
17. **Publicly queryable**: everyone can look up the plan; not hidden
18. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
19. **First principles**: why must training; worst consequence of not doing it
20. **Inversion thinking**: how much can self-study solve; if solvable, do not introduce training
21. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after training (capability / retention / trust / recruiting)
22. **Occam**: training the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
