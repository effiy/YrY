## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: hypothesis + experiment + retrospective + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; no empty slogans
3. **Hypothesis**: opportunity / prioritization / hypothesis / metric; none missing
4. **Experiment**: A/B / multivariate / impact / time; none missing
5. **Retrospective**: learning / landing / model / cadence; none missing
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / docs / drift; none missing
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; none missing
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from hypothesis → experiment → retrospective → governance → measurement; no skipping levels
9. **Not report-only**: experiment count is only the starting point; not the endpoint
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must have landed evidence; no ambiguity
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with marketing-analytics**: growth marketing + marketing analytics co-build
13. **Link with growth-loop**: growth marketing + growth flywheel co-build
14. **Link with product-led-growth**: growth marketing + product-led growth co-build
15. **Link with customer-acquisition**: growth marketing + customer acquisition co-build
16. **Link with marketing-operations**: growth marketing + marketing operations co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Amplitude / Mixpanel / Optimizely / GrowthBook / Statsig
18. **Publicly accessible**: strategy accessible to everyone; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why growth marketing strategy is a must; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler growth marketing is the better; cut redundant layers
