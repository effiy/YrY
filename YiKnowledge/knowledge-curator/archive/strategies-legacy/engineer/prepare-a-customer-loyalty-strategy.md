## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: tiering + value + experience + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Tier**: gold / silver / bronze / diamond / black; do not omit
4. **Value**: points / exchange / discount / experience; do not omit
5. **Experience**: personalization / channel / feedback / care; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from tiering → value → experience → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: points are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with loyalty-program**: customer loyalty + loyalty program co-build
13. **Link with customer-retention**: customer loyalty + customer retention co-build
14. **Link with customer-advocacy-program**: customer loyalty + customer advocacy program co-build
15. **Link with customer-success**: customer loyalty + customer success co-build
16. **Link with customer-experience**: customer loyalty + customer experience co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Kobie / Astound Commerce / Smile.io / Yotpo / LoyaltyLion
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must customer loyalty strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: customer loyalty the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
