## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: process + tooling + data + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by ARR + conversion rate + sales cadence + win-rate + sales per capita; not sloganeering
3. **Process**: lead / opp / quote / order / renew + stage + entry criteria; do not omit
4. **Tooling**: CRM + CPQ + CLM + enrichment + dialer + sequencer; do not omit
5. **Data**: lead + account + opp + performance + forecast + commission; do not omit
6. **Governance**: committee + transparency + data quality + exception + retirement; do not omit
7. **Measure**: ARR + conversion rate + sales cadence + win-rate + sales per capita; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from process -> tooling -> data -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with sales-strategy**: Sales operations + sales strategy co-built
13. **Link with revenue-operations**: Sales operations + RevOps co-built
14. **Link with sales-compensation**: Sales operations + sales compensation co-built
15. **Link with territory**: Sales operations + territory co-built
16. **Link with quota**: Sales operations + quota co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce / HubSpot / Microsoft Dynamics / Outreach / SalesLoft / Apollo / Clari / InsightSquared / Gong / Chorus
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must sales operations strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can sales self-learning solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / channel / business)
23. **Occam**: Sales operations the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
