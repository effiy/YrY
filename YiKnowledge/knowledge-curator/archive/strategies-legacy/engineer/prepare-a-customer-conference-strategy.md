## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + content + channel + cadence + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: set priority by attendance -> revenue + renewal + NRR + NPS + expansion; not sloganeering
3. **Audience**: customers / prospects / executives / partners / media; do not omit
4. **Content**: keynote / session / workshop / roundtable / case; do not omit
5. **Channel**: offline / online / hybrid / community / media; do not omit
6. **Cadence**: kick-off / monthly / weekly / day-of / follow-up; do not omit
7. **Measure**: attendance -> revenue + renewal + NRR + NPS + expansion; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from audience -> content -> channel -> cadence -> measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with event-marketing**: customer conference + events co-build
13. **Link with customer-advocacy-program**: customer conference + customer advocacy co-build
14. **Link with product-launch**: customer conference + product launch co-build
15. **Link with customer-engagement**: customer conference + customer engagement co-build
16. **Link with brand-identity**: customer conference + brand co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Cvent / Bizzabo / Hopin / Zoom-Events / Swapcard / Salesforce / Marketo / Splash
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must customer conference; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by relying on small gatherings; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequence after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / brand) 
23. **Occam**: customer conference, the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
