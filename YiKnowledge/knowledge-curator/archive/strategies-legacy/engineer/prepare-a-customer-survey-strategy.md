## Action recommendations

1. **five dimensions**: goal + design + distribute + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **goal Objective**: questions / assumption / decision; do not omit
4. **design Design**: question type / logic / length / test; do not omit
5. **distribute Distribute**: channel / timing / incentive / recovery; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from goal -> design -> distribute -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: questions are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **links with customer-feedback**: survey + feedback co-build
13. **links with customer-satisfaction**: survey + satisfaction co-build
14. **links with customer-interview**: survey + interview co-build
15. **links with customer-pulse**: survey + pulse co-build
16. **links with user-research**: survey + user research co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Qualtrics / SurveyMonkey / Typeform / Google Forms / Microsoft Forms
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must customer survey strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: survey the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
