## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: evaluation + design + launch + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Evaluate**: fit / risk / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Design**: positioning / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Launch**: channels / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from evaluation → design → launch → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with brand-strategy**: BrandExtension + BrandStrategy co-built
13. **Link with brand-portfolio**: BrandExtension + BrandPortfolio co-built
14. **Link with brand-management**: BrandExtension + BrandManagement co-built
15. **Link with product-launch**: BrandExtension + ProductLaunch co-built
16. **Link with brand-equity**: BrandExtension + BrandEquity co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Interbrand / Landor / Siegel+Gale / McKinsey / BCG
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must BrandExtension; the worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by a new brand; if solvable do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler BrandExtension is, the better; cut redundant layers
