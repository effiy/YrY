## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: model + selection + development + measurement + iteration; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by per-capita revenue + retention + key role supply + decision speed + strategy implementation; no empty slogans
3. **Model**: values + behavior + capability + boundary + counter-examples; no leakage
4. **Selection**: high-potential + assessment + 360 + calibration + transparency; no leakage
5. **Development**: coaching + experience + role rotation + executive education + feedback; no leakage
6. **Measurement**: 360 + eNPS + retention + business results + succession rate; no leakage
7. **Iterate**: feedback + update + version + retire + upgrade; no leakage
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from model → selection → development → measurement → iteration gradual; no skipping levels
9. **No report-ism**: report is just the start; not the end
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must mark implementation evidence; no vagueness
11. **Versioned**: strategy is versioned; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with hr**: leadership + HR co-built
13. **Link with talent**: leadership + talent co-built
14. **Link with culture**: leadership + culture co-built
15. **Link with learning-and-development**: leadership + L&D co-built
16. **Link with succession-planning**: leadership + succession co-built
17. **Toolchain**: DDI / Korn Ferry / BTS / Linkage / NLI / Center for Creative Leadership / Lominger / Hogan
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy is publicly discoverable; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must leadership strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion**: how much can be solved by natural evolution; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequence after strategy (cost / risk / retention / business)
23. **Occam's razor**: simpler leadership is better; cut redundant steps
