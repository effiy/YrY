## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identify + measurement + improvement + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by speed + cost + quality + risk + trust; not sloganeering
3. **Identify**: value stream / bottlenecks / waste / variation / processes; do not omit
4. **Measure**: CT / cadence / first-pass yield / capacity / takt; do not omit
5. **Improve**: DMAIC / DMADV / PDCA / Kaizen / 5S; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: speed + cost + quality + risk + trust; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from identify → measurement → improvement → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with operating-model**: Process + Operating co-build
13. **Link with okr**: Process + OKR co-build
14. **Link with capability-model**: Process + Capability co-build
15. **Link with kpi**: Process + KPI co-build
16. **Link with change-management**: Process + Change co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Minitab / Lucidchart / Visio / SigmaXL / JMP
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why Process Excellence is a must; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can experience solve; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (speed / cost / quality / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler Process Excellence is the better; cut redundant methods
