## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: representation + agreement + collaboration + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Representation Representation**: certification / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Agreement Agreement**: negotiation / compliance / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Collaborate Collaboration**: day-to-day / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from representation → agreement → collaboration → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with employee-relations**: LaborRelations + EmployeeRelations co-built
13. **Link with performance-management**: LaborRelations + PerformanceManagement co-built
14. **Link with hr-operations**: LaborRelations + HROperations co-built
15. **Link with culture**: LaborRelations + Culture co-built
16. **Link with employee-engagement**: LaborRelations + EmployeeEngagement co-built
17. **Tooling**: Labor Relations Suite / UnionTrack / PractiStaff / Cogniview / LaborSmart
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why LaborRelations is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by crisis response; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: LaborRelations the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
