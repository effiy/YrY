## Action recommendations

1. **five dimensions**: benchmark + practice + recognition + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritise by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **benchmark Benchmark**: external / internal / peer / historical; do not omit
4. **practice Practice**: identify + landing + retrospective + replicate; do not omit
5. **recognition Recognition**: selection + recognition + incentive; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from benchmark → practice → recognition → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: honours are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with hr-transformation**: excellence + transformation co-built
13. **link with hr-operating-model**: excellence + operations co-built
14. **link with hr-capability**: excellence + capability co-built
15. **link with hr-competency**: excellence + competency co-built
16. **link with hr-metrics**: excellence + metrics co-built
17. **toolchain**: Workday / SAP SuccessFactors / HRO Today / Josh Bersin / McKinsey
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must HR excellence strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: HR excellence the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
