## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + content + cadence + tooling + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by margin + revenue + speed + win-rate + risk; not sloganeering
3. **Audience**: standard / non-standard / strategic / renewal / partner; do not omit
4. **Content**: discount + terms + risk + resources + time limit; do not omit
5. **Cadence**: daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly + upgrade; do not omit
6. **Tooling**: CPQ / CRM / CLM / e-sign / approval; do not omit
7. **Measure**: margin + revenue + speed + win-rate + risk; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from audience -> content -> cadence -> tooling -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with CPQ**: Deal desk + CPQ co-build
13. **Link with mutual-close-plan**: Deal desk + mutual close co-build
14. **Link with deal-review**: Deal desk + retrospective co-build
15. **Link with sales-ops**: Deal desk + operations co-build
16. **Link with revenue-ops**: Deal desk + RevOps co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce-CPQ / Clari / Gong / Salesforce-CRM-Analytics / -kdocspring / DocuSign CLM
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must deal desk strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can sales self-governance solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / margin / business)
23. **Occam**: Deal desk the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
