## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Access + observation + interview + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: Prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Access Access**: recruitment / trust / consent / role; do not omit
4. **Observe Observe**: work / decision / tools / interruption; do not omit
5. **Interview Interview**: main axis / interpretation / assumption / summary; do not omit
6. **Governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: Progress from access → observation → interview → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: questioning is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with ethnographic-research**: contextual + ethnographic co-built
13. **Link with diary-study**: contextual + diary co-built
14. **Link with customer-interview**: contextual + interview co-built
15. **Link with user-research**: contextual + user co-built
16. **Link with design-research**: contextual + design co-built
17. **Toolchain**: UserTesting / Lookback / Dovetail / Otter.ai / Reframer
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must the contextual-inquiry strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by defaults; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: contextual inquiry — the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
