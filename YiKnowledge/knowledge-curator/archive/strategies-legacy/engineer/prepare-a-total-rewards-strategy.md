## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: direct + indirect + non-monetary + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by talent + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Direct Direct**: base / variable / equity / allowance / incentive; do not omit
4. **Indirect Indirect**: insurance / leave / retirement / benefits / family; do not omit
5. **Non-monetary Non-monetary**: development / recognition / work / culture / experience; do not omit
6. **Governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement Measure**: talent + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from direct → indirect → non-monetary → governance → measurement progressive; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with compensation**: TR + Comp co-build
13. **Link with benefits**: TR + Benefits co-build
14. **Link with performance-management**: TR + Performance co-build
15. **Link with recognition**: TR + Recognition co-build
16. **Link with chro**: TR + CHRO co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Mercer / Aon / Radford / Salary.com / Pave
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must TR; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by raises; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (talent / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: TR the simpler the better; cut redundant components
