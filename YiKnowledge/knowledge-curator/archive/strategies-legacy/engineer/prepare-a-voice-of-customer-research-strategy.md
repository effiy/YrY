## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: source + cleansing + theme + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **source Source**: survey / interview / review / customer service; do not omit
4. **cleansing Clean**: dedup / tag / sentiment / denoise; do not omit
5. **theme Theme**: open / axis / prioritize / landing; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from source → cleansing → theme → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: listening is only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with customer-feedback**: VoC + feedback co-build
13. **link with customer-research**: VoC + customer co-build
14. **link with qualitative-research**: VoC + qualitative co-build
15. **link with customer-interview**: VoC + interview co-build
16. **link with customer-survey**: VoC + survey co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Medallia / Qualtrics / InMoment / ChurnZero / Dovetail
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must VoC research strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can defaults solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: VoC research the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
