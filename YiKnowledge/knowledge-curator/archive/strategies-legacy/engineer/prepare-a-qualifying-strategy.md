## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: criteria + signal + stage + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by conversion + speed + revenue + win rate + experience; not sloganeering
3. **Criteria**: BANT / MEDDPICC / ICP / role / pain; do not omit
4. **Signal**: behavior / intent / data / event / content; do not omit
5. **Stage**: MQL / SQL / SAL / conversion / exit; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: conversion + speed + revenue + win rate + experience; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from criteria → signal → stage → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with lead-scoring**: Qualifying + Scoring co-build
13. **Link with discovery-call**: Qualifying + Discovery co-build
14. **Link with meddpicc**: Qualifying + MEDDPICC co-build
15. **Link with sales-process**: Qualifying + Sales Process co-build
16. **Link with icp**: Qualifying + ICP co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce / Marketo / 6sense / Outreach / Gong
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must Qualifying; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can sales self-filtering solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / revenue / experience)
23. **Occam**: Qualifying the simpler the better; cut redundant criteria
