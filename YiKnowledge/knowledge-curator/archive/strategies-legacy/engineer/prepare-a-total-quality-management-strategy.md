## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: culture + system + method + governance + measurement; none missing
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Culture**: customer / kaizen / all-staff / leadership; do not omit
4. **System**: ISO / system / process / records; do not omit
5. **Method**: PDCA / 7 tools / 5-Why / fishbone; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from culture → system → method → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: tools are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with quality**: total + quality co-built
13. **Link with quality-management**: total + system co-built
14. **Link with continuous-improvement**: total + continuous improvement co-built
15. **Link with six-sigma**: total + Six Sigma co-built
16. **Link with operational-excellence**: total + operational excellence co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Minitab / JMP / InfinityQS / KaiNexus / iObeya
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a total-quality-management strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, don't introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: total quality management the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
