## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: stages + emotion + painpoints + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **stages Stages**: awareness / consideration / purchase / closed-loop; do not omit
4. **emotion Emotion**: curve / highs-lows / closed-loop; do not omit
5. **painpoints Painpoint**: friction / opportunity / closed-loop; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from stages → emotion → painpoints → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with customer-experience**: CustomerJourneyMapping + CustomerExperience co-build
13. **link with customer-persona**: CustomerJourneyMapping + CustomerPersona co-build
14. **link with voice-of-customer**: CustomerJourneyMapping + VoiceOfCustomer co-build
15. **link with touchpoint**: CustomerJourneyMapping + Touchpoint co-build
16. **link with loyalty**: CustomerJourneyMapping + Loyalty co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Miro / Lucidchart / UXpressia / Custellence / Smaply
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must CustomerJourneyMapping; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can scattered interviews solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: CustomerJourneyMapping the simpler the better; cut redundant stages
