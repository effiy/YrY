## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: booth + activities + lead acquisition + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Booth**: location / design / materials / experience; do not omit
4. **Activities**: talks / demos / raffles / interactive; do not omit
5. **Leads**: scan / form / rating / follow-up; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from booth → activities → leads → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: booth setup is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with event-management**: trade show + event management co-built
13. **Link with conference**: trade show + conference co-built
14. **Link with webinar**: trade show + webinar co-built
15. **Link with event-marketing**: trade show + marketing co-built
16. **Link with content**: trade show + content co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Cvent / Bizzabo / A2Z Events / Eventbrite / Swapcard
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must trade-show strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can default solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: trade show the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
