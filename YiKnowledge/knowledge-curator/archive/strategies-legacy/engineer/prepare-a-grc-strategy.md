## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: governance + risk + compliance + integration + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; no empty slogans
3. **Governance**: direction / decision / closed-loop; no leakage
4. **Risk**: identify / assess / closed-loop; no leakage
5. **Compliance**: policy / execution / closed-loop; no leakage
6. **Governance oversight**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; no leakage
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; no leakage
8. **Not one-shot**: gradual from governance → risk → compliance → governance oversight → measurement; no skipping levels
9. **No report-ism**: report is just the start; not the end
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must mark implementation evidence; no vagueness
11. **Versioned**: strategy is versioned; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with compliance-management**: GRC + Compliance Management co-build
13. **Link with risk**: GRC + Risk co-build
14. **Link with audit-compliance**: GRC + Audit Compliance co-build
15. **Link with internal-controls**: GRC + Internal Controls co-build
16. **Link with policy-management**: GRC + Policy Management co-build
17. **Toolchain**: ServiceNow GRC / RSA Archer / MetricStream / IBM OpenPages / LogicGate
18. **Publicly discoverable**: strategy is publicly discoverable; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why GRC is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion**: how much can each-team-its-own solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequence after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam's razor**: simpler GRC is better; cut redundant modules
