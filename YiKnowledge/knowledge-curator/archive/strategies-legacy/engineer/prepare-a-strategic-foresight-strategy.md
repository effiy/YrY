## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: scan + vision + option selection + Governance + Measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; no empty slogans
3. **Scan**: STEEP / weak signals / inflection points / disruption; no leakage
4. **Vision**: baseline / optimistic / pessimistic / disruption; no leakage
5. **Option selection**: strategy / drill / milestone / exit; no leakage
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; no leakage
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; no leakage
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from scan -> vision -> option selection -> Governance -> Measurement; no skipping levels
9. **No report-ism**: planning is only the start; not the end
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must mark implementation evidence; no vagueness
11. **Versioned**: strategy is versioned; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with futures-research**: foresight + futures co-build
13. **Link with trend-research**: foresight + trend co-build
14. **Link with innovation-research**: foresight + innovation co-build
15. **Link with corporate-strategy**: foresight + company strategy co-build
16. **Link with business-strategy**: foresight + business strategy co-build
17. **Toolchain**: IftF / Sohail Inayatullah / Scenario.io / Futures Toolkit / Hawkewood
18. **Publicly discoverable**: strategy is publicly discoverable; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why strategic foresight strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion**: how much can be solved by relying on defaults; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam's razor**: the simpler strategic foresight is the better; cut redundant layers
