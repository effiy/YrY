## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: scan + assess + incubate + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **scan Scan**: technology / business / customer / cross-industry; do not omit
4. **assess Assess**: feasibility / value / strategic-fit / timing; do not omit
5. **incubate Incubate**: prototype / pilot / ship / exit; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from scan -> assess -> incubate -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: ideas are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **linked with trend-research**: innovation + trends co-built
13. **linked with futures-research**: innovation + futures co-built
14. **linked with innovation**: research + strategy co-built
15. **linked with market-intelligence**: innovation + intelligence co-built
16. **linked with competitive-research**: innovation + competitors co-built
17. **Toolchain**: CB Insights / PitchBook / Innoget / IdeaScale / Hype
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why innovation research strategy is required; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: innovation research the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
