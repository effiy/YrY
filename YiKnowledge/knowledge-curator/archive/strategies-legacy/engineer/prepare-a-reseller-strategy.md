## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: recruit + enable + compensation + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by channel revenue + registration + conversion + retention + speed; not sloganeering
3. **recruit Recruit**: persona / standard / onboarding / audit / pilot; none missing
4. **enable Enable**: training / certification / assets / demo / support; none missing
5. **compensation Compensate**: discount / rebate / SPIF / MDF / tier; none missing
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / docs / drift; none missing
7. **Measure**: channel revenue + registration + conversion + retention + speed; none missing
8. **Not one-shot**: from recruit → enable → compensation → governance → measurement progressively; no skipping levels
9. **Not report-only**: reports are only the starting point; not the endpoint
10. **No sloganeering**: every principle must have landed evidence; no ambiguity
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with channel-partner**: Reseller + Channel Partner co-build
13. **link with partner-program**: Reseller + Partner Program co-build
14. **link with partner-compensation**: Reseller + Compensation co-build
15. **link with partner-success**: Reseller + Partner Success co-build
16. **link with partner-marketing**: Reseller + Partner Marketing co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Salesforce PRM / Crossbeam / Allbound / Impartner / Channelize
18. **Publicly accessible**: strategy accessible to everyone; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why Reseller must exist; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion**: how much can direct sales alone solve; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / channel conflict / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: Reseller the simpler the better; cut redundant tiers
