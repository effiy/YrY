## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + content + cadence + tooling + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by ramp + revenue + quota attainment + retention + NPS; not sloganeering
3. **Audience**: new-hire / ramp / on-the-job / continuous / alumni; do not omit
4. **Content**: playbook / case / demo / battle card / talk track; do not omit
5. **Cadence**: daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly / annual; do not omit
6. **Tooling**: LMS / CMS / coaching / call-coaching / AI sparring; do not omit
7. **Measure**: ramp + revenue + quota attainment + retention + NPS; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from audience → content → cadence → tooling → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with sales-enablement**: field enablement + sales enablement co-built
13. **Link with field-marketing**: field enablement + field marketing co-built
14. **Link with sales-playbook**: field enablement + sales playbook co-built
15. **Link with sales-coaching**: field enablement + sales coaching co-built
16. **Link with partner-enablement**: field enablement + partner enablement co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Highspot / Seismic / Showpad / Mindtickle / Second-Nature / Gong-Enablement / Lessonly
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must field enablement; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by one-time training; if solvable, don't introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: field enablement the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
