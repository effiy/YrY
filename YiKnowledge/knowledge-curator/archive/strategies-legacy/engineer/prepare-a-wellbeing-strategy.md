## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: physical + mental + financial + social + governance; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by talent + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Physical**: checkup / exercise / sleep / nutrition / disease; do not omit
4. **Mental**: EAP / counseling / mindfulness / stress relief / resilience; do not omit
5. **Financial**: retirement / savings / education / planning / emergency; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: talent + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from physical → mental → financial → social → governance progressively; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with benefits**: WB + Benefits co-built
13. **Link with employee-experience**: WB + EX co-built
14. **Link with culture**: WB + Culture co-built
15. **Link with employee-engagement**: WB + Engagement co-built
16. **Link with chro**: WB + CHRO co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Virgin Pulse / Limeade / Wellable / Castlight / Headspace for Work
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must WB; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by insurance; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (talent / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: WB the simpler the better; cut redundant items
