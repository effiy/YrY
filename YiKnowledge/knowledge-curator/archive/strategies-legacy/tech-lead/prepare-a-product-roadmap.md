## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: vision + topic + cadence + priority + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Vision driven**: vision + strategy + topic; not gut call
3. **Topic**: every quarterly topic; no ambiguity
4. **Cadence**: Now / Next / Later; not tied to dates
5. **Priority**: RICE / ICE / value vs effort; not by feel
6. **Measurement**: every topic must tag a success metric; no ambiguity
7. **Continuous**: not one-shot; regular review
8. **Adjustable**: architecture evolve and update; not one-shot
9. **Not tied to dates**: Now / Next / Later; not dates
10. **Not feature dump**: topic + vision; not just column feature
11. **Not promise**: route graph is not a promise; no ambiguity
12. **Communicate**: internal + external + customer; not hidden
13. **Link with tech route graph**: product route graph + tech route graph co-build
14. **Link with backlog**: route graph + backlog co-build
15. **Link with quarterly retrospective**: route graph goes through quarterly review
16. **Link with PRD**: every topic has a PRD
17. **Link with GTM**: route graph + GTM co-build
18. **Link with pitch**: route graph + pitch co-build
19. **Toolchain**: Productboard / Aha / Notion / in-house
20. **Publicly accessible**: route graph accessible to everyone; not hidden
21. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot
22. **First principles**: why must route graph; worst consequence of not doing it
23. **Inversion**: how much can be solved with backlog; if solvable then do not introduce route graph
24. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after route graph (coherence / trust / hiring / expansion)
25. **Occam**: the simpler the route graph the better; cut redundant steps
