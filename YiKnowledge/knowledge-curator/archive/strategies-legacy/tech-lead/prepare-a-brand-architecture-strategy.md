## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: structure + relationship + naming + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by adoption + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Structure**: master / sub / endorsed / individual / matrix; do not omit
4. **Relationship**: clear / independent / mutually supporting / isolated / evolving; do not omit
5. **Naming**: rules / vocabulary / trademarks / domains / drift; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: adoption + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from structure → relationship → naming → governance → measurement progressively; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with brand-identity**: BA + Brand co-build
13. **link with corporate-communications**: BA + CorpComms co-build
14. **link with marketing-operations**: BA + MOps co-build
15. **link with product-marketing**: BA + PMM co-build
16. **link with corporate-governance**: BA + Governance co-build
17. **toolchain**: Brandwork / Webdam / Frontify / Bynder / Brandfolder
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why BA must exist; the worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can identity alone solve; if solvable don't introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (adoption / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: BA the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
