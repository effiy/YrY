## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: levels + sequences + bands + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by fairness + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Levels**: L1-L9 / scope / impact / autonomy / complexity; do not omit
4. **Family**: engineering / product / design / sales / function; do not omit
5. **Bands**: compensation / scope / median / overlap / adjustment; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: fairness + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from levels → sequences → bands → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with competency-model**: JA + Competency co-built
13. **Link with compensation**: JA + Comp co-built
14. **Link with career-development**: JA + Career co-built
15. **Link with workforce-planning**: JA + Workforce co-built
16. **Link with chro**: JA + CHRO co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Mercer / Korn Ferry / Radford / Salary.com / Pave
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must JA; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can JDs solve; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (fairness / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: JA the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
