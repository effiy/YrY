## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: capability + process + value + organization + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Capability**: identify / classify / map / evolve; do not omit
4. **Process**: end-to-end / subprocess / role / improvement; do not omit
5. **Value**: value stream / stages / customer / output; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from capability → process → value → organization → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: drawing diagrams is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with business-capability**: business architecture + business capability co-built
13. **Link with business-process**: business architecture + business process co-built
14. **Link with value-stream**: business architecture + value stream co-built
15. **Link with enterprise-architecture**: business architecture + enterprise architecture co-built
16. **Link with operating-model**: business architecture + operating model co-built
17. **Toolchain**: BiZZdesign / Sparx EA / ARIS / ArchiMate / Signavio
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must business architecture strategy; the worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler business architecture is, the better; cut redundant layers
