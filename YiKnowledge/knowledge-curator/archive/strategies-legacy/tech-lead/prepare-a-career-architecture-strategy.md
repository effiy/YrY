## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: family + stream + equipment + governance + measure; no missing dimensions
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not empty slogans
3. **Family**: engineering / product / design / business; do not omit
4. **Stream**: individual contributor / management / dual-track; do not omit
5. **Equip**: capability / level / path / resources; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from family → stream → equipment → governance → measure progressively; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: family count is only the starting point; not the end
10. **Not empty slogans**: each principle must mark landing evidence; no ambiguity
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with career-framework**: career architecture + career framework co-build
13. **Link with career-pathing**: career architecture + career pathing co-build
14. **Link with career-development**: career architecture + career development co-build
15. **Link with job-architecture**: career architecture + job architecture co-build
16. **Link with job-leveling**: career architecture + job leveling co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Fuel50 / Eightfold AI / Workday Career Hub / Mercer / Korn Ferry
18. **Publicly accessible**: strategy is accessible to all; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolves and updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a career architecture strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: career architecture simpler is better; cut redundant layers
