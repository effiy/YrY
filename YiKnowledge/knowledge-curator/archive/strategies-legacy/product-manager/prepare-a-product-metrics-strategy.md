## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: North Star + metrics + closed loop + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **North Star**: direction / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Metrics**: layered / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Closed loop**: monitoring / decision / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from North Star → metrics → closed loop → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with product-management**: ProductMetrics + ProductManagement co-built
13. **Link with product-analytics**: ProductMetrics + ProductAnalytics co-built
14. **Link with product-roadmap**: ProductMetrics + ProductRoadmap co-built
15. **Link with growth**: ProductMetrics + Growth co-built
16. **Link with product**: ProductMetrics + ProductStrategy co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Amplitude / Mixpanel / Heap / Pendo / Productboard
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must ProductMetrics; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can instinct solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: ProductMetrics the simpler the better; cut redundant metrics
