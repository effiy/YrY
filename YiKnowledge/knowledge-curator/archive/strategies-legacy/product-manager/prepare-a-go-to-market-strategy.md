## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + value + channel + cadence + measure; no missing dimensions
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by revenue + speed + penetration + retention + experience; not empty slogans
3. **Audience**: ICP / role / segment / channel / region; do not omit
4. **Value**: pain / solution / difference / evidence / ROI; do not omit
5. **Channel**: direct / channel / self-serve / inbound / outbound; do not omit
6. **Cadence**: stage / time / milestones / SLA / adjustment; do not omit
7. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
8. **Measure**: revenue + speed + penetration + retention + experience; do not omit
9. **Not one-shot**: from audience → value → channel → cadence → measure progressively; no skipping
10. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the starting point; not the end
11. **Not empty slogans**: each principle must mark landing evidence; no ambiguity
12. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
13. **Link with positioning**: GTM + Positioning co-build
14. **Link with pricing**: GTM + Pricing co-build
15. **Link with sales-enablement**: GTM + Enablement co-build
16. **Link with product-launch**: GTM + Product Launch co-build
17. **Link with business-model**: GTM + Business Model co-build
18. **Toolchain**: Snowflake / Salesforce / Marketo / Clari / ProductBoard
19. **Publicly accessible**: strategy is accessible to all; not hidden
20. **Regular review**: evolves and updates; not one-shot
21. **First principles**: why GTM is necessary; worst consequence of not doing
22. **Inversion**: how much can be solved by organic product growth; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
23. **Occam**: GTM simpler is better; cut redundant moves
