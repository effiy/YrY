## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: Trigger + path + closed-loop + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Trigger**: events / moments / channels; do not omit
4. **Path**: segments / journey / intervention; do not omit
5. **Closed-loop Optimize**: experiment / winning / push; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from trigger → path → closed-loop → governance → measurement gradual; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with product-usage**: ProductEngagement + ProductUsage co-built
13. **Link with product-adoption**: ProductEngagement + ProductAdoption co-built
14. **Link with customer-engagement**: ProductEngagement + CustomerEngagement co-built
15. **Link with product-metrics**: ProductEngagement + ProductMetrics co-built
16. **Link with product-success**: ProductEngagement + ProductSuccess co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Amplitude / Mixpanel / Pendo / Appcues / Braze
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must ProductEngagement; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by push; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
