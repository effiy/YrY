## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: ideation + evaluation + incubation + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Ideate**: sources / triggers / conversion; do not omit
4. **Evaluate**: feasibility / value / risk; do not omit
5. **Incubate**: prototype / pilot / promote; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from ideation → evaluation → incubation → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with innovation**: ProductInnovation + Innovation co-built
13. **Link with research-and-development**: ProductInnovation + R&D co-built
14. **Link with product-discovery**: ProductInnovation + ProductDiscovery co-built
15. **Link with product-strategy**: ProductInnovation + ProductStrategy co-built
16. **Link with business-model**: ProductInnovation + BusinessModel co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Hype / Qmarkets / Brightidea / IdeaScale / Spigit
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must ProductInnovation exist; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by brainstorming; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: ProductInnovation the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
