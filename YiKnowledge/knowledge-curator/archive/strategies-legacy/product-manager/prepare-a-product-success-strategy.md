## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: goal + experience + loop + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **goal Outcome**: value / outcomes / north star; do not omit
4. **experience Experience**: onboarding / habit / depth; do not omit
5. **loop Optimize**: intervention / path / retrospective; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: from goal → experience → loop → governance → measurement progressive; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with customer-success**: ProductSuccess + CustomerSuccess co-build
13. **link with product-management**: ProductSuccess + ProductManagement co-build
14. **link with product-health**: ProductSuccess + ProductHealth co-build
15. **link with customer-experience**: ProductSuccess + CustomerExperience co-build
16. **link with product-metrics**: ProductSuccess + ProductMetrics co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Pendo / Gainsight PX / Amplitude / Mixpanel / Productboard
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must ProductSuccess; worst consequence of not doing
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can usage volume alone solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: ProductSuccess the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
