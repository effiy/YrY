## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: vision + roadmap + loop + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **vision Vision**: direction / loop; do not omit
4. **roadmap Roadmap**: cadence / loop; do not omit
5. **loop Loop**: discovery / delivery / loop; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from vision -> roadmap -> loop -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **links with product**: ProductManagement + ProductStrategy co-build
13. **links with product-roadmap**: ProductManagement + ProductRoadmap co-build
14. **links with product-metrics**: ProductManagement + ProductMetrics co-build
15. **links with product-discovery**: ProductManagement + ProductDiscovery co-build
16. **links with product-design**: ProductManagement + ProductDesign co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Productboard / Aha! / Pivotal Tracker / Jira Product Discovery / airfocus
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must ProductManagement; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can requirements documentation solve; if solvable, don't introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: ProductManagement the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
