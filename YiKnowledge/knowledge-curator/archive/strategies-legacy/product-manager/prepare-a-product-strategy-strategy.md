## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: vision + roadmap + closed-loop + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Vision**: mission / differentiation / closed-loop; do not omit
4. **Roadmap**: theme / cadence / closed-loop; do not omit
5. **Closed-loop Optimize**: prioritization / resources / closed-loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from vision → roadmap → closed-loop → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-only**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with product-vision**: ProductStrategy + Vision co-build
13. **Link with product-roadmap**: ProductStrategy + Roadmap co-build
14. **Link with product-management**: ProductStrategy + Management co-build
15. **Link with product-portfolio**: ProductStrategy + Portfolio co-build
16. **Link with product-strategy-management**: ProductStrategy + StrategyManagement co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Productboard / Aha! / airfocus / Jira Product Discovery / Pivotal Tracker
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why ProductStrategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by roadmap alone; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: the simpler ProductStrategy is, the better; cut redundant layers
