## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: experiment + path + closed loop + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Experiment**: assumption / test / closed loop; do not omit
4. **Path**: AARRR / north star / closed loop; do not omit
5. **Closed loop Optimize**: winners / push / closed loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from experiment -> path -> closed loop -> governance -> measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with growth-operations**: ProductGrowth + GrowthOps co-built
13. **Link with growth-loop**: ProductGrowth + GrowthLoop co-built
14. **Link with product-management**: ProductGrowth + ProductManagement co-built
15. **Link with product-metrics**: ProductGrowth + ProductMetrics co-built
16. **Link with product-lifecycle**: ProductGrowth + ProductLifecycle co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Amplitude / Mixpanel / Optimizely / GrowthBook / PostHog
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must ProductGrowth; worst consequence of not doing it
