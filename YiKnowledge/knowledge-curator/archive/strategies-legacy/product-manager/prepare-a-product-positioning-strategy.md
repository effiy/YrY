## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: market + differentiation + value + mind + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by win-rate + conversion + revenue + retention + NPS; no empty slogans
3. **Market**: category + segment + timing + trends + counter-examples; no omissions
4. **Differentiation**: unique + defensible + evidence + story + counter-examples; no omissions
5. **Value**: functional + emotional + social + financial + time; no omissions
6. **Mind**: category words + associations + first + counter-examples + placeholder; no omissions
7. **Measurement**: win-rate + conversion + revenue + retention + NPS; no omissions
8. **Not one-shot**: progress gradually from market → differentiation → value → mind → measurement; no skipping levels
9. **No report-ism**: reports are just the start; not the end
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must have implementation evidence; no vagueness
11. **Versioned**: strategy is versioned; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with positioning**: product + brand positioning co-built
13. **Link with messaging-house**: product + messaging house co-built
14. **Link with value-proposition**: product + value proposition co-built
15. **Link with ideal-customer-profile**: product + ICP co-built
16. **Link with product-strategy**: product + strategy co-built
17. **Toolchain**: April Dunford Positioning Canvas / Miro / Mural / Product Marketing Alliance / Klue / Crayon
18. **Publicly discoverable**: strategy is publicly discoverable; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a product positioning strategy is necessary; the worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion**: how much can be solved with functional descriptions alone; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / mind / business)
23. **Occam's razor**: the simpler product positioning is, the better; cut redundant steps
