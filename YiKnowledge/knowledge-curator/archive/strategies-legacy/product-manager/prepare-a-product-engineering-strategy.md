## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: requirements + architecture + delivery + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Requirements**: user / scenario / value / validation; do not omit
4. **Architecture**: module / boundary / interface / evolution; do not omit
5. **Delivery**: cadence / quality / experience / data; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from requirements → architecture → delivery → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: delivery is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with engineering**: product engineering + engineering co-built
13. **Link with product**: product engineering + product co-built
14. **Link with product-delivery**: product engineering + delivery co-built
15. **Link with platform-engineering**: product engineering + platform engineering co-built
16. **Link with operational-excellence**: product engineering + operational excellence co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Jira / Linear / GitHub / Figma / Amplitude
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must product-engineering strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: product engineering the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
