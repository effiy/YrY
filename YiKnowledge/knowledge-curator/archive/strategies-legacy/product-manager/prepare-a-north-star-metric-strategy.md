## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: core + decomposition + alignment + measurement + retrospective; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by business growth + user value + retention + LTV; not sloganeering
3. **Core**: north-star metric single + reflects user value + long-term business + decomposable; do not omit
4. **Decomposition**: input metrics / leading metrics / lagging metrics + factor tree; do not omit
5. **Alignment**: company / department / team / individual + top-down alignment + horizontal collaboration; do not omit
6. **Measurement**: real-time + dashboard + weekly + quarterly + trend + exception; do not omit
7. **Retrospective**: periodic + lessons + adjustment + retrospective + next-cadence improvement; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from core → decomposition → alignment → measurement → retrospective; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with okr**: north star + OKR co-build
13. **Link with kpi**: north star + KPI co-build
14. **Link with product-strategy**: north star + product strategy co-build
15. **Link with product-analytics**: north star + measurement co-build
16. **Link with pirate-funnel**: north star + funnel co-build
17. **Link with heart-aarrr**: north star + pirate co-build
18. **Toolchain**: North-Star-Metric Framework / Amplitude / Mixpanel / Heap / Looker / Tableau / Metabase / Google Analytics
19. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
20. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
21. **First principles**: why north star is a must; the worst consequence of not doing it
22. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved with KPI; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
23. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / growth / business)
24. **Occam**: the simpler the north star the better; cut redundant steps
