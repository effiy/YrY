## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: question + hypothesis + method + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value-driven**: prioritize by insight + decision + speed + conversion + retention; no empty slogans
3. **Question**: user / market / product / technology / competition; nothing missed
4. **Hypothesis**: problem / solution / value / risk / validation; nothing missed
5. **Method**: interview / survey / experiment / prototype / data; nothing missed
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; nothing missed
7. **Measurement**: insight + decision + speed + conversion + retention; nothing missed
8. **Not a one-shot exercise**: progress gradually from question → hypothesis → method → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not just reports**: reports are only the starting point; not the end
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must mark the implementation evidence; no vagueness
11. **Versioning**: the strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with user-research**: Product Research + User Research built together
13. **Link with market-research**: Product Research + Market Research built together
14. **Link with product-strategy**: Product Research + Product Strategy built together
15. **Link with customer-feedback**: Product Research + Feedback built together
16. **Link with voc**: Product Research + VoC built together
17. **Toolchain**: UserTesting / Maze / Sprig / Pendo / Amplitude
18. **Publicly accessible**: everyone can look up the strategy; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not a one-shot exercise
20. **First principles**: why Product Research is necessary; the worst outcome of not doing it
21. **Inversion**: how much can intuition solve; if it can be solved, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / decision / retention)
23. **Occam**: Product Research should be as simple as possible; cut redundant steps
