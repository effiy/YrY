## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: audience + content + channel + cadence + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by adoption + revenue + retention + NPS + SOV; no empty slogans
3. **audience Audience**: customer + partner + analyst + media + internal staff; none missing
4. **content Content**: narrative / demo / docs / video / case study / blog; none missing
5. **channel Channel**: product + website + email / social / media / event / sales; none missing
6. **cadence Cadence**: pre-launch + launch + post-launch + iterate; none missing
7. **measure Measurement**: adoption + revenue + retention + NPS + SOV; none missing
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from audience → content → channel → cadence → measurement; no skipping levels
9. **Not report-only**: reports are only the starting point; not the endpoint
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must have landed evidence; no ambiguity
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link to product-launch-checklist**: launch + checklist co-build
13. **Link to go-to-market**: launch + GTM co-build
14. **Link to beta-program**: launch + beta co-build
15. **Link to positioning**: launch + positioning co-build
16. **Link to campaign-planning**: launch + campaign co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Productboard / Aha! / Aha-Ideas / Loom / Notion / Asana / Monday / Wrike / Confluence
18. **Publicly accessible**: strategy accessible to everyone; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must we have a product launch strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion**: how much can be solved by relying on the product's natural launch; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / adoption / business) 
23. **Occam**: the simpler the product launch the better; cut redundant steps
