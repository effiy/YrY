## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: goal + path + intervention + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **goal Outcome**: reach / activate / habit; do not omit
4. **path Path**: awareness / trial / repeat / habit; do not omit
5. **intervention Intervene**: guide / incentivize / remind; do not omit
6. **governance Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **measurement Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from goal → path → intervention → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **link with product-activation**: ProductAdoption + ProductActivation co-built
13. **link with product-usage**: ProductAdoption + ProductUsage co-built
14. **link with product-success**: ProductAdoption + ProductSuccess co-built
15. **link with digital-adoption-platform**: ProductAdoption + DAP co-built
16. **link with product-onboarding**: ProductAdoption + ProductOnboarding co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Pendo / Appcues / Whatfix / WalkMe / Userflow
18. **publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must ProductAdoption; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by pushing alone; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: ProductAdoption the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
