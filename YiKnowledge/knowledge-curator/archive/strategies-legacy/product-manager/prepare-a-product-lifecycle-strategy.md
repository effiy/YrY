## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: introduction + growth + maturity + decline + governance; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by adoption + value + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Introduction**: discovery / validation / MVP / PMF / GTM; do not omit
4. **Growth**: scaling / channels / growth / retention / extension; do not omit
5. **Maturity**: optimize / maintenance / profit / defense / innovation; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: adoption + value + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from introduction → growth → maturity → decline → governance; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with product-strategy**: PLM + Strategy co-built
13. **Link with product-management**: PLM + Mgmt co-built
14. **Link with product-operations**: PLM + Ops co-built
15. **Link with product-launch**: PLM + Launch co-built
16. **Link with product-discovery**: PLM + Discovery co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Productboard / Aha! / Jira Product Discovery / craft.io / ProductPlan
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must PLM; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by relying on launch; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (adoption / value / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: the simpler PLM the better; cut redundant stages
