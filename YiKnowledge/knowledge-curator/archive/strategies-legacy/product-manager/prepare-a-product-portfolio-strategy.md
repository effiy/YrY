## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: architecture + role + closed loop + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Architecture**: layers / relationships / boundaries; do not omit
4. **Role**: core / adjacent / new / harvest / retire; do not omit
5. **Closed loop Optimize**: investment + evaluation + adjustment; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from architecture → role → closed loop → governance → measurement progressively; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with brand-portfolio**: ProductPortfolio + BrandPortfolio co-built
13. **Link with product-strategy**: ProductPortfolio + ProductStrategy co-built
14. **Link with product-management**: ProductPortfolio + ProductManagement co-built
15. **Link with product-innovation**: ProductPortfolio + ProductInnovation co-built
16. **Link with product-roadmap**: ProductPortfolio + ProductRoadmap co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Productboard / Aha! / airfocus / Jira / Planview
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must ProductPortfolio; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by a product list; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: ProductPortfolio the simpler the better; cut redundant layers
