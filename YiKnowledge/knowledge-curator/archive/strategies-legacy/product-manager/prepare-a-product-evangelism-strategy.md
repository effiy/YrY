## Action recommendations

1. **five dimensions**: audience + content + channel + cadence + Measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: with evangelism contribution to revenue + retention + NPS + growth + brand set priority; no empty slogans
3. **audience Audience**: founder / exec / dev-advocate / pm / customer; no leakage
4. **content Content**: story / talk / Case study / demo / content; no leakage
5. **channel Channel**: meeting / podcast / blog / community / media; no leakage
6. **cadence Cadence**: daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly / milestone; no leakage
7. **Measurement Measure**: evangelism contribution to revenue + retention + NPS + growth + brand; no leakage
8. **Not one-shot**: from audience → content → channel → cadence → Measurement gradual; no skipping levels
9. **no report-ism**: report is just the start; not the end
10. **no empty slogans**: every principle must mark implementation evidence; no vagueness
11. **Versioned**: strategy is versioned; evolution is traceable
12. **and developer-advocacy Link**: product evangelism + developer advocacy co-build
13. **and customer-advocacy-program Link**: product evangelism + customer advocacy co-build
14. **and content-marketing Link**: product evangelism + content co-build
15. **and brand-identity Link**: product evangelism + brand co-build
16. **and product-marketing Link**: product evangelism + product marketing co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Notion / WordPress / YouTube / LinkedIn / X / Substack / Beehiiv / Medium
18. **Publicly discoverable**: strategy is publicly discoverable; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: Evolve and update; Not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must product evangelism; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion**: how much can be solved by relying on ads; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequence after strategy (cost / Risk / revenue / brand)
23. **Occam's razor**: product evangelism simpler is better; cut redundant steps
