## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: experience + activation + conversion + expansion + retention; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by business growth + LTV + CAC + time-to-value; not sloganeering
3. **Experience**: first experience + time-to-value + aha-moment + frictionless + in-product-onboarding; do not omit
4. **Activation**: activation events + PQL + trial-to-paid + conversion funnel + friction-log; do not omit
5. **Conversion**: product-qualified-lead + self-serve + upgrade triggers + price presentation + payment friction; do not omit
6. **Expansion**: cross-sell + account expansion + viral loop + network + ecosystem; do not omit
7. **Retention**: core loop + habit + notifications + education + churn warning; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress gradually from experience -> activation -> conversion -> expansion -> retention; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with growth-strategy**: PLG + growth co-built
13. **Link with growth-experiment**: PLG + experiment co-built
14. **Link with ab-testing**: PLG + A/B co-built
15. **Link with product-strategy**: PLG + strategy co-built
16. **Link with pricing**: PLG + pricing co-built
17. **Link with customer-success**: PLG + customer success co-built
18. **Toolchain**: PLG-Framework / Pendo / Appcues / Userflow / Whatfix / Vitally / Catalyst / ChurnZero / Calixa
19. **Publicly queryable**: everyone can look up the strategy; not hidden
20. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
21. **First principles**: why must PLG; worst consequence of not doing it
22. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by sales-driven alone; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
23. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / growth / business)
24. **Occam**: the simpler the PLG the better; cut redundant steps
