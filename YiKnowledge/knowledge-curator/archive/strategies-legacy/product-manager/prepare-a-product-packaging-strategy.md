## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: bundling + tiering + closed-loop + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Bundle**: core / add-on / option; do not omit
4. **Tier**: starter / professional / enterprise; do not omit
5. **Optimize**: test / adjust / evaluate; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: gradual from bundling → tiering → closed-loop → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with product-pricing**: Product Packaging + Product Pricing co-built
13. **Link with product-monetization**: Product Packaging + Product Monetization co-built
14. **Link with product-strategy**: Product Packaging + Product Strategy co-built
15. **Link with product-portfolio**: Product Packaging + Product Portfolio co-built
16. **Link with configure-price-quote**: Product Packaging + CPQ co-built
17. **Toolchain**: Chargebee / Zuora / Stripe Billing / Paddle / Productboard
18. **Publicly queryable**: everyone can look up the strategy; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why Product Packaging is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can a single version solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: simpler Product Packaging is better; cut redundant layers
