## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: sources + collection + analysis + governance + measure; no missing dimensions
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not empty slogans
3. **Source**: primary / secondary / tertiary / expert; do not omit
4. **Collect**: scrape / subscribe / buy / exchange; do not omit
5. **Analyze**: clean / tag / cluster / prioritize; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from source → collection → analysis → governance → measure progressively; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: data is only the starting point; not the end
10. **Not empty slogans**: each principle must mark landing evidence; no ambiguity
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with competitive-intelligence**: market + competitive co-build
13. **Link with market-analysis**: market + analysis co-build
14. **Link with trend-research**: market + trends co-build
15. **Link with market-research**: market + research co-build
16. **Link with innovation-research**: market + innovation co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Crunchbase / PitchBook / CB Insights / PrivCo / AlphaSense
18. **Publicly accessible**: strategy is accessible to all; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolves and updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a market intelligence strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: market intelligence simpler is better; cut redundant layers
