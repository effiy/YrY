## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: dimension + segment + persona + application + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **business-value driven**: prioritize by revenue share + LTV + CAC + retention + penetration; not sloganeering
3. **Dimensions**: demographic + firmographic + behavioral + needs + value; do not omit
4. **Segments**: TAM / SAM / SOM + segment size + growth + reachable + serviceable; do not omit
5. **Persona**: customer + decision-maker + influencer + user + detractor; do not omit
6. **Apply**: product + marketing + sales + customer service + strategy; do not omit
7. **Measure**: revenue share + LTV + CAC + retention + penetration; do not omit
8. **not one-shot**: progressive from dimension → segment → persona → application → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with customer-segmentation**: market + customer segmentation co-built
13. **Link with customer-insights**: market + insights co-built
14. **Link with ideal-customer-profile**: market + ICP co-built
15. **Link with product-strategy**: market + product strategy co-built
16. **Link with go-to-market**: market + GTM co-built
17. **Toolchain**: 6sense / Demandbase / ZoomInfo / Clearbit / Apollo / Bombora / Cognism / LinkedIn Sales Navigator
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **first principles**: why must market segmentation strategy; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **inversion thinking**: how much can mass marketing solve; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (cost / risk / channel / business) 
23. **Occam**: market segmentation the simpler the better; cut redundant steps
