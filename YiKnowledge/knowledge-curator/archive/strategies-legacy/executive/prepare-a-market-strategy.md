## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identify + assess + choose + govern + measure; no missing dimensions
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not empty slogans
3. **Identify**: size / growth / trends / boundaries; do not omit
4. **Assess**: attractiveness / competition / entry / win-probability; do not omit
5. **Choose**: prioritize / exit / innovate / convert; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from identify → assess → choose → govern → measure progressively; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: size is only the starting point; not the end
10. **Not empty slogans**: each principle must mark landing evidence; no ambiguity
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with marketing**: market + marketing co-build
13. **Link with competitive**: market + competition co-build
14. **Link with business**: market + business strategy co-build
15. **Link with positioning**: market + positioning co-build
16. **Link with customer-segmentation**: market + customer segmentation co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Gartner / Forrester / IDC / Statista / IBISWorld
18. **Publicly accessible**: strategy is accessible to all; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolves and updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why a market strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: market strategy simpler is better; cut redundant layers
