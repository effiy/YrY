## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: structure + players + evolution + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Structure**: five forces / value chain / boundary / ecosystem; do not omit
4. **Players**: leaders / challengers / niches / new entrants; do not omit
5. **Evolution**: S-curve / cadence / disruption / reshape; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progress from structure → players → evolution → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: Porter is only the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with market-analysis**: industry + market co-built
13. **Link with competitive-intelligence**: industry + competition co-built
14. **Link with market-intelligence**: industry + intelligence co-built
15. **Link with market-forecast**: industry + forecast co-built
16. **Link with trend-research**: industry + trend co-built
17. **Toolchain**: IBISWorld / Statista / Gartner / Forrester / Bain
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why industry analysis strategy is necessary; worst consequence of not doing it
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable, do not introduce a heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after the strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk) 
23. **Occam**: the simpler industry analysis is, the better; cut redundant layers
