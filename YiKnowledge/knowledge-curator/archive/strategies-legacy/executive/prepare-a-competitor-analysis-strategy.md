## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: competitors + strengths + weaknesses + tactics + measure; no missing dimensions
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by win-rate + revenue + cycle-time + ACV + renewal; not empty slogans
3. **Competitor**: direct / indirect / adjacent / emerging / substitute; do not omit
4. **Strength**: product / technology / price / service / relationship; do not omit
5. **Weakness**: product / technology / price / service / relationship; do not omit
6. **Tactics**: positioning / attack / defend / retreat / escalate; do not omit
7. **Measure**: win-rate + revenue + cycle-time + ACV + renewal; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from competitor → strength → weakness → tactics → measure progressively; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the starting point; not the end
10. **Not empty slogans**: each principle must mark landing evidence; no ambiguity
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with vendor-analysis**: competitor + vendor analysis co-build
13. **Link with competitive-intelligence**: competitor + intelligence co-build
14. **Link with competitive-positioning**: competitor + positioning co-build
15. **Link with win-loss-analysis**: competitor + win/loss co-build
16. **Link with sales-playbook**: competitor + sales playbook co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Klue / Crayon / Gartner / Forrester / G2 / Salesforce
18. **Publicly accessible**: strategy is accessible to all; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolves and updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why competitor analysis is necessary; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion**: how much can be solved by sales intuition; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / revenue / retention)
23. **Occam**: competitor analysis simpler is better; cut redundant competitors
