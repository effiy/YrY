## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: dimension + modeling + validation + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; not sloganeering
3. **Dimension**: demographic / behavioral / psychographic / value; do not omit
4. **Modeling**: clustering / decision tree / hypothesis / boundary; do not omit
5. **Validation**: identifiable / measurable / reachable / actionable; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: progressive from dimension → modeling → validation → governance → measurement; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: slicing the pie is the start; not the end
10. **Not sloganeering**: every principle must have landing evidence; not vague
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with persona-research**: segmentation + roles co-built
13. **Link with customer-needs**: segmentation + needs co-built
14. **Link with market-analysis**: segmentation + analysis co-built
15. **Link with market-research**: segmentation + research co-built
16. **Link with customer-research**: segmentation + customers co-built
17. **Toolchain**: SPSS / R cluster / SAS EM / Knime / Alteryx
18. **Publicly queryable**: strategy everyone can look up; not hidden
19. **Periodic review**: evolution updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must there be a market-segmentation-research strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion thinking**: how much can be solved by default; if solvable do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: market-segmentation-research simpler is better; cut redundant layers
