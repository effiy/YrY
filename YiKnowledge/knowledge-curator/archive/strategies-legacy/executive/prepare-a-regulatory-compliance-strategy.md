## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: regulations + mapping + filing + governance + measure; no missing dimensions
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; not empty slogans
3. **Regulate**: identify / subscribe / close-loop; do not omit
4. **Map**: clause / business / close-loop; do not omit
5. **File**: reports / deadlines / close-loop; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: efficiency + trust + speed + risk + cost; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from regulation → mapping → filing → governance → measure progressively; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the starting point; not the end
10. **Not empty slogans**: each principle must mark landing evidence; no ambiguity
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with compliance-management**: RegulatoryCompliance + ComplianceManagement co-build
13. **Link with policy-management**: RegulatoryCompliance + PolicyManagement co-build
14. **Link with audit-compliance**: RegulatoryCompliance + AuditCompliance co-build
15. **Link with risk**: RegulatoryCompliance + Risk co-build
16. **Link with records-management**: RegulatoryCompliance + RecordsManagement co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Thomson Reuters / LexisNexis / Compliance.ai / Encompass / RegHub
18. **Publicly accessible**: strategy is accessible to all; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolves and updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why RegulatoryCompliance is necessary; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion**: how much can be solved by outsourcing; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (efficiency / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam**: RegulatoryCompliance simpler is better; cut redundant filings
