## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: competitors + dimensions + signals + governance + measure; no missing dimensions
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by insight + win-rate + revenue + speed + risk; not empty slogans
3. **Competitor**: direct / indirect / adjacent / emerging / substitute; do not omit
4. **Dimension**: product / price / channel / customer / finance; do not omit
5. **Signal**: hiring / patents / earnings / releases / investment; do not omit
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; do not omit
7. **Measure**: insight + win-rate + revenue + speed + risk; do not omit
8. **Not one-shot**: from competitor → dimension → signal → governance → measure progressively; no skipping
9. **Not report-ized**: reports are only the starting point; not the end
10. **Not empty slogans**: each principle must mark landing evidence; no ambiguity
11. **Versioned**: strategy has versions; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with competitor-analysis**: Research + Analysis co-build
13. **Link with competitive-intelligence**: Research + CI co-build
14. **Link with battle-card**: Research + Battle Card co-build
15. **Link with positioning**: Research + Positioning co-build
16. **Link with market-trends**: Research + Trends co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Klue / Crayon / Crossbeam / SEMrush / SimilarWeb
18. **Publicly accessible**: strategy is accessible to all; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolves and updates; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why Research is necessary; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion**: how much can be solved by sales experience; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequences after strategy (cost / risk / win-rate / revenue)
23. **Occam**: Research simpler is better; cut redundant competitors
