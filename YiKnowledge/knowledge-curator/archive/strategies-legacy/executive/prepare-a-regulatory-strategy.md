## Action recommendations

1. **Five dimensions**: identify + interpret + implement + governance + measurement; no missing dimension
2. **Business-value driven**: prioritize by growth + trust + speed + risk + cost; no empty slogans
3. **Identify**: regulation / change / impact / scope; no leakage
4. **Interpret**: requirements / exceptions / practical operations / case studies; no leakage
5. **Implement**: policy / process / controls / evidence; no leakage
6. **Governance**: owner / cadence / review / documentation / drift; no leakage
7. **Measurement**: coverage + adoption + cost + risk + satisfaction; no leakage
8. **Not one-shot**: from identify -> interpret -> implement -> governance -> measurement gradual; no skipping levels
9. **No report-ism**: compliance is only the start; not the end
10. **No empty slogans**: every principle must mark implementation evidence; no vagueness
11. **Versioned**: strategy is versioned; evolution is traceable
12. **Link with legal**: regulation + legal co-build
13. **Link with compliance**: regulation + compliance co-build
14. **Link with governance**: regulation + governance co-build
15. **Link with risk**: regulation + risk co-build
16. **Link with policy**: regulation + policy co-build
17. **Toolchain**: Thomson Reuters / LexisNexis / OneTrust / Compliance.ai / Navex Global
18. **Publicly discoverable**: strategy is publicly discoverable; not hidden
19. **Regular review**: evolve and update; not one-shot
20. **First principles**: why must regulation strategy; worst consequence of not doing
21. **Inversion**: rely on defaults to solve how much; if solvable, do not introduce heavy strategy
22. **Second-order thinking**: second-order consequence after strategy (growth / trust / speed / risk)
23. **Occam's razor**: simpler regulation is better; cut redundant layers
