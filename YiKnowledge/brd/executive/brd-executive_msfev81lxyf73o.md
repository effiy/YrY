## Context
Core AI market trend forecast for the first half of 2026. Five major trends: reasoning model proliferation / Agent adoption / multimodal commercialization / on-device AI / open-source catching up with closed-source. 

## Decision
Increase AI investment: YiAi BRD agent + YiPet aicr; follow up on reasoning models + Agent + multimodal trends. 

## Alternatives Considered
B. Maintain status quo — miss the AI wave; C. Full self-build — cost out of control; D. Full buy — vendor lock-in. A selected (selective self-build + buy combined). 

## Expected Outcomes
3 AI products launched; BRD writing efficiency -79%; extension DAU +30%; after-sales CSAT 4.5+; AI share of revenue 20%+
