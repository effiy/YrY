# data retention strategy

**Decision Type**: strategic | **Scope**: company | **Horizon**: annual | **Status**: stable
**Strategic Theme**: Data Governance | **OKR**: O7: data governance / KR1: retention strategy 100% landed | **Owner**: CTO + Legal | **Investment**: $0

## Context
data retention strategy: user data / log / backup / model output. Need to comply with GDPR / privacy protection law + cost controllable.

## Decision
data retention strategy: user data 90 days / log 30 days / backup 1 year / model output 30 days; quarterly review + automated execution.

## Alternatives Considered
B. retain all data permanently — cost out of control; C. retain all data short-term — compliance risk. A chosen (partition by type + 90 days/30 days/1 year/30 days).

## Expected Outcomes
retention strategy 100% landed; quarterly review; automated execution; compliance passed

CTO + Legal (Decision); CFO (cost); engineering Team (execution)
