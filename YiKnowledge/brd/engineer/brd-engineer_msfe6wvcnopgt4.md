# BRD-2026-066 Internal Developer Platform and DevEx Build-out

**BRD ID**: BRD-2026-066  |  **Project**: yipet  |  **Domain**: Developer Experience (DevEx)  |  **Quarter**: 2026 Q3
**Priority**: P1  |  **Status**: In Progress  |  **Owner**: DevEx Team
**KB Source**: engineer/projects/yipet/brd/brd-2026-066-developer-experience

## Context
Via Backstage + Golden Path + Service Catalog + AI assist + Plugin governance, reduce new hire onboarding from 14 days to 5 days, R&D NPS from 12 to 50 within 3 years, self-service rate 80%, platform adoption 95%.

## Objectives & Key Metrics
New hire onboarding 14d→5d (down 64%); R&D NPS 12→50 (up 317%); self-service 0%→80%; documentation scattered 3→1 locations; scaffolding 5→1 sets; Service Catalog 217 resources 100% coverage; platform adoption 0%→95%; ROI 0.76x + intangible value

## Acceptance Criteria
1. End of Q3 adoption 65% + new hire 8 days + NPS 35
2. End of Q4 adoption 80% + new hire 6 days
3. 2027-Q4 new hire 5 days + NPS 50 + self-service 80% + adoption 95%
4. Champion 5 people complete in 90 days, Service Catalog 217 resources 100% coverage
5. Documentation unified to 1 location, scaffolding unified to 1 set
6. Platform adoption 95%

## Stakeholders
CTO Office (decision + OKR 15%); DevEx Team 2 FTE (execution); 5 business teams (consumption); SRE/DevOps (operations); HR (recruiting); Champion 5 people (driving); Backstage Community (support)

## Milestones
M1 (2026 Q3): DevEx team formed + Backstage MVP + Champion recruitment
M2 (2026 Q4): Service Catalog 217 resources 100% + adoption 80%
M3 (2027 Q1): Champion 5 people complete in 90 days + adoption 85%
M4 (2027 Q2): AI assist pilot + adoption 90%
M5 (2027 Q3): Golden Path full coverage + adoption 95%
M6 (2027 Q4): Self-service 80% + new hire 5 days + NPS 50

## Risks
1. Aggressive goal (Dec new hire 5 days + NPS 50) (P1) — CTO quarterly OKR + Champion mentoring
2. Documentation unification resistance (P1) — SCP + 30-day alert + Champion training
3. Scaffolding migration (P1) — 5 team Champions each own 1 team
4. Plugin governance (P2) — usage < 30% retire + quarterly review
5. Weak permission model (P2) — custom plugin + OPA
6. AI assist (P2) — 2027-Q2 LLM integration
7. ROI 0.76x not high (P2) — intangible value + long-term value persuasion

## Long-term Evolution
3 years later: new hire onboarding 5 days, R&D NPS 50, self-service 80%, documentation unified to 1 location, scaffolding unified to 1 set, Service Catalog 100% coverage, platform adoption 95%.
