## Anti-patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why it fails | What to do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Manager sets the agenda | Becomes a status update meeting, not a coaching session | Report owns the agenda; manager asks questions |
| No feedback exchange | 1on1 becomes a friendly chat with no growth | Give and ask for specific feedback at least once a month |
| Status update disguised as 1on1 | Wastes the only dedicated time for growth and relationship | Status updates go in standup or async; 1on1 is for growth, blockers, feedback |
| No running notes | Each meeting starts from zero; long-term growth isn't tracked | Keep a running doc with open topics and growth goals |
