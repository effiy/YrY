# BRD-2026-064 Cost Optimization and FinOps Platform Build — Business Targets and Success Standards

> **As an** engineer, **I want to** objectives, **so that** project context preserved.

## 1. Project background and targets
Business targets: (1) monthly bill reduced from $3.8K to $1.9K (down 50%); (2) cost attribution rate raised from 42% to 99%+; (3) Tag coverage raised from 42% to 99%+; (4) Champion coverage 100% of teams; (5) optimization execution rate 80%+; (6) quarterly QBR held 100%; (7) per-request cost down 30%. Success standards: end of Q3 bill < $2K, end of Q4 < $1.5K, 2027-Q2 < $1K. ROI: investment $0 + 2 FTE × 12 months = $192K, savings $3.8K × 50% × 12 = $22.8K/month × 12 = $273K/year, ROI 1.4x.

## 2. Quantitative metrics and data
Target quantification: Q1 monthly bill $3.8K → Q2 $3K → Q3 $2.4K → Q4 $1.9K → 2027-Q1 $1.5K → 2027-Q2 $1K. Attribution rate: Q1 42% → Q2 70% → Q3 90% → Q4 99%+. Tag coverage: Q1 42% → Q2 80% → Q3 95% → Q4 99%+. Champion 5-person 90-day plan: 2026-09-01 recruitment complete → 2026-12-01 5 people complete W12 → 2027-03-01 5 people all authenticated. ROI: investment $0 + 2 FTE × 12 months = $192K, savings $22.8K/month × 12 = $273K/year, ROI 1.4x, payback within 12 months.

## 3. Execution path and challenges
Execution challenges: aggressive targets (12-month 50% reduction); slow Champion development (90-day plan); Tag enforcement resistance (engineers habitually not tagging); Cost Center OKR tie-in resistance (engineers feel cost is ops' affair); first QBR engineers unprepared with data (resolved via Champion submitting two weeks before quarter-end); ROI 1.4x not high (resolved via savings amount + long-term value persuasion). Improvements: CTO quarterly OKR weight 15% + team bonus pool + Champion 90-day plan + 12 case studies + 4 workshops.

## 4. Long-term evolution and strategy
Long-term evolution: (1) rolling targets — quarterly review + adjust; (2) AI prediction — ML predicts quarterly cost + optimization items; (3) targets tied to business — per-user cost as a SaaS metric; (4) cross-company benchmarking — FinOps Foundation. 24-month target: targets 100% rolling + AI prediction ±5% + business tie-in 100% + cross-company 1 time/quarter.
