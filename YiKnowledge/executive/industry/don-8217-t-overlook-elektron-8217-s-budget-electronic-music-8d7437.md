<p class="wp-block-paragraph">When I'm asked what to buy if you want to get into making electronic music, I often recommend Elektron's budget-minded <a href="https://www.elektron.se/explore/modelsamples">Model:Samples</a> and <a href="https://www.elektron.se/explore/modelcycles">Model:Cycles</a> grooveboxes. They don't grab headlines the way <a href="https://www.theverge.com/reviews/608609/teenage-engineering-op-xy-review">Teenage Engineering's gear</a> or the <a href="https://www.theverge.com/entertainment/967485/telepathic-instruments-orchid-tame-impala-synth-review">Telepathic Instruments Orchid</a> do, and even compared to the company's higher-end and more niche musical offerings like the <a href="https://www.elektron.se/explore/digitakt-ii">Digitakt</a>, <a href="https://www.elektron.se/explore/octatrack-mkii">Octatrack</a>, or <a href="https://www.theverge.com/news/776487/elektron-tonverk-polyphonic-sampler-groovebox">Tonverk</a>, they're often overlooked. But as my collection has grown to include fancier and far more expensive instruments, I still find myself coming back to the Model series.</p>
