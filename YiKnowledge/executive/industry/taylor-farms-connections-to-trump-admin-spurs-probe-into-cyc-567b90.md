<p>As Americans cook their vegetables to at least 158°F to kill a diarrheal parasite that has sickened over 24,000 people and killed two this summer, one US Senator is turning up the heat on Health Secretary Robert F. Kenney Jr. over the government's "calamitous response" to the record outbreak.</p>
<p>In <a href="https://www.warren.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Final-Letter-to-RFK-on-Cyclospora-Outbreak-Response.pdf">a letter sent Thursday</a>, Elizabeth Warren (D-Mass.) called the federal response to the <em>Cyclospora </em>outbreak "botched" and "marred by missteps." She pointedly questioned whether corruption and incompetence within the Trump administration and its clear ties to Taylor Farms—the mammoth produce distributer that sold Mexican-grown iceberg lettuce linked to the outbreak—played a role in the federal government's failures.</p>
<p>"[P]olitical contributions and direct White House lobbying from Taylor Farms... raise urgent questions about the role this influence-peddling may have played in loosening federal food safety oversight and hampering the federal government’s response to the outbreak," Warren wrote in the letter.</p><p><a href="https://arstechnica.com/health/2026/08/taylor-farms-connections-to-trump-admin-spurs-probe-into-cyclospora-response/">Read full article</a></p>
