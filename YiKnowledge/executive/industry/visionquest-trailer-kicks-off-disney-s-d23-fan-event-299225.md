<p>It was a busy first day at D23: The Ultimate Disney Fan Event, in Anaheim, California, with Marvel Studios releasing the first trailer for <em>VisionQuest</em>. We also got the first teaser for <em>Ahsoka</em> S2; the public release of the <em>Avengers: Doomsday</em> special footage featured last month for attendees only at San Diego Comic-Con; a special sneak peek (for attendees only) for <em>Star Wars: Starfighter</em>; casting news for the MCU's <em>X-Men</em> movie; and a trailer for an animated special, <em>LEGO Star Wars: The Mandalorian</em>, coming to Disney+ next month.</p>
<p><strong>(Some spoilers below. One MAJOR spoiler for <em>Spider-Man: Brand New Day</em>; we'll give you a heads-up when we get there.)</strong></p>
<h2><em>VisionQuest</em></h2>
<div class="ars-video ars-video--horizontal"><div><div class="relative" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/oSVTAUKyrL8?start=0&amp;wmode=transparent"></div></div></div>
<p>Marvel's streaming TV portfolio has kind of been all over the place: a handful of hits, a few misses, and a bunch of middling offerings. One of the standout hits was <em>WandaVision</em>, followed by an excellent sequel (of sorts), <em>Agatha All Along</em>. <em>VisionQuest</em> will complete the trilogy. Yes, Vision (Paul Bettany) died in <em>Infinity War</em>, but <em>WandaVision</em> resurrected an all-white version whose memories were restored in the finale. We finally get to learn what happened afterward.</p><p><a href="https://arstechnica.com/culture/2026/08/visionquest-trailer-kicks-off-disneys-d23-fan-event/">Read full article</a></p>
