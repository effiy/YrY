## Anti-patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why it fails | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Report without data | Opinions dressed as analysis; no basis for decisions | Every claim must cite a data source or be marked `[HYPOTHESIS]` |
| Annual reports in fast-moving markets | 12-month-old data is irrelevant in AI | Quarterly reports for fast-moving industries; monthly trend monitoring |
| Copying analyst reports | Regurgitating Gartner/Forrester without synthesis | Add your own analysis: what does this mean for OUR business? |
| No recommended actions | Report is interesting but not actionable | Every report must end with 3-5 concrete recommended actions |
