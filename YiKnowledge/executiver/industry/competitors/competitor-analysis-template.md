## Anti-patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why it fails | What to do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Competitor analysis as a one-time exercise | Market shifts; competitors evolve; analysis rots | Update quarterly; track competitor changes via RSS/alerts |
| Copying competitors feature-for-feature | You're always behind; no differentiation | Learn from competitors but build what they can't or won't |
| Dismissing competitors ("they're not real competition") | Underestimating competitors leads to surprises | Be honest about strengths and weaknesses; assume competence |
| Analysis without action | Insights sit in a document; nothing changes | Every analysis must produce at least 3 concrete action items |
